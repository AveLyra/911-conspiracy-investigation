# FBI explosives tests and the Urban Moving Systems van: results and sample custody

**Answer:** The examined FBI records report a canine alert, swabbing and a laboratory submission, with final results pending in September 2001. No final positive or negative chemistry result has been located in the records reviewed. This does not establish whether testing was completed or what it found. [FBI lab record review](../urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/README.md).

## What the records say

A September 23 memorandum reports the alert and pending final results. The inventory lists fabric and blanket samples intended for explosives-residue examination and separate controls. A 2003 direction to discontinue outstanding laboratory leads does not specify whether testing had been completed or canceled. An alert and a sample label are not chemical identifications. [September 2001 memo, PDF p. 44](../urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-5.pdf#page=44) · [Inventory, PDF p. 7](../urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-1.pdf#page=7).

A January 2014 communication reports disposal of items associated with **E01889035, E01889036 and E01889037**. It does not provide the chemical result, a separate destruction date for each item or a full accession-to-sample crosswalk. Disposal does not itself establish an attempt to conceal a finding; routine authorized disposal and other explanations require records. The inventory identifies glove and bomb-suit swabs as controls, not proof the detainees possessed bomb suits. [Disposition review and source excerpt](../urban-moving-systems/van-sample-disposition-recheck-2026-11-06/README.md).

## What to seek

Original laboratory accession and submission records, methods, blanks and controls, final reports, retained extracts, custody and disposal authorization could resolve the examination and disposition. Even a verified positive result would require contamination, provenance and attribution analysis and an independent link to any alleged WTC placement. [Records test](../urban-moving-systems/explosive-record-test-2026-10-29/README.md).
