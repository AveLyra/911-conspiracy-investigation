# Urban Moving Systems and 9/11: FBI records and the Mossad allegation

**Answer:** FBI records establish an investigation of five men associated with Urban Moving Systems (UMS) after September 11, 2001. They do not establish that UMS was a Mossad cover, that the men had advance knowledge, or that they participated in the attacks.

## What the investigation documented

A New Jersey police stop and the FBI investigation generated interviews, property inventories, vehicle and laboratory records, and intelligence inquiries. Newark's July 2003 closure reported no basis for finding advance knowledge. Its statement that no pictures developed from film in one identified 35 mm camera showed the towers before the attack addresses that film; it does not establish the contents of every possible image. [Police report](../urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/police.pdf) · [Newark closure review](../urban-moving-systems/newark-closure-followup-2026-11-05/README.md).

An early FBI assessment reported no active clandestine activity by the five within its scope, and a linguist found no intelligence significance in the particular personal materials reviewed. An April 2004 record described a separate headquarters matter as still pending; its final disposition remains unlocated in the reviewed materials. Pending status is not an affirmative finding of intelligence affiliation. [Intelligence and counterevidence review](../urban-moving-systems/counterevidence-and-records-2026-11-06/README.md).

## What would test the allegation?

The final headquarters assessment, the August 2002 visit report, underlying database hits with subject crosswalks, tasking records, and original interviews could distinguish an intelligence connection from an ordinary moving business or an unsuccessful investigation. Actual commercial moving work is also documented; commercial activity alone cannot rule out covert work. A verified affiliation alone would not prove advance knowledge or participation.
