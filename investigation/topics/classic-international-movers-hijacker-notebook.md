# Classic International Movers, the FBI notebook and the unnamed 9/11 hijacker lead

**Answer:** FBI summaries report that Classic International Movers' number appeared in a notebook seized from the detained men and that Miami believed Classic had moved an unidentified hijacker. The reviewed records do not establish the customer, a transaction, a call or coordinated activity. [Newark and Classic source review](../urban-moving-systems/newark-closure-followup-2026-11-05/README.md).

## What is known and what is missing

A September 15 FBI communication reports Miami's belief and the detention and interviewing of four Classic employees. A September 23 account reports the notebook number. These accounts may repeat the same underlying lead; repetition is not independent confirmation. The originating Miami lead, its source and reasoning, the actual notebook entry, who wrote it and any contact remain unresolved. [FBI September 15 communication, section 1, PDF p. 39](../urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-1.pdf#page=39) · [September 23 memorandum, section 5, PDF p. 50](../urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-5.pdf#page=50).

An address and U.S. entry-date list was compiled for the five detainees and another for hijackers and suspected associates. The available form does not report whether the lists matched; the underlying lists and comparison remain missing. A shared date or location alone would not establish coordination. [FBI list review](../urban-moving-systems/wtc-hijacker-source-recheck-2026-11-06/README.md).

## What would change the assessment?

Recover Miami's originating lead and disposition, employee interviews, the original notebook page and translation, customer and payment records, and phone records identifying participants. A reliable mismatch or negative comparison with known coverage would weaken the particular contact lead. A verified transaction would establish a narrower business relationship requiring separate evidence of knowledge or purpose. Direct UMS–hijacker contact is not a necessary part of the project's compartmented hypothesis.
