# Developing 9/11 theory: UMS, intelligence, WTC access and evidence tests

**Answer:** The working hypothesis proposes a compartmented operation involving particular U.S. and Israeli intelligence-linked actors, possible Saudi-linked involvement, and a possible UMS role in placing destructive material at the WTC. The evidence reviewed does **not** establish that operation, UMS's alleged Mossad affiliation, explosives placement, or a shared preattack plan. [Full claim ledger](../urban-moving-systems/developing-theory-2026-11-06/README.md) · [Cross-chat assessment](../cross-chat-evidence-integration-2026-11-10/README.md).

The hypothesis does not require UMS personnel to have met or coordinated directly with the hijackers. The working assumption that hijackers probably did not know of an alleged Israeli role is speculative, not a calculated probability. Saudi authorities' knowledge of any wider plan remains open; knowledge should not be imputed to an entire government or population.

Independent ordinary moving work, separate intelligence activity, Saudi-linked assistance, agency failures, fire-driven structural collapse and subsequent political exploitation can explain aspects of the record without a single coordinated plan. A negative UMS–hijacker contact search would not alone disprove a compartmented hypothesis; conversely, a badge, an intelligence affiliation or a later benefit would not establish the wider operation. Each proposed link can be tested separately.
