# January 2000 CIA hold and Saudi-linked support for 9/11 hijackers

**Answer:** A CIA hold on a particular draft intended for the FBI is documented. Saudi-linked assistance to some hijackers is supported by substantial records, but the hold's purpose and any coordination with a wider operation remain unresolved. No authenticated connection to Urban Moving Systems has been established in the reviewed material. [DOJ Inspector General](https://oig.justice.gov/sites/default/files/archive/special/s0606/chapter5.htm) · [Integrated source review](../cross-chat-evidence-integration-2026-11-10/README.md#cia-hold-and-saudi-linked-assistance).

## The hold and information sharing

In January 2000 a CIA officer held a draft containing information about Khalid al-Mihdhar's U.S. visa that was intended for FBI dissemination. Some Malaysia-related information nevertheless reached FBI personnel. The hold is therefore not proof of a total information blackout, a recruitment operation, advance knowledge of the attacks or deliberate permission for them. Mistaken assumptions about prior sharing, compartmentation, organizational failure and protection of an operation are possibilities to test against the original decision and transmission records. [DOJ review](https://oig.justice.gov/sites/default/files/archive/special/s0606/chapter5.htm).

## Saudi-linked assistance and competing findings

A 2017 FBI record reports a Saudi intelligence relationship involving Omar al-Bayoumi, while relevant source details remain redacted. The FBI's 2021 closure assessment and a 2025 federal court's preliminary jurisdictional findings addressed different questions and applied different standards. The court's preliminary inferences about assistance were not a final liability verdict or a finding that Saudi leadership ordered the attacks. Neither establishes the proposed U.S.–Israeli–Saudi operation. [FBI material](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11-attacks-investigation-and-related-materials/9-11-material-released-in-response-to-executive-order-14040/documents-responsive-to-executive-order-14040-section-2-b-ii-part-01-of-01) · [2025 ruling](https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/2025-08/03-md-01570%2011182.pdf).

## Next records

The original draft, hold instruction, routing and receipt trail, contemporaneous liaison communications, source statements and payment records could distinguish competing explanations. A reliably documented routine reason and complete transmission trail would weigh differently from authenticated instructions showing intentional protection of a specific operation. Knowledge of assistance, attack plans and an alleged wider plan are distinct questions.
