## Observed building motion and model validation

- **Predicted upper-wall deformation is absent from the footage.** NIST acknowledges a late-stage model/observation mismatch and omitted contributions from cladding, partitions and interior walls. Its implications for initiation and later propagation must be tested separately. [M001–M003](MASTER-LEADS.md#m001).
- **Penthouse and roofline timing do not uniformly agree.** Published comparisons use different event definitions and alignment conventions; the reported screenwall/west-penthouse ordering can reverse. A simple clock shift cannot fix a genuine same-event reversal, but matched views and events remain necessary. [M004–M008](MASTER-LEADS.md#m004), [M068](MASTER-LEADS.md#m068).
- **The approximately 2.25-second free-fall interval requires a force-accounted sequence.** Lower-floor support failure can in principle permit upper stories to approach gravitational acceleration. The unresolved question is whether the specified historical sequence reproduces the observed interval, coherence and residual forces; a roof point is not automatically a fixed body's center of mass. [M009](MASTER-LEADS.md#m009), [M067](MASTER-LEADS.md#m067).
- **Some printed timing comparisons contain internal offsets or event ambiguities.** Panel/caption clocks, displacement endpoints and a measured/predicted 12.9-second comparison need run/event provenance. An inferred interior-event proxy is not an independently observed initiating event. [M085](MASTER-LEADS.md#m085), [M093–M096](MASTER-LEADS.md#m093), [M129](MASTER-LEADS.md#m129).
- **Current motion measurements remain conditional.** Saved-project versus printed series alter fitted costs; image-localization diagnostics do not yet supply calibrated material-point trajectories. Original camera clocks, geometry, edit/cadence history and unused validation observations remain important. [M118–M119](MASTER-LEADS.md#m118).
- **“Within its own footprint” has not been established by this investigation.** FEMA maps/photos and the received human observations supply testable geometry and provenance questions; they do not substitute for a measured footprint or a full failure-sequence comparison. [Consolidated lead 36](CONSOLIDATED-AND-RECENT.md#consolidated-36).

- **Column 44 has a precise event-sequence question.** Bolt and buckling times must be reconciled with the narrative using actual component states and run identity. Those are accelerated-calculation times, not independently measured historical failure times. [M045](MASTER-LEADS.md#m045).
- **Cross-core and exterior propagation require quantitative load paths.** The floor cascade, truss counterfactual, exterior unloading/sway and Floor 5/substation boundaries cannot be validated by a verbal domino sequence. - **Material-law and fracture questions have both positive and contrary controls.** Later fracture research motivates testing; an apparent published A36/A572 duplication was not reproduced in the examined released definitions. A populated field/old-manual mismatch does not select the historical solver interpretation. [M082](MASTER-LEADS.md#m082), [M120–M121](MASTER-LEADS.md#m120).
- **“Conservative” is response-specific.** Some assumptions enhance capacity while others remove mechanisms. Matching free thermal expansion does not guarantee matching restrained forces; a synthetic counterexample demonstrates that inference limit, not an identified error in NIST's historical run. [M061](MASTER-LEADS.md#m061), [M087–M088](MASTER-LEADS.md#m087).
