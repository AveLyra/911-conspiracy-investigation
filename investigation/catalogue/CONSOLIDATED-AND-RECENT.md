# Consolidated leads and recent breadcrumbs
This is a source-backed research catalogue, compiled October 29, 2026. The evaluation chat began September 2, 2026. Coverage is against the frozen registers listed in PUBLICATION.json; it is not a claim to have replayed every historical chat turn or independently checked every primary source.

The entries overlap. A file discrepancy, a reported event, an allegation, a missing input, a model-dependent result and a failed control are different kinds of record. Counts are not independent anomalies or probabilities of conspiracy. Source-level checking, historical authenticity, physical replication, intent and cause remain separate. Read each note with its limits and the linked owning record. Existing stable IDs are retained; this is navigation, not a replacement authority.

External citations are inherited from the held research. They are not represented as freshly fetched or independently authenticated in this compilation. Local citations point to pinned entry copies and, where resolvable, their owning reports. Missing or unavailable originals remain open.

The 49 consolidated items below retain their October 28 wording. Operational states in that snapshot are historical; newer owning reports and the recent source units govern later acquisition/completion claims. The thirteen following items are the immediately preceding recent-findings view, not thirteen new independent leads.
