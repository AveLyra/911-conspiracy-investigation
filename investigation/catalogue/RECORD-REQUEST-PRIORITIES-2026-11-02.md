## RR-FBI-01 — high: original Ong media, transcription and routing evidence

The [official FBI September release, part 04](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11%20Commission%20Report/9-11-interviews-2001-09-sep-04-of-08) supplies unusually specific retrieval keys. Physical page 10, lead controls **DL267/CE66**, describes material copied from a CD-ROM onto analog tape for transcription. Physical page 7 of the **DL-336** interview/playback record reports an inquiry into why recording ended despite the emergency button reportedly remaining on. Potentially corresponding passages print passenger on physical page 5 and purser on physical page 12. These are documentary observations, not independently heard speech.

Seek the following existing FBI-held or controlled records:

- The airline-supplied CD-ROM and retained native files, analog transcription tape, recorded retrospective interview/playback session, and source/track/segment identifiers.
- Acquisition and evidence inventories, copying/duplication logs, equipment/settings, generation history, custody transfers and existing preservation or actual disposition records.
- Transcript drafts, transcriber notes, review/correction records and existing source-to-transcript mappings for DL267/CE66 and DL-336.
- Engineering findings, communications-check records, timer/configuration information and alternate-recording search results supplied to or retained by the FBI concerning the reported cutoff. The airline's internal inquiry is not automatically an FBI-held record.
- Marquis's original interview/source notes, the supplied notes and annotated crew manifest, the referenced FD-340 envelope/item crosswalk, and any recording-verification result or notification to the FBI. Do not invent an envelope number.
- Existing received carrier, switch, bridge, conference and receiving-office records that identify the relevant call legs, participants, routing and clock definitions.

**What this could test:** whether printed differences reflect transcription or source coverage; whether copying changed recoverable content; what explains the cutoff; and whether transfer and relay descriptions concern the same call phase. Match actual source segments before comparing words or durations.

**Contrary findings and alternatives:** ordinary rerecording, unclear speech, transcription choices, different call phases and an evolving technical inquiry remain plausible. The later reported timer explanation must remain beside the earlier inquiry. Neither a transcript difference nor a reported unanswered technical question establishes altered audio. Do not combine approximate call and recording durations into a missing-minutes total.

**Open TODO:** acquire and identify the actual source generations, then perform a bounded source-aligned comparison with explicit uncertainty. Published source reports: [conversion/cutoff and transcript findings](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/17aef02c7d79169234dc04c4a67f38425900ea30/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-transcript-generation-chain-2026-11-02/README.md) and [routing and original-note targets](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/24aa45f77933861bfbe240b40d3bbcf59e4fd9b0/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-transfer-relay-records-2026-11-02/README.md).

## RR-FAA-01 — high: Boston tape identities, copying and delivery

The source review identifies **A90-71, A90-74, A90-98 and A90-99**. A90-99 reports two tapes; A90-71 lists one TMU tape and two OSIC tapes, with the OS numeral absent. The reports have not been joined to one output batch. A90-71 also discloses skip-silence processing; a held historical order extract describes continuous direct copying in a specified context. Their applicability and copy-generation relationships remain unresolved.

Seek existing FAA records identifying the source originals and each derivative: tape/file identifiers, channel and time coverage, batch and enclosure manifests, copying instructions and logs, skip-silence settings, preparation and approval records, custody/preservation instructions, and dispatch/receipt acknowledgments. Include the full applicable historical orders/amendments and source-specific approvals or preservation/release notices. A general retention schedule does not establish actual disposition.

Include the collection instructions and collected-item/response crosswalk underlying A90-74. Its certification says employees were asked for material; it does not certify complete responses. Preserve the distinction between required government records and encouraged personal notes. Do not infer a response rate from unexplained staffing-sheet marks.

**What this could test:** whether the tape quantities concern the same batch, whether speech survived shortening, which copying rules governed each generation, and what actually reached the recipient. Copying, dispatch, receipt and completeness are different propositions.

**Contrary findings and alternatives:** different batches or reporting cutoffs could explain the quantities. A continuous preserved original and a shortened listening copy could serve different purposes. Neither arrangement has been authenticated here. No lost-tape count, missing speech, rule violation or intentional suppression is established.

**Open TODO:** obtain identifiers and manifests before treating the numerical contrast as a loss, and acquire the original/derivative pair before testing speech retention or timing. [Published source review and exact page/provision locators](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/f350a1fa82e0ab1ebdf8f883a8a400b2bc888a92/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-a90-certification-worklogs-2026-11-01/README.md).

## RR-NARA-01 — medium: hearing preparation, edits and call routing

The [January 27, 2004 hearing, pages 106–107](https://govinfo.library.unt.edu/911/archive/hearing7/9-11Commission_Hearing_2004-01-27.pdf#page=106), contains Kean's introductory description of Gonzalez relaying information between two lines because transferring or patching the call was unavailable. The reproduced Marquis FD-302 describes a transfer. The introduction is not Gonzalez's own firsthand testimony; the same leg, phase and terminology have not been established.

On [page 109](https://govinfo.library.unt.edu/911/archive/hearing7/9-11Commission_Hearing_2004-01-27.pdf#page=109), Kean explicitly announces a small edit to protect a family member and four minutes selected from a separate approximately twenty-minute call. The editing paragraph does not identify the exact file, call leg or interval affected.

Seek Commission-held source recordings and received media inventories; playback masters and exports; edit instructions, decision lists, project/version files, selected and removed intervals, preparation approvals and delivery records; and interview/source records underlying the routing introduction. Request exact archival item/box references and withdrawal notices for located restricted items, then use the appropriate review route.

**What this could test:** how each source became the hearing presentation and whether a specific edit or source generation explains a particular derivative feature. A later court representation is not automatically the same export.

**Contrary findings and alternatives:** editing was disclosed. This weighs against describing it as necessarily secret. Privacy protection and time-limited presentation remain reported purposes, without authenticating the actual intervals or every derivative. Different call phases or shorthand could explain transfer/relay terminology.

**Open TODO:** recover the source-to-hearing mapping and routing evidence before inferring deliberate interference. The Commission's legislative records are not subject to FOIA; use [NARA's archival-access/review procedures](https://www.archives.gov/research/9-11/faqs.html). Executive-agency copies require their own agency-record analysis. [Published source comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/24aa45f77933861bfbe240b40d3bbcf59e4fd9b0/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-transfer-relay-records-2026-11-02/README.md).

## RR-COMMERCE-01 — low: ASCE letter versions and receipt

The reproduced ASCE letter to Commerce Secretary Donald Evans shows **November 15, 2001** on printed page 163 and **September 15, 2001** on continuations 164–165. The owning study confirmed these visible date fields; it did not establish the native letter's date or the cause of the difference.

Seek agency-held authoring files or contemporaneous scans, complete page sets and attachments, materially different versions, incoming correspondence-register entries, routing and acknowledgments from Commerce Secretary correspondence files and NIST-held copies. Records of a congressional submission or assembly are a separate access/custody question; do not presume Commerce holds them.

**What this could test:** whether the inconsistency originated in the letter, a mixed version set or later reproduction. Header reuse, drafting error and assembly differences are ordinary competing explanations. No connection to an error in NIST's collapse calculations is established by this date discrepancy.

**Open TODO:** recover a contemporaneous complete version plus dispatch/receipt evidence. Bundle this with a defined cooperation-record search where useful; do not prioritize it above the original-media and FAA custody tests. ## Overlap controls and execution boundary

The private overlap assessment checked saved submitted wording and the request tracker. This public derivative excludes those payloads, submission receipts, personal details and correspondence. It does not certify the full scope or status of every older request or conduct a new mailbox/portal check.

- An Airphone Usage-report/source-table request does not automatically cover the entire Ong recording, retrospective playback, engineering and transcription chain. Compare actual wording and cross-reference related processing; avoid charging twice for identical records while retaining materially different versions and distinct categories.
- WOCC/DEN configuration and follow-through records concern a different operational subject from the Boston TRACON copying/delivery package. Do not silently substitute one for the other.
- Existing archival inquiries identifying particular Commission sources do not automatically include hearing source audio and edit preparation. Identify the added item-level targets expressly.
- The ASCE letter's 2001 correspondence is distinct from a later recovered-steel contractor task. Preserve each existing request's scope; coordination does not amend, replace, narrow, withdraw or close it.

Request existing records, including native formats and retained metadata where available. Do not ask an agency to create a new transcription, engineering study, reconciliation, clock reconstruction or explanation. FOIA does not require those new analyses: [FOIA.gov guidance](https://www.foia.gov/faq.html). It also does not directly provide access to private-only airline or ASCE files: [DOJ explanation](https://www.justice.gov/oip/about-foia).

All six leads retain their contrary findings and missing-record TODOs. Unknown custody, an access failure or an unrecovered original is not proof of destruction, withholding, fabrication or motive. Publication documents the research plan; it does not submit any of these packages or complete a scientific, historical-action or conspiracy test.
