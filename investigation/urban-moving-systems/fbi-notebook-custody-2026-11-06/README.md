# FBI notebook-group custody and disposition records

**Working research, November 6, 2026.** An FBI translation-return record, inventory and later disposition communication share an evidence barcode associated with a group containing notebooks. This is a group-level custody lead. The reviewed pages do not establish the fate of every notebook, the content or survival of every translation, a final intelligence assessment, or a motive for evidence disposal.

## What the released records show

1. **Translation and return.** An October 4, 2002 FBI communication says a contract linguist considered the translated notebooks, journals and address books personal and found nothing of foreign-intelligence significance. The assessment concerns the materials reviewed and is relevant contrary evidence for an intelligence interpretation of those materials, but does not resolve all seized property or the later headquarters inquiry. The same page lists **1B4 E01889578** among evidence being returned to Newark and says copies of translated documents were attached. The complete translations and an item-to-translation crosswalk remain unlocated. [FBI section 6, PDF pp. 5–6](../fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-6.pdf#page=5).
2. **Inventory.** An FBI property inventory associates barcode **E01889578** with a group including organizers and notebooks alongside other items. Group membership does not identify which individual owned or wrote a notebook or link any specific item to the separately reported Classic International Movers entry. [Selected inventory page, derivative p. 3](raw/fbi-selected-disposition-and-inventory-pages.pdf#page=3).
3. **Reported disposition.** A January 27, 2014 FBI communication says its author destroyed or disposed of listed evidence as requested and includes **E01889578** on its first page and the three separately described van-sample barcodes on its second. This supports a same-barcode disposition trail; it does not establish a separate destruction date for each item, the fate of every individual article or surviving derivative, or an independent chain-of-custody certificate. The communication bears a visible third-party routing label, while case fields are redacted; that label alone does not establish the entity's connection to the UMS investigation. [Selected disposition communication, derivative pp. 1–2](raw/fbi-selected-disposition-and-inventory-pages.pdf#page=1).

The disposition communication reports that the writer sought legal advice. Neither this sentence nor the fact of disposal establishes that the action was proper or improper, that a laboratory result was destroyed, or that anyone sought to conceal a plot. The final chemical result for the separate van specimens remains unlocated. [Van-sample recheck](../van-sample-disposition-recheck-2026-11-06/README.md).
