# Bayoumi intelligence report, Exxon video and possible connections

**Source review, November 5, 2026.** The FBI recorded a specific account of Omar al-Bayoumi's Saudi intelligence relationship; a separate FBI video examination sought to identify white vans near Urban Moving Systems (UMS). The records reviewed do not establish a Bayoumi–UMS connection. No new moving contract, CCTV identification, authenticated communication between them or completed comparison was found.

## The 2017 FBI communication

The June 14, 2017 Washington Field Office electronic communication titled **“Albayoumi / GIP Cooptee,” EO14040-002638–2639**, records that, from the late 1990s until September 11, 2001, Bayoumi received a monthly stipend as a Saudi General Intelligence Presidency (GIP) cooptee via then-Ambassador Prince Bandar bin Sultan. It describes intelligence reporting about persons of interest in Saudi communities being forwarded to Bandar for possible GIP follow-up, and says the information confirmed intelligence-affiliation allegations not confirmed at the time of the 9/11 Commission report. The underlying reporting source and other material are redacted; the public report does not permit independent assessment of the source's access or the complete payment and reporting trail. [FBI communication reproduced in a U.S. Senate hearing, printed pages 55–56](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-118shrg53703/pdf/CHRG-118shrg53703.pdf); [six-page release copy](https://911familiesunited.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Bayoumi-is-GIP-Cooptee.pdf).

Plaintiffs' [2024 public court filing, paragraph 869](https://www.motleyrice.com/sites/default/files/documents/AT_HR/9-11/20240802%20Redacted%20public%20averment.pdf), describes a **separate June 15, 2017** Washington Field Office report; a cited expert report identifies it as **EO14040-003282–3283**. That is a locator from the parties' filing and expert account, not a newly inspected independent source or an independent corroboration of the June 14 report. The records' shared sources, if any, remain unknown.

Bayoumi's assistance to Hazmi and Mihdhar is a separate issue, supported by housing and other records reviewed elsewhere. The 2017 intelligence report does not by itself establish that Bayoumi knew the attack plan, that Saudi leadership ordered the attacks or that he communicated with UMS. Nor does it resolve the differing conclusions in the 2025 jurisdictional ruling and the 2021 FBI closing assessment. See the [integrated research context](../../broader-evidence/cheney-saudi-israel-record-trace-2026-11-05/README.md#saudi-support-and-israeli-intelligence-hypotheses).

## Exxon recording: source and result

The [September 28, 2001 laboratory request, section 5, PDF pages 64–66](../fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-5.pdf#page=64), sent an original surveillance tape and twelve color photographs for examination. The stated 8:29–8:31 a.m. September 11 interval is a displayed recording time requiring clock verification, not an independently calibrated time or a known van identification.

The [October 18 examination report, section 5, PDF pages 69–71](../fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-5.pdf#page=69), identifies videotape **K1966** and twelve submitted prints, K1967–K1978. Examiners digitized frames showing white vans and applied image adjustments, reporting minimal improvement and recording outputs on prints and CD-R. The examination sheet shows laboratory number **011001250 JD**; the transmittal shows **011001250 HC JD**. Twelve submitted prints and thirteen generated images are different input and output quantities, not proof of a missing frame. The visible report does not identify either van as belonging to UMS or establish the camera's clock accuracy. See the [full source and custody review](../record-chains-2026-10-26/README.md#4-the-exxon-surveillance-examination).

Newark's 2003 closing account concluded it had no factual or substantive circumstantial evidence of advance knowledge by the five detainees and cited other timing checks. This is an investigative conclusion, not a successful identification of either van in the Exxon recording. [Closing account, section 6](../fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-6.pdf#page=52).
