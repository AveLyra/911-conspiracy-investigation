# Urban Moving Systems: six evidence questions and their limits

Research and publication review dated October 26, 2026. This is an additive
public research record of the six points explained in the accompanying chat.
It preserves conflicting observations and identifies tests that could strengthen
or weaken the hypotheses. It is separate from litigation records and does not
establish attack foreknowledge, attack participation, a WTC 7 operational link,
or manufacture of an al-Qaeda attribution.

## How to read this review

**Recorded statement** means a source says something; it does not mean the
underlying event has been independently reproduced or authenticated. **Inference**
is our interpretation of the stated evidence. **Unknown** identifies an open
join or missing input. No estimate here is a probability of a conspiracy.

FBI citations use physical PDF pages, starting at one, rather than internal page
numbers. These are publicly mirrored copies of apparent FBI records. Acquired
bytes were checked against the mirror's metadata; the complete chain from FBI
release to the mirror has not been independently authenticated. The official
Bush Library inventory has a different, agency-hosted provenance. Source URLs,
access dates, file hashes and page counts are in [SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json).

The [preserved source review](preserved/REPORT.md) and
[eleven-chain ledger](preserved/chains.json) are byte-for-byte copies of the
preceding local research. Their references to native acquisition/rendering
files describe that original study; those complete local holdings are not all
included in this publication. In particular, the original report's final
no-publication statement records its status before this separately authorized
publication. It has not been rewritten to change that history.

## 1. The reported government connection

**Recorded statement.** The July 10, 2003 account says physical evidence and
statements supported investigators' belief that a redacted individual
"was working for the Israeli Government through his employment at the
[redacted]." The account also reports no established U.S. clandestine tasking or
prior knowledge. Both the affirmative assessment and the negative conclusions
remain subject to checking their underlying evidence.

A parallel communication names the **Jewish Agency** in one paragraph where
another copy has redacted wording. The copies redact other role/employment
wording differently. That does not identify the hidden employer in a different
paragraph, establish that separate paragraphs concern the same person, or
permit reconstruction of redactions.

**Inference and limits.** This is a specific government-association lead with a
reported evidentiary basis. Government employment, intelligence employment,
operational tasking, foreknowledge and attack participation are distinct
propositions. The inspected passages do not establish Mossad membership or the
last three propositions. Nationality or religion supplies none of those joins.

**What would test it.** Obtain the actual physical exhibits, originating
interviews and person-to-evidence cross-references behind the assessment, plus
employment or tasking documentation. An unrelated government role would weaken
an attack-related interpretation; authenticated operational instructions could
strengthen it. Neither outcome is assumed.

## 2. Witness and photographic chronology

**Recorded statement.** The principal interview was conducted September 11,
2001 and transcribed September 12. It reports a neighbor's call at approximately
**09:00**, followed by the witness's observation of smoke and **three men** on a
white van, apparent still/video recording, smiling, hugging and high-fives.
She said the second explosion had not occurred when she first noticed them.
She recorded **JRJ-13Y** and contacted police later, after her husband returned.
The neighbor's call and later police call are different events.

This is a contemporary interview report, not an acquired original recording or
independently authenticated signed statement. Demeanor is the witness's
observation and interpretation, not an independent measurement of motive. Three
observed men must be connected carefully to the five later detained. A detective
relaying the complaint and plate is not a second eyewitness to the behavior.

Separately, a September 15 summary records another witness's white-van sighting
**as early as 08:00**. The underlying interview and verified vehicle/occupant
identification remain unlocated in this pass. That allegation is preserved
beside the later observations, not discarded or merged into continuous watching.

Other accounts remain separate: a maintenance observer gives both **less than
five minutes after the first impact** and **09:00-09:10**, did not recognize the
pictured men, and supplied a diagram reportedly enclosed in a **1-A envelope**.
An employee report refers to an **08:58 time card**, but the actual card and
clock were not inspected. The later closing account acknowledges that its
follow-up did not verify the earlier statement as intended. Office roof,
parking-garage roof and van roof are different observation locations.

**Inference and limits.** The principal witness's reported observation began
after the attack was underway. The closing account reports no recovered 35 mm
photograph showing the towers before the attack, but the complete negative
sequence has not been independently examined here. After-impact photographs do
not date first arrival or independently decide prior knowledge. The 08:00
vehicle/occupant join remains open.

**What would test it.** The original 08:00 interview, plate note, complaint and
dispatch records, neighbor's account, 1-A diagram, actual time card and camera
negatives could establish or undermine a linked chronology. Approximate clock
recollections are not silently converted to precise times.

## 3. Helicopter/FAA timing and documentary differences

**Recorded statement.** The earlier comparison identifies the pictured
helicopter as **most likely N8BQ**, places it in the relevant vicinity around
**08:50-09:00**, and offers an opinion that the photograph was taken around
**09:00**. It states that radar information was **not viewable from 09:03-09:07**.
The July 2003 closing account gives **08:50-09:03** and expressly calls the
photo/video comparison **non-scientific**. Neither supplies a precise photo time.

Both bounds remain recorded. They overlap, and their three-minute endpoint
difference has not been reproduced from raw tracks or reconciled. The source
does not establish why the interval was not viewable.

The earlier memo's first-page header reads **09/23/2001**, while its continuation
reads **09/19/2001**. Its time wording includes **08:46:35am EST** and
**12:46:35am GMT**. These are retained exactly as recorded, without clock or
date normalization. Their cause and effect on the historical analysis are unknown.

The September 17 cassette receipt, lead **NK2405**, describes a compilation:

| Elapsed position on tape | Recorded source |
| --- | --- |
| 00:00-21:00 | N8BQ, Metro Traffic |
| 21:00-25:40 | N42F, Metro Traffic / WABC-TV |
| 25:40-end | N27M, WCBS-TV |

**Inference and limits.** Tape positions, recording-clock values and historical
times are different quantities. Radio and television affiliations cannot be
interchanged. A repeated receipt is not an independent recording. The visible
analysis supplies a qualified timing assessment, not a reproducible precise
timestamp or proof that radar was destroyed. It also does not establish arrival
time from the time of a photograph.

**What would test it.** Obtain the NK2405 compilation and source masters, identify
the segment actually used, and obtain the ATC CD-ROM, viewer, raw tracks,
original photograph and comparison worksheets. Establish whether the accounts
describe the same field of view and explain the not-viewable interval through
records rather than assumption.

## 4. The Exxon surveillance examination

**Recorded statement.** A September 28, 2001 request concerns surveillance from
an Exxon station near the business, seeking distinctive markings on **two white
vans**. It specifies an event-on-tape interval **09/11/2001, 08:29:00 to
approximately 08:31:00**. Acquisition-method wording is redacted; the visible
text does not expose a camera/channel, exact location, recorder or clock check.

The October 18 response references that request and October 1 receipt. It
identifies **K1966**, a TDK T-160 VHS marked **AGBH009**, and **K1967-K1978**, twelve
submitted prints. Examiners digitized selected frames and adjusted brightness,
contrast and sharpness; enhancement gains were described as **minimal**.

The transmittal prints laboratory number **011001250 HC JD**, and the examination
sheet prints **011001250 JD**. Both forms remain. Outputs include sets of
**13 generated images** and CD-Rs distributed to multiple FBI recipients. Twelve
submitted photographs and thirteen generated images are distinct input/output
counts, not an established missing or substituted frame.

**Inference and limits.** There is an actual request-to-examination trail with
concrete media targets. The visible report does not claim a successful vehicle
identification or certify the recorder's clock. Its 08:29 label therefore cannot
yet put a particular Urban Moving vehicle at a calibrated pre-impact time.
Routing through an Explosives Unit examiner is not chemical confirmation.

**What would test it.** Retrieve K1966 and its FD-340a acquisition envelope,
source camera/recorder and clock checks, the twelve input prints, thirteen-image
output set, CD-R files, examiner notes and any later vehicle-match decision.

Sources: [P5 pp64-66](https://archive.org/download/fbinewarkdivisionfullfieldinvestigation/FBI%20Newark%20Division%20Full-Field%20Investigation%20Part%205.pdf#page=64),
[P5 pp69-71](https://archive.org/download/fbinewarkdivisionfullfieldinvestigation/FBI%20Newark%20Division%20Full-Field%20Investigation%20Part%205.pdf#page=69).

## 5. Individual attribution and photographic quantities

**Recorded statement.** A September 15 summary expressly notes that the then
recovery log did not make clear which collected items belonged to which detainee.
This is an early attribution problem, not proof of a permanent custody failure.

A September 14 memo records development/reproduction of seized original 35 mm
film on September 13. A separate inventory records **1B14**, three copies each
of **76 black-and-white 8x10 photographs and film negatives**, barcode
**E01889076**. That inventory does not by itself establish 76 sequential exposures
from a detainee's camera. The photographic job and negative-to-print join remain
missing from the material inspected here.

**Inference and limits.** A person-specific allegation based on a recovered
camera, document, contact or image requires a reliable ownership/possession join.
The early note gives a concrete reason to check that join. Later records may
have clarified attribution; we have not established whether they did. No
planting, substitution or deliberate misattribution is inferred from the gap.

**What would test it.** Obtain later property cross-references, seizure locations,
camera identifiers, negative envelopes, contact sheets and processing records.
Map each relevant image to roll/frame, source camera and documented possessor.

## 6. Computer, chemical and White House records still needed

**Computer records.** A January 10, 2002 receipt describes hard-drive data or
mirror images from CART associated with seized Urban Moving equipment. It has
**16 references: 15 CPU labels and one Iomega Peerless entry**. This extends the
trail beyond the October 2001 pending-status memo. It is not sixteen established
completed forensic examinations and does not disclose substantive findings,
image hashes or an authenticated communication bearing on the attacks.

**Chemical records.** The inventory identifies item **15**, a fabric sample for
explosive residue (**E01889035**); items **16/17**, control swabs from gloves/bomb
suits (**E01889036**); and item **34**, blanket samples (**E01889037**). A specimen's
test-purpose label or a canine alert is not a positive chemical result. Controls
can test handling/background contamination; their existence does not establish
that contamination occurred. No matching final chemical finding was located and
verified in this bounded pass. Imperfect OCR, redactions and absent enclosures
limit that search; absence here is not proof of absence from the full case.

The July 10, 2003 communication instructs discontinuance of outstanding CD-2C /
Laboratory Division leads in ACS. That administrative instruction does not
establish whether a particular examination was completed, cancelled, superseded
or reported elsewhere. Pending in October 2001 does not mean pending today.

**White House records.** Official request **2025-0218-F** lists **13 assets**:
one fully released, two partially withheld and ten fully withheld. ARMS 1 has
the three released-form assets; Exchange 1 has two fully withheld and Exchange 2
has eight fully withheld. The aid describes a security notice and press-guidance
emails, but its OR-term request also covers other people, companies and Iraq/WMD
subjects. We cannot describe these as thirteen Urban Moving emails. No message
bodies or asset-specific search/withdrawal lists were inspected.

**Inference and limits.** These are identifiable retrieval targets, not positive
forensic findings or evidence of improper concealment. An official inventory
authenticates the existence and stated scope of a collection; it does not reveal
what each email says or why an exemption was applied.

**What would test it.** Obtain CART image/acquisition logs, hashes, source-item
crosswalks, substantive results and disposition records; chemical accession,
specimen/control submissions and final findings; and the three ARMS assets in
their released forms, plus processed search-result lists, dates, subjects and
withdrawal descriptions for all thirteen. No fee, request or outreach is
represented as performed by this review.

## Overall inference and strongest objection

Post-attack photography, a government association and imperfect investigative
documentation could coexist without attack foreknowledge or participation.
That is the strongest ordinary alternative to an attack-related interpretation,
not an established explanation of every record. Conversely, an association or
gap cannot substitute for the missing link to an operation. Circumstantial
evidence may matter; each link still requires a source, uncertainty and a test
capable of weakening it.

These sources establish concrete records questions and unresolved differences.
They do not establish a link to placing explosives in WTC 7 or manufacturing
al-Qaeda attribution. We have not inferred that final laboratory results were
positive, negative, never completed, or improperly concealed.

from the preceding seven-group native-source check. Hashes and coverage checks
do not independently authenticate FBI custody or reproduce historical forensic
examinations. No new PDF or layout rendering was performed for publication.

OCR, private correspondence, case records, credentials and personal contact
