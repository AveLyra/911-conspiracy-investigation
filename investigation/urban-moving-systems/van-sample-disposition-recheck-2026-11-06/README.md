# Van samples: later FBI disposition and unresolved test results

**Working research, November 6, 2026.** A later requester-held FBI production reports disposal of items corresponding to the three van-sample barcodes. It does not establish a final positive or negative chemical finding, the purpose or propriety of disposal, or a WTC explosives connection. This qualifies the earlier public search, which did not include that separate production.

## Records and limits

The January 27, 2014 FBI communication, pages 1–2 of the later production's first PDF, says the writer destroyed or disposed of listed evidence as requested. It lists **E01889035, E01889036 and E01889037**. The communication is dated January 27, but does not supply a separate item-specific destruction date. [Selected production pages](../fbi-notebook-custody-2026-11-06/raw/fbi-selected-disposition-and-inventory-pages.pdf#page=1) are now provided as a labeled derivative; the complete production, complete custody and original authorization are not established by the excerpt. The [earlier public UMS review](../ums-rooftop-affiliation-followup-2026-11-05/README.md) also describes the same production and the repeated February communication.

An FBI inventory maps those barcodes to a fabric sample identified for explosive-residue examination, control swabs from gloves and bomb suits, and blanket samples identified for explosive-residue examination, respectively. Exact barcode matches support the identification of the reported disposed items, but redacted case fields and missing accession and custody records limit the join. Labels are not test results; the control descriptions do not establish that detainees possessed bomb suits. [Public inventory scan, section 1, PDF p. 7](../fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-1.pdf#page=7).

The September 2001 FBI memorandum reports a canine alert and vehicle-interior swabs with final results pending at the time. It does not map each swab to the later disposition barcodes. A July 2003 account says routine moving equipment including blankets was not found; the relationship between that statement and the separately inventoried blanket samples is unresolved. The provenance and scope of each description must be established before inferring an error or contamination. An April 2004 retention communication describes evidence as having no value to the PENTTBOMB investigation while noting counterintelligence relevance; it is not a chemical result. [September 2001 account](../fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-5.pdf#page=44); [2003 narrative](../fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-6.pdf#page=43); [2004 communication](../fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-6.pdf#page=61).

## Discriminating records

Seek laboratory accession and submission crosswalks, final reports and controls, raw data or surviving extracts, complete inventory and transfer history, actual disposal authorization and item-level custody. An authorized routine disposition after examination and an effort to conceal unfavorable results are possibilities to test; neither follows from the disposition record alone. A positive result would still need source, contamination, attribution and relevance analysis. No final chemistry report or independently authenticated physical chain was obtained in this pass.
