# UMS: address-list, early-van and timeline source recheck

**Working research, November 6, 2026.** Selected released FBI page images were rechecked. They do not establish a UMS–hijacker connection, WTC explosives placement or advance knowledge. Original list attachments, underlying timing records and a final comparison were not obtained in this pass.

## Address and U.S. entry-date lists

A September 16, 2001 FD-302 says agents compiled addresses and U.S. entry dates for hijackers and suspected associates and a comparable list for the five UMS detainees, and that the two lists were attached. Visual review of the released page confirms the earlier OCR-based reading. The form does not report a positive or negative match; the attachments and any actual comparison remain unlocated. A shared location or date alone would not establish coordination. Seek the attachments, source inputs, comparison, limitations and disposition. [FBI section 5, PDF p. 28](../fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-5.pdf#page=28).

## Early white-van descriptions

A September 15 FBI interview reports that a witness saw a white van at the rear lot of the apartment-building observation site at approximately 8:15 a.m. on September 11. It describes utility-related lettering, one visible driver, no visible passengers and no recorded plate; the witness did not see the van on returning around 9:20. The report cites lead **NK1148**. It does not identify the van as the detainees' vehicle. [FBI section 5, PDF p. 29](../fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-5.pdf#page=29).

The Newark closing account separately reports another white-van complaint at the same lot as early as 8:00 a.m. It does not establish that this was the same witness or vehicle. A third account describes an Urban-marked vehicle at Liberty State Park without a stated time, a different location. Do not combine these sightings without a vehicle, witness and time crosswalk. [FBI section 6, PDF p. 42](../fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-6.pdf#page=42).

## Later timing assessment

The closing account reports a 7:48 a.m. E-ZPass record, a later gas receipt, still photographs, helicopter footage and FAA radar checks. It calls one photo/video comparison non-scientific and interprets it as supporting arrival at the observation point after the first impact. It says the toll record did not determine the van's exact location during the attack and the helicopter comparison did not produce a precise time. The underlying recordings, original toll/receipt records and timing calibration were not independently acquired for this review. This evidence is relevant to a narrower photo timeline, not proof of every person's location throughout the morning or resolution of the separate Exxon footage. [FBI section 6, PDF pp. 47–49](../fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-6.pdf#page=47).

Retrieve the original witnesses' interviews, identification and follow-up records; compare plate, vehicle details, occupants and clock basis; and obtain the underlying stills, news footage, radar, toll and receipt records where they survive. A verified different utility van would weaken the identification of that specific 8:15 sighting with UMS but would not by itself resolve the separate 8:00 account. A unique, corroborated match could strengthen the early-presence lead; presence alone would not prove knowledge of the attacks or installation of explosives.
