It does not independently establish agency custody or complete public availability. The original September 10 move order, dispatch or driver roster, Doric-specific certificate, original array images and photo-number/name crosswalk were **not located** in this search. No witness, tenant or agency was contacted.

In those inspected messages, the September 10 Doric move, the reported faxed certificate, and the described 1A photo arrays are not identified by those locators; possible generic coverage and FBI interpretation remain unknown. ## Impact and discriminating next steps

The reports support a specific company–site commercial association and a separate eyewitness identification lead. They **do not establish** that any of the five men participated in the move or that the move was connected to the hijackers, the WTC or intelligence activity. The move witness's nonrecognition is contrary evidence for the claim the pictured people were the observed crew, subject to viewing and photo-identification limits; it is not a complete worker alibi. An attributed personnel roster and job dispatch matched to a contemporaneous bill or certificate could support a particular person's participation; verified incompatible assignments, a confirmed different crew or an identified array/worker mismatch would weaken it. The array alone cannot establish work assignment.

At the existing response/status review, compare the FBI's actual search and production with the Sept. 10 job, certificate, array and NK1148 1A locator. If not addressed, a narrowly scoped search-lead supplement could ask for the original order, booking, invoice and dispatch; vehicle and crew identifiers; the faxed certificate and FBI receipt; photo arrays and name/number crosswalk; witness-administration and comparison results; and 1A indices, inventories and disposition, including negative results. The task-specific locator can be evaluated without publishing redacted tenant personal information. No supplement was drafted or sent, and no new fee was authorized in this cycle.
