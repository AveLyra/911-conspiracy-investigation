# Developing hypothesis: Urban Moving Systems and September 11

**Working research, November 6, 2026.** This outline identifies a proposed theory, the evidence that motivates particular leads, contrary evidence and tests. It is not a finding of fact or a conclusion that any named person or organization committed a crime. The records reviewed do not establish that Urban Moving Systems (UMS) was a Mossad shell, that its employees planted explosives, or that specified U.S., Saudi-linked and Israeli actors coordinated an attack or a plan to create a pretext for war. Direct UMS–hijacker coordination is not required by this theory. A bounded search cannot establish that undiscovered records do not exist.

## Proposed sequence

1. **Assistance to hijackers.** Saudi-linked individuals supplied practical help to some hijackers; the hypothesis asks whether any assistance was knowingly connected to a larger operation.
2. **Possible intelligence activity and compartmentation.** The hypothesis proposes UMS as a Mossad shell. It also proposes coordination among specific U.S. and Israeli intelligence-linked actors, with possible Saudi-linked involvement; Saudi knowledge of a wider plan is open. The working assumption is that the hijackers probably did not know of the alleged Mossad role; this is not an established fact or a calculated probability. The hypothesis does not require them to communicate or coordinate directly with UMS. The proposed affiliation, tasking and higher-level coordination remain unproved; affiliation alone would not show advance knowledge or participation.
3. **WTC access and explosives.** The hypothesis proposes that UMS or associated personnel gained access and planted explosives or incendiaries in one or more WTC buildings. The available record does not establish an explosives installation or justify ranking the detainees as likely perpetrators. The WTC visitor card, moving-company records and testing trail are leads to investigate.
4. **Coordinated attacks and political purpose.** The theory proposes that the hijackings and alleged demolitions were coordinated to create an engineered spectacle used to support U.S. military action, including against Saddam Hussein. Testing that proposition requires evidence of a shared plan and prior knowledge among the specific alleged decision-makers; the hijackers and installation personnel need not share knowledge of the whole plan. Later consideration of Iraq or the subsequent war is not proof of the proposed plan.
5. **Relationships and later consequences.** The theory asks whether personal, political, Saudi and commercial relationships shaped decisions, and whether beneficiaries knew of an operation beforehand. Later access, lobbying, relationships or contracts can occur without advance participation.

These propositions require separate evidence. A connection at one step cannot supply the missing steps. They do not assume a shared purpose among all Saudi nationals, members of a family, UMS customers or Israeli nationals. A direct UMS–hijacker link, if confirmed, would be a lead; its absence would not alone disprove a compartmented plan. The hypothesis is agnostic about whether Saudi authorities knew of any proposed wider plan. See the [November 10 integrated source review](../../cross-chat-evidence-integration-2026-11-10/README.md).

### WTC 7: model dispute and acoustic limits

NIST presents a developed fire-triggered sequence; the project has identified material uncertainties concerning fire-history inputs, damage transfer, residual support and the independently unverified transition to global collapse. Some simulated burning or spread differed from visible indications; the consequences for the actual structural sequence remain unquantified. NIST performed some sensitivity checks, and a reported stable case supplies a relevant control; these should not be described as nonexistent. [Causal-chain assessment](../../wtc7/causal-chain-synthesis/report.md); [NIST WTC 7 FAQ](https://www.nist.gov/world-trade-center-investigation/study-faqs/wtc-7-investigation).

The University of Alaska Fairbanks study concludes that fire did not cause WTC 7's collapse and models near-simultaneous column failures. Its findings are model-dependent; reproducing motion with imposed failure conditions does not by itself identify what produced those conditions. The study concerns WTC 7, not the Twin Towers, and does not identify UMS. NIST reports approximately 2.25 seconds of near-gravitational acceleration at a tracked north-roofline point; that is a constraint for any explanation, not by itself an explosive signature or proof about every support. [UAF report](https://www.wtc7report.org/); [NIST FAQ](https://www.nist.gov/world-trade-center-investigation/study-faqs/wtc-7-investigation).

NIST's reported blast nondetection can carry conditional negative weight against the particular conspicuous blast scenarios studied. The project's acoustic audit identifies unresolved recording calibration, obstruction, propagation and receiver-response questions. These limitations neither show that every predicted blast was inaudible nor establish an actual explosive mechanism, and the modeled scenarios do not automatically exclude quieter interventions. [Acoustic source audit](../../wtc7/nist-acoustic-detectability/report.md).

An [October 25 subjective scorecard](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/a5f55e4e9269c5f8895089aa9d34092dc899301e/investigation/wtc7/assessment-release-2026-10-25/README.md) placed deliberate support removal first (+1.5), fire generally second (+1), and NIST's particular sequence third (-1). These are analyst-chosen points, not measured probabilities. NIST's sequence is nested within the broader fire family; removing discretionary acoustic credit ties the first two, and another stated sensitivity reverses them. An October 26 follow-up treated this as a subjective scenario rather than a demonstrated comparative probability advantage. No model or acoustic calculation was rerun for this outline.

Its [endnotes](https://www.9-11commission.gov/report/911Report_Notes.htm) cite the September 17 NSC conclusions memorandum in note 71, but Rice’s April 2004 testimony and Woodward in note 72. This verifies the Commission’s citation chain; it does not establish the precise text of the uninspected NSC memorandum or a pre-attack decision.

### Pentagon FOIA search: count and video scope

In [Bingham v. DOJ, PDF p. 6](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-dcd-1_05-cv-00475/pdf/USCOURTS-dcd-1_05-cv-00475-0.pdf#page=6), the court summarizes the FBI declaration: 56 of 85 potentially responsive tapes were excluded using custody/supporting documentation; the agent viewed 29, of which 16 did not show the crash site, 12 showed it after impact and one showed impact. This is the court’s account of the agency search, not evidence that 85 tapes showed the impact or that each tape’s original custody was independently reconstructed. Separately, the [official P200022 exhibit catalog](https://coop.vaed.uscourts.gov/moussaoui/exhibits/prosecution/P200022.html) describes two impact clips, six aftermath clips and 12 still images; the exhibit and the search count use different units. The source-to-recorder chain remains a separate question.

### United Defense credit dates and capacity

The [August 13, 2001 credit-agreement reproduction](https://contracts.justia.com/companies/united-defense-industries-inc-64892/contract/1059558/) is dated as of August 13, while United Defense’s [2004 annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1051719/000095013305000956/w06569e10vk.htm) describes a refinancing on August 31 and a facility consisting of $600 million in term loans and a $200 million revolving facility. The date difference may reflect different transaction stages but is not resolved by these texts alone. Facility capacity is not a demonstrated cash distribution, profit, or proof of advance knowledge; closing, drawdown and distribution records are needed.

**Further source qualification:** The [April 8, 2004 Rice testimony transcript](https://9-11commission.gov/archive/hearing9/9-11Commission_Hearing_2004-04-08.htm) does not contain the phrase “oil fields.” The Commission cites that testimony alongside Woodward for the planning passage; the cited book page and the original NSC memorandum have not been independently inspected here. The precise origin of the oil-field detail therefore remains unresolved.

### Iraq reconstruction contracting: audit of costs and oversight

[GAO's July 2007 RIO I audit](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-07-839.pdf) documents delayed negotiations and weaknesses in award-fee administration. DOD paid nearly all of about $221 million in questioned costs, while excluding about $112 million from the fee base, reducing fees by about $5.8 million; DCAA described about $26 million as sustained cost reductions. These categories cannot be added together as stolen or recovered money. Changing requirements, funding challenges and inadequate proposals were among the factors GAO identified in the delays. The findings support scrutiny of postattack contracting and oversight; they do not themselves establish advance knowledge or participation in the attacks.
