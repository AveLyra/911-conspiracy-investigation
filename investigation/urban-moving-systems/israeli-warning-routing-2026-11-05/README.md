# Reported Israeli warning, CIA/FBI routing, and Urban Moving Systems

Research note, 2026-11-05. Scope: identify the claimed original Israeli warning and the documents behind the October 2002 *Die Zeit* report; trace the contemporaneous CIA/FBI action on Khalid al-Mihdhar and Nawaf al-Hazmi; and test for a connection to Urban Moving Systems (UMS) and the Saudi-linked assistance network. This is a bounded public-source search, not an exhaustive search or a finding about classified files.

## Result

The alleged Israeli list or warning and the underlying documents relied upon by *Die Zeit* were not located. A **redacted, contemporaneous August 21, 2001 CIA email** was located, as were official later accounts of an August 23 CIA watchlist cable, its FBI routing, and the search for al-Mihdhar. The email is not the Israeli warning or the August 23 cable. The later accounts describe the cable but are not the cable itself.

Nothing located identifies UMS as a source of the alleged warning, connects UMS to the CIA/FBI routing decisions, or links UMS to Omar al-Bayoumi, Fahad al-Thumairy, or the Saudi-linked assistance network. An additional foreign warning or undisclosed relationship is not excluded by this search. Shared names in separate records, coincident dates, and a need for further investigation are not evidence of an operational link.

## What would discriminate the hypotheses

1. The alleged original Israeli-to-U.S. warning and any list, with dates, originating office, names, scope, and contemporaneous receipt and dissemination acknowledgments; preserve negative and nonresponsive results.
2. The documents *Die Zeit* said it possessed, their provenance, and any original underlying reporting distinguishing a surveillance list, a warning, and the CIA's watchlist request.
3. The August 23 CIA watchlisting cable/intelligence report, metadata, attachments, dispatch and receipt records; the August 23–24 FBI headquarters/New York telephone notes, emails and electronic communication, plus search, follow-up and disposition records. Related contemporaneous July–August analyst correspondence matters to causation, as do any foreign-liaison references or explicit statements that none were used.
4. Any authenticated pre-September 11 tasking, communications, payment, contact, or source reporting tying named UMS personnel to a specific individual in the Saudi-linked network or to a specific warning. Obtain attribution and dates; a common name, address book entry or shared geographic area alone would not establish a call, intelligence tasking, or shared purpose.

The current record is consistent with an analyst-driven review, with a separate foreign warning, or with some combination; it does not establish which additional information was available or intentionally withheld. Intelligence surveillance, ordinary moving work, and assistance to hijackers are separate questions. A successful falsification test would retain contemporaneous records and agency findings that disconfirm as well as support a proposed relationship.
