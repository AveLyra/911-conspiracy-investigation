# Urban Moving Systems: counterevidence and records to resolve it

**Working research, November 6, 2026.** This note evaluates evidence relevant to the hypothesis that Urban Moving Systems (UMS) personnel were part of a coordinated intelligence and explosives operation connected with the September 11 attacks. The hypothesis remains unsubstantiated: the materials reviewed do not establish that UMS planted explosives, coordinated with the hijackers or Saudi intermediaries, or acted on instructions to create a pretext for war. The absence of a record in a bounded search is not proof it never existed.

## Affiliation, interviews and translated records

**Early FBI assessment.** A September 24, 2001 FBI headquarters communication states that the Newark and New York field offices had determined that the five detainees were not actively engaged in clandestine intelligence activity in the United States. It is headquarters reporting field findings, not an independent second investigation. It was made early; a separate later headquarters inquiry was still incomplete in the July 2003 account, and its final result has not been identified. [FBI section 5, PDF pp. 58–60](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/1113ee2e24243d7d7b0a4167ccf7cb52f6913c59/investigation/urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-5.pdf#page=58); [FBI section 6, PDF pp. 56–57](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/1113ee2e24243d7d7b0a4167ccf7cb52f6913c59/investigation/urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-6.pdf#page=56).

Some later administrative entries record an identified front/cover organization and positive intelligence, but the visible subject and supporting references are redacted. They do not identify UMS as the organization, or establish Mossad affiliation. Seek the underlying field reports, supporting serials, subject crosswalk, later HQ report if created, final conclusions and both positive and negative findings. [FBI section 5, PDF pp. 103–104 and 109–110](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/1113ee2e24243d7d7b0a4167ccf7cb52f6913c59/investigation/urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-5.pdf#page=103).

**Translation finding.** An October 4, 2002 FBI communication reports a contract linguist's opinion that translated notebooks, journals and address books were personal and contained nothing of foreign-intelligence significance. That assessment concerns the material translated and reviewed; it does not resolve every seized item or the later headquarters inquiry. Seek the original pages, translations, evidence identifiers and subsequent analyses. [FBI section 6, PDF pp. 5–6](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/1113ee2e24243d7d7b0a4167ccf7cb52f6913c59/investigation/urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-6.pdf#page=5).

**Photograph comparison.** A September 2002 response describes two witnesses who did not recall seeing five photographed individuals. The subjects' identities are redacted; this is not yet a confirmed negative comparison involving UMS employees or hijackers. Seek the original lead, photo array, subject identities, complete interviews and follow-up. [FBI section 5, PDF pp. 128–129](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/1113ee2e24243d7d7b0a4167ccf7cb52f6913c59/investigation/urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-5.pdf#page=128).

## Physical evidence and WTC access

**Structural evidence.** NIST reports that the towers' collapses began at the aircraft-impact and fire floors and rejected controlled demolition; its WTC 7 study concluded that fire caused that building's collapse. These are substantive alternatives to a planted-explosives hypothesis. NIST also says it did **not** test tower steel for explosive or thermite residues: its conclusion is not a negative chemical test on that steel. A University of Alaska Fairbanks study disputes NIST's WTC 7 account; it concerns WTC 7 and does not establish an explosive mechanism or identify an installer. The competing models' assumptions and predictions require direct comparison, and no model rerun is claimed here. [NIST towers FAQ](https://www.nist.gov/world-trade-center-investigation/study-faqs/wtc-towers-investigation); [NIST WTC 7 FAQ](https://www.nist.gov/world-trade-center-investigation/study-faqs/wtc-7-investigation); [UAF project](https://www.wtc7report.org/).

**Corrosion.** FEMA Appendix C documents unusual steel corrosion but leaves the source and timing unresolved. A 2006 paper's abstract suggests that a WTC 7 beam experienced metal removal in the rubble after collapse. That interpretation is contrary evidence for an argument that the corrosion itself proves a pre-collapse incendiary. The authors overlap the FEMA team; the abstract alone is not independent replication or conclusive proof of timing. Obtain the full methods, specimen identification, custody and alternative explanations. [FEMA Appendix C](https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/2017/05/09/AppendixC-fema403_apc.pdf); [Sisson and Biederman abstract](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1361/154770206X129006).

**Zim relocation.** An April 3, 2001 trade report announced a planned September relocation of Zim's regional headquarters. It weighs against describing the move itself as a sudden secret evacuation, but identifies no moving company. Seek contemporaneous Zim and vendor contracts, payments, dispatches and WTC tenant, loading-dock and access records; a confirmed job would show an opportunity, not explosives installation. [Contemporary American Shipper report](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/zim-moves-regional-head-office-from-new-york-to-norfolk).

**Unresolved physical leads.** A property receipt lists a WTC visitor card in a fanny pack, but the cardholder, building numeral, issuance and use remain uncertain. Obtain the card and original receipt crosswalk, issuer and host records, and any surviving entry or job records. The FBI also described collecting van samples for examination; the available inventory is not a final chemical result. Seek laboratory accessions, analyses, controls, remaining reports and disposition records. [FBI property receipt, section 2, PDF p. 81](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/1113ee2e24243d7d7b0a4167ccf7cb52f6913c59/investigation/urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-2.pdf#page=81); [public sample-record review](../fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/README.md).

## Iraq and causation

The 9/11 Commission describes senior officials considering Iraq after the attacks while also reporting that President Bush initially prioritized Afghanistan. That complicates an assertion that the immediate agreed response was an Iraq invasion; it does not determine private earlier motives or exclude later political exploitation. Seek dated pre-attack instructions, communications or financial records connecting actual attack participants to a shared Iraq objective. Later benefit, a preexisting policy preference or an individual relationship does not establish prior participation in the attacks. [Commission report, chapter 10](https://9-11commission.gov/report/911Report_Ch10.htm).

## Evidence that would change the assessment

The highest-value tests are authenticated records linking a specific person to WTC access, materials and installation; independent analysis of identified physical specimens with controls; and contemporaneous communications linking the participants and their shared purpose. Well-supported negative comparisons and documented alternative explanations also matter. A result about intelligence affiliation, WTC access, a collapse mechanism or later policy benefit should not be treated as proof of the others.
