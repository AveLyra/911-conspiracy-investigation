## Faraday Research Machine

[Faraday](../../faraday/README.md) is a self-contained research-software project, not an evidence report or proof of any historical claim. Its [operating contract](../../faraday/AGENTS.md), [license](../../faraday/LICENSE), executable, source code, schemas, documentation and tests belong together. Moving it without auditing internal relative paths, scripts, links and reproducibility could break the tool. Its presence in this investigation repository does not transfer its governance or evidence standards to historical findings.

## Relocation inventory and protection

The [proposed-path inventory](RELOCATION-INVENTORY.txt) lists the two top-level historical documents and all 256 Faraday paths in the reviewed Git tree. It is a planning list, not a record that anything was moved or deleted. A future relocation needs explicit approval of the exact source-path removal list, preservation and recovery verification, link rewriting for the historical documents, executable/import/configuration tests for Faraday, and any required publication checks. User files, old links, LFS objects and research history have not been deleted or moved by this navigation update.
