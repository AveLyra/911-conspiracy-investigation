# September 11 research: compartmented hypothesis and evidence checks

**Working research, November 2026.** This paper connects separate public-record investigations and states what could test a proposed coordinated-operation hypothesis. It is not a finding that any named person, government, agency or business took part in an attack. Repeated summaries can derive from the same underlying document and are not independent corroboration.

The proposed hypothesis is that particular individuals linked to U.S. intelligence or the Bush administration and Israeli intelligence may have coordinated before September 11; a possible role for Saudi-linked people is also under investigation. In this hypothesis, Urban Moving Systems (UMS) was a cover for Mossad and its personnel may have obtained access to the World Trade Center and installed destructive material, with the attacks subsequently used to support an Iraq-related military agenda. These propositions remain unestablished.

The scenario permits **compartmented branches**: its working assumption is that the hijackers probably did not know of an alleged Israeli intelligence role; that is a hypothesis, not an established fact or a calculated probability. They need not have known of a wider plan, and UMS personnel need not have met, contacted, or shared a purpose with the hijackers. Whether Saudi officials knew of assistance, a coming attack, an intelligence role, or a wider plan are separate open questions; none should be imputed to an entire government or its people. A UMS–hijacker contact would be an optional lead, not a necessary element, and a well-supported negative contact search would not by itself disprove a compartmented hypothesis. Claims of intelligence affiliation, knowledge, physical installation, cooperation and motive each require independent evidence.

## CIA hold and Saudi-linked assistance

A January 2000 CIA hold on a particular draft intended for the FBI is documented. Its precise purpose remains unresolved. Some Malaysia-related information did reach FBI personnel; the surviving record does not establish total information blackout, CIA-directed Saudi recruitment, or deliberate permission for an impending attack. The original decision, distribution, recipient, liaison and source records remain material. [DOJ Office of Inspector General](https://oig.justice.gov/sites/default/files/archive/special/s0606/chapter5.htm); [CIA source review](../broader-evidence/cia-lead-verification-2026-10-26/README.md).

Saudi-linked support for some hijackers has evidentiary support. A 2025 federal court made **preliminary jurisdictional inferences** concerning knowing or deliberately indifferent assistance; it was not a final liability verdict or a finding of Saudi leadership knowledge of the attacks, Israeli involvement or a WTC explosives operation. An FBI closure assessment took a different position on criminal prosecution. The records and standards are not identical and should not be conflated. [Court ruling](https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/2025-08/03-md-01570%2011182.pdf); [FBI released material](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11-attacks-investigation-and-related-materials/9-11-material-released-in-response-to-executive-order-14040/documents-responsive-to-executive-order-14040-section-2-b-ii-part-01-of-01).

A declassified 2003 Commission follow-up asks about conflicting FBI dates for the Los Angeles meeting, the basis for a Bayoumi–Thumairy connection, a source's credibility and reported transfers. The questions are valuable locators, not answers establishing that the questioned events or a broader operation occurred. Obtain the cited FBI papers, underlying evidence and agency replies. [NARA packet, PDF pp. 1 and 4–6](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-048-doc18.pdf).

## UMS, WTC access and the unresolved FBI record

**Visitor card.** An FBI property receipt lists a WTC visitor card among property associated with the detained men. Its holder, issuer, building, date and actual use are unresolved. Records describing larger badge lists do not identify this card's holder or prove an entry. A verified card-to-person, dated entry and tenant/job chain would support a specific access claim; neither a card nor an entry alone would establish installation or foreknowledge. [Badge and records review](../urban-moving-systems/wtc-badge-record-trail-2026-11-06/README.md).

**Classic lead.** FBI summaries report that Classic International Movers' number appeared in a seized notebook and that Miami believed Classic moved an unnamed hijacker. The original Miami basis, customer identity, transaction and any call remain unverified; repeated accounts may repeat one source. This is an investigative lead whose resolution could establish or weaken a narrow contact claim; it is not required for the compartmented hypothesis. [FBI review](../urban-moving-systems/newark-closure-followup-2026-11-05/README.md).

**Affiliation and knowledge.** Newark reported no basis for finding the detainees had advance attack knowledge, a meaningful counterweight within the visible inquiry's scope. It reported that the pictures developed from film in an identified camera did not show the towers before the attacks; that finding does not cover every possible image. The separate headquarters matter was unfinished in the visible historical record; a final headquarters result has not been located. The unfinished status does not establish a positive finding or the current status of that inquiry. [Newark and headquarters review](../urban-moving-systems/newark-closure-followup-2026-11-05/README.md).

**Van samples.** An FBI account reports an alert and sampling, with final laboratory results pending at the time. A later communication reports disposing of items corresponding to three inventory barcodes. The examined records do not establish a positive or negative final chemical result, the specific reason for disposal, or WTC placement. An alert is not a chemical identification; disposal does not itself prove concealment. [Disposition and inventory review](../urban-moving-systems/van-sample-disposition-recheck-2026-11-06/README.md).

## WTC 7: model checks and their limits

NIST's WTC 7 model offers a detailed fire-triggered sequence. The public record also identifies several material validation questions:

- **Exterior motion and timing.** NIST acknowledges that modeled late-stage upper-wall inward deformation is not visible in available footage and offers breakup uncertainty and omitted nonstructural stiffness as possible explanations. NIST's published table reports west-penthouse passage below the roofline at 9.3 seconds observed, compared with 6.9–7.3 seconds with debris damage and 10.6–10.9 seconds without; those are NIST's event comparisons, not a new independent video measurement. Different viewpoints and event definitions matter. [NIST FAQ, Q25](https://www.nist.gov/world-trade-center-investigation/study-faqs/wtc-7-investigation); [timing source check](../wtc7/c79-and-timing-crosschecks-2026-10-21/README.md).
- **Fire reconstruction.** NIST derived floor 9's fire history from a time-shifted floor 8 history and floors 11 and 13 from shifted floor 12 histories. It acknowledged discrepancies between portions of the simulated burning and observations, with a possible mild overestimate of north-side heating. The effect on the relevant structural members has not been independently quantified. NIST conducted limited sensitivity checks, so it is inaccurate to say none were performed. [Fire-input source check](../wtc7/contracting-access-custody-audit/wtc7-property-independent-review.md); [NCSTAR 1-9](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-9.pdf#page=446).
- **Damage-state handoff.** NIST describes the numerical application of accumulated fire damage to its global model. Its earlier-damage case used 3.5-hour transferred damage with the 4.0-hour temperature field and reportedly stabilized; the later-damage case progressed to global collapse. A real threshold is possible. Version-matched inputs and controlled transfer, contact and sensitivity tests are needed to evaluate this comparison. Instantaneous numerical initialization is not proof of instantaneous historical removal or deliberate manipulation. [Source check](../wtc7/c79-and-timing-crosschecks-2026-10-21/README.md).
- **Connections and fracture.** NIST describes using a common strain-failure criterion at room and elevated temperatures while varying other properties; its model also addressed mesh dependence. Its reduced connection models were compared with detailed modeling. Later experiments found some differences in predicted and observed failure modes, while also providing positive evidence on other aspects of response. These results do not directly recreate a heated composite WTC 7 assembly or prove a different historical outcome. [Connection audit](../wtc7/connection-calibration-audit/report.md); [Seif et al.](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=919460).
- **Thermal input and reproduction.** A public trace reports repeated unequal thermal coefficients for selected node identifiers in a released input. The effective historical solver state and the effect on the result remain unestablished. [Thermal trace](../wtc7/thermal-assignment-trace/report.md).

The public [WTC 7 master lead register](../wtc7/master-lead-register-2026-10-26-cia/MASTER-LIST.md) documents these and related criticisms, counterevidence and record targets. A difference in one model is not, by itself, proof of another mechanism or its perpetrators. The proposed fire mechanism involves thermal expansion and failures of framing and connections; it does not require the steel columns to melt. NIST modeled some protected and unprotected column examples and higher- and lower-temperature cases, although the condition of particular components in 2001 remains a distinct question. [NCSTAR 1-9](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-9.pdf).

NIST states it did not test tower steel for explosive or thermite residues; that prevents describing its investigation as a negative residue test. It does not supply a positive chemical result. FEMA examined a sample that appeared to come from WTC 7 and reported high-temperature corrosion; its exact original building location and the timing of alteration remain uncertain. Neither observation attributes an installed material to UMS. [NIST towers FAQ](https://www.nist.gov/world-trade-center-investigation/study-faqs/wtc-towers-investigation); [FEMA Appendix C](https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/2017/05/09/AppendixC-fema403_apc.pdf).

## Tests that discriminate among explanations

| Question | Evidence that could strengthen a specific link | Evidence that could weaken that link |
|---|---|---|
| Was UMS an intelligence cover? | Authenticated affiliation records, tasking, source identity and contemporaneous agency communications. | A complete, well-supported negative assessment and alternative business history covering the alleged personnel and period. |
| Did its personnel access a relevant WTC area? | Card-to-holder crosswalk, dated entry, tenant authorization, job and dispatch matched to a person and place. | Reliable identification of an unrelated holder or a complete negative search with adequate coverage. |
| Was destructive material placed, and by whom? | Attributed physical material, procurement, installation and independently corroborated instructions tied to specific access. | Valid contrary forensic results and reliable records inconsistent with the specific proposed installation. |
| Did particular officials coordinate a wider plan? | Independently authenticated preattack instructions, communications, payments or witness evidence establishing participants, knowledge and common purpose. | Well-supported alternative explanations and complete contemporaneous records inconsistent with a specified alleged interaction. |
| Do fire and structural models explain the observations? | Reproducible, version-matched runs with realistic inputs, defined connection behavior, complete force/energy histories and independent video comparisons. | Robust observation-consistent alternatives showing the proposed sequence fails or arrests under justified assumptions. |

An ordinary moving business, separate intelligence activity, Saudi-linked assistance, agency error, fire-driven failure and later policy exploitation are competing explanations that also require testing. A lack of direct UMS–hijacker contact does not itself decide a compartmented plan; conversely, model uncertainty, missing records or later benefit do not themselves establish one.

This page synthesizes source-linked research and limited checks. It does not claim that every investigative source, historical chat attachment or tool output was read, that a complete solver was run, or that later records could not change the assessment.

## Coverage map: sixteen audit tasks

The following rows map the sixteen scoped audit topics to public records and research already available. A mapping is not independent corroboration, a completed historical reconstruction or a claim that every original has been obtained. The task labels identify topics; the source-specific limitations govern each conclusion. The public [eight-track audit request](../broader-evidence/audit-backlog-2026-10-26/REQUEST.md) preserves the research questions, and [the documentary claim crosswalk](../broader-evidence/documentary-crosswalk-2026-10-26/CROSSWALK-v2.md) maps earlier film-related claims without treating overlap as authentication.

| Audit | Source-backed question and limitation |
|---|---|
| **AUD001–003: CIA hold, claimed notification and later oversight** | The hold, asserted FBI notification, actual receipt and later discovery are distinct events. The CIA draft and claimed delivery do not alone prove why information was held or that a recipient saw it. [DOJ review](https://oig.justice.gov/sites/default/files/archive/special/s0606/chapter5.htm); [CIA assessment](../broader-evidence/cia-lead-verification-2026-10-26/README.md). |
| **AUD004: FBI source handling** | Source reports, housing, a later payment and the payment's purpose must be evaluated separately. Obtain original handler and payment records, while retaining the OIG's relevant contrary findings and examination limits. [DOJ review](https://oig.justice.gov/sites/default/files/archive/special/s0606/chapter5.htm). |
| **AUD005–006: FAA/NORAD and Mineta** | Operational notice times, a later inaccurate and uncorrected liaison answer, and Mineta's recollection are different questions. The DOT OIG found insufficient evidence of knowing falsehood in the notification matter and separately reported the uncorrected liaison answer. The testimony does not itself identify a stand-down. [DOT OIG](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf); [public chronology review](../broader-evidence/cia-faa-plain-english-assessment-2026-10-26/README.md). |

| **AUD008: presidential warnings** | Threat products, delivery to specific recipients, what they conveyed and actions taken are separate facts. A release announcement or product count does not prove that every item was pre-attack or read by a particular person. [CIA release description](https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/cia-releases-presidents-daily-briefs-in-commemoration-of-the-25th-anniversary-of-9-11/). |

[FAA OIG](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2004003.pdf); [Senate study excerpt](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/media/16533/ocr); [steel-record review](../broader-evidence/steel-commission-preservation-meeting-2026-11-09/README.md). |
| **AUD012–014: options, alleged transfers and insurance** | Event-window trades, beneficial ownership, the origins of a particular transfer, gross insurance payments, costs and ultimate beneficiaries are distinct questions. ## Five original-record tracks

1. **FAA correction and disposition:** seek the exact corrected answer, transmission and Commission receipt, plus signed action or no-action decisions. A recommendation to correct does not prove a correction was delivered. [FAA records review](../broader-evidence/faa-dot-recording-determinations-and-recertification-2026-11-09/README.md).
2. **Telephone source and custody:** reconcile original call records, service and duration definitions, source audio, transcript enclosures, clocks and recipient records. A zero-duration field alone does not establish that no conversation occurred. [NYSP-transcript review](../broader-evidence/telephone-nysp-transcript-delivery-2026-11-09/README.md).
3. **Flight recorders:** compare native inputs and decoder outputs with the recovery chain, installed configuration and independent clock anchors. Decoding a candidate file does not by itself authenticate its aircraft or time. [Recorder-clock sources](../broader-evidence/recorder-clock-sources-2026-10-27/README.md).
4. **Materials and chemistry:** preserve the collection-to-chip-to-test crosswalk, calibrated native spectra, controls and matched-specimen comparisons. Different samples are not a direct same-specimen replication, and a laboratory finding would still require separate cause and personnel attribution. [Harrit et al.](https://benthamopenarchives.com/contents/pdf/TOCPJ/TOCPJ-2-7.pdf); [Millette report](https://www.911facts.dk/resources/Millette/9119ProgressReport022912_rev1_030112web.pdf).
5. **Steel preservation and attribution:** obtain the decision, cutting and accession chain, yard and shipping records, and building-attribution worksheets for specific specimens. Evidence of rushed recovery or missing records does not establish the composition of an untested object or a concealment motive. [Commission-preservation review](../broader-evidence/steel-commission-preservation-meeting-2026-11-09/README.md).

These source reviews are dated and may not reflect later agency releases. They test particular historical, physical and custodial claims; none establishes a UMS installation or coordination by the proposed actors.
