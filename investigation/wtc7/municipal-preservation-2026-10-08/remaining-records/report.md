# Municipal preservation instructions and implementation leads

October 8, 2026, America/New_York. Research only, under WP5/Q09/Q10 of the
full investigation charter. **Six documents, 20 pages, now have separately
frozen paired readings. Eight documents, 50 pages, remain sensitivity-gated.**
All 14 selected PDFs were acquired and all 70 pages rendered and technically
checked. Acquisition is not completed content review.

The reviewed records support a documentary preservation and collection process,
but do not establish that it was fully implemented. Two passages supply concrete
follow-up leads: a June 2003 report of concern about some agencies' electronic
preservation practices, and a November 2004 report of outstanding questionnaires.
Neither identifies a lost WTC7 item, actual destruction, or deliberate concealment.
These are findings about held public access copies, not independently
authenticated historical compliance or a change in collapse-cause ranking.

The June concern must constrain any claim that preservation directives prove
successful preservation. Conversely, the November incomplete returns do not
establish that the underlying records were destroyed: delayed questionnaires
and surviving records can coexist. Ordinary preventive or corrective records
management is consistent with these documents. That explanation does not
certify compliance or dispose of the specific follow-up leads.

The concrete objects in the six reviewed items are documents and electronic
information, including environmental records and their storage media. They
do not supply a WTC7-specific physical-material custody chain. A documentary
collection could nevertheless contain records about physical operations or
custodians; this review does not establish that such records were never collected
or are absent elsewhere. Nor does the common folder prove these 2003-2004
communications define the scope of the separately read 2007 reminder note.
