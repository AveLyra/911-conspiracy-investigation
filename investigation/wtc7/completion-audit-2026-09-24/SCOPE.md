artifacts, not report count or accumulated activity. Do not narrow the charter
to completed work or treat a cause ranking, a passed tool test, a preserved
hash, an AI review or an unmet approval as empirical completion.

Deliver one requirement-level matrix, with separate reviews for WP1/WP2,
WP3 and WP4/WP5 and root coverage of WP0/WP6, the ten questions, scientific
controls, operating/feedback boundaries and completion conditions. Every row
identifies: exact requirement; located evidence and verification ceiling;
completed portion; missing/weak/contradictory evidence; genuinely feasible
next test or precise dependency; and what result could change the assessment.
Separate completed, partial, unverified, contradicted and dependency-limited
states. No percentage-complete or manufactured probability score.

At least one highest-value remaining scientific test must be selected from
the inspected evidence, with prerequisites, authorizations, discriminating
outcomes and an execution-ready first action. Execute a safe feasible first
action in this turn where available; a dependency inventory alone is not a
substitute for investigation. Do not rerun completed tests solely to create
activity. A review may disclose that the result is not recoverable under
present conditions; document the exact limit, not a global source-absence claim.
