**No ranking change from this audit.** It establishes neither innocence nor
guilt. Observed fire plus a developed model is more evidence than a bare
possibility, but it does not verify the disputed full mechanism. Missing
operation evidence limits an intervention claim, but does not prove fire
initiation. Equally, unresolved historical model reproduction and dependent clocks do not
affirmatively demonstrate deliberate support removal. The strongest objection
to a confident fire-first conclusion remains the unverified building-specific
causal bridge; the strongest objection to demolition-first remains the missing
historically executed mechanism/operation bridge. This audit does not assign
numerical priors or settle that comparison by counting missing files.
