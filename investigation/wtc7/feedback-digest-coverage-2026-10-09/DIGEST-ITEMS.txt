01. Acquisition and perceptual states — SFB-002/005.
Keep located, approval-pending, initiated, reference-returned, partial, materialized, byte-checked, parsed, admitted, perceptually accessible, listened-to and human-reviewed states distinct. A successful reference-only response with an expiring locator is not acquired bytes. Provide a supported privacy-preserving materialization path, size/hash/type checks and secret-free receipts. Distinguish a reader projection, error body and original artifact with separate identity and coverage. A failed reader route can coexist with successful later acquisition; a denied route, DNS failure, deferred approval and unattempted item differ. Unsupported audio perception leaves events unknown, not absent. Synthetic fixtures: a reference-only response; partial transcription plus an error body; valid emitted audio that the receiving interface cannot perceive. Do not retry an unchanged unsupported capability indefinitely.

02. Search scope and bounded extraction — SFB-005.
Preserve literal and prospectively normalized queries, requested restrictions versus observed results, per-query membership, submitted-query budget, caps, pagination and termination uncertainty. Typed, submitted, pending, populated and explicit-zero states differ; bounded observation is not resubmission. A metadata miss is not a content search and a locator control does not establish OCR/index recall. Distinguish tokens, lines, occurrences, unique IDs, typed records and source families. Enforce declared page, section and purpose boundaries before extraction. Synthetic fixtures: batched union results without query joins, delayed population, punctuated/embedded identifiers, giant OCR objects, single-line HTML, heading-crossing previews, and a header extraction that would accidentally include the first dummy-sensitive data row. Fail closed on unisolatable scope; retain spill and failed attempts.

03. Catalog grain, representation and document roles — SFB-004/005.
Retain schema version, actual populated collections, selectors, requested-but-omitted metadata, source snapshot and count disagreement. A normalizer omission is not provider absence. Folder totals and first-document links do not promise a whole-folder artifact. Preserve displayed link versus target; acquisition-time joins versus later enrichment; covers versus attachments; physical/printed page namespaces; item versus component; request, comment, response, revision, installed state and completed review. Differently annotated copies remain one source family. Synthetic fixtures: conflicting catalog snapshots, alternate populated fields, a text mirror missing a chart, an attachment cover without content, and equivalent plain/base64/quoted-printable inline markers with a deliberately excluded attachment. Report decoded coverage and errors without printing bodies or inventing missing contents.

04. Redact before display and disclosure — SFB-002/005.
Public availability and preservation are not disclosure approval. Allowlist diagnostics, status and integrity fields before output; raw headers, complete transfer summaries, provider context and export objects may leak protected metadata. Later saved-file redaction does not undo prior display. Synthetic tests insert dummy cookies, signed references, source-locator fields and private sentinels into both success and default CLI-error paths. 05. Exact bytes, parser contracts and path safety — SFB-004/005.
Compare literal byte boundaries, not language encoding metadata: a prefix retaining its final newline must not gain another newline during verification. A final successful command cannot mask an earlier failure. Preserve each operation status. CSV-like parsers must retain quoted commas, doubled quotes, blanks, zero and unresolved rows. Whole-input integrity limits and interpreted-subset coverage differ; pin-specific metadata allowances must not silently relax native-source limits. Canonicalize both root and child consistently across path aliases without widening allowed paths. Archive safeguards must follow the effective format the actual parser interprets. Retain failed versions and boundary-control results rather than silently repairing originals.

06. Dependency closure, namespace and owner identity — SFB-004/005.
Matching listed pins and complete ID rosters are insufficient. Require relevant evidence/transform edges or explicit consequential gaps, traversal reachability and independently reconstructed closure. Separate input/derivation/output locators; shared modalities may share origin without proven synchronization. Resolve each manifest against its declared owner. Cached byte pins must not suppress expansion: pre-pin A and B in A-to-B-to-C, then changing C must fail. Verify separately recorded helper pins and every chunk's mandatory dependency map. Preserve typed namespaces, include transformations, consumer defaults, conditional field meaning, relation types, supplied/resolved/applied states, exact repetitions and contradictory duplicates. Same-number objects in another namespace or an interface relation cannot substitute for a missing dependency. Lexical absence does not establish absence of generated definitions or execution.

Exclude inherited overrides without displaying values and distinguish runtime-added keys. Require terminal successful transfer plus range/size/hash/type checks before parsing. 11. Native-coordinate human review — SFB-002/005.
Test Fit/native/enlarged views across zoom, scroll, resize and loading failures. Preserve pixel-center/boundary convention, hover/locked state, stale selection clearing and keyboard refinement. Require fine-view/keyboard checks or abstention. Serve allowlisted local assets without upload or automatic disclosure. An automated click, draft row, software pass or completed checklist is not an actual human response or acceptance.

12. Lossless annotation schemas and ownership — SFB-002/004/005.
Separate identity, visibility, localization, correspondence, material state, appearance, opportunity, caution and decisive exclusion. Preserve nonexclusive positives outside incomplete denominators; unknown does not become absent. Equal outer pixel sets can hide different core/fringe labels. Dispatch only declared schemas and aliases; reject conflicting aliases and unsupported list-valued membership fields rather than dropping them. Retain region/source/transform keys, explicit candidate-route lists, reciprocal same-column references, nonempty conflict material and multiple bands keyed by coordinate plus band ID. 13. Conditional support and correlated graphical uncertainty — SFB-004/005.
One visible band cannot become two independently identified series. A fringe-only boundary fragment can be valid without an in-crop core, but unknown continuation is not an endpoint. Keep identity, full-column existence, selected-cell enclosure, omitted halo and calibration assumptions separate. Establish overlap in shared geometry before uncertain mapping: two disjoint intervals under one common uncertain shift can have overlapping marginal hulls without joint overlap. Keep unaffected intervals and unresolved ownership; missing support is not a zero curve. Conditional review samples differ from accepted-support samples; excluded endpoint targets remain unresolved, absent primary coverage cannot be replaced by a peer, and same-position alternatives remain visible. Identical raster output can conceal different subpixel support/extrema.

14. Calibration, observables, fits and event censoring — SFB-002/004.

Last visibility, disappearance, local crossing, final disappearance and physical failure differ. Preserve joint place/time/phenomenon coverage and each display's codebook: separate observations cannot create unobserved combinations, cumulative union displays cannot establish time-specific presence or absence, and persistent effects and transient events require different temporal coverage. Retain moving references, opaque samples, left/right censoring and observer-specific one-sided bounds; extraction endpoints do not supply missing positive states or upper bounds. Enlargement adds no historical resolution or independence. Synthetic tests combine different endpoint pairs of equal length, a contour changed by horizontal motion, moving occluders, clips starting after an event, and overlapping windows that disagree jointly. A fitted coefficient or feasibility witness is not a recovered observation or probability.

15. Clock provenance and exact extraction coverage — SFB-002/004/005.
