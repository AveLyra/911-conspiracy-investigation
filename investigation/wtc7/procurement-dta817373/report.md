## What changes, and what does not

maintenance context, and exact exhibit references. It is no longer only an
unverified identifier list. The ordinary-maintenance account is affirmative
evidence and must receive weight when evaluating this particular transaction
lead. The refund outcome is not an adjudication of criminal or structural
misconduct. Pp36–37 affirm the ALJ, deny the exception/petition, and sustain
the prior partial refund allowance as modified by the1999 conciliation order.

The strongest objection is that legitimate maintenance and improper activity
could coexist. Correct: this source cannot exclude that possibility. But it
also cannot bridge from equipment maintenance to actual structural-area access,
improper purpose, preparation or causation. Such bridges need their own
evidence; political context, capability and missing exhibits cannot supply it.
No contradiction is imposed as a prerequisite to pursuing further existing
records. This result does not validate NIST's mechanical sequence.
