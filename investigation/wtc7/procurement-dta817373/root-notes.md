## Scope and strongest objections

The source materially corroborates the identifier lead and a1990s commercial
CCTV/access-control maintenance relationship. It does not supply a complete
security contract, personnel list, schedule, location-by-location access,
exclusive control, or proof the same vendor remained responsible in2001.
Ordinary maintenance can create access opportunities in principle, but an
opportunity inference cannot stand in for demonstrated operational access,
preparation, or intent. Conversely, a tax decision's maintenance finding does
not prove every historical activity lawful or rule out a separate operation.
