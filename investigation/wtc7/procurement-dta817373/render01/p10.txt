By a Conciliation Order dated July 23, 1999, petitioner was allowed an additional refund 
of sales tax in the amount of $20,556.91, which would equate to the conferee’s considering 
purchases of approximately $250,000.00 as not being subject to sales tax. According to the 
auditor, the additional purchases determined not to be subject to tax were represented by 
invoices of Horticultural Creations, Inc. for “the planting of trees and things of that nature” (tr., 
p. 69).
