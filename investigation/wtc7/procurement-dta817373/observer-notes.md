## Contrary evidence, ordinary explanation, and limits

1. **Claimed versus paid tax matters.** P11's Henry Brothers figure is
   $3,314.84 **claimed refundable**, whereas p23/n6 records $2,602.05 paid
   on the maintenance services after excluding $712.79 billed but not paid
   on invoice 12318. It would be wrong to report the larger figure as all
   paid or the excluded tax as refunded. P31 preserves the possibility that
   certain CCTV equipment purchases could have qualified for exemption,
   but identifies the absence of paid tax on the referenced check. The
   invoice/check difference has this ordinary tax/accounting explanation;
   it does not establish covert procurement or misconduct.
2. **Routine service and equipment purchase are distinct.** The recurring
   maintenance billing and an equipment-purchase invoice both appear in the
   same exhibit. The decision reports that the equipment was already working
   when the early-1990 contract was executed; it does not establish that
   Henry Brothers first installed the entire system then. The apparent
   monthly-fee reduction is the decision's inference from invoices, not an
   inspected amendment or evidence of an unexplained clandestine change.
3. **Agency is not permission.** Pp30,34–35 reject the claimed Port Authority
   purchasing-agency basis for exemption. That tax finding does not mean
   that the vendor lacked the lessee's authorization to do maintenance, or
   that the Port Authority expressly approved each job. Described maintenance
   and ordinary contracts support an ordinary-service reading, while specific
   approval records and actual access events remain unestablished.
4. **Dates/buildings/subjects stay bounded.** This record links the supplier
   to WTC7 CCTV maintenance in the stated context; it does not establish
   2001 access, a tower-wide system or contractor relationship, support-member
   alteration, sabotage, sponsorship, intent, or collapse cause. Government
   tenant work by other suppliers (pp20–21,27–28) must not be reassigned to
   Henry Brothers merely because it occurs in the same decision. The
   contextual September 11 destruction sentence in p2 n1 is not an engineering
   collapse-mechanism finding.
5. **Missing originals remain missing, not proven destroyed or withheld.**
   P7's documentation problems during the 1998 refund audit predate September
   2001. Neither those gaps nor absent itemization in this decision proves
   absence of work, concealment, or loss in the building's destruction.
