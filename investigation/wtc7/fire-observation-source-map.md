## Protocol to freeze before fire-extent scoring

This is a proposed first annotation protocol, not a claim that it has run. Prior familiarity and the present caption-informed screening are disclosed. Freeze a version/hash and log changes before assigning extent scores.

1. **Separate source selection, clocks, geometry, and flame annotation.** Inventory every available source, shot boundary, frame timestamp, crop, and known copy family. Select eligible shots by whether WTC 7 and identifiable facade openings are visible, not whether flames are apparent. Retain every ineligible interval with its reason. Include all located fire-period stills and candidate contrary/no-flame images; render the caption-only candidates above before finalizing inclusion. Keep original clocks, NIST-assigned clocks, and independent timing bounds in separate fields; leave unsupported times unknown.
2. **Define the geometric grid without fire labels.** Use architectural/undamaged views and full image context to map facades, floor slabs, columns, and window openings, recording alternative floor assignments and uncertainty. The report's north-face Fig. 5-13 (physical p. 149), south-face Fig. 5-20 (p. 154), and east-face Fig. 5-23 (p. 157) are starting references to verify, not independent camera calibration. Use native pixel scale; do not fill or interpolate hidden windows. Record projection and occluding buildings for each shot.
3. **Use a deliberately limited blind process.** An annotator receives stable randomized IDs and image regions without report prose, fire maps, model output, or assigned event times. Keep a separate provenance key. Embedded report labels cannot be invisibly erased or inpainted; identify their pixel masks and disclose that they compromise blinding. Prefer unannotated originals when obtained. Annotator familiarity remains a limitation; another AI pass is a second annotation, not an independent human/expert validation.
4. **Sample without choosing flame peaks.** For each continuous eligible video shot, take every 1.0 s from its first decodable presentation timestamp through its end, preserving exact frame number/PTS and duplicate-frame flags. Include every source photograph once per exposure; group alternate crops/encodes under that exposure. Shorter shots contribute their first frame. Inspect full-resolution frames; do not infer anything about the unsampled subsecond intervals. Additional event-focused frames may illustrate a transient but stay out of the fixed-sample denominator and are labeled exploratory. This rule must be finalized after the separate frame-timing audit.
5. **Annotate visibility before fire.** For each window/grid cell, record in-frame fraction, smoke/dust, reflection/glare, intervening structure, blur/compression, source window size in pixels, and label overlays. Mark low resolution if either native opening dimension is under 8 pixels; mark non-evaluable if less than half the opening is assessable or glare/smoke prevents a decision. Preserve the assessed visible fraction; examine 25%/75% thresholds as sensitivity, never as hidden tuning. A visible outer pane does not imply visibility of its interior.
6. **Keep distinct categories.** Code visible flame structure, glow/ambiguous red-orange region, smoke only (source known/unknown), no flame discernible in the assessable region, and non-evaluable. Record location, confidence and reason, not an intensity/temperature scale. Mark unknown glass condition rather than treating a dark pane as broken. Do not turn “no flame discernible” into “no fire.”
7. **Independent reannotation and disagreements.** Reannotate all borderline cells and a deterministic 20% sample of remaining eligible windows, stratified by facade, floor band, time range, source family, and first-pass category. Select within each stratum by the ascending SHA-256 of `protocol-version|asset-id|frame-pts|cell-id` so selection is reproducible. Preserve both records before adjudication. Report category confusion counts, visibility disagreement, and changed floor/time assignments. If one stratum is empty, report it rather than borrowing reassuring agreement from another.
8. **Publish a bounded map internally.** Use a facade × floor × 30-minute coverage grid, with exact source time intervals retained beneath each entry; an image spanning two bins remains interval-censored, not present at both exact times. Report flame-bearing assessable openings / assessable openings alongside the number and projected area of unobserved/obscured openings. Cluster summaries by underlying source family/exposure. Show sightings and gaps instead of interpolating fire continuity. Compare NIST's prescribed windows and shifted floor histories only after locking annotations; maintain a separate column for unmeasured structural consequences.

This would produce a retrospective measurement with declared dependence. A truly unused image family can be held out only after establishing it was not used for timing, grid construction, threshold selection, or earlier NIST input comparison.
