4.7 Summary and Conclusion 
In summary, several findings were made from the analyses above: 
 
1. Columns 79, 80, and 81 did not fail at the lower floors of the building. Instead, they 
needed to have failed at the upper floors of the building all the way to the penthouse. Yet 
there were no documented fires above Floor 30. Therefore, fire did not cause the 
collapse of Columns 79, 80, and 81 nor the collapse of the east penthouse. 
2. The hypothetical failure of Columns 79, 80, and 81 — the three easternmost core 
columns — would not trigger a horizontal progression of core column failures.
