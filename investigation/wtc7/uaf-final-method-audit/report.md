2026-09-20 UTC. Research only. This closes the **report-level question** in
[Luna's technical-review update, item3](../contracting-access-custody-audit/technical-review-update.md),
not an independent reconstruction of UAF or the comprehensive investigation.
Luna asked whether the relevant effects were included; it did not establish
that they were absent. [Protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [coverage](PAGE-SELECTION.md),
[execution](execution.md), [root reading](root-notes.md),

## Result

**UAF's March2020 final report explicitly excludes P-delta from its described
global analysis results. Its best-matching support-loss sequence is prescribed,
not generated by an independently demonstrated initiating mechanism.** This
weakens reliance on Chapter4 as decisive proof that progressive collapse was
impossible. It does not establish that adding P-delta would produce collapse,
validate NIST's fire-to-failure sequence, or prove deliberate intervention.

P-delta concerns the extra bending effect when gravity loads act through a
displaced structure. Its potential relevance to stability is not a numerical
estimate of its effect here. The report also contains nonlinear connection
models, material/geometric studies and a separate Column79 buckling calculation.
It would be incorrect to describe all UAF work as linear or to say buckling
was never considered.

## What the source actually supports

All page numbers below are **printed report pages**; add13 for the physical
PDF page. Both readers inspected the same37 complete selected pages, including
all of Chapter4, separately freezing notes before exchange. These are two
prior-informed AI readings of one source, not independent experimental evidence.

The motion match remains a useful conditional comparison. Prescribing removal
does not make a response calculation worthless. But agreement after selecting
a removal pattern/timing does not show **what caused** that pattern, or prove
it was the only pattern consistent with the observations. The report itself
says two different eight-story removal bands produced the same tracked corner
velocity/acceleration (p108). That is a concrete limit on inverse identification,
not merely a generic objection.

## Qualifications that change the weight

- **Visual tilt is amplified.** The report warns that displayed displacements
  are exaggerated (p94 and figure captions). They cannot be read as real-scale
  historical tipping simply from their appearance.
- **One salient feature is not reproduced.** The authors acknowledge inability
  to simulate the proposed north-roof kink mechanism and describe numerical
  limitations (p95). Penthouse joints are simplified and six southwest exterior
  losses are assumed from NIST's debris account (p94).
- **The exterior was not described as wholly undisturbed.** The report itself
  acknowledges upper-floor window breakage after the east-penthouse event (p92).
  Its limited-distortion argument must not become a claim of no exterior change.
- **No-fire inference needs an additional step.** Upper structural failure
  does not itself require direct upper-floor fire exposure; indirect propagation
  has to be tested. This does not assert that such propagation actually happened.
- **The study's own six-column case progresses.** Its objection is to the
  initiation/motion match of particular sequences, not demonstrated impossibility
  of any progressive collapse of any starting state.

The strongest favorable reading of UAF is retained: it supplies a substantial
alternate-load-path investigation with nonlinear local work, conditional motion
comparisons, and explicit limitations. Those findings merit testing. The stronger
historical exclusions in pp109–110 nevertheless exceed what this source review
and the unreproduced calculations establish.

## What would change this assessment

The decisive next **run-level** evidence is a hash-linked crosswalk from each
figure to native inputs, solver/version, gravity-equilibrated initial state,
geometric/material options, connection and member failure rules, impact/contact
treatment, prescribed timing/functions, and numerical convergence controls.
Matched calculations with and without the disputed effects could show the
outcome is robust or materially changed. Independent, unfitted motion targets
and their uncertainty are needed to test the claimed match rather than merely
repeat it. These are proposed tests, not solver executions or permission to
reopen closed acquisition routes.

**Assessment update:** reduce the weight assigned to UAF Chapter4 as a conclusive
exclusion of progressive failure. Do not transfer that lost weight automatically
to NIST, demolition or equal odds. The [interim causal assessment](../causal-chain-synthesis/report.md)
still does not establish a strict probability ordering. Apply the same separation
of stated method, actual run, prescribed input and historical inference to NIST.
