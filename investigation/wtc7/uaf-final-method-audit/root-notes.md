## Dispositions, significance and possible falsifiers

1. Luna item3 was a verification question, not a proved allegation of omission.
   Now answered at report level: explicit P-delta exclusion exists, including
   §4.4's case-results context. Historical execution and quantitative consequence
   are not independently reproduced. Exact run settings could narrow/correct
   the report statement; they cannot be guessed from software capability.
2. No-global-progressive-failure inference is weaker than a validated nonlinear
   dynamic exclusion. P-delta represents gravity-load effects in a displaced
   geometry; retaining or neglecting it can matter to stability. But an exclusion
   alone does not prove an important numerical error or that its inclusion
   would cause this building to collapse. Exact paired runs/convergence tests
   with other effects controlled could demonstrate negligible or large impact.
3. Material/geometric-study and nonlinear-connection/buckling content materially
   counter any blanket dismissal of UAF as only an animation/linear model.
   Their existence does not validate the specific large-deformation sequence.
4. Prescribed all-column-loss timing plus qualitative/velocity agreement is a
   conditional response calculation, not independent evidence of devices, fire,
   all-column historical failure times, or deliberate action. A priori predictions
   on independent views/events and numerical uncertainty are needed for a strong
   model-selection claim; no equal priors or numerical odds invented here.
5. Unmatched kink, amplified displacement graphics, simplified penthouse joints,
   and equal motion for two loss-height bands remain relevant limitations.
   More complete outputs or independent measurements could resolve them.
6. This weakens use of UAF Chapter4 as decisive proof excluding progressive
   failure; it does not corroborate NIST's disputed fire-to-failure inputs or
   establish a strict fire-versus-intervention causal ranking. Apply the same
   run-level, missing-physics, calibration and source standards to both.

No further acquisition or solver work is needed to close this bounded report
question. A future expert/run review needs exact input/output hashes, software
version, case/load-combination mapping, gravity initial state, geometric and
material options, connection/member deletion laws, contact/impact treatment,
time step/damping/convergence, imposed timing/function data and unfitted motion
targets. Archive-version ambiguity is not a finding of incompatibility or intent.
