## Controlling distinctions

The report is by Hulsey, Quan and Xiao, prepared for AE911Truth (cover).
Printed iii identifies external reviewers, funding, and an authors' conclusions
disclaimer. These statements establish attribution, not independent validation.
Printed ii states categorical fire-exclusion and near-simultaneous-column-failure
conclusions; those are the authors' claims, not findings adopted by this note.

The three approaches are separate: local thermal response, review of NIST's
initiation hypothesis, and hypothetical building-level failures (printed 21–25).
The documented existence of local nonlinear models does not establish the exact
formulation used for every global result, and prescribed support loss does not
identify its cause. Conversely, a prescribed-removal experiment can be useful
for conditional load redistribution and response comparisons; it is not invalid
merely because the initiating loss was imposed.

Gravity/heat loading is expressly discussed for Column 79 and the report
discusses self-weight, mass and acceleration. Thus gravity cannot be declared
absent. What is not established by these selected pages is the global run's
pre-removal gravity-equilibrium stage, retained stress state, gravity-ramp/start
conditions, or a case-linked time-integration/configuration record. A deformed
result image alone does not establish those prerequisites.

## Claim dispositions and strongest opposing reading

**Supported at report level:** local nonlinear connections and a separate
buckling calculation were considered; the published global comparison includes
linear-static and time-history cases, prescribed removals, and explicit P-delta
exclusions. **Contradicted as an unqualified summary:** the report never treats
nonlinearity or buckling; or, conversely, its mention of geometric nonlinearity
proves P-delta inclusion in the described global results. **Not established:**
verified run settings, comprehensive post-buckling/contact/failure propagation,
gravity initialization, unique historical initiation, or universal fire exclusion.

The strongest favorable reading is that the study supplies physically motivated
alternate-load-path comparisons, nonlinear connection work, redundancy checks,
multiple hypothetical patterns, and explicit limitations. Its modeled mismatch
with a particular progressive sequence is evidence worth testing, not something
to discard because the initiation was prescribed. But this does not justify
upgrading finite tested scenarios into exhaustive exclusion, nor turning a
post-loss motion match into proof of near-simultaneous historical failures.
The broad conclusions on printed 109–110 outrun what this limited independently
read/reproduced record can establish. This audit does not establish the contrary
historical cause or quantify how much any omitted effect changes a result.

The decisive missing link is case-specific input/configuration/output lineage
and a controlled comparison retaining initial stress, nonlinear instability,
connection failure, and integration/contact assumptions as appropriate. A
documented matched comparison with and without the disputed effect that leaves
the material outcome unchanged would weaken the concern; a changed outcome
would strengthen it. Native run evidence could also clarify the reach of the
exclusion statements. This identifies a falsifier, not authority to retrieve
closed archives, run an unavailable solver, or broaden this audit.
