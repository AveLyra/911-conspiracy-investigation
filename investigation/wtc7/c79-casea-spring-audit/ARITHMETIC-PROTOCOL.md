## Arithmetic, declared before point evaluation

Evaluate exact rational arithmetic on each finite binary64 value in the
frozen JSON (via its integer ratio). This proves arithmetic for those parsed
values, not preservation of every original decimal digit or physical accuracy.
Keep exact numerator/denominator strings plus display approximations.

1. Preserve all supplied points in source order, all blank/zero header fields
   and all source locators. Do not sort points into a preferred curve.
2. Apply the documented zero/blank-to-one defaults only to SFA/SFO; OFFA/OFFO
   use their documented zero defaults. Compute supplied-point transforms
   `x = SFA*(A+OFFA)` and `y = SFO*(O+OFFO)`.
3. Report point count, x/y extrema, whether x is strictly increasing,
   decreasing or has equal steps, ordinate monotonicity, sign coverage,
   explicitly supplied/transformed origin, and adjacent secant slopes.
   A zero x-step gives an explicit undefined slope, never a numerical
   division or suppressed point. Mark positive/zero/negative slope counts.
4. Multiply transformed ordinates and secants by each selected element's
   supplied S, using only its documented blank default1. An explicit S0
   stays0; no blanket zero=blank rule. Preserve OFFSET separately rather
   than inventing a displacement history. Report quantities in model units,
   not N, m or physical connection capacity.
5. If positive OFFA with DATTYP0 would generate extra points per the manual,
   flag that branch and retain supplied-point diagnostics only; do not call
   the transformed list a complete internally generated solver table.
   Unsupported headers/field arities/dependencies fail the semantic join.
6. Report first/last supplied point and adjacent endpoint secants as table
   diagnostics. Do not extend them into an asserted solver-exact law. The
   manual establishes constitutive extrapolation, not its exact S04 formula.

SECTION_DISCRETE DRO0 identifies translation. KD/V0/CL/FD/CDL/TDL and S04 LCR
remain their actual numeric entries, with blanks separate. Do not declare
FD0 a demonstrated disabled failure rule or immediate failure: the reviewed
pages lack that explicit truth table. No nonzero FD can be invented from
the final curve point. The underlined origin/quadrants sentence on PDF2175
is literally in the LCR row; do not move it to LCD to manufacture a violation.

## Controls and claim limits

Pre-evaluation controls cover offset-before-scale, explicit zero SFA/SFO
defaults, explicit S0 versus blank S, decimal/binary rational distinction,
duplicate x/undefined slopes, decreasing/nonmonotone and negative/zero/positive
segments, full point order, missing/duplicate references, source-pin refusal,
selection coverage, and create-only output. Use small synthetic non-sensitive
tables for independent arithmetic verification, then reconcile the complete
historical selected output with a separately implemented calculation.

No load-reversal/energy-dissipation algorithm, temperature-dependent law,
post-failure state, historical forces, spring-axis coordinates, member/floor
identity, physical capacity or collapse mechanism is determined here.
Tests of data and arithmetic do not replace physical validation. A plateau,
softening segment or missing zero-point is not by itself a fraud or cause
finding. Preserve all disagreements; further source/build/state dependencies
remain named rather than guessed.
