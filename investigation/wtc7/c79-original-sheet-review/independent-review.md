## 2. Minimal primary-sheet chain and fields

This is staged evidence collection, not a requirement to obtain every possible
record before assigning any conditional weight. For each claim, retain the
strongest completed stage and name the unresolved field; do not silently fill
it from a neighboring floor, another model or a drawing convention.

| Stage | Minimum record/field to preserve | What it can discriminate |
|---|---|---|
| A. Copy/source identity | Origin and acquisition path; original bytes/hash; complete sheet/page extent; design/erection/shop/alteration role; sheet/detail identifiers; issue/revision/date/status as actually shown; legibility/cropping limitations | Whether the cited object is an original-sheet copy, a crop, a redrawing or an unidentified diagram. A hash alone is not authenticity. Record status without exporting sensitive signatures/contact data. |
| B. Floor/member applicability | Floor and grid/endpoints on plan/elevation; erection mark and member schedule; column segment/splice limits; referenced end-detail and general-note sheets; piece-mark multiplicity; applicable revision and supersession/cross-reference chain | Whether this actual detail applies to this floor/member/end, and whether one mark refers to multiple pieces. A typical-floor label alone cannot resolve exceptions. |
| C. Both-end geometry | Girder/column section designations and local orientation; effective end section/reinforcement; bearing seat/plate extents and thickness; column cover/side plates; end setbacks, approach gaps and clearances; dimensions and their local directions | Which surfaces can bear or contact, and what travel/rotation is geometrically available. Distinguish seat width from axial bearing length and actual available travel. Do not read dimensions from an uncalibrated screenshot when labeled dimensions are illegible. |
| D. Distinct connection components | Column-side seat and its support/stiffener plate; girder-web stiffeners separately; top clip; bolt count/diameter/grade, hole type/size/slot direction and clearances; weld type/size/extent and joined parts; source-specified material/connection requirements | Whether the proposed model detail represents the physical component being discussed. Missing or ambiguous details stay unknown; no fastener or plate is substituted for another. Shop versus field attachment and construction-only notes matter. |
| E. Adjacent restraint/modification links | Slab/deck/stud callouts and schedules; connected transverse members or braces; relevant splice/diaphragm details; added plates and connection reinforcement; affected-floor and revised-load references | Whether a claimed isolated seat or girder omits a source-specified parallel restraint, composite action, modification or demand change. This is a traceable local neighborhood, not a demand for all building documents. |
| F. Installed-condition corroboration | Location/date-linked construction or renovation photographs, inspection/acceptance or change records that actually show the selected detail; distinguish what is visible from hidden/obscured items | Whether the issued design was installed and whether later changes apply. No photograph, seal or revision label is automatically proof of every hidden component or its state on September 11. |

## 3. Original-sheet finding is not the model/run finding

Use separate status fields for: `original_copy_reviewed`,
`applicable_revision_identified`, `installed_condition_corroborated`,
`released_input_mapping_verified`, and `historical_run_link_verified`.
Use yes/no/unknown with an exact source locator and ceiling, not a single
undifferentiated verified flag. A cropped diagram can still provide a limited
specified measurement or source lead, but cannot be labeled complete-sheet
reviewed. An original-sheet copy can supply conditional design evidence before
installed corroboration is available.

The later input join requires units, signed axes, origin and floor elevations
or other independent geometric controls, then the exact member/end/seat nodes,
elements, contacts and property namespaces in pinned files. The historical
run/state join separately requires initialization, transferred damage, active
attachment/contact state, material/curve version and directional histories.
None is supplied by matching a C79 label, PID179, a photograph or a seat width.
The effect on stability requires demand, engagement, stiffness/capacity and
evolving relative motion; it is not decided by this checklist. No new transform,
force law, candidate-contact run or capacity calculation is proposed here.
