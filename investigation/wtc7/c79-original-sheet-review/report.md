# Column 79 original-sheet evidence boundary

## Result

The targeted search has not supplied a complete original construction sheet
that independently fixes the selected Column 79 connections. It has located
specific drawing routes, separated access states, and defined the sheet-to-
member comparisons needed to test the reported restraints. The two public
packet links remain unreviewed pending the repository's informed-approval gate;
they have not failed a download or been shown to be unavailable. The
[search ledger](search-ledger.md) preserves the six exact queries and four
resource attempts.

This is a bounded source-access result, not a new physical discrepancy or a
demonstration that either NIST or its critics used the correct installed detail.
The [prior crosswalk](../c79-member-detail-crosswalk/report.md) still provides
the established source/version distinctions and three released column-part
bridges. The missing step is a location- and revision-specific connection join,
followed by a test of that connection's actual modeled engagement and capacity.

## 1. Three different claims must not be collapsed

A **full drawing copy** can establish what the acquired sheet depicts, provided
its labels, dimensions, notes and revisions are legible. It does not alone
establish what was installed. **Installed condition** needs a defensible link
to the applicable construction/modification history, photographs or inspection
records. **Historical model use** additionally needs the input version and
run lineage. Even a perfect drawing-to-mesh match would not supply the force,
temperature or failure history of the actual building.

NIST reports using shop drawings and finding agreement with renovation
photographs; its connection discussion also cites construction photographs.
Those are positive source leads, not evidence to discard because the account
is disputed. Their exact target-floor correspondence has not been independently
checked here. Conversely, the lack of a professional-engineer seal on shop
drawings does not by itself discredit their contents.[^nist]

The UAF 2017 presentation depicts an attributed C79-end section associated with
Frankel 9114. It is an inspectable published detail, but it is not the complete
original sheet with a revision register and floor applicability. The new
standalone TIFF route did not yield an image. Repeated or redrawn versions of
the same detail do not supply independent evidence of installed condition.[^uaf]

## 3. What could actually change the model assessment?

Each selected full-sheet comparison should record drawing type, sheet and
detail identifier, issue/revision date, member mark, floor/elevation range,
both endpoints and local orientation. Required physical fields include seat
width and bearing overlap, side-plate projection/clearance, girder-end/stiffener
geometry, bolts and slots, welds, and relevant slab/stud details. Unreadable or
unspecified fields stay unknown; do not fill them from a schematic.

Keep the column's bearing/side plate distinct from the girder's web stiffeners.
A plate shown in one role does not prove that a plate serving another role was
included. Likewise, a total seat width does not alone determine a walk-off
distance. The earlier crosswalk preserves NIST's 2012 correction to axial and
lateral distances and its assertion that correct values were used historically;
that assertion still needs an input/run check.[^dimensions]

| Possible finding | What it would strengthen or weaken | Further test before a collapse conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Applicable original and location-specific photographs agree with the represented detail | Strengthens the geometry/installed-state link and weakens that specific omission criticism | Confirm model version, element/property assignment, temperatures, engagement and force history |
| Applicable original or modification differs materially from the represented detail | Supports a specific fidelity concern; weakens use of an unqualified typical detail for that location | Controlled sensitivity with the differing feature, keeping load/history and other choices explicit; include collapse and non-collapse outcomes |
| A competing analysis substitutes a different floor's detail | Weakens that analysis's building-specific applicability | Re-run its proposed mechanism with the correct applicable detail and comparable boundary conditions |
| Original-sheet dimensions agree but predicted and observed motion differ | Geometry agreement cannot resolve the mismatch alone | Check thermal/loading, constitutive behavior, solver handoff, contact and observation alignment |
| No applicable original is obtained | Limits independent verification of that link | Preserve the exact outstanding source, not a fabricated dimension, missing-force estimate or cause ranking |

These tests apply to all proposed mechanisms. An intervention hypothesis does
not become demonstrated because a connection detail remains uncertain; a
fire-driven sequence does not become independently validated because it has a
published schematic. Nor is every geometry discrepancy necessarily large
enough to change the outcome. That requires the relevant physical sensitivity.

The [integrated causal assessment](../causal-chain-synthesis/report.md) is
unchanged: NIST's developed pathway carries conditional technical weight, but
the independent investigation has not established a strict comparative
probability ordering. This is neither equal odds nor a demolition finding.

## 5. Next executable decisions

With exact archive approval, first declare bounded inspection/expansion limits
and preserve acquisition provenance; then select the applicable full technical
sheets without displaying unrelated correspondence. If the named packets do
not contain a required sheet, record actual member coverage and identify the
specific missing source. Do not repeat generic searches or silently substitute
the previously denied TIFF route. A user-supplied, appropriately cleared
complete sheet is another possible source, not a required new submission.

Without that approval, this acquisition branch remains paused. Independent
work on already admitted evidence can continue. The next scientific task should
address an unmet charter discriminator, not keep rechecking the unchanged
supplementary production. No main/legal record, accepted Sherlock finding,
Faraday invocation, external outreach, commit or push results from this unit.
