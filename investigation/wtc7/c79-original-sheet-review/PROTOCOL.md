installed condition separate. Record title/revision/detail/floor/member scope,
but do not expose sensitive signatures, contact information or accompanying
correspondence. The public bulk archives identified in the prior review remain

Originals are preserved; derived reading notes belong only in this investigation
worktree. No main/legal edits, FACT/EXH promotion, accepted Sherlock evidence,

## Acceptance

exact revision/member/floor and physical-role limits, or a specific bounded
nonfinding plus actionable next source decision. A drawing cannot establish
historical run identity, a mesh namespace, an active contact or capacity by
itself. Do not use the newly acquired source to manufacture a floor-to-Z or
force law. Preserve contrary results and source disagreements. This is a step
toward the full structural discriminator, not completion of the investigation.
