## Instrumentation, processing and data limitations

**Direct sensor record versus derived force.** On **pp.70–73**, load was measured with two load cells, column displacement with two draw-wire transducers, and beam strain with eight gauges. The instrumentation used a 12-bit DAQPad 6020E / LabView acquisition route. The report states load-cell verification within 100 lb (0.1% of rated capacity), DWT verification within 0.01 in, and gauge resistance checks. These are **reported checks**, not our calibration certificates or an uncertainty budget. A sampling frequency, raw DAQ export and complete filtering/zeroing configuration were not recovered in this pass.

**pp.75–78, §4.2, Eqs.(23)–(34):** axial forces and moments are derived from strain using linear-elastic steel modulus and section properties, paired gauges, and moment extrapolation assuming zero moment at the true pin. They are not independent direct axial-force transducer observations. Gauge 1 on the left and gauge 6 on the right gave erroneous data; the report uses pairs 2/3 and 5/8 instead. Table/plot labels saying “measured axial” must retain that derivation lineage.

**Rotation convention and printed-equation issue, p.78:** three different distances are defined: gauge distance, bolt-line distance and pin-to-column-flange-face distance. Equation (34) visibly prints `theta_n = tan^-1(x_f / Delta_n)`, defining `x_f` as the horizontal pin-to-column-flange-face distance and `Delta_n` as vertical DWT displacement. Preserve that literal printed ratio for independent scrutiny; do not silently replace it, claim the underlying processing used a correction, or calculate new rotations without a declared comparison rule. The report's tables/graphs show small beam-end rotations, so the printed equation and actual processing require reconciliation through original calculations/raw data or a documented correction. This note makes no numerical correction.

**5ST1, pp.95–97, §4.3.7, Figs.4.25–4.27:** the hydraulic hoses were reversed, limiting pump output. The test was stopped, the hoses corrected, and data collection restarted near maximum flexural capacity. Data before approximately 0.06 rad were neglected. This is more specific than merely saying that an initial record was unavailable; the loading history itself was interrupted. The original includes the later trace and failure observations. The later NIST omission must not erase the original test or imply an uninterrupted usable full history.

**3ST3, pp.106–107, §4.4, Table4.4:** the author reports severe unexplained spikes in 3STL3 and a statics inconsistency, questions that side's data accuracy, and excludes it from further sections because the failure was localized to 3STR3. Do not assume the later NIST averaged axial history either includes or repairs the affected data without a traceable transfer/processing record.

**3ST1, pp.79–81, §4.3.1, Figs.4.1–4.4:** manual hole reaming is a specimen-specific geometry departure. The author proposes it as the reason this 3-bolt test had bolt shear rather than tab tension rupture; that cause attribution is an inference, not an isolated controlled intervention. Plaster whitewash was unavailable for this test, unlike the general procedure; observations used mill-scale stress marks.

## Strength, transfer and unresolved dependencies

- **A, documentary observation:** exact original identity, reported test setup, eighteen-connection/nine-test structure, operator-controlled loading, recorded data defects and table-event definitions are directly established by the preserved original pages. Experimental apparatus/calibration records were not independently inspected, so this grade is not a guarantee that every reported measurement is accurate.
- **B, bounded physical support:** the original supplies physical assembly observations with instrumentation and explicit consistency checks, strengthening the provenance behind TN 1749's later comparison. Reuse, test-specific modifications, derived forces, interrupted loading and incomplete raw data limit independence and extrapolation.
- **D, unresolved transfer:** this original does not identify the exact data file/version delivered to NIST, every later averaging/exclusion step, the corrected rotation-processing implementation, or a WTC 7 property/run crosswalk. Obtaining those records could change whether a particular later comparison is reproducible; no new outreach is authorized here.

The exact Sadek 2008 full paper remains a separate unavailable source. Thompson's **2009** physical experiments and TN 1749's **2012/2013** models cannot be backdated into it or silently substituted for its original validation chain. No physical fire-to-collapse or cause-ranking conclusion follows from this source intake.
