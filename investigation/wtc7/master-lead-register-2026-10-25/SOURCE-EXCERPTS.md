### M067: source text lines 181–189

Exact text lines before citation relocation.

```text
67. **Does the proposed lower-floor fire sequence reproduce the upper-façade free-fall interval?** NIST identifies the sustained uncontrolled fires on Floors 7–9 and 11–13, including Floor 13, while acknowledging approximately 2.25 seconds of essentially free-fall descent of the north face. This spatial separation makes the propagation and loss-of-support sequence a specific quantitative question. It does not establish a contradiction merely because the descending upper structure was above the principal fire floors.

    **The question to preserve:** Does NIST’s specified lower-floor fire damage and ensuing failure sequence actually produce sufficiently extensive loss of support, with the timing and residual forces necessary to reproduce the observed upper-façade acceleration, coherence, and motion? Relevant tests include matched model displacement/acceleration, member-force and contact histories, the failure chronology, and measurement and model uncertainty. This makes explicit the connection implicit in item 9 and historical inventory item 58.

    **NIST’s explanation and the mechanical qualification:** Upper stories need not themselves be heated to descend near gravitational acceleration if their supports fail below them. NIST attributes the interval to negligible support after lower exterior-column buckling. Compatibility with that explanation in principle does not independently validate its particular historical sequence; describing the process as progressive collapse does not replace the quantitative comparison.

    **Force constraint and observation limit:** For a defined fixed-mass body, sustained center-of-mass acceleration near gravitational acceleration requires a correspondingly small net non-gravitational vertical force. Substantial upward resistance cannot coexist with that motion unless physically justified downward forces offset it. A tracked roof point is not automatically that body’s center of mass. The body, interval, relative motion, projection, and uncertainty must be established or bounded. This limitation must be tested, not used to invent unlimited hidden motion or unexplained cancelling forces. The linked project force calculations are conditional analyses of separately identified published motion data, not an independent reproduction of NIST’s exact 2.25-second measurement or a measured historical force history. Neither the spatial separation nor free fall alone establishes simultaneous failure of every column, deliberate intervention, or a conspiracy.

    Sources: [NIST FAQ, questions 8–9 and 32](https://www.nist.gov/world-trade-center-investigation/study-faqs/wtc-7-investigation); [finite-interval force analysis](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/b016bd3bf61bc664eb8b69d3e4b1828646c91fcc/investigation/wtc7/finite-interval-force/report.md); [roof-point and center-of-mass identifiability analysis](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/b016bd3bf61bc664eb8b69d3e4b1828646c91fcc/investigation/wtc7/body-com-identifiability/report.md).
```
