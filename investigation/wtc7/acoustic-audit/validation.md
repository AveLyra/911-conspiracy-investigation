**This is a newly written replacement summary, not the original validation report.** It summarizes the [public status entry](../STATUS.md). It does not reproduce the source media, tests, code, plots, run outputs or independent review, and no such test was rerun in preparing it. It does not purport to recover the original validation document or attest its full contents.

The September 11, 2026 status reports two fresh runs with nine synthetic checks each, 21 byte-identical product pairs and 22 verification checks, including an overwrite-refusal check. It also reports intact hashes for four selected source streams and six legacy artifacts, and inspection of four plots and 19 machine-transcript entries. The status states that exact independent-review coverage was described in the unavailable validation and separate review; that coverage cannot be reconstructed from the status entry alone. These are historical reported claims, not checks newly verified here.

The same status distinguishes reproducibility of the same code from acoustic validation. It says an independent reviewer reproduced four windows using a separate metric implementation and localized seek residuals, while their codec cause remained unresolved. This is not an established clock correction. The later audiovisual continuation reports independent agreement on digital-level comparisons but expressly does not claim actual listening, original audiovisual synchronization, sound identification or calibrated detectability.

To extend the analysis, the public status calls for a full-decode, channel-preserving reference and a declared source-segment/edit/audiovisual correspondence audit. A claim that an explosive sound was present or necessarily detectable would additionally require original-chain and distance information, suitable controls, and a defensible opportunity-to-detect assessment. The [NIST WTC 7 FAQ](https://www.nist.gov/world-trade-center-investigation/study-faqs/wtc-7-investigation) provides its conditional modeled-blast claim and discusses a responder's reported booms; neither the FAQ nor these digital checks substitutes for calibration of the recording under study.
