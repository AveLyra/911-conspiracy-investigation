## Prospective review criteria, before new historical frame inspection

1. A change between adjacent encoded frames may bracket a **change of shot/content**. It does not by itself show the duration or direction of a jump in historical time. A pan, exposure change, graphic overlay or fade is an alternative to a hard cut.
2. To check the old assertion that descent is absent from the entire approximately 435–441.9-second passage, the whole intervening span must be inspected at appropriate temporal coverage. Endpoints or isolated cut neighborhoods cannot establish absence throughout the gap.
3. In the MSNBC neighborhood, distinguish foreground action, camera reframing, tower/roof visibility, apparent relative displacement, obscuration, and off-screen material. Transcript-segment boundaries are navigation coordinates only. Neither a quoted reaction time nor a word onset is verified by viewing frames.
4. A first visible changed scene is not necessarily the first physical building movement: earlier movement may be out of frame, behind an obstruction or too small for the selected sample/raster.
5. Separate-stream zero PTS origins permit an uploaded-track coordinate crosswalk but do not authenticate original scene synchronization. Equal or continuous timestamps do not falsify editing or supply a sound-travel correction.
6. A positive-control image sequence may show visible change without independently establishing the mechanism label or synchronized acoustic onset. Those remain different evidentiary questions.
