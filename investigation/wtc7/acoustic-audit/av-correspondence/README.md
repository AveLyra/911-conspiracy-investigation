# Audio/visual correspondence: recovered historical audit

This package preserves the prior investigation’s review of edits in a WTC 7 footage compilation and a comparator recording. It reports abrupt shot changes, distinct foreground turns and camera movement, and digital audio-level comparisons around selected edit coordinates. The review expressly did **not** listen to or identify sounds during that pass, establish the original audio/video synchronization, or measure the physical sound field. An edit or waveform change alone does not establish an explosion, concealment, or a collapse cause.

The [report](report.md), [validation](validation.md), [separate computational review](independent-review.md), [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), and [audio comparison review](audio-review/review.md) are historical records. A preface and explicit unavailable-link labels distinguish this package from the original reports. Selected scripts, JSON outputs, and frame-index maps are copied byte-for-byte; they may reference omitted inputs. Raw video, audio, frame images, and original run receipts are not included and remain local. The files were not executed or independently reproduced for this publication; parsability is not scientific validation. The original receipts include local paths and are omitted. The [manifest](manifest.json) gives source and derivative hashes and the paths that were not transferred. The original local package is unchanged.

The image/frame and digital audio measurements depend on the stated uploaded access copies and coordinates. Original footage custody, alignment and clock settings remain open. A source-matched listening review and authenticated timing/capture chain could test event-specific sound claims, but the summarized measurements neither establish an intentional editing operation nor rule out every possible audible event. The separately labeled computational reviewer was not an independent historical witness or a licensed audiovisual expert.

For this package, a read-only count of the four saved index maps found 454 listed frame views and 438 unique indices after separating the two source videos; the two saved audio-measurement JSON files are byte-identical. A separate arithmetic check confirmed that the saved before/after window RMS and dBFS values agree for all three edit coordinates, including the zero-valued window. These are checks of saved files, not a rerun against the media.
