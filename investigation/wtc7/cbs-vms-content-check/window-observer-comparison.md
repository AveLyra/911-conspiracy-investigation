## Result

this fixed Peskin set. Both remain unresolved for cross-source attribution.**

but I cannot demonstrate the required two distinctive spatial relationships
across the sources. This is not evidence that they depict different buildings,
that a matching view is absent elsewhere in either recording, or that the
positive fire-like appearances are spurious.

and close fire views, which retain distinctive foreground relationships. Those
relationships are not supplied by the tightly cropped CBS building insets.

only generic window bands or the catalog title.

## Distinctive relations versus generic resemblance

Two useful relationships are positively visible **within the Peskin views**:

1. The background luminous row is partially occluded on its left by a pale
   stepped/projection facade, with separated luminous groups continuing to its
   right in the background plane.
2. A different, closer brick facade with projecting balcony slabs bounds the
   same gap on the right; its window/balcony arrangement is distinct from both
   the pale foreground facade and the banded background facade.

These are stronger than orange color or a repeated grid. But they cannot be
transferred to CBS when neither joint relation is visible there. Conversely,
the CBS close damaged/opening-detail candidates are too small and insufficiently
distinct in the selected Peskin windows to establish a join. The apparent
similarity of a fire row and damaged-looking adjacent openings is a lead,
not two independently discriminating matched architectural relationships.

I examined the foreground/background conflict explicitly: Peskin has large
foreground occluders, whereas CBS's crop is filled mostly by facade. That
difference prevents claiming the same **complete composition**. It does not
exclude CBS being a tighter view of a region between those occluders, or a
different view/time of the same facade. Calling their absence proof of another
building would overstate what the crop permits.

## Fire-positive preservation and inference limits

Conspicuous luminous/fire-like regions remain affirmative observations in all
six CBS samples and in the Peskin close/wider fire-window samples described
above. The unmatched result does not erase them. The wide smoke views are not
negative fire observations for obscured or unresolved areas.

The PNG metadata differ: CBS aspect=(0,1); coarse Peskin aspect=(8,9), gamma
and chromaticity; dense Peskin no PNG info entries. Viewer interpretation is
uncharacterized. I therefore did not use hue, brightness, apparent saturation,
metric aspect, measured angle or apparent flame size as a match/mismatch
criterion. No color discrepancy was converted into a severity claim.

No same-building, facade, floor, historical time, exposure-duration, common
camera, exact exposure, shared footage or independent-witness status is newly
established across CBS and Peskin. Even a future scene join would not prove
common camera-original bytes; conversely, multiple containers would not create
independent cameras. Existing source-family labels are not modified by this
unresolved visual test.

## Claim strength, objection and closure

The positive visible fire-like imagery is an appearance observation (B), not
an interior heat-field measurement. A cross-source scene/view association here
is underdetermined (D). I have high confidence that this bounded pass did not
demonstrate the declared two-distinctive-relationship acceptance criterion;
that is not a probability that the buildings or events differ.

The strongest objection is missing context and sampling: a wider/higher-quality
CBS source, another legitimately selected view, or an authenticated shot/edit
record might establish the relationship that these small broadcast insets and
nine fixed comparison frames cannot. The absence of a usable architectural
join in this set has little power to exclude the same building, particularly
when smoke and cropping hide the discriminating landmarks.

Close this fixed pass. The precise prerequisite is a source-grounded bridge
showing the CBS window pattern in relation to nonrepetitive building/neighbor
geometry, or reliable source-shot records identifying the camera/view; any
future display/source expansion needs its own declaration. Neither unbounded
image browsing nor another arbitrary crop is authorized here. No NIST fire-input
correction, fire-to-failure conclusion, cause ranking, legal/engine promotion,
outreach, publication, commit or push follows.
