**The fixed comparison does not establish a strong CBS–Peskin scene/view
association. Both CBS compositions remain unresolved.** Two separately frozen,
prior-informed AI observations agree on that limited result. They also retain
the affirmative fire-like window appearances; failure to identify a counterpart

conditional mechanistic weight, without an independently earned strict

## Strongest objection and next prerequisite

The limited resolution, tight broadcast crop and fixed sampling may hide the
very landmarks needed for a valid match. A wider/higher-generation CBS view or
a reliable clip-to-camera record could establish a relationship this test could
not. Consequently, this unresolved result has little exclusionary force against
the same underlying building. Conversely, resemblance alone cannot supply the
missing identity, floor, time or source independence.

Close this 15-image route. A further CBS identity test needs a source-grounded
architectural bridge or populated clip/source crosswalk, with a separately
declared selection. Do not keep enlarging, recoloring or browsing arbitrary
frames within this test. Naming a prerequisite authorizes no outreach or
sensitive transfer. Other charter work can proceed independently.
