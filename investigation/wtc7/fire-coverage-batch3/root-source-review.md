## Comparison boundary

Both readers independently identify flame-like forms in multiple close views;
we cannot reasonably describe all selected imagery as mere weak indistinct
glow. Yet facade photographs alone do not give gas/steel temperatures,
heat-release rates, total burning area or sufficient structural failure.
Many images are report-processed and many share sources. Independent labeling
of report images is not independent acquisition or authentication.

The strongest objection to this batch as model validation is that selected
imagery was already part of the report's reconstruction, and some assigned
times depend on the same fire/window changes a later comparison might test.
The strongest objection to treating that dependence as proof of fabrication
is that approximate relative dating from physical scene changes can be
reasonable, is disclosed, and is not shown false by the dependency alone.

The129/130wide-view/enlargement pair is a concrete same-exposure warning:
both frozen readers recorded no luminous feature in the small wide image,
but both recorded luminous features in the report enlargement (one calls
some flame-like, the other retains only ambiguous glow). This is not a
controlled enhancement experiment: source processing and resolutions differ.
It nevertheless directly defeats the rule that no identified flame in a
distant frame establishes no fire or mild interior heating.

The source sometimes gives stronger room/flashover/ventilation interpretations
than this native-image pass independently supports. Unsupported here is not
proved false: the report discusses additional moving clips and floor plans
not remeasured in this bounded batch. Conversely, those unperformed checks
cannot be credited as this investigation's validation. No quantitative
temperature correction, structural result, cause ranking or intent follows.
