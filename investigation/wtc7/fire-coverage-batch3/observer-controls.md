1. A visible veil and absence of an identified flame form can coexist. Record
   smoke separately. A nondetection locator is justified only for the part
   with an actual visible opportunity to identify form; an opaque veil or dark
   interior does not establish absence of fire.
2. If foreground obstruction prevents secure target localization, record
   `unresolved_target`, a null target rectangle, and the reason. An empty
   luminous-feature list records only no identified feature in this review;
   it cannot establish no fire behind the foreground.
3. Smooth orange light without resolved irregular flame morphology is an
   `ambiguous_glow`. Reflection, illuminated material, saturation, and projected
   depth remain alternatives; no temperature or heat estimate follows.
4. A printed red numeral is a source-added overlay. Locate it as an overlay,
   not as a luminous fire feature, and do not authenticate its meaning from
   its appearance.
5. Overlapping rectangles are approximate locators. They do not support an
   area sum, segmentation, a count of burning windows, or a fire-extent score.
6. Repeated camera views or exposures must retain dependency when the source
   join is later permitted. Different filenames do not prove independent
   observations, independent clocks, or independent corroboration.
