## Timing and source limits

The per-figure records include credited source, report processing, viewpoint, floor/window labels, source-page hashes and explicit timing basis. These remain NIST attributions. Original camera files, clocks, authorship and custody were not authenticated.

Several clocks depend materially on source interpretation:

- 5-121/123 use approximate aerial timing tied to shadow/sunlight comparisons; the complete earlier calibration method is outside this bounded span. 5-122 is only said to be around the time of 5-121.
- 5-129's time is estimated by comparing fire distributions to 5-128; 5-130 inherits the same exposure/time.
- The CBS lobby/street sequence was not time stamped. 5-132/133/134 use clip relationships and fire/window comparisons; 5-136 and subsequent street views additionally use assumed travel and elapsed times.
- 5-144's time could not be determined directly and is estimated by comparison with 5-139.
- 5-145's approximate 4:10 p.m. depends on a proposed 15-minute difference and typical roughly 20-minute intense-burning assumption. Its caption says +/-5 minutes while prose says at least five minutes. 5-146's roughly 4:20 p.m. follows a ten-minute growth assumption, with a later conditional limit also depending on typical burning duration. These cannot independently test those same assumptions.
- 5-152 is crudely timed from sunlight/shadow, with at least ten minutes uncertainty.
- The reviewed passages give Rabanne frame times without establishing absolute calibration. 5-156's published +/-120-second interval extends beyond the report's stated collapse time and is retained without silent truncation. The narrower +/-15 seconds for 5-159 is retained with calibration unresolved here.
- The +/-3-second Didik times in 5-124 and 5-155 are source statements; their camera-clock calibration is not verified in this page span.

Approximate or at-least uncertainty is not converted to a hard interval. Explicit +/- intervals are expanded arithmetically only as bookkeeping of the source claim, not as probability distributions or validated timing bounds.

Some floor/column positions are assigned by comparison with other frames. The proposed floor-14 location for 5-153 is expressly conditional. The discussion of 5-144 allows reflection as an alternative to apparent floor-9 reddish color. Native appearance alone does not authenticate location, temperature or emitting material.

Statements about smoke direction, within-second changes, earlier unshown frames, interior pathways, flashover or wind are distinguished from a single static image. Physical 272 leaves unresolved whether the described lower plume came from fire on floors 5/6 or smoke transported there. Physical 286 explicitly limits the observational basis to exterior faces and notes weak information about fires remote from exterior walls.

The strongest objection to independent model validation is that some times and architectural assignments depend on the same fire/window comparisons the eventual test might use. Retain that dependency; compare only supported quantities, locations and times; do not claim independent temporal validation from that circular comparison.
