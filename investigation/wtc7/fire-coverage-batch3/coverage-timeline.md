## Ambiguities and falsifiers

The strongest objection to treating this timeline as independent fire-model validation is that several assigned times depend on interpreted fire/window changes. Agreement using those same clocks and source-derived ventilation may be circular. The timeline preserves that dependency rather than converting the timestamps into independent evidence.

The original source conflicts remain visible:5-109's caption has a p.m. interval while preceding prose gives an a.m. lower bound;5-111 literally prints12:28 a.m. despite midday surrounding prose. The source bounds for5-156 extend past the report's stated collapse reference and have not been silently clipped. A collapse reference in5-157/158 is not the exact time of either image.

A changed input hash, mismatched image placement, missing asset or wrong figure membership falsifies the affected join. Authenticated original timing or correction could change chronology; geometric registration could change a floor/window assignment; original footage/custody could revise a source-family or repeated-exposure grouping. Those changes would require a versioned revision without rewriting frozen image-only observations.

A complete model/input export with corresponding quantity, place and time could enable a comparison now missing. Until then, the highest supported result is an inspectable map of available report representations and their dependencies.
