While difficult to discern in Figure 5–130, a number of windows are open on the 7
th floor.  Most, if not 
all, of the windows between window 7-47A and 7-53D are open.  There is no indication of fire on the 
floor in this photograph.  Apparently, fire present in this area around 3:13 p.m. had stopped spreading and 
had died down by 3:41 p.m. 
There are no indications of fires on other floors in this time interval, between 2:57 p.m. and 3:42 p.m.  
With the exception of Figure 5–123, which indicates that fire was present on the north edge of the east 
face around 3:10 p.m., there are no images available during this time period that show the fire 
distributions on the east, west, or south face of WTC 7.
