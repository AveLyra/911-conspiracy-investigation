 WTC 7 Visual Evidence 
NIST NCSTAR 1-9, WTC Investigation  221
 
Figure 5–135.  Cropped photograph showing the east edge of the north face 
and an oblique view of the east face of WTC 7, taken from near the corner 
of West Broadway and Park Place. 
It was likely taken between 3:20 p.m. and 3:40 p.m.  The buildings burning in the 
background are WTC 5 (left) and WTC 6 (right), and the U.S. Post Office is on the 
left.  Floor numbers have been added and intensity levels have been adjusted.
