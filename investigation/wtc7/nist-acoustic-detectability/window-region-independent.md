## What this can and cannot test

An early, specified scenario predicts a visible effect in a specified region.
If that region was demonstrably resolved and retained intact glazing after
the hypothesized event, the observation could weigh against that scenario.
Source-reported intact regions are affirmative potential test information,
not merely an absence of data. No universal continuous-video, full-face or
before/after requirement is imposed for a sufficiently informative persistent
post-state.

Prior open/broken windows and mixed fire/glass map categories create a real
confounder: apparent agreement with a failure pattern may not discriminate its
cause, and existing damage can remove the opportunity to detect additional
glass loss. Conversely, reported intact regions matter only if the specified
prediction reaches them. Without a model-station/architectural-region join,
the source's intact bands cannot be declared a falsification of Figure3-1,
and the reported damaged bands cannot be declared confirmation of a blast.

The strongest favorable case for the published visual argument is now stronger
than generic facade metadata: there are identifiable source figures, explicit
time-specific derivatives, a window-condition code and prose reports of
intact as well as broken glass. The strongest remaining objection is the
non-equivalence between those agency-reported observations, the illustrative
model region and the exact later absence comparison's selection. A coarse
northeast match can be useful without pretending that this missing exact
crosswalk has already been supplied.

The3:57/4:00p.m. map labels do not convert the broad source intervals into
synchronized times or extend them to collapse. Nothing in this unit establishes
the lower-corner state during a hypothetical new later event, above-Floor25
initiation coverage, or the absence of all possible intervention mechanisms.
