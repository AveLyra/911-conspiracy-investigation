# What NIST's acoustic evidence can—and cannot—exclude

Research-only source audit, 2026-09-20 UTC. **The reviewed pages support some
conditional negative evidence against the modeled loud blast scenarios, not a
demonstrated exclusion of every blast or deliberate support-removal mechanism.**
The critical unverified step is from predicted sound under simplified conditions
to reliable detection at the actual, obstructed recording locations. This is a
narrowing of the acoustic inference, not a finding that an explosion occurred.

## The strongest support and strongest objection

NIST did not rely only on a visual animation. It identifies three soundtracks,
describes a waveform review and a travel-time allowance, and reports that one
recording picked up quiet street sounds without sound it attributed to WTC7
before global collapse. Its modeled source/propagation chain predicts intense
sound across several modeled cases. If that prediction applies to the actual
receiver paths, the absence of that sound is meaningful negative evidence.
A microphone need not be laboratory-calibrated before any qualitative absence
can count at all. [pp.289,355–357,706–709]

But NIST says the cameras were at street level, at least640m away, with numerous
buildings between them and WTC7. Its propagation calculation explicitly omits
adjacent buildings and qualifies the approximately130–140dB-at1km prediction
as applying to unobstructed paths. The later urban-reflection discussion is
qualitative and cites correspondence; the supplied empirical check concerns
close-range literature values, not those actual obstructed camera paths.
Thus a substantial source-to-receiver inference remains untested **in these
pages**. That is not proof that all attenuation would be sufficient to hide
the event—or that no additional analysis exists elsewhere. [pp.289,356–357,706]

## Important distinctions

- **Predicted pressure is not recorded digital loudness.** FigsD-17/18 show
  overpressure and sound-pressure-level axes. The reviewed pages do not give a
  recording-specific calibration or common weighting/averaging definition that
  makes the common-noise comparison table interchangeable with the predicted
  transient, dBFS or LUFS. No conversion or new pressure estimate is made here.
- **Sound character must match the tested prediction.** NIST describes a
  low-frequency rumble; merely looking for a sequence of sharp bangs would not
  test that full prediction. Conversely, a rumble alone does not distinguish an
  explosive event from collapsing structure. [pp.706–707]
- **Reported silence and witness silence are different observations.** An
  operator's lack of an audible reaction does not directly measure what that
  person heard. Three clips are not three controlled trials or an exhaustive
  witness census. Their availability, selection and processing need provenance.
- **Visibility limits apply to the window argument too.** p.357 acknowledges
  obstructed northeast views at collapse. Its earlier-time/upper-floor coverage
  does not establish continuous visibility of every relevant window. This audit
  does not retest the window model or independently classify breakage.
- **No absent-record finding.** Missing calibration, sensitivity or urban-path
  validation in this page scope does not establish that no such records exist,
  that they are presently withheld, or that anyone deliberately concealed them.

## Consequence for the investigation

Retain conditional negative weight against the **studied loud scenarios**;
withdraw any unqualified statement that these recordings or this calculation
establish universal explosive impossibility. The strongest counterargument is
that the predicted source is so conspicuous that substantial attenuation might
still leave an unmistakable signal. That deserves testing; this review neither
quantifies that margin nor demonstrates that urban obstruction eliminates it.

The [current causal synthesis](../causal-chain-synthesis/report.md) therefore
remains: NIST's sequence is a technically developed candidate, but this
investigation has not earned a strict probability ranking. This acoustic audit
does not independently establish fire-first, demolition-first or equal odds.
It cannot authenticate an initiating mechanism, preparations, actors or intent.
