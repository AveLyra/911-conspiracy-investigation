Strongest favorable reading: a traceable time-specific source chain and positive
intact-state assertions give NIST's visual argument more substance than a generic
claim that no damage was seen. A clearly resolved persistent effect can support
a qualitative test without an exact pane mask or uninterrupted footage.

Strongest contrary reading: the same source's earlier damage and limited coverage
can prevent the proposed comparison from distinguishing causes or later events.
An intact observation matters only where and when the specified model predicts
failure; an existing opening does not identify why it opened. This window test
concerns the published pressure-driven visible-effect prediction, not every
thermal, mechanical or unspecified intervention. No physical probability or
innocence/guilt conclusion is computed.
