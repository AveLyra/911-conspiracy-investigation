While these designations are qualitative in nature, in practice, consistent ratings were provided by a
number of individuals who participated in the generation of the data sheets.

The third worksheet described the condition of windows and required fairly close-up imagery.

    •   If the window glass was broken out, it was assigned a value of “0.”

    •   If the glass was intact, it was assigned a value of “1.”

    •   If a given window could not be observed, it was assigned a value of “9.”
