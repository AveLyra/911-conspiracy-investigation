## Material checks

- **Darkness, transparency and reflection:** The protocol correctly refuses
  to infer missing glass from darkness, visible flame, brightness or absent
  reflection alone. Transparent intact glass can reveal an interior; reflections
  can come from materials other than glazing. Glass-like and opening-like are
  appearance descriptions, not intact/broken physical-state certifications.
  A candidate irregular edge plus interior detail can justify recording the
  feature, but not calling it verified surviving glass while shade, facade
  member or interior material remains plausible.
- **Mixed-state semantics:** A positive-looking patch does not establish a
  whole band's state. The requirement to retain mixed appearances, smoke,
  source-label occlusion and unresolved apertures prevents majority voting or
  an all-intact/all-open label. Uncertain mapping and uncertain material/state
  are distinct uncertainties and should both remain in each applicable row.
- **Eight-target completeness:** The fixed selection gives three targets for
  each of the first two images and two for the third. Out-of-frame, unmappable,
  obscured and unresolved targets still receive records. The closer Floor 8
  views cannot supply missing Floor 12 detail; the Floor 11 context is not an
  independently validated negative control. No post-view target replacement,
  new region or advantageous floor selection is allowed.
- **Selection and transfer:** The chosen near-corner areas use a recovered
  qualitative orientation, not an exact predicted window mask. The Floor
  12-like illustration and shared clock estimates remain conditional. This
  fixed preparation does not authorize a new cross-floor model test. That is
  not a universal prohibition: a later transfer could be justified explicitly
  by layout, predicted effect, prior glazing state and timing under a new
  declared comparison. Three related source-processed images are not three
  independent historical witnesses.
- **Source-assisted mapping:** Keeping report labels is proper preservation,
  but those labels cannot independently validate the floor/face assignment.
  Caption/map assertions about glass must not supply the appearance finding.
  The human packet must distinguish checking the proposed locator from agreeing
  with a physical state or collapse explanation.
- **Human and synthetic boundaries:** The six disclosed text cases test rule
  application, not visual accuracy, source authenticity or physical glazing
  detection. Passing them and opening the images do not satisfy synthetic
  ground-truth or actual-human checks for consequential automated measurement.
  This unit authorizes no such measurement. A mapping spot-check alone also
  cannot turn these early stills into a collapse-time model test.
