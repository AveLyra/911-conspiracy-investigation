## Floor, prior-state and time qualifications

The most accurate present description is **an illustrated highly partitioned,
Floor12-like layout**, not a verified reconstruction of the exact historical
Floor12 response inputs. The shared11th/12th fire-plan statement is relevant
public layout evidence, but fire-model identity does not prove response-model
identity. A comparison on another floor requires an explicit justification of
layout, predicted effect and glazing state; intact glass elsewhere cannot be
silently substituted for the illustrated floor context.

The [previous region/time crosswalk](window-region-crosswalk-2026-09-28.md)
recovers map5-172/image5-141 and map5-173/image5-142 citations. It also records
NIST's reports of intact glass and prior openings, including open areas in the
mapped Floor12 band. Geometry does not turn an already-open aperture into a
test of additional loss, identify why it opened or independently verify an
agency map class. No new historical window state was labeled here.

The early candidate exposure intervals remain approximately4p.m., not collapse
time. A later event cannot be tested with an earlier photograph. The summary's
above-Floor25-at-initiation assertion is a separate observation domain. The
published absence argument's exact selected-image ledger is still unrecovered
in this route. None of these limits is evidence that the missing material was
withheld, destroyed or falsified.

Neither complete-face coverage, uninterrupted footage, before-and-after images
nor an exact universal pane mask is required for every useful qualitative test.
A resolved persistent intact state can be informative where a specified model
predicts loss. But place, floor/layout, time and the meaning of the predicted
effect must be matched, and obscured/partly present/open/intact states must not
be collapsed into one category.
