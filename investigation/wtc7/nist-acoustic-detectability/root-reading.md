## Provisional evidential assessment before comparison

Strongest positive support: NIST identifies real candidate recordings, reports
quiet background sounds in one, allows for travel time, distinguishes different
listener responses, and carries an explicit physical source/propagation model
to a prediction of conspicuous sound. If the modeled event and real receiver
paths would indeed produce a conspicuous signal, its non-observation would
count against that event. It is wrong to dismiss this merely as animation or
to say that recording calibration is logically required for any qualitative
negative evidence.

Strongest limitation: the calculation explicitly assumes unobstructed paths
and omits adjacent-building effects, whereas the described microphones were
behind numerous buildings. Qualitative correspondence-backed claims do not
themselves quantify that gap. Close-range comparison is not receiver-path
validation. No demonstrated full source→urban path→microphone→processing→
background/detector chain or false-negative bound is supplied in these pages.
Consequently, some conditional qualitative negative weight is defensible;
categorical exclusion of every blast or every deliberate support-removal
mechanism is not established by this acoustic argument. Missing here is not
proof that missing records do not exist elsewhere or were concealed.

Early audible activity reported for the third camera is not an affirmative
explosion finding. Structural impacts, collapse and background activity remain
competing source explanations. Also the source predicts low-frequency rumble,
so an expectation of only isolated sharp bangs would be a mismatched test.
Neither waveform shape nor ear-level adjective alone determines cause.

No numerical likelihood, equal-odds claim, reversal to demolition-first, or
restored fire-first ordering follows. The appropriate change is narrower
weight for claims of universal acoustic exclusion. An original recording with
traceable timing/processing, source-specific receiver-path evidence, and a
validated sensitivity test could materially strengthen or weaken this bridge.
The two cited correspondence records are concrete, bounded documentary leads.
