## Geometry is recoverable at face/corner scale

Figure 5-7 and its printed 98 prose provide the decisive orientation anchors.
The long north side and shorter south side form the trapezoidal perimeter.
The rectangular core is offset to the west, with additional structure between
its east side and the exterior. The text says perimeter numbering starts at
the northwest corner and runs counterclockwise. In the displayed plan,
architectural Column 1 is at upper left, Column 42 at upper right, Column 28
at lower right and Column 15 at lower left. These are architectural labels,
not the numbered measurement stations in the response diagram.

Thus displayed top is building north, right is east, bottom is south and left
is west. This follows from the text and labeled asymmetric geometry, not a
presumption that north is always page-up. The Figure 2-3 plan on printed 14
agrees in footprint, offset core and architectural corner labels. Its caption
says Floors 8-45; its prose qualifies typical framing to Floors 8-20 and24-45.
The present Floor12 context lies in that stated range; no uniformity across
every intervening floor is inferred from the broad caption.

Figure 3-7 on printed 56 supplies a schematic specifically named Floor12.
Its trapezoid, offset elevator/core arrangement, perimeter rooms and interior
corridors support the same orientation. The text describes similar11th/12th
floor layouts and use of one plan for fire simulation, and attributes the plan
to material supplied by the SEC. This is a published derivative, not inspection
of the underlying SEC drawings or authentication of the exact as-built state.
The text also describes a southwest convenience stair and west-side library;
these qualitative asymmetries are consistent with the displayed orientation.

NCSTAR1A Figure3-1 shows the same general trapezoid/offset-core/partitioned
arrangement. In conjunction with its explicit northeast description, the
response's upper-right area is therefore the northeast corner, not a reflected
northwest region or an arbitrary right-hand elevation. This is a supported
qualitative model-output/architecture join, not proof that the schematic and
response model share identical meshes, wall properties or source versions.
The source illustrates a highly partitioned tenant floor, with Floor12 as an
example; it is not evidence that the hypothetical event happened on Floor12.

## What changes and what does not

The coarse geometry gap is resolved by public material. Requiring native model
files or an exact universal pane mask merely to say which ends of the two
facades are relevant would now be an unnecessary obstacle. A clearly resolved
observation from the appropriate predicted region could inform a qualitative
test without every pane being registered or both complete faces being visible.

However, the exact station/window correspondence and actual modeled glazing
initial conditions remain unrecovered here. Similar-looking partition plans
are not a native-model version check. The existing early map/image crosswalk
retains already-open Floor12 windows and uncertain exposure times. No new
image review established whether particular predicted windows were intact,+open, partly present or unobservable. Glazing on another floor cannot be
silently substituted, and early observations cannot test a new event at collapse.

Strongest competing reading: apparent plan similarity alone could be misleading
if the model were reflected or substantially different in layout. The explicit
northeast prose, perimeter/corner labels and asymmetric core placement jointly
support the coarse orientation despite that objection. They do not resolve
fine station registration or physical model fidelity. Evidence of a different
model coordinate convention or floor/layout would require revisiting this join.

Printed97 provides wind/photograph context, not the required architectural
orientation proof. Its photograph is explicitly described as cropped/enhanced;
no wind, smoke or damage measurement is extracted here. Printed56's surrounding
text includes occupancy/fire-material and prior south-window-damage assertions;
these are not new northeast glazing observations and are not used as such.

Next action should not repeat the geometry locator. The consequential residual
is the matched observable: a specified predicted window region and its actual
initial/post-event state at an applicable time, retaining existing openings.
Separately declared native-image review can assess resolvability under its
human/method controls; above-Floor25 collapse-time coverage is a distinct route.
No source reading clears those gates or changes a WTC7 cause ordering.
