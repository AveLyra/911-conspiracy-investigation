## Observation contract

For each full-image view save figure/asset/hash, display success, prior exposure,
and each fixed target's:

- locator: architectural/visible landmarks, optionally an approximate native
  pixel rectangle; origin upper-left, half-open bounds if used. A locator is
  not segmentation, measured area or pixel-accurate geometric registration.
- mapping: supported candidate, uncertain, or not covered; explain without
  borrowing a glazing state from the source caption/map.
- detail/obstruction: what can actually be resolved, including smoke, viewing
  angle, scale, blur, compression, foreground or source-added marks.
- appearance evidence: specific visible cues and plausible alternatives.
  Multiple appearances can coexist within a region; do not force a mixed band
  into one all-intact/all-open category. A glass-like reflection/surface supports
  apparent presence only, not proof of an intact assembly. A partial-glass claim
  needs a discernible candidate surviving surface/edge and a plausible missing
  portion; explicitly retain shadows, facade members or interior material as
  alternatives. An opening-like claim needs positive spatial/interior/frame cues;
  darkness, visible flame, brightness or absence of a reflection alone is
  insufficient. Transparency does not necessarily mean glass is missing.
- provisional conclusion: the narrowest supported appearance, or unresolved;
  any apparently discriminating example must identify its specific locator and
  the cue that would survive the strongest alternative explanation.
- human check: what the reviewer needs to verify or correct about location,
  surface/edge identity and uncertainty, without asking them to endorse a cause.

Do not count panes, compute fractions/areas, infer temperatures, infer pressure,
label the whole floor, derive failure times, fit a classifier or calculate
causal likelihood. No observation is accepted as a historical model test yet.
