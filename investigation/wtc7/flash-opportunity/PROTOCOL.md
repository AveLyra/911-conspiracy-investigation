## Per-frame schema

Record source index and exact displayed path, successful/failed presentation,
then the following fields with a short physical description:

1. **Roofline/raised rooftop region:** V = substantially visible outline;
   P = partly visible/partly masked; H = hidden by foreground/smoke;
   O = outside frame; U = cannot judge. V does not mean every rooftop surface
   or interior is visible. If mixed, P with named portions.
2. **Exposed upper exterior wall:** same codes, distinguishing a visible face
   from the entire perimeter, interior or opposite façade. Visibility does
   not certify brightness sensitivity or resolve structural members.
3. **Ground/base interface:** same codes, specifically the target building's
   ground-contact region, not a foreground roof. If hidden and outside-frame
   cannot be distinguished, U with both possibilities, not an invented base.
4. **Obstructions and spatial opportunities:** name foreground structures,
   smoke/cloud or framing limits; separately name any clearly exposed exterior
   surface or adjacent sky where a sufficiently conspicuous outward luminous
   event could in principle appear. Do not assign a detection probability.
5. **Brightness confounders:** describe conspicuous bright areas if present
   and plausible sky/cloud/reflection/edge ambiguity. White-looking pixels
   are not a measured clipping statistic; no intensity/area calculation.
6. **Temporal/detection ceiling:** one still does not show transience. No
   flash present/absent classification, charge identification, original shutter
   timing, footage-wide nondetection or specific internal source visibility
   follows from this pass. Preserve any unexpected appearance as descriptive
   and a separate follow-up candidate, without silent extra-frame inspection.

After both freezes, compare categorical labels and explanations, preserving
both. No forced consensus, coordinate averaging, onset fit or historical
automated measurement. Synthetic detector controls and actual human checks
remain prerequisites for any later consequential automated flash measurement.
