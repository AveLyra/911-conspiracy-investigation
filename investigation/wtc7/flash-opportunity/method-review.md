## Source and schema cautions to retain

1. The declared Camera2 inputs are full native luminance images from
   `camera2-penthouse-event/run01`, not the Stage C roof crop or a reduced
   overview. Stage B defines 640x480 Y-plane derivatives and exact native
   PTS/index joins. Source integrity does not establish original shutter
   timing, independent exposures or uninterrupted historical acquisition.
2. Camera3's `camera3-late-reannotation/views01` images are native 720x480
   grayscale derivatives of the held WMV. Its `validation.md` reports **three
   corruption-warning mentions** despite matching prior decoded hashes.
   Preserve that diagnostic status in the new source check. Do not call the
   source clean, assume a warning is irrelevant to the selected images, or
   infer historical exposure fidelity merely from a reproducible decode.
3. These derivatives do not retain full color information. A light area may
   remain visually describable, but color, spectral interpretation, saturation
   and calibrated photometry are not recovered by viewing it.
4. V/P/H/O/U describe the named region in that frame. They are not measured
   exposed-area fractions, equal-area categories across cameras, or whole-
   building visibility scores. Reasons must name mixed visible and masked
   portions, especially where roof outline and rooftop surface differ.
5. The base means the target building's ground-contact region. A foreground
   roof, apparent screen bottom or dust front must not substitute for it.
   Uncertain hidden-versus-out-of-frame classification stays U.
6. In the brightness field, distinguish the visible description from proposed
   explanations such as reflection, sky, smoke, edge motion or processing.
   A conspicuous white-looking patch is not measured clipping or a transient.
7. Record positive spatial opportunity as well as blind spots. A hidden
   internal source might illuminate an exposed surface/sky; a visible surface
   does not guarantee recording of an internal event. Neither one-sided
   assertion is warranted without a specified emission/visibility model.
