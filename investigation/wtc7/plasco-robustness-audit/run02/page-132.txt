(h) The secondary columns form the critical load-carrying components of the tower after 
the core columns and primary beams. The robustness analysis involved applying 
thermal load to the edge compartments over multiple floors. The analysis results did not 
predict collapse-causing conditions when the fire load was limited to a single floor. 
However, when the thermal load was applied to multiple floors, the results indicated the 
potential buckling of secondary main columns due to loss of lateral support. In multiple-
floor fires affecting the edge compartments, the load shed caused by the secondary main 
columns may result in a temporary stable condition if adjacent columns and edge beams 
remain stable under additional compressive forces.
