In the later stages of the fire, the collapse of the floors near the Southeastern (SW) corner 
was initiated due to the failure of at least one primary beam. Primary beams in the Plasco tower 
are the key components of the primary load-carrying system. The Plasco tower investigation 
report was published by Shakib et al. (2020) explains that the failure of primary beams in the 
SW area caused a large portion of floor failure. The dynamic effects generated by the falling 
floors caused the collapse of a few lower floors, thereby pushing the structure into relatively 
unstable conditions. The failure of any single structural member did not trigger the collapse of 
the Plasco tower . Instead, the collapse incident should be seen as a combination of local 
pancake-style collapses that lead to a condition favourable towards a disproportiona te style 
collapse.  
 
3.2.1. Classification of the collapse 
WTC tower collapses are a good example of pancake-type collapses (NIST (2005)). 
The loss of vertical load-bearing capacity (limited to a few stories) induced the pancake -style 
collapse. The upper damaged part of the structure moved downwards and accumulated kinetic 
energy. The subsequent collision with the lower part of the structure caused large dynamic 
forces beyond the capacity—this chain of events led to the ultimate collapse of the WTC towers.
