Pancake type 
(WTC collapse) 
a. Initial failure of vertical load-bearing elements 
b. Separation and fall 
c. Transformation of potential energy into kinetic 
energy 
d. Impact loading  
e. Vertical collapse propagation  
Initial collapse of floors in the NW corner 
of the 11th and 12th floors (Shakib et al. 
(2020)).  
Zipper type 
(Tacoma Narrows 
Bridge) 
a. Initial failure of one or two key elements 
b. Redistribution of forces 
c. Impulsive loading due to the suddenness 
d. Collapse progression in a direction transverse to 
the principal forces in failing elements 
It is not relevant as this type of failure 
occurs in thin structural members under 
tensile forces.  
Domino type a. Initial failure of one element 
b. Overturning effect and rigid body motion 
c. Like the pancake-type collapse, but the impacts 
occur at an angle
