3.5. Robustness analysis of the Plasco tower 
3.5.1. Core columns 
In the first study, the core column on the SE (Southeast) corner of the tower was chosen 
for the investigation. Figure 3.7 shows the location of all core and perimeter columns of a single 
floor; for clarity the secondary truss beams, Vierendeel beams, and slab members are hidden. 
The SE core column was subjected to heating until it reached a point of failure in order to 
investigate the impact of the failed column on the surrounding columns caused by load 
shedding.
