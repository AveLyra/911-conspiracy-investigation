## Competing explanation reassessment

The two argument reviews use the same fourteen complete documents under the
[comparison scope](COMPARISON-SCOPE.md) and
[fixed input hashes](comparison-inputs.sha256). The
[fire argument](fire-case-review.md) and
[intervention argument](intervention-case-review.md) were each saved and hashed
before either author read the other's new argument. Root then read both in
full. Shared source material and prior involvement prevent treating agreement
as independent corroboration.

**The evidence supports a partial comparison, not a robust overall cause
ranking.** Fire has more affirmative case evidence as an initiating stressor
than any specified intervention in this packet. Observed fires, empirical
thermal/component behavior and a developed propagation account give the fire
case real weight. They are not dismissed simply because full historical
reproduction is unavailable. However, they still leave the decisive bridge
from actual heating to rapid, widespread loss of surviving capacity uncertain.
Real fire is also compatible with an intervention occurring during that fire;
its presence alone has little power to distinguish those two histories.

Deliberate support removal is physically compatible with rapid coordinated
descent. The motion and remaining load paths give a serious reason to test it,
not a reason to assume it. Its weakest bridge is a constrained, historically
executed support-loss mechanism with discriminating predictions. Neither UAF's
prescribed removal timing nor adding conveniently quiet, precisely timed
failures supplies that evidence. Identifying a responsible person is not a
prerequisite for a physical conclusion; the missing link here is mechanism
specificity and case evidence, not an actor's name.

There is a narrower directional result. The reviewed acoustic record gives
conditional negative weight against the **studied conspicuous-blast scenarios**.
That favors alternatives without those predicted signals, including fire,
relative to those particular scenarios. It does not uniquely select fire or
exclude all intervention. The fire reviewer expressly favors fire in that
limited comparison; the intervention reviewer agrees on adverse acoustic
weight but declines a strict overall ordering. Root accepts the conditional
comparison, not a recording-calibrated exclusion. Urban paths, processing,
contrary reported reactions and the uncompleted listened-event table remain.

The strongest objection to declining an overall rank is that perfect evidence
is unnecessary: the fire account has observed initiating stress and developed
mechanics, while intervention adds an unobserved operation. That can reasonably
support a weak working fire preference if one gives the published propagation
and parsimony substantial weight. The limitation is not a missing-file veto.
The disputed propagation transition and the poor detection bounds for quiet
methods are precisely where that comparative weight turns. This packet does
not establish those weights or a robust mechanism-specific discriminator.
Accordingly, root does not convert that conditional preference into an earned
overall ranking, and equally does not award intervention first place from
unresolved fire-model weaknesses. This is not equal probability or innocence
versus guilt.

NIST's exact sequence remains separately testable and vulnerable: reported
damaged-case west-penthouse timing differs from the observation column;
prescribed ventilation, shifted histories, stable outcomes and surviving
restraints matter. These are more specific criticisms than “the model is
only animation.” Other fire pathways do not inherit NIST's claimed successes
while escaping its failures. Thermal/chemical, mechanical and mixed
intervention likewise need their own predictions; absence of a loud-blast
signature cannot grant them unlimited explanatory freedom.

**What changed:** the one-pixel comparator result is now numerical, native
fire coverage is larger, conditional source chronology is sharper, our own
window-target ambiguity is corrected, and the Plasco model contains genuine
reported heat-driven local failure as well as stable responses. Some broad
skeptical claims therefore weaken while specific input and residual-support
questions sharpen. **What did not change:** no complete historical causal
chain or executed intervention is demonstrated, and no robust strict overall
ordering is earned. Neither the newly located software export nor the number
of audits changes those judgments. The review is not a claim that all Luna
contributions have been located: it retains the twelve-artifact scientific
audit and separately scoped three-turn/eight-message chat audit.

The most consequential change would come from fixed, authentic stable/failing
state comparisons that preserve surviving load paths and account for timing,
forces and energy, or from authenticated mechanism-specific pre-collapse
physical/operational evidence. Robust failure under justified states would
strengthen the tested fire pathway; robust arrest or incompatible sequencing
would weaken it. Neither alone proves every fire pathway false or intervention
true. Source and engineering prerequisites are specified below, not replaced
by another acceleration fit or an actor search.
