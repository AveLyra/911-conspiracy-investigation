## Conclusion

**Deliberate support removal remains a serious physical alternative, but this packet does not justify making it the most probable explanation.** Its strongest
case concerns the rapid coordinated loss of effective support that must be
explained, coupled with unresolved residual-load-path and timing challenges to
NIST's particular sequence. Its weakest bridge is turning those constraints
into affirmative evidence of an executed intervention, rather than an unstable
gravity-driven response following another initiating process.

This is not a rule that an actor must be identified first or every model must be perfectly reproduced. Distinctive, well-supported physical predictions
could favor intervention without an identified perpetrator. Here the positive
motion constraints are insufficiently mechanism-specific, while the proposed
intervention has substantially more freedom than a fixed causal prediction.
No equal-odds claim, numerical probability or innocence/guilt finding follows.

## The strongest supported argument

1. **Rapid coordinated descent is real evidence, not an artifact to explain
   away by hypothetical uncertainty.** The packet preserves gravity-scale
   roofline curvature, two annotation arms around9.60/9.93 source-assigned
   m/s² and approximately common apparent motion over twenty joint samples.
   A slow serial failure picture is inadequate unless its hidden damage and
   propagation actually produce that visible response. Deliberate removal is
   physically compatible with rapid effective support loss.
2. **Connectivity is a substantive burden on the initiating mechanism.**
   Residual bearing, north-side restraints and Floor14 attachments cannot be
   assumed absent because bolts or a column failed. Plasco's retained cold
   paths and stable redistribution illustrate the point; NIST's reported
   3.5-hour stable state makes the failure-producing transition a specific
   sensitivity question. Intervention could in principle supply additional
   loss of capacity, but “could” is not evidence it supplied it here.
3. **NIST's claimed correspondence has actual limits, not only inaccessible
   files.** The published damaged-case west-penthouse passage is6.9/7.3s
   against9.3s in its observation column; later deformation is expressly
   qualified. Those mismatches deserve a same-feature test. Overlong simulated
   burning, substituted floor histories and prescribed ventilation also
   prevent treating the reconstruction as uniquely determined by photographs.
4. **Several common exclusions of intervention overreach.** Interior-first
   motion is not unique to fire. The acoustic study tests specified loud
   scenarios, not every possible means; actual obstructed receiver paths lack
   a demonstrated detection margin in the reviewed material. Genuine handling
   misconduct defeats a blanket claim of flawless preservation. None of these
   points supplies the missing mechanism-specific observation or suppression
   chain, but they justify continued discriminating investigation.

The strongest version therefore does **not** need “uniform freefall of every part into its own footprint,” “only mild fires,” “all supports disappeared
instantaneously,” or “every failure in NIST was imposed.” Those stronger
premises are unsupported or conflict with the packet. Near-g motion of a
defined body's COM would constrain net non-gravitational force; an arbitrary
roof point is not automatically that COM. Missing COM bounds cannot erase the
observed curvature, and invented hidden motion cannot rescue a failing model.

## Reassessment and qualitative ordering

The October additions **weaken**, rather than strengthen, the sweeping case that fire was practically incapable of relevant local failure: new Plasco
reading distinguishes heat-generated buckling from deletion; thermal/physical
test support is substantive. Stable cases simultaneously strengthen the demand
to demonstrate propagation through surviving capacity. Additional fire images
weaken “all mild,” not prove sufficient steel heating. R1's human result concerns
a demolition comparator, not a WTC7 causal classifier.

CBS counters sharpen a conditional chronology conflict, but an edited master can reconcile it; no original filming clock is established. The8-42B/C
correction prevents our own mistaken window substitution and leaves the claimed
glass loss unresolved. Neither is evidence of model fabrication or intervention.
These changes justify more precise, sometimes stronger challenges; they do
not cumulatively become independent evidence of a deliberate operation.

**A partial assessment is warranted; a robust total cause ordering is not.** Fire has more affirmative evidence as an initiating stressor than any particular
intervention in this packet. That asymmetry gives the fire case real weight;
it is not neutralized by saying that its whole chain is unverified. Specified
conspicuous blast scenarios also face an adverse acoustic constraint. But
the rapid-motion fit is not uniquely diagnostic, actual residual-capacity
transitions remain consequentially uncertain, and quiet/mixed interventions
lack constrained historical predictions. I cannot turn these unlike evidential
advantages into an earned intervention-first **or strict overall fire-first**
ordering. A fire preference based on confidence in disputed model transfer
would be conditional on that judgment, not an independently established result.
This conclusion follows from the discriminating power and uncertainty of the present evidence, not a requirement for perfect replication.

explicitly retaining plausible residual restraints, with stable controls and
force/energy checks. That would answer its strongest mechanical objection;
an actor search would not be necessary. Mechanism-specific acoustic/material
counterevidence could separately weaken a specified intervention. Conversely,
authenticated pre-collapse modification/material evidence or robust arrest
under justified states would change the comparison; the latter rejects that tested route, not every fire mechanism, and does not prove intervention by elimination.
