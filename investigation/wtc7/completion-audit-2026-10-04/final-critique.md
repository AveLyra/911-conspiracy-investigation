## Adversarial assessment of the causal comparison

**1. The refusal of a robust total ranking is a defensible qualified judgment, not a derived theorem.** The report directly states the strongest objection: observed fire, developed physics and an additional unobserved intervention can reasonably support a weak working fire preference. It does not demand full reproduction or numerical likelihoods before *any* historical inference. It nevertheless declines a robust total ordering because the sufficiency of the disputed propagation bridge and the comparative predictions remain inadequately constrained in this packet. That position is permissible; it must not later be paraphrased as “nothing favors fire,” equal odds, or proof that a weak fire preference is irrational.

The strongest remaining objection is still substantive: one can assign more weight to the observed stressor and less to a flexible, unobserved quiet operation without first measuring that operation's detectability. Incomplete detection opportunity reduces the force of nondetection; it supplies no affirmative support for quiet intervention. The integrated report preserves this asymmetry, explicitly forbids unlimited explanatory freedom, and identifies the conditional judgment required for a working preference. I do not require it to adopt that preference, nor treat non-ranking as compelled by physics.

**2. Acoustic directionality is correctly narrow.** The fire argument favors fire over the studied conspicuous-blast scenarios. The integrated report correctly states the broader inference: negative evidence against those predictions favors *alternatives without those signals*, including but not uniquely fire. Imperfect calibration is not zero weight; obstruction, processing and contrary reported reactions limit rather than invert the negative weight. Lack of a listened-event table is preserved without pretending that earlier source-reported acoustic observations cease to exist. No inference excluding all explosive, thermal, mechanical or mixed methods follows.

**3. Fire appearance does not establish thermal severity.** The frozen fire argument's “only uniformly mild heat is visible” and the intervention argument's “weaken ‘all mild’” are looser than a measured proposition. Heat intensity/member exposure was not measured by these images. Read them only as challenges to an unsupported sweeping characterization, not proof of a thermally severe history. The integrated report's narrower wording—coverage does not support calling all fires faint/mild, followed immediately by the temperature/area/history limits—is acceptable. Do not propagate the frozen shorthand into an asserted thermal bound. This is a qualification of the argument wording, not a demand to revise preserved freezes.

**4. The motion burden is retained, not dissolved by uncertainty.** Positive gravity-scale curvature, common apparent motion and surviving-load-path questions remain. The body/COM counterexamples show insufficiency of reduced observations, not mechanically feasible alternative WTC7 histories. The final report does not assert that large projection errors, hidden motion or compensating forces actually occurred.
