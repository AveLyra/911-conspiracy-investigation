## Common questions

Each reviewer will develop the strongest evidence-supported version of its
assigned explanation and the strongest objection to it, then address:

- Physical compatibility with observed motion versus overall explanatory weight.
- Initiation, local support loss, propagation and motion as separate links;
  intent/actor identification is separate and is not required merely to favor
  a physical mechanism if discriminating evidence supports it.
- Positive evidence, adverse results, model-dependent support and unavailable
  information. Do not count an unavailable result as an unfavorable result or
  evidence copies as corroboration.
- What changed since September 24 and which earlier claim should be withdrawn,
  strengthened or left conditional. Do not inherit the previous ranking by default.
- Whether a qualitative ordering is defensible despite uncertainty. Do not
  demand perfect information or full reproduction as an automatic prerequisite
  for historical inference; explain any remaining asymmetry in actual evidence.
  Conversely, no hypothesis gains unlimited adjustable failures or an invented
  likelihood merely because it can accommodate the observations.
- Exact evidence that would weaken the assigned explanation, and the highest-
  value remaining discriminator with its current prerequisites.

Keep NIST's exact pathway separate from other fire pathways. Keep conventional
loud explosive, thermal/chemical, mechanical and mixed intervention hypotheses
distinct where their predictions actually differ. Do not supply operational
device designs or invent a conveniently undetectable historical mechanism.
Allow partial orderings, disagreement and non-identifiability without calling
them equal probabilities. No numerical odds are authorized by this packet.

## Review procedure

The fire-case and intervention-case reviewers save and hash their concise
findings before seeing the other's new argument. Root then compares both and
records disagreements rather than averaging them away. A third reviewer
checks the resulting synthesis against the shared packet and charter exits.
Source, model and historical validation remain distinct; no AI review supplies
a human gate or professional engineering acceptance.
