# Strongest fire case on the fixed October 4 packet

2026-10-04. Research-only review of the fourteen files in [comparison-inputs.sha256](comparison-inputs.sha256), under the [common scope](COMPARISON-SCOPE.md).
I authored the structural/comparator component and earlier audits; no blindness,

No opposing October 4 argument was read before this review was saved and hashed.

## Judgment

**Fire-triggered progressive collapse remains an affirmatively supported causal
candidate, not merely a possibility rescued by missing information.** The
strongest directional comparison favors it over the *studied conspicuous-blast
scenarios*, conditional on their predicted signals reaching the recordings.
The packet does not establish a robust overall fire-over-intervention ordering,
especially against thermal/chemical, mechanical or mixed intervention classes.
That is a partial comparison, not equal odds or a demand for perfect replication.
NIST's more developed fire account is not thereby established historically superior.

## The strongest affirmative chain—and where each link stops

1. **Initiating stress is observed.** The media component and fire-coverage
   report preserve resolved flame-like regions and prolonged, changing fire
   appearances. They undermine the premise that only uniformly mild heat is
   visible. They do not establish collapse-producing steel temperatures,
   independently calibrate every image's time, or measure interior heat from
   brightness. Real fires need not be assumed into existence to begin the case.
2. **Thermal loading can alter real load paths.** The structural component
   identifies material-specific thermal/mechanical treatment, connection and
   floor tests, organized temperature assignments, and physically motivated
   expansion, capacity loss and instability mechanisms—not just an animation.
   Empirical component behavior supports the mechanism class; transfer to these
   installed members/conditions remains an inference. Melting is not required.
3. **There is a developed propagation account.** NIST connects local floor and
   restraint damage to C79 instability, interior propagation and perimeter
   destabilization. Its global calculation starts from calculated damage and
   temperatures; it is not one uninterrupted, independently verified simulation
   from photographed fire to rubble. The reviewed sources do not establish that
   every subsequent failure was prescribed. Its reported stable versus failing
   starting states are meaningful sensitivity evidence, not proof of inevitability.
4. **Broad sequencing is compatible.** East-penthouse/interior-related changes
   precede the late exterior descent in the reviewed accounts. The source also
   describes earlier exterior changes; an entirely untouched shell until one
   instant is not established. Rapid instability need not appear as leisurely
   column-by-column tilt. That counters alleged impossibility; it does not prove this chain.
5. **Some competing predictions face negative evidence.** The acoustic report
   preserves reported nondetection of the modeled conspicuous blast signal and
   useful quiet-background sensitivity clues. The documentary component supplies
   ordinary emergency-warning and maintenance context, but no authenticated
   intervention-to-support-loss chain within its declared coverage. Neither
   microphone calibration nor identifying a perpetrator is an automatic
   prerequisite to drawing any physical inference.

This cumulative case is not five independent experiments: model development,
fire reconstruction and motion evaluation share NIST sources; two readers are
not two cameras. Fire's presence is stronger against “no significant fire” than
against intervention during an acknowledged existing fire.

## The fire case's weakest bridge and strongest counterevidence

**The weakest bridge is sufficient, correctly timed loss of the actual surviving
load paths—not whether heated steel can weaken in general.** The packet leaves
historical heating, residual restraint/capacity, effective material definitions,
state transfer and timed propagation materially unconstrained. C79's retained
seats/attachments matter: failed bolts are not automatically absent bearing or
restraint; no shared node does not establish no contact. Unknown forces cannot
be set to zero, nor does a surviving seat establish adequate remaining restraint
without its geometry, stiffness and loading.

Gravity-scale roof-point curvature and approximately common apparent motion
survive the audits and constrain the fire account. A correct fixed-body COM
near-g result would tightly constrain net non-gravity force; roof-point data do
not identify every hidden support or the initiator. The body/COM study's
alternatives are not demonstrated WTC7 histories. Hidden motion cannot freely
cancel excessive resistance without geometric, mechanical and image support.

There is adverse evidence, not only missing records. NIST's damaged-case west-
penthouse passage values precede its observation column by 2.0/2.4 seconds;
undamaged alternatives bracket it from the other side, not as one accurate run.
View/event definitions affect a formal rejection test but do not erase that
reported discrepancy. UAF's imposed support-loss scenario reportedly fits a
corner trajectory and fails to reproduce the kink; it is a useful mechanical
comparator, not independent proof that fire cannot produce the required state.

Severe-fire survival and retained load paths oppose inevitability. Plasco Chapter 3
adds reported heat-generated local buckling/redistribution without a described
initial deletion schedule, but also stable cases, omitted connection failures and
idealized heating. Its global language outruns some local responses; Chapter 7's
thermal-to-removal hybrid does not close the gap. Plasco is neither a matched
WTC7 control nor a frequency estimate. Rarity does not quantify this building's
collapse probability under its actual conditions.

## What ordering is justified?

| Comparison | Judgment from this packet |
| --- | --- |
| NIST's exact pathway versus other fire pathways | NIST is more specified and therefore more testable here, not shown more probable. Other fire accounts cannot inherit its successes while evading its failures. |
| Fire versus the studied loud explosive scenarios | A limited fire-favoring comparison is defensible from conditional acoustic negative evidence plus an observed initiating stress. It is not an established recording-specific exclusion: urban transfer, processing and the uncompleted listened-event table matter. |
| Fire versus thermal/chemical or mechanical intervention | No robust ordering established. The acoustic argument does not transfer automatically; neither class has a demonstrated case-specific initiating mechanism in this packet. Their terminal-motion compatibility is not proof of preparation or efficacy. |
| Fire versus mixed causes | Unresolved unless the added intervention makes constrained, discriminating predictions. Adding an unconstrained extra cause cannot claim a superior explanation merely by accommodating everything. |

A weak working preference for fire can reasonably be expressed by someone who
weights the developed thermal chain and penalizes an unobserved intervention.
My objection to calling that an established evidentiary ranking is specific:
the penalty has no well-bounded detection basis
for quieter methods here, while the fire chain's decisive propagation bridge
also remains assumption-dependent. I therefore retain the narrower partial
ordering above. This is not a presumption of institutional innocence, an actor
identification requirement, or a claim that missing one file defeats inference.

## What changed, and what would change this judgment?

Post-September-24 work strengthens limited thermal-mechanism content (Plasco
Chapter 3), not end-to-end reproduction. Continuous CBS counters do not
authenticate original chronology; the conditional Clip3/7 reversal remains.
The prominent aperture patch is B, not C: NIST's C-specific glazing claim is
unverified, not falsified. R1's human arm succeeds where original AI arms fail:
a measurement correction, not WTC7 causation. The records component preserves
actual handling misconduct and ordinary accounts, not a collapse operation or
universal innocence. New bridge software supplies no scientific corroboration.

The highest-value causal discriminator is a **fixed comparison of the reported
stable and failing NIST states**, with authenticated case links and a complete
inventory of their actual input differences before calling it a controlled pair.
Trace surviving C79 restraints and downstream resistance through the finite
published event interval; compare event order/shape under one fixed observational
alignment, with force/contact/energy accounting. Applicable D3–D6 records,
qualified structural review and solver capability are missing prerequisites;
upstream fire-history uncertainty can remain explicit in a conditional state test.
Sustained adequate restraint, failure arrest or incompatible timed propagation
would weaken that implementation. Reproduced emergence of the observed sequence
with supported surviving capacities would strengthen it, even without a perfect
rubble match. It would not alone authenticate the historical thermal inputs.
Stop at these two fixed cases or identify the precise failed input dependency;
do not retune until a favorable movie appears or invent a surrogate run.

The nearer held-input discriminator remains the seven-pair spring/shell comparison
in the structural review, after its actual-human and native uncertainty gates.
Robust disagreement would bound calibration error; agreement would narrow that
criticism, not validate the building. Stop after seven pairs, retaining unresolved
ones. Authenticated mechanism-specific physical or operational evidence could
also reverse the ordering without identifying an actor first. No such test,
acquisition, human acceptance or engine action is executed or authorized here.
