## Fresh source check: multipoint roofline paper

The actual Chandler/Walter/Szamboti June 2023 paper is now [preserved and hashed](chandler-walter-szamboti-2023.pdf) from its [publisher](https://ic911.org/journal/articles/the-instantaneous-free-fall-of-world-trade-center-building-7-and-nists-attempt-to-hide-it/). Root viewed complete pages 15, 43–47, 50 and 51. Pages 43–46 report Camera 2 accelerations of **9.30, 9.79, 9.81 and 9.92 m/s²** for four roofline points. Table 1 places the reported free-fall starts at 8.0–8.2 seconds relative to east-penthouse onset, with 0.2-second sampling. Pages 47 and 50 contain numerical tables; page 50 also provides media links. These are the authors' fitted results, not our independently reproduced historical measurements.

Our assessment: this is a concrete, testable challenge, not merely a general demolition assertion. Finite sampling does not prove mathematically instantaneous onset or failure of every column; it also does not refute a rapid transition. Multiple points sharing one camera and calibration are not independent witnesses to all hidden failures. A local roof-point acceleration is not whole-building center-of-mass acceleration. Before changing causal weight, reproduce the published numbers with explicit fitting windows, scale/clock assumptions, smoothing and rounding, then compare the same observables with model predictions. Advocacy affiliation neither verifies nor falsifies a measurement.

The [existing NIST camera-method audit](/Users/admin/docs/911/research/sherlock-wtc7-investigation/nist-camera-method-audit.md) also survives reinspection with an important distinction: the printed smooth-curve form forces initial zero acceleration for its published exponent, but NIST's separate reported gravity-stage regression uses differenced measured positions, not simply that curve's second derivative. Root reran the saved printed-form arithmetic: all 11 time rows and 10 finite-difference checks exactly match the saved JSON. This validates that limited calculation, not NIST's chosen model, original positions or causal conclusion.
