## Conclusion and comparative claim

Fire is an **affirmatively evidenced initiating stress**, not merely the default left after rejecting demolition. NIST supplies a technically developed route from that stress to instability. Together these make fire-triggered collapse a serious, evidence-grounded working explanation. They do not yet demonstrate building-specific sufficiency or authenticate NIST's exact historical sequence.

A narrow, conditional comparison is defensible: the reported absence of the conspicuous sound predicted for NIST's studied loud-blast scenarios favors fire-triggered initiation over those scenarios **if** the prediction is approximately applicable at the recording locations. This is a directional evidentiary comparison, not an overall probability ranking. The applicability qualification is material, not decorative. No strict ordering between the broad fire and deliberate-removal families is earned here; this is not equal odds. An unspecified, conveniently undetectable intervention also receives no affirmative support merely by surviving that test. [Acoustic report](../nist-acoustic-detectability/report.md).

## The positive case

**Observed exposure.** Selected photographs contain appreciable flame-like activity at WTC7, including multiple east-façade locations. This contradicts a blanket account of only negligible visible burning. It is positive evidence of a potential initiating process. These are visibility-qualified observations, not measured steel temperatures, full-floor burning durations or a representative fire-intensity sample. Fires could also coexist with intervention, so their existence alone does not distinguish the families. [Fire coverage](../fire-coverage-batch2/report.md).

**A specified mechanism with some empirical foundation.** The structural synthesis records material tests, connection-capacity calculations, calibrated models and explicit contact/resistance routes. Loss of restraint can reduce column stability without requiring added weight. NIST's proposed sequence is regional damage and bracing loss, interior instability, then exterior response—not eight identical intact floors falling in a simple stack. Reported assembly tests support relevant behavior classes, but are not heated-composite WTC7 validation; later tests are not contemporary validation of a 2008 result. [Causal-chain synthesis](../causal-chain-synthesis/report.md), “What strengthens the technical basis.”

**A non-collapse comparison exists.** NIST reports a 3.5-hour damage case stable at the analysis end and collapse in the 4-hour case. That is evidence against the allegation that every supplied damaged state was compelled to collapse. It is model-output evidence, not an independently reproduced threshold, an observed half-hour transition or proof that the actual building occupied the collapsing state. [Causal-chain synthesis](../causal-chain-synthesis/report.md).

**Observed motion does not identify the initiator.** Rapid, gravity-scale roof motion remains a serious positive constraint. The joint-coordinate test removes one proposed internal-inconsistency objection; the transition study cannot distinguish instantaneous onset from all short ramps reliably. Neither measures simultaneous failure of all hidden supports. Localized damage can precede rapid instability in principle, but that principle is not a WTC7 propagation calculation. Conditional lateral-motion evidence also prevents “perfectly vertical rigid translation” from serving as an unquestioned premise. [Joint consistency](../multipoint-joint-consistency/report.md), [transition test](../acceleration-transition-test/report.md), [lateral geometry](../lateral-geometry-audit/report.md).

**Categorical impossibility arguments are too strong.** UAF's inspected global analysis excludes P-delta in the expressly identified figure family; local nonlinear/buckling work nevertheless exists. Its close motion reproduction prescribes support removals, rather than deriving their cause, and more than one removal-height band gives the same corner trajectory. This weakens claims of demonstrated uniqueness or universal fire impossibility; it does not validate NIST by subtraction. [UAF method audit](../uaf-final-method-audit/report.md).

## Weakest bridge and what would change this assessment

The weakest bridge is **independently justified exposure → actual timed loss of capacity/restraint → sufficient propagation matching the observations**. Released input organization is not authenticated execution; retained contact is not surviving capacity; motion after imposed removals is not an initiating mechanism. Other fire pathways cannot inherit NIST's support without specifying their own bridge.

The highest-value physical discriminator remains the exact paired stable/collapsing states tested against observationally credible thermal histories and surviving restraints, with staged motion scored consistently rather than tuned away. Robust arrest across credible inputs would weaken that route; reproduction of previously untuned timing and motion would strengthen it. This requires authenticated run state and qualified execution not established locally; no solver run is proposed as immediately available. The existing reports identify dependencies, not completed tests. [Causal-chain synthesis](../causal-chain-synthesis/report.md), “Priority discriminators.”

Finally, legitimate CCTV maintenance is affirmatively documented but does not establish 2001 structural access or preparation. Conversely, documented post-collapse handling misconduct must not be rewritten as no wrongdoing. Neither supplies the missing pre-collapse causal bridge, and neither absence of that bridge nor ordinary contracting validates fire mechanics.
