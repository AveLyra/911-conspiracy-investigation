## Bottom line

**The audit of the twelve located Luna-attributed investigation artifacts is complete; the investigation and several empirical follow-ups are not.** The earlier closeout must not be used as a certification that the model, video, or operational-evidence questions were resolved. This update integrates completed follow-ups without attributing their underlying calculations to Luna or pretending to have located every Luna conversation and edit everywhere.

The scientific corrections do not all point in one direction:

- The skeptics' published position data retain substantial downward curvature; their small velocity-table discrepancies have a jointly consistent clock/rounding explanation. They are not established fabrication errors.
- Those data do not establish a mathematically instantaneous acceleration transition, simultaneous disappearance of every support, or the initiating mechanism.
- NIST's published acoustic analysis retains conditional negative weight against the studied conspicuous blast scenarios, but does not establish a recording-specific detection limit through the actual intervening buildings.
- UAF's reported global methods and imposed failure sequences do not provide a decisive exclusion of progressive collapse. That shortcoming does not validate NIST's sequence.

**No strict fire-first or demolition-first ranking is earned by these independently checked results.** That is not a 50–50 assignment or a claim that historical inference requires perfect information. It is a specific judgment that the important remaining assumptions could materially affect the comparison and have not been adequately constrained here. A preference based on trusting one published reconstruction more than another must be labeled that conditional judgment, not an independently reproduced physical result.

## How I compare the explanations

**NIST's exact pathway is the most fully specified fire-to-failure account in this reviewed set, not an independently demonstrated superior historical explanation.** Real fires, relevant material/connection behavior, and a published damage-to-instability sequence give it affirmative technical content. The reported non-collapse control also matters. But the [causal-chain audit](../causal-chain-synthesis/report.md) leaves the decisive historical heating, residual resistance, solver handoff and timed propagation insufficiently verified. Merely invoking coupling does not discharge that burden.

There is also concrete reported counterevidence, not just unavailable inputs: NCSTAR 1A Table 3-1 gives west-penthouse disappearance at 9.3 seconds in its observation column versus 6.9/7.3 seconds in the debris-model views. The report separately limits later-motion reliability after the north-facade kink. These are published event/view values, not newly measured times or a calibrated residual. Their discriminating strength needs the same event definitions, camera correspondence and uncertainty; they cannot be omitted from a claimed full-motion validation.

**Deliberate removal remains a serious, testable candidate class, not the demonstrated winner.** Rapid loss of effective support can produce rapid descent, and an interior-first sequence does not uniquely identify fire initiation. But an adjustable pattern of removed members can be made to fit motion without explaining how that state arose. The present reviewed record does not authenticate a particular initiating intervention or a preparation-to-collapse chain. A quiet hypothetical method does not gain affirmative support solely because an acoustic test cannot exclude it.

**Other fire pathways and mixed causes remain separate hypotheses.** They cannot inherit NIST's favorable results while avoiding its specific tests; nor does failure of one exact NIST sequence prove the whole family impossible. Each useful alternative needs constrained initial conditions and predictions.

This is not a choice between innocence and guilt. Actor/intent findings require their own evidence. Conversely, identifying a perpetrator is **not a prerequisite** to finding a physical removal mechanism more likely if robust physical evidence supports it. The missing step here is discriminating evidence, not permission to consider a conspiracy.

## What could actually change the assessment

1. **Fire history to failure or arrest (Q02/Q06).** A source-grounded, executable paired collapse/non-collapse reconstruction, with justified alternative thermal histories and surviving connection capacities, could strengthen fire causation or expose a consequential failure of the proposed sequence. Authenticated case files, solver capability and qualified engineering review are prerequisites; none is supplied by another animation or file-count check.
2. **A shared motion test (Q03–Q05).** Independently calibrated, human-checked tracks spanning the transition, with camera/clock/deformation uncertainty, could determine which predicted onsets and trajectories remain compatible. Existing rapid-motion observations remain positive evidence while exact physical values are unresolved. Repeating fits to the same rounded table is not this test.
3. **Method-specific detection or physical provenance (Q07/Q09).** A recording-specific acoustic margin or an authenticated, building-specific material/operational record could discriminate mechanisms beyond terminal motion. The same standard permits either corroboration or disconfirmation. No outreach, new disclosure or laboratory work is authorized by this report.

Another comparator-paper version check is lower priority than these WTC 7 dependencies. The failed 2008 article route is closed for this unit, not proof of worldwide nonavailability. Newly located source leads can be revisited under a separate scope when their expected discriminating value warrants it.
