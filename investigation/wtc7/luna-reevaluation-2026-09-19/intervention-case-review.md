## Strongest case

**Deliberate support removal remains a serious candidate, but this record does
not establish it as the most probable cause.** Its strongest argument concerns
the required loss of effective restraint, not political motive or visual
resemblance alone.

1. **Rapid descent needs a mechanically adequate explanation.** The reviewed
   motion work retains strong downward curvature and approximately common
   visible descent. A deliberately prepared loss of critical load paths offers
   an intelligible way to create rapid gravity-driven instability. This is a
   genuine mechanical possibility, not an explanation of the actual preparation,
   member sequence or remaining resistance. The [structural synthesis](../causal-chain-synthesis/report.md)
   does not establish instantaneous failure of every support; a roof point is
   not the building's center of mass. Near-gravitational motion constrains net
   force, not each individual connection force.

2. **The competing fire chain has consequential, particular uncertainties.**
   The [fire-input audit](/Users/admin/docs/911/research/sherlock-wtc7-investigation/fire-annotation/source-comparison.md)
   documents observation-derived/interpolated ventilation, shifted Floor 12
   histories substituted for Floors 11/13, and acknowledged spread/duration
   mismatches. These are stronger objections than “fires looked small.” The
   [structural synthesis](../causal-chain-synthesis/report.md) identifies
   incomplete thermal/run handoffs and unverified links from heating through
   capacity loss to propagation. If independently justified alternatives removed
   the initiating failure, the specific NIST account would lose material weight.
   That result has not been obtained; uncertainty is not demonstrated
   overstatement, and these disclosed assumptions are not evidence of concealment.

3. **Survival controls make the residual-support question substantive.**
   [Comparators](/Users/admin/docs/911/research/sherlock-wtc7-investigation/comparator-expansion/report.md)
   include severe office-fire damage with retained support, partial failure
   within a mixed frame, and documented deliberate mechanical as well as
   explosive initiation. Thus neither fire severity nor an explosive label
   alone determines global outcome. Fire-triggered propagation must explain
   why WTC7's particular restraints failed when other damaged structures
   retained them. But unmatched survival cases do not demonstrate that WTC7
   could not fail; documented preparation elsewhere does not transfer to it.

These points justify investigating intervention alongside fire pathways. Only
the first is direct mechanical compatibility with removal; the others challenge
the strength of a rival explanation, not establish intervention independently.

## Mechanism, arbitrary removal and intent are different claims

Prescribing which supports disappear and when is a boundary condition, not a
demonstrated cause. The [UAF audit](../uaf-final-method-audit/report.md) retains
its conditional motion match, but the selected core/exterior losses and timing
were inputs; different removal bands yielded the same tracked response.
Consequently, that match does not identify where historical failures began,
whether people caused them, or whether those inputs were uniquely necessary.

Conventional explosive cutting, thermitic intervention and mechanical
preparation are distinct hypotheses. Each needs a physically workable,
building-specific account and predictions not chosen after seeing the motion.
The [acoustic audit](../nist-acoustic-detectability/report.md) supplies
conditional negative evidence against the modeled loud-blast scenarios; urban
propagation and recording limits qualify but do not erase it. Quiet intervention
is not excluded by that argument. It earns no positive support merely by being
harder to detect. This review establishes no workable WTC7-specific thermal or
mechanical intervention implementation.

Intent is an additional inference. The [Luna reevaluation](report.md) preserves
affirmative post-collapse improper souvenir removal and uncertain member
provenance. Those facts matter to custody; they do not identify pre-collapse
preparation or a concealment directive. The [procurement audit](../procurement-dta817373/report.md)
corroborates a 1990s CCTV-maintenance relationship and invoice/exhibit leads,
with positive ordinary-service context. It does not establish 2001 access,
structural work or commissioning of removal. Legitimate and improper activity
could coexist, but coexistence is a possibility, not the missing link.

## Strongest contrary evidence

Real fire is affirmatively supported by the [photograph review](../fire-coverage-batch2/report.md).
Neither darkness nor localized visible flames measures member temperature or
interior extent. NIST also has a technically developed account with empirical
connection/capacity foundations and stable alternatives, not merely an animation
([synthesis](../causal-chain-synthesis/report.md)). This gives fire a more directly
observed candidate initiating condition than any intervention identified here;
it does not by itself establish successful propagation.

Several purported exclusion arguments weaken under follow-through. UAF
explicitly excluded P-delta in its described global analysis; its own six-column
case progresses, and its motion match omits a salient kink. These are substantial
objections to treating that study as proof of fire impossibility, not proof NIST
is correct. [Printed motion samples](../multipoint-joint-consistency/report.md)
are jointly consistent with ordinary near-30-fps timing and rounding. The
[step/ramp test](../acceleration-transition-test/report.md) does not resolve
instantaneous versus short-duration acceleration change. [Lateral geometry](../lateral-geometry-audit/report.md)
supports qualified lateral deformation, although 11 m ± 3 m was not independently
reproduced. “Perfectly vertical, all supports suddenly gone” overstates this set.

The [Windsor draft discrepancy](../windsor-thermal-check/report.md) is real under
its stated interpretation, but neither a native-model error nor WTC7 evidence;
the [later publication](../windsor-2008-version/report.md) remains uninspected.
Collecting such discrepancies cannot supply a missing mechanism.

## Ranking, weakest bridge and next discriminator

**No strict fire-versus-intervention ordering is earned here.** Qualitative
ranking does not require numerical probabilities or perfect evidence. It does
require reasons why discriminating observations are more expected under one
bounded hypothesis. Terminal-motion compatibility is insufficient when removal
can be adjusted freely, while fire's observed presence does not validate the
disputed capacity-loss chain. This is not equal odds or an innocence/guilt vote.
Within intervention hypotheses, the loud modeled scenarios face a specific
adverse observation that quiet unspecified variants evade, not overcome.

The intervention case's weakest bridge is **from required effective support loss
to a particular historically executed deliberate mechanism**. Neither fitting
prescribed loss nor weakening NIST supplies that bridge. Strong disconfirmation
would be a robust fire-driven calculation predicting unused motion observations
and survival controls from independently bounded inputs. Strong support would
be authenticated mechanism-specific physical/operational evidence, or a
specified intervention model's successful distinctive held-out prediction.

Locally feasible follow-through is to define those contrasting observable
predictions from the existing reports without another curve-fitting exercise.
Actual comparative runs require authenticated native cases, qualified engineering
review and separately authorized solver work; custody/access hypotheses require
their identified original records. Missing prerequisites remain limitations,
not permission for endless acquisition or grounds to reject the hypothesis.
