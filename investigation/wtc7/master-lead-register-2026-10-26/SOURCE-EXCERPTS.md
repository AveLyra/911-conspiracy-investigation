## M135 / RF001 — An underlying witness memorandum is now acquired

Source: [investigation/broader-evidence/operational-record-followup-2026-10-26/REPORT.md, lines 59–84](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/838af60393017b74e3fee08bd190bb7a9e8a0f04/investigation/broader-evidence/operational-record-followup-2026-10-26/REPORT.md#L59-L84); whole-source SHA-256 `f991c4f06423b2de8b1838464de36b60407c8dd4afa6c74ceee1195f4da17a0c`; exact excerpt SHA-256 `892422f4b0deed36eb63db6e119e9eb144607764dd8c544a3bc53f17ea7dfaf7`.


The MFR is dated April20,2004 and identifies Team1A and its preparer. It is a
staff memorandum of an interview, not a verbatim deposition transcript or audio
recording. Its six physical pages were read and visually inspected in full.

Physical4 records the following sequence: Bin Don and Bayoumi ordered food and
sat waiting; Hazmi and Mihdhar entered speaking Arabic; Bayoumi addressed them
and invited them to sit. It reports an invitation to San Diego, an apparent
telephone-number exchange whose precise sequence the witness could not recall,
and departure after about **thirty minutes**. It also records the witness's
emphatic denial of advance information about the meeting and description of it
as **purely a coincidence**. Both assistance-related observations and that
contrary interpretation remain together.

The MFR says his Arabic was **limited**, the exchange was vague to him, and
Bayoumi sometimes translated. This qualifies use of him as an independent
witness to the conversation's content. It does not permit either treating him
as fluent or assuming that he understood no words whatsoever. Compare the
2016 FBI summary's reported inability to understand Arabic, rather than silently
choosing a language-proficiency description.

The memoir-like interval and spoken recollections are not a calibrated record
of minutes, advance knowledge or the conversation itself. The later two-minute
account, thirty-minute accounts and the coincidence belief must not be merged.
