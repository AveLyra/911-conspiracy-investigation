## Strongest warranted case and its central objection

The intervention-side argument starts with positive motion constraints:
qualified early interior-related movement, subsequent rapid facade descent
and apparent co-motion require an explanation of changing effective support.
A specified deliberate process could in principle supply that change without
requiring ordinary fire to generate every initiating failure. The unresolved
transition from local damage to widespread loss of surviving capacity is a
real burden on the specific fire sequence, not an objection erased by naming
progressive collapse. These are inherited constraints, not new post-v3 motion
measurements. [Baseline B01](../reconciliation-2026-10-05/report.md)
and [B02](../changed-evidence-review-2026-10-05/report.md).

The strongest objection to this case is decisive: a requested support-loss
history is not evidence of an executed mechanism. Choosing removals after
seeing the motion can fit the outcome by construction. No addition supplies
building-specific physical capability, application, timing and consequences
for a particular intervention. Actor identification is not required to test
physical evidence, but that does not waive the mechanism-to-observation link.
Near-gravity roof motion also does not prove simultaneous elimination of every
support or establish the acceleration of the entire building's center of mass.

Actual fires, relevant component/thermal tests and developed conditional
structural accounts remain affirmative competing evidence. In particular,
the published stable/arrest case is positive counterevidence to treating
NIST's model as automatically collapsing after any local damage. Conversely,
failed bolts do not erase surviving bearing, attachment, contact or lateral
restraint. Their timed capacities must be accounted for by either proposed
pathway. [B01](../reconciliation-2026-10-05/report.md),
[B02](../changed-evidence-review-2026-10-05/report.md).

## Physical compatibility versus overall comparison

Potentially quieter thermal/chemical intervention must not inherit every
conventional high-explosive sound prediction. Nor are these recordings silent:
the user's C bang and D explosion-like sounds remain unclassified observations.
An incidental structural sound could coexist with a quiet initiating process;
that compatibility does not identify one. The pre-existing conditional negative
weight against conspicuous blast scenarios remains where predicted signals had
a defensible detection opportunity. Imperfect calibration does not automatically
erase that weight. [B03](../acoustic-update-2026-10-07/report.md), A08.

Thus broad support-removal compatibility remains conditional, while historical
intervention attribution remains underdetermined. No new robust ordering over
fire follows. A weak provisional fire preference remains defensible if it
explicitly weights observed fire and published mechanics and acknowledges
unverified historical transfer. It is not compulsory from these additions,
and failure of NIST's exact pathway would not prove every fire pathway false.
Other fire routes need their own specified causal predictions. No ranking is
not equal odds. [B02](../changed-evidence-review-2026-10-05/report.md).

## Highest-value discriminators and falsifiers

1. Reproduce authenticated collapse/arrest states with justified remaining
   supports, contact, loads and time/energy outputs. Robust fire-triggered
   propagation matching independent sequence constraints would materially
   weaken the claim that intervention is mechanically necessary. Robust arrest
   would weaken that tested fire route; it would neither refute all fire pathways
   nor prove intervention. Native
   matched inputs/states, resources and competent engineering review remain
   prerequisites; this packet does not make execution ready. [B01](../reconciliation-2026-10-05/report.md).
2. Obtain the third shot's continuous source and associated soundtrack, then
   test a specified acoustic prediction against recording opportunity. A proven
   ordinary/edit-linked origin would weaken the bang-based intervention argument;
   a source-authenticated mechanism-specific signature could strengthen it.
   Lawful source acquisition and actual event review remain dependencies, not
   authority to retry stopped routes. [B03](../acoustic-update-2026-10-07/report.md), A06.
3. Resolve the factual basis of the June preservation concern and actual
   questionnaire/collection outcomes for identifiable records. Demonstrated
   compliant preservation would weaken this handling concern; affirmative
   intentional destruction could strengthen a specific concealment hypothesis,
   still requiring its own connection to collapse evidence. Exact follow-through
   records and any applicable approval are prerequisites; gated contents cannot
   be guessed. A02.
