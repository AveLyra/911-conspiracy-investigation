## Equal review criteria

One reviewer develops the strongest warranted fire-based interpretation and
one the strongest warranted deliberate-intervention interpretation. Both receive
these same inputs and criteria. Each must:

1. State which new observation, calculation, interpretation, metadata, access
   disposition or software result actually changes a material proposition.
   Separate source-copy content from authenticated historical conduct.
2. State the strongest case for the assigned family, its strongest objection,
   and a concrete result that would materially weaken it. Do not rescue either
   family with unconstrained alternative failures.
3. Separate terminal-motion fit, initiation/propagation, and intent. Do not require
   identifying an actor to assess physical compatibility, or infer intervention
   solely because the specific NIST sequence remains unverified.
4. Separate NIST's exact pathway from other fire pathways; separate conspicuous
   explosive scenarios from potentially quieter thermal/chemical intervention.
   Missing loud blasts cannot automatically exclude the latter. Nor does a
   generic thermal mechanism establish silence, structural capability or use.
5. Assess physical compatibility separately from overall evidence. Give no
   invented probabilities, forced total ranking or false 50–50 equivalence.
   Explain any conditional preference and what assumption drives it.
6. Account for dependent sources and contrary results, including the published
   stable/arrest case, user-reported sound candidates, actual remaining contact
   paths, incomplete municipal compliance, and uncertain tool/source lineage.
7. Identify no more than three highest-value next discriminators within the
   assigned interpretation; distinguish currently executable work from exact
   human, source, expert or approval prerequisites.

The two reviews are frozen separately before counterpart exchange. Each reader
must state prior involvement; these are separate AI analyses of shared inputs,
not independent historical sources, licensed engineering opinions or human
acceptance. Root reconciles disagreements rather than counting reviewer votes.
