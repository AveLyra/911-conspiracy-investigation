## Judgment

The additions justify **no new robust overall cause ranking**. I retain a weak,
conditional working preference for fire-triggered failure because the baseline
contains observed fires, relevant empirical component behavior, early
interior-related motion and a developed propagation account. That preference
weights positive mechanistic evidence against intervention's unestablished
case-specific initiating process; it does not presume institutional correctness
or a quantified prior. Its strength depends on uncertain transfer to the actual
building. [B01](../reconciliation-2026-10-05/report.md),
[B02](../changed-evidence-review-2026-10-05/report.md).

The new administrative records supply limited ordinary explanations and real
implementation concerns. Better graph coverage and software checks supply
readiness, not a newly demonstrated support-loss mechanism. Failed retrievals
supply neither exculpatory nor incriminating source content. No ordering against
quieter intervention is established by sound absence; silence itself is not
established. [B03](../acoustic-update-2026-10-07/report.md).

## Strongest physical case and its own strongest objection

Fire is an evidenced initiating stressor, not an explanation invented solely to
fill missing data. Thermal expansion, weakening and redistribution have
empirical support; NIST specifies a chain sufficiently concrete to expose
errors. The reported stable/arrest case is valuable positive model evidence
against automatic collapse from every local failure state. It also shows why
“fire plus damage” alone is insufficient: the actual state differences matter.
None of A01–A11 reproduces that paired comparison. [B01](../reconciliation-2026-10-05/report.md),
[B02](../changed-evidence-review-2026-10-05/report.md).

The strongest objection to my preference is the unclosed bridge from historical
exposure to correctly timed loss of surviving capacities. Failed bolts need not
remove bearing, contact or lateral restraint. Rapid near-gravity-scale roof
motion, apparent co-motion and the reported timing discrepancies constrain the
fire account; invoking nonlinear instability does not demonstrate the required
sequence. Conversely, a roof point is not automatically a defined body's COM.
The DistantView additions and A10 do not close that relation or authenticate a
capture clock. [B01](../reconciliation-2026-10-05/report.md),
[B04](../distant-view-integration-2026-10-08/report.md).

Broad physical compatibility is therefore not historical identification.
NIST's exact pathway remains assumption-dependent; another fire pathway must
specify its own mechanism rather than inherit successes and evade failures.
Deliberate removal remains compatible with rapid support loss but does not gain
historical support merely by prescribing it. Actor identification is unnecessary
to test physical signatures. Existing conditional negative acoustic weight
applies only to studied conspicuous signals with credible detection opportunity.
The user's reported C bang and D sounds remain observations, not authenticated
explosions or evidence of silence. A08 cannot extend that acoustic comparison
to all thermal/chemical interventions. [B02](../changed-evidence-review-2026-10-05/report.md),
[B03](../acoustic-update-2026-10-07/report.md).

Ordinary preservation/design activity can coexist with noncompliance or improper
acts. A02/A05 therefore narrow specific allegations without proving universal
innocence. Duplicate municipal copies, common NIST photo/model inputs, repeated
readers, F7 versions and synthetic results are not independent witnesses.

## Highest-value discriminators and present prerequisites

1. **Collapse versus arrest with retained support.** Obtain authenticated native
   case/input/restart differences, applicable contact/connection laws, loads,
   warnings and force/energy/time histories, then reproduce both cases before
   variants. D3–D6/D9 and competent engineering/solver resources remain missing
   prerequisites, not a currently executable run. Robust arrest or incompatible
   propagation would weaken that fire route; observed-compatible propagation
   retaining justified capacities would strengthen it, without authenticating
   upstream heating. [B01](../reconciliation-2026-10-05/report.md).
2. **Actual F7 packet inspection.** The local packet is ready for attributable
   human observations; this AI review cannot supply them. Wrong ownership or
   mapping would weaken proposed graphical support; agreement narrows that issue
   but leaves enclosure and physical transfer separate. This is nearer-term
   benchmark work, not building-cause adjudication. [A04](../../connection-curve-comparison/historical-applicability/f7-review-packet-2026-10-08/report.md).
3. **Bang-bearing third-shot provenance and detection opportunity.** A lawfully
   materialized continuous source with its associated soundtrack is still needed.
   Source-authenticated mechanism-specific signals could weaken a fire-only
   interpretation; demonstrated ordinary/edit-linked origin would narrow this
   candidate, not exclude intervention generally. A06 supplies locators, not
   those bytes; stopped routes must not be replayed automatically.
   [B03](../acoustic-update-2026-10-07/report.md), [A06](../../audio-listening-2026-10-05/c-source-lineage/nbc-folder-2026-10-08/report.md).
