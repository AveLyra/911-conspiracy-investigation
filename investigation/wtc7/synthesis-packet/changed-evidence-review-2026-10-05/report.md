# What the latest additions change in the competing explanations

The shared updated packet does not justify a new robust overall cause ranking.
It does justify narrower changes: a persistent light feature weakens one
single-frame flash interpretation; municipal records support ordinary explanations
for particular transactions; and the published stable case positively distinguishes
local damage from global collapse within NIST's reported model. None demonstrates
the actual building's transition from surviving support to rapid global descent.

This October 5, 2026 research supplement completes a **changed-evidence opposed
review**, not a new physical test or full investigation. Two fresh AI reviewers
read the same nineteen pinned files and separately froze their
[fire-family review](fire-review.md) and
[intervention-family review](intervention-review.md) before either saw the other's.
The thirteen additions include the eleven previously reconciled reports and the
stable-case and Figure 5-121 supplements. The
[scope](SCOPE.md) and [manifest](inputs.json) fix that selection.
Shared sources, common model environment and prior arguments prevent treating
review agreement as independent historical corroboration or expert validation.

## Assessment after the opposing reviews

**Fire-triggered pathways have affirmative support as candidates.** Fire is an
observed initiating stressor, empirical thermal/component behavior is relevant,
and NIST supplies a developed conditional propagation account. The reported
arrest case makes that account more informative than a claim that all damage
inputs inevitably collapse. A weak provisional fire preference remains defensible
if its dependence on historical model transfer and evidence weighting is explicit.
This is not an institutional presumption or a numerical likelihood result.

**The strongest fire-side problem remains sufficient, correctly timed loss of
the actual surviving load paths.** Failed bolts do not automatically eliminate
bearing, contact or lateral restraint. Existing motion and timing discrepancies
remain constraints, not inconveniences erased by missing inputs. The additions
do not close the exposure-to-state-to-propagation chain. Other fire pathways
cannot inherit NIST's successes while escaping its specific failures without
their own specified causal sequence.

**Deliberate support removal remains physically compatible but is not newly
identified as the best-supported historical cause.** Its strongest argument is
the rapid support-loss constraint and the unresolved propagation burden, not
proof that every support vanished at once. Its weakest link remains evidence
that a particular intervention actually produced that state. Thermal/chemical,
mechanical and mixed proposals cannot purchase a fit with unconstrained,
conveniently timed removals. Identifying an actor is not a prerequisite for
discriminating physical evidence.

**A limited comparison still favors fire over the studied conspicuous blast
scenarios**, conditional on their predicted signals having a reasonable chance
of reaching the recordings. This is inherited conditional acoustic evidence,
not fresh listening or recording-specific exclusion. It does not automatically
apply to quieter intervention classes. Against those classes the packet does
not establish a robust total ordering. No ordering is not equal odds, and
unavailable native reproduction is not a rule against making any historical
inference.

## Review differences and root corrections

The two reviews agree on the overall disposition and principal discriminator,
but agreement is not the reason to accept them; their claim-by-claim constraints
are. Both preserve affirmative fire evidence, real propagation uncertainty and
the absence of a newly established intervention mechanism. Neither requires
perfect information or proof of a perpetrator.

One wording limit needs explicit correction. The intervention review's A13 row
says flame-like morphology weakens “uniformly mild-fire descriptions.” Carry
that forward **only as a challenge to an absence/negligibility claim about
visible flame**, not as a demonstrated thermal-severity finding. The underlying
A13 report expressly does not measure temperature, heat release, interior
exposure or structural sufficiency. The fire-family review states that limit
more accurately. The frozen intervention opinion remains unchanged; this
disposition controls the shorter synthesis.

A12's displayed states would be 3.70 and 4.26 seconds after damage application
**if the caption and application labels share the global clock**. They are not
termination times or a stability criterion. The adjacent no-debris case's
one-second caption inconsistency must not be assigned to the stable run or
treated as demonstrated numerical failure. These source limits qualify both
summaries rather than removing the positive reported arrest result.

## What would materially change the comparison

The highest-value physical test remains a paired collapse/arrest reproduction
using authenticated native case identities and states. The
[stable-case supplement](../../causal-chain-synthesis/stable-control-2026-10-05/report.md)
specifies complete active inputs/properties, applied temperatures and damage,
continuation/restart state, solver/build diagnostics, termination criteria,
matched force/contact/energy histories and frame-time mapping. Recover and
enumerate actual differences before calling the cases controlled; reproduce
both reported outcomes unchanged before justified variants. A thirty-minute
difference in a label is not an isolated causal variable.

Robust observed-compatible propagation while retaining justified remaining
capacities would weaken the argument that intervention is mechanically necessary.
It would not independently exclude an intervention or authenticate upstream
fire history. Robust arrest or incompatible timing would weaken that tested
fire route, not prove every fire pathway impossible or establish intervention
by elimination. Authenticated mechanism-specific physical or operational
evidence could change the comparison independently.

Actual listening to the four prepared excerpts, the partly completed window
review and the separate curve human checks remain unperformed observations at
their respective gates. R1 is already complete and must not be requested again.
Native paired states/outputs and competent engineering resources remain distinct
dependencies. No repeated missing-file scan or second review of these same
reports supplies those results. The held matrix-save and archived-feedback
routing decisions remain user-owned.
