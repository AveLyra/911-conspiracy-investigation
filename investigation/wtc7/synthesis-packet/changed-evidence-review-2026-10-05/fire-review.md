## Strongest fire case and its limits

The affirmative chain in [P01](/Users/admin/docs/911-worktrees/sherlock-wtc7-investigation/research/sherlock-wtc7-investigation/completion-audit-2026-10-04/fire-case-review.md)

fire is observed; empirical thermal/material/connection behavior supports
expansion, weakening and instability; NIST specifies floor/restraint damage,
C79 instability, interior propagation and perimeter destabilization; broad
interior-before-exterior motion is compatible. A12 adds detail to the already
known noncollapse control, rather than a new experiment. Its reported common
loading/parameters deserve evidentiary weight without native reproduction.
Yet whether temperature changed alongside damage is unresolved, so “thirty
more minutes caused collapse” is not an isolated-variable result.

The weakest link is building-specific, correctly timed loss of surviving
capacity. Failed bolts do not automatically eliminate seats, Floor14
attachments, contact or lateral restraint. The packet preserves actual adverse
timing evidence: damaged-case west-penthouse passage precedes the observation
column by 2.0/2.4 seconds. Gravity-scale roof curvature and approximate selected
co-motion constrain propagation; neither can be dismissed by hypothetical
measurement error. A roof point is also not automatically a defined body's
center of mass. Hidden motion cannot be invented to cancel excessive resistance.

NIST's exact sequence is more specified and testable than other fire pathways,
not thereby established as historically superior. Other pathways must specify
their own exposure, load paths and propagation. OEM fuel/control records do
not merge its system with Salomon's, establish release/heat transfer, or convert
office-fire evidence into a diesel scenario.

The retained partial ordering favors fire over the **studied conspicuous blast
scenarios**, conditional on their predicted signal reaching the recordings.
A11 supplies no new acoustic verdict. Thermal/chemical intervention lacks an
authenticated building/member/time mechanism trace here; that deficiency is
not repaired by criticizing NIST. Mechanical intervention gains no executed
operation from authorized notches or coring. Mixed intervention needs a
specific additional act and distinct predictions. None inherits an acoustic
exclusion meant for loud blasts, and none earns explanatory credit by freely
prescribing support loss. Motion compatibility does not establish initiation
or intent; perpetrator identification is unnecessary for useful physical proof.

Thus the existing no-overall-ranking-change conclusion survives a substantive
test, not a vote among reports. Its proper form retains local changes and the
fire-favoring evidential asymmetry. It is neither equal odds nor a requirement
for perfect reproduction before any inference.

surviving capacities would weaken this objection. Mechanism-specific physical
