## Common questions and acceptance

Each reviewer independently develops the strongest evidence-supported version
of its assigned family: fire-triggered pathways or deliberate support removal.
The assigned role does not require favoring that family.

1. Address every A01–A13 addition with a concise disposition: what actually
   changes, whether it discriminates between specified explanations, and the
   strongest alternative reading. Keep unavailable results distinct from
   negative results; copies and multiple readers do not multiply event evidence.
2. Explain the strongest affirmative causal argument and its weakest link.
   Separate initiation, support loss, propagation, visible motion, and intent.
   Distinguish NIST's exact sequence, other specified fire pathways, conspicuous
   explosive, thermal/chemical, mechanical, and mixed intervention where relevant.
3. Test the existing no-overall-ranking-change conclusion. State any justified
   qualitative ordering, change or partial ordering and why. No numerical odds,
   equal-probability inference, institutional presumption, perfect-reproduction
   requirement, or perpetrator-identification prerequisite.
4. State the strongest objection to your assigned case and an observation/test
   that would materially weaken it. Unconstrained support deletion is not a
   historical explanation; an available physical mechanism is not proof it occurred.
5. Identify the highest-value discriminator and its exact remaining dependencies.
   Do not restart completed searches or turn a report about a missing input into
   a performed scientific test.

Outputs are concise attributed review files with exact local source links,
coverage statement, byte checks and disclosed prior involvement. Both freeze
before root compares them. Root preserves disagreements and writes a short
decision on whether the current assessment changes, with a record of material
objections. A third reviewer checks that decision against both freezes and the
fixed packet. Corrections to root's synthesis do not rewrite frozen opinions.
