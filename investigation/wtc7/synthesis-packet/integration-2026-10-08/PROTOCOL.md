## Relationship and interpretation rules

For each selected claim, join the applicable question and WP exit components,
dependencies and causal links. Distinguish `supports`, `contradicts`, `limits`
and `requires`; a shared Q-ID does not establish scientific support. WP0
provenance and WP6 review are process requirements, not extra causal evidence.
Dependencies remain metric-specific, not universal prerequisites to every test.

Bind each evidentiary reference to an exact artifact and locator with its role,
underlying origin/source family and basis for that assignment. A processed
photograph and model description inside one publication are not automatically
one underlying observation; copies, renders and multiple readers are not
independent sources. Preserve genuinely unknown origin relationships.

For derived calculations, locate existing inputs, protocol/code version,
output and verification receipt, or state the missing transformation and its
consequence. Keep source inspection, arithmetic reproduction, human acceptance
and professional qualification separate. A byte check verifies a pinned file,
not its scientific or historical truth.

Correct only affected current dispositions, recording the precise superseding
source. For example, reported A/B/C/D listening cannot remain globally
“unperformed,” but partial user reports are not a completed authenticated
event inventory. Old reports and failed AI arms stay preserved. Do not restore
an unqualified absence-of-sounds argument against every intervention mechanism.
No unsolicited new hypothesis ranking, legal promotion, human attestation,
engine/bridge admission, external transmission, commit or push follows.
