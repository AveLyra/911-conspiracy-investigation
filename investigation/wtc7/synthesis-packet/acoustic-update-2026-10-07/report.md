## Apply the same evidence to competing mechanisms

**Fire-triggered failure:** a presently unlocalized bang does not distinguish
it from intervention. Structural, ambient and edited-recording origins remain
alternatives, not established explanations of this sound. The comparator's
reported sounds do not establish equivalent microphone sensitivity, source
distance, obstruction, gain or editing history at WTC7. A fire preference
cannot be strengthened by calling C silent or equating an unverified charge
sequence with proof that none was recorded.

**Quiet thermal or other deliberate support removal:** the absence of a
particular predicted loud signature can constrain that loud scenario without
excluding every quieter alternative. This was already a limit of the October 5
assessment, not new positive evidence of thermal intervention. A quiet
initiating process would also be compatible with incidental structural sounds;
the hypothesis need not predict silence throughout the subsequent collapse.
It still needs evidence connecting the proposed process to the actual timed
loss of support. No actor identification is required to test that physical link.

Nanothermite itself is not a guarantee of silence. A primary laboratory study
measured formulation-dependent pressure generation that did not simply track
temperature. That is enough to reject a universal “thermal means no pressure”
assumption, not enough to predict outdoor audibility, structural capability or
historical use. [Jacob, Kline and Zachariah (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.5021890).

**Conventional conspicuous-blast scenarios:** retain only the existing
conditional negative weight from the
[detectability audit](../../nist-acoustic-detectability/report.md).
These additions neither establish the predicted signature nor validate its
detectability at each actual recorder. Do not test only sharp comparator-like
bangs: the reviewed NIST prediction also describes a low-frequency rumble.

The strongest objection to excessive caution is that imperfect calibration
does not make every non-detection worthless. A sufficiently conspicuous signal
could supply qualitative negative evidence if its predicted source and
recording opportunity are well supported. Conversely, merely naming ordinary
origins for C's bang does not establish one. Both cautions control this update.

## Consequence and next discriminator

No new robust total ordering between fire-triggered failure and quiet
intervention is justified by these additions. This is not equal odds or a
finding that either explains the complete structural sequence. The broader
thermal-to-support-loss and intervention-to-support-loss burdens remain.

The next useful acoustic source task is **the bang-bearing third shot's own
continuous source and associated soundtrack**, through a genuinely new,
authorized route. Establish sequence/provenance before associating the bang
with a structural event; then evaluate a specified acoustic prediction against
recording opportunity and competing origins. More first-shot refinement,
repeating denied transfers or choosing a convenient time shift cannot supply
that source link. A more precise listener onset would improve the annotation
but would not authenticate the recording.
