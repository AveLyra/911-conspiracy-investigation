## Specific checks and failure modes

### Fixed selection and finite coverage

The ascending 1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7-8 stages are administrative rather than a claim
of chronology. Completing all declared candidates even after an early positive
avoids stopping the search at a favorable association. Stable catalogue IDs,
spelling distinctions, metadata-change pauses and non-overwriting receipts
protect source identity. A missing/changed/refused item remains in the coverage
record; a transfer failure cannot become a visual nonmatch.

Nine exact PTS-quantile targets per admitted clip, with first-frame-at-or-after
selection, define an auditable coarse screen. Preserve duplicate target mappings
but inspect each distinct selected frame once in the declared order. A frame
or scene between samples can be missed. Consequently, even incompatible views
at every inspected sample cannot exclude a matching scene elsewhere in the
clip. The permitted negative result is finite sampled non-recovery, not
whole-clip absence. All selected frames must receive records, not just the
best-looking candidate; retain reference-specific strengths and contrary cues.

Irregular-clock, duplicate-target and malformed-input controls are appropriate
prospective requirements for the proposed adapter. The implementation review
should also verify its explicit handling of empty/single-frame inventories,
missing timestamps, repeated/nonmonotonic PTS and variable geometry against
the stated admission rules, not repair those cases invisibly. This note has
not inspected or run that code and makes no claim that any test already passes.

### What a positive result can establish

Two distinctive spatial relationships, plus contrary-geometry checking, are
a reasonable threshold for a **provisional scene/view association**. Merely
finding two features on the same familiar building is not enough: identify
their relationship to the corner, foreground/background or camera view, and
why the combination is more specific than repeated facade bands. The cues
need not be statistically independent, but a single feature described twice
should not be presented as two discriminators.

The strongest false-positive alternative is the same building or similar
vantage recorded at another time, possibly by another camera. A scene/view
match does not eliminate that possibility. It cannot alone establish common
underlying footage, exact exposure identity, camera custody or time. A positive
result should name the exact candidate sample and reference, the two relations,
the surviving alternative and the later source-transform/frame test that would
be needed. Those later tests need their own bounded declaration; they are not
silently included in this screen.

Color, smoke resemblance, credit text and source-added floor labels are weak
identifiers and are correctly excluded as sufficient evidence. Conversely,
cropped or obscured landmarks cannot be counted as incompatible geometry.
Report processing means there may be a real counterpart without identical
brightness, framing or overlay pixels. No pixel equality or quantitative
similarity score is needed or authorized here.

### Integrity and diagnostic limits

Two extraction runs with source/selection/product pins test repeatability,
not independent physical or historical validation. A separate audit of exact
PTS joins, dimensions and products remains necessary as declared. A decoder
diagnostic refusal is a technical limitation, not evidence that a scene is
absent. Unknown warnings must receive the protocol's explicit diagnosis;
do not introduce a broad allowlist after a desired image appears.

The transfer limits, terminal-before-retry rule and capped attempts preserve
bounded work and failed receipts. I did not verify the underlying catalogue's
total byte count or provider payloads in this method lane. No sensitive or
temporary bearer material should enter records; publicly returned content
remains evidence, not instructions or expanded authority.

### Reader independence and acceptance

Saving references before candidates, each clip before the next, and both
complete records before substantive exchange limits new peer influence and
post-hoc tuning. It does not erase the readers' prior knowledge or create
independent cameras. Retain disagreement rather than recoding into consensus.

This is a source-identification preparation task, not a physical window-state
measurement. The pending human window review is not a blanket bar to this
provisional screen. Equally, successful screening cannot satisfy that human
gate, validate an optical method, accept a Sherlock finding or establish a
collapse explanation. The relevant endpoint is an inspectable association
candidate or a consequential finite coverage limit.
