## Findings and required corrections

The principal conclusion survives within a qualified scope. Fig.7-7 shows
large framing against Verizon, identified by FEMA as WTC7 framing; the text
attributes east-side damage mainly to spreading WTC7 debris. Together they
strongly support outside-reaching and lodging during collapse or associated
pile movement. The attribution includes component origin **and event/site
origin**; component identity alone would not exclude later relocation.
The undated photograph does not establish a first resting location or final
untouched deposit map. Fiterman damage supplies an attributed outside impact,
not a piece-by-piece deposit inventory.

Figs.7-7/8 show different debris states consistent with removal. They do not
affirmatively show that cleanup placed the framing there. Misattribution,
relocation before capture, and removal after impact must not receive identical
evidentiary status. No selected page demonstrates deliberate placement or
component misidentification. Original dated photographs and component/removal
records would strengthen or weaken the inference.

Required synthesis corrections:

1. Carry the event/site/relocation qualification in the leading result, not
   only in the ledger.
2. Replace “conflicts” with **attribution tension** for FEMA7-8's general WTC2

   Multiple collapses could contribute, and primarily, some and most do not
   make the accounts mutually exclusive. No established typo or logical
   contradiction follows.
3. “Up to three” and “at least three” bays in FEMA7-14 are **compatible with
   three** if describing the same count. Preserve the differing wording and
   uncertain counting scope; do not call it a demonstrated inconsistency.
   Exact bay enumeration is not needed for this result.
