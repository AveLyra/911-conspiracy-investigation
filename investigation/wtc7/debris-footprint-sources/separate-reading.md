## Assessment returned

all-structural-debris containment if their component attribution is correct.

Identifying it as WTC7 framing depends on the report's attribution; the reader
did not reproduce component identity independently.

Predominantly vertical descent remains compatible with outside deposition.
Static aftermath images do not time lateral motion, distinguish fragmentation
from impact/sliding/removal, or determine a mass fraction. A concentrated pile
and localized neighboring damage are compatible with compactness, but no mass
denominator, volume/density reconstruction, or compactness threshold was
established. Repeated accounts across chapters and a credited background figure
do not supply independent corroboration.
