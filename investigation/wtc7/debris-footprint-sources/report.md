## Result and the actual question

The selected evidence strongly supports WTC7 structural debris reaching and
lodging outside its building outline during collapse or associated pile movement,
conditional on FEMA's component and event/site attribution. Undated photographs
do not independently exclude later relocation or supply a final untouched
deposit distribution. The clearest
selected evidence is Fig. 7-7's large framing assembly against Verizon, explicitly
identified in its caption as WTC7 framing. The photographed neighboring-building
damage is visible; the origin of each damaging component is not established
from pixels alone. Fiterman's WTC7-impact attribution supports the result, but
damage alone does not identify which pieces remained deposited there.

This **does not resolve the user's concern about rapid, largely downward,
apparently coordinated collapse**. The protocol converted “own footprint” into
universal containment of every identifiable structural fragment. That is a
legitimate narrow test but a stricter claim than the user's underlying question;
it cannot stand in for measuring symmetry, direction of descent, timing of
support loss, or the proportion of mass deposited nearby. Even largely vertical
descent can be followed by outward fragmentation and sliding. These static
aftermath pages cannot allocate the observed spread to those phases.

Neither a predominance of mass within a specified boundary nor a comparative
demolition/fire likelihood is measured here. No preferred cause is established
or changed. The potentially decisive remaining task is a common-observable
comparison of pre-impact motion and physically specified support-loss histories,
with actual mass/force boundaries and measurement uncertainty.

The ensuing [Q04/Q06 finite-interval force test](../finite-interval-force/report.md)
examines the force inference from already-held motion evidence. A roof point
is not automatically the center of mass of a defined body. The test establishes
a conditional net-force constraint, not the actual historical force or a cause.
Its remaining dependency is physical calibration and justified bounds on a
specified body's changing roof-point/center-of-mass separation. Do not use an
unspecified downward pull to dismiss low-resistance descent. Remaining
measurement and engineering-review requirements in the charter continue to apply.
