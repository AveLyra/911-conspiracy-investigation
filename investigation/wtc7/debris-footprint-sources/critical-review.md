# Critical review of the root reading

This is a text-and-inference review of `root-reading.md`, not a second visual
reader or an independent historical witness. It is intentionally conservative
about what the FEMA captions establish.

## Strongest supported point

The selected Chapter 7 pages do more than show a generic rubble cloud. FEMA's
Figure 7-7 caption identifies framing from WTC 7 leaning against Verizon, and
the Fiterman text attributes large WTC 7 debris to damage in an exterior bay.
If the report's photo identification and attribution are accepted, those are
outside-building structural effects and defeat a universal literal statement
that every identifiable WTC 7 structural piece remained within the pre-collapse
WTC 7 outline.

## Vulnerabilities and alternative readings

1. The photos and captions do not provide an original forensic inventory,
   surveyed coordinates, member serial/mark identification, or a full custody
   chain. “Contradicted at the report-attribution level” is therefore narrower
   than “independently proved.”
2. The nearby-street photographs in Chapter 5 cannot be counted as WTC 7
   debris merely because they are beside WTC 7. Figure 5-15 is expressly
   attributed to WTC 1, and Chapter 7 separately attributes south Verizon
   damage primarily to WTC 1. The result must not double-count these fields.
3. The 70-foot statement is not a measurable radius for this record. “Average”
   could refer to an undocumented observational summary, and the page supplies
   no center, boundary, scale, denominator, or uncertainty. It supports a
   compactness description only at low resolution.
4. Figure 5-26 is an aerial post-collapse depiction with no page-level date,
   scale, or untouched-scene certification. It can show the report's depicted
   spatial pattern, but cannot establish a mass fraction or eliminate cleanup
   movement.
5. The distinction between the WTC 7 outline and the Con Edison substation
   footprint is necessary. A result stated relative to the substation would be
   a geometry error, not an additional physical finding.

## Corrected scope

The review supports a narrow update: “own footprint” is not a safe literal
description if FEMA's stated WTC 7-framing/debris attributions are admitted;
“most mass was compact” remains an unquantified, report-level description.
Nothing in these pages favors fire, conventional explosives, thermitic removal,
or another mechanism. The next evidentiary upgrade is an independent reader's
frozen review plus original photographs/site plans or a documented debris
inventory with dates and coordinate geometry.
