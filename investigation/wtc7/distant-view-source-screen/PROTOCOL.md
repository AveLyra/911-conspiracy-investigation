## Question and value

Does the already held DistantView AVI supply a useful additional view or
coverage lead for the north roof and penthouse sequence, or only related or
insufficiently resolved imagery? This fills the explicit native-video gap in
the [alternate inventory](../camera2-bright-point-provenance/alternate-inventory.md).
That inventory and the [saved project review](../camera2-calibration-source/alternate-review.md)
are complete; neither decoded or inspected this AVI's native frames.

Do not proceed straight to the optional publication-table comparison. The
source distinguishes earlier Dan Rather/global-collapse time zero from the
later Camera2/east-penthouse time zero. A shared nominal-time join would not
establish a common event, and an optimized lag could fit away the discrepancy.
Native coverage is a prerequisite test, not a substitute for exposure timing.

The particular missing relationship is the outer north-roof top/side corner
and the lower feet of the raised roof-outline step, versus obscuring smoke
and the remaining facade. The older Camera2 upper-step target must not be
substituted for the paper's lower west-center junction. A positive view of
one of these junctions in both a standing and visibly descending selected
scene would justify a later event-correspondence and continuity protocol for
that feature. A different perspective exposing roof-to-facade attachment
would justify a later geometry-source test. Similar-looking images alone do
neither. Failure to see either relationship in eight samples does not exclude
its presence in uninspected frames; report that limited coverage, not absence.
