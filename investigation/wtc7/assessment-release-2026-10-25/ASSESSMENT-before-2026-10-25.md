#### How qualitative weights are assigned

Weight means bearing on a **named claim**, not estimated cause probability or
the practical importance of an unresolved question. **High** means a direct,
well-supported result materially decides or constrains that narrow assertion;
**moderate** means a consequential discrepancy or methodological limitation
whose historical effect or comparative fit remains partly unquantified;
**limited** means conditional compatibility, a weakly discriminating observation,
or an unresolved application. **No demonstrated directional weight** means we
have not established which competing claim the finding favors. These are
retrospective reasoned judgments, not measured likelihood ratios or numerical
scores. Confidence in a document/input fact can be high while its causal weight
is limited. No row is a new physical observation made by this synthesis.
