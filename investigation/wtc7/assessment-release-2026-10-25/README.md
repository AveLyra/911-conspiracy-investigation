# WTC7 assessment and provisional weighting — October 25, 2026

This packet preserves the full assessment text and its immediately preceding
version. It is not the complete underlying investigation archive, a physical
reproduction package, a legal filing or an expert certification.

- [Current assessment](ASSESSMENT.md): includes all weighted reasons, contrary
  findings, source limitations and sensitivity cases.
- [Immediately preceding assessment](ASSESSMENT-before-2026-10-25.md): preserved
  unchanged so the revision can be compared with its prior judgment.
- [Machine-readable scorecard](scores.json): contribution points and the five
  sensitivity cases, not measured probabilities or likelihood ratios.

Under the stated subjective scoring choices, deliberate support removal scores
+1.5, fire-driven collapse generally +1, and NIST's particular sequence -1.
Removing the provisional acoustic half-point ties the first two; also reducing
removal's motion-compatibility credit reverses them. The other-fire and
other/mixed categories remain unscored. NIST's sequence is a member of the fire
family, not a mutually exclusive alternative to it. No historical cause is
established by this arithmetic.

The scorecard does not erase the underlying acoustic audit's inability to
identify a cause. Conditional reproduction of motion after prescribed support
loss does not establish deliberate initiation or intent. UAF's criticism of
NIST's particular reconstruction remains distinct from UAF's broader no-fire
conclusion; its methodological limitations and findings favorable to NIST remain
in the full assessment. No new simulation or physical observation produced the
October 25 ranking revision.

## Scope and source availability

Both assessment files and scores.json are exact source-file copies, not
rewritten public summaries. The assessment copies retain dated local-only and
uncommitted-status statements as historical context, along with their original
source locators. Relative and absolute local references inside those snapshots
do not necessarily resolve in this packet. They must not be represented as
publicly available or independently reproduced merely because their assessment
text is published. This wrapper's three links identify the files actually
included here.

The packet excludes source PDFs, media, correspondence, human-review exports,
the historical execution log, filesystem inventories, private chat receipts,
legal drafts and the private repository's Git history. It does include the
full assessment's interpretations of previously local-only material and its
original source references and local-path strings. It is not anonymous or a
lossless substitute for the private technical record.

Earlier conclusions are preserved, not retroactively changed into findings
supporting the newer preference. Publication does not change the authority or
verification status of any research claim.
