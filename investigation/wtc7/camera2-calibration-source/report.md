## Result

Both saved calibration queries appear on the visible, horizontally banded
building facade in all three inspected Tilted frames. Neither is clearly
covered by a separate foreground object. Two separately frozen descriptive
passes agree on that limited positive observation. They **do not identify
the exact floors or architectural edge phases** at the queries.

The previously unreviewed counted-window image supplies explicit floor labels
and a useful facade-reference illustration. It is an annotated software
screenshot, not an original architectural elevation or an authenticated
mapping to the saved Tilted coordinates. Its duplicate appearances in two
kit folders are one source, not independent corroboration.

The other saved project contains **two PointMass motion tracks and 150 rows**,
not the eight motion tracks/334 rows in the Tilted project. It cannot substitute for that complete
multipoint state. Its possible relevance to a subset or earlier analysis
remains untested; a different track count is not evidence of falsification.

The strongest counterweight to dismissing the scale is that visible facade
banding, an explicit floor-based method and a compatible dimensional lead
exist. The strongest objection to accepting the scale as independently
validated is the still-missing exact feature/phase-to-dimension and processing-
geometry relationship. Neither observation licenses a cause inference.

## Alternate project: a finite source-version result

The [alternate inventory](alternate-review.md) uses the frozen safe parser
through a pinned adapter and a distinct DOM verifier. All PointMass arrays,
missing domains, numeric values, indices, key flags and locators are retained;
arbitrary names and author paths are omitted.

Each of its two PointMass motion tracks contains 75 finite x/y rows, indices 0,6,…,444, all also
in its saved key list. One safe source label is NW Corner; the other remains
an ordinal/hash identity. The saved clip has 962 video frames and 161 selected
steps 0,6,…,960. Each track therefore has 86 selected steps without a saved row.
Those missing states are not evidence of deletion or failed measurement.

Its saved origin, scale, tape length 66.0654 and assigned start time 0 differ
from the Tilted project's configuration. Its panel size 704×480 is only a
saved UI setting, not a newly probed media raster. No alternate video was
decoded, no world coordinates transformed, and no paper series was fitted
or compared. The limited conclusion is that this exact saved state is not
the complete eight-motion-track project; subset or historical relationships remain
possible and would need a separately declared test.

These motion-track counts exclude utility objects: the alternate project has
four total collection items (axes, tape and two PointMass objects), while the
Tilted project has ten (axes, tape and eight PointMass objects).

be counted as a new independent dimensional witness.
