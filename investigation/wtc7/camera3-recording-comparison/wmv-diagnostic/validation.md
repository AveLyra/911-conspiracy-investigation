# WMV warning localization: public validation and limits

> **Replacement note.** This is a summary of checks described in the previously published [status](../../STATUS.md#2026-09-11--wmv-warning-localization-continuation), not the missing original validation file and not a new independent verification. The public summary does not include the original video, selected images, decoder outputs, scripts, logs, point arrays or receipts needed to repeat those checks.

The status reports that all 442 decoded-frame byte/hash sequences agreed across the recorded settings and runs and matched an earlier rejected raw hash; that stop-on-error decoder prefixes ended before index 37; and that two reviewers separately inspected the 15 selected images. It also reports synthetic and parser checks and two closeout verifications, each described as passing 100 checks. Those are **historical claims about the prior work**; they have not been rerun for this replacement.

The status preserves qualifications: an initial synthetic-only failure occurred; an archived script copy had one extra final line-feed byte relative to its execution pin; and final process output for run03 was unavailable after an environment change, although the status reports a receipt and products supporting completion. These qualifications must not be silently converted into a claim of byte-identical preservation of every executable or direct observation of every process.

Reproduction requires the specific access-copy video and source/custody information, pinned decoder and settings, original warning logs, frame hashes and images, saved PointMass arrays, scripts and receipts, plus a documented comparison of the indices and time bases. Even successful reproduction of warning localization would not establish the original exposure clock or validate a physical collapse trajectory. The [NIST FAQ](https://www.nist.gov/world-trade-center-investigation/study-faqs/wtc-7-investigation) provides NIST's account of its own analysis, not independent validation of this warning-localization test.
