# Root primary-document observations

Saved after individually displaying complete rendered PDF pages1,145–148,156,
before receiving the separate PDF review. These are readings of a published
description/example, not independent validation of NIST's historical workflow.
The corresponding 110dpi PNGs are retained under sources/. The cover is the
June2004 Progress Report, Special Publication1000-5, volume4, appendicesG/H/I;
it is not the final WTC7 report or a live inventory export.

- H-3/PDF145 says CBS and other broadcasters supplied material including
  airchecks and outtakes; it does not identify CBS-Net Dub5 or a WTCI mapping.
  NIST's judgment about collection sufficiency is its assertion, not adopted.
- H-4/PDF146 describes an Access application called VideoList, assigning an
  arriving video a unique ID and recording duration, network/broadcast date,
  physical format/location, original/copy status, source, digitization and
  timecode information. Mini-DV copies are separately logged. It describes
  marking natural breaks/camera changes and exporting clips with Adobe
  Premiere. It also says the then-system's1GB AVI limit led to splitting long
  continuous footage into roughly four-and-a-half-minute pieces. Thus both
  discontinuous excerpts and capacity-driven contiguous pieces are possible;
  this does not identify why or how CBS14/15 were made.
- H-5/PDF147 FigureH-1 is an example form, not a supplied full database. Its
  tape table has separate Tape ID, Tape name, Copy, Format, Duration, Location,
  Source, Derived from, Batch, Clips and Timecode columns. The visible example
  is not CBS/Dub5. Do not equate its numeric Tape IDs with WTCI numbers.
- H-6/PDF148 begins TableH-1, explicitly photographic attributes, not the
  promised video TableH-2. Do not transfer its fields automatically to video.
- H-14/PDF156 FigureH-5 shows a clip-timing form with tape identity and separate
  DV in/out and actual in/out times, duration, notes and row selection. The
  text conditions broadcast alignment on real-time filming and excludes
  replays. It also discusses continuous segments split across clips and
  extracting mini-DV metadata with CatDV. It does not authenticate the
  historical capture time of the acquired CBS excerpts.

The document makes a specific source lead stronger: VideoList tape/copy rows,
derivation links, clipping records, selected replay exclusions, reference-event
times and underlying clip timing records could test the unresolved join.
It does not establish their present retention, public availability, withheld
status or any concealment. No CBS→WTCI identity is established on these pages.

Text extraction was performed over all224 pages, not a full visual review of
them. Its concatenated words, scanned form image and noisy OCR make negative
keyword results non-exhaustive. The schema figure fields were absent from the
initial text scan even though visible in the image. The separately preserved
regex scan is a discovery derivative, not a complete inventory transcription.
