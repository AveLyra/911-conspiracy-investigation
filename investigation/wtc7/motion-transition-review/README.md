# Camera 3 transition review: public availability summary

**Public replacement summary, November 2026.** This is not the original complete review. The [published WTC 7 status](../STATUS.md) reports that an earlier image-tracking pilot stopped at a search boundary, while a separate visual review observed continued roof-outline movement with increasing occlusion. The status also reports recurrent near-identical image content.

The reported algorithmic stop is not itself proof that the building stopped moving. Regular presentation timestamps and different encoded-frame hashes do not by themselves establish independent camera exposures. The original images, detailed receipts and complete review are not included here, and no new measurement or rerun is claimed. The earlier review does not establish a physical acceleration, original camera rate or collapse cause.
