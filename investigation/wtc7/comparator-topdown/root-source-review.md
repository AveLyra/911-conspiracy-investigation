## Interpretation and comparison

This is a stronger project/mechanics account than the earlier publicity pages.
It supports **reported executed top-down mechanical dismantling under maintained
temporary support**, not a spontaneous or intentionally initiated whole-building
gravity drop. The central distinction is what is moving: a prepared cap and
work enclosure, while material below it is removed in stages and loads are
transferred. A shared steel label or roof descent does not make it a matched
WTC7 collapse control.

For this reported slow, controlled cap process, near-static force balance would
require substantial upward support. That is a mechanics interpretation of the
specified process, not a new measurement of its acceleration/reactions. The
arithmetic1,500/15=100 is only the reported average allocation in source units;
it does not recover the actual distribution or establish reserve strength.

The paper does not furnish raw sensor traces, a complete support/cap structural
analysis, connection capacities, independent as-executed verification, exact
duration of each lowering episode, original camera timing or a WTC7 thermal
history. It identifies preparation and differing final phases; it supplies no
case-specific explosive or thermitic operation. That last absence in this paper
does not exclude unreported methods throughout the entire site's history.

Useful narrow inference: documented demolition methods must be stratified by
load-transfer/removal process before comparing footage. This case cannot be
used either to show WTC7-like free fall from staged dismantling or to exclude
other intentional mechanisms because they would have to look slow. Likewise,
it cannot validate a fire-induced collapse sequence. All remain subject to the
same independently constrained force/geometry/timing questions.

The attributed published method/use claim is strongly supported (B); its
presence and wording in these captured bytes are directly checkable (A within
document scope). Full as-executed physical verification and WTC7 motion/cause
equivalence remain underdetermined (D). Contractor authorship, selected images,

ceilings, not reasons to erase the detailed method account.

The strongest objection is that a participant paper can simplify or omit actual
phase changes, operational variations or adverse results. Exact jack/strain/+displacement logs and independently verified preparation/operation records

every construction record. No scientific need for another illustrative video
