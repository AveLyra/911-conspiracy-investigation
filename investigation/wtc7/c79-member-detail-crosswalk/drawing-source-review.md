## Scope declared before external queries

Trace original design, fabrication/shop and possible as-built drawing provenance
for the C44-C79 north seats at Floors 8-12/14 and the C79-C76 Floor 14 west
attachment. The parent independently examines NIST plan/detail geometry; this
review concentrates on original-source identifiers, revisions, status, custody
and concrete public availability.

The finite search is existing admitted local public NIST source leads, then at
most six targeted web search queries and six directly linked public pages or
documents. Search result metadata is a lead, not inspected drawing evidence.
Initial searches prefer NIST/official origins and exact drawing references;
follow-up queries may resolve an identified original source. No model execution,
new numeric scan, held/confidential packet, denied-source mirror, external
contact, payment, case-material transmission, main/legal change or ranking.
Before acquiring a payload whose sensitivity is uncertain, stop and identify the
risk without reproducing sensitive contents. Ordinary already-public NIST
technical reports are the initially admitted source class.

Distinguish NIST redrawn/idealized figures, identified design/shop originals,
an acquired public copy, and authenticated installed/as-built condition. A hash
does not establish historical authenticity. Inspect an entire relevant drawing
page, including detail/title/revision context, before treating it as reviewed.
Record unsuccessful paths and limit absence claims to this finite search.

The model-generation hierarchy on adjacent physical 71 / printed 20 is a model organization, not a drawing authenticity certificate. |

W1 supplies a more specific official plan-source position: S-8 is the typical
Floors 8-20 structural plan; E10/11 and E12/13 are identified erection plans.
NIST says those plans do not indicate girder studs, while S-8-10 depicts added
studs, plates and reinforced connections for a Floor 10 modification. It
describes omitting that modification from the 16-story representation because
revised loads/connections were not fully specified. These are NIST's stated
source-selection reasons, not this review's independent inspection of those
originals. The FAQ does not supply their revision registers. Thus Floor 10
cannot be treated as an unqualified typical-floor as-built match merely because
it is in the requested 8-12 band.

W2 does not establish a precise source custodian for each original C79 sheet.
It identifies a concrete follow-up class: alteration/audit records and WTC 7
construction photographs, which could help distinguish original design from
installed or later-modified details. Its generic cooperation claims are not
independent verification of every drawing.

## Version and geometry questions that remain live

1. **North seats, separately by floor.** Retrieve the actual erection/detail
   chain for Floors 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 14: plan sheet and revision, member
   fabrication mark, column segment/detail sheet, both girder ends, detail
   references, applicable modifications, and drawing status. The available
   Floor 13 A2001/1091 leads must not silently supply all six target floors.
2. **West attachment at Floor 14.** Its own plan/erection identifier and both-end
   connection details remain unresolved by this search. Do not relabel a
   Floor 13 north-girder detail as the C79-C76 Floor 14 west attachment, or infer
   the required original sheet number by a numbering pattern.
3. **Revision and installed-state check.** S-8, its floor-specific modifications,
   erection-sheet cross-references and shop-sheet revisions should be compared
   together. Then identify which dated construction/renovation photographs or
   acceptance/inspection records actually show these connections. NIST's
   reported photo agreement is favorable evidence to pursue, not omit.
4. **Seat/clearance dimensions.** Use the corrected NIST axial/lateral prose
   alongside the original detail's local axes, plate dimensions and support
   contacts. The original 2008 prose, June 2012 correction and W4's contrary
   interpretation are different source propositions. A 12-inch seat width is
   not itself a demonstrated axial walk-off distance or a dynamic capacity.
5. **Model-to-member edge.** Even an authenticated drawing does not identify a
   released mesh part/node namespace or verify the historical model's active
   restraint/force history. It can constrain the geometry that the separate
   numerical crosswalk must test. Conversely, a static input match cannot
   establish installed condition or historical damage.

These are concrete opportunities to disconfirm either an oversimplified NIST
detail or an overstated criticism. A revised/approved sheet matching the model,
with location-specific photographs, would strengthen the source-to-model edge;
an applicable conflicting detail would warrant explicit sensitivity work. A
conflict alone would not quantify a different collapse outcome, prove
intentional falsification, or identify a cause. No ranking is assigned here.
