## Whole-page context and interpretation checks

All 15 selected full-page renders were inspected, including captions, credits and adjoining text. The contextual images were retained without treating them as additional primary target samples:

- P. 197, Figs. 5-57/58: smoke-obscured facade and a separate rubble scene containing a small orange point. Rubble fire is not a WTC 7 window observation. P. 200, Fig. 5-62: a narrow distant target between foreground towers and smoke, useful geometric context; labels and early-afternoon time remain report assignments.
- P. 215, Figs. 5-80/81: aerial and ground views expose patches of upper facade amid smoke. Fig. 5-80 resolves many rows on the broad left face but the adjoining south-facing area is substantially veiled; Fig. 5-81 contains warm-colored small features without clean flame morphology at this render scale. They support preserving mixed visibility and ambiguous appearances. WABC-TV and Jay Comella credits do not establish independent timing or source authenticity.
- P. 239, Figs. 5-109/110 and pp. 245/247, Figs. 5-115/117 are the previously screened Spak family. They supply useful neighboring geometry, but rereading them does not add exposures or independently validate development between frames.
- P. 240 explicitly describes the problem of smoke accumulating against the south face and uncertain smoke origins. P. 241 reports the absence of later flame images in NIST's database. That is a claim about that selected collection's coverage, not proof of no fire. The following no-visible-flame/decay chronology includes another literal “12:45 a.m.” in an afternoon discussion. Both clock-text discrepancies are preserved instead of normalized silently.
- Pp. 245-247 infer breakage, progression, declining intensity, possible flashover and possible pathways to another floor from source sequences. This extension has inspected selected stills, not those full videos. P. 246 also describes window 11-35 as having neither smoke nor flames at 2:28:43, then describes an orange interior glow in the same opening in its Fig. 5-118 comparison. This local wording tension should remain an annotation question; it does not warrant selecting whichever description suits a theory.
- P. 248's suggested cordon explanation for reduced later east imagery is expressly uncertain in the report. No new causal account of missing imagery is established here.

## Geometry that can be annotated without adopting the report's labels

The page images are 935 x 1210 pixels, rendered at 110 dpi from unrotated 612 x 792 point pages. the historical lineage.json (not included in this package) records exact embedded-image dimensions and placement matrices. Use intrinsic embedded-image dimensions for resolution eligibility: page rendering enlarges several small report images, and the additional display pixels are not extra observations. The original camera resolution is unknown.

For an image CTM `[a,0,0,d,e,f]`, let `s=110/72`. Its top-left placement in page pixels is `(e*s,(792-f-d)*s)` and its displayed extent is `(a*s,d*s)`. For a page-pixel location `(x,y)`, embedded-image coordinates are `((x-e*s)*W/(a*s),(y-(792-f-d)*s)*H/(d*s))`, where `W,H` are the recorded intrinsic dimensions. Retain fractional coordinates; integer raster edges may differ by one pixel due to rounding. These transforms recover report-image geometry only, not a camera calibration.

## Reproducibility and verification

The first Poppler attempt had a missing default Fontconfig file and repeatedly reported unwritable cache directories. It was interrupted after producing pages 197-199. Those three PNGs are retained in `fire-coverage-extension/diagnostics/unconfigured-first-attempt/`. A local Fontconfig configuration then allowed all 15 pages to render without stderr warnings. The three common pages have identical SHA-256 hashes across both attempts. This is a rendering consistency check, not independent historical validation.

The successful run used Poppler 26.05.0 and a local `FONTCONFIG_FILE`; pypdf 6.10.0 on Python 3.12.14 read page text and embedded-image dimensions/placement. All final page renders were visually reviewed at their preserved size. The raw PDF's final hash matched the declared input hash. the historical lineage.json (not included in this package) contains per-page output hashes, software/configuration details, exact command expansion and diagnostic lineage. Original PDFs, earlier derivatives and manifests, shared source-map/status/charter files, software and legal records were not changed by this extension.
