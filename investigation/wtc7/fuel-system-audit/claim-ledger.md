# MFR-to-fuel-evidence crosswalk

2026-09-10. Research-only. Frozen propositions are in [mfr-claim-selection.md](mfr-claim-selection.md); the [report](report.md) supplies interpretation and references. MFR means the three-page Commission staff summary already acquired in the warning audit. N9 means NCSTAR1-9; NA means NCSTAR1A. NIST publications repeat one investigation/source family; their underlying interviews or equipment documents are not automatically independent of the MFR.

Grades describe the specified empirical/inferential proposition, not the speaker's honesty: **A** directly established; **B** strongly supported; **C** plausible but materially assumption-dependent; **D** weak/underdetermined; **E** unsupported or contradicted as stated. A quoted statement's existence can be directly verified while its historical content remains D. No grade is a causal probability. Documentary reading and bounded bookkeeping are the only reproduced results here; no historical fire or solver is reproduced.
