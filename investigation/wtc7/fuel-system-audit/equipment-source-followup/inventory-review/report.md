# NCSTAR 1-1J inventory and configuration reconciliation

2026-09-11. Research-only independent computational-agent reading. This is not a blind review, an independent historical source or a licensed engineering opinion. No cause ranking follows from these checks.

The equipment report supplies a detailed design-based inventory and identifiable original-record leads. It does not independently establish event-day tank contents, recovered quantities, sustained pumping or combustion. Its stated 43,283.8-gallon total adds correctly, but assumes full tanks and specified pipe filling. A consequential correction to the earlier audit is that a later reference to three day tanks totaling 600 gallons does **not by itself prove** that removed Floor 8 equipment was still counted: two different combinations produce that same total.

### The three-tank/600-gallon ambiguity

N9 physical 106/printed 62 says three day tanks totaling 600 gallons were assumed full. NA physical 54/printed 12 similarly refers to three day tanks without listing their identities. The earlier parent audit treated this as a retained pre-removal configuration. That interpretation is possible but not uniquely established:

- Base Floor 5 (275) + American Express Floor 8 (275) + Ambassador Floor 9 (50) = 600 gallons.
- Base Floor 5 (275) + OEM Floor 7 (275) + Ambassador Floor 9 (50) = 600 gallons after American Express removal.

The report taxonomy matters: 1-1J groups OEM under the broader base-building arrangement, whereas later passages often discuss OEM separately. The sentence at N9 printed 62 could be stale text or could use a different remaining-component grouping. A bare count and equal total cannot decide. Require tank IDs and one explicit inventory boundary to determine whether the OEM day tank is included or separately counted, and how removal changed the lines. Do not simply subtract every AmEx pipe volume: the later figures retain a riser and the decommissioning scope has not been acquired.

The stronger, correctly bounded discrepancy is **1-1J's explicit September 11 representation of the Floor 8 generator/day tank versus the later removal statement**. The later three/600 reference remains a membership ambiguity, not independently proved stale equipment.

## Unit consistency and recovery

1-1J physical 65/printed 31 specifies 70 gpm without an accompanying metric rate. N9 physical 105/printed 61 pairs 70 gal/min with 5.3 L/s; physical 435/printed 369 pairs it with 4.4 L/s and separately pairs 12,000 gallons with 55 m³. The declared check uses two explicitly named volume conventions:

| Check | U.S. liquid gallon (3.785411784 L) | Imperial gallon (4.54609 L) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 70 gallons/minute | 4.416313748 L/s | 5.303771667 L/s |
| 12,000 gallons | 45.424941408 m³ | 54.55308 m³ |
| 23,000 gallons | 87.064471032 m³ | 104.56007 m³ |
| 1,000 gallons | 3.785411784 m³ | 4.54609 m³ |

Thus the printed 5.3 and 4.4 values fit rounded conversions under different gallon conventions; the 55 m³/12,000-gallon pair fits imperial rounding. This is a plausible explanation of the numerical pattern, not proof of which convention was intended or which numerical value entered a model. The original pump specification and native input deck remain necessary. There is no demonstrated 20% simulation error merely because two textual conversions differ.

N9 physical 106/printed 62 attributes an estimated 23,000-gallon recovery to GZA and places it in the days following the attacks, with uncertain volumes and assumed initial fullness. NA physical 54/printed 12 says several months and presents initial fullness categorically. N9's 10 m³ ±10 m³ unaccounted is not the same numerical interval as NA's 1,000 gallons ±1,000 gallons under either convention. These reported quantities are not replaced with a silently selected value. Stock minus recovery does not measure pre-collapse combustion without release, time, system and uncertainty accounting.

This selected 1-1J equipment inventory supplies no original recovery ticket or gauge record. A whole-text **locator**, not a whole-report visual review, found no `GZA`, `23[ ,]?000`, `Trammel` or `2000` hits; generic `recover` appeared only on physical 24–25 and `remov` on 43/61. That search is limited to extracted text and does not authenticate a source absence. The [separate errata review](../errata-review/report.md) reports no listed correction for the specified fuel issues in its two-page artifact; this analyst read that review, not the errata pages, so it is not a second errata attestation.
