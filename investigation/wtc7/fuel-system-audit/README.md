# WTC 7 fuel-system research: recovered source audit

This package preserves historical source reviews concerning WTC 7 fuel systems and NIST’s diesel-fire scenarios. The reviews distinguish available electrical power, commanded pump operation, liquid released, a burning pool and effects on structure. They identify missing equipment and wiring details while preserving NIST’s modeled shutdown and fuel-balance arguments. Missing specifications do not establish that the equipment would survive or that a diesel fire occurred; the scope of this package does not establish a collapse mechanism or deliberate intervention.

Start with the [main audit](report.md), [equipment follow-up](equipment-source-followup/report.md), [installed-records review](equipment-source-followup/installed-records/report.md), [inventory follow-up](equipment-source-followup/inventory-review/report.md), [errata review](equipment-source-followup/errata-review/report.md), and [NIST source review](nist-source-review/report.md). The previously published [diesel scenario relevance review](equipment-source-followup/installed-records/relevance-review/README.md) adds distinctions among the individual scenarios. Historical protocols and computational-agent reviews describe earlier work, not current instructions or independent historical witnesses.

For this package a bounded primary-source check of [NIST NCSTAR 1-1J](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-1j.pdf) confirmed its description of an unlocated point-to-point control-panel wiring schematic, its specified Salomon pump model and its separately stated OEM transfer-pump capacity. A prior check of [NCSTAR 1-9](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-9.pdf) found the published pump-power shutdown discussion. These are published source descriptions, not recovered event-day operating records. The entire package’s scientific assertions, component assignments and historic review checks have not been independently rerun.

The original LFS PDFs, images, headers, raw source captures, sensitive logs, other data and solver inputs are not included in this package. Missing local references are labeled explicitly. Each research document is a derivative with a historical-context preface and, where necessary, unavailable-link labels. The one arithmetic receipt is byte-identical to its historical source. [PROVENANCE.json](PROVENANCE.json) records original and derivative hashes. Historical results and request dispositions are not evidence of current status.

For this package, the eight saved U.S. and imperial unit-conversion fractions and the addition of the two saved category totals were independently recomputed and matched. This confirms the arithmetic in the receipt; it does not verify the underlying inventory transcription, actual fuel quantities or the model inputs.

A separate selection and source-specific details are omitted from this derivative under the project’s disclosure boundary. No scientific content from omitted material is used in the included assessment. Original local records remain preserved.
