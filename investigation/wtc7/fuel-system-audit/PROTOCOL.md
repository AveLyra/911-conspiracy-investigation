## Testable question

Which statements in the acquired January16,2004 Catalano MFR describe direct fire/smoke observations, which infer equipment involvement or causal sufficiency, and which can be independently corroborated or contradicted by equipment, supply/recovery or contemporaneous incident records? Distinguish installed capacity, estimated contents, recovered fuel, released fuel, burned fuel, heat-release rate, ventilation and structural exposure. Do not count NIST's restatement of an interview as independent confirmation of it.

Compare the MFR's morning event sequence and generator/fuel assertions with actual local NIST source pages and a bounded search for relevant original non-NIST or underlying documents. Treat both witness and agency statements as claims to test. No exact wall-clock join, unseen lower-floor fire map, thermal magnitude or causal ranking follows from a source title or stored-gallon figure alone.

## Method and admission gates

- Preserve source bytes, SHA256, requested/final URL and acquisition date; explicitly distinguish agency original, public mirror, transcription, table, scan, witness account and later analysis. Raw transport headers/process logs stay local-only and are not export/feedback/commit cleared by hashing.
- Freeze meaningful page/case selections before consequential calculations; text search locates pages, full-page reading verifies tables, captions and qualifications. Render into new destinations, retain failed attempts and source hashes. Do not repair PDFs or use generated historical imagery.
- For every material claim record exact document/page, layer, source family, whether underlying record was acquired, assumptions, strongest alternative, falsifier and grade. Apparent contradictions must compare the same equipment/location/time and units before being treated as contradictions. Preserve uncertain joins.
- Do not compute heat exposure from tank capacity. Any later bounded arithmetic must have declared inputs/units, distinguish a conditional bound from measured consumption and propagate missing release/burn/ventilation/location dependencies. No new detailed destructive device model, large CFD/structural solver, inverse-cause classifier or unsourced parameter tuning.
- Include ordinary explanations and evidence against the favored interpretation. No inferential leap from a witness's technical role to validated calculation, or from agency disagreement to intentional deception.

Eight queries or repetitive/out-of-scope/denied results end the search cycle. An unacquired underlying source bounds the conclusion; it is not evidence of its nonexistence.
