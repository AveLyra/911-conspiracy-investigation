## Finite next test

The remaining requirement is a defensible graphical measurement model, not
exact recovery of an unknowable pre-raster curve. The current uncertainty
adapter propagates supplied allowances around exact toy centerlines; it does
not estimate historical linewidth/compression error or bound all uncertain
knots and integral/peak-location metrics.

Use one prospectively bounded admissibility exercise. Declare the primary
recovery method, how native stroke extent becomes conservative x/y bounds,
the additional raster/compression assumptions, and a no-gap-bridging policy
or another explicitly tested rule. Test known-path controls covering the
relevant widths, slopes, dash ends, crossings and compression conditions;
then independently recover finite historical candidate footprints. Do not
tune allowances to make a model difference significant. State the source
conditions outside which the method cannot claim coverage.

If defensible local support results, preserve all unknown intervals and use
the unchanged [42-slot rule](../HUMAN-SAMPLE-SELECTION.md), followed by actual
source-to-sample human checks. If it fails, close that particular method with
the concrete failed condition and evidence that would change the result—not
a claim that no graphical method could work. Unknown support remains unknown,
not L=0. No new expert-approval or original-arrays-only gate is imposed.

This review concerns the published connection benchmark. Even exact plot
recovery would not establish the actual building's thermal history, residual
restraint or dynamic propagation. No collapse-cause ranking, accepted engine
state or legal finding changed. The full charter remains active and incomplete;
work is local and uncommitted on `research/sherlock-wtc7-investigation`, base
