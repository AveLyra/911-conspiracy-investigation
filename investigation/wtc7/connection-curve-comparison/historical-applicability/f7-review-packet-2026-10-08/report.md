## Evidentiary ceiling and next test

The new positions make F7 available for the declared human mapping check;
they do not measure model error. C_H is conditional candidate coverage, not
admitted support D. Hidentity, Hsupport and Hink0 remain unvalidated assumptions
about stroke attribution, within-column support and enclosure inside the
selected cell edges. Arithmetic agreement and human locator agreement cannot
establish those assumptions globally.

The strongest objection remains that correctly mapped raster ink need not
uniquely identify or enclose the curve that generated it. The existing
[synthetic counterexamples](../envelope-packet-2026-10-08/report.md#arithmetic-and-raster-limits)
demonstrate that general limitation without establishing a historical error
rate. This update neither repairs nor worsens that limitation by itself.

The immediate next test is attributable human inspection of the proposed local
mappings. A partial review of CE-F7Q1–Q3 is useful; it does not accept the other
39 slots. Corrections must remain alongside
the frozen proposals and trigger the declared versioned regeneration rule,
not convenient replacement points. Uninspected entries stay uninspected;
completed axes/legend and R1 observations need no repeat. Consequential graph
comparisons still require the applicable source/support qualifications. No
force difference, integral, model validation, intent finding or cause ranking
is computed here. The full investigation remains active and incomplete.
