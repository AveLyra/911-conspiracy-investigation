October 7, 2026. Research-only preparation under the unchanged main charter,
parent numerical protocol and human-review boundary. This declaration precedes
the new geometry screen, not prior source viewing or annotation. The readers'
earlier examples, disagreements and synthetic results are known. No blinding
or new historical observation is claimed.

## Decision and scope

Decide what the existing evidence permits for all fourteen force and energy
pairs. For each pair identify actual native footprints versus visual locators,
matches and mismatches to the completed manual-envelope trial, separate bounds
and identity/support assumptions, and one finite next recovery or limiting
result. Keep local readable pieces even when other parts remain unidentified.
Exact original arrays or encoding equality are not universal admission gates.

For F3 only, derive an annotation-geometry eligibility table from each frozen
180-record descending-corridor reading. This answers which saved records meet
a conservative single-fragment rectangle rule. It does not determine whether
the rectangle contains the historical generating curve. No new pixel reading,
annotation repair, physical ordinate, centerline, curve-domain union, discrepancy
or 42-slot selection is part of this screen.

## Eligibility rule

Evaluate every condition independently for each literal record:

1. Status is `identified_local_fragment`.
2. Core cells are nonempty.
3. The union of core and fringe is nonempty and contiguous in row coordinate.
4. There is exactly one explicit, nonblank `fragment_id`. If a membership map
   exists, it has exactly that key and its core/fringe lists equal the record's
   lists. A map is not required when the record supplies a single identifier.
   Multiple pieces, even with a contiguous combined union, remain excluded.
5. Boundary flags are empty. Existing strip and target-column boundary rules
   apply; no boundary continuation is inferred.

Retain all applicable reasons: nonidentified status, empty core, empty outer,
disconnected outer, boundary, missing identifier, multiple fragment members,
or inconsistent single-fragment identity. Empty maps with no cells are normal
empty observations. A fragment union mismatch remains a validation error.

Only an eligible record receives native cell-edge rectangle
`[column, min(outer), column+1, max(outer)+1]`. This is the exact enclosure of
the selected cells, not a statistical or historical source-error bound. Do not
fill holes, join neighboring rectangles, combine readers, or reinterpret
fringe-only/clipped records. Exclusion here does not imply that no other
defensible measurement method could use that observation.

## Acceptance and stopping point

Before the historical screen, test eligible implicit/explicit membership,
negative status, empty and fringe-only sets, disconnected sets, contiguous
multiple pieces, inconsistent identity, strip/target boundaries, malformed
rows/coverage, input preservation and overwrite refusal. Preserve failures.
Run the screen twice with exact byte agreement. Independently reproduce all
360 statuses, reasons and rectangles without importing producer classification.
Per-reader and per-route totals must account for every original record.

Complete the fourteen-pair applicability table and critical review. Record
what the geometry screen changes about F3's readiness, not another accuracy
claim. Finish this bounded decision; do not repeat the synthetic trial or
start another generic calibration cycle. Any later conditional graphical
estimate must explicitly assume a relationship between visible ink and the
underlying curve, retain correlated registration uncertainty, and separately
justify local model identity and support. Human axes/legend checks are complete;
selected curve-to-coordinate checks remain required before consequential use.
Unknown support is not zero. No solver, cause ranking, engine acceptance,
legal promotion, external disclosure, commit or push is authorized here.
