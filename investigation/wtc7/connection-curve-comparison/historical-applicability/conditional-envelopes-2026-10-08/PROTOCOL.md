## Conditional assumptions

Hidentity: the original reader's local style/model attribution is correct.
Hsupport: that fragment's generating curve exists across the entire native
column [x,x+1]. Hink0: its ordinate throughout that column lies inside the
cell-edge rectangle of the selected core plus fringe, with zero additional
unselected-halo allowance. These are assumptions, not findings from agreement.
The straight-line synthetic trial motivates testing this enclosure convention
but does not validate historical curvature, compression or faint edges.

No arbitrary extra pixel radius is assigned. Full cell edges retain source
quantization. Both reader alternatives and both original axis allowances are
reported separately; neither an average nor a union is a confidence interval.
A nonzero halo would require a separately justified sensitivity assumption.
Conditional windows can prepare human review, not silently become support.

## Selection and exclusions

Use the existing single-fragment classifier: identified status, nonempty core,
contiguous core-plus-fringe rows, one explicit consistent fragment identifier,
and no recorded boundary flags. Normalize only the inspected schema variants;
malformed rows or membership unions are errors, not scientific missingness.
Any explicit unassigned-band reference additionally withholds the rectangle
under this conservative rule. It does not prove the other route is unmeasurable.

Exclude a column unless both immediate neighbors in the same reader, route and
strip also satisfy those rules with the same single fragment identifier.
This removes observed ends/interruption boundaries and potential dash caps;
it does not prove Hsupport or bridge a gap. Labels are reader-local, never
matched across readers or strips. No adjacent columns are interpolated.

Apply the composed-page style/key exclusion rectangles from the frozen root
registration and comparison: force [850,225,1280,315] and
[1040,310,1160,470]; energy [470,1055,900,1148] and
[575,1138,695,1302], in continuous render coordinates. These conservatively
contain the explicit white masks. Touching an exclusion is withheld, not

Specifically require the full render rectangle strictly inside every possible
plot interior under both registrations: xlow > max(L), xhigh < min(R),
ylow > max(T), yhigh < min(B). Touching any target edge is also withheld.
The neighboring-column test uses these base exclusions before the end-column
exclusion is applied; it is not an iterative erosion of the entire fragment.

## Coordinate calculation

Use each of the twelve actual positive-axis CTMs from the preserved PDF
representation. Reuse registration_controls.Strip; do not substitute a uniform
stitched y scale. Render coordinates follow the original 1700 by 2200 page:
Xrender = Xpdf/(9/25); Yrender = (792-Ypdf)/(9/25).

Root axis allowances are [i-3,i+4] including the full selected cell. Independent
reader allowances are continuous center +/-6, without another cell expansion.
Keep their separate force and energy registrations. Human anchor/legend
confirmation is retained, not interpreted as acceptance of curve samples or
tighter uncertainty. Registration variables are shared across every column
and both models in that panel; the saved marginal windows are NOT independent
per-column errors.

For horizontal position u and uncertain axis endpoints L,R, displacement is
(8/5)*(u-L)/(R-L). For rendered ordinate v and uncertain T,B, ordinate is
Ymax*(B-v)/(B-T), with Ymax=1,000,000 N or 800,000 N-m. Enumerate all box
vertices using exact decimal-derived Fractions; strictly positive denominators
make coordinate-wise fractional-linear extrema occur at vertices. This returns
marginal hulls, not a polyline or joint independent uncertainty model. Do not
clip results or invent a fixed physical query interval from uncertain x windows.
