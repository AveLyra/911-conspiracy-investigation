# What the published connection curves can support

October 7, 2026. The fourteen-pair applicability decision is **partial local
recovery, not whole-curve numerical admission**. The saved three-bolt force
annotations yield single-fragment cell rectangles eligible under this screen.
Every other pair has identifiable candidates for a finite manual recovery,
but not yet complete manual stroke bounds. Several merged or crossing regions
cannot presently identify both model curves. They remain unknown rather than
being duplicated, interpolated or treated as zero.

This tests the recoverability of a published spring-versus-shell connection
benchmark, not the complete WTC 7 collapse model. Neither good nor poor graphical
agreement establishes the building's temperatures, residual support or cause.

## Assumptions that must remain separate

The [synthetic trial](../manual-envelope-trial/report.md) tested isolated
straight strokes in seven colors under two rendering conditions. Local isolated
historical pieces resemble those targets in limited respects. Historical
curvature, dash ends, same-color contacts, seams, unknown stroke geometry and
different JPEG quantization prevent automatic transfer of its containment
result. Matching encoding is not required for every useful graphical estimate;
the trial simply does not guarantee coverage for this historical image.

**Bounds assumption:** a proposed local estimate must state how selected visible
ink encloses the underlying plotted curve over the full queried column, and
what raster/compression or unselected-fringe allowance is assumed. A rectangle
enclosing selected cells proves only that geometry. Wider bounds must be
justified independently of a desired model difference. Keep native cell extent
and correlated axis registration uncertainty, using the actual strip transforms.

**Identity and support assumption:** local solid/dashed appearance and source
legend must identify the relevant model on that fragment. Neither color,
vertical order, a large error bar nor the continuation of a nearby fragment
resolves a crossing, hidden contribution or physical endpoint. No ordinary dash
gap or strip seam is bridged in the present method. Unknown support is not `L=0`.

These are conditional graphical assumptions, not uncertainty in actual
connection capacity. If local containment cannot be defended, retain an
annotation-only result or report sensitivity to explicit bounds; do not label
it a calibrated historical confidence interval. If identity is unresolved,
withhold the affected comparison regardless of rectangle width.

## F3 geometry result

The [prospective rule](PROTOCOL.md) requires an identified status, nonempty
core, contiguous nonempty outer rows, one consistent explicit fragment and
no strip/target boundary flag. Each reader is screened independently.
Missing optional membership maps are permitted when a single identifier is
explicit; multiple fragments never become one merely because their combined
rows are contiguous. Every excluded record and all applicable reasons remain
in [run01.json](run01.json).

| Frozen reader and route | Records | Eligible rectangles | Excluded here |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Root solid | 90 | 63 | 27 |
| Root dash | 90 | 43 | 47 |
| Separate reader solid | 90 | 61 | 29 |
| Separate reader dash | 90 | 41 | 49 |

This establishes annotation-geometry eligibility only: those specific records meet the declared
single-fragment rectangle rule. It prevents treating all saved annotations
as automatically admissible envelopes. Five root and fourteen separate-reader
records have disconnected outer sets; four and five respectively contain
multiple fragment members. Reasons overlap, so their counts are not added to
estimate excluded records. All 360 literal records remain recoverable.

For example, root's solid-column-300 outer cells are rows 33–36, producing
native rectangle `[300,33,301,37]`. The separate reader selected rows
33,34,35,36,38 at that location. Its missing row 37 is not silently filled;
that record receives no rectangle under this rule. This is a difference in
annotation geometry, not proof that one reader recovered the true curve.

Nonempty-core and boundary exclusions are conservative choices for this
screen. Excluded observations are not universally unusable, and eligible
rectangles are not source-valid measurement bounds. No neighboring rectangles,
readers or separated dashes are joined into physical support.
