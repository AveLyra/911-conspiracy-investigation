## Bounds and identity require different decisions

A selected pixel rectangle encloses selected pixels. It does not necessarily
enclose the plotted mathematical curve. The existing synthetic trial tested
finite straight strokes, not every historical curvature, dash end, contact or
compression condition. Shared-reader agreement does not supply that missing
relationship. F3's previously screened 106 and 102 eligible rectangles remain
annotation geometry only.

This does not make useful approximate graph measurement impossible or require
original arrays for every estimate. A conditional graphical estimate can state
an enclosing-ink assumption and its raster allowance, propagate the existing
correlated registration intervals, and show sensitivity to those assumptions.
Such a result must not be labeled a calibrated confidence interval. The
allowance must be justified without tuning it to obtain a desired difference.

Unknown model identity is a separate limit. Wider bounds cannot recover a
hidden solid trace, allocate a shared band twice, bridge a dash gap or establish
a strip join. Conversely, one unresolved contact does not erase identified
local pieces elsewhere. The source-specific disposition must preserve both.

The strongest objection to withholding all measurements is valid: exact
knowledge of the pre-raster drawing process is not necessary for every useful
bounded estimate. The current finding is narrower—these saved annotations
have not yet supplied and defended that estimate. It is not a claim that
all fourteen curves are unreadable.

## Next measurement decision

The next task is one finite bounds and identity admission decision using the
already saved footprints, not another general calibration cycle or wholesale
pixel census. Apply the existing tested geometry and strip-registration rules;
for each local candidate either justify a conditional graphical envelope or
name the specific reason the available evidence cannot bound it. Keep the
two readers' alternatives and all ordinary gaps, contacts and seams explicit.
Do not automatically turn every eligible cell rectangle into supported curve
length or interpolate between neighboring records.

The outside-target inventory remains a separate coverage obligation. Each
item needs recovered footprints, a source-specific unresolved disposition,
or an explicit pending recovery—not exclusion merely because it was outside
a convenient crop. E3/E4's separate approaches, F5's Im3 descent and F6's
corrected Im1 crest are concrete remaining candidates. Reuse completed regions.

Only after the full supported/unsupported inventory and its assumptions are
frozen should the existing quantile rule select all 42 paired slots. Keep
unavailable and duplicate slots; do not choose clearer replacements after
seeing a discrepancy. The user's axes and legend confirmation remains valid
and should not be requested again. Actual selected curve-coordinate checks
are still required before consequential use.
