The strongest objection to blanket withholding is valid: clearly separated
local strokes need not await an exact reconstruction of every hidden pixel or
universal release of native arrays. Conversely, repeated calculation cannot
turn Hidentity/Hsupport/Hink0 into measured truths. Human mapping review can
test local interpretation; it cannot certify rigorous pre-raster enclosure or
uninspected support. The next method must name its graphical estimand and
assumptions, rather than advertise unknown generating curves as recovered.
