## Conditional common-page geometry, not admitted D

Call the constructed set C_H, not D. It remains conditional on Hidentity,
Hsupport and Hink0 in the existing conditional-envelope protocol. D, accepted
support, quantile targets, model differences and human acceptance remain null
or false. The accepted numerical protocol's single-valued piecewise-linear
branches are not supplied merely by having set-valued rectangles.

Use only rows with `conditional_window=true`. The existing column-end,
identity, band, mask and plot-interior rules stay unchanged. Infer reader role
from its fixed ordered pair in the preserved inventory, not path-name guessing.
Each included row retains reader path, source/target, native column/rectangle,
local fragment ID and exact rendered rectangle. Keep all exclusions by reference
to the old result and explicitly in the two new reading records.

For each pair and all four solid-reader/dash-reader combinations, partition
the common rendered-page x geometry at every eligible cell's exact left/right
edge. Each positive-length open segment records all active solid and dashed
row references. Endpoint touching alone has zero length and is not a usable
comparison interval. Interior cell support is still an assumption. Classify:

- neither candidate present: gap within this candidate extent;
- one style only: no local paired candidate;
- more than one active row for either style: overlapping candidate coverage,
  with uniqueness withheld; not automatically proven physical multivaluedness;
- one row per style but overlapping selected native cells in the same source:
  shared-ink ownership conflict, not validated identical physical curves;
- one per style without that conflict: paired local candidate only.

Do not delete repeated/overlapping coverage or choose the preferred ordinate.
The four reader combinations are sensitivity scenarios, not four independent
observations. Cross-reader scenarios can combine incompatible allocations;
the shared-ink check is mandatory. Two strips may cover the same page x without
requiring a seam join; such overlap must not imply a through-seam trajectory.
Retain provenance and do not match local fragment labels across readers/strips.

Retain all elementary segments. Any grouped paired run may extend only across
adjacent segments with the same source/reader/local-fragment pair. Do not join
gaps, changed fragments or different source regions. Summed candidate length
may be reported, clearly conditional, without being called validated support.

Intersect before uncertain axis mapping. Never intersect marginal physical-x
hulls, which could combine different values of one shared axis. For each of
the two existing axis registrations, a candidate page length S maps to
`(8/5)*S/(R-L)` with common L,R throughout the panel. Retain exact rational
extrema over the stated box and optional descriptive decimal values. Zero C_H
in one reader scenario is not proof of no historical/model support. No model
difference, ordinate ranking, integral, peak, terminal value or quantile target

## Scientific disposition and acceptance

Separate (1) reproduced source/geometry facts, (2) conditional local candidates,
(3) justified supported D and branch/envelope representation, (4) actual human
sample acceptance and (5) engineering/historical interpretation. Never advance
automatically between them. No primary reader is selected because its candidate
coverage is longer. Carry all source-specific alternatives and outside-target
obligations into the report without demanding an endless full-curve census.

Discuss separately local point/envelope comparison, signed/absolute/squared
integrals and maxima over D, each curve's own/common supported peak, local
rightmost-supported comparison, whole-curve peak/terminal/area, cross-panel
work-energy equality and historical causal interpretation. Missing global
coverage does not by itself prohibit a justified *supported-domain* peak or
integral. A candidate cell is not a unique polyline or a physical endpoint.

The unchanged human rule selects 42 paired slots only after an actual support
inventory is justified and frozen. Do not ask again for axes/legends already
confirmed or silently substitute C_H quantiles for actual-D samples. Human
mapping checks cannot prove uninspected support or rigorous pre-raster enclosure.
If a reconstruction/bounding rule needs amendment, state it prospectively
before discrepancy results and keep the earlier contract; no hidden shortcut.

## Verification and stopping rule

Pin all directly used artifacts and existing complete input closures before/
after execution. New F6 rows use unchanged, hash-checked helpers. Preserve old
objects; do not overwrite prior outputs. Test rational partitioning, gaps,
endpoint-only contact, all four role combinations, cross-strip overlap,
duplicate/competing coverage, cross-reader shared-ink allocation, fragment
changes, shared-axis length bounds, invalid values and exclusive creation.
Two saved results must match. A separate implementation must check every new
F6 decision/mapping and every candidate partition/length without importing the
producer's decision/mapping/partition functions. Retain failures and repairs.

Finish with a disposition for every pair, not an automatic additional crop.
Further recovery must name a specific admission uncertainty or metric it can
change. Reproduced geometry alone does not complete this unit or the charter.
No accepted metric, physical validation, ranking change, source/legal promotion,
engine/bridge activation, external transfer, commit or push.
