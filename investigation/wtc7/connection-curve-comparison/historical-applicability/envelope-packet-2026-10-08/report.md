## Arithmetic and raster limits

The new [exact arithmetic](envelope_math.py) has only been exercised on
synthetic inputs. It carries one shared axis state through both models and
all cells, cancels common offsets before bounding, and does not fill gaps.
Signed, absolute and squared integral/mean bounds have distinct meanings;
an energy-panel area has units N-m², not dissipated work. A separately coded
scalar-assignment oracle checks 16 synthetic combinations of panel, domain and
axis conditions. Passing those controls verifies those calculations, not the
existence of the original generating curve inside the graphical envelopes.

The two [raster challenge](raster-challenge01.json) results are byte-identical:
16 declared artificial scenes, two codec settings, 32 queried columns per
scene/codec, 1,024 records. The fixed maximal-nonwhite diagnostic returns:

- 462 enclosures containing the declared synthetic truth;
- one constructed enclosure failure;
- two unsupported full-column proposals;
- 280 disconnected and 279 missing diagnostic outputs.

These are **not error rates for the historical annotations**. Missing cases
include deliberately blank and absent-support controls. Disconnected JPEG ink
does not establish human unreadability. The diagnostic is neither frozen
manual reader nor the historical eligibility rule.

The informative result is an identifiability limit: under this specified
finite renderer, an artificial curve that briefly rises four pixels and a
flat curve produce identical pixels; a curve with a 1/8-column support gap
and a continuous curve also produce identical pixels. All six constructed
pair/codec comparisons preserve equal encoded bytes. The examples are
deliberately constructed, not representative estimates of the historical
renderer or evidence of defects in these published curves.

Thus Hidentity, Hsupport and Hink0 remain assumptions. Human locator agreement
can improve local attribution; it cannot generally prove hidden continuity or
subpixel enclosure. Original exact-polyline, peak, endpoint, full-domain and
outside-target obligations remain open. No historical ordinate discrepancy,
integral, robust sign, peak or cause ranking was computed in this unit.
