## What this establishes and what could defeat it

The positive conditional overlap and recorded disagreements are reproducible
calculations from fixed annotation inputs. They are not new measurements of
connection capacity. The strongest objection is that two internally consistent
readings can still misattribute ink or fail to enclose the curve that generated
the published raster.

The three inherited assumptions remain unvalidated: Hidentity assigns the
local stroke to the correct model; Hsupport assumes its generating curve exists
across the entire native column; Hink0 assumes that curve stays within the
selected core-plus-fringe cell-edge rectangle without an additional halo.
Agreement of readers or arithmetic does not prove these assumptions. Thus C_H
remains conditional coverage, while admitted support D and model-discrepancy
fields remain null and human_accepted remains false.

A source-supported ownership correction, a demonstrated enclosure failure or
a geometry/bookkeeping error could change these conditional results. A native
curve export with clear model identity and quantity definitions, or the required
human sample review plus separately justified support/enclosure validation,
would be a stronger route toward a consequential graphical comparison. Neither
route by itself validates historical connection behavior or global collapse.
