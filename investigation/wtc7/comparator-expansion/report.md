# Fire and demolition comparator assessment

The expanded comparison supports a narrower, more useful conclusion than a visual vote between fire and demolition. Severe office fires can coexist with retained gravity support; a different mixed-frame fire can produce local column, connection and floor failure while substantial structure survives; and a deliberately prepared collapse can be initiated mechanically as well as explosively. None of those observations, by itself, identifies WTC 7's initiating mechanism.

The strongest new limitation concerns the Plasco literature: the two full manuscripts inspected here compute fire development and member heating, not completed global structural collapse. Their titles and publication cross-references must not be allowed to supply the missing mechanical result. The comparison strengthens the requirement for a building-specific explanation; it does not independently validate NIST's sequence or establish deliberate intervention.

This is a research-only documentary and method assessment, not an expert engineering opinion, an exhaustive population study or the final investigation synthesis. Selection and inference rules are in [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md). The existing WTC 7 observations and source audits remain separate; no new acceleration, footprint, acoustic or thermal measurement is claimed here.

**2026-09-09 follow-up:** The [deposited Plasco thesis, Chapter 6](plasco-thesis/report.md) has now been read separately. It supplies a top-eight-floor structural model, computed displacement/rotation, inferred connection failure, an explicit corner-floor-removal stability test and an unresolved upper-floor response mismatch. It does not turn the two thermal manuscripts below into mechanical studies, or the still-unread 2025 journal paper into a reviewed full text. The new chapter-specific result preserves both stable response and calibration limitations; no global solver reproduction or WTC 7 ranking change follows. The following matrix remains the preceding batch's baseline rather than silently absorbing a different source version.

**Subsequent Chapter 7 follow-up:** The [full global-method chapter audit](plasco-thesis/chapter7/report.md) now confirms the upper-eight-floor scope, rigid connections and exclusion of dynamics. The revised southeast-damage calculation prescribes missing floor sections and is expressly limited to failure initiation; video interpretations supply later progression. Successful redistribution and stable responses are retained. This is conditional structural analysis, not an uninterrupted dynamic whole-tower reproduction. Its exact timing labels also require native-array reconciliation. These findings further constrain Plasco's use as a causal control without contradicting its observed fire-associated outcome or establishing an intervention.

## Scientific distinctions that change the evaluation

### Floor deformation versus loss of the supporting system

One Meridian is particularly useful against an overly general fire-collapse story. A proposed chain must explain why deformation would destroy the critical restraints or load paths in the target structure when severe deformation did not inevitably do so elsewhere. Conversely, survival does not establish that the other building had identical connections, load ratios, bracing or exposure. The correct test is whether a proposed model predicts both damaged survival and propagation under independently justified differences, not whether one photograph looks more fiery.

Windsor adds a within-building comparison: local failure and retained structure need not be mutually exclusive. A post-fire damaged connection does not alone tell when it failed, whether it initiated collapse, or whether it failed during cooling or later movement. This matters directly to claims that all connections must either resist continuously or fail simultaneously. The evidence needed is an actual sequence and force/load-path accounting, not a verbal appeal to “coupling.”

### Fire reconstruction versus mechanical reconstruction

The Plasco manuscripts expose the distinction especially clearly. In one study, an alternative fuel arrangement produces greater local heating but fails to spread as required by the observed event. That is a non-spread result, not a no-collapse structural run. In the later fire-reconstruction manuscript, fuel placement and opening/ceiling timings are calibrated from imagery; some floor histories are reused, and actual firefighting is not simulated. The underlying papers and exact pins are retained in the [full-text note](plasco/report.md).

Those choices can generate testable candidate exposures, but the image match is not an independent test of every chosen exposure. The next discriminating question is whether plausible alternative interior histories consistent with the same exterior observations yield materially different structural outcomes. A 2025 structural follow-up has been located, but its full text was inaccessible in this batch; its abstract must not be promoted into inspected methods or a reproduced global-collapse result.

### Motion compatibility versus initiation evidence

The HBI crosswalk now provides affirmative participant documentation of different methods at one site. Its mechanical operation is therefore a usable candidate for later visual comparison without first labeling it from appearance. It does not show that WTC 7 had the same preparation, geometry or failure sequence. Nor does an explosive operation validate a thermal/chemical intervention proposal; those require their own discriminating evidence.

No resemblance percentage, gravity-compatible interval, synchronized blast sequence or footprint measure was computed for these comparator recordings. There is no new empirical basis here for saying their terminal motion matches WTC 7 better than a specified fire pathway. The source-label repair is a prerequisite to that test, not its result.

## Consequence for the WTC 7 assessment

This batch changes the quality and permitted use of the comparator evidence, not the causal ranking. It provides better counterexamples to simplistic inevitability claims and better operation-specific demolition labels. It also narrows how confidently a later fire-associated collapse and its thermal models can be used as a validated precedent. It neither supplies the missing building-specific NIST reproduction nor supplies affirmative WTC 7 intervention evidence.

The full evaluation must still distinguish initiation, support loss, propagation, visible motion and intent. Historical rarity deserves investigation but cannot supply an unmeasured likelihood ratio. Likewise, a published model deserves examination but cannot be its own independent validation. A ranking should change when a discriminating observation or robust mechanism-specific test changes, not merely because the library gains more differently labeled videos.

## Highest-value next tests

1. Obtain the full 2025 Plasco structural follow-up through a usable public route, then check its actual spatial scope, connection/failure criteria, stable alternatives and dependence on the calibrated fire history. Preserve the present abstract-only ceiling until then.
2. Resolve the HBI as-executed structure-to-recording crosswalk and original recording editions before measuring their motion. Add a separately documented top-down removal case; the present lower-section mechanical account does not establish a top-down video control.
3. Seek the original post-fire structural assessments for the survival cases and the Windsor member/sample maps. Require models to explain retained support as well as failure, with stated differences and without post hoc retuning for each outcome.
4. Continue WTC 7 calibrated motion/fire coverage and model-file work under the existing work packages. Comparison does not replace that direct evidence. No new expert engagement, correspondence or paid acquisition is authorized by these next tests.
