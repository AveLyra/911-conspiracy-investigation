# Comparator expansion protocol

Research-only WP4 working protocol under CHARTER.md (supporting item not included in this public package). No canonical source, fact, exhibit or pleading is changed. Declared 2026-09-08 before cross-case scoring or new quantitative video measurement; case selection and some source reading already occurred. This is an exploratory expansion, not retroactive preregistration.

## Selection and unit of comparison

The current bounded cohort deliberately spans outcomes: One Meridian Plaza (1991) and First Interstate Los Angeles (1988) for steel high-rise fire survival; Windsor (2005) for mixed-frame partial collapse; Plasco (2017) for fire-associated total collapse; the existing Capital One/Hertz (2024) lead for a prepared high-rise explosive demolition; and HBI/Boodarie for separately identified mechanical, cut-and-pull and explosive industrial operations. Selection is purposive and historically informed, not random, exhaustive or matched by collapse appearance. Surviving fire-exposed cases must remain in the report.

An event is not a paper, camera or repost. An operation is not an entire demolition project. HBI's operations remain distinct within one site/source family. Different Plasco analyses are competing/revised analyses of one event, not additional examples. Documentary attribution of a method, visible outcome, and demonstrated causal sequence receive separate labels.

## Required comparison fields

For each case record: structural system, primary load paths, height/storeys with conflicting conventions retained, floor construction, fire protection, occupancy/loading, pre-existing damage, fire extent and its observation basis, local exposure versus total incident duration, suppression, intentional preparation, outcome, intermediate failure sequence, source/edition and dependence, recording generation/clock/coverage, and model scope. Unknown is an allowed value; never fill it from appearance or another component's dimensions.

Use participant/investigator records and original engineering research for technical claims. Public mirrors may preserve the same report but are not independent corroboration. Preserve acquisition failures and explicit exclusions. Do not bypass safety refusals or acquire/pay for private records. No new remote video download is authorized merely to overcome display limitations.

## Measurement and inference gates

This batch establishes documentary labels and comparability, not new acceleration, blast or footprint measurements. Later video analysis requires a versioned selection of features/intervals, original/edit/cadence limits, calibrated geometry, uncertainty and event-level independence. No event receives a cause label from the motion that a classifier would later be asked to classify.

Compare narrow features, not an overall visual resemblance score: localized failure versus system survival; fire-to-member exposure versus incident length; preparation versus spontaneous support loss; partial versus whole-building motion; observable timing versus hidden sequence. No numerical causal ranking, likelihood ratio or base rate follows from this cohort. A similar outcome is not a validated identical mechanism; a different outcome is not a falsification without relevant matching.
