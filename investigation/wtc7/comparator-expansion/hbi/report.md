# HBI operation and video provenance

Research-only WP4 follow-up, 2026-09-08. This note supplements the primary-source pilot (supporting item not included in this public package) under the investigation charter (supporting item not included in this public package). It creates no canonical case facts or engineering findings.

The HBI structure-to-method gap is substantially narrowed. Liberty Industrial's published project-video description explicitly distinguishes the briquette, reactor, and gas-plant operations. A preserved DERT capability statement supplies the same crosswalk and links to the same project video. A specifically named briquette-drop recording is also linked from the participating engineer's project page. These are participant-origin records; their repetition is not independent corroboration or an engineering validation.

## Declared scope and selection

This follow-up selected the existing DEM-07 lead before inspecting new recordings. Its targets were the named HBI operations and their primary publishing links. No additional nonexplosive event was selected because a usable HBI crosswalk was found. This is an exploratory provenance repair, not a representative demolition sample or a preregistered motion study. The existing manifest was read only at DEM-07 and was not changed.

No video files were downloaded, no motion or audio measurements were made, and no clip was labeled by its collapse appearance. The browser was used to verify public player titles, publisher names and destination IDs. Brief playback established player availability; it did not constitute a complete recording or edit-chain audit.

No acoustic absence, apparent free fall, symmetry, dust pattern or software animation was used as a method discriminator.

## Dates and contrary evidence

HBI-2 states completed in 2011 and separately lists award shortlisting in 2012 and 2013. HBI-4 p.15 instead gives April 2011 as start and April 2012 as finish. These are genuinely different completion statements; the present evidence does not establish whether they refer to individual falls, main-stage clearance, or a larger contract. Preserve both claims with their sources. An award year is not an event date, and the earlier DEM-07 value `2012 project` remains too ambiguous. No day-level date is assigned to any clip.

## Permitted use and next missing record

The briquette upper-section operation is now a useful documentary example of a deliberately prepared, mechanically initiated industrial collapse. Its specifically titled contractor recording is a candidate for future motion inspection with an explicit provenance ceiling. It is not a matched WTC 7 control: its preparation, industrial geometry, load paths and recording conditions differ or remain unknown. It supplies no numerical base rate, likelihood ratio, causal ranking or proof that one visible motion implies a unique initiation mechanism.

The highest-value next record is a dated, structure-ID execution crosswalk tying each fall and camera original to the preparation/as-executed record. For the briquette case it should resolve the upper/lower sequence and original clip identity; for the reactor it should explicitly connect the named operation to `zhrtQbCeOp8`; for the gas plant it should name the individual components. Detailed geometry, loading and calibrated camera information would then be prerequisites for a physical comparison. Such records were not located in this bounded public search; outreach or private access was not attempted.

Grades follow the evidence audit convention: A directly established; B strongly supported; C materially assumption-dependent; D underdetermined; E unsupported or contradicted. Documentary claims were rechecked, but no structural simulation was replicated and no independent engineering test was performed.
