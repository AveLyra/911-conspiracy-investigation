## Source-family review

The survival note correctly groups the heavy LAFD-to-USFA dependence, allows claim-specific partial independence for the NFPA/ICBO field contribution, and distinguishes Nelson's original calculations from independent historical observations. The One Meridian mirror/reissue is not represented as original 1991 publication bytes. These are useful evidence sources, but their number is not a number of independent structural tests.

The Plasco note correctly treats the two manuscripts, institutional mirrors and shared incident imagery as a related research programme. Original modeling work can contribute different tests without creating a new incident dataset. The missing TFSD/interview/image records, version discrepancies and unacquired final structural paper remain consequential. The report does not promote a secondary citation chain into directly inspected national-investigation evidence.

The most serious overclaims to guard against during compression into the synthesis are: (1) turning the survival pair into a claim that fire cannot cause global steel-frame collapse; (2) treating unspecified suppression, protection and restraint as equivalent across buildings; (3) calling P5's non-spread a global non-collapse result; and (4) treating P6's calibrated thermal histories or P7's abstract as a completed, independently validated global-collapse mechanism. The reviewed case notes already avoid all four.
