# Methods and source identities

This bounded account of the October 15, 2026 municipal review preserves
substantive findings, adverse evidence, source pinpoints, review coverage and
unsuccessful searches. It is not a lossless substitute for the private working
record. Local paths, chat receipts, private correspondence, personal contact details
and internal operational records are excluded. No original source was changed.

## Reading and reconciliation

Eight held municipal PDFs contain 50 pages. All were visually read by the lead
reviewer and assigned primary/rotated AI readers using existing 150-dpi renders,
without fresh OCR, enhancement or cropping. Prior context was known; this was
not blinded review. Multiple readings of shared copies are not independent
historical witnesses or human/expert acceptance. Frozen first notes and source
PDFs were retained; reconciliation was additive.

Material corrections keep gone PCs distinct from former-employee accounts,
preserve uncertain control IDs, decline ambiguous handwritten expansions,
distinguish proposed actions from implementation, and treat blanks as unknowns.

A subsequent seven-PDF acquisition contains 50 pages: two official audits,
two public City affirmations and three mirror-hosted archiving documents.
Nineteen selected pages were visually read by the lead and separate readers,
including all seven archiving pages and both affirmations. The 39 audit pages
were not all reviewed. The affirmations are party-hosted public copies, not
fresh clerk-authenticated downloads.

The 113-page deposition was acquired from its exact public link after a header/
size check. Acquisition began 2026-10-15T20:16:48Z: HTTP 200, 14,685,906 bytes,
application/pdf, zero redirects. Native Poppler text extraction was screened
for preservation, salvage, archive, recovery and search terms. The lead read
full images of pages 1–2, 75–81 and 90–93; a separate reader checked the same
11 substantive pages. This is not a complete 113-page substantive review.

One counterreader initially missed the answer at 91:15 and called the question
unanswered. The lead challenged that against the image/native text; the error
and page 77 pinpoints were corrected with an explicit retained correction
history. The source did not change. The findings retain the actual answer.
