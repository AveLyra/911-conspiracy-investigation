# Collapse-warning source-chain audit protocol

Version 1, 2026-09-10. Research-only WP2/Q07 and WP5/WI-01–02. Frozen before new public retrieval or annotation in this branch. The investigation charter, AGENTS.md, WORKFLOW.md and START-HERE.md control scope, preservation and privacy; no litigation or case-state promotion occurs.

## Question and prior knowledge

Can original public testimony, contemporaneous broadcast text and accessible archival interview records identify an observation-to-warning-to-broadcast chain for WTC 7? Distinguish observation, interpretation of risk, withdrawal decision, warning transmission, newsroom receipt and on-air wording. Earlier research already identifies Peter Hayden's Firehouse interview, CNN segment 74 and a NARA Catalano interview locator. Those memos are leads, not independent evidence. Previously viewed/quoted footage and NIST clocks are disclosed dependencies, not blind validation.

This pass addresses warning-source provenance, not measured collapse motion, sound identification, structural causation, a conspiracy verdict or legal advice. Keep precise advance knowledge, broad risk warnings and premature reports of completed collapse distinct. A later first-person recollection is not a contemporaneous instrument log; several witnesses may share one command briefing. Contradictions and ordinary explanations must both remain.

## Declared source coverage

Start with the actual public source URLs in `research/wtc7-operational-evidence-plan.md` and `research/wtc7-video-comparison/collapse-warning-and-premature-reporting.md`: Firehouse Hayden interview, CNN September 11 segment 74, NARA MFR index and NAID 2609722. Inspect only linked or independently located task-relevant originals. Use at most eight new focused queries for the underlying Hayden/transit/warning or specific Catalano record. Do not broadly search private people, addresses, relationships or financial histories. Preserve query and access failures; no exhaustive-archive or absence claim.

Prefer official original interview/transcript or contemporaneous source records. Recheck full relevant context, including interview date, speaker/role, question wording, creation versus event time, page headings and caveats. A publisher's page is a preserved account, not independent authentication of everything it reports. Where a later account credits a wire service, seek the actual cited bulletin rather than count the later credit as the bulletin itself.

Do not reacquire or redownload the existing long BBC/CNN access copies in this source-chain pass. Their actual clock calibration and audio provenance remain separate unfinished measurements. No audio listening/transcription or precise historic timestamp is claimed without actual inspection. No new broad media pipeline or solver work.

## Acquisition and checks

Preserve successful original response bodies unchanged, exact requested/final URL, capture date, status, bytes/hash and source edition. Keep failed bodies/diagnostics separate from accepted files. Store any raw response headers locally only; do not print session/cookie fields or clear them for export based on hashing. A tool-readable page without retained origin bytes is navigation/read evidence, not a preserved download. An item index is not the item or its attachments.

For PDFs, preserve original bytes; text extraction is navigation, and relevant complete pages must be visually inspected with captions/footnotes/qualification. Record actual page and media coverage, not merely file existence. Derivatives remain separately named and hashed; no source repair, reconstruction or replacement. Proportional source/hash/link checks and a separate critical review are required before integrating material claims.

An explicit unsafe/nonretryable or access-denied route stops. Do not use alternate domains, browser/curl routes, identities, credentials or transformed URLs to bypass it. Ordinary transient DNS/cache errors may receive a normal approved public acquisition attempt. No outreach, expense, account, access request, publication, external upload, feedback send, source promotion, commit or push. Existing archives, legal spines, frozen methods and Sherlock/Faraday state remain untouched.

## Result and stopping rule

Deliver an actual source/coverage log, claim-level warning/chronology ledger, source-linked report, validation and the nearest missing record capable of changing each conclusion. Separate document observation (A) from attributed historical account (usually B/C) and an unverified source-chain join (D). General warning does not establish precise foreknowledge; missing recorded instructions do not establish that none existed. No numerical probability or ranking change without a discriminating result.

Stop at the eight-query ceiling or earlier when new results are repetitive, denied or outside the narrow record families. A newly accessible exact original can warrant a declared additive follow-up, not an unlogged expansion. Root owns this directory and integration; parallel agents have separate Peskin-clock and W14B-request branches. Deduplicate any generic workflow pain point locally; the designated Sherlock feedback task remains archived pending routing.
