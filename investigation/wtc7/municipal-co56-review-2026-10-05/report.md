# Change Order 56 and the missing structural plan

**Change Order 56 does not supply the missing structural-plan link.** It is a
January 22, 1999 request for approval of electrical work at a first-floor fiber
rack, not a supplied framing plan or structural-revision record. Coring and
patching are included, but neither their geometry nor an affected structural
member is identified. This is a scoped negative result, not proof the work

## What the source says

The complete one-page [public archive letter](NYC-WTC_000168526.pdf), Bates
168526, is from Silverstein Properties to DCAS. Its multi-floor OEM project
subject names the first, seventh and twenty-third floors; its specific scope
is an outlet on a fiber rack **at column 42 on the first floor**, with conduit
from **panel ELP-76**. It attributes the scope to Cosentini's **January 18, 1999
memo** and requests approval for an Ambassador Construction change order of
**$19,242**. The letter date, memo date and unspecified performance date must
not be conflated.

The page specifies a double-duplex NEMA 5-20R outlet, conductors/ground and a
one-inch conduit, and explicitly includes coring and patching. It does not
say column 42 itself was cut or cored, locate a penetration within a member,
supply a penetration size, or connect the work to the earlier notch sketch.
Neither the referenced memo nor the stated attachment appears as a separate
page in this admitted PDF. Their absence here is not archive-wide absence.

The printed costs are $14,600 electrical work plus $2,000 patching allowance,
then $1,328 general conditions, $1,076 overhead/fee and $238 insurance. The
subtotal and $19,242 total add correctly. That arithmetic neither verifies
price reasonableness nor establishes payment or construction. The closing asks
for signature/return if approved; no recipient approval, installation
certification or field inspection is supplied by this page.
