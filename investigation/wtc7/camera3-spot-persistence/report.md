## What could make the interpretation wrong?

The narrow finding concerns the selected derivative images, not what emitted
light in the scene. A persistent window/panel pattern, reflection, variable
illumination or persistent/propagated processing artifact could all produce
this appearance. None is established as the physical explanation. A shorter
brightness change superposed on a persistent feature could remain unresolved;
constant intensity was not measured. C could also conceal a changing constituent
inside a persistently pale group. These are unexcluded alternatives, not findings
that such changes occurred. Other times, views and flashes were not tested.

The result therefore removes neither deliberate intervention nor fire from
consideration. It supplies no charge identification, acoustic conclusion or
full-video nondetection. The [charter-wide assessment](../completion-audit-2026-10-04/report.md)
remains the broader evaluation; this small source test cannot settle its
mechanical and evidentiary disputes.
