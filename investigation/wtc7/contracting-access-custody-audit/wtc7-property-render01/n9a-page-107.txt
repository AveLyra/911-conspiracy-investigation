Temperature Application 
As shown in Figure 3-48, temperatures were applied to the model slowly and smoothly. Temperatures 
applied in the global model analysis were Case B temperatures at 4.0 hours (NCSTAR NIST 1-9,  
Section 10.3.3). Elevated temperatures occurred between Floors 7 and 14. Temperature profiles were 
mapped onto the LS-DYNA model as nodal properties and followed the prescribed time-dependent load 
curve shown in Figure 3-48. The resulting temperatures applied to the model are shown in Figure 3-55 
and Figure 3-56.
