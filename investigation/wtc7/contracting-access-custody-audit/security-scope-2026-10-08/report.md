# What the security contractor filings establish

October 8, 2026. Working research for charter Q09/WP5; not a legal fact,
expert opinion, or attribution of wrongdoing. Review status is recorded in
[review.md](review.md); preservation and coverage limits are in
[execution.md](execution.md).

The inspected Stratesec filings identify a World Trade Center project, not a
building-specific WTC7 assignment. They support a company-reported project
closeout and a historical client relationship. They establish neither continuous
control of WTC7 security through September 2001 nor the termination of every
possible service or access arrangement. This bounds the existing security-scope
lead; it does not decide a collapse mechanism.

The general services descriptions are broader than guarding or CCTV alone,
but cannot be assigned wholesale to this project. None of the reviewed passages
establishes structural work, unrestricted building control, unauthorized access,
or support removal. The later historical listing and earlier closeout are not
inherently contradictory: both could be accurate, with or without subsequent
maintenance. The record does not select between those alternatives.

## Disposition and next discriminating record

Disposition: **company-reported complex-level work is supported within a
representation limit; WTC7 scope and continuing access remain unresolved**.
No actor, unauthorized operation, concealment, or causal ranking is established.
The three filings are one issuer's reporting family, not three independent
witnesses. A separate AI reading of the same projections is interpretation
review, not an independent acquisition or forensic authentication.

The next useful record for this lead is the original project scope and
building schedule, linked to amendments, closeout acceptance, and any subsequent
service agreement. Access histories would answer a different question from
contract status. Their existence, accessibility and custody have not been
established here. This finite filing pass stops; no further search, outreach,
request, or investigation of associated people follows automatically.

Original source preservation remains incomplete: the saved HTTP responses are
403 error pages, while the usable content is a partial reader projection saved
after first inspection. Do not relabel either as original filing bytes or treat
this bounded result as completion of WP5 or the full investigation.
