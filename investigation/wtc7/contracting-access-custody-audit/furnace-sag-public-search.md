### Limited finding from the complete reviewed sections

The minutes describe structural-model development and validation, and report
roughly 32 inches of sag in a model discussion. Separately, they describe
four prepared floor-test specimens and testing expected in August. They also
describe future use of visual observations to validate calculations. These
are meeting summaries, not a verbatim transcript. Thus the June statement
does not itself establish comparison against the later furnace measurements.
It does preserve evidence that structural sag was being modeled before the
tests; it is not evidence of no structural modeling.

Within these sections, the validation reference is not joined to named
furnace specimens, measured displacement channels, thermal histories,
residuals or tuning chronology. This does not prove that later comparisons
were absent. The minutes' closing comment also reverses the directional
wording used in the preceding temperature question; no scientific inference
is drawn from that inconsistency without the underlying presentation.
[Source: June 2004 minutes](https://www.nist.gov/el/ncst-advisory-committee-mtg-minutes-june-22-23-2004).
