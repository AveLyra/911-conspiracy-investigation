**Printed 35 / physical 79.** Table 2-1 distinguishes the composite-floor D739 rating (1/2 in. beams and 3/8 in. deck, with material/member/slab qualifications) from column X704 (7/8 in., W14x228 or larger). This table is more specific than the preceding narrative's generic D739 reference; it must not be flattened into one identical rating basis for all components.
