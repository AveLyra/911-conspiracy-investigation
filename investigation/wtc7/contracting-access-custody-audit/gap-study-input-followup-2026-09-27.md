### Strongest competing reading and discriminating evidence

NIST did not simply overlook uneven coating: these materials openly discuss
it, demonstrate heating consequences and offer a response-based approximation.
An abbreviated presentation need not carry every implementation detail; the
normal/lognormal mismatch may be a reporting error without an executed-model
error. The recorded restraint plan may have been followed by work not reached
in this search. These are material reasons **not** to convert this audit into
proof of wrong outputs or intentional concealment.

Conversely, recognition and a plan are not validation. To establish the exact
calculation, obtain case-linked generator/distribution parameters, seed or
realized thickness arrays, tail/zero handling, smoothing implementation,
mesh/material/exposure definitions and spatial output histories. To establish
the broader response transfer, obtain the follow-through under restraint and
the mapping to the relevant WTC7 member/connection, including force, deformation
and failure comparisons. A reproducible connection/system insensitivity result
over justified defect patterns would weaken the applicability criticism.
