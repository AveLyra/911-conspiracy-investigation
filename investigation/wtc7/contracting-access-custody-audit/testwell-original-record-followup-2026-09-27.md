### A material countercheck discovered in the locator pass

FAQ23 cites two analyses beyond the specific NCSTAR1-9 examples previously
reviewed: NCSTAR1-6A Chapter5 on thickness variability for the towers' trusses,
and NCSTAR1-6 Chapter2 on a steel plate with gaps in its protection. The latter
is described as finding little thermal effect from occasional gaps. These are
NIST's summaries of cited studies, not independently verified model results.

This matters because a broad claim that localized gaps were never studied
would omit an explicit cited counterexample. It does **not** establish that
the cited gap dimensions, plate geometry, exposure, response metric or boundary
conditions represent the1997 WTC7 deficiency sites. Nor does a component's
thermal response establish a global structural outcome. The prior crosswalk's
narrower statement about its selected uniform-thickness/bare-member examples
remains correct, but must not be extended to every NIST analysis.

The next useful scientific step is therefore a bounded source audit of those
two cited studies and their claimed applicability to WTC7, not another loop
through the same Testwell queries. Original measurements remain a specific
documentary dependency that could be reopened by an actual new catalog or
accession lead. Acquisition of1986 records would still not prove2001 condition.
