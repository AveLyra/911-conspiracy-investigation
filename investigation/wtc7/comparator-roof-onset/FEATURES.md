## Separate event definitions

**L: localized appearance-change detection.** Record a new localized facade/
edge configuration or outgrowth relative to the opening state, distinguishable
from a global camera shift, simple compression shimmer or an already missing
window. Confirm on at least three consecutive inspected native frames. Record
its location and morphology neutrally (e.g., pale outgrowth, altered edge),
not as a charge, column cut or support-removal time. An ambiguous candidate
does not become an unchanged/negative frame. This is a first recognized event
in the stated coverage, not proof that no earlier smaller event occurred.

**R1–R3: sustained downward image-motion detection.** Track the same material
corner through its neighboring geometry. Confirm a change over at least three
consecutive native frames. For any numerical detection bracket, record the
reader's subjective inclusive y-placement envelope at the baseline, comparison
anchor and confirming frames for the R feature and both B1/B2 references.
Do not call these statistical confidence intervals. Enlarge an envelope or
declare unlocalizable when detail/identity is ambiguous; never fill a missing
coordinate by interpolation. Integer pixel bounds are adequate here; no
subpixel precision is supported by visual placement.

For each usable reference separately, form the exact interval for change in
`R_y - B_y` from the baseline. Positive downward detection requires that the
**entire displacement interval exceeds zero for both references** and remains
so over three consecutive localizable frames. If references disagree or are
unlocalizable, record apparent image motion only; no camera-corrected event
is established. These checks test a simple common image-translation alternative,
not a full rotation/zoom/parallax correction. Any visible rotation, scale
change or reference instability leaves a stronger structural-onset claim
unresolved. A persistent positive is a finite-resolution detection, not proof
of the first physical movement or exact onset of acceleration.

The last comparison anchor without demonstrated positive detection is not
necessarily physically stationary. If its placement interval permits positive
motion, call that ambiguity, not a negative. Report a positive-by bound and
the earlier uncertainty region rather than inventing a narrow onset interval.
This condition can prevent a strict localized-change/roof-onset ordering even
when the gross visual sequence looks clear. It must not be weakened afterward.

## Agreement and acceptance

Each reader freezes the coarse selection before refinement and final notes
before new findings are exchanged. Each records actual overview/native indices,
repeats, failed displays and unresolved categories. A shared source or matching
bracket is not independent historical corroboration. Separate observations,
calculations, uncertainty and interpretation in the result table.

Use nonoverlapping uncertainty-qualified brackets only for strict detected-event
ordering. If physical onset can lie within overlapping ambiguity, say so. Do
not claim simultaneous loss of support, universal demolition behavior, measured
symmetry/rigidity, real-world timing, free fall or WTC7 causation. Agreement or
a successful extraction cannot supply absent source authenticity, physical
calibration, qualified human review or a matched structure.
