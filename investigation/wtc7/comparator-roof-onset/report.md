## Documentary label and comparison limits

The [separate source review](source-label-review.md) supports the label
**Astro95Media access copy attributed to the Capital One/Hertz explosive-
demolition project; original-camera and capture-clock authentication
unestablished**. The saved description says September7,2024, while the title
says September9. That conflict is preserved, not silently corrected. The
method label comes from documentary attribution, not the observed morphology.
The earlier city source is advance planning; contractor completion attribution
remains inherited indexed text, not newly verified original page bytes.
Video/audio from one platform item are one source family.

The strongest relevant objection to generalizing is substantial: this is one
prepared demolition, with its own structure, preparation and source chain,
not a matched untreated steel-office-building experiment. It can constrain
claims about what this demolition looks like; it cannot establish a universal
signature, a demolition/fire likelihood ratio, or a numerical base rate.
Uneven progressive visible changes can occur in an attributed deliberate
demolition; they therefore must not automatically be labeled fire-specific.
Conversely, resemblance to a demolition is not identification of the cause of
another collapse. Neither observation resolves the disputed WTC7 initiation
and force-history questions.
