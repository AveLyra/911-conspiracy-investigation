## Recording rules and observations

The JSON has exactly **34 frame-major rows**, T1 then T2 per frame. Coordinates are native integer pixels. Positive halfwidths are subjective envelopes for the plausible **center location**, not the visible feature extent or statistical confidence intervals. The baseline centers were estimated again visually, with the shared proposal already known rather than treated as exact truth.

Localization and correspondence are separate. A localized corner is not automatically the continuing material point. The final rows contain 23 localized, 1 ambiguous and 10 unavailable localizations; correspondence is appearance-consistent for 22, uncertain for 2, and not assessable for 10. None is labeled changed: lack of a confidently continuing feature was retained without claiming a particular replacement identity.

In f7081 and f7104 it cannot be identified through smoke/foreground overlap; nearby nearer-building corners are not substituted.
- T2's distinct raised-outline upper endpoint remains appearance-consistent through f6931. - Every unavailable row was checked against the full native image, not inferred from crop absence alone. Ambiguity and unavailability describe observable identification limits, not destruction, support failure, or the reason an image feature disappeared.

Detailed per-row notes in the JSON preserve appearance qualifications and failures. These sampled observations do not establish uninterrupted material continuity, the earliest physical motion, whole-building symmetry or a physical trajectory.
