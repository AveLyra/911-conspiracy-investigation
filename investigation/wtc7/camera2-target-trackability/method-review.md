## Prospective questions for the protocol

These are pre-protocol requirements/questions, not claims that an unseen protocol violates them. Final dispositions must cite the actual protocol version.

1. **Define the observable before following it.** Each accepted baseline target needs a unique ID, source-pixel convention, semantic/geometric localization rule and explicit type: proposed persistent material feature, appearance-defined intensity/texture feature, or changing silhouette/outline. A precisely localized visible edge can cease to identify the same material point. Temporal smoothness, expected collapse direction or agreement between annotators cannot supply missing identity evidence.
2. **Separate localization from identity and visibility.** A row may be visually sharp but ambiguous as to identity, or unavailable through occlusion. Preserve such rows without a forced center, a zero displacement, last-value carry-forward or substitution of the nearest convenient edge. Specify what counts as the same feature after disappearance; do not silently join a reacquired lookalike into a continuous track. Record the strongest plausible competing identity/visibility explanation where material.
3. **Make annotation independence exact.** Freeze the target definitions, selected frames, annotation rules and each annotator's access boundary before new coordinates. Two computational reviewers share source imagery, instructions and prior event familiarity; they are not independent historical witnesses or human reviewers. A shared baseline seed is construction, not independently measured baseline agreement. Either independently localize the baseline or explicitly exclude shared-seed agreement from the validation claim. Do not expose one annotator's new coordinates/crops with markers to the other before both outputs are frozen.
4. **Keep complete denominators and uncertainty visible.** With 17 fixed frames and N accepted targets, require 17×N expected rows per annotator, including ambiguous/unavailable outcomes. Report center differences, each envelope's width, rectangle overlap/disjointness and status/identity disagreement, not only an overlap pass count. Subjective rectangles are not confidence intervals, standard errors or probabilities. Broad envelopes can overlap while providing little useful positional constraint; agreement alone cannot certify trackability.
5. **Limit temporal statements to observed sampling.** Seventeen selected images cannot establish continuity between them, an exact onset, monotonic material motion, or a visibility-independent event-time bound. Disappearance can arise from occlusion or loss of appearance; it is not automatically physical loss of the feature. Any displacement interval requires the same defined observable at both endpoints and remains conditional on that identity assessment. No velocity, acceleration, gravitational or causal inference is licensed here.
6. **Declare what reference-model sensitivity means.** The frozen maps take baseline reference coordinates p to current coordinates q=A p+b. Mapping an observed current target q into baseline image coordinates requires A⁻¹(q−b), not subtracting b alone. Evaluating A p+b at a fixed baseline point is a different legitimate sensitivity quantity and must be named separately. Preserve raw coordinates; include each declared template/model's admission/rejection and finite/invertibility/conditioning state without selecting a favorable model post hoc. The old exact-cutoff rejection remains rejected.
7. **Carry rectangles and spatial support honestly.** For an inverse affine map, transform all four rectangle corners and retain the resulting polygon or explicitly labeled axis-aligned enclosing rectangle. Center-only mapping with unchanged halfwidths fails under rotation/shear. Such propagation covers only the supplied annotation rectangle under the chosen map, not source/geometry/model uncertainty. Flag whether targets lie outside the reference-point convex hull: extrapolation and unvalidated depth-plane transfer remain material even for a small apparent correction. Model spread is sensitivity among those maps, not a calibrated camera-error bound.
8. **Keep the scientific disposition separate from pipeline completion.** A complete, reproducible table can validly conclude that a feature is only an outline observable, materially disputed, too broad to constrain motion, or unavailable over part of the sample. Report that consequence explicitly rather than assigning a blanket scientific pass to a successful script.

The following precise cautions were returned to root before annotation/calculation:

- **Envelope semantics:** give both annotators the same explicit instruction that an envelope bounds plausible locations of the *defined point/center*, not the visible spatial extent of the roof element or patch. Feature size and localization uncertainty are different quantities. Rectangle overlap should never be an invitation to broaden the envelopes post hoc.
- **Downward interval predicate:** because image y increases downward, definite sampled downward displacement requires the interval's lower y endpoint to be strictly greater than zero. An interval wholly below zero also excludes zero but indicates upward displacement. Preserve inclusive contact at zero as unresolved; do not declare downward evidence from center motion alone.
- **Presentation coverage:** the fixed crop is a viewing aid, not the available field of view. A point outside `[270,95,475,400)` may still be visible in the full native image. Do not mark it unavailable based solely on the cropped display. Frame coverage/occlusion statements must distinguish which surface was actually inspected.
- **Spatial and inverse sensitivity:** passing the old fit screens does not bound inverse-map amplification. Record the fitted matrix's conditioning/smallest scale separately from the source-design conditioning, and identify targets outside the reference-point convex hull. A near-identity map transferred from nearby building references can still be an unvalidated extrapolation across depth. Transformed rectangle corners or their enclosing boxes carry only the supplied annotation extent under the fixed map, not a complete registration/model error interval.
- **Limited target spread:** if only two proposed points on the same upper-outline side are accepted, retain that restricted spatial scope explicitly. It cannot support a whole-façade, whole-roof, rigid-body tilt or symmetry-of-support-loss conclusion. The rejected/unsupported third region must remain visible in the selection record rather than disappear from the narrative.
