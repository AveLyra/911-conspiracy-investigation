## Disposition of the report's further-loss assertion

The selected text makes a definite historical claim: Figure 5-143 comes from
a clip recorded roughly one minute after the Figure 5-142 clip, and window
8-42C lost additional glass between their represented frames. It is not a
claim that every aperture became fully open. The captions also disclose
intensity adjustments and give overlapping absolute time ranges.

This reading supports a useful aperture-location result: under the report's
north-facade naming scheme and the stated eighth-floor join, C is the first
central-band aperture after the pale pier, not the conspicuous B remnant-like
feature in the broader 143-N8 region. Its counterpart is present but clipped
at the right of Figure 5-143 and every selected Clip 3 candidate. It is not
wholly absent from the close-up. Both compositions therefore permit a bounded
same-slot comparison, with poorer full-perimeter coverage in the close-up.

The pictures contain differing tones and irregular appearances, but I cannot
identify the same physical glass piece in the wide image and demonstrate its
absence in the close-up. Small/soft detail, incomplete close-up coverage,
view geometry, light/reflection, interior or background material, veiling and
source processing remain relevant alternatives to treating a contrast change
as lost glass. Those alternatives do not prove that no glass was lost.

**Disposition: the further-loss assertion is not independently established or
falsified by this finite reading.** Treating the pale B feature as a direct
test of the assertion about C would be an aperture-identity error. Correcting
that possible substitution removes that line of criticism; it does not
authenticate the report's transition. The prior whole-clip counter conflict
also remains conditional: these images do not supply original filming order.

Evidence that would change this result includes a supported contradictory
floor/bay mapping, a corresponding uncropped original exposure resolving C's
material/perimeter, or paired recording/edit/still records plus visible
tracking of an identified glass boundary. The existing window-condition
worksheet and native model opening/removal record remain separate dependencies;
neither the diagram nor this visual location assigns their definitions or
values. No heating, structural consequence, intention or collapse-cause ranking
is calculated or inferred.
