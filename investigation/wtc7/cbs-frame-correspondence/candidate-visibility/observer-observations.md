resolved material discriminator. It does not prove that the relevant units are
open, closed, intact, broken or absent. A more precisely authenticated target
mapping with resolved material/background geometry could change that limit;

specific observable cues that support a conditional appearance comparison, not

belongs to a background, frame, non-glass panel or another target would defeat
the glass interpretation without defeating observation of the contrast edge.

discriminating. Nothing here establishes such evidence.
