# NIST timing for the paired late fire images is partly inferential

2026-10-04. Research only. **NIST does not present Figures 5-141–143 as three
independently timestamped observations.** Its published chain uses comparisons
of windows/fire distributions, assumed news-crew travel, and stated relative
clip separations. The held attribution and access-copy records do not supply
the paired original/edit timing join needed to resolve the reverse tape-label
order found in the [previous check](../sibling-lineage/report.md).

This is a specific verification vulnerability, not proof of false timing or
deliberate alteration. It limits how independently this image sequence can
validate time-dependent fire/window behavior. It does not remove the images'
spatial evidence or establish a different collapse cause.

## Effect on the ordering conflict and physical inferences

The reverse label order remains real for the pinned access copies. Under the
within-clip associations and an order-preserving recording-time mapping, every
Clip3 exposure precedes every Clip7 exposure. A neighboring-frame choice cannot
fix that sign. The current primary-source review neither supplies that mapping
nor proves a particular reordering. Incorrect source association, copied or
edited-master labels, and a publication timing mistake remain alternatives.

NIST's maps5-172 and5-173, printed254–255 / PDF298–299, explicitly derive from
5-141/5-142 and their clips, respectively. Their rounded labels around3:57 and
4:00 do not furnish two more clocks or an independent replication of the
chronology. Nor does a derived map independently corroborate its source image.

The resulting evidentiary rule is narrow but important:

- An image may support a location-qualified visible fire/window observation
  without an exact exposure time. This unit does not reclassify those pixels.
- Window/fire appearance used to infer time must not then be counted as an
  independent validation of that same time-dependent window/fire trajectory.
  Joint reconstruction can still be legitimate; its dependencies must remain
  explicit and it needs an independent check for an independent-validation claim.
- The specific claim of additional8-42C glass loss over about a minute remains
  a report assertion pending comparable-view state and chronology checks. No
  new human window acceptance or actual glass-transition finding is made here.
- This does not show that every fire date is inaccurate, that structural
  temperatures were exaggerated, or that a corrected interval would prevent
  collapse. A model consequence requires the particular input dependency and
  sensitivity, not a jump from uncertain timing to a cause ranking.

The strongest counterargument is that the original continuous tape or populated
timing worksheet may establish reliable relative order even with poor absolute
clock accuracy. Well-resolved irreversible window breakage and independently
timed comparison images can also legitimately establish order without a camera
timestamp; this unit has not independently refuted those observations. Such
evidence would materially weaken this concern. Conversely, a
verified original sequence opposite to the publication, with robust figure
joins, would strengthen a specific chronology-error finding. Neither record
has been located within this unit's selected materials.
