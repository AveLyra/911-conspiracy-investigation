## Three worksheets and two different displays

Physical164/printed120 says separate files with three Excel worksheets were
generated for each face and analyzed time. Its stated fire assignments are:
0, window observable and no flame visible;1, spot fire;2, larger visible fire
inside without flames emerging;3, external flaming;9, fire condition not
determinable because of factors including obscuration, resolution, or missing
suitable imagery. These are the report's qualitative coding definitions, not
newly accepted temperature or heat-release measurements.

Physical164-165/printed120-121 describes the smoke sheet separately:
0 no smoke observed,1 light smoke,2 heavy smoke,9 window not observable.
Physical165 describes the window sheet as requiring fairly close-up imagery:
0 glass broken out,1 glass intact,9 window not observable. The Figure5-28 key
labels the0 window category "Window open." Neither this binary/open-intact
scheme nor its key supplies a quantitative partial-glass area or a detailed
rule for apertures retaining part of a pane.

**Display warning:** Figure5-31 on physical168 has its own explicit simplified
color rule:0 white,1-3 red,9 yellow. Its yellow therefore means an unresolved
fire code, not the yellow spot-fire category of the different Figure5-28 key.
Its red does not distinguish spot fire, internal fire, and external flaming.
Figure5-29 on166 and Figure5-31 are fire-sheet representations, not glass
worksheets. Do not read the fire-sheet0 as broken glass or fire-sheet9 as
an observation that no glass was present.

The paragraph on physical167/printed123 explains the Figure5-32 composite:
when fire is present **or a fire value cannot be assigned**, display the
fire-sheet code; otherwise display the window-sheet code. Consequently:

- A fire-present color does not expose the underlying glass assignment.
- A fire-unassigned color can conceal a known or unknown glass assignment;
  it does not prove that the glass worksheet itself had9.
- Under the stated rule, an eligible no-fire cell allows the window category
  to supply the displayed color. This is a description of the encoding, not
  a newly checked window observation or worksheet reconstruction.
- One cannot recover a complete window sheet from this composite. In
  particular, a red target aperture cannot settle intact/broken/partial glass
  by its composite color alone. The schematic's architectural/background
  fills are not automatically data-cell assignments.

Physical164 says the fire-sheet example is based **primarily** on Figure5-30;
physical167 similarly describes the fire/window map as based primarily on it.
Do not silently strengthen "primarily" into exclusive provenance or assume
every underlying window-sheet judgment came from this single reproduced
photograph. The pages show source-selection and coding claims, not an audit
of the originals, annotator consistency, worksheet completeness, or FDS
geometry/schedule transfer.
