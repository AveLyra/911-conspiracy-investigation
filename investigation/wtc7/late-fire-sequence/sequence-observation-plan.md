## Result vocabulary and acceptance ceiling

Use distinct findings for: admitted imagery; scene counterpart; ordered sampled framing change; particular exact-still candidate; visual boundary compatibility; observable edit clue; continuity unresolved. State confidence and the decisive observation for each inference. Source acquisition/authentication and software decoding results belong to their separate records and are not visually authenticated here.

A positive boundary result may state that observed endpoints and short spans are visually compatible with immediate adjacency. It cannot state that file order or historical continuity is proved without evidence that excludes omitted material, overlaps and editing. A negative boundary result should state exactly which observation conflicts and whether alternative camera/transfer explanations remain plausible. A nondetection must retain visibility and sampling limits.
