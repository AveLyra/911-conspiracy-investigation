## Dub5 14: observations independent of the proposed join

- **Indices 0 and 30:** broad city view partly blocked by thick dark near-camera vertical window/frame elements. The broad central gridded facade and pale neighboring structures appear through the opening. Between these selected images the opening and building placement shift substantially; that is a framing change, not by itself an established cut.
- **Indices 60, 90 and 120:** near-camera vertical obstructions no longer occupy the central view. The left foreground building with pale parapet, small dark openings and gridded enclosure, the broad central facade, gray rooftop mass and right pale banded neighbor form the familiar wider scene. Dense rolling gray material increasingly covers the scene, especially from the left/lower area. The apparent facade appearance/extent changes markedly, with distortion/sloping lines visible. No calibrated motion inference is made.
- **Indices 150 and 180:** the broad central facade's upper edge is much lower in the image relative to the neighboring structures and becomes lost amid the expanding gray cloud. This is an observed collapse-like sequence; neither the cause nor the detailed deformation/kinematics is determined in this task. Changes in framing and obscuration preclude treating these prose descriptions as a measured trajectory.
- **Indices 210, 240, 270 and 300:** textured cloud occupies the former broad-facade view and rolls among/behind the foreground and neighboring buildings. Bright, near-white backlit areas occur above and within the cloud. I do not classify those bright areas as flames from their brightness alone.
- **Indices 330, 360, 390 and 420:** the selected view shifts/widens toward the right, showing more surrounding blocks and the horizontally banded, stepped building. The recognizable left gridded structure approaches/leaves the left edge while the gray cloud fills more of the intervening streets/building gaps.
- **Indices 450, 480, 510, 540 and 570:** the framing follows the cloud farther right and tightens on a large rounded/textured cloud behind a pale rectangular foreground building with dark windows and a small pointed/hipped rooftop structure. The original R158 gray-roof corner/capped-pole composition is not the endpoint view.
- **Indices 572–577 (all six consecutive frames individually viewed):** that same pale foreground building and rooftop structure remain in nearly stable composition while the cloud's fine contours change slightly. No reset to the R158 tight roof/pole scene occurs in this supplied final span. I did not resolve a definite orange flame-like form in the selected 14 images; cloud, clipping/brightness and limited view prevent any no-fire inference.

There are large framing changes between some approximately one-second selections, notably early removal of the window obstructions and later movement toward the right. Camera movement/zoom is a plausible explanation; intervening frames have not been inspected, so these changes neither establish nor exclude edits. The short consecutive final span itself shows no obvious cut to another composition.
