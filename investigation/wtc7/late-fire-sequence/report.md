## Strongest alternative reading

Access clips may be separately selected excerpts from one tape, with missing
camera movement, a replay or reordering. Thus a clear endpoint mismatch does
not establish a manipulated original, concealment, elapsed gap or unrelated
cameras. Conversely, ordinary shared landmarks cannot repair an incompatible
endpoint or authenticate continuity. This result narrows the permissible
source use, not the universe of physically possible collapse mechanisms.
