## What the proposed source-family logic can discriminate

**Conditionally, it can constrain where an image appearance entered a known
processing chain without authenticating an emitter.** The useful hypothesis
must name a stage: for example, "the point first arose solely during conversion
T of this access copy." An authenticated upstream representation containing
the matched appearance would contradict that *first-arose-at-T* claim. It
would not rule out earlier processing, reflection, another object, or later
enhancement of an already present detail. No such result is established here.

Different filenames, hosts, codecs, raster sizes or container hashes alone
do not show that a representation bypasses T. A re-encode of the same converted
MOV can have different bytes while inheriting its artifacts. A branch from a
shared earlier recording can preserve artifacts introduced before divergence.
Repeated pixels/scene content are not a second independent historical capture.
Conversely, same-family ancestry does not make every comparison useless: a
documented upstream/downstream relationship can test a particular processing
boundary even though it cannot authenticate physical light.

The prior Tilted study supports common recorded scene content, but not exact
exposure identity at the two selected points. Its eight query matches have
adjacent runner-ups; one query changes selected Camera2 frame by resampling
branch. Its report expressly rejects a globally exact recovered offset.
Do not extrapolate a fixed offset to6925/6970, interpolate a match as if
measured, or choose a join because it makes the bright points agree. The
NEAREST/BOX sampled-score equivalence was a known redundant branch, not two
independent confirmations. Numeric filename or scene similarity is insufficient.

## Concrete acceptance test for a later alternate-copy comparison

This is a proposed gate, not authorization to perform new processing/views.

1. **Declare the comparison boundary and lineage.** Pin both complete held
   files, their catalogue/receipt evidence and the tested transformation T.
   Mark each ancestry link as documented, supported inference or unknown.
   State whether the alternate is upstream of T, independently branches before
   T, is downstream of T, or has unresolved ancestry. If unresolved, admit only
   a cross-copy appearance comparison—not a stage-specific exclusion.
2. **Admit corresponding coverage without using the point as the alignment
   target.** Establish candidate source-frame sets and surrounding order from
   preserved maps and non-point scene evidence under a fixed declared method.
   Retain all unresolved adjacent-frame/field alternatives. The focal Camera2
   comparisons include6924/6925/6926 and6969/6970/6971, plus whatever wider
   neighborhood the declared join uncertainty requires. No point absence may
   be claimed from one best-match frame while admissible alternatives remain
   unexamined. An unresolved join is an accepted limiting result, not a pass.
3. **Declare representation and visibility controls before target comparison.**
   Preserve native bytes, dimensions/aspect, channel/field handling, warnings
   and exact display/derivation receipts. A different raster or compression
   path may erase or exaggerate a small point. Confirm that the relevant scene
   region is present and comparably inspectable; do not treat an unreadable,
   cropped, blended or dropped counterpart as a negative. Any resampling or
   tonal branch requires a prospectively fixed reason; no branch selection by
   whether it retains the point. The already-tested local presentation's exact
   pixels do not validate a new decoder or conversion.
4. **Verify the new method's claims, not only its execution.** If conversion,
   alignment or quantitative image classification is proposed, use declared
   synthetic known-present/known-absent small-detail cases and loss/aliasing,
   ambiguous-neighbor and missing-coverage cases appropriate to that method.
   Require independent material arithmetic/representation checks and applicable
   actual-human gates before consequential automated measurement. A successful
   synthetic example does not calibrate the unknown historical camera response.
   A qualitative comparison must remain qualitative without numerical accuracy
   or false-negative claims.
5. **Preserve target and neighboring observations separately from attribution.**
   Freeze prior-informed readers' descriptions before exchanging conclusions;
   retain disagreements, alternate joins and assessed/unassessable states.
   Report each point independently, including nested6970. A shared apparent
   location is not proof of the same emitter, depth or attachment. Report
   physical provenance as a distinct unresolved question unless supported by
   additional independent scene/record evidence.

### Permitted outcome language

| Possible later result | Maximum warranted conclusion |
|---|---|
| Matched point retained in a demonstrated upstream-of-T representation | The visible precursor was not first created solely at T; earlier artifacts and physical alternatives remain. |
| Point retained across a demonstrated pre-T independent branch | Evidence against an artifact exclusive to that one downstream conversion; shared upstream and common-algorithm artifacts still require consideration. |
| Point retained in differently encoded copies with unknown ancestry | Cross-copy appearance agreement only; no identified conversion boundary or independent exposure. |
| Point not noticed in an alternate with uncertain match, missing field or poorer detail | Non-comparable or sensitivity-limited result, not proof of artifact or physical absence. |
| Reproducible difference with a secure join and adequate comparable detail | Representation-dependent discrepancy; its responsible processing stage still needs evidence. |

Even a securely matched discrepancy alone does not show which version is
historically faithful. Stronger conversion attribution would need linked
inputs and reproducible transformation evidence explaining the change while
testing alternatives, not merely the presence of two unequal images.

## Review ceiling and pending claims

The new protocol is methodologically suitable for the finite locator stage:
it prohibits unplanned image comparison and promises a ledger with bounded
negative-search coverage. It does not yet establish that any useful alternate
exists, that its source is independent, or that an upright has been identified.
Those claims await the assembled ledger and checks of its underlying records.

Neither the two observed point descriptions nor a future two-copy agreement
identifies a WTC7 emitter, physical duration, light intensity, a beacon or a
collapse mechanism. The current local presentation check already makes a
newly introduced panel-composition artifact less plausible within its exact
verified transform; repeating that check would not test a much earlier source
conversion. The highest-value next dependency is therefore a *documented
branch/processing boundary plus defensible local correspondence*, not more
copies counted as witnesses or more unsupported causal inference.
