# Camera2 bright-point provenance and scene locator

2026-09-24. Working research for charter Q07/Q10. The main repository charter
and source-preservation rules control. This is a distinct continuation of the
completed qualitative light screen, not a reopening of its frozen observations.

## Question and declared scope

Can already-held records supply a genuinely different representation of the
small apparent points at Camera2 source indices 6925 and 6970, or authenticate
the scene feature with which those points align in projection? A common scene,
similar filename or repeated projected position does not establish independent
capture, exact exposure, an emitter, attachment, depth or a beacon.

Before any new images, perform a finite local source/metadata investigation:

1. Reconcile the main video acquisition/source manifests, WP0 inventory and
   preserved source-index pages for the exact Camera2/CBS-Net Dub6 48 identity.
   Record aliases and source-family uncertainty, not just matching names.
2. Inspect the existing Tilted Camera and Distant View kit source receipts,
   frame maps and completed correspondence results. Rehash located candidate
   media and their selected controlling receipts. Determine actual held native
   frame coverage without interpreting unviewed pixels. No assumed fixed offset.
3. Search only the declared media manifests, relevant camera source/calibration
   records, preserved video-index HTML and already-held catalogues for explicit
   identification of the foreground upright, recording location or source
   operator. Record exact queries and all substantive matches in those scopes.
   A third-party attribution is a lead unless independently corroborated.

Main will examine source/catalogue records; a separate lane will inventory
alternate media/joins; another will examine scene attribution. Root will check
their material pins and exact source passages. A separate critical pass must
challenge any claim of source independence, complete search or identified light.
Reviewers are reused prior-informed AI, not independent human experts.

Read first: the completed light-screen report/source-check and the
tilted-camera-source-join report/scene declaration. Those reports are leads to
the preserved evidence, not additional historical sources.

## Acceptance and stopping rules

Deliver a source-family/representation ledger, exact positive and negative
locator coverage, a reproducible stored-frame/map crosswalk where available,
and a claim-strength assessment with competing interpretations. A successful
locator may identify a next test; it cannot manufacture its result. Distinguish
an independently hosted or differently encoded copy from independently acquired
historical evidence. Retain the possibility that a shared artifact preceded
both copies.

No new image viewing, decoding, similarity fit, coordinate annotation or
physical timing/intensity calculation is covered by this stage. If the records
locate a useful additional representation, declare a specific follow-up scope
and applicable controls before opening images or running comparisons. Do not
choose an offset because it reproduces a bright point. Preserve failures and
ambiguous joins; absence within this finite search is not global absence.

All authored outputs stay in this unit except existing status/map/feedback
updates. No raw/main/legal edits, external acquisition, private packet, outreach,
transmission, accepted Sherlock/Faraday transition, staging, commit or push.
No actual-human gate is waived and no overall causal ranking is promised.
