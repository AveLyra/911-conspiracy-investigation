September 20, 2026 UTC. Research-only. This is the Stage B result, following
the [Stage A source-definition study](report.md) and [verified paired
extraction](stage-b-execution.md). Full investigation and the bounded Luna

## Finding

**The two AI passes support a conditional east-associated downward-change
bracket, but neither establishes a finite west-penthouse disappearance time.**
Both retain a west-associated raised remnant at stored frame 6958. Later the
tiny contour merges with smoke and the moving foreground roof edge. Neither
observer can certify the required material crossing plus two post-event
confirmations. This is an unresolved measurement, not proof that NIST's
reported time is wrong or that the penthouse remained standing until the end.

Consequently, we have **not independently reproduced the complete 9.3-second
interval**. We have a conditional elapsed lower enclosure of approximately
8.04 seconds in the held file's clock, with no finite upper end. This narrow
test does not alter the overall ranking of collapse mechanisms.

The calculation views are northwest/below and north/at-roofline, not precisely
registered to this historical distant camera. The experimental east criterion
is first confidently resolved outline change, not shown to match the published
model's east-penthouse timing origin. Earlier undetected east motion, if it
belongs to the same event, would lengthen the observed elapsed interval; it
cannot by itself explain a shorter model interval. View/identity correspondence
and source-clock limits still prevent a precise discrepancy claim. The two model damage cases are not an uncertainty
distribution. The earlier street-view 8.0-second occlusion threshold and the
nearby figure's 10.0±0.1-second caption must not replace the distant-view claim.

This result does not decide whether fires initiated the failure, whether some
other support-removal mechanism operated, or whether a model was contrived.
Temporal compatibility is necessary evidence for a particular reconstruction,
but it cannot validate unseen mechanisms by itself. Conversely, an unresolved
measurement does not supply affirmative evidence for a competing mechanism.

## Strongest objection and next discriminating step

The strongest objection is that both observers may be making the same
over-conservative visual decision: they share a method, source and prior
context. An expert or a lossless enlarged display might identify a continuous
west outline crossing in these same pixels. The opposite risk is component
aliasing: if frame 6958's remnant is not the west penthouse, even the one-sided
bound is too strong. Exact rational arithmetic does not repair either risk.

The next local test should therefore target **component identity and the
moving occluder**, not perform another identical decode or copy a published
time. Before execution, declare a bounded lossless roof-detail display/
annotation sensitivity pass on these existing frames, with explicit pixel
rectangle, integer nearest-neighbor enlargement, same fixed time interval,
separate material/parapet traces and an ambiguity stop rule. It adds no source
resolution and cannot be called blind replication after these results.
Preserve both current freezes regardless of the outcome. If the crossing
remains unresolved, stop that display route and carry a concrete original-
quality-source or qualified-human-review requirement forward; do not keep
adjusting the display until a desired number appears.
