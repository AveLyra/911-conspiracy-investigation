## Findings, separating source claims from new observation

Table 5-2 (physical 306, printed 262) identifies Camera 2 as a high-building
view near midtown, due north of WTC 7, recording the entire sequence. Camera 3
is the West/Harrison street-level view. The camera map on physical 307 supports
the distinction. Figure 5-185 (physical 308) explicitly labels Camera 2's
pre-descent image. Its building, foreground and skyline composition strongly
match the held baseline. The source caption discloses intensity adjustment.
This is a **scene/view-family match**, not exact frame, pixel, original-tape
or historical-clock authentication.

The labeled roof photographs on physical 143 distinguish two enclosed
penthouses from the intervening screenwalls, cooling-tower area and parapet.
Combined with the due-north view, they support coarse east-penthouse/image-left,
screenwall/middle and west-penthouse/image-right assignments. They do not
provide a native-pixel polygon for every hidden edge. Physical 134–135's
different photo/video pair explains an attributed time comparison; it is not
the held Camera 2 baseline or independent clock validation.

Physical 319 (printed 275) explicitly warns that the Camera 3 screenwall and
west-penthouse disappearance behind the north parapet at 8.0 ±0.1 seconds does
not establish complete sinking through the roof: setback and viewing angle
matter. Physical 320's paired 8.1-second images still show the rooftop features
in the distant Camera 2 view. Physical 321's 9.0-second distant view shows the
remaining west-penthouse outline sloping down from its western side. The north
facade is itself moving; the occlusion reference cannot be its fixed initial
image position.

Physical 326's prose connects complete west-penthouse loss in the **distant
view** to **around 9.3 seconds**. Its Figure 5-208 is captioned **10.0 ±0.1
seconds**, not 9.3. The prose, preceding camera references and paired figures
join the approximate published endpoint to Camera 2; they do not give an exact
9.3-second source frame or justify assigning ±0.1 seconds to that prose value.
Physical 325 supplies the preceding text. Physical 323's Figure 5-205 is
Camera 3 difference imagery, not the missing Camera 2 event frame.

These are source-supported definitions to test, not independent measurements
of support loss. Alignment zero remains the beginning of east-penthouse
descent. The report's maximum relative inter-video assignment estimate does
not independently calibrate the within-file clock of our converted copy.
