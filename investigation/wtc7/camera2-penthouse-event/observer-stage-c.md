## Confidence, strongest objection and stop

Confidence is moderate that the enlarged pixels show a continuing low raised
image feature through 6978, and high that this pass does not meet the declared
criterion for a confident three-frame below-parapet event. Confidence remains
low-to-moderate in attributing the last protrusion specifically to the west
penthouse rather than another overlapping/deforming roof component. No
probabilities or physical dimensions are estimated.

The strongest objection is **component substitution through an apparently
continuous silhouette**: the early tall shape can be genuine roof structure
while its final bump is a different structure, parapet deformation, debris or
smoke-edge combination. Lossless enlargement improves inspection but cannot
rule that out, and both observers share the same source and prior geometry.
Agreement would therefore not supply independent source corroboration.

Close this display route at its hard stop. Resolving the event requires an
original-quality or demonstrably better component-resolving source, a defensible
geometry/camera join that separates the relevant edges, and independent human
frame review; further crop/zoom/threshold tuning of these same pixels is not
authorized or warranted by this pass. No causal ranking, legal/engine
promotion, new source acquisition, publication or commit is made here.
