## What could overturn or limit the next test

The strongest practical objection is censoring: smoke can obscure east onset,
while overlap, dust and limited resolution can hide the last west outline.
Even a correct coarse identity does not guarantee adjacent confident endpoint
frames. A moving facade also means that a fixed image-height threshold is
wrong. A measurable timing discrepancy would qualify a specific model/video
agreement claim; it would not uniquely distinguish fire from deliberate
support removal. Agreement after a chosen zero alignment would not validate
unseen interior failure either.
