## Disposition

The completed work supplies affirmative constraints, not merely a list of
measurement gaps: appreciable visible window flames; staged changes of raised
rooftop features before conspicuous outer-facade descent; pronounced late
downward image curvature, with gravity-scale coefficients under stated metric
and timing assignments; and compatible common apparent motion at two
recognizable facade locations. These deserve weight even though their
historical calibration is incomplete.

They do not by themselves identify member temperatures, first hidden support
failure, uniform whole-building free fall, a complete symmetry/footprint
measure, or initiation. The completed acoustic work supplies no calibrated
absence-of-blasts result. Missing clock/calibration closure is not evidence
that descent was slow, that the published gravity-compatible interval was
false, or that the historical scene was silent.

## Common-observable tests for the structural synthesis

These are achievable, graded tests, not a demand for perfect certainty before
any evidence counts. Use the same observations and explicit uncertainty for
all specified mechanisms; do not require identical mechanisms or grant an
unspecified alternative unlimited adjustable removals.

1. **Exposure-to-temperature:** predict the correctly identified frontages,
   floors and visibility-censored progression, while reporting which images
   set inputs and which test outputs. Vary supported ignition, ventilation,
   floor substitutions and thermal transfer; compare member heat histories
   and both collapse/non-collapse outcomes. A robust failure across supported
   alternatives strengthens that pathway; failure over well-supported
   alternatives weakens the specific chain, not all possible fire pathways.
2. **Initiation-to-visible sequence:** forward-project identifiable roof and
   facade features and their relative motion. Compare observed ordering before
   fitting absolute camera offsets; show where hidden failures are predictions
   rather than initialization or event-alignment conventions. Preserve earlier
   small/perimeter motion and occlusion, not a perfectly stationary shell by fiat.
3. **Rapid propagation and motion:** compare full position histories and
   retained window sensitivities, including the pronounced late curvature and
   conditional gravity scale, rather than one endpoint time or best fit.
   Explain the transition from earlier staged features to approximately common
   apparent late facade motion without silently prescribing the entire result.
4. **Resistance/force accounting:** if converting a track into resistance,
   define its relation to a moving mass system, geometry, contact and mass
   transfer. Near-g for a verified constant-mass body's center of mass would
   constrain its net nongravitational force; the present roof/louver features
   do not independently establish that body or every separate connection force.
5. **Acoustic opportunity to detect:** a mechanism-specific sound prediction
   should be compared with source-linked recording location, chain, noise,
   gain/bandwidth and propagation uncertainty. First obtain actual reviewed
   sound-event annotations and source/A-V joins. An adequately detectable
   predicted signal absent in qualifying records would be adverse evidence;
   these selected uncalibrated plot windows do not yet supply that test.

Highest-value observational dependencies are the original/source-linked
Camera 3 pixel/time and architectural-endpoint records; specific Peskin 35/37
catalog/conversion records paired with floor-specific model histories; and
genuinely reviewed, source-linked acoustic events with detectability analysis.
They are distinct tasks. Repeating the completed literal file search, adding
another transformed copy, or obtaining another spectrogram without those joins
does not resolve them. Existing positive observations retain conditional weight
while these records remain missing; no probabilities or cause decision follow
from this review alone.
