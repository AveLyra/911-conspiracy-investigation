## Assessment

**NIST's specific pathway warrants positive but conditional weight as a developed
mechanical explanation. These completed audits do not demonstrate its historical
chain or supply a defensible probability advantage over competing specified
mechanisms.** The positive basis is not NIST's authority: it is the documented
material work, component mechanics, physical experiments, represented residual
load paths and a reported nonbuckling control. The limiting basis is not mere
incompleteness: important transfer, failure-law and surviving-restraint links
remain unverified, and selected model/target and model/experiment differences
concern quantities potentially relevant to propagation.

This is not a requirement that a model be perfectly reproduced before it can
have any evidentiary value. It is a distinction between a physically informed
candidate and a sufficiently tested building-specific explanation. A preference
previously justified by claiming independently validated connections, verified
remaining support or a complete released run cannot retain that justification.
Conversely, these audits do not demonstrate that the actual remaining support
would have prevented fire-triggered instability. They supply no new affirmative
intervention mechanism. Non-ranking here is not equal odds, a verdict of innocence,
or a presumption that an earlier cause ranking must remain unchanged.

### Strongest case for taking the pathway seriously

The work is more than matching an exterior animation. It articulates sequential
local-to-global mechanisms, incorporates several kinds of resistance and has
physical constraints beneath parts of its constitutive modeling. The later
original experiments establish that connection slip, bending, catenary transfer,
fracture and steep load drops are real behaviors, not invented solver devices.
They also establish survival after some initial failures. Neither branch may be
discarded simply because one better suits a proposed collapse sequence. [3–4]

Several concrete findings disconfirm blanket skeptical criticisms. NIST reports
added vertical support and continuing seat/contact mechanisms. Its capacity
choices include strength-enhancing assumptions, not exclusively weakening ones.
The full printed-table checks permit rounding explanations for all 55 connection
ratios and all 12 later model-deviation entries. The explicit 44 ksi Algoma seat
model narrows the earlier 42/44 concern. No systematic capacity reduction or
arithmetic fabrication follows from those observations. [2–4]

The reported 3.5 h nonbuckling run is especially relevant: it shows that the
published modeling account is not simply 'any regional damage causes collapse.'
It is still a source-reported comparison, not an independently run control or
proof that the 4.0 h damage state was historically correct. [5]

### Strongest case against strong historical confidence

The pathway must succeed through a chain of particular failures, while several
accessible checks stop before the quantities governing that chain. Matching a
spring target's total energy does not require matching its force at each
displacement. The later models' lower ultimate rotations, differing failure
modes and imperfect axial-force histories directly caution against treating
peak-load agreement as complete failure-physics validation. These are actual
observed model-comparison limitations, not merely requests for more paperwork.
Their magnitude and direction in WTC7's global response remain uncalculated. [3–4]

The original experiments' residual load carrying makes 'first failure equals
zero support' an empirically unsafe generalization. It does not prove that
WTC7's specific heated seats/floors retained enough useful restraint. The tests
were bare, slowly pulled two-span assemblies with reused beams and particular
restraints—not suddenly removed loaded supports, heated composite floors or a
global building. Their later publication/comparison cannot retroactively supply
an authenticated 2008 calibration history. [4]

The missing local force laws and source-to-run mapping matter because they sit
on candidate restraint paths, not merely because some files are absent. Yet
their present absence from the inspected package does not show that the historical
solver lacked them. More importantly, none of these audits has calculated that
admissible remaining restraint defeats the proposed initiation or that admitted
fire loads cannot produce the requisite damage. The appropriate adverse update
is to confidence in the claimed validation and reproducibility—not a demonstrated
fire impossibility or affirmative mechanical proof of intervention. [1–2,5–8]

## Discriminators and two-sided falsification

1. **Test the initiating state, not only the final motion.** Link authenticated
   thermal histories and ANSYS results to the exact LS-DYNA damage, geometry,
   materials, restart and executable. Preserve the 3.5/4.0 h pairing. A failure
   of that identity/transfer check weakens claims about the specific published
   run; it does not by itself falsify every possible fire pathway.
2. **Test the retained restraint that could change the result.** Map the
   C44–C79 north seats at Floors8–12/14 and C79–C76 Floor14 attachment to actual
   parts, initialized contacts and seat/clip/bolt histories. Evaluate directional
   restoring response, not just one instantaneous reaction. If independently
   justified surviving restraint robustly prevents the asserted instability,
   that would be genuine adverse physical evidence. If relevant validation and
   sensitivity cases robustly reproduce instability with that restraint retained,
   the claim that attachments make the sequence unattainable would weaken.
3. **Separate the constitutive and global tests.** Exact curves, group/outlier
   mappings and original test histories can resolve local ambiguities. They do
   not alone reproduce cascade timing. A later global comparison needs force,
   displacement, contact and energy histories plus uncertainties and noncollapse
   cases, not just a visually persuasive render.
4. **Constrain each intervention hypothesis independently.** An unspecified
   ability to remove whatever support is needed can imitate a target motion but
   is not a tested initiating explanation. Require a specified class of support
   loss and its observable consequences, without presuming thermite or any other
   means necessary. A validated fire-only pathway would weaken necessity claims
   for intervention, but not logically exclude intervention. Conversely, a robust
   failure of NIST's pathway would not establish an operation; distinct physical
   or documentary evidence must bridge to deliberate action. A named intervention
   hypothesis should be weakened by incompatible timing, required effects absent
   under adequate detection, or disconfirmed operational evidence—not dismissed
   merely for lacking a complete actor narrative.

The current structural evidence therefore supports serious, discriminating
investigation rather than either automatic institutional deference or an
automatic intervention preference. Broad 'all fires' and unrestricted 'some
intervention' claims are not made falsifiable by treating the other as their
only competitor. This WP3 review does not independently reassess the footage,
fire-comparator base rates, acoustics, physical residues or motive evidence.

### Substantive disposition

No objection to the central confidence update within this structural-review
scope. The report preserves positive conditional model weight, does not require
perfect reproduction before any inference, and does not claim that a local
missing input establishes physical absence or global model invalidity. It
explicitly distinguishes the changed aggregate assessment from a newly proved
mechanical contradiction, demolition-first conclusion or equal probabilities.
The causal-edge grades refer to their stated propositions rather than a
probability of the cause. Withdrawing the earlier fire-first sentence is not
presented as proof that another mechanism is more likely.

The main favorable and adverse structural evidence survives: real material/
experimental foundations, residual bearing/contact and added support, the
reported noncollapse state, typed supplied/missing properties, correlated
dependencies, later failure-mode/peak-ordering differences, and the rounding
counterevidence. Prescribed damage transfer is separated from subsequent

The priority drawing/member-to-initiation-restraint crosswalk is well targeted.
