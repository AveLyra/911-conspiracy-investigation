# WTC 7 causal-chain assessment

The completed audits support treating NIST's proposed sequence as a technically
developed, physically motivated candidate. They do not independently establish
that it was the historical sequence, or that fire-triggered collapse is more
probable than deliberate support removal. The recent releases make important
parts of the model inspectable, but the critical bridge from justified heating
to actual, timed loss of load-carrying capacity remains unverified.[^1][^2]

The earlier synthesis's conditional fire-first judgment is therefore not carried
forward as a conclusion earned by this independent investigation. It depended
on assigning weight to disputed case-specific model outputs. The present
assessment finds reasons to assign those outputs some conditional weight, but
does not establish how much or a defensible comparative ordering. This is not
a demolition-first finding or an equal-probability allocation. A failure of
NIST's exact pathway would leave other fire pathways requiring their own tests.
This is an interim scientific assessment, not an expert structural opinion,
legal conclusion or completion of the broader investigation.[^1]

## The proposition to test

NIST's account describes fire-induced floor/connection damage, regional floor
collapse, loss of directional column restraint, interior instability and
propagation, followed by exterior failure. The proposed regional cascade
involves portions of Floors 13 and 14 around Columns 79–81, not an initially
intact building-wide Floor 13 falling through eight identical intact floors.
The global analysis receives an already calculated damage state from a
different analysis; it is not a single uninterrupted reconstruction of hours
of fire through final collapse.[^2][^3]

That is a coherent mechanism to investigate. Coherence does not demonstrate
that the temperatures, damage state, remaining restraints, load transfers and
event times were correct. Conversely, numerical transfer and element removal
are not admissions that the real building was deliberately cut. The transferred
damage needs validation; later computed failures must not all be mislabeled
as individually prescribed.[^2]

established within its stated source/calculation scope; B is strongly supported
but qualified; C is materially model-dependent; D is unresolved; E is an
unsupported or contradicted overstatement. Documentary A does not turn a
reported model event into an observed historical event.

The chain is not validated by counting how many cells contain something. A
material name is not a force history, a contact selection is not demonstrated
surviving restraint, and calibrated model-to-model agreement is not another
physical experiment.

## What strengthens the technical basis

The corrected released inventory has 3,006,910 shells, 3,190 beams, 33,364
discrete elements and 2,461 solids, matching the corresponding published model
counts. Thermal-loading structures and member/part/set relations are real and
inspectable. This supports association with the described model, not identity
with an executed historical case. An earlier shell-count overstatement was
this investigation's inventory error, not an agency discrepancy.[^6][^14]

NIST describes tensile data, engineering capacities and explicit additional
support mechanisms. The original Thompson assembly record later used in
TN1749 documents physical load transfer, different failure modes and residual
capacity after some initial failures. This strengthens the empirical foundation
for relevant classes of behavior. It is not a heated composite WTC 7 test;
later publication does not establish contemporaneous validation of the 2008
implementation.[^7][^8]

Several findings weaken simplified criticisms. Failed bolts do not necessarily
mean lost bearing; distinct node IDs do not necessarily mean disconnection;
initial connection failure need not be ultimate capacity; static connection
load ratios are not floor-impact capacities. Some reported assumptions increase
resistance, and some model-target differences retain more late resistance.
The audits do not establish a uniform pattern of choices made to weaken the
building.[^7][^8][^10]

The reported 3.5-hour damage run also matters: some floor/girder failures occur
but the structure is stable at the end of that analysis. This is not indefinite
stability, but contradicts a claim that every
damage state necessarily collapses. It is also an essential sensitivity
benchmark. The physical credibility of its difference from the collapse-
producing state remains to be tested. Two model states do not establish a sharp
real-world half-hour threshold or a probability distribution over fire histories.[^2]

## What limits confidence in the exact sequence

The central uncertainty is a connected set of dependencies, not a count of
independent suspicious coincidences. The released assembly terminates at its
gravity stage as written; later stages require their applicable continuation
and run records. A known temperature input does not authenticate its derivation.
A present connection card can reference an absent definition; a nearby candidate
interface need not be effectively tied or retain adequate capacity.[^3][^5][^9]

Recent findings make the questions concrete:

- All 63 selected Column 79-region nodes with unequal thermal coefficients
  connect only to the same part. This defeats the explanation that every repeat
  merely reflects different-part sharing. It does not establish inflated
  effective temperatures: later coefficients are lower at 45 nodes and higher
  at 18, and the historical processing rule is unresolved.[^6]
- Nineteen material IDs used by 23 master-region parts are unresolved in the
  checked assembly. Counterparts exist in a transformed namespace but are not
  authenticated replacements. This is narrower than claiming no connection
  properties were supplied.[^9]
- Selected spring cards reference LCD602/803. Applicable definitions remain
  unresolved after the declared searches of the six supplementary streams and
  selected June numeric package. Literal absence is not absence from generated
  inputs, restarts or every agency record.[^15][^16]
- A contact/damage join finds nine shells and 17 discrete numeric matches to
  a beam list. Different-family matches do not authenticate deletion semantics.
  The separate Case A test is negative across the fixed selected groups.
  Neither result establishes an executed support-loss history.[^11][^15]

Repeating LCD602/803 in several audits supplies one increasingly specified
gap, not several independent witnesses against fire. The gap obstructs a
calculation; it does not measure the missing result. Likewise, failure to
close an evidence link does not imply that its physical process was absent.

There are actual validation limits beyond file availability. Published shell/
spring comparisons match some accumulated-energy features better than full
force histories. Later physical comparisons miss some failure modes and
initial-versus-secondary peak ordering. Post-ultimate extrapolation and
restraint sensitivity remain important. Choices selected for agreement with
tests are not wholly untouched predictions; their use is not automatically
improper, but their transfer and sensitivity require testing.[^7][^8]

Conversely, suspected printed-table arithmetic errors did not survive declared
rounding tests. All 55 selected capacity-ratio rows and all 12 later deviation
entries are compatible with input display precision. This does not authenticate
hidden values, but removes those particular arithmetic allegations as established
errors. Small remaining formula/processing issues are not demonstrated global
instability errors.[^7][^8]

### September 27 response-transfer follow-through

The selected tower-bar studies report favorable elongation comparisons under
tension. Their June 2004 appendix calls fully restrained work ongoing; the
inspected final findings report use of equivalent thicknesses without supplying
that particular restrained-bar comparison. A separate synthetic thermoelastic
test now shows why matching elongation at one load—even throughout a constructed
heating path—does not necessarily preserve restrained force when compliance
differs. Adding equal compliance to that one-load match, or matching responses
at two distinct loads, restores transfer within that linear,
load-independent-temperature model.[^18][^19]

This refines one response-transfer dependency, not a newly demonstrated defect
in NIST's actual cases or another independent historical failure. The actual
thermal histories and compliance could make the approximation adequate. The
selected source also reports four physical floor assemblies, including three
restrained tests, sustaining design loading despite damage; its approximate
two-hour summary requires the detailed-duration correction below. It reports
sag consistent with finite-element calculations. That is affirmative technical
support, but not the missing matched-coating comparison. Temperature-based
ratings, load-bearing survival and differing slab/deck protection must remain
distinct. Neither these tests nor the synthetic bar result directly identifies
WTC7 behavior. Causal grades and the comparison below are unchanged.[^18][^19]

ordering changes. A case-linked model-to-test comparison with tuning history,
valid measurement intervals and declared metrics is the next dependency.[^20]

## What the motion requires

Progressive failure need not produce slow, separately visible failure of every
external column. Damage can precede visible descent, and an unstable system can
respond rapidly after a threshold is crossed. These are mechanical possibilities,
not evidence that this building crossed the necessary threshold by NIST's route.
A useful mechanism must predict both precursors and rapid descent while retaining
realistic surviving restraints.

For a defined constant-mass body whose center of mass translates downward,
Newton's law gives `M a = M g + D − U`, where D and U are other downward and
upward external force components. Acceleration near g constrains their net
difference, not each force individually. This relation does not apply unchanged
to an arbitrary roof point or a subsystem gaining mass. A proposed downward
pull cannot be assumed to cancel resistance without measured or validated force
histories. Nor does a momentary zero reaction establish no available restoring
stiffness.

Recent motion work supplies positive constraints, not merely caveats. Fresh
late annotations retain pronounced downward curvature. A constant image
separation can fit both observers' retained placement envelopes in the louver-
bank/roof-corner study. This qualifies an earlier suggestion that between-track
differences established asymmetric motion, without proving rigid motion or
simultaneously absent supports. Incomplete calibration is not evidence that
the observed rapid descent was slow; it bounds physical acceleration, timing
and force claims.[^13]

NIST's exterior account is not an unaffected shell standing until one instant.
It describes early east-face inward movement, unloading as floors fall away,
and south/west buckling before north/east buckling. Those are testable predictions.
Aligning the penthouse reference is not independent validation of a hidden
column event, and broad final-motion resemblance cannot compensate for a
consequential mismatch in lateral deformation or onset ordering.[^2][^12]

There is an actual published timing difference to retain, not only a proposed
future test. NCSTAR1A Table3-1 gives west-penthouse disappearance below the
roofline at9.3 seconds in its observation column, versus6.9/7.3 seconds in the
debris-damage simulation's two views. Those are reported event/view values,
not newly measured camera times or uncertainty intervals. Section3.5.2 also
limits the reliability of later simulated movement after the north-façade kink.
The degree to which these discrepancies discriminate initiation remains
unresolved, but a full-motion validation claim must address them.[^12]

The current audio work does not establish a calibrated absence-of-explosions
test. It documents digital levels, sample differences, edits and processing
limits, not independently identified acoustic transients. A loud sound
is not an identified charge; failure to identify a charge is not a validated
detection limit. Whole-window channel checks disfavor wholesale stereo
cancellation, without excluding short local effects or establishing original
detectability. NIST's separate blast analysis has not been reproduced
by these audio audits.[^17]

## Priority discriminators

1. **Actual member detail → initiation resistance.** Reconcile the corrected
   seat/travel description, drawings, stiffeners, slab/stud restraint and
   connection groups with the specific model. Preserve north-side bearing and
   the Floor 14 west attachment. Test whether credible heating and loading
   remove the necessary restraint—not merely whether an assumed unsupported
   column buckles.[^2][^10]
2. **Thermal and run-state closure.** Locate the actual mapping code, repeated-
   load behavior, executable/build and input/include/continuation package.
   Match echoes and temperatures to sources. Do not choose first/last/max
   coefficients or substitute materials to force a runnable surrogate.[^5][^6][^9]
3. **Failure versus survival.** Compare exact paired 3.5/4.0-hour states and
   native outputs with independently justified intermediate histories, residual
   capacities and transfer schedules. Retain arrest/non-collapse, energy/force
   balances, numerical warnings and the actual stability criterion.[^2]
4. **Common-observable challenge.** Compare matched-camera multi-feature
   positions, lateral motion, onset and deformation across justified runs;
   distinguish calibration from held-out evaluation. Another selected roofline
   fit is not this test.[^12][^13]
5. **Mechanism-specific evidence in parallel.** Preserve acoustic detection
   limits, physical attribution/custody and documentary provenance. They can
   discriminate initiation or intervention in ways prescribed removal cannot.
   Missing evidence warrants specific retrieval, not a finding of misconduct.[^1][^17]
