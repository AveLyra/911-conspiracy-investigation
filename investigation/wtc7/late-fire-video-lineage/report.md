## Question, method and actual coverage

The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [fixed selection](selection.json) and
[execution declaration](EXECUTION-2026-09-19.md) ask whether held recordings
contain a defensible counterpart to either report still, comparing each
still's visible scene anchors separately, and whether matched footage supports
the attributed wide-to-zoom sequence. Those anchors include facade, rooftop
enclosure, upright and neighboring-building geometry where visible; shared
scene identity is not presumed. Orange color alone is not an identity test.
The complete stills were inspected before this new screen.

Each source underwent a whole-file integer presentation-timestamp inventory.
The helper selected the first decoded frame in each occupied two- or fifteen-
second bin, then decoded from the beginning to produce native-sized RGB PNGs
and complete overview sheets. It did not seek to convenient moments, retime,
stabilize or listen to audio. The overviews are resized navigation aids;
native images retain decoded raster dimensions, not authenticated original
camera resolution. Repeated files and scene families are not independent
cameras. The count includes countdown graphics and obscured/unusable views.

geometry. The Peskin candidates have a different foreground arrangement,
including a pale stepped building and a brick/balcony high-rise. **Visible
flame-like forms remain affirmative observations** in several samples,
including a conspicuous horizontal row in Peskin168. Heavily obscured orange

BBC84 are not discarded because they show fire; they are not demonstrated
counterparts to these two particular report images. Obscured geometry remains

The strongest objection to the negative matching result is substantial:

image may not be recognized. No sensitivity or false-negative rate was
established for this descriptive search. Agreement between reviewers does
not remove those limitations.
