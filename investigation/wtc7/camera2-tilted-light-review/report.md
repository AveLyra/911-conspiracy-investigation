## Evidentiary weight and strongest alternative

prior-informed qualitative review. It narrows a representation question: the
conspicuous appearances are not noticed in only the selected Camera2 PNGs.

changing visibility or other image-generation explanations. The copies have
unresolved ancestry and do not become independent cameras through file count.

location. An actual physical source remains unidentified. Failure to notice a
conspicuous cap in another image is not physical absence. No explosion,
