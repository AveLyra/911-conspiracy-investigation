## Quantitative and review gates

Once a source supplies concrete experimental/calculated comparison values,
freeze a separate narrow arithmetic plan and source transcription before
computing a discrepancy. Report both agreement and disagreement with rounding
and selection limits. Do not digitize a plot into a replacement historical
material law or choose a threshold after seeing the result. If exact tabular
data are absent, document that limit rather than invent precision.

Delegate original-paper/test provenance and a separate source/inference check;
root traces the NIST handoff and available release inventory. Independent numeric
verification must use fresh source transcription, not merely rerun root values.
Acceptance: a source-pinned calibration/validation dependency map, feasible
reproducible comparison or exact unavailable-data limit, and a report stating
what changes confidence in a specific model claim. It does not require a cause
ranking change or establish an independent forensic conclusion.
