### Prospective interpretation caveat

The control instruction calls each target the shape's “top-left outer
corner.” A is a polygon rather than a rectangle: its builder intends the
leftmost roof/wall junction (521,181), while its higher horizontal roof also
starts at an upper-left vertex (675,131). The literal intended corner and its
pixels are unambiguous in the builder, but the natural-language description
alone admits that competing vertex reading. This concern was reported to
root before this checker read any observer annotation. Do not change truth
or retune the pass rule; preserve an alternative vertex reading as a possible
identity/instruction issue rather than necessarily a coordinate-estimation
error. This checker did not visually judge the fixture's label placement.
