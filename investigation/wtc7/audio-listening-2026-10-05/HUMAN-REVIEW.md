## What to report

Listen to each entire excerpt once before drawing conclusions. Describe speech,
rumble, sudden reports or other sounds in ordinary language. A sound can be
clear while its source remains unknown. Distinguish someone **talking about**
a bang from a bang actually audible in that passage. Note masking by voices or
other sound, apparent abrupt edits, and anything you cannot identify.

If useful, replay an entire excerpt up to twice more, for a maximum of three
passes. Record the number of passes and whether each played completely. Give
approximate onset ranges in **seconds from the start of that excerpt**, using
only the precision your player and listening support—for example, “somewhere
between 4 and 5 seconds,” not an invented millisecond timestamp. If you cannot
read reliable times, give an untimed description and say so. Channel identity
is optional when genuinely distinguishable; otherwise mark it unknown.

A useful reply format for each excerpt is:

> A: played completely [yes/no], [number] passes. I heard [description] at
> approximately [local interval or timing unavailable]. It overlaps [other
> sound, if applicable]. Possible source [tentative/unknown]. Uncertainty: […].

Do not feel obliged to find a candidate. “I heard no distinct sudden report”
is different from “there was no explosive event.” An interrupted or failed
playback must remain unreviewed. There are no supplied event times or transcript
answers to agree with.

## What this review can establish

Your response can supply actual human auditory observations of these copies.

It cannot by itself identify a charge, authenticate original A/V synchronization,
measure sound pressure, or establish an opportunity to detect every mechanism.
Expertise and remaining timing/detection uncertainty will remain explicit before
any observation is assigned causal weight.

The [source check](source-check.md) verifies the exact files and their declared
track-time offsets. Adding an excerpt's start offset to a local observation
does not authenticate original camera timing or account for sound-travel delay.
