## What the audio can and cannot decide

A proposed lower-blast thermal pathway must not inherit an acoustic penalty
derived only for conventional explosive charges. Conversely, "nanothermite"
does not specify a silent material or a demonstrated WTC 7 mechanism.
[Malchi and colleagues' 2008 experimental abstract](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/the-effect-of-added-alsub2subosub3sub-on-the-propagation-behavior/)
reports formulation-dependent pressure behavior in laboratory nanoscale
thermite tests. It does not predict the exterior recording signature of a
building-scale operation. No formulation, preparation or deployment inference
is made here.

Even an established absence of loud blasts would distinguish a sufficiently
loud mechanism from quieter alternatives only if the recordings could have
detected its expected signature. It would not by itself distinguish quiet
deliberate weakening from fire-related structural failure. In these excerpts,
the user's reported bang and the unauthenticated compilation soundtrack make
a blanket premise of acoustic silence particularly unwarranted. The strongest
alternative to the proposed source link is simply that similarly located
cameras, different versions or independently assembled audio/video are being
conflated. None of those alternatives establishes tampering or concealment.
