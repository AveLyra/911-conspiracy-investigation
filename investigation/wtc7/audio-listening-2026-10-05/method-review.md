The strongest relevant objection is that clean synthetic speech is a different
task from finding or timing masked transients in historical sound. The protocol
already limits the result to elementary access and requires subsequent actual
human or qualified acoustic review before event times carry causal weight.
That makes it a reasonable access screen for provisional listening, not a
validation dataset. If the runtime supplies only text, a player attachment or
successful transport without perceptual input, accurate-looking text must not
be reported as proof of listening. Record the actual available modality and
its uncertainty; do not infer runtime internals from a score.

### Fit to the unmet sound requirement

The October 4 media review, especially lines 39–45, 79 and 88, calls for an
actual capable listener, four held windows, uncertainty-aware source-relative
candidate times and an end after those windows. The prospective protocol
matches that bounded route: 18 + 38 + 25 + 17 = 98 seconds in total, with the
three WTC 7 excerpts dependent on one compilation and the comparator from a
different building. Duration is not independent event coverage.

Using the preserved full-decode stereo derivatives avoids silently returning to
the old seek/mono path whose behavior the acoustic audit found different.
Source-review verification of their actual hashes, formats, sample lengths and
offsets remains a separate dependency; this method review has not performed
that check. Adding a held excerpt start to an interval yields encoded-track
time, not authenticated camera event time or propagation-corrected timing.

The initial neutral pass, at most three whole-excerpt passes, alternative
interpretations and unresolved categories are proportionate controls for this
exploratory task. There is no requirement here to invent exact onsets, timing
precision, channel judgments or an empty-event result. A complete-coverage row
with no heard candidate describes only that listener's result for that passage.

The existing acoustic audit independently preserves the key limitations:
sample-level reproducibility is not sound authentication; old machine segment
boundaries are navigation labels; full/seek sample differences do not supply a
clock correction; and no source-specific false-negative calibration exists.
The new protocol does not erase or contradict those limitations. Qualitative
speech/rumble/transient/edit candidates can improve the missing descriptive
record while those broader limitations remain.

If the trial fails, the defensible result is no established listening route
for this session and an explicitly unheard human-review packet. Such a packet
is preparation, not completion of the listened-event table. Failure does not
prove inability of all agents/models or inability of a person to play the WAVs.
The actual-human review, privacy, external-contact and expenditure boundaries
remain in force. No new external service or disclosure is needed for this
bounded initial review.
