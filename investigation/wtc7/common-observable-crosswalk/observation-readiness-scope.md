measurement actually exists and what would prevent its valid use in a matched
NIST/UAF test. Distinguish source-reported observations, qualitative image
descriptions, encoded-time/native-pixel calculations, calibrated physical
measurements and model predictions. Passing software or metadata tests do not
convert one category into another.

one bounded independent review and a prioritized next discriminating action.

human-review status, calibration reuse and source dependence, admissible
present use, missing prerequisite and smallest meaningful next test. Explicit
positive and negative evidence must survive the matrix; missing calibration
must not erase observed motion or become a measured discrepancy.
