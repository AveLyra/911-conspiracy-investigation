## Consequences to carry into the joint crosswalk

Keep NIST's concrete event discrepancies and UAF's unreplicated kink. Treat
both hypotheses with the same feature/time/camera requirements, but do not
pretend the two studies perform identical forward calculations. None of the
five families provides a common, independently reproduced residual yet.
That does not make each source observation worthless or establish equal odds.
The highest-value comparison is matched-view onset/shape histories using
authenticated native cases, fixed input roles and fixed alignment, with
landmark uncertainty independently assessed. Unknown clocks, amplified plots
and adjustable inputs must remain visible. No causal ranking is newly earned.
