## Conclusion

The existing drafts already support this descriptive crosswalk. They name NIST's fire/Column 79 account, UAF's connection/progression objections and prescribed-removal cases, shared-geometry controls, multiview annotation, and an underdetermined category. They do **not** yet specify the five observable contracts. Completing those row-level definitions is useful; filling every generic statistical field or creating another engine framework is not required for this unit.

## Minimum mapping

Every row needs the current protocol's source/version/page/figure and case identifiers; feature, event criterion and view; native time origin and any reported alignment; imposed/fitted/computed/reported-observation role; calibration/shared evidence versus unused evidence; reported prediction and observation with units/uncertainty or explicit absence; independent measurement status; comparability limit; and falsifier or indispensable missing input. These are source-review contents, not proposed engine-schema changes.

| Fixed observable | Necessary discriminating row detail |
| --- | --- |
| East-penthouse onset | Name visible-motion criterion and feature/view; distinguish it from a hidden structural-failure event. Identify whether this event sets or fits the alignment. |
| West-penthouse roofline passage | Define the component and roofline, including whether the reference moves; distinguish first/final passage and censoring. Do not substitute generic west-penthouse failure. |
| North-façade onset/trajectory | Identify tracked roof feature, onset criterion, displacement/velocity/acceleration quantity, units, projection and clock. It is not automatically center-of-mass motion or directly measured support loss. |
| Lateral deformation | Specify feature, component/direction, reference, registration and available calibration. Missing components are unknown, not zero; corner motion is not footprint containment. |
| North-roof kink | Specify the shape-change criterion and view/visibility limitations; distinguish appearance time from initiation and computed emergence from an imposed condition. |

Reported source quantities can be transcribed with their limitations without making new kinematic measurements. The draft's video/clock/geometry/point-identity calibration requirement remains applicable to any future independent measurement. An unavailable shared view or clock may make a row noncomparable; it does not justify an invented shift or tolerance.

## Existing fields versus missing decisions

`primary_outcome`, `methodology`, `analysis_mode: exploratory`, `causal_claim: false`, inclusion rules, controls and stopping rule already provide the appropriate descriptive frame. The collection plan already requires source pins, assumptions, missingness reasons and distinct observation/independent-unit identifiers. Its unavailable-input list is a declared limitation, not a fresh determination that no relevant release exists.

What remains genuinely missing is the concrete row content above and the exact matched contrast, if comparison is possible. The analysis commitment's empty `contrast_definition` does not erase the contrast vocabulary already present elsewhere. Conversely, that vocabulary does not establish matched cases or independent evidence. The protocol's `sampling_unit`—a proposition or reproducible run—is an accounting grain, not proof of statistical independence: five rows are not five independent experiments, and model runs may share observations, inputs and tuning.

`Claim-level evidence grade` in the draft calibration field is not a physical measurement unit or a numerical uncertainty budget. Preserve that distinction rather than mechanically supplying bounds. The exclusion language concerning unsupported intentional-demolition claims concerns warranted inference/evidence eligibility; it should not suppress contrary observations or prescribed-removal outputs, nor require identifying an actor before examining physical predictions. The draft decision-owner label supplies no user authorization.

## Optional scaffold and prospective testing

Confidence levels, null values, expected-effect direction, sample-size/power rules, multiplicity, randomization, assignment masking and canaries are not automatically necessary for this source crosswalk. Applicable privacy/provenance controls remain necessary. A later quantitative confirmatory test would need its own relevant estimator, event/clock/calibration definitions, tolerances, exclusions, missingness treatment, independence assumptions, decision rule and reproducible inputs fixed before that test—not merely completed placeholders.

These remain drafts, not an accepted or frozen comparison; the JSON provenance specifies review-only authority and scientific-evidence ineligibility. The historical Luna section reports an earlier ledger review, not a current recount performed here. No present ledger state was inspected. This protocol precedes the bounded rereading, but the historical material and prior findings are already known: this is neither a blind holdout nor a new confirmatory causal experiment.
