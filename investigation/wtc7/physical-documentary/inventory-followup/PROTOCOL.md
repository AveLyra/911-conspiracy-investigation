# Steel-inventory follow-up protocol

**Declared:** 2026-09-09, before new source retrieval and inventory-row interpretation. Research-only WP5/Q09; controlled by charter (historical local reference `../../CHARTER.md`; not included here). This is a bounded continuation of sample-provenance SP-L04 and SP-L05, not a new causal theory or population survey.

## Question and fixed targets

Find the original or a provenance-disclosed public copy of (1) the SEAoNY/FEMA Appendix D steel spreadsheet as of March15,2002, and (2) the specific shipment/Exhibit E record cited on pages84–85 of the public April15,2020 request for correction. Preserve the existing14-page Appendix D mirror unchanged. Determine whether acquired material contains actual item rows, an aggregate, an index, a cover, a receipt, or an attachment locator.

Start with the already preserved RFC pages and their hyperlinks. Follow directly linked public original attachments first, then bounded exact-title/filename/item-ID searches on FEMA/NIST/SEAoNY and the cited public host. At most two targeted query rounds after resolving the RFC's exact identifiers; stop when the original row/attachment target is obtained or those source routes yield a documented access/completeness limit. No broad personnel, political-motive, laboratory or media search.

## Comparison fields and controls

For every useful row retain source/page, inventory identifier, building attribution and qualification, member type/dimensions, collection date/location, photo/video reference, coupon status, shipment reference, destination and any later renumbering. Keep unspecified fields blank/unknown, not inferred from neighboring rows. Reading two copies of the same spreadsheet is not independent corroboration.

Test a Sample1 join only if shared member/coupon/photo identifiers or an explicit contemporaneous crosswalk support it. Similar steel grade, appearance, a WTC7 label, candidate count or aggregate NIST shipment total is insufficient. Preserve source uncertainty and conflicting revisions. Distinguish actual shipment from a future plan, custodian receipt from sender list, and receipt of an object from acceptance of its building identity.

## Safety and output

Use only ordinary public non-sensitive sources. No outreach, accounts, payment, FOIA, private-person profiling, canonical promotion, publication, or external feedback. Explicit unsafe refusals stop the route. Headers remain local and must not be printed. Failed/in-progress downloads are not acquired sources. Use read-only PDF review, source hashes, exact physical/printed-page pins and disclosure of derivative OCR/extraction warnings.

Outputs stay in this directory: source inventory/access log, source-pinned report/lead disposition and actual integrity/inspection checks. Record generic software pain points for parent deduplication only. A missing record after this bounded search is not evidence of historical destruction or concealment.
