# FEMA steel inventory: recovered source review

This historical September 2026 follow-up reports an examination of the full 27-page FEMA Appendix D and its steel spreadsheet. It preserves four WTC 7-labeled component entries across three numbered items, records the original photo identifiers and asks how those pieces were attributed and tracked. The inventory labels do not independently prove building origin, receipt by NIST, or a connection to metallurgical Sample 1. The report also distinguishes a now-located full version from an earlier incomplete mirror; the earlier missing pages did not prove destruction or withholding.

The [report](report.md), [source inventory](source-inventory.md), [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [search log](search-acquisition-log.md), and [validation](validation.md) are historical research, not a new acquisition or independent confirmation of every statement. This package omits the original PDF, archived HTML, image derivatives and HTTP headers; the [historical checksums](SHA256SUMS) identify local source material, not included files. Raw media and LFS objects remain local. The currently linked historical FEMA address returned an access error during a later review; availability today is not established by the older receipt’s reported HTTP 200. The historical 404 for Exhibit E likewise reflects an earlier search, not a claim that the item is unavailable now.
