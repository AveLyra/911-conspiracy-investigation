# WP4 comparator primary-source check: three-case pilot

Date: 2026-09-08. Status: research working note, independently rechecked against public source pages but not independently replicated engineering; not a case fact, expert opinion, or completed WP4 study. Governing scope: [CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md), especially Q08 and WP4.

The three existing comparator leads remain useful, but their labels require different qualifications. Capital One has a contractor completion account explicitly identifying explosive demolition, beyond the city's advance announcement. HBI is a mixed-method project and cannot receive a single nonexplosive event label. Plasco has primary investigator attribution to fire and proposed structural reconstructions; agreement with selected photographs does not establish the unique physical mechanism.

## Scope, selection, and preservation

Selection was fixed to the three cases already used in [the video source manifest](../../research/wtc7-video-comparison/source-manifest.csv): DEM-01/02, DEM-07, and ACC-01/02. This is a retrospective convenience sample to audit label provenance, not an exhaustive search for fires, demolitions, survival outcomes, or counterexamples. No causal ranking, population frequency, training label, or numerical likelihood follows from it. No new footage was acquired or measured, no source was contacted, and no legal SRC/FACT/EXH records were created.

The evidence-falsification skill governed the distinction between source statements, observed event records, inference, and model output. PDF read-only guidance was used for the city FAQ: extracted pages were available, but visual screenshot attempts failed. No claim here depends on its graphic layout. Only this analytical note was saved; original webpage/PDF bytes were not acquired, so there are no new source-byte hashes or native-file custody claims. URLs, access outcomes, editions, and available section/page pins below make the check repeatable, subject to later website changes. Search-index text is explicitly distinguished from a directly retrieved page; it is an access derivative, not a second source.

## Claim ledger and disconfirming checks

Grades assess the **specified claim**, not an entire source: A = directly established; B = strongly supported; C = materially assumption-dependent; D = underdetermined; E = unsupported/contradicted. None of the structural simulations was rerun.

| Claim under review | Evidence layer / support | Replication and assumptions | Strongest alternative or weakening evidence | Grade and permitted wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Capital One's comparator event was an explosive demolition. | Inferred from official plan plus contractor execution account, C1/C2. | Source text rechecked; no raw execution records or independent field replication. Assumes indexed account accurately represents the contractor's page and refers to the clip event. | Source error or a mislabeled clip would weaken that link. A generic advance schedule alone would not prove completion. | **B** for event method; keep clip authentication separate. |
| HBI demonstrates a documented mechanical induced-collapse method. | Participant project accounts, H1/H2. | Documentary recheck only. An identified video could therefore show an explosive operation. | **B** for use of mechanical methods somewhere in the project; **D** for any unassigned clip's method. |
| The existing HBI 2012 project date is established. | Existing manifest versus H2 completion status and award entries. | No day-level execution chronology inspected. | H2 says completed 2011; its 2012 entry concerns award shortlisting. | **E** as an unqualified completion year; use “completed 2011, per contractor,” pending execution records. |
| Plasco's proposed structural failure chain has been independently validated. | P1/P2 model outputs; P3 inferred reconstruction. | No code, inputs, recovered evidence set, or independent holdout evaluated here. | P1 selects the scenario matching observed imagery. That imagery cannot also count as an independent test of scenario selection. Other pathways may fit the same visible terminal behavior. | **D** for unique mechanism identification; **C** for reported model-based pathways. |
| The inspected fire models establish collapse irrespective of uncertain inputs. | P4's own reported sensitivity contradicts this generalization. | Abstract-level result, not rerun. | P4 reports failure for its estimated 1900 MJ/m² fire load and no structural failure at 511 MJ/m²; it also reports columns retaining load capacity under modeled masonry protection. This differs from P2's external-column-buckling result, without proving either study wrong: their assumptions and modeled stages require comparison. | **E** for input-independent collapse; retain the non-collapse run and protection difference as material uncertainties. |

The P4 result is contrary evidence against treating all published fire reconstructions as one validated, uniform account. P2's reported agreement with photographs is evidence of compatibility at the reported level, not physical proof. Neither uncertainty nor model disagreement establishes explosive intervention. Conversely, a published fire attribution does not by itself close all alternative-mechanism questions. This pilot supplies no causal ranking for WTC 7.
