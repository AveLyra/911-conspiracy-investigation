# Fire-image annotation: public availability summary

**Public replacement summary, November 2026.** This is not the original complete annotation package. The [published WTC 7 status](../STATUS.md) describes earlier image reviews that distinguish visible flame-like features from obstruction and no-detection limits, and reports differences in some paired annotations. It also records that differing treatment of ambiguous openings affects comparisons.

Those reports are not direct measurements of interior temperatures or complete floor-by-floor fire exposure. The underlying images, detailed annotation sheets and original full review are not included here, and this page does not claim a new image review or cause determination. The [public source-comparison note](source-comparison.md) identifies the separately documented modeling questions.
