## Interpretation checks

- **Positive evidence and claim ceiling.** The report properly credits the
  saved right-corner array's late conditional gravity-scale curvature rather
  than treating imperfect provenance as affirmative evidence against it. It
  consistently calls the fitted values assigned-unit coefficients and separates
  their physical application from clock, calibration, projection and feature
  correspondence. Neither a near-gravity example nor short-window excursions
  above the reference are made into whole-building free fall, a force estimate,
  universal support loss or a mechanism finding.

- **Two tracks and sample 348.** The feature descriptions agree with my frozen
  eight-frame review: track01 plausibly follows the image-right outer top/side
  corner, including its small final visible remnant; track02 is a less distinct
  main upper-rim location near the raised structure's base, not that structure's
  top. The report does not turn the arrays into a raised-structure/main-facade
  timing pair. Its final-sample ambiguity and foreground/smoke qualification for
  track02 remain explicit, including beside the terminal displacement. Eight
  sampled associations do not verify all 71 positions or persistent material
  identity. Similar terminal displacements are correctly distinguished from
  equal acceleration histories, rigidity and symmetry.

- **Window and model presentation.** The report states that all declared
  windows are retained, describes the overlap and duplicate-clock dependence,
  and distinguishes window-wise quadratic coefficients from instantaneous
  measurements. The 11.0–13.4-second example and other displayed examples are
  expressly post-run illustrations, not prospectively identified physical
  intervals. A low full-span coefficient is not used to erase a late segment;
  a late coefficient is not extrapolated over the whole record. Linear versus
  curved position fits can supply affirmative curvature evidence in the saved
  data, while smaller cubic residuals are not a physical-model certificate.
  This assesses the wording, not whether the embedded figure or linked arrays
  actually contain every promised datum.

- **Keyframe and clock semantics.** The post-fit keyframe check is identified
  as exploratory. The report preserves its narrow positive result: the current
  saved state lists all 71 positions as keys rather than labelling some as
  interpolation gaps. Manual-or-automatic marking and older-data loading
  behavior prevent that membership from authenticating manual work, independent
  errors or an interpolation-free editing history. More precisely, the source
  fallback concerned missing or empty keys; this project's explicit nonempty
  keys do not establish whether an earlier save had such a history. The report
  does not claim otherwise. Exact agreement of nominal and current encoded
  times at the selected indices is properly described as redundant calculations,
  not a second independent historical clock authentication.

- **Calibration and perturbations.** The report keeps the affirmative
  approximately fifteen image-repetition lead distinct from identified physical
  endpoints. Its 14/15/16 interval table is a set of dimensional scenarios, not
  a measured error distribution. The small saved-angle/zero-angle difference
  is not promoted to a perspective bound. Unit-coordinate response bounds and
  the conditional clock-scaling relation likewise are not claims that those
  perturbations actually occurred. No physical floor count is established by
  this review.

- **Differences, symmetry and cause.** The report distinguishes differences in
  the exported arrays from a unique explanation of those differences. Its
  failure to establish one uniform gravity acceleration does not become proof
  that no region had gravity-compatible motion. Material motion, changing
  silhouette, projection and annotation remain alternatives without a selected
  mechanism. The defined-system/contact/force-accounting requirement correctly
  prevents point-history kinematics alone from proving disappearance of all
  support. Fire-induced and deliberate support-removal accounts are not
  discriminated by these fits alone.

- **Next discriminating work.** Dense late-segment re-annotation, intervening
  mark inspection, stationary-reference checks and visible calibration
  alternatives would test concrete motion/measurement propositions: whether the
  track difference and fitted curvature persist under independently recorded
  feature associations and image-motion corrections. This advances the physical
  applicability of the trajectories without assuming that such checks must
  eliminate or preserve the difference. It is not itself completed work,
  permission from a human reviewer, or a substitute for structural initiation
  and force/mass evidence. Fitted event boundaries remain distinct from observed
  support-failure times.
