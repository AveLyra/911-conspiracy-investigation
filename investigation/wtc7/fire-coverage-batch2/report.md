## How the observation was kept separate from the caption

The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) and six disclosed text controls preceded new
structured observations. Each observer viewed all 13 complete report-native
JPEGs at original dimensions. No new crops, enhancements, resampling or flame
classifier were used. Root knew prior research and figure/page associations;
the fresh reviewer did not read captions, prior labels or root's labels before
freezing. Visible source marks and general knowledge limit blindness. Both
reviewers are AI agents, not qualified human forensic examiners.

Both records were frozen before root opened the fresh review or new source
attributions. Root then viewed all ten complete source-page renders: physical
pages 198, 199, 235, 239, 243, 244, 246, 255, 265 and 285. A separate source
reviewer checked all 23 preserved page renders and selected surrounding text.
Root additionally read surrounding text on physical pages 238, 254, 264 and
284. See [source review](source-review.md) and
[source attributions](source-attributions.json) for exact coverage and lineage.

Smoke appearance, luminous morphology, target evaluability and localized
nondetection are separate fields. A smoke veil and a qualified no-flame
observation can coexist. Rectangles are approximate locators, not flame
segmentations, aperture counts or coverage fractions. No interior absence is
inferred from an empty list or an obscured region.

## What this changes in the model audit

The earlier [source-input comparison](/Users/admin/docs/911/research/sherlock-wtc7-investigation/fire-annotation/source-comparison.md)
remains the seven-question FA-C01–FA-C07 audit, not a validated reconstruction.
This extension supplies specific additional constraints and dependencies:

1. **Fire timing and ventilation (FA-C01/02).** There are useful visible
   features on additional frontages, but several assigned times use the same
   fire/window appearances later evaluated. The independently timed anchors,
   source-to-window observations, interpolation choices and held-out evidence
   must be separated before claiming independent spread validation. This

2. **Floor-specific thermal histories (FA-C03/04/07).** North luminous forms
   attributed to Floor 12 around 3:11–3:17 p.m. add early-phase visual coverage;
   they do not cancel the report's separately acknowledged later-duration

   useful floor-registration target, but its steep angle and inferred time
   cannot supply a precise thermal correction. Test same floor, location,
   observable and time interval against actual inputs/outputs.
3. **South-side uncertainty (FA-C05).** The newly reviewed aerial pair does
   not resolve south exposure: it is a distant, obscured photograph plus an
   adjusted enlargement used in the report's damage discussion. Lack of a
   luminous identification is not evidence that modeled south fires did not
   occur. It also does not provide affirmative confirmation that they did.
4. **Late transient (separate source lead).** The report associates 5-158
   with a roughly six-second sequence of pulses near a Floor 13 window,
   but expressly lacks an exact event time and does not identify a known
   internal event. Both reviews recognize a luminous form; neither a still
   nor that recognition tests duration, pressure, local failure or initiation
   mechanism. Locate and authenticate the full clip and its zoom/time lineage
   before synchronizing it with collapse or using it to favor any mechanism.
