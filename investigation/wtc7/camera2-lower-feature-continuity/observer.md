## Coding meaning

Visibility and correspondence are separate fields. V means a distinct candidate
of the defined appearance is visible; A means its relevant region/edge is
visible but the junction identity is ambiguous; U means I cannot distinguish
a candidate of the defined appearance after complete-frame inspection.

`appearance-consistent` concerns the stated shape/context, not uninterrupted
material identity. `uncertain` retains competing edge/corner identifications.
`changed` concerns a changed visible silhouette that no longer unambiguously
supplies the predefined junction; it is not a diagnosis of the material point's
physical fate. `not-assessable` means correspondence cannot be judged from
the visible information. A/U does not mean the physical feature ceased to exist.

NE is the left outer lower-roof corner neighborhood, EC the lower-left raised
step foot, WC the lower-right raised step foot, and NW the right outer
top/side corner. Names are source labels, not authenticated compass or member
identities. The clear early WC/NW and later EC appearances serve as visual
references within this declared sample. This does not impute continuity across
earlier ambiguity or between sparsely selected frames.
