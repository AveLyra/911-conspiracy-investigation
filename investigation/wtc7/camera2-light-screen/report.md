# Camera2 full-interval light-change screen

2026-09-24. Working research for charter Q07, not an expert opinion, legal
finding or completed investigation. [Final critical review](critical-review.md)
completed; its two corrections are incorporated and its limits remain.

## Result and significance

The complete primary review of **421 consecutive held Camera2 frames** retains
small bright-point appearances and changing pale roof/cloud boundaries.
Neither “no light changes” nor “identified explosive flashes” describes this
result. The reviewed record does not establish a WTC7-origin emitting source
for these candidates and does not establish that any physical flashes were
absent elsewhere, behind obstructions or below the readers' sensitivity.

The most specifically localized points are at source indices **6925 and 6970**,
projected near the top of a thin upright above a left foreground roof.
Adjacent-frame comparison supports their relative prominence in the images;
it does not establish physical attachment, depth, emitter identity, a beacon,
recurrence rate, or a relationship to WTC7. Reflection/illumination/visibility
and access-copy processing remain alternatives alongside a physical light.

**This screen does not change the causal assessment.** It neither validates
NIST's fire-to-failure sequence nor supplies affirmative evidence of deliberate
support removal. It narrows what can responsibly be said about these images:
candidates exist and deserve preservation, but their physical interpretation
is unresolved. No strict cause ordering or numerical probability is earned;
that is not a 50–50 conclusion.

## Disagreement is part of the result

In the fixed 90-row comparison, one candidate selection is shared: 6925.
There are 15 C/N differences on 6954–6958, 6984–6988 and 7009–7013. Both readers
describe the evolving pale roof/cloud boundary; they differ about selecting
that continuing appearance under the qualitative C/N rule. Their descriptions
do not establish opposing observations of whether a pale boundary exists.
Context and subjective salience can contribute, but their respective effects
are not measured. No consensus vote or post-result threshold change resolves it.

Fourteen other differing rows are the sampled reader's missing incoming
context, not contradictory negatives or failed images. The matching initial
U/U makes 15 sampled page-first U entries overall. Primary block-boundary rows
also remain separate: 6733 root C/A U and 6873 A N/B U. None is overwritten.

Primary records contain 98 C-coded **presentation rows**, including repeats and
the long continuing rim description. This is not 98 flashes or independent
positive detections. The code schema incompletely standardizes the distinction
between a retained ongoing candidate and “no additional candidate.” The raw
descriptions, candidate locations and uncertainty are more informative than
a single C-count. No agreement percentage or sensitivity estimate is justified.

## What remains unknown; strongest objection

The strongest objection is shared-source and shared-method error: prior-informed
AI readers inspected the same converted grayscale access copy, not authenticated
camera originals, independent light sensors or a calibrated detection task.
Full stored resolution cannot restore discarded color, original exposure,
interlacing/processing history or a measured response to brief light sources.
Presentation-test success does not validate visual salience or physical origin.

Cloud and foreground obscure substantial regions; the target ground-contact
line is not securely located by this screen. Some annotations say the base
is hidden, but that does not distinguish geometric occlusion from an unlocated
or out-of-frame boundary. No N applies to every possible source position.
The underlying extraction's one stereo-layout diagnostic remains disclosed;
there is no new video decode or warning-free-source certification here.

Conversely, these limitations do not show that the video is useless or that
no conspicuous outward light could be seen. The small-point selections show
that a brief apparent change can be noticed in this access copy; they do not
calibrate sensitivity to a different-sized, different-position physical event.
A fair mechanism comparison still needs a specified predicted outward effect,
spatial/recording detection opportunity and competing ordinary explanations.

No original exposure clock, physical duration, intensity, recurrence rate,
cross-camera synchronization, structural-failure time or cause was measured.
R1 and connection-curve actual-human measurement gates remain unmet. This work
does not clear all Luna work or authorize Sherlock/Faraday acceptance, legal
promotion, external disclosure, filing, commit or push.

charter's applicable actual-human and synthetic controls.
