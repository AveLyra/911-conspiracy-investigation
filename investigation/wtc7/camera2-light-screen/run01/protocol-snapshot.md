## Observable and record schema

Look across each consecutive six-frame page for **conspicuous apparent light
changes**: localized light patches, bright edge appearances or broader image
brightness changes. Keep uncertainty and possible one-frame candidates; do
not require multi-frame persistence, a particular location, explosive-like
shape or agreement with any collapse hypothesis. Every frame must receive:

- **C — candidate/uncertain change:** a conspicuous possible light change
  relative to in-scope neighbors worth retaining, even if cloud, newly exposed
  background, reflection, moving edges or processing could explain it. Describe
  what/where, which frames supply context, and alternatives; do not label it
  an emitted flash. Do not discard a plausible ambiguous candidate by choosing
  its ordinary explanation as fact.
- **N — no additional candidate noticed in this reading:** no conspicuous
  candidate beyond described ordinary scene evolution was selected. This is
  an observer screening disposition, **not** physical absence or a calibrated
  negative test. Explain page-level evolution/visibility and limitations.
- **U — cannot assess:** the frame/display/region or comparison is too
  uncertain for this screen. State why. Hidden regions are always outside the
  observation, even if the visible part receives N; N never covers an interior.

Record page number/path, actual successful/failed display, ordered six indices
and six codes, then location/appearance/context and plausible alternatives.
Within-page descriptors may be shared where genuinely applicable, but a blank
row or automatic default is not an observation. Retain border repeats and any
changed judgment. No inferred physical onset, duration, intensity, calibration,
pixel coordinates, detector score, cross-camera offset, support failure or
cause. Source-index spans locate observations, not authenticated event timing.

The broad candidate definition is intentionally qualitative. Different readers
may have different salience thresholds; agreement is not sensitivity. Near-
static glints and evolving cloud boundaries can be recorded as context without
being uniquely identifiable sources. No post-result threshold tuning, smoothing
or deletion to obtain a preferred count or causal answer.
