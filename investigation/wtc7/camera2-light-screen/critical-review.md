### 1. Keep the falsifier on the same evidence layer

**Report line129:** the claim is that the two point descriptions are supported
"at image level," but the final column includes "demonstrating an artifact"
as something that would change that claim. A processing artifact could explain
the visible point while leaving the appearance description entirely correct.
Conflating those propositions weakens the intended falsifiability discipline.

**Correction:** distinguish a mistaken description/location or failure to
reproduce the appearance in the specified held bytes from evidence that the
appearance arose in processing. The latter changes physical provenance or
interpretation; it does not by itself refute the visible appearance.
Example: "Reinspection showing the specified visible point or relative
prominence was misdescribed would weaken the image-level claim; establishing
a processing artifact would explain its origin without erasing that appearance."

This is a real inferential distinction, not evidence that the candidate is
either an artifact or a physical emitter. The alternative remains unresolved.
