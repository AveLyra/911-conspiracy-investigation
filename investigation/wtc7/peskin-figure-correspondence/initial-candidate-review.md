## Evidence strength and next discrimination

- **Directly observed in these derivatives (A):** the shortlisted samples
  contain changing upper/lower close-ups and wider views; the upper strong
  flame groups and lower small patches occur in the same recognizable scene.
  This is not an independent source path for the report's historical account.
- **Visually supported but conditional (C):** 2521/T148 and 2524/T149 are useful
  coarse-layout leads. The strongest contrary evidence is the residual
  framing and dynamic-shape difference, with genuine encoding/contrast/blur
  alternatives to a different exposure. A fixed structural alignment followed
  by better held-out dynamic agreement in other frames would weaken either
  preference. This shortlist is insufficient to bound either candidate set.
- **Not established (D):** unique exposure, original frame number, exact source
  separation between the report images, historical clock/cadence, edit-map
  continuity, or a NIST timing error. One-second samples can skip the sought
  image or represent blended/repeated frames; mere failure to find an exact
  sampled match is not adverse proof about the report.
