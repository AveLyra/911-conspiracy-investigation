not independently observed plumbing.

## Comparison and falsifiers

The OEM project context, tank-to-pump arrangement and10-gauge/protection
description are consistent with a related enclosure scheme, but are not an
explicit same-segment/as-built join. The first-floor location is a report-based
candidate, not a location read off E5. The municipal6/8-inch difference remains
unresolved. It is separate from the smaller fuel-carrying pipe dimensions in
the report; no conduit diameter is converted into hydraulic capacity.

NIST already describes enclosure protection. Thus the newly held drawing
cannot alone support a claim that the published OEM description omitted all
such protection. Conversely, the report cannot authenticate the unlocated
construction/inspection records. Similar wording could derive from the same
design sources and must not be counted as independent confirmation of installation.
No actual model-input comparison or collapse-mechanism test occurred.

The concrete next discriminator is a location/reference join through the cited
mechanical plan or Figure6-1, followed by actual E5/SK-58 disposition and
installation/inspection evidence. A source identifying different runs would
weaken the same-run discrepancy; an explicit same-run revision record would
strengthen a change-history interpretation. No ranking or intent finding follows.
