## What the difference means for the investigation

The **document-content claims are directly established within the acquired
copy** (grade A). A relationship within the same OEM design project is well
supported; treating E5 as an approved replacement for SK-58 or as a description
of the actual installed conduit remains underdetermined (grade D).

Possible explanations of the diameter difference include a later shop-design
change, a different portion of the route, or an unresolved drawing/error issue.
Neither the later listed date nor matching channel designation chooses among
them. The strongest objection to treating them as contradictory installed
conditions is that neither is established as an as-built record of the same
location. Conversely, their shared project identity is not enough to dismiss
the differing specifications as immaterial.

This source supports neither an unprotected-conduit assumption nor a verified
intact-protection assumption for the event. It supplies technical detail that
can be tested against location, approval, construction and inspection records.
No exact link to a NIST fuel scenario or consequential building member is
demonstrated here. A model discrepancy requires that link and the model's
actual input; it cannot be inferred from an isolated design difference.

No causal ranking changes. These pages do not affirmatively identify deliberate
weakening, concealment, adequate capacity, a fuel release or its absence. Missing
cross-references in this item do not establish their absence from the archive.
