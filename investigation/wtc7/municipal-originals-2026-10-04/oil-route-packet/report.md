## What the actual designs add

FSK-50 requires coordination and field verification through the pit area,
with existing steel, new steel/mezzanine slab, elevator clearances and routing
up to the fuel tank. Those requirements are not completed field checks. Its
RM#23-09 plan label cannot alone fix the entire route on the twenty-third
floor when its separate section labels ground, third and fourth floors.
Partial W16/W14 labels do not identify complete structural sections. The
six-inch pipe callout is not independently assigned a precise fuel-line role.
The stated protection detail is evidence against describing this design as
simply bare, unprotected fuel lines, but does not demonstrate installed protection.

S-S-1 and SKS-S-2 now provide actual engineering content, not just titles.
The first permits a notch at indicated beams subject to the companion detail;
the second shows reinforcement on both sides. The opening response on page7
also conditions its OK on plates and welds. It would be inaccurate to omit
these conditions and describe the record as unconditional removal of support.
Conversely, a drawn reinforcement condition is not a demonstration that it
was sufficient, followed, inspected or still present in2001. These records
do not supply all necessary member sections, loads, joints and field changes.

FSK-50's fuel-line encasement detail and S-TS-7's apparent three-inch minimum
encasement around depicted structural framing are not automatically conflicting
dimensions of one object. Exact segment/component and revision joins must
precede that comparison. Roof antenna support additions likewise do not by
themselves identify a connection to the observed penthouse failure.
