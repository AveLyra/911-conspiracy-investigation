# OEM addendum and fuel room hatch request

October 4, 2026. Working research. Two further official-portal records narrow
the follow-up from the [1998 design comments](../report.md): a dated drawing
index and a March 2000 request to modify a fuel-room hatch. Neither supplies
the actual structural revisions or completed fire-protection work. This adds
specific documentary tests, not a demonstrated cause or a ranking change.

## What was actually found

**The addendum is dated May 20, not May 4.** Page 2 of
[NYC-WTC_000173199.pdf](NYC-WTC_000173199.pdf), Bates 173200, identifies SHCA
project 5576A, OEM/7WTC and Addendum 1 dated May 20, 1998. It modifies specifications
and drawings dated May 4. Pages 3-6 are a drawing list dated May 15, with varied
sheet issue dates. Page 1 is a routing/cover version partly obscured by an
overlaid address block, with a May 20 received stamp. These are source date
fields and reproduced marks, not independently authenticated delivery events.

The list expressly limits issuance under the addendum to bold-italic entries.
On p.5/Bates 173203, S-1 through S-5 are listed with May 13, 1998 issue dates:
first-floor, seventh-floor, twenty-third-floor, roof framing and sections.
Their presence in an index does not establish new issuance under this addendum,
actual drawing contents, correction of an April comment, approval or installation.
The six-page file contains neither the S-sheets nor the mentioned specification
table of contents. The earlier selection based on a catalog phrase about May 4
has therefore been narrowed, not treated as a proven deadline-response link.

**A March 21, 2000 memo identifies a specific requested protection change.**
Page 1 of [NYC-WTC_000172166.pdf](NYC-WTC_000172166.pdf), Bates 172166, is an SHCA
memo to DCAS/DRES for the same explicitly named project 5576A. It reports that
a preliminary walk-through for final inspection led the Port Authority to
request a self-closing tank-room access hatch connected to the Inergen discharge
system. It says the contractor had been contacted and a wiring diagram and
pricing were being prepared. That is an attributed report of an inspection-stage
request and preparation, not a final inspection certificate or completion record.

Page 2/Bates 172167 is a Bilco Type FR product/specification sheet describing a
two-hour fire-rated door and automatic closing. Its rating language is conditional
on specified testing/exposure and closed-door conditions; this is not testing of
the building's installed assembly or proof that this product was installed.
The small diagram and blank model/material fields do not establish an as-built
configuration. Fax counters show 2/3 and 3/3, while this PDF has two pages. That
is a context gap, not evidence that a substantive page was intentionally withheld.

| Does this identify a collapse mechanism or deliberate intervention? | No. No member exposure/capacity calculation, event-day control state or operation is documented. | Building/system-specific links to actual exposure, load paths and motion; intervention would additionally require affirmative operational/material evidence. |

The strongest ordinary explanation is continuing design coordination and
inspection identifying a correction. These are affirmative records of that
process; they make it inappropriate to assume no review occurred. The strongest
remaining concern is whether the identified work was completed and functional.
These sources do not justify assuming either successful completion or a defect
persisting until September 2001. A commissioning/acceptance record could weaken
the latter concern; evidence of incomplete work could strengthen it, but still
would not demonstrate a collapse-causing fire path.

The new memo does not by itself establish the tank capacity, floor, actual fuel
inventory or connection to a particular structural member. Do not transfer
the hatch issue to other tenant fuel systems or equate it with the parent's
generator ventilation comments. These sources improve document specificity,
not the verified physical model.
