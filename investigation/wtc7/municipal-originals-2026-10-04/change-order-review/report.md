## What the letters and drawings establish

CO38's operative description names additional drains in H.C. toilets
23-32 and 23-3 and a new drain in sprinkler closet 23-22. It includes plumbing
overtime for the floor below and ceiling patching. Preserve the literal
23-3 room string; do not silently supply a missing digit from an overlapping
plan label. The contractor's seventh-floor-louver heading could reflect a
reused or broader job heading, but its origin is not resolved here. It does
not override the body or put this work at a first-floor beam.

The contractor's statement that it is proceeding is affirmative reported
progress or commitment. Blank recipient acceptance fields do not erase that
statement or the later approval table. Conversely, neither the statement nor
the table establishes completed drains, inspection, payment or event-day
condition. The table's CO38 row reports $8,346 proposed and $7,000 approved,
consistent with the handwritten reductions. Its condensed description omits
the sprinkler-closet detail; condensation alone does not prove its removal
from the agreed scope.

Matching dates, order/job identifiers, scope and amounts strongly link the
letter manifestations to one commercial transaction. Annotations and a fax
overlay are retained as differences. These are source-family inferences, not
byte-identical-document findings or independent performance corroboration.
The table may itself depend on the same administrative chain.

The accompanying SKP-3/SKP-4 sheets supply actual technical attachments for
drainage, not S-S-1, SKS-S-2 or S-TS-7 structural details. Their relationship
to earlier held plumbing sheets is supported at the identifier/subject level;
