## What the letter establishes

CO016. It explains items from an Ambassador Construction letter dated
October15,1998. Item4 attributes the station change to a Port Authority
comment and calls for a credit. Other items concern space, roof UPS,
dishwasher and dimmer-panel requirements; none supplies a generator-stop
identifier, circuit or relevant drawing number.

Physical page2/Bates171906 concerns shop-drawing changes. It reports qualified
drawing approval while also stating that requested information and responses

work justified without that information. The closing cost paragraph requests
backup and a breakdown and questions the stated costs. Those are the writer's
positions, not an independently demonstrated improper charge.

Past-tense change descriptions are affirmative source assertions, not merely
a proposal title. Their design-review context nevertheless does not establish
