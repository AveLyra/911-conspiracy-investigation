## Service promises are not delivered service

The HVAC packet deserves neither blanket dismissal as an empty proposal nor
promotion to a complete maintenance history. Its terms are specific:

- Physical page 3 proposes quarterly hurricane-damper testing, by powered
  closing and manual opening. The DCAS memo's separate weekly recommendation
  concerns generators; these are not competing frequencies for one system.
- Page 6 promises one-hour response for OEM-deemed emergencies and priority
  over other Kaback customers. Ordinary-hours and extra-charge clauses do not
  establish that emergency response was unavailable outside ordinary hours.
- Page 7 gives a March 1, 1999–February 29, 2000 effective period, distinct
  from the February 11 document date. Neither that period nor renewal language
  establishes actual commencement or service into 2001.
- Page 8 lists equipment and monthly, quarterly and semiannual service groups,
  including 88 hurricane dampers. Filters and belts are expressly included;
  the general parts-payment clause should not erase that specific inclusion.
  Listed ratings are not measured performance or authenticated serial identities.
- Page 9 is a seasonal scope sheet without completed checks, technician
  findings, actual visit dates or test readings. Conditional burner/ignition
  tasks do not establish installation of a particular fuel-burning device.

Pages 4–6 contain exclusions and contingencies. References to fires, explosion,
power failure, contaminants or payment arrears do not establish actual events,
foreknowledge, nonpayment or nonperformance. Chemical water treatment and
excluded chemical coil cleaning are different scopes. This review gives no
opinion on legal enforceability.

ordinary technical/commercial reasoning on the face of the letter. It does
not independently verify that reasoning, the actual engines or any test result.
