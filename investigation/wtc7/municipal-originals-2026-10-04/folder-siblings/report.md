## Evidence weight and disconfirmation

Directly readable dates, clauses and fields establish what these held copies
say (grade A for those source-content observations). Their matching operative
content strongly supports two underlying correspondence families (grade B),
not five independent confirmations of construction. No pixel identity,
signature authentication or exact historical copy chain is claimed.

The strongest limitation of the negative result is document purpose:
proposals and quotations ordinarily need not contain completion records.
Their missing acceptance/closeout fields therefore have little exclusionary
weight beyond these exact copies. Another accepted version, actual drawing,
inspection report or installed-condition record could change the work-status
assessment. The held documents neither prove nor disprove such records exist
elsewhere. No concealment inference or collapse-cause ranking change follows.
