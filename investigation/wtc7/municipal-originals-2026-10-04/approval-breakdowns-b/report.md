$1,884,322. The amount match is a reconciliation lead, not independent proof
that this particular condition was satisfied; a voucher and test-result join

Order 14 in 173949 page 1 and 174004 page 2 credits deletion of **Cosentini's
witnessing of a generator field test**. It does not say all testing was
canceled. The July testing condition is affirmative counterevidence to an
inference that the credit itself proves no testing was contemplated. It does
not establish who ultimately witnessed testing or whether it was adequate.
