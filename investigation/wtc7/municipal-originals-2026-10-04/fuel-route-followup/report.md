## Effect on the engineering comparison

The [earlier comparison](../enclosure-framing/oem-comparison/report.md)
correctly kept conduit and fuel-carrying pipe dimensions separate. This letter
supplies another concrete example of that distinction. The 8-inch conduit is
dimensionally consistent with SK-58, but the shared project and later date
do not establish a same-segment revision. In particular, E5's later date than
SK-58 cannot by itself prove a final universal change from 8 inches to 6:
this still-later proposal again refers to 8 inches.

Two other apparent differences also require identity checks. The letter's
1-1/4-inch oil return is not yet joined to NIST's 1-1/2-inch generator return.
Eight-foot hangers are not necessarily the same support elements as E5's
four-foot straps/cradles. A changed route, different segment, distinct support
type or documentation error remains possible. None is selected merely to
erase a difference, and none is a demonstrated defect without the matching
drawings and dispositions.

The list does not mention concrete fill or a fire rating. Omission from an
additional-work proposal does not prove that base-specification protection
was removed or absent. Conversely, a separate protective detail does not
prove this proposed route was built to it. There is no demonstrated bridge
here to event-day fuel release, a thermal field, a failed member or collapse.

The strongest supported ordinary reading is proposed coordination and pricing
for a route change. The strongest unresolved concern is whether the reviewed,
approved and installed configuration differed materially from the descriptions
used in later analysis. A matched route/revision and installation record could
answer that; the price and fax markings cannot. No intent or concealment
inference is justified by this page.
