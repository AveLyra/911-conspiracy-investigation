## Disagreement and representation limits

The readers agree that S-TS-7 directs restoration of the slab and replacement
of cut top reinforcement after conduits are in place. **Root reads the rebar
overlap as 1 foot 0 inches; the other reader reads 1 foot 6 inches.** Neither
value is adopted as an agreed engineering input. Do not average the readings,
choose one because it appears more plausible, or compute capacity from either.
A clearer source or separately authorized, declared transcription check is
needed to resolve the exact dimension.

Root reads the base date as 10/15/98 and the work-with reference as SKS-S-1;
the other reader regards those fine fields as tentative. The log supplies its
own clear S-TS-7 date field; it does not repair every drawing character.
Reading the work-with text as a sketch prefix plus S-S-1 is a plausible
cross-reference hypothesis, not a verified alias. Other small member,
connection and encasement dimensions remain unsuitable for quantitative use.

The saved page-1 repeat is 3588 by 4800 pixels. The tool displayed it to root
at 2750 by 3680 and to the other reader at 1363 by 1824. The latter is the
same displayed size as that reader's initial view. Higher saved resolution
therefore did not ensure equally improved legibility. Byte-for-byte rendering
reproduction does not resolve this display limitation or the transcription
disagreement. No crop, OCR, enhancement or image-based dimension measurement
was substituted, and no further view occurred during synthesis.

The strongest ordinary interpretation of this packet is coordinated design
revision and transmission, including an affirmative restoration prescription.
That weighs against describing the represented design as simply leaving an
unremedied slab opening. Conversely, prescribed repair is not proof of good
design or compliance; departure from design remains untested. Neither a
column-removal instruction nor a mechanism connecting this local work to
building-wide support loss is established. Fire and deliberate-intervention
explanations gain no demonstrated causal discriminator from these drawings
alone. No numerical probabilities, intent, concealment or legal conclusions
are assigned.
