# Agency approval of OEM change orders

October 4, 2026. Research only. Two public City PDFs supply scans of one
DCAS/DRES letter expressly approving ten OEM change orders, subject to audit.
This strengthens the evidence for commercial authorization of the trench and
reinforcement work. It does not establish what was built, inspected or present
on September 11, 2001, or what effect it had on structural capacity.

The letter is dated January 25, 1999 and marked reissued February 3, 1999.
Its approval total is $78,288. These are not two independent authorizations
merely because two scans survive. No collapse-cause ranking changes from
this result alone.

## Implications for the structural leads

Order 23 explicitly describes **reinforcing beams on the seventh floor**.
That is affirmative stated scope, not an unrestricted permission to weaken
members. It does not show which members were actually reinforced, whether
the work complied with the design, or whether its capacity was adequate.
Order 41 adds the ten-location detail for antenna support; it should not be
relocated to the trench or interpreted as column removal.

Order 40 has a named approving agency, a $21,300 approved amount and a
December 21, 1998 underlying-submission lead. Its description still does not
identify a floor, drawing, member or exact joint. The earlier
[trench drawing review](../oil-route-packet/report.md) preserves a disputed
overlap dimension. The present three-inch welding description cannot resolve
that reading or establish a contradiction without a verified common joint and
governing revision. Different joints, revisions or condensed commercial
descriptions remain possible. No splice-capacity calculation is justified here.

The clearer FSK12 reference is a reading of these new scans; it does not
retroactively amend the uncertainty in the earlier clarification-table notes.
Likewise, neither of these letters supplies order 44R, order 55 or the final
fuel-routing drawings. That is a negative result limited to four pages, not
evidence that those approvals or drawings never existed.
