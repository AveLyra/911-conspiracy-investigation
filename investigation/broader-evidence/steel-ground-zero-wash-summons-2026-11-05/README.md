## State and City instruments

- **New York Executive Order 113.29, October 9, 2001.** The order as reproduced in 9 NYCRR § 5.113 names specified permit requirements in 6 NYCRR Part 364 and specified hazardous-waste requirements in Parts 370–374 and 376. It conditions the suspension on work under State and/or City supervision and gives site, transport and City-owned or operated destination terms. The text does not identify individual loads or suspend an identified City wash-down order. The same compilation includes later extension language, including the July 8, 2002 order that lists 113.29 and states continuation through August 5, 2002. This copy is a publication of the order text, with the State Register issue and executed image still unacquired. [NYCRR compilation](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-york/9-NYCRR-5.113)
- **NYC Council Int 0166-2002.** The proposal specified hours for debris transport and water saturation for material carried in non-airtight containers, with an exception for recovery and transport of human remains. The docket records introduction and referral **April 24, 2002** and **Filed (End of Session)** on **December 31, 2003**, with blank enactment date and law number. The proposal supplies no basis to treat its text as the operative City order or as the rule used for the 2001 summonses. [Council docket and text](https://nyc.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=437739&GUID=5CA3208C-F56D-4C94-BAB9-DEBA232B1D6C)
