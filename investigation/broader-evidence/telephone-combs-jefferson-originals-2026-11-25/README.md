## Observations and competing records

- **Combs FD-302, p. 6**, transcription and investigation dated September 11, 2001: handwritten bottom notation reads **302 107853**. The report states that Phyllis Johnson and Lisa Jefferson each made conversation notes, and that their notes, Jefferson's typed summary and a screen print were given to the interviewing agent. These are the report's claims; the items themselves and their receipts were not acquired. Do not reproduce payment information.
- The **2004 Commission memorandum**, p. 3, records Jefferson saying the FBI had her notes but that she was **not sure** whether Johnson took notes. Preserve the earlier report's affirmative account and her later uncertainty side by side. They reflect different persons' reported knowledge, not necessarily direct contradictions or independent corroboration; neither establishes current custody or contents.
- **Combs FD-302, pp. 6–7**, says paper copies of spreadsheets and a copy on a **3.5-inch diskette in Microsoft Excel 97** were provided to the interviewing agent. It describes a billing-system compilation for Flights 93 and 175 and calls them “cellular calls” while separately describing airphone fields. Preserve that wording; this document alone does not establish mobile-handset service. The diskette, files and receipt remain unacquired. Its relationship to the separate November 6 email and courier package is untested.
- **Time descriptions:** Combs p. 6 says `CALL_DIAL_TIME` values were **believed** to reflect the originating caller's time zone, described there as **“eastern standard time.”** A [June 2002 carrier interview](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/6609c84408b4e4f0d175b6d96be372412227bb61/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-ua93-airfone-clock-attachment-2026-11-06/README.md) describes two specified entries as **Central Time** and attributes them to Fort Wayne. These are reported field descriptions from different interviews; the native versions and switch settings have not been joined. Preserve both without correcting or deciding the clocks.
- **Jefferson FD-302, pp. 8–9:** the image's handwritten footer reads **302 95630**, alongside document **000000127**, supporting identification of the previously text-only FBI Vault release. Page 8 gives approximately **8:45 a.m. Central** and two successive conversational estimates; p. 9 gives approximately **20 minutes** holding after the last exchange. The released interview does not itself determine the correct **9:45 / 9:36 (sic)** entries in the separate worksheet. Retain the later **10- and 15-minute** descriptions and the source-defined event boundaries from the [prior evidence-transcript review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/21b9e9511cc3a0e89df5a014239ef42d9e7edc33/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-beamer-leonard-2026-11-25/README.md).
