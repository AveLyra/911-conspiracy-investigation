# Star-Fix interviews: separately reported time labels

November 25, 2026 · TEL02–TEL04 / AUD016-A1. Two interviews in the same released FBI packet contain different time descriptions. Their speakers and events cannot be equated without additional records.

## Source and observations

We inspected the [2009 Commission archive ZIP](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip) member `911 NARA/Team 7 Files/Team 7 Box 12/T7 B12 DOJ Doc Req 35-13 Packet 4 Fdr- Entire Contents- Response Letters- Reports 403.pdf`, acquired by bounded range November 25; size **3,529,428 bytes**, ZIP CRC `930f2025`, SHA-256 `7ed35efb7825f26cb0c712d334a8b286c74bcc25c5b44e01f227059f446c6dba`. Physical pages **34, 36 and 37** were visually inspected. These are released FBI interview reproductions; their original notes and audio were not acquired.

- On **p. 36 / document 000000158**, the report describes a Star-Fix technician receiving a Flight 93 call **“shortly after 6:00 am pacific time”** on September 11. The subject's name is redacted; we do not reconstruct it. The same report says the technician provided a handwritten statement, and reproduces a typed statement describing the initial call and a manager's taking over. The typed statement itself does not state a time in the inspected passage.
- On **p. 34 / document 000000156**, a separate interview reports that manager Richard Belme took control at **approximately 6:40 am**. It reproduces a statement attributed to him that says **“approximately 0640 PST.”** Neither estimate is a verified switch timestamp. The two reports describe a handoff and different participants; their source-to-event joins and clock bases are unverified.
- The [earlier 2003 Commission-interview review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/1e4baca5c7b93418afab59edf60a4776d85feae6/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-ua93-starfix-maintenance-2026-11-06/README.md) preserves a first-recipient recollection that the call occurred **before the second aircraft hit**, Belme's **6:40 a.m.** estimate, a **6:30 a.m. Pacific** value attributed to the FBI, and an airline description of **9:35**. Do not assume the redacted 2001 subject and a later interviewee are the same individual. These descriptions are not independent synchronized clock measurements.

## Open tests and alternatives

Obtain the actual two handwritten statements and interview notes, their **SF 162** enclosures and receipts, independent clock records, Star-Fix queue/transfer logs and carrier switch data. Establish which event each time labels and whether sources refer to the same call. A transcription, time-zone, recollection, clock or event-boundary difference is possible; none has been tested. The apparent mismatch does not establish a different historical timeline, falsification or motive.
