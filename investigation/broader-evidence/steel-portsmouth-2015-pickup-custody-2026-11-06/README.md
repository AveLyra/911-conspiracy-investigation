# Portsmouth's 2015 World Trade Center steel pickup

November 6, 2026 UTC · STL02–STL03 / B188 / H005

Portsmouth Police's report for July–December 2015, dated April 26, 2016, places the delivery of a World Trade Center steel beam on **September 2, 2015**. The city's memorial page names **Novel Iron Works** as the provider of the flatbed and driver and places collection with the Port Authority at **JFK International Airport**. The recipient, reported pickup location, carrier and date give four keys for locating the shipment record. The artifact number and original structural position are still unknown. [Police report, PDF pp. 14–15](https://files.portsmouthnh.gov/agendas/2016/police/pc052416pp.pdf#page=15) · [City memorial account](https://www.portsmouthnh.gov/city/911-memorial)

The September 2 police account and the September 11 story's “Last month” wording remain unresolved. The AP preview establishes the plan, and it may draw on the same police information as other public descriptions. Approval, physical pickup, receipt, installation and later landscaping are separate events. A difference in which event was described or a reporting error are untested possibilities. The sources support a reported transport and a later physical display. They leave the building, member, warehouse location within JFK and any association with a NIST specimen open.

The 2015 city calendar links the **September 28 Police Commission packet and minutes** at HTML lines 3775–3783. A text-layer search across the 42 packet pages and 10 minutes pages returned zero matches for the specified beam and transport terms. This limited check supplies no positive item locator. OCR misses, other dates and separate records remain possible. The July 2010 list previously examined for Clearwater is a separate, earlier authorization source. No Portsmouth artifact number was assigned from a size or weight resemblance.
