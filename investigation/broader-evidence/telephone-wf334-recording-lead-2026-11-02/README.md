# WF334: a reported saved-message lead and an unlocated follow-up outcome

A newly read intake/lead record says a masked recipient had saved an in-flight message attributed in the text to **“TODD RUBEN,”** described as a passenger on **Flight #77**. It assigns contact about the message’s contents under **WF334**. This is a concrete recording/disposition target, **not a recovered or authenticated message**.

The three pages supply no recording, completed contact report, carrier match or disposition. They do not establish another genuine Flight 77 call or a fabricated call. The reported name is preserved literally; no spelling correction, passenger-list match, recipient relationship or masked identity is supplied.

WF334 gives a specific alleged saved-message lead whose verification and disposition can be checked against that account. **It does not by itself refute the canvass or establish an additional call.** It cannot be assigned to one of the unknown rows, to a device or to a caller/session merely from a name and flight label.

If an authenticated message and carrier/session evidence established another relevant Flight 77 recipient, the scope and attribution inference would need reassessment. An inaccurate or unrelated tip, a misattributed or earlier message, an inconclusive follow-up, and a genuine additional message remain untested possibilities. No one is selected as the explanation. The actual message, contact/disposition and full canvass scope/inclusion rules are the discriminating records.

No association to the separately reviewed operator calls is made from a similar first name or message terminology. This record’s literal name and flight description remain its own unverified allegation.
