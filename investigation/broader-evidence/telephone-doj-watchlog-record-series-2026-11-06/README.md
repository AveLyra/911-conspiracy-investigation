# DOJ Watch Log custody and the separate correspondence index

November 6, 2026 UTC · **TEL03–TEL05 / AUD016-A1 / TC05–TC06**. A NARA-approved schedule identifies the DOJ Justice Command Center's **paper Watch Log and Message Log reports dated 1986 to June 2006**. This is the first located schedule in this review that explicitly covers this record type and includes September 2001 in its date range. The schedule separately describes an electronic system developed in June 2006. For the September 11 Watch Officer Log cited by Commission staff, the paper series and any copy transmitted to the Commission are the most direct retrieval routes. Actual custody of that day's log remains open. ## The 9:33 entry remains a separate record target

Note 268 attributes **9:33** to an entry indicating that the Solicitor General had contacted the center about his wife's call and requested someone come to his office. That is a staff description of a notification to DOJ. The note supplies neither the original log field and clock convention nor an aircraft-to-office connection time. Keep that description beside the Keyton account of a live-operator collect call followed by a later direct call and the Voss belief that both calls were collect. The schedules supply no timing or routing observation that selects either account.
