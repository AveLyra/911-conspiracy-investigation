November 9, 2026 Eastern · B129 / PD01 / PD07. It refers to an unclassified copy of the memo circulated without its attachment. The archive folder label gives the **April 10** Commission meeting. The memo date and filing context are separate. Page 3 lines 25–38 distinguishes an index of interview reports from reports outside that index. Page 4 lines 6–22 separately seeks agency-origin records, including DOT as an example, the compilation of answers to the initial **13-question interrogatory**, and the **index of document files held at the Ford Building**. ## AWA-1022 is a report extract

NARA's FAA Box 99 finding aid and object record identify **AWA-1022 / NAID 7601607**. The acquired PDF contains 12 pages and labels itself a publicly released extract of the December 2002 Joint Inquiry report. Its last physical page reproduces portions of Manno’s testimony on FAA access to the Phoenix memo. The testimony excerpt says Joint Inquiry staff brought it to the FAA’s attention. The October 1, 2002 hearing transcript also records Manno saying he did not know the exact date and offering to obtain it. The shared account derives from one witness. A dated showing or visit record would test its timing.
