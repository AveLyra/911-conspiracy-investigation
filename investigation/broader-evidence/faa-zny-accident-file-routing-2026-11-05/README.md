# FAA October 2003 tasking found in the Boston TRACON files

November 5, 2026. B129 / PD07. The [preceding review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/8c169cde263a06e82f18bc69742430f540101402/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-october-tasking-records-2026-11-05/README.md) held references to an October 14 Administrator memorandum and October 8 questionnaire tasking. National Archives scans from the Boston TRACON files preserve a faxed Administrator memorandum with the two Commission requests, its separate fax cover sheet, and an earlier regional questionnaire packet. These are released scans of contemporary records.

## October tasking and its scope

| Source | Observation |
| --- | --- |
| **A90-70**, October 8 regional fax, physical pp. 1–3 | The New England Region Quality Assurance cover names **BOS, A90, ZBW, K90 and PVD** and describes a question sheet and certification guide. The questionnaire asks about government and personal records received or generated within the September 11 calendar day. Government records were required, personal notes encouraged. The question sheet leaves deadline fields blank, with a close-of-business instruction for government records. The certification guide also leaves its deadline fields blank. The visible cover date is **10/08/03**. [A90-70](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7599619) |
| **AWA-785**, October 8, physical pp. 1–2 | New York AFSS reports an enclosed certified re-recording of calls with eyewitness reports and describes it as its only official government record of the events. Its attached certification uses **“close of business today”** for both government records and encouraged personal notes. The two-page scan contains no audio payload. This is New York **AFSS**, distinct from the New York Center and the NY TRACON. [AWA-785](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913615) |
| **A90-72 and A90-73**, October 14 directive in an October 15 regional fax | The outer cover is page **01/08** and the next seven pages are numbered **02/08–08/08** in the outer fax header. The Administrator tells the Management Board to circulate the memo and attachments, search for responsive records, and provide a written confirmation if none were found. The memo acknowledges that some earlier responses may have been incomplete, and highlights after-action material, personnel lists and interviews. It sets **Thursday, October 16** for responses. The Director of Air Traffic cover sets **noon** that day and routes material to **AAT-20**, while the Administrator memo routes responsive documents to **AOA-2**. The two instructions address different points in the routing chain. The fax headers give October 14 and October 15 dates, separate from the response deadline. A handwritten cover annotation records receipt on October 15 at 8:29 and says an email was sent to the AAT-20 addressee that day. The actual email and the addressee's acknowledgment are unacquired. [Cover](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7599621) · [Directive and requests](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7599622) |
| **A90-73**, physical pp. 3–7 | The attached requests include No. 1 item **5** for tracking records and item **7** for after-action material, and No. 2 items **3, 7, 16 and 19** for after-action records, interviews, NORAD communications and internal communications. No. 2 item **20** also references a separate memorandum listing materials turned over to NTSB or FBI. That referenced memorandum is absent from these seven sheets. [A90-73](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7599622) |
| **AWA-781 and AWA-783** | Great Lakes Regional Office and New England Region returns describe employee canvasses. The New England return lists three contributors routed through three paths, **ANE-500**, **AAT-20** and the senior FAA advisor. It lists one survey pending and three people it could not contact. Its visible date is **October 14, 2003**, while the OCR gives **2002**. Both layers are retained. The scans document reporting paths and exceptions, with no inventory of what arrived by each path. [Great Lakes return](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7602291) · [New England return](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913613) |

The regional October 8 fax documents tasking to the five named facilities, including Boston Center. It gives a dated comparison point for the [October 8 Commission field memo](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7602299), which says managers at four centers reported they had never been asked to gather the materials. The fax and memo do not give a common time, recipient acknowledgment or identical search scope. The October 8 regional packet and the New York AFSS certification have parallel questions. Their deadline wording differs, and neither identifies the exact internal AAT-1 memorandum cited in the AAT-20 certifications. Preserve the different document and recipient lists without treating the records as a complete transmission chain.

## New York operational record routes

- **September 14–16, 2001.** The September 16 New York forward reproduces the September 14 regional direction to retain administrative and operational records from **11 0000 UTC to 12 0400 UTC**, with both corresponding local calendar windows stated in the email. A separate September 15 forward reproduces September 14 assignments for the formal accident files **AA11 → Boston, UA175 → New York, AA77 → Washington, UA93 → Cleveland**. These are retention and assembly directions issued in 2001. The Commission sought a wider production in 2003. [Retention direction](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7604059) · [Assembly assignments](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7604061)
- **October 3, 2001.** A read receipt for a message titled **“ODAPS 87/Huntress Transcript”** gives **1:07:00 PM** sent and **1:34:14 PM** read. The scan has no transcript body. It documents the stated message acknowledgment, leaving the attachment, clock basis, AWA-784 item 10 and later Commission delivery unresolved. [Read receipt](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7604065)
- **October 15–16, 2001.** A question about whether Boston would omit personnel statements is followed by headquarters guidance to collect statements from eligible personnel. The response separately leaves Indianapolis contributions to Washington's determination and qualifies its advice on splicing original voice recordings by saying staff believed a hold was in place. Preserve the question and the response as different statements. The package actually assembled and any implemented hold require independent evidence. [Package correspondence, physical pp. 1 and 3](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7604066)
