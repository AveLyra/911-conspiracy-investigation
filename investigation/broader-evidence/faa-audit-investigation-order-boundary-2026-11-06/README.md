# DOT OIG audit status records and the FAA investigation boundary

November 6, 2026 America/New_York · **B129 / PD01–PD04**

A November 2016 DOT Inspector General letter to Congress gives an actual example of the records behind recommendation tracking. Its aggregate reports **540 open audit recommendations**, and its public enclosure, labeled **Non-Sensitive Open Recommendations**, contains **514 numbered rows**. It also expressly distinguishes draft-audit comments from its practice for investigations. The letter and enclosure supply evidence about their own reporting scope and period. The FAA investigation and its August 2006 recommendations remain a separate case. [CC-2017-002, physical pp. 1–4 and 50](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/Johnson-Grassley%20Response_Second%20FY16%20SAR%20Period.pdf#page=2)

## What the order and the congressional record actually cover

- **DOT 8000.6B**, §§1–2 and 7(e)(4)–(5), separates audits and reviews from investigations and points the latter to **8000.8**. It names **Chapter I of 8000.4** and **8000.6A** in its cancellation provisions. It separately attributes cancellation of **Chapter II of 8000.4** to the January 9, 2001 order **8000.8**. Section 7 gives **15 days** for draft comments and **30 days** for responses to outstanding final recommendations when practical, in the audit workflow. It refers to **8000.1C** for audit follow-up. The agency's index leaves the effective date blank. The official PDF was readable through indexed text, with raw PDF acquisition and visual verification still open. [Official order, printed pp. 1 and 7–9](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/docs/dotorders/DOT8000.6B_Office%20of%20Inspector%20General%20Responsibilities%20and%20Departmental%20Coordination%20for%20Audits%20and%20Reviews_NoDate.pdf) · [Agency order index](https://www.transportation.gov/mission/dot-orders?combine=&field_order_effective_date__value%5Bvalue%5D%5Bstring%5D=&order=field_order_number&page=0&sort=desc)
- **CC-2017-002**, physical p. **2**, lines **10–17**, reports that all **11** draft-audit responses in the period arrived within **60 calendar days** and says OIG's practice was to refrain from submitting investigations to the department for review and comment. The **60-day** reporting criterion, the order's **15- and 30-day** qualified audit targets, and later management follow-up are distinct. Page **3**, lines **4–9**, identifies the semiannual report as a route for information about closed, non-public investigations. The letter addresses the second half of FY 2016. Whether a similar practice governed the 2006 investigation remains open. [Congressional letter, pp. 2–3](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/Johnson-Grassley%20Response_Second%20FY16%20SAR%20Period.pdf#page=2)
- A **March 26, 1997** OIG motor-carrier audit expressly cited **8000.4** when discussing referrals for potentially false statements. This shows the predecessor order being cited before the cancellations recorded above. The historical audit concerns FHWA and does not classify the FAA case. Preserve this citation beside the earlier documented **8000.5A → 8000.1C** investigative follow-up reference. [AS-FH-7-006, physical p. 59, lines 8–21](https://www2.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/asfh7006.pdf#page=59) · [Earlier directive review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/e993bc5647918f6bb014f10d0648b232e42bae6c/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-investigative-directive-lineage-2026-11-05/README.md)

**Preserved count difference.** The cover letter (p. **2**, lines **5–9**) gives **540** open recommendations from **191** audit reports, with report dates **July 18, 2007–September 19, 2016**, and describes its enclosure as giving their details. The first enclosure face (p. **4**, line **1**) labels the table **Non-Sensitive Open Recommendations**. The last face (p. **50**, lines **100–112**) ends at row **514** with a totals line. The reproducible census found row numbers **1–514**, with **491 Resolved** and **23 Unresolved** status cells. The arithmetic difference is **26**. The public title gives a reason to test whether the counts cover different sets. The source does not identify the omitted or differently counted entries. The complete dated tracker export and the enclosure as delivered would distinguish a scope filter from a version or assembly difference. The August 2006 investigation predates the aggregate's reported date range, and this snapshot cannot answer its recommendation status.
