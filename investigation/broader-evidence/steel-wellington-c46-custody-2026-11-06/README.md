# C-46 steel from the NIST inventory to Wellington's Patriot Memorial

November 6, 2026 UTC · **STL02–STL03 / AUD011 / AUD016 / B188 / H005**. Wellington identifies its displayed South Tower panel as **C-46** and transcribes a surviving construction stencil. NIST's inventory identifies C-46 by the same column and other stencil values. A county oversight report supplies dated selection and design records, and the surveying contractor identifies a **laser scan and point cloud** of the panel. The shared identifiers give a testable link between an engineering specimen and a memorial artifact. The transfer documents and native scan remain open. ## Selection, movement and surviving geometry

- The **January 18, 2012** Palm Beach County Inspector General report records a January 2010 request and a conceptual **five- to six-foot** artifact. It reports a **March 31** request for at least **25 feet**, a **May 20** photograph supplied by the Port Authority, and a **June 30** inspection in New York where village representatives selected a **36 by 8 foot** piece weighing **over 27,000 pounds**. The May photo and June selection are separate records. The report leaves open whether they concern the same piece. It identifies a **September 13** design-services authorization. [OIG report, pp. 2–3, p. 2 lines 11–31 and p. 3 lines 3–16](https://pbc.gov/OIG/docs/reports/C00_2011-O-0005-Wellington-Final_Version_Issued.pdf#page=3)
- The OIG reports that village representatives traveled to New York **late November 2010** to obtain the artifact. A former participant's account published in **2026** places arrival at JFK to load it on **December 2, 2010**. Preserve the trip and reported load date as separate observations. They could describe different events in a single trip. An actual release and carrier receipt would establish the movements and dates. [OIG, p. 3 lines 16–18](https://pbc.gov/OIG/docs/reports/C00_2011-O-0005-Wellington-Final_Version_Issued.pdf#page=3) · [Published participant recollection, indexed line 22](https://www.blog.wellingtonthemagazine.com/featured/the-patriot-memorial-from-ground-zero-to-its-permanent-home-in-wellington/)
- Surveying contractor **WGI** says multiple field setups produced a dense point cloud of **C-46** and that a 3D model was used to design its support and orient the installation. This is a direct project account describing a potentially recoverable measurement artifact. The web page provides no native points, frame, tolerances, scan date or registration report in the inspected passage.
