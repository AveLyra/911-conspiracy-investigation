## Access, uncertainty and TODOs

The guide says the collection is remotely housed, requires **24 hours** advance contact, is open for research, and directs readers to inquire about copyright (pp. 11–12). The confidentiality-release entry is distinct from those collection-access statements. None establishes permission to republish the underlying correspondence or photographs. No staff contact, request or fee occurred. Only original research notes and source metadata are included in this public derivative.

The current ArchivesSpace collection link was located, but its body was not acquired. An attempt to retrieve a separate indexed catalog item returned **HTTP 429**; that route was stopped, not treated as a negative holdings result. Current box locations, item availability and digitization remain unverified. [Linked collection endpoint, not body-verified here](https://archivesspace.lib.lehigh.edu/repositories/7/resources/217).

Priority TODOs: obtain the notice/agreement; photo-identification notes and original image registers; steel-connection drafts with dated review comments; column test records; and team/procedure records. Inspect them for preservation, assignments, specimen identifiers and both favorable and unfavorable conclusions. A shared institution, engineer or appendix title cannot establish a missing custody link. Actual records could strengthen a preservation concern, document adequate handling, or prove irrelevant to a particular candidate. The catalog does not decide between those outcomes.

## Work actually performed

This is a newly acquired primary **catalog source**, not newly acquired 2001 investigation correspondence. Eight complete page images were read: **1, 8, 11, 12, 58–61**. All 169 pages were mechanically extracted and searched; that is not a full human review of every page. The collection description and linked guide page were inspected; the university's July 15, 2014 announcement was used only to contextualize the archive project. No physical specimen, laboratory measurement, source-native inventory or historical cause was tested. The missing telephone, FAA, recorder, materials and steel inputs remain open under the original plan. Existing evidence and prior assessments are unchanged.
