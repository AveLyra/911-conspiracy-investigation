## Dates and custody descriptions to preserve

| Source | What it describes |
| --- | --- |
| Commerce final rule, **88 FR 36469–36470**, physical pp. 1–2 | Page 1 describes the shared system's **September 30, 2023** decommission date, a successor procurement in **September 2022** and anticipated request/appeal intake through the new portal by **April 21, 2023**. The speaker identifies the departing partner as custodian of its data and describes the loss of public FOIAonline access upon partner departure. The briefing does not specify the export scope for closed Commerce cases from 2013 or confirm transfer of this request. [NARA transcript, indexed lines 119–124](https://www.archives.gov/ogis/foia-advisory-committee/2022-2024-term/foiaac-mtg-transcript-2023-03-02) |

The March and September dates describe different scopes. The September EPA capture is after the Commerce cutoff. A match for that control cannot establish that older Commerce records were exposed during the same crawl. The Commerce rule and migration briefing give a basis to look for the legacy export inventory, the successor-system cross-reference and the case file itself. They provide no disposition of **DOC-NIST-2013-001311**. The index observations leave open whether the request was public, indexed under another URL, migrated, retained elsewhere or disposed of under an applicable authority.
