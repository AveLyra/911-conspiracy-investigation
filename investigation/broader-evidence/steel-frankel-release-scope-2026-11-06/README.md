## What NIST described and what the mirror contains

The response scan is date-stamped **November 23, 2011**. Its opening identifies an **October 15, 2011** request for the cited Frankel erection and fabrication shop drawings. The disposition paragraph states that NIST was releasing **2,587 electronic files** on **four disks**. The mirror's central directory has **2,565 TIFFs and 22 PDFs**, also **2,587**. The response scan adds one PNG, and nine directory entries bring the total to **2,597 ZIP entries**. These are counts at different levels. Matching counts do not identify the contents of the original disks or establish a count of unique drawing sheets. [Public ZIP, response member and drawing directory](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NIST_WTC7_FOIA_12-009.zip) · [Source pins](SOURCE-PINS.json)

Across the indexed archive, **2,502** TIFF basenames are entirely numeric. They include **2000.TIF** and **3001.TIF**, with zero numeric TIFF basenames in **2001–3000** inclusive. A2540 falls within that interval. Other discontinuities exist in the same numeric filename population. The gap describes filenames in this mirror. The expected numbering scheme, any independently held inventory and any disposition for an individual drawing remain open. The four-disk statement and the filename observations are both preserved.
