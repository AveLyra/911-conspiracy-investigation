# Flight 93 Airfone package: receipt date in a second scan

TEL01–TEL04 / AUD016. On November 25, 2026, visually compared two publicly released reproductions of FBI interview serial `265A-NY-280350-302-96202`, both bearing production number `M-INT-00112509`. The earlier [Box 33 visual review](../telephone-gte-96202-visual-2026-11-25/README.md) remains unchanged.

## Source observations

* Team 1A Box 33, physical page 45: the package-date line is partly covered by a privacy marking. The date cannot be read in full from this reproduction. The page lists a Flight 93 seating diagram, three-page Airfone call-activity spreadsheet and separate seat-location spreadsheet; it says the items were placed in a 1A envelope.
* Team 7 Box 19, physical page 33: the corresponding line visibly states **11/6/2001** as the date an agent received a Federal Express package. This page carries the same interview identifiers and lists the same three categories of material. It likewise reports placement in a 1A envelope.
* A [separate later interview report](../telephone-gte-116622-visual-2026-11-25/README.md), serial 116622, reports a spreadsheet supplied November 6, 2001 and describes a later update. The connection of actual spreadsheet versions to the 96202 package requires item-level source evidence.

The two 96202 pages are reproductions of the same report, not independent carrier evidence or two independent delivery reports. The obscured and legible appearances are preserved separately. This comparison verifies the date visible in the Box 19 reproduction; it does not establish the contents of the obscured area of the Box 33 copy, the cause or date of markings, actual present custody, or the contents and clock basis of the spreadsheets. No redacted information was reconstructed.
