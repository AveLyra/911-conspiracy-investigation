# UMS headquarters inquiry: recovered report claims and original FBI pages

November 12, 2026. This compares selected claims from the captured body and table of Scooby report 5 with the released FBI pages the research chat could not inspect. A prior project review had already identified the visit email; this pass visually rechecked it and the contemporaneous closure records. The full report, all its attachments, and every claim in the wider 30-report set remain open to verification.

## The three separate record nodes

1. **August 2002 visit.** The [August 26 email, section 5, PDF p. 130](../../urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-5.pdf#page=130), describes an August 20–21 visit by FBI headquarters personnel to Newark concerning sensitive matters. It says they saw the surroundings and buildings/company involved, spoke to a New York polygraph examiner, and were expected to write a report for the Newark file. It calls the visit part of a national initiative and says it was not an audit. The page's redactions obscure names and identifiers. It documents a visit and an expected report, not that the report was completed, its contents, a final affiliation assessment, or a connection to a specific April inquiry. Thus the recovered report's claim that no qualifying August visit record was located accurately described that chat's limited access, but must not remain the project's current source status.
2. **April 2002 analytical inquiry and July 2003 status.** The [July 10, 2003 Newark communication, section 6, PDF pp. 56–57](../../urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-6.pdf#page=56), says FBIHQ and other intelligence-community personnel began an in-depth inquiry in April 2002 and physically traveled to Newark to examine the case. The stated questions included possible links between the five detained men and other similarly profiled Israeli nationals, and possible membership in a clandestine human intelligence collection network. It says headquarters' findings were incomplete as of July 2003 and Newark's investigation was complete. A question being investigated is not a finding of affiliation or operational coordination. The page itself does not identify the April visitors with the people in the August email.
3. **April 2004 retention record.** The [April 14, 2004 communication, section 6, PDF p. 61](../../urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-6.pdf#page=61), says evidence was considered of no value to PENTTBOM but warranted a counterintelligence inquiry; Newark's TWIN TOWERS inquiry closed in July 2003 while a redacted FBIHQ matter remained pending. Newark awaited a redacted summary to determine disposition of retained evidence. The identity of the pending HQ matter is obscured; the page alone does not prove it is the same April 2002 analytical inquiry.

## Additional related lead and limits

A [September 10, 2002 Newark communication, section 5, PDF pp. 128–129](../../urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/raw/section-5.pdf#page=128), says an August 29 FBIHQ telephone lead resulted in interviews and a photographic comparison. The people interviewed and the five persons shown in the pictures are redacted; the witnesses reported no recollection of the pictured individuals. It does not establish that these photographs depicted the UMS detainees or that the telephone lead followed from the August 20–21 visit. Their identities, the underlying lead and any report are separate retrieval targets.

The separately published [Serial 368 source check](../scooby-ums-saudi-claim-checks-2026-11-11/README.md) records different examiner interpretations for prior-knowledge and intelligence-affiliation questions and headquarters quality-control concurrence. A polygraph interpretation is not proof that someone belonged to an intelligence service or had no prior knowledge. Serial 459's visible cover does not resolve the redacted subject or join the investigations.

These source pages strengthen the documentary record of a real headquarters visit and an unresolved intelligence-affiliation inquiry. They do not establish UMS was an intelligence front, reveal a headquarters conclusion, identify a Saudi-network connection, or demonstrate advance knowledge. The visit report and its routing, the April inquiry case identifiers, any final assessment, and the August 29 lead and comparison records remain the discriminating originals.
