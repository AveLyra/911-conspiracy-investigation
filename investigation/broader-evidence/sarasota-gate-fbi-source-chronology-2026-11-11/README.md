# Sarasota, the al-Hijji family and the 9/11 investigation: source chronology and unresolved records

**Source review, November 11, 2026.** The original Prestancia visitor logs, license-plate images and any underlying plate-to-person comparison were not located in this bounded search and therefore could not be examined. A released April 2002 FBI communication reports connections with people associated with the attacks; it does not identify the redacted people or disclose the supporting chain in its visible text. A later FBI review and the 9/11 Review Commission disputed the connection. The contradiction is consequential, but neither side's summary substitutes for the underlying gate, telephone and investigative records.

No verified link was identified between this Sarasota inquiry and Urban Moving Systems (UMS), Max Movers or their personnel. This is a bounded search result, not proof no connection exists. Association, Florida geography, an intelligence inquiry, incomplete searches and competing accounts do not establish coordination, foreknowledge, a WTC job or explosives placement. The proposed compartmented theory does not require direct contact between UMS and the hijackers.

## Gate and phone records: the decisive unresolved comparison

The identified reports conflict over whether gate records were obtained and what they showed. Neither the actual log entries, plate images, retention chain, plate-registration crosswalk nor a carrier-certified call comparison was located in this pass. The 2018 declaration reports an analyst's negative search of domestic telephone numbers and says the director briefing described gate records as apparently not obtained; the former security official and an unnamed source report a positive gate comparison. Both need original inputs, complete search scope and dates before ranking either account. A plate association would need to distinguish owner, driver, passenger, time and destination.

Priority records: original gate logs and images, their retention and transfer inventories, interviews or notes describing searches, plate-registration and rental records, name/plate/date crosswalks, full source documentation for the April 3 and 16 communications, the July 2002 interview, the full 2011 briefing and analyst search inputs, contemporaneous subscriber and toll data, and review-commission inquiry files and agency replies. Compare positive **and negative** results and preserve any ordinary explanation. No new request was sent in this review.

## Relationship to UMS and the working theory

The [Classic/Miami lead](../../urban-moving-systems/newark-closure-followup-2026-11-05/README.md) separately records Miami's belief that Classic moved an unidentified hijacker and a Classic number in a notebook. It does not identify a customer or link the Sarasota family or property to UMS. The [UMS address and entry-date list review](../../urban-moving-systems/wtc-hijacker-source-recheck-2026-11-06/README.md) found the FBI compiled lists for the detainees and for hijackers/associates but did not locate their attachments or a comparison result. Neither source supplies a Sarasota–UMS match.

A documented gate entry for a particular hijacker could strengthen a narrowly defined Sarasota contact claim; an adequately scoped, authenticated negative comparison could weaken it. An independently authenticated shared person, transaction, telephone communication, job or instruction might support a specific UMS connection, subject to date and purpose. Establishing a coordinated intelligence operation or WTC placement would require additional evidence about knowledge, tasking and acts. Ordinary relocation, unrelated visitors, mistaken plate matches and limitations of incomplete searches remain alternatives. The larger U.S.–Israeli–Saudi hypothesis remains unestablished irrespective of the outcome of this one lead.
