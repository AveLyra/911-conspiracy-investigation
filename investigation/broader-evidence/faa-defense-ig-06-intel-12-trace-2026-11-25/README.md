## Source observations

- A [Defense inspector general semiannual report](https://www.dodig.mil/Portals/48/Documents/SAR/SAR-1-apr-2006-30-sep-2006.pdf?ver=2017-02-02-141740-470), printed page 16, reports a September 12, 2006 Defense IG report on testimony, describing a joint review with the Transportation IG. It says the review did not substantiate intentionally false statements to the Commission and concluded DoD reported inaccurate air-defense response information. Those are the IG's reported conclusions; the underlying supporting records were not reviewed in this step.
- A later [DoD IG report](https://media.defense.gov/2010/Aug/27/2001712247/-1/-1/1/SDIG-SWA-10-01Final_508.pdf), printed page 48, identifies **06-INTEL-12**, “Review of Testimony to the National Commission on Terrorist Attacks Upon the United States,” dated September 12, 2006, and labels that entry classified. This is a historical label in that source, not a verified present classification or release determination.
- The [DoD IG fiscal 2016 FOIA log](https://media.defense.gov/2017/Mar/10/2001774220/-1/-1/1/FOIA%20Case%20Log%20Fiscal%20Year%202016.pdf), printed page 39 / 46, lists **FOIA-2016-00681** with request summary “06-INTEL-12 (Classified) (ISPA)” and a July 25, 2016 completion date. The request log does not state what was released, withheld, or found; a completed FOIA request is not proof that the report was released or withheld in full.
- An [Air Force historical publication](https://media.defense.gov/2025/Jun/10/2003736334/-1/-1/0/FIRST_109_MINUTES%282018%29.PDF), printed page 9 and note 22, printed pages 61–62, reports that NORAD's commander wrote the Commission in March 2004 acknowledging its timeline and cites 06-INTEL-12 among its sources. This is a reported source trail, not the original letter or verification of its precise scope. The citation does not demonstrate the FAA corrected its separate 2003 question-for-the-record answer.

## Interpretation, alternatives and limits

The Defense report number and prior request are useful retrieval keys; the historical publication provides an additional lead to a named agency and month for a correction-related letter. Different subjects and time periods must remain distinct: Defense's reported 2004 timeline acknowledgment, the 2006 FAA-specific recommendation to correct an answer, and any later delivery are not interchangeable. The OIG's non-substantiation finding does not prove the absence of intentional wrongdoing, and missing or historically classified records do not establish it.

All four links were read through public indexed PDF text on November 25, 2026. Direct attempts to download the linked PDFs returned HTML rather than the documents; consequently no original PDF bytes or SHA-256 are claimed here, and no page-image or independent full-report verification was completed. Do not use this as a substitute for the underlying report or letter.
