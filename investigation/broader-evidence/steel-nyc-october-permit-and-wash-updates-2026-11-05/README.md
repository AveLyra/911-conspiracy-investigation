## Permit and hot-load trail

- **Report subject October 11, printed DATE October 9.** The subject and summary identify October 11, 2001, while the date field says October 9, 2001. The entry reports no FEMA reply yet. It attributes to a DEP official the position that DEP would not require permits for the ordered demolition to correct a dangerous situation and says City permits were not typically applied for at this State facility. This is a reported DEP position about the post-attack work. It supplies no copy of the referenced demolition order or an agency-wide decision covering other permits. [City PDF p. 1, lines 14–27](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000154444.pdf#page=1)
- **October 16 and 17.** The already-pinned October 16 report records FEMA trying to schedule a meeting about review procedures, permit responsibility and environmental review. Both October 17 copies report that the FEMA official called and said he was not ready for the meeting. The same October 17 copies record FDNY's two proposals for hot debris. Install a deluge system at the truck wash station or allow hot material to cool before loading. Both also show that comments were sought on a health order about hygiene and vehicle washing. The update supplies no installation, implementation, issued order, load history, or permit meeting outcome. [First scan](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000154467.pdf#page=3) · [Second scan](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000154472.pdf#page=3)

The scan differences are **5 samples** for the stated total and **1 case** for iron oxide. The silica difference is between extraction and scan in the first release. No sequence of correction or revision was established for the two visibly different counts. The matching date and other entries support treating these as related versions. Both enclosures describe sampling at Pier 25 where debris was being transferred from trucks to a barge by crane, with no detectable asbestos in the cited sampling. That is the reported test result for the sampled conditions. It supplies no denominator or result for all Pier 25 loads and no particular steel load, material composition or chain of custody.
