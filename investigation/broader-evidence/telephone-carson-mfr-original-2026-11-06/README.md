# Flight 77: the Carson interview and the family-to-airline relay

November 6, 2026 UTC · **Telephone / TEL01–TEL05.** The National Archives' two-page scan of the Commission's **November 19, 2003 Patty Carson interview memorandum** identifies who Carson recalled speaking to, records her uncertainty about the time, and identifies two follow-up leads: the airport manager she said she told during evacuation and a same-night FBI call to her. Both scan faces were read. This is a Commission staff summary of a retrospective interview, not a recording, airline call log, or direct witness account of the onboard conversation. **What changes:** Carson is a named recipient describing a ground-to-ground relay that she said she personally answered. She is a secondhand source for the conversation aboard the aircraft. The staff narrative cites her interview, so the narrative and the interview are not independent corroboration of her account. The Dallas relays share an airline source; the September 12 response reports a separate interview with the family. The November 2003 memo adds a lead to an earlier FBI contact with Carson; it does not itself show what agents recorded in 2001.

**What remains open:** Nancy, Ronald, and the parents are described in different records and at different relay points. Whether Ronald's reported contact is the same communication Carson describes, a separate contact, or a summary using a different attribution is untested. Preserve **6:05 Pacific**, **6:13 with no printed zone**, **9:11/9:12**, and the draft's **9:12–9:37:45** as sourced. The staff report’s separate **9:12–9:37:46** bracket is retained as printed. These times cover different descriptions or legs; no timezone is supplied for the unlabelled field, no value was selected, and no numerical delay was calculated. The draft and the memo do not independently authenticate the caller, aircraft handset, call routing, or call time. The spelling difference does not establish two managers or which spelling was used on the native draft. Separately, the memo opens p. 2 with **“Mrs. Carson replied”** before the clarification about Flight 77 still being in the air. The draft reader, p. 35, attributes that clarification to **Mrs. May**; the staff report, p. 31, attributes it to **May’s mother**. Speaker attribution remains open pending original notes or a recording.
