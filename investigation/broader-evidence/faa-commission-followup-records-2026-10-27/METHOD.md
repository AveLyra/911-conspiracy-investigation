# Method, provenance and limits

1. Existing B129 / PD01–PD04 and the five-track work plan controlled the question; titles were locators, not conclusions. The NARA Boxes96–107 finding aid led to five exact catalog-linked downloads. Anonymous GETs returned PDF entities with HTTP200 and application/pdf. Original files, full transport metadata, page-text extracts and QA rasters are preserved locally. No native agency email headers or complete original custody chain were authenticated merely by retrieval.
2. SOURCES pins exact URL/catalog/access time/bytes/SHA256/page count and every extracted page-text hash. SHA256 identifies the entity examined; it is not proof of truth, original authorship, historical completeness, receipt or absence of alteration before release. An official NARA host establishes the public release route, not every underlying assertion.
3. All pages of F01–F03 were visually reviewed. F04 pages 1–3 and F05 pages 1, 2, 11 were visually reviewed. F05 page3’s heading was text-inspected only. The rest of F04/F05 remains a detailed-reading dependency. Text extraction is not human review. These distinctions also account for the original protocol’s still-unmet77page inspection requirement.
4. SOURCE-EXCERPTS preserves selected raw text-layer lines verbatim. It excludes personnel/contact/health text. One-based locators refer to PyMuPDF page.get_text().splitlines(), not original numbered lines. Visual transcription and OCR differ in places; no silent repair is made. Status counts use visible rows/prose and are manual calculations; literal Completed counts are also checked against the text layer. Software does not prove the interpretation.
5. F02’s statements retain their note-taker/regional official/Commission/HQ attribution layers. F03’s author/date remain incomplete. Never count a quoted source, evidence envelope, draft transcript and later summary as independent corroboration. Disputed scope/production is preserved without assigning motive or adjudicating facts.
6. Quantities are retained with their source units: hours,channels,person-days, phone lines. No cross-unit discrepancy or realized workload is calculated. F01 listed people are not the full workforce. F04 is an acquired package, not 72 independent records or a completed Flight 77 comparison.
7. Native research is the source of these notes; the public addition is a reviewed redundant mirror. No main case facts/timeline/pleading/correspondence authority changed. Public content excludes raw scans, signatures, personal health/contact details, private correspondence, credentials and local network paths. Existing public official links remain visible.
8. verify_sources.py checks all five local PDF entities, decoding, page/text hashes, selected line quotations and 27 literal Completed markers. The resulting receipt is an integrity/reproducibility check, not authentication of any event or completion of the original-record goal. Publication account/commit/ref/file checks are a separate subsequent operation.
9. Six prior plan/review documents were hashed before this pass; REVISION-STATUS compares them afterward. Existing source bytes are checked against acquisition hashes. New text has no preceding version, so its diff is a full addition; previous assessments and all conflicting values remain available. No source overwrite, deletion, normalization, private push, outreach, request, fee, personal-mailbox access or history rewrite.

Official finding aid: https://www.archives.gov/research/foia/faa-finding-aid-96-107 . NARA source links and access timestamps are in SOURCES. The finding-aid listing is not an independently authenticated transmission manifest.
