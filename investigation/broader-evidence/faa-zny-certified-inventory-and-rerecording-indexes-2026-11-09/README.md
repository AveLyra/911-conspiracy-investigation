## Certified holdings and reported transfer

The **August 6, 2004** memorandum from New York Center to **ADA-2** lists three attachments. Item 1 is a certified tagged-material index. Item 2 is the unedited voice rerecording index. Item 3 is the prepared cassette index. Their catalog descriptions match the three listed subjects. The released memo PDF has two pages and zero standard embedded files or FileAttachment annotations. The two separately cataloged indexes supply matching titles and contents. Item-level attachment and transfer identifiers remain unacquired. AEA-2598 p. 1 lines 6–23, p. 2 lines 1–6. [Certified inventory, NAID 7600997](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7600997) · [Box 112 finding aid](https://www.archives.gov/research/foia/faa-finding-aid-108-126)

The memo says the cassettes outside item 2 contain selected transmissions and that the original full recordings remained on file at New York Center. Its p. 1 separately reports that the **original ARINC rolls** went to the Commission with regional concurrence and that the facility no longer held copies of that material. Keep the two statements tied to their media and date. The release supplies a report of transfer and retention. The corresponding dispatch, intake and later custody records remain open.
