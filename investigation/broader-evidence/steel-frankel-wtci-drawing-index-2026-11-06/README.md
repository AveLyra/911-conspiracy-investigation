# WTC 7 Frankel drawing identifiers and the E120 hole-size note

November 6, 2026 · Steel track · STL02–STL03 / AUD011 / AUD016 / B188 / H005

Frankel's **E120** general notes specify **13/16 in.** holes unless the drawings show otherwise. FEMA's field entry for **A or W14A** records **15/16 in.** The difference is **1/8 in.** The same general note is legible in the TIFF and both pages of the numbered PDF. A single hole dimension supplies a comparison to a drawing default. A part-specific drawing or a matching hole pattern and joint geometry is still needed to test the field attribution. [Public 12-009 archive](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NIST_WTC7_FOIA_12-009.zip) · [FEMA Appendix D, PDF p. 16](https://www.fema.gov/pdf/library/fema403_apd.pdf#page=16)

## E120 and the field measurement

E117 and E117A direct the reader to E120 for general notes. The E120 title block identifies **East West Wind Bracing Elevations**, and its notes area identifies customer drawing **S-20**. The separately numbered scan is **WTCI-000280-I.PDF**. On physical pages 1 and 2, the lower notes block includes Structural Steel Note **7**, with **13/16 in.** hole diameter and an exception for dimensions shown on drawings. The same value and exception appear on **E120.tif**. Note **13** calls for milled stiffeners under column connections and over columns. It gives no dimensions for the field stiffener. The companion customer S-20 and issue history remain open. [Archive members and exact crops](SOURCE-PINS.json)

The values are preserved as separate observations. The field hole is **1/8 in. larger** than the general note and **1/8 in. smaller** than the **1-1/16 in.** callout. Neither comparison joins equivalent features. The drawings contain explicit variation, so a general note alone cannot determine which shop hole the field record measured or whether the field piece came from WTC 7. ## What the WTCI numbers identify

This pass acquired the remaining **15** numbered PDFs in the mirror and inspected both page views in each. The [preceding pass](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/9eae3c08856e37947937a0471de17314cf3e1169/investigation/broader-evidence/steel-frankel-release-scope-2026-11-06/README.md) had already checked seven. The combined title inventory covers all **22 PDF containers** and **44 page views**. The internal sheet number is legible or identifiable from the title block and floor title in **21** containers. In **WTCI-000268-I.PDF** the bottom edge obscures the title-block number on both pages. Its body shows truss details and a plate girder schedule. The exact internal designation stays unassigned. The [ledger](SOURCE-PINS.json) keeps the prior and new acquisitions distinct.

The title inventory found the referenced **E120** and five additional floor-framing PDF routes covering floors **8–17**, identified by each page's title. It found no PDF visibly titled **E117B** or identified as a piece-specific shop detail for **A2540** or **A2547**. This is a screen of sheet identity, selected notes and title blocks. One title remains unreadable and the study did not inspect every interior mark or the entire TIFF collection.
