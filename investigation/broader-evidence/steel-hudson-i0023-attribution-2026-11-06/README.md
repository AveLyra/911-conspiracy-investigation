# Hudson's World Trade Center steel and the I-0023 inventory lead

November 6, 2026 UTC · STL02 and STL03 · New Hampshire, Benson Park

The July 2010 court exhibit reproduced in a Clearwater agenda lists **I-0023** as **“Steel Box Column.”** The indexed view of a 2017 University of Pittsburgh dissertation describes the same identifier on a framed tag in Hudson Central Fire Station. This is a specific lookup key for the transfer and provenance records. The exhibit row gives no recipient, tower, or floor. The dissertation PDF was unavailable for direct image verification in this pass. [Court exhibit, PDF p. 74](https://edocuments.myclearwater.com/WebLink/edoc/243567/12132010.pdf?dbid=0&repo=Clearwater#page=74) · [University catalog, Figure 25 in the dissertation](https://d-scholarship.pitt.edu/concern/etds/f49d642c-2ef6-4215-9e85-309eac756511)

## What the records can test

- **Origin.** Keep the **21st** and **23rd** floors open. The label match is between a visually checked list row and an indexed image description. Its strength is limited by the unverified photograph and detached tag. A match would identify an archive object. Attribution to an original member needs a further link to a recovery record, marked photograph or drawing. The reviewed accounts attribute the piece to a tower or the Twin Towers generally. None attributes it to WTC 7.
- **Description and transport.** Keep **“Steel Box Column”** and **beam**, the **20-foot** plan and **23-foot** account, and the **several thousand pounds** and **nine-ton** accounts as recorded. The prospective **La Guardia** reference and retrospective **JFK** reference have different scopes. The municipal wording **Queens** does not specify an airport. Plans may have changed and sources may be repeating others' information. The dispatch record and measurements remain unacquired.
- **Town milestones.** Dedication in **2011** and completion in **2012** could refer to separate events. The minutes distinguish the hearing's listed value and the recorded vote amount. Their underlying appraisal or itemization remains unacquired.
