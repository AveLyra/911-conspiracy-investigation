# Visitor-card and Zim relocation: recovered report-body source checks

11 November 2026. This checks selected claims and missing-record leads from the saved body and table of Scooby report 8. It does not establish a complete review of that report, all attachments, or the wider 30-report set.

| Claim in recovered report | Check and limitation |
|---|---|
| A WTC visitor card appears in an FBI receipt but its holder and use are unresolved. | A prior [visual source check](../scooby-sarasota-visitor-source-checks-2026-11-11/README.md) read the [FBI property receipt](https://www.themoreuknow.com/pdf/fbi%20report%20section%202.pdf#page=81): it lists a WTC visitor card among contents associated with a black fanny pack, with the received-from identity redacted. It does not identify the cardholder, issuer, date, destination, or any entry. This does not independently establish the card's face, the East Rutherford inventory crosswalk, a particular person carrying it or a WTC job. |
| Zim announced a September relocation in April 2001. | The [April 3 trade report](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/zim-moves-regional-head-office-from-new-york-to-norfolk) says the company planned to move the US corporate office from New York to Norfolk in September. It names no physical mover. This source predates the attacks and supports advance planning, not knowledge of the attacks. |
| Norfolk opened in early September, and the move was complete. | A [September 18, 2001 Gleaner report](https://old.jamaica-gleaner.com/gleaner/20010918/shipping/shipping2.html) states the Norfolk headquarters opened September 4 and attributes a statement about the move and planning to the company president. This is post-attack reporting, not the original move invoice or direct proof that all WTC activity had stopped. A [2002 Norfolk city financial report](https://norfolkpubliclibrary.org/DocumentCenter/View/2195/2002-CAFR?bidId=) says relocation was completed in September but supplies no day and identifies no mover. |
| The September opening means all Zim personnel and office equipment had left the WTC before September 11. | **Not established.** An [October 18 Journal of Commerce article reproduced in a secondary archive](https://stevenwarran.blogspot.com/2012/05/zim-israel-navigation-company.html) reports remaining staff and loss of computer systems. Its chain and exact original should be authenticated; it is materially contrary to treating the Norfolk opening as proof of complete withdrawal. The two descriptions can refer to different elements of a phased relocation. |
| A builder or owner’s representative can be treated as the moving carrier. | **Unsupported.** Construction, representation and physical transport are distinct roles. An invoice, contract, dispatch, carrier waybill or receiving record is needed to identify who moved particular contents. |
| Library of Virginia folder 40173 / Box 84 / Folder 27 is a plausible route to contemporary Zim records. | An [ARVAS catalog result](https://search.arvasarchive.org/catalog/vi_vi00538_c05_c435) identifies a Zim April 2, 2001 folder in Box 84, Folder 27 and lists 40173 among the wider collection IDs. The indexed catalog result reports zero items available online. This is a location lead, not an inspection of the folder or proof it contains a mover invoice. Direct opening returned an access error in this check, so the result has not been independently read beyond its indexed description. |

The key access question remains a matched card, property-custody record, holder/issuer evidence and dated entry or authorization record. A mover-specific WTC job requires matching customer, carrier and dispatch/access evidence; either a card or a company association alone would not establish explosives placement.
