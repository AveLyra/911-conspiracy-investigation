## What the actual 2006 report establishes

**S06 physical page 46 / printed page 39**, heading *Results of OIG Investigation of 9/11 Commission Staff Referral*, dated **August 31, 2006**, contains the agency's summary of its investigation. It reports both of the following:

- OIG did not find evidence sufficient to conclude that FAA officials knowingly made false statements, purposely omitted accurate information, or intentionally failed to correct an inaccurate statement after becoming aware of it.
- OIG discovered that **three FAA executives did not act to correct** an erroneous Commission QFR response after learning it was inaccurate. It recommended correction and consideration of appropriate administrative action for **two current executives**.

Both statements remain side by side. A finding of inaction after awareness and a conclusion concerning proof of intent are distinct propositions; the summary does not itself establish motive. The three-executive and two-current-executive descriptions must not be silently collapsed into the same population or called a numerical contradiction. The body says that OIG **recommended** correction and consideration of action. It does not document a subsequently delivered correction, completed personnel decision or recipient acknowledgment.

This is another agency publication about the same investigation, identified here through its heading, date and substantive account. It is not a second independent investigation or independent corroboration of the underlying events. It does not display the CC-2006-085 identifier in this passage; the source relationship is not inferred from an identifier match that does not exist. The previously held [CC-2006-085 report](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf) and [earlier follow-up review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/055ef54a857b46846d0038109bab7e108970f03a/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-postreport-disposition-2026-10-27/README.md) remain unchanged.

The two FAA recommendations concern pilots receiving medical disability benefits and revisions to an airman medical-certificate application. Their status wording says FAA work on open issues continues. Those rows are not the Commission QFR correction or the recommendation concerning executives. A generic FAA label or open-issues phrase cannot be assigned to this case. Adjacent physical pages 60 and 62 contain other statistical presentations and establish the table's page boundaries in this copy.

This narrows the usefulness of a previously located source: it is an actual example of investigation-recommendation reporting, but its specific FAA rows do not provide the sought case disposition. Its limited entries also do not establish a complete inventory of every investigation recommendation, a selection rule, or that an unlisted recommendation was resolved, implemented, withheld or never made.
