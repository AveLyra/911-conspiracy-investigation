November 6, 2026 UTC · **STL02–STL03 / B188 / H005**. A preserved public image reproduces page **II-400** of *WTC Archive Volume I: Artifacts Inventory Report*. ## Boundaries on the match

- **Different dates.** The journal's image caption identifies **December 19, 2006** and says it obtained the material under FOIA. The image itself has a **2007** Port Authority copyright footer, credits **Voorsanger & Associates Architects, PC**, and prints **06/16/2004** alongside each photo. Keep all three date fields separately. Earlier records separately reference a **November 2002 Draft 2**, an **October 2004** two-volume inventory and a **January 31, 2007** inventory. This page does not settle which complete edition accompanied the 2009 or 2010 legal instrument.
