# FAA Document Request No. 1: request versions and June production scope

November 6, 2026 UTC · **B129 / PD01–PD04 / PD07**. A copy of the **May 7, 2003 DOT request** appears in both the Commission's Dunne Box 5 packet and the official NARA FAA compilation **AWA-831**. It gives a **May 14** deadline and **eight** numbered items. The separate Marcus Box 3 FAA request face still gives **May 9** and **seven** items. Rechecking the original June cover scans also found a specific difference in which request item the **June 9** installment addressed. The June 9 cover and index and the July 10 recap are different attributions of scope. Material sent under item 1 might also have been counted under item 7 later. A separate attachment, later supplement or reporting error are other untested possibilities. The present scans give no complete June 9 enclosure set. They also do not establish a June 4 dispatch corresponding to a June 5 receipt. Preserve Dunne's **June 4** completion statement alongside the reported **June 5 and June 9** receipts and the later item-specific qualifications.
