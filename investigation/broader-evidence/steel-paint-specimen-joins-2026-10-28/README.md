## ID01 — M-14 and M-4 are different printed identifiers

The main paint-study selection paragraph names **C-11 and M-14** as unidentified additional panels. Table E1 instead contains **C-11 and M-4** as unidentified samples. WJE's worksheet and NIST's contractor review also name **M-4**. [Main text, V1 physical 71 / printed 218 §6.2](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-3cv1.pdf#page=71); [Table E1, V2 physical 170 / printed 454](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-3cv2.pdf#page=170); [worksheet p.236 / B-520](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-3cv2.pdf#page=236); [review p.257 / printed 479](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-3cv2.pdf#page=257).

The inventory gives **N-13 or M-14** an identified WTC 1 location: column 130, floors 99–102. Its **M-4 or M-5** row has accepted building/column/floor fields blank. These are published labels and aliases, not proof of historical label stability. [I3B TableA2 physical 93 / printed 65](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-3b.pdf#page=93); [TableA1 physical 91 / printed 63](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-3b.pdf#page=91); P01–P05.

This creates a specific selection/identity question. An editorial label, an alias/version change or a different selection could be relevant; none is selected as the explanation. **Do not silently change M-14 to M-4**, infer a specimen swap, or infer a wrong numerical result. Obtain the native selection list, original paint worksheets, photographed labels, alias changes and item/region records.

Table E1 contains **eight M-4 rows**, each negative for mudcracking with sufficient paint, but with **unknown** exposure and duration. Its generic middle/top column and spandrel descriptions do not authenticate a one-to-one match to WJE's **Z2/Z4** possible-fire regions. A negative mudcracking entry is not a zero temperature or proof of no fire. The actual region/paint/calibration/photo join remains required. P03–P04.

Table E1 repeatedly gives **M-10a/b: WTC 1,North face**, while the main method paragraph calls them **WTC 2** samples and the inventory's accepted-building fields also say **WTC 2**. [V2 p.169 / printed 453](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-3cv2.pdf#page=169); [V1 p.71](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-3cv1.pdf#page=71); [I3B p.91](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-3b.pdf#page=91); P06. A descriptive-field problem would not alone establish a changed paint observation, thermal reconstruction or collapse result; a wrong item/location join could matter if confirmed in the actual analysis inputs. Native worksheets and photographic/alias/as-built mappings would test that distinction. No preferred building/location is substituted in this review.

## Limits, preservation and next records

The strongest objection to treating these findings as misconduct evidence is that publication labels and source summaries can differ while underlying measurements remain unchanged. Conversely, that possibility does not supply the missing provenance or authorize correcting the record. The next useful originals are the actual selection list, native paint/region/alias/location worksheets and source images, plus the separate WJE-on-NIST draft review, signed terms, version/delivery and acceptance chain.
