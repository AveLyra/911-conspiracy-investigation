## The correspondence-control route

- The **June 4, 2001 DOJ/003 notice** describes department-wide notice coverage while components continued to operate correspondence tracking systems. Its field examples include control number, organization, document date, receipt date, subject and response status. Original correspondence, routing and enclosures may be present. The **October 30, 2006** notice lists DOJ–003 and its 2001 and 2002 notice history. DOJ's separate description names the Departmental Executive Secretariat as manager of the Attorney General's Correspondence Management System and says scans and responses are present in some folders. A period-matched control record and any attachment manifest would test receipt and scope. [2001 notice, physical pp. 1–3](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2001-06-04/pdf/01-13860.pdf) · [2006 listing, physical p. 1](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2006-10-30/pdf/E6-18009.pdf) · [DOJ system description](https://www.justice.gov/ag/major-information-systems)
- The **N1-060-94-002** predecessor, item **1a(1)(a)**, describes permanent controlled subject correspondence maintained by the Executive Secretariat, annual cutoff and National Archives transfer **15 years after cutoff**. Its status sheet maps that item to **DAA-0060-2017-0002-0001**. The successor describes permanent controlled correspondence and tracking and index information. It says cutoff is **the end of the official's tenure** and transfer **25 years after cutoff**. Its planned initial transfer span says **2001 to 2005**. These are distinct schedule descriptions, transfer triggers and planning fields. The schedule cross-reference gives a series to check. The actual classification, custody, accession and transfer status of any 2006 peer-review record remain unverified.
