# WTC 7 shop-drawing acquisition: NIST's dated accounts and the release gap

November 14, 2026 UTC · STL02–STL03 / B188 / H005. Two NIST documents establish a more specific **reported acquisition chronology** for WTC 7 connection drawings. They identify a potentially useful custody and version-history trail; they do not identify the drawings in the later release or establish the provenance of W14A.

[Prior source-pinned comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/4057193d/investigation/broader-evidence/steel-frankel-release-accounts-2026-11-10/README.md) |

The spring 2007 acquisition and the 2011 release are **not joined** by an accession number or file-level inventory in these passages. The 2003 pending-or-unlocated description, 2007 acquisition account, and later completeness descriptions have different dates and scopes; preserve each. It is untested whether the 2007 group contained the missing series or whether either completeness account described the same holdings. The two missing-series accounts and the release remain unresolved, not evidence of concealment or proof that every item existed at NIST.

## Records that could distinguish the accounts

- [ ] The spring 2007 transmittal, supplier/source, acquisition register, inventory, drawing IDs, revision dates and receipt acknowledgment, including any restrictions and supplements. Compare the actual records with the original 12-009 disk manifest and public mirror at file level.
- [ ] The statements and underlying correspondence behind the 2012 incomplete-set account and the 2018 complete-set account, including date and scope. Compare actual 2001–3000 sheets, including A2682, against an authoritative drawing index. Do not assume both accounts concern the same set or time.
- [ ] For the independent W14A attribution question, obtain the fabrication sheet, field measurements and marks, custody records and NIST attribution rationale. A drawing-acquisition chronology does not establish a specimen's building of origin.
- [ ] Any technical analysis of which connection models changed, and their sensitivity, belongs to the existing technical review; the two reports here are not model files or an independently reproduced calculation.
