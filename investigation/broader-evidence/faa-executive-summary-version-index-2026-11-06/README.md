## The briefing and the reported communications

- **GAO's dated document trail.** The photographed fax cover bears **07/08/2004 15:25**, **01/02**. Its handwritten note says GAO gave an **oral briefing on August 30, 2002** to House aviation subcommittee counsel and describes the attached page as the briefing's substance. Page 2's extracted fax header also gives **07/08/2004 15:25**, **02/02**. The 2002 briefing, 2004 fax and the Committee's actual intake are separate events. The final Commission report cites an August 30, 2002 GAO briefing summary and says GAO could not corroborate the allegation. The exact custody join between the cited copy and this fax remains open. The Team 7 reproduction separately contains a comment that GAO said it conducted no such investigation. Keep the comment alongside the fax and the final citation. Its source, time and scope remain unverified. [GAO cover image](https://imgv2-2-f.scribdassets.com/img/document/13723754/original/85250bf024/1?v=1) · [GAO reader, lines 1–28](https://www.scribd.com/document/13723754/T7-B16-Flight-11-Gun-Story-Fdr-GAO-Briefing-on-Flight-11-Shooting-Story) · [Commission notes, chapter 1 note 25](https://govinfo.library.unt.edu/911/report/911Report_Notes.htm)
- **The alleged call and a separate report.** The GAO briefing describes a **9:20 a.m.** call from the FAA principal security inspector to American Airlines and says the reported information was entered in both crisis centers and used in the summary. It separately describes a Washington Operations Center report at **8:44 a.m.** and says that center did not pass it to security personnel at the primary crisis center. These are attributed routing accounts. The text also records airline personnel denying that they reported a shooting, a former employee recalling an anecdotal report, and GAO finding no corroboration for a shooting. Staff Statement No. 4 separately records a denial by the alleged source and reports that the flight-attendant call evidence it reviewed did not mention a gun or shooting. Similar allegations at two offices supply no independent corroboration of what happened on board without tracing the information to its origin.
