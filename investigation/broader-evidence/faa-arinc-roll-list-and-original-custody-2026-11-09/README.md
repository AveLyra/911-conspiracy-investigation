## Custody and verification

The August 6, 2004 AEA-2598 memo says that original ARINC rolls were submitted to the Commission with regional concurrence and that the facility no longer had copies of that material. The AWA list and digitized printouts sit in an FAA archival collection. They supply a list and candidate objects for comparison. An item-matched dispatch, recipient acknowledgment and preservation history would establish their relationship to the originals mentioned by the memo.
