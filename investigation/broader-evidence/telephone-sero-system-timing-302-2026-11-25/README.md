## Observation and comparison

- Packet 6, physical p. **73**, footer **000000463**, is an FD-302 recording an interview with an on-site Rockwell representative. It prints investigation **09/11/2001**, dictation **09/12/2001** and transcription **09/13/2001**, and references lead controls **DL267 and CE66**. The representative states that, according to the reservation center's computer system, the emergency button was activated to begin recording at **8:20 A.M., EST** on the call. That is the reported time and zone label; it does not say the representative personally observed the button being pressed or supply the underlying system output. The report also identifies a potential source for proprietary playback software; no current custody or availability is established.
- The earlier [September 13 Charlotte inventory review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/d5b279a04f2fe18b78548222aea75451bfa7f60a/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-sero-evidence-inventory-2026-10-31/README.md) preserves its own **approximately 8:20 A.M.** and **precisely 8:20 A.M., EST** descriptions, and its qualification that recorded times used a custodian's knowledge rather than witness estimates. It also preserves the approximate end time and the distinction between a four-minute recording and a longer call. Do not assume the inventory and interview are independent measurements: their underlying information may overlap.
- A separately reviewed operator account describes difficulty locating an emergency button and a colleague activating one; preserve the account and source context without inferring an exact activation time from it. ## Limits and TODOs

The interview is a reproduced account of what a system representative said, not the system's original event record. The printed **EST** label, minute precision, device timezone, daylight-saving configuration, clock calibration, and relationship between call answer, button activation and first audible sample remain unverified. Do not silently convert or align the time, infer a call start, or treat matching reports as independent clocks. Obtain the native event/activity report and field definitions, configuration and clock history, original interview notes, Rockwell-format media and playback software/version records. Compare those with the two evidence CDs and the work copy; independently establish any join before asserting audio duration or completeness. The AA77 carrier questions remain separate.
