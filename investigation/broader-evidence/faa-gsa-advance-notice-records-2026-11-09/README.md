# GSA advance notices for telephone monitoring and recording

**November 9, 2026 | B129 / PD03–PD04.** An earlier version of the rule cited by the FAA order had a separate GSA filing route. GSA's December 1990 rule directed specified advance notices, supporting determinations and later review records to its Regulations Branch. Two 1995 corrections give a later correspondence symbol. Amendment 7 ended the GSA reporting requirement effective February 1, 1996, and kept the review documentation with the agency. This identifies distinct places to look for approvals and reviews from different periods.

## Parallel citations and discrepancies

- **FHWA and FAA references.** The September 30, 1998 FHWA Telecommunications Service Manual M1740.1B, paragraph 3 Authority item 1 as displayed in the reader, describes DOT Order 1600.17B dated September 21, 1990 as implementing **`Title 41 CFR, Part 201-6.2`**. Paragraph 8's fourth item also references 1600.17B or a subsequent revision. The October 10, 1995 FAA order p. 1 cites the same DOT order and **`Title 41 CFR Part 201-21.603`**. These are different agency documents, dates and literal citations. A 1989 GSA interim rule also uses the former subpart number. GSA's December 1990 republication postdates the cited September 1990 DOT order. The full DOT 1600.17B body and dated implementing and transition records are needed to test the relationship between those references. The FHWA manual's agency scope is FHWA. [FHWA manual, indexed reader lines 23–30, 46–49 and 115–116](https://highways.dot.gov/laws-regulations/directives/orders/m17401b) · [FAA order, physical p. 1 lines 4–13](https://www.faa.gov/documentlibrary/media/order/1600.24d.pdf#page=1) · [1989 GSA interim rule, explanation (3)(c)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1989-10-16/pdf/FR-1989-10-16.pdf)
- **1991 correction representation.** The 1990 page text reads **`No recording or identification of public callers`** in (d)(4)(iii) and **`(2) and (3)`** in (e). The June 26, 1991 correction as displayed in the GPO index reader gives **`No recording of identification of public callers`** and **`(2), (3), and (4)`**. The correction scan was above the acquisition cap. Preserve the displayed values without adjudicating the wording or assigning an explanation. The 1991 items address separate clauses from the (d)(1) and (d)(3) recipient instructions checked here.
