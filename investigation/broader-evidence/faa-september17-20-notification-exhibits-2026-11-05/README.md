# FAA September 17 and 20 notification records

November 5, 2026 · B129 / PD01 / PD07. The [April 20 Commission interview memorandum](https://www.scribd.com/document/17218071/T8-B6-FAA-HQ-Darlene-Freeman-Fdr-4-8-04-and-4-20-04-MFR-852) lists a September 17 FAA summary and a September 20 notifications sheet among the records used in the interview. This follow-up obtained a 59-page released scan of the September 17 report and the public text of a dated September 20 sheet in a reproduction attributed to the Commission's NYC Office files. ## The September 17 row in the released scan and OIG reprint

The National Security Archive's release page identifies document 7 as the September 17 FAA *Summary of Air Traffic Hijack Events*. The PDF has **59 physical pages**. The OIG describes its Appendix 3 as **13 pages** of the September 17 summary. In the FAA scan at physical p. 28 and Appendix 3 at physical p. 27, the same passage describes Indianapolis notifying Great Lakes of a possible AA77 accident and Great Lakes then notifying the Washington Operations Center of the loss of communications and radar identification. The following passage describes a Dulles primary-target observation between **0925:00 and 0930:00**, qualified as approximate and based on personnel statements. Three ordered text anchors match. The FAA scan's OCR gives **924:00** for the Great Lakes-to-Washington row. The leading glyph in the scan is degraded. The OIG rendering clearly gives **0924:00**. Preserve both readings and the row wording. The 59- and 13-page counts describe different archive objects. The comparison did not establish full document equivalence. ## Keep the three 09:24 descriptions distinct

- The September 17 summary describes **internal FAA routing** from Great Lakes to the Washington Operations Center. The OIG says the summary supplies no time for an FAA-to-DOD AA77 notification. [OIG, physical pp. 5 and 27](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=5)
- The September 20 sheet attributes a **military notification about AA77** to NEADS at **9:24** and suggests a separate possible **9:26** FAA-net source. The prior briefing-book fax also reproduces **0924** under AA77. A shared attribution supplies no independent confirmation. [Sheet](https://www.scribd.com/doc/14274669/NYC-Box-2-Azzarello-Brown-FAA-Docs-Fdr-Notfications-to-Military-NOIWON-CIA-Redacted) · [Briefing-book fax comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/479f19594b1dec4ffb12947554e7b456cf078d61/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-april19-briefing-book-received-copy-2026-11-05/README.md)
- The OIG says that cross-checking the tail number in the NEADS log would have identified **AA11**, and cites the Commission's **9:34** finding for notice that AA77 was missing. These are the OIG and Commission accounts. The raw recordings, logs and clock alignment were outside this test. [OIG, physical pp. 5–6](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=5) · [Commission report, ch. 1](https://www.911commission.gov/report/911Report_Ch1.htm)
