## Observations

The September 17, 2001 forwarding chain reproduces a regional list of materials described as still needed at Washington headquarters. Under UAL175, it states a ZNY R-42 transcript is held and seeks other relevant transcripts, flight-progress strips, employee statements and certified cassettes. Under AAL77, it lists some transcripts as held and requests other transcripts, strips, employee statements and certified cassettes. The author asks for approximate times on controller statements and discusses timelines, with the sentence continuing onto page 2. These are contemporaneous descriptions of needs and reported holdings, not an inventory of everything delivered or evidence that any particular item was never preserved.

A wording discrepancy is visible on page 1: the fourth numbered item under the **AAL77** section reads “Certified cassettes of all communications with UAL175 and coordination of said A/C.” The UAL175 section also names UAL175 in its fourth item. Preserve the section heading and item text together. An editorial carryover is one untested possibility; the scan does not establish the intended flight, any later correction, or how recipients acted. A native original, associated corrections and fulfillment records could distinguish those possibilities.
