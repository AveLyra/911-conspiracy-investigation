# FBI Commission-request certification: scope and limits

**AUD016-A1 / TEL04.** A June 24, 2004 FBI director's letter provides an agency-wide account relevant to the production trail: it asserts that reasonable searches for the Commission's 47 FBI document requests were completed and located responsive documents produced or made available. This is relevant contrary evidence to any unsupported claim of a blanket failure to respond. It is also a qualified agency assertion, not an item-level inventory or independent verification that particular logs were delivered.

## Source and scope

The letter appears on physical pp. 12–13 of `DM B1 Certifications Fdr- Certifications of Completion and Compliance with Document Requests- DOD-DOT-Treasury-TSA-DHS-CIA-NSA-DOJ-FBI-State 206.pdf`, a 14-page member of the [January 2009 Commission archive](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip). The 632,248-byte PDF was acquired November 25, 2026; SHA-256 `634edda33e2ba741fa591dd05d703f842b765fb3d532f4bda130c10a963040e2`, ZIP CRC `bebb2b41`. All text layers were screened for FBI, certification and 41-1; pp. 11 and 12 were visually inspected. The PDF is an archive reproduction, not the native letter or proof of receipt; signatures, personal contact details and raw images are excluded here.

## Distinct accounts and limits

The June 24 letter, p. 12, qualifies its statement by the writer's knowledge and inquiry of appropriate officials, and describes documents **located** in FBI possession, custody or control. It gives an aggregate **47** FBI document requests. It does not identify Request **41-1**, list specific enclosures or explain the disposition of individual SIOC logs. The related June 28 Department of Justice letter on p. 11 separately gives **16** DOJ requests and its own qualified account. Those numbers have different stated scopes; they are not silently combined or treated as an inconsistency.

A prior [Commission staff correspondence review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/6f567c83d4d10b8a50659af1c3e8b72ab9a29009/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-doj-fbi-logs-request-2026-11-25/README.md) and the [May 4 transmittal](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/398ad800a2cac944c0c5f62399b7a9fdee3a7c25/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-fbi-request-41-1-may4-2026-11-25/README.md) document different stages and scope. The [2008 withdrawal notices](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/48bfdd7a971cab6428d0a1903260028993b230c2/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-fbi-notice-visual-2026-11-25/README.md) describe archive review and do not establish what the Commission received in 2004.
