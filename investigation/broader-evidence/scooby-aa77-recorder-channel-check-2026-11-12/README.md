# Scooby report 25: Flight 77 recorder-channel source check

November 12, 2026. Visually inspected the NTSB’s scanned recorder report, PDF pp. 1–4, 8, 10 and 13, and flight-path study, pp. 1–2. This checks selected recovered report claims; it does not decode raw bits or inspect aircraft wiring, source data or the separate clock-correlation report.

| Recovered claim | Original-page result |
| --- | --- |
| The report distinguishes validated from not-working-or-unconfirmed parameters. | [NTSB recorder report, PDF p. 2](https://www.ntsb.gov/about/Documents/AAL77_fdr.pdf#page=2), says only validated parameters appear in its plots and tables; the remainder were either not properly recorded **or** not confirmed as properly recorded. The report does not assign an individual cause to each entry. |
| Pressure altitude was in the validated list; flight-deck door and two time fields were not. | [PDF p. 8, Attachment I-3](https://www.ntsb.gov/about/Documents/AAL77_fdr.pdf#page=8), lists pressure altitude among validated parameters. [PDF pp. 10 and 13, Attachments I-5 and I-8](https://www.ntsb.gov/about/Documents/AAL77_fdr.pdf#page=13), place FLT DECK DOOR, captain-clock GMT and GPS TIME under the not-working-or-unconfirmed grouping. A named parameter is not proof that a functional, aircraft-specific door sensor was installed or that its open/closed history was validated. Nor does the grouping prove it was physically absent. |
| The report describes a 256-word-per-second recorder stream and a separate time-correlation report. | [PDF pp. 2–4](https://www.ntsb.gov/about/Documents/AAL77_fdr.pdf#page=2) describe 256 twelve-bit words per second, four-second frames and correlation to Eastern Daylight Time. The report refers to a separate time-correlation report and says the last recorded data occurred at **09:37:44 EDT**. The correlation report and native bitstream were not reviewed here. |
| The independent flight-path study used several sources but the final radar altitude is not necessarily independent after transponder loss. | [NTSB Flight Path Study, pp. 1–2](https://www.ntsb.gov/about/Documents/Flight_Path_Study_AA77.pdf), lists the FDR, FAA centers, Dulles approach radar and Air Force radar, says transponder returns stopped about 08:56 and gives impact as **09:37:45 EDT**. The study’s combined-source description alone does not specify an independent radar-altitude measurement for each final sample or reproduce the time correlation. The one-second difference from the recorder report’s last data timestamp should not be silently treated as a demonstrated contradiction or as an independently calibrated uncertainty interval. |

**Open:** N644AA’s configuration/effectivity, installation, wiring and functional-test records; the cited data-frame attachments and native raw recorder bytes; the separate time-correlation report; and radar-source-by-source synchronization. These documents may determine what a given channel can support. The checked records do not establish an explosives theory or a UMS role. Other report-25 claims and attachments remain unreviewed.
