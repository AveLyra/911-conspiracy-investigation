Missing authorship, source-event joins and clock/creation/version records prevent such inferences. See selected readings V08–V09. These are 9/11-titled chronologies enclosed in a 2003 submission, not proven native contemporaneous logs merely because of the title.

The WOC footer labels also progress **Page 1 of 1, Page 2 of 2, Page 3 of 3, Page 4 of 4**, with no footer count visible on the fifth page (PDF pp. 276–280). Preserve those printing observations.
