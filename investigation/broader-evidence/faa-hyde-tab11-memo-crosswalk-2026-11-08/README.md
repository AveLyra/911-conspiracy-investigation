## What the cited pages say

- **The log-source hypothesis.** Physical and printed p. 8, text lines 27–33 and 39–48, describes the two public notification times as apparently derived from the MCC/T log. The staff compare **9:16 / United 93** with **N612UA / United 175** and **9:24 / American 77** with **N334AA / American 11**. The memo also names several sources for the report of American 11 heading toward Washington, including **9:24:39** in the Headquarters Intel chat, **9:27** in the Air Warning Center log, **9:28** in the Headquarters chat and **9:21** in the memo's description of the mission crew commander's report. These are staff's cited observations. This comparison tested the memo's wording and page provenance. [Previously compared released ledger, physical p. 4](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2013-002-doc1.pdf#page=4)
- **An unresolved transcription difference.** Both memo images on p. 8 visibly give **75 50B/** in the passage quoting the **N612UA** entry. The handwritten ledger reading in the preceding review is **75 SOB/**, and the Rutgers HTML representation gives **75 S0B/**. Keep the three strings in their respective sources. The date discrepancy and these differing renderings give no basis to select a cause or change the underlying entries. [Ledger image](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2013-002-doc1.pdf#page=4) · [Rutgers, section 8.3](https://rutgerslawreview.com/8-united-93/)
- **The FAA attachment and use gap.** On p. 9 lines 17–36 staff describe Moore's September 16 email as reporting that information from the logs and tapes had gone to the FAA for a White House briefing. Staff expressly say they had yet to obtain the records named in that email. They had the **September 17 FAA summary**, which they describe as reporting the earlier American 11 and United 175 notification times. On lines 38–45 they characterize the omission of American 77 and United 93 notification times as suspicious. Earlier work here already holds a released September 17 summary. The exact material sent, received and available to later drafters still needs an attachment and receipt chain. - **Separate time fields.** The Tab 11 memo p. 3 lines 17–20 quotes the **1408Z** and **1415Z** queries and names **NCT 0053404**. Its own footnote 2, text line 50, attributes **10:07** to the NEADS log. P. 9 line 44 and p. 10 line 34 give **10:08** in their respective staff and internal-chart descriptions. Preserve these alongside the questioned **9:16** entry. No event-to-clock alignment was performed.

The memo leaves open the possibility that officials failed to check the tail numbers (p. 9 lines 6–11).
