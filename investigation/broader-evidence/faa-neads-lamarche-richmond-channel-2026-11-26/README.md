# NEADS channel in October 27, 2003 Commission interview

## Source and observation

The National Archives hosts the Commission's [LaMarche and Richmond interview memorandum](https://nara-media-001.s3.amazonaws.com/arcmedia/9-11/MFR/t-0148-911MFR-00775.pdf), a six-page document downloaded November 26, 2026, SHA-256 `5e36ac8f5a59a7d25df1fec3ad5b00fabd29b599c5854be97c80c1eec036da2c`. Its first page identifies the interview date as October 27, 2003, and Geoffrey Brown as preparer. Page 4 reports Commission staff reviewed with the interviewees a recording supplied by NEADS, labeled `DRM1 DAT 2 Channel 15 AST`; it associates several observations with positions on that recording. Page 5 says staff ended playback. We inspected these passages and the heading; the memo is a record of the interview, not the underlying audio or an independent measurement of the events described in it.

## Comparison and limits

The separately preserved [Fox interview memo](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/77147e66859b42f6f60615bb1508b5caca6bb289/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-neads-fox-first-interview-channel-2026-11-26/README.md) describes staff using a different NEADS-provided file, `DRM1 DAT 2 Channel 2 MCC`, in an interview dated October 29, 2003. These are two channel labels in two interview records, not proof of two independent original tapes or complete deliveries. The original interview audio, copies played, and transfer chain have not been matched.

The earlier [November 2003 delivery and recovery account](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/c35defa43beaf0b804b2ca3f51fa465ca54d8eaa/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-neads-prior-review-recovery-correction-2026-11-25/README.md) remains preserved separately. These observations do not establish that every requested recording was supplied, establish why later requests or recovery were needed, or determine whether any omission was deliberate. The later schedule is not a receipt. Recording positions in the memo are not a calibrated clock comparison.
