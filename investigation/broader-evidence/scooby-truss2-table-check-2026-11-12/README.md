# Scooby report 30: recovered Truss 2 evidence table source check

November 12, 2026. This checks selected entries from the recovered report table against NIST’s publications, the Orabi manuscript, and Dai et al.’s experimental paper. These are published records and model descriptions; this is not a rerun, historical temperature measurement, native case-deck audit or exhaustive review of the report and attachments.

| Recovered table or body claim | Original-source check and limits |
| --- | --- |
| Two 6,000-gallon Salomon tanks supplied fifth-floor generators; their post-collapse condition did not establish 12,000 gallons burned there. | NIST’s [fuel-system report, table 9-2](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-1j.pdf) gives 12,000 gallons as the combined **tank capacity**, based on full-tank assumptions, not a measured September 11 inventory. [NIST NCSTAR 1-9, fuel accounting](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-9.pdf) describes the two tanks as damaged and empty after collapse, with no significant fuel found below them; it treats the capacity as potentially available. [2006 NIST testimony](https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/2017/05/09/TestimonySept8_06.pdf) describes the pressurized system serving nine fifth-floor generators and the environmental contractor’s observations. Empty tanks and modeled availability do not measure fuel actually consumed in the room before collapse. |
| The unprotected-diagonal result is conditional, and modeled fire protection mattered. | [Orabi et al., manuscript pp. 17–21](https://ira.lib.polyu.edu.hk/bitstream/10397/92640/1/Orabi_Collapse_World_Trade.pdf#page=17), reports the protected and unprotected model cases and subsequent diagonal buckling under the assumed fire. The condition in a simulation is not an observed historical absence of protection on Truss 2. [NIST’s WTC 7 FAQ](https://www.nist.gov/world-trade-center-investigation/study-faqs/wtc-7-investigation) describes installation measurements and renovation photographs, and explains that its model assumed protection intact except in the debris-impact region. Those observations and assumptions do not establish the actual condition of every individual member at the time of the fire. |
| Model 2’s 2.3-second endpoint was computational, and the approximately 0.8 m was local floor deformation. | The [manuscript, p. 21](https://ira.lib.polyu.edu.hk/bitstream/10397/92640/1/Orabi_Collapse_World_Trade.pdf#page=21), says the intended five-second dynamic analysis was terminated at 2.3 seconds after roughly a week and a 400 GB output; it describes approximately 0.8 m of floor deflection after two seconds. Termination establishes neither later physical arrest nor complete global collapse, and the reported floor displacement is not a measured roofline trajectory. |
| Dai’s reconstructed connection axial forces are not measured force values. | [Dai et al., §5.1–5.2](https://www.open-access.bcu.ac.uk/14548/1/s10694-023-01452-1.pdf) state that their reconstruction assumes intact composite action, fixed column bases and linearly elastic columns; they advise using measured strains and displacements for model validation. The reconstruction is a derived quantity with assumptions, not a direct force measurement. Comparing a solver with these experiments also requires a documented input deck, measured histories and agreed success criteria. |

**Open dependencies:** the native WTC case decks and exact build, the actual Truss 2 protection and thermal history, raw fuel-remediation and delivery records, the solver’s thermal-to-dynamic state transfer and energy accounting, and the original experimental data/model comparison. The observed fuel infrastructure and model predictions do not identify an explosive mechanism or establish a role for UMS.
