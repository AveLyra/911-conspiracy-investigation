# A transcript-message read receipt: scope and limits

The [Commission archive](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip) member `T8 B17 FAA Trips 3 of 3 Fdr- Satori Review Timeline- FAA Data.pdf`, physical PDF p. 39, displays a read receipt for a message titled “Transcripts.” It lists a send time of **February 27, 2002, 11:43:00 a.m.** and a read time of **11:58:10 a.m.**; no timezone is shown. Page 40 is a separate undated message requesting an electronic transcript and describing material as not final pending review. Both pages were visually inspected November 25, 2026. The 46-page source member SHA-256 is `cee78be0535e9cb6e5834da575c9fceb55791afcf6331a31b44a5e11af2cab0c`; archive member length and CRC were verified.

The receipt is a reported read event for a message. It does not display the message body or attachment, prove an attachment was opened or establish that it corresponds to page 40 or the [February 26 instructions](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/f0cac088dffda2c6f033b94f070562fb94a03088/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-transcript-transmission-2002-2026-11-25/README.md). A common subject and physical adjacency are leads, not a verified join. The undated note's description of nonfinal material is likewise an account, not proof of what the receipt covers. Do not infer delivery to the FBI, complete transcript coverage, destruction, concealment or motive.

**Open records:** native messages and headers, attachment identifiers and hashes, transmission and recipient records, final reviewed versions and source-record custody. Preserve the earlier certified-mail and email descriptions separately. Raw scans and personal contact details are excluded.
