The whole page was rendered and inspected. The [NARA finding aid](https://www.archives.gov/research/foia/faa-finding-aid-72-83) describes it as a formal UAL175 accident-package memorandum.

## Observation

The October 30, 2001 memorandum from New York ARTCC's air traffic manager to the regional air traffic division says two copies of the original UAL175 aircraft accident package are attached. It states that, following regional review and concurrence, the two remaining copies shall be forwarded to the FAA investigator in charge under Order 8020.11. The scan ends after the memorandum's “Attachments” notation; it does not contain the packages, an inventory, proof of delivery, concurrence, or evidence of later forwarding. It identifies copies of an original package rather than explicitly transferring original documentation.

This gives a case-specific source to compare with the four-copy distribution provisions of the [2000 and 2003 order editions](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/4b2941a2cd4ff73e1b868c65c676c16aff09d65e/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-zny-523-order-comparison-2026-11-16/README.md). The proposed routing and the order's general wording are preserved separately. The memo does not establish that every contemplated transfer occurred, nor how these copies relate to the [2003 custody entry](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/4c305be9a15db31c4e061a4c6d92629823dfe9d1/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-zny-528-custody-2026-11-16/README.md) or the [2004 request for originals](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/bbe1d23a97dc3a797e824246e0470af30e9a1073/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-zny-513-2004-request-2026-11-16/README.md). No noncompliance or motive is established.
