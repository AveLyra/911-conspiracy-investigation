## Why this is useful, and what could change it

A recorded future-testing condition is contrary evidence to a blanket assumption that memorial distribution necessarily ended all testing access. It does not establish that any test occurred, a complete specimen remained available, or every requester could demand access. The exact agreement and any incorporated court order are needed to identify eligible parties, notice, conditions and later amendments. A summary in minutes cannot substitute for the operative instrument.

The new resolution/agreement identifiers provide concrete original-record targets. They can link municipal authorization to an executed instrument, then to actual dispatch and receipt. Permission, legal transfer, physical delivery, later custody and scientific use remain separate events. A shipment record inconsistent with the requested item, a changed condition, a failed transfer or a later loss would materially change the chain. None is presumed.
