replacement for the completed WTC 7 audit and not acceptance of that assessment's

## Order and acceptance criteria

1. Preserve the complete 406-logical-line (405 newline characters), 92,009-byte assessment supplied in the related
   chat attachment and the later highlighted-lead assessment. Record their hashes.
   They are derivative claims to test. A clipped chat-reader excerpt is not a
   complete source; the full pasted file is used instead.
2. Give every substantive table row and every remaining substantive paragraph,
   list item and summary a separate B-ID. Keep exact original text, source line,
   film attribution and edition uncertainty. Also split compound prose into
   traceable child spans. Classify coverage against all 134 original register
   entries as covered, partly covered or outside the register. Coverage is not
   agreement or evidence verification. Separate definitional/navigation text
   from empirical/historical claims and account for it explicitly.
3. Start source tests with Saudi assistance and FBI source handling, including
   Bayoumi/Thumairy, the landlord asset, blocked CIR dissemination and the later
   oversight disclosure. Preserve reported findings, conflicting recollections,
   negative findings and unretrieved underlying exhibits separately.
4. Retain interrogation dependence, FAA/NORAD/Mineta chronology, presidential
   warnings, money, politics and historical analogies. Prioritize by proximity to
   a testable attack-related mechanism, record specificity and discrimination,
   not dramatic presentation. No numerical conspiracy probability is inferred.
5. Keep assistance, knowledge, deliberate nonintervention, demolition and later
   concealment as separate propositions. An association or a common nationality
   does not connect them. Circumstantial evidence need not be dismissed, but its
   independence and actual connecting links must be demonstrated.
6. Publish only an additive inventory and accurately bounded source findings.
   Clearly mark untested claims and edition/timestamp gaps. Verify AveLyra
   authentication, author and committer for each new public commit, destination,
   payload, parent history and remote outcome. Preserve predecessors and actual
   Git text diffs. Never force-push or silently edit old M-IDs.

## Evidence and reproduction rules

Use exact source URLs, access dates, SHA-256, page/section/line locators, source
wording/context, dependencies, contrary evidence and the smallest missing record
that would discriminate interpretations. A report repeating another report is
one chain; a government source can authentically contain an unverified assertion.
Record what was actually read, rendered or compared. Do not call a narrative
summary an acquired bank record, interview original, operational recording or
certified historical run. Preserve access failures and our own analysis errors.

PDFs are read-only original records. Visual review is separate from text/coverage
verification. Any raster output uses the guarded Intel operational media path;
small documents, source code and ordinary small data remain local. The main
repository's current AGENTS.md, WORKFLOW.md, START-HERE.md, WORKTREES.md and storage
layout govern; old worktree snapshots never supersede those controls.
