# Research audit: *Fahrenheit 9/11* and *Loose Change*

**My overall assessment is that the two films have substantially different evidentiary records.**

- **Michael Moore’s *Fahrenheit 9/11* is strongest on the misleading presentation of the Iraq war, failures before September 11, government secrecy, and the human costs of war.** Subsequent investigations support important parts of that criticism. Its weaker arguments turn real business relationships into conclusions about hidden motives, compare incompatible financial figures, or use selective editing to imply more than the underlying evidence establishes. [Senate Select Committee on Intelligence](https://www.intelligence.senate.gov/2008/06/05/press-senate-intelligence-committee-unveils-final-phase-ii-reports-prewar-iraq-intelligence/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **The *Loose Change* films identify genuine failures and inconsistencies, but do not establish their central alternative explanations for the attacks.** Several prominent early claims—no airliner at the Pentagon, Flight 93 landing elsewhere, and passenger calls being technologically impossible—conflict with substantial documentary and physical evidence. The building-collapse arguments require more careful treatment: some observations and criticisms are valid, but the inference to a demonstrated demolition operation remains unsupported. [ntsb.gov](https://www.ntsb.gov/about/Documents/Flight_Path_Study_AA77.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Rejecting those alternative explanations does not require accepting every official statement.** The record includes inaccurate early air-defense accounts, serious intelligence failures, limits on investigation and disclosure, and important unresolved questions about assistance to hijackers. Those deserve investigation on their own terms. [9-11commission.gov](https://www.9-11commission.gov/archive/hearing12/9-11Commission_Hearing_2004-06-17.htm?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## Scope and standards

I treated your request as covering the **2004 *Fahrenheit 9/11*** and the **main *Loose Change* releases**, including claims that were revised or qualified in later editions. The strongest transcript coverage is for *Fahrenheit 9/11*, *Loose Change: Second Edition*, *Final Cut*, and a **52-minute distributor version dated 2015**. That last version is not interchangeable with the complete 2009 *American Coup*. I could not establish a complete, reliable frame-by-frame transcript of every original and revised cut, so this is a broad audit of substantive claims and insinuations, rather than a certified concordance of every caption in every edition. [SS](https://www.springfieldspringfield.co.uk/movie_script.php?movie=fahrenheit-911\&utm_source=chatgpt.com)

I distinguish:

| Assessment | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Supported** | Reliable evidence supports the particular factual claim. |
| **Mixed or misleading** | A real fact is presented with omitted context, an inaccurate comparison, or an inference stronger than the evidence. |
| **Unsupported** | The proposed explanation has not been established. That is different from proving it impossible. |
| **Contradicted** | Stronger evidence directly conflicts with the claim. |
| **Unresolved** | The available record does not justify a confident conclusion. |

I also distinguish **what was observable**, **what investigators reconstructed**, and **what a filmmaker infers about intent**. A criticism can expose a real error without supplying a complete alternative account. Establishing the alternative, however, requires its own positive evidence.

The research includes relevant later findings through **October 26, 2026**.

# 1. *Fahrenheit 9/11*

## A. The election, Bush’s presidency, and the film’s editing

That is a legitimate conflict-of-interest issue. A television projection, however, did not legally determine Florida’s electoral votes. ### Assessment of *Fahrenheit 9/11*

The film’s strongest case survives correction of its weaker details: **the public justification for Iraq was seriously misleading; opportunities to address the al-Qaeda threat were missed; decisions and contracts warranted scrutiny; and war imposed grave costs on people with limited influence over those decisions.**

Its larger implication—that a particular financial network explains those decisions as a coordinated private scheme—requires evidence the film does not supply. The distinction matters because the well-supported criticism does not depend on accepting every insinuation.

# 2. *Loose Change*: aircraft, crash sites, and telephone calls

Edition matters here. An assertion made confidently in an early cut may be presented more cautiously, supplemented with contrary testimony, or omitted later. The following assesses the **claim families across the series**, without attributing every formulation to every edition.

# 4. *Loose Change*: the Twin Towers and WTC 7

This is the area where a careful answer needs to avoid both casual dismissal and claims of certainty that the studies do not support.

## A. Twin Towers: fire, structure, and collapse motion

| Claim or implication | Assessment |
|---|---|
| **Jet fuel cannot melt steel, therefore collapse was impossible** | **A false mechanical premise.** Structural steel need not melt to lose sufficient strength or stiffness. Thermal expansion, buckling, connection failure, and redistribution of loads can produce failure well below melting temperature. [tsapps.nist.gov](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=912399\&utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| **The fuel burned off too quickly** | **Incomplete fire model.** Jet fuel ignited building contents; it was not the sole continuing fuel. The relevant analysis includes office fires and damage to structure and fire protection. [tsapps.nist.gov](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=912399\&utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| **Dark smoke, survivors, or firefighters prove low temperatures everywhere** | **Overgeneralization.** Conditions varied across floors and over time. A survivable location, a local report of limited fire, or smoke color cannot establish temperatures or structural capacity throughout the impact region. |
| **The towers were designed for an airliner impact** | **A real design consideration, not a guarantee for every impact-and-fire combination.** The historical Boeing 707 analysis did not establish immunity to the exact damage, fuel distribution, fireproofing loss, and prolonged fires of September 11. The buildings survived the initial impacts. [NIST](https://www.nist.gov/world-trade-center-investigation/study-faqs/wtc-towers-investigation?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| **Other high-rise fires did not cause collapse** | **Not a matched comparison.** Different structural systems, damage, fire protection, loads, and firefighting conditions matter. A hotter-looking or longer-burning building is not automatically a control experiment for these structures. |
| **Redundant columns made collapse impossible** | **Static capacity is not the same as damaged-system dynamic capacity.** Load redistribution can preserve a structure initially but also overload remaining elements. Once a large upper mass moves, stopping it involves energy and momentum, not simply its original static weight. [richardgage911.org](https://richardgage911.org/wp-content/uploads/March-2008-Bazant-Le-Greening-Benson-What-Did-and-Did-not-Cause-Collapse-of-WTC-Twin-Towers-in-New-York.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| **The South Tower fell first, so fire cannot explain it** | **Invalid comparison.** Time since ignition is only one variable. Impact geometry, damage distribution, the weight above the impact, and fire development differed. A later-struck building can fail first without an added demolition mechanism. [tsapps.nist.gov](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=910120\&utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| **The towers fell at complete free-fall speed** | **Not an accurate description of the whole event.** Dust obscures different structural features, and exterior debris falls ahead of parts of the structure. A single stopwatch interval cannot establish that the entire building fell with zero resistance. [richardgage911.org](https://richardgage911.org/wp-content/uploads/March-2008-Bazant-Le-Greening-Benson-What-Did-and-Did-not-Cause-Collapse-of-WTC-Twin-Towers-in-New-York.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| **Vertical collapse or an upper block that did not topple proves demolition** | **Not a unique signature.** A damaged, fragmenting building is not a rigid tree. A valid comparison must account for loss of support and breakup. Apparent verticality alone does not identify the initiating mechanism. |
| **Pulverization, ejected steel, and “squibs” require bombs** | **Not established by appearance.** Gravity supplies substantial energy; impact, fragmentation, and air displacement must be quantified before assigning an explosive source. Simplified published calculations address some of these issues, though they are not complete three-dimensional reproductions of every fragment. [richardgage911.org](https://richardgage911.org/wp-content/uploads/March-2008-Bazant-Le-Greening-Benson-What-Did-and-Did-not-Cause-Collapse-of-WTC-Twin-Towers-in-New-York.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| **The “pancake” account changed, proving fraud** | **An evolving explanation is not itself fraud.** FEMA’s early hypothesis and NIST’s later collapse-initiation explanation differ. NIST emphasized floor deformation pulling exterior columns inward. Initiation must also be distinguished from subsequent propagation. [tsapps.nist.gov](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=912399\&utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| **Witnesses heard explosions** | **The reports are real evidence of what people experienced.** It would be wrong to say nobody reported explosions. The word alone does not identify an explosive material: electrical failures, pressure events, impacts, and structural failures can also produce violent sounds. Specific reports need synchronized location and timing. |
| **Seismic spikes establish bombs** | **Not a unique identification.** The original study had no known recording at or immediately beside the WTC; its nearest station was 34 km away, with approximately two seconds’ timing uncertainty. It also notes that the 1993 truck bomb was not detected even at a closer station. Neither a large pulse nor absence of a separate pulse is a universal bomb test. [ldeo.columbia.edu](https://www.ldeo.columbia.edu/~richards/my_papers/WTC_LDEO_KIM.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| **Recovered steel showed temperatures too low for collapse** | **An unrepresentative-sample problem.** Tests on recovered pieces constrain those specimens. They do not establish the maximum temperature or condition of every critical member in the buildings. Recovery and selection limitations must be included in the inference. [NCSTAR 1-3.doc](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=101016\&utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| **UL certified that the steel would survive** | **Material and assembly tests are being conflated.** Fire-resistance testing of a floor assembly is not certification that all structural steel survives every temperature, load, connection condition, and impact. Kevin Ryan’s employment dispute also cannot settle the engineering question. [NCSTAR 1-6B Fire Resistant.doc](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=909152\&utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| **An expert’s early demolition opinion proves the cause** | **An initial impression is not a completed investigation.** An expert changing an early opinion is neither proof of demolition nor, without additional evidence, proof of coercion. I did not independently retrieve the complete original interviews underlying every such claim, including the Van Romero sequence. |

## B. Molten material, sulfur, microspheres, and thermite

These are **different evidentiary questions** that are often combined as if they were one chemically identified sample.

| Evidence or claim | Assessment |

### What the chemistry establishes—and what it does not

Harrit’s paper makes claims about material identification and energetic behavior. Its heating measurements were conducted in flowing air, and the paper acknowledges contributions from organic combustion. Therefore, the measured energy cannot automatically be assigned entirely to an oxygen-independent thermitic reaction. [Jones_TOCPJ](https://benthamopenarchives.com/contents/pdf/TOCPJ/TOCPJ-2-7.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

A convincing causal case would need to connect:

1. An independently established material identity.
2. Reliable sample provenance.
3. A quantity and location relevant to structural failure.
4. A demonstrated mechanism.
5. A chronology linking the reaction to collapse initiation.

That is a larger evidentiary chain than showing that an unusual particle can react when heated. Conversely, calling every disputed particle “paint” without testing the same specimens is not a complete resolution either.

### What the University of Alaska Fairbanks study actually contributes

The 2020 Hulsey–Quan–Xiao report is more substantive than a demolition-style animation. It raises specific objections about restraint, connection geometry, stiffeners, side plates, and supporting beams. It received external review and public comments, so describing it as having received no review would be inaccurate.

Its visual-fit scenario nevertheless **imposes** loss of core support over eight stories, followed by exterior support loss approximately 1.3 seconds later. That demonstrates the modeled consequences of the prescribed failure pattern. It does not demonstrate what physical process caused that pattern. The report also acknowledges that alternatives cannot be entirely excluded and that its model did not reproduce one observed exterior feature. Its AE911Truth funding is relevant disclosure, not an engineering rebuttal. [Civil Engineering](https://www.scribd.com/document/466947797/A-Structural-Reevaluation-of-the-Collapse-of-World-Trade-Center-7-March2020?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

I did not independently rerun either the UAF or NIST models. The proper unresolved questions concern model inputs, connection behavior, failure criteria, sensitivity, and agreement with multiple observations—not which animation looks more familiar.

# 6. Later evidence that changes how the films should be assessed

## A. Saudi assistance: a serious issue that cannot be dismissed

The **August 28, 2025 federal ruling** is important. The court found sufficient evidence concerning Omar al-Bayoumi, Fahad al-Thumairy, assistance to hijackers, and their relationship to Saudi Arabia to retain jurisdiction despite Saudi Arabia’s sovereign-immunity challenge.

This was more than an allegation being mechanically repeated: the court assessed direct and circumstantial evidence. But it was **not a final merits judgment establishing that the Saudi government ordered the attacks**. The opinion expressly distinguishes those stages.

The same judge also rejected overstated interpretations of a welcome-party video and a Washington sightseeing video. That combination is instructive: the case could contain substantial evidence while particular dramatic characterizations remained unjustified. [nysd.uscourts.gov](https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/2025-08/03-md-01570%2011182.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

An appeal was argued on October 7, 2026. The record reviewed here does not establish a subsequent final merits determination. [ABC7 San Francisco](https://abc7news.com/post/saudi-arabia-face-appeals-court-911-families-lawsuit/19917319/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**Implication for the films:** blanket Saudi exoneration is unwarranted. So is treating later evidence about a specific assistance network as proof of every Bush–Saudi financial insinuation in *Fahrenheit 9/11*.

## B. Newly released presidential warnings

In September 2026, the CIA released **71 presidential daily briefing items** connected with the pre-attack threat record. A declassified Commission review memorandum describes, among other reports, a 1998 warning involving an explosive-laden aircraft and a possible U.S. target. It also records limitations in the underlying information. The 1998 material belongs to the Clinton presidency, not Bush’s. [CIA](https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/cia-releases-presidents-daily-briefs-in-commemoration-of-the-25th-anniversary-of-9-11/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

These records strengthen criticism of categorical claims that using aircraft as weapons was unimaginable. They do not establish knowledge of the specific date, aircraft, teams, and plan of September 11.

## C. Coercion and the reliability of the planning narrative

On **August 28, 2026**, the military judge suppressed Khalid Sheikh Mohammed’s January 12–16, 2007 FBI statements because the government failed to establish voluntariness. The ruling expressly distinguishes voluntariness from reliability. [mc.mil](https://www.mc.mil/Portals/0/pdfs/KSM2/KSM%20II%20%28AE630BBBBBB%29.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Two limits are essential:

- The **2004 Commission could not have relied on interviews conducted in 2007**. Its reliance on earlier CIA interrogation reporting is a related but separate issue.
- Suppression does not establish that every statement was false, and it does not invalidate evidence generated independently of interrogation.

The appropriate consequence is closer scrutiny of which historical claims depend on coercive questioning, which have independent corroboration, and which remain uncertain.

# 7. What the research supports

The strongest conclusions are uneven rather than all-or-nothing.

**For *Fahrenheit 9/11*, substantial parts of the policy indictment withstand scrutiny.** Its Iraq argument is considerably stronger than its attempts to infer hidden loyalties from aggregated money, family connections, and suggestive meetings. Correcting the latter does not undo the former.

**For *Loose Change*, the strongest material concerns failures, inaccurate accounts, incomplete investigation, and disclosure.** Those points do not establish aircraft substitution, fabricated passenger calls, surviving hijackers, or a demonstrated demolition operation. Several of those claims fail on their own factual premises.

The remaining questions worth pursuing are specific:

- What assistance did particular people provide to hijackers, with what knowledge, and under whose authority?
- Why did particular intelligence reports fail to reach people who could act?
- Who was responsible for inaccurate early air-defense accounts?
- Which details of the planning narrative are independently corroborated, and which depend on coercive interrogation?
- Which engineering conclusions are reproducible from available inputs, and which remain sensitive to unavailable or uncertain information?

Those questions can yield evidence capable of changing a conclusion. A general accumulation of suspicious-looking details cannot do the same work unless the details are accurate, connected, and shown to distinguish the proposed explanation from its alternatives.

The defensible position is to **accept the well-supported evidence of failure and misleading conduct, preserve genuine uncertainties, and require positive evidence for claims of participation in the attacks**. That standard exposes serious misconduct without granting either the films or official institutions an exemption from scrutiny.
