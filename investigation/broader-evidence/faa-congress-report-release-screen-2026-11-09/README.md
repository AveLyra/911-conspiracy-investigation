# FAA congressional report release and the missing management-response enclosures

November 9, 2026 Eastern · B129 / PD01–PD04.

The FAA's October 28, 2010 release letter says its **Office of Government and Industry Affairs** searched for FAA reports to Congress that had not been posted on the agency's website. The released packet has **109 distinct FAA report numbers** in its printed index, each matched in order to a PDF bookmark. This is a case-specific example of an FAA report-number register and the agency's congressional transmission records. The release letter describes an FAA component search. The Secretary's separate management reports and the specific OIG investigation outcomes remain unacquired. ## A transmission packet that refers to an enclosure

The first index entry, **FAA-041222-013**, describes action on GAO report **GAO-03-405** and gives **4/20/2005**. Its bookmarked section starts on physical p. 10. The next numbered section begins at p. 15. Pages 10–13 are congressional cover letters. Each says a response detailing action was prepared and marks an **Enclosure**. Page 14 renders blank. The tested section contains no separate response document, and the PDF has no standard document-level embedded file. These are properties of the released PDF. The original diskette contents and the attachments transmitted to Congress remain open. The report number appears as **GAO-03-405** on the selected images of pp. 10 and 13 and as **GAO-03-40S** in their embedded text. The embedded text of pp. 11 and 12 reads **GAO-03-405**. Keep these as separate observations. The cover letters concern a GAO purchase-card review. Their report number, office and recipient fields show how to search for an enclosure without assuming that they use the same process as an OIG investigation or a post-Commission correction. ## Scope of the case-specific screen

The packet's publisher describes a **20-April-2005 – 24-February-2010** range. The PDF has **1,155 pages** and **218 bookmarks**, comprising one index bookmark, 109 report bookmarks, and 108 blank-labeled separators. Index and bookmark matching tests organization and sequence. It cannot verify that every body or attachment in the original agency production is represented. [Source and test scope](SOURCE-PINS.json)

The embedded-text screen found no match for the seven declared case-specific patterns. These include the project and investigation identifiers, the management-report titles, the investigation title and scoped correction terms. Of 1,155 pages, **1,025** had nonempty embedded text and **132** had fewer than 50 embedded characters. The 24 sparsely indexed pages outside the blank-labeled separators were OCR screened and gave no target matches. One oversized page used a smaller raster after the first pass stalled there. These are bounded search results for this packet and these patterns. Search terms can miss alternate titles, OCR errors or information divided between pages.
