For **09-48**, the intake log prints **February 5, 2009**. The agency acknowledgment in the processing file gives a **February 4** request date and says it was received at the **FOIA Control Desk on February 6**. A follow-up references a **February 18** referral to the Building and Fire Research Laboratory for search and duplication. The same public file also contains a final-response letter identifying the release disc and withholding scope. Keep the February 5 and 6 receipt values separate. They could describe different intake stages or a discrepancy in the records.
