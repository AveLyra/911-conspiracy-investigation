# FAA hearing draft versions and staff notebook entries

November 6, 2026 UTC · **B129 / PD01–PD04 / PD07**

A **May 18, 2003** Commission draft asks why NORAD was notified about Flight 77 at **9:24 a.m.** This is three days earlier than the previously held May 21 draft and four days before the May 22 hearing. The May 18 question invokes published accounts in the surrounding passage without naming the source of the 9:24 figure. The question therefore places that figure in Commission preparation before the FAA statement was read into the record. The exact drafting inputs and transmission chain remain open. ## Notebook and transcript comparison

- **Team 8 Box 15 hearing notebook, physical p. 6, lower half.** In a block following a **Cleland** heading, the handwritten entries include **0813**, **0821** and **NORAD 0840**. A following block labeled **her own notes** includes **0820** beside a Boston indication of a problem and **0834** beside Otis. The official transcript separately records Garvey recalling a Boston controller contacting Otis at **8:34**, an earlier indication of a problem around **8:20**, and her acknowledgment that she might be mixing up Flight 11 and Flight 77. The notebook's two blocks preserve separate contexts for the numbers. [May 22 transcript, PDF pp. 118–119](https://digital.library.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metadc1228311/m2/1/high_res_d/9-11Commission_Hearing_2003-05-22.pdf#page=118)
- **Same notebook, physical p. 8, final line.** The shorthand appears to read **2 notifications re AA77 0834**. In the transcript Garvey says she recalls **two notifications before** the time asked about, **three in total**, with the first at **8:34**. The notebook does not label its count as a total or specify which notifications it counts. Preserve both entries alongside her subsequent qualification. They are two records of the hearing exchange. [May 22 transcript, p. 117 lines 30–37 and p. 119 lines 1–5](https://digital.library.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metadc1228311/m2/1/high_res_d/9-11Commission_Hearing_2003-05-22.pdf#page=117)
- **Same notebook, physical p. 13, final two lines.** A NORAD block gives **0916** alongside a possible hijack of **93** and **0924** beside ditto marks and **77**. Physical pp. **14–15** also contain QFR and phone-bridge notes. The source is a staff notebook of the hearing period. The lower block does not identify a native log.
