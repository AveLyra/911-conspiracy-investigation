# Flight 93 Airfone package: visual check of the FBI reproduction

TEL01–TEL04 / AUD016. On November 25, 2026, visually examined physical PDF page 45 of the [Team 1A Box 33 packet previously pinned here](../telephone-gte-96202-box33-locator-2026-11-25/README.md). This advances the preceding text-layer review; the earlier wording remains intact.

The FBI interview reproduction carries header identifiers `M-INT-00112509` and `265A-NY-280350-302-96202`, with a January 16, 2002 transcription date. Its visible text describes an October 31, 2001 interview with a GTE Airfone network-operations manager and lists a received package containing a seating diagram, a three-page Flight 93 call-activity spreadsheet, and a separate unit-to-seat-location spreadsheet. The page states that these listed items **“have been placed in a 1A envelope and retained in the 1A section of this file.”** This is the report's assertion of filing, not an inspection of the envelope, underlying native files, chain of custody after filing, or present availability.

The packet's received-package date line is partly covered by a privacy marking. A [prior Team 7 reader assessment](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/6bd3db3f64615be0409e7ce912b1cc5572ec49e9/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-ua93-airfone-clock-attachment-2026-11-06/README.md) separately reported a November 6, 2001 date for the same serial. This visual check does **not** recover or independently verify the obscured date; retain both descriptions, and do not count the reproductions as independent carrier records. The prior reader assessment had not authenticated its source image, and its reported package date remains attributed to that source.

The page describes field categories including call date, time, billing information, number dialed, distribution unit and terminal unit; it does not show the actual rows, clock configuration or a caller-to-event join. The later updated spreadsheet, the 1A envelope and associated inventory remain unacquired. No call was independently timed or attributed, and no inference of suppression or intent follows from the missing inputs.
