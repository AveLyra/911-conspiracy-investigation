# Flight 175 call: acquired 2003 Commission interview memorandum

**AUD016-A1 / TC05-B105 / TC06-B110.** A two-page Commission interview memorandum dated November 21, 2003 is now available in an archived scan. Page 1 describes the recipient's recollection as approximately two minutes before the caller ended the conversation. This gives a specific source for a later duration account, but does not establish an independently timed conversation or prove the source of every later duration claim.

## Conflicting accounts retained

- The [previously reviewed FBI interview representation](https://www.scribd.com/document/15072623/T1A-B33-Four-Flights-Phone-Calls-and-Other-Data-Fdr-Entire-Contents-FBI-302s-843), referencing SF29, reports a recollection of **less than one minute**. It is a third-party reproduction; original interview and timing records remain unverified.
- The newly acquired 2003 Commission memorandum, p. 1, reports **about two minutes**. It documents a later interview, not a direct recording of the 2001 conversation.
- The [separate United letter, email and carrier-summary comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/b5e023e675f3ec30dc99e3a17f2f65132e030c34/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-ua175-summary-review-2026-10-28/README.md) preserves 75- and 31-second reported durations for two listed connections, versus one substantiated conversation; corresponding summary fields and native field definitions remain in dispute. Do not equate those records, attempts, connections and conversations without a demonstrated join.

The memorandum does not establish which account is correct, how either recollection was estimated, or that the difference was deliberate. The Commission's later report and interview memorandum share institutional sourcing and should not be counted as independent timed measurements. The scan has not been authenticated against interview notes or an audio recording. Its 2003 description of recording practices is an interview memorandum's account, not proof of a full historical records search.
