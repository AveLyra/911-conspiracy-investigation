## Source and actual inspection

Its [2005 release page](https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/NSAEBB/NSAEBB165/index.htm) identifies it as document 4 and describes it as undated. No original operational recordings or independent clocks were tested.

## Observations kept separate

* FAA chronology, physical page 1: “8:43 a.m.: FAA notified NORAD’s Northeast Air Defense Sector concerning the United Flight 175 suspected hijacking.” It also lists a 9:02 a.m. South Tower impact.
* The [9/11 Commission report, printed p. 23](https://www.9-11commission.gov/report/911Report_Ch1.pdf#page=23), says the first indication to NORAD air defenders of the second hijacked aircraft came in a New York Center call at 9:03, about the time of impact. The same report, printed p. 8, gives impact at 9:03:11.
* An earlier [preserved source comparison](../outstanding-records-verification-2026-10-26/REPORT.md) records that the September 2001 NORAD release also reported 8:43. The current visual reading is a separate published document, not an independently observed call.

The documents give different times and remain unresolved in this record. The 20-minute difference between the 8:43 and approximately 9:03 labels is an arithmetic comparison, not a measured delay. The chronology contains events through October 24, 2001; its actual preparation date and immediate sources have not been established. It may share a source with the earlier NORAD release; this has not been tested. The Commission account is a later reconstruction and is likewise not a substitute for independent inspection of the underlying recordings and clocks. These records do not establish intent or demonstrate that a call occurred at either time.
