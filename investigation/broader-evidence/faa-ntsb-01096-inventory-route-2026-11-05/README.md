# FAA inventory dates for the Operations Center report listed for NTSB

November 5, 2026 · B129 / PD07. Following the [September 20 NTSB support request](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913717), this pass recovered an FAA inventory circulated in October 2001 and two separately archived copies of related inventory sheets. They give a specific **September 21** NTSB forwarding date for the Washington Operations Center Activity Report. The NTSB receiving copy and actual transmitted report version remain open. [Prior request and net-log review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/4cf3b8d9b7bc7ae36f94bd44b6e0780c314196e4/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-net-log-source-candidates-2026-11-05/README.md)

## The inventory and the date fields

**AEA-2354 physical p. 1** is an **October 3, 2001** FAA email with the subject **AAT-200 Inventory/NTSB & FBI Request**. It asks staff to supply blank items and identifies **Data Inventory.doc**. Its archived 17-page PDF includes printed inventory sheets and additional messages. No native Word attachment was verified. The sheets distinguish **Rec’d AAT-200** from **To NTSB**. The UAL175 sheet bears footer **9/27 1400**. The AAL77 sheet bears **9/26 1400**. These are table footer markings, distinct from the recorded routing dates and the October 3 message date. The five ADA-30 rows agree on the date pair. The **blank versus 9/21** differences are separately preserved. Shared titles, dates and placement support a common inventory lineage. The repeated ADA-30 rows may refer to the same physical copy, with no distinct copy identifiers in these rows. The source supplies an FAA-recorded routing date and a specific report title. It leaves the meaning of the other blank cells, revision sequence, actual handoff and recipient acceptance unresolved.

## What this narrows

The earlier AWA-910 request names teleconference, transcript, log and landline evidence. The newly matched inventory rows identify a specific report to seek on the recipient side. The exact September 21 payload must be compared with the separate ADA-30 releases and the September 20 sheet's account of a **09:26** FAA-net discussion. Neither the inventory title nor the forwarding date identifies the underlying event-recorder export.
