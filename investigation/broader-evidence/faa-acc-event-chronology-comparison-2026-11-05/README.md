# FAA AWA-619 and the two 09:24 entries

November 5, 2026 · B129 / PD01 source check · Public derivative of the separately preserved source study

This study follows the public correction's source trail from [the prior ADA-30 comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/f425336303bbad671cb6943ff69941d4148b1aeb/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-ada30-layout-2026-11-05/README.md) to four National Archives copies of the FAA's *Chronology of events on 9-11-01*, catalogued under AWA-619. These copies contain a NEADS attribution that was missing from the scoped 09:24 rows of the previously reviewed FAA operations fax. No source examined identifies AWA-619 as the native computerized event log.

## Preserve the 09:24 entries and their different attributions

| Source and location | Observed wording or source report |
| --- | --- |
| AWA-619, physical p. 1, final row | **0924** describes an internal report to the FAA Washington Operations Center from its Great Lakes Regional Operations Center about AA77 disappearing from radar. |
| AWA-619, physical p. 2, first row | **“0924 NEADS Log shows FAA reporting AA77”**. The chronology attributes this entry to a log. The line gives no call, dispatch or receipt identifier and does not specify what was reported about the aircraft. |
| AWA-612, physical p. 1, **0924** row | Records the FAA regional radar-loss report, including the separate **1256Z** radar-loss time. The row names no military recipient. |
| Released NEADS MCC/T log, physical p. 4, Bates **NCT0000134**, handwritten **1324** row | **“American Airlines #N334AA hijacked”**. The Commission identifies that registration with American 11 and attributes the 09:24 military report to the erroneous account of American 11 still airborne. The released ledger does not name AA77 in this row. |
| FAA public chronology, June 2004 capture, raw HTML line 104 | **0924** says FAA notified NEADS of a suspected American 77 hijacking. The edited public row, November 2004 capture, lines 109–111, says **0920** and describes a general interagency information-sharing line. |

**Source dependence.** The AWA-619 page 2 line is a candidate antecedent for the older public claim because it shares the aircraft, minute and NEADS attribution. The draft or transmission chain linking them is still unacquired. It also remains open which log and version AWA-619's compiler read. The released MCC/T row and the Commission footnote that cites it form a source-and-citation pair. This comparison gives no evidence of who knew of the discrepancy during drafting and establishes no military receipt of an AA77 hijacking message at 09:24.

The AWA-619 **0920** block groups named parties, printing NMCC at **9:20** and State at **9:28** among other labels. The FAA executive summary's **0926** group entry includes State and NMCC. Preserve the group and party-specific times as printed. The AWA-619 **0916** line is crossed by a thick horizontal mark visible in the two inspected page images. Part of the UA93/NEADS attribution and a question mark remain legible. Do not treat that line as a verified notification. The Commission separately rejects a 09:16 UA93 hijack notification. ## Four files, one catalogue title

The AAL77 (**7596160**), UAL93 (**7597164**) and UAL175 (**7598471**) copies have different whole-PDF hashes. All three have identical corresponding page geometry, grayscale raster samples and sorted extracted text on each of their three pages in PyMuPDF 1.27.2.2 at 115 dpi. The AAL11 copy (**7597932**) is listed as redacted. Its three pages differ in the same raster and text checks from the other group. Visual checks of the selected areas on pp. 1–2 in 7596160 and 7597932 show the same two 0924 entries. The four accessions supply dependent copies of the account. The comparison does not classify every differing pixel or assign a revision order.
