# A September 11 telephone account in FBI report 6390

November 14, 2026 UTC · TEL01–TEL04 / AUD016-A1 / TC05–TC06. An acquired Commission-release mirror contains a secondhand FBI report of an **evening September 11 telephone conversation** about a Flight 93 passenger's call. It should be compared with, not merged into, the September 12 Glick family interview previously examined. The report carries names rendered **“ELIZABETH BLICK”** and **“JERIMAH BLICK”**. Its family, place and flight details resemble the later Glick account, but the name discrepancy and redacted source fields mean the identity and each relay remain to be established. Neither record supplies the original aircraft call or carrier switching data.

## Discrepant fields and alternate explanations

* Report **6390 p. 9**, numbered item 1, attributes a statement that the Newark–San Francisco flight **“detoured in Cincinnati, Ohio.”** Report **11722 p. 71** describes Flight 93 as **“en route, non-stop, to San Francisco.”** The reports are different records with different source chains and wording. A transcription, recollection, attribution, itinerary or other error is possible; no explanation has been tested. The wording alone does not establish an actual diversion or demonstrate fabrication.
* The opening of **6390 p. 9** describes the stated purpose of the agent interview as information about **American Airlines Flight 77 at the Pentagon**. The subsequent account concerns **United Flight 93 at Shanksville**. Preserve both passages. The reason for the juxtaposition and whether the interview covered additional material remain unknown.
* On **6390 p. 9**, the outside production label reads **265A-NY-280350-302-6390** while the rendered internal file line reads **265D-NY-180350-302**. The **11722** production label reads **265A-NY-280350-302-11722** and its form's internal file line reads **265D-NY-280350**. These are different fields and reports; no numbering correction or common-origin conclusion is assumed.

The source scan itself contains spelling and OCR-like irregularities, but no original notes, audio or production history were acquired to determine when any irregularity entered the record. A third-party book describes 6390 as an interview with the wife; the report's own visible opening identifies an agent as the interview source and describes a telephone conversation, making the underlying relay important. The agent identity and redacted portions have not been reconstructed. [Book, printed p. 227](https://dimnaut.info/truther/resource/books/Elias%20Davidsson%20-%20Hijacking%20America%27s%20Mind%20%282013%29.pdf).
