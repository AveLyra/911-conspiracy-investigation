# DOJ records prepared for the 9/11 Commission and the Watch Log search

November 6, 2026 UTC · **TEL03–TEL05 / AUD016-A1 / TC05–TC06**. DOJ had a separate permanent records series for copies of source material loaned, shown or briefed to the 9/11 Commission. Its successor also covers certain records documenting how senior leadership collected and prepared material for an investigative body. These are specific places to look for the copy, inventory or production paperwork behind the Commission staff citation to the September 11, 2001 DOJ Watch Officer Log. The original Watch Log and its inclusion in either collection remain unverified. ## Keep the custody routes separate

The preceding study located the **15-year destruction instruction** for the DOJ Command Center's paper Watch Log series. The separate DOJ production schedules both describe permanent records and give different transfer instructions with different starting points. Preserve all three descriptions. They concern the paper operational files, the earlier Commission-specific collection and its successor, respectively. The schedule change and the stated initial date span supply no accession date, file inventory or record of what happened to this log. A 2001 source record could be copied or described during 2002–2004. Whether this happened remains open. [Prior paper-log custody study](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/0deff1f3f7ad30466ba0b0375399c061cc253c19/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-doj-watchlog-record-series-2026-11-06/README.md)

NARA says it took legal custody of the Commission's own records when the Commission closed in August 2004. A copy in those files, a DOJ Executive Secretariat production copy, a record made available for review, and the operational original can have different custody and access histories. Staff note 268 describes a **9:33** entry about a contact with the Command Center. Its citation establishes a specific document title and date. It leaves open how staff saw it and which copy, if any, they kept. The earlier collect/direct witness accounts and carrier timing and count differences remain unresolved. ## Open TODOs and tests

1. **DOJ production side.** Locate the segregated collection's transfer or accession identifier and folder inventory. Seek the request, search or compilation documentation, cover letter, enclosure list and any record of on-site review. Use both schedule numbers and the exact September 11 log title. Check which organization created and held each copy and when. Record any actual hold, migration, transfer or disposition separately.
2. **Commission recipient side.** Obtain the Box 20 DOJ request and any matching Box 21 working papers or Box 13 production materials. Check requested categories, dates, document controls and whether access was by briefing, on-site review, loan or delivered copy. Use the actual record identifiers and pages to test the join.
3. **Log and call test.** Obtain the complete daily log or authenticated copy, including surrounding entries, amendments, time fields and any dispatch or message references. Compare its wording and clock basis with the staff note and the office, carrier and operator records if available. The September 20 usage report's source inputs and the zero-duration entry's field meaning remain separate TODOs.
