# Cheyenne Mountain WTC beam transfer and surface treatment

Study date 2026-11-06 UTC. This steel-custody follow-up traces the 750-pound beam described by the University of Colorado Colorado Springs (UCCS) and the Air Force. It extends the recipient-side provenance search to a new public institution. Dates below retain each source's wording and event label.

## Surface treatment and the upstream custody lead

The Air Force story describes sandblasting to remove rust, application of sealant, and electrostatic powder coating with oven curing to recreate the beam's earlier appearance. This is a report of completed preparation for display. If confirmed by the treatment file, the process limits what can be inferred about original coatings, corrosion and residues from the displayed surface. UCCS had anticipated preservation measurements and tests. Their completion, scope and results remain open. [Air Force report](https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/112414/cheyenne-mountain-receives-piece-of-world-trade-center/), paragraph beginning “Local artist”. [UCCS design announcement](https://communique.uccs.edu/?p=1804), lines 614 and 617.

The 2011 Air Force story says the foundation had **one more** artifact available when Cheyenne Mountain inquired, and its caption calls the beam **“one of eight artifacts.”** A 2016 21st Space Wing story attributes a selection of **five** pieces at JFK Hangar 17 to the foundation's president and says the piece originally intended for **Buckley AFB** went to Cheyenne Mountain. The two counts describe differently worded groups. Keep the available-piece and reassignment accounts side by side. The sources supply candidate records to search and leave the relationship between the groups and the piece-level identity unresolved. [Air Force report](https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/112414/cheyenne-mountain-receives-piece-of-world-trade-center/), photo caption and paragraph beginning “After calling”. [21st Space Wing report](https://www.petersonschriever.spaceforce.mil/Newsroom/News/Display/Article/936357/sculptures-in-colorado-springs-memorialize-911/), paragraphs beginning “The following January”, “They took me” and “The piece originally intended”.

The university articles distinguish a reported ownership event from subsequent campus custody and the later transport report. They support a bounded custody search. They leave the original building, structural member and recovery location unverified beyond the publishers' general tower attribution. The six UCCS article bodies were retrieved and checked as HTML text. The Air Force and Space Wing accounts were read through indexed article text because direct HTTP requests returned 403. The image files, original agreements, base property books and condition records were outside the material reviewed. [Source pins and access details](SOURCE-PINS.json)

- [ ] **Movement.** Locate the November 19 load sheet, carrier or motorcade transfer record, and Cheyenne Mountain receiving entry. Check the item ID, weight, marked dimensions, pickup and delivery times against the university and Air Force descriptions.

Look for pre-treatment photographs or samples and for actual surface-preparation, coating and cure parameters. Any comparison with an untreated object would require a documented identity and treatment history.
