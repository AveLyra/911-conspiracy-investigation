# What the 13:16 NEADS log entry identifies

November 8, 2026 Eastern · **B129 / FAA01 / PD01–PD04**. Two newly retrieved Commission archive members reproduce the handwritten **1316** entry with registration **N612UA**. The National Archives' FAA glossary maps that registration to **United 175**, and lists **N591UA** for **United 93**. One Commission copy has a margin annotation that explicitly makes the same distinction. This supplies a specific source candidate for the tail-number discussion located in the Marr interview. Which log copy Marr consulted and when the annotation was made remain open. ## Compare with the claims about United 93

The June 2, 2003 reply asserts that records showed the United 93 hijack was reported at **0916L**. The January 2004 recording's provisional machine passages describe a possible assumption about a tail number at **916** and an interjection identifying it with **175**. The **1316** ledger entry is consistent with the tail-number alternative. It gives no Flight 93 identifier. The staff monograph reads the entry at **9:16** and cites NEADS Identification Technician **Channel 5 at 9:16:19** for a corresponding call. The acquisition did not establish the exact log Marr used or acquire or align that underlying channel segment. Keep the June claim, the qualified later account and the actual row distinct.
