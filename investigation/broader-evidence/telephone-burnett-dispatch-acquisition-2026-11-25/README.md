## Interpretation and limits

The webpage's **9:28 a.m. (EST)** and the scan's **6:31**, with no scan time zone stated, are preserved as separate descriptions. They cannot establish a timing discrepancy on synchronized clocks without independent clock and event records; no numerical correction is applied here. The webpage and scan are presented together by the same publisher and must not be counted as independent accounts. A download and hash establish the bytes received, not their original source, completeness, authenticity, date, contents or chain of custody. We did not listen to the audio, transcribe it, identify speakers, or compare its audible contents with the published narrative. No assertion of manipulation or motive follows.
