## Observations

The [FBI Vault September interviews part 02](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11%20Commission%20Report/9-11-interviews-2001-09-sep-02-of-08/at_download/file), physical PDF page 58, visibly bears transcription date September 11, 2001. Its second-call passage states that the interviewee heard a woman screaming in the background. It places a call to police after the first reported call and before the second.

Physical PDF page 65 is a continuation page labeled June 20, 2002. Its second-call passage states that the interviewee could not hear any background noise. It describes an attempted call to the airline that received a busy signal, a call to police, then another call from his son. Preserve these descriptions separately: the image check supports the previous reading of the words and contexts, not an explanation of the differing descriptions. The pages are accounts attributed to the same recipient, not independent witnesses. The 2001 bomb description belongs to the second-call narrative, whereas the 2002 no-mention description is in the first-call narrative; treating these as mutually exclusive reports of the same call would overstate the discrepancy.
