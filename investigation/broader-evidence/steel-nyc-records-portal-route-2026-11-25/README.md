# New York City 9/11 records portal: preservation-record route

**AUD011 / AUD016 / B188 / H005.** A newly identified city document portal is a potentially useful route for original municipal operational records. Its existence does not establish that the specific steel-preservation decisions, yard logs or WTC 7 specimen-attribution records are present.

## Sources and actual scope

The [New York City Mayor's Office announcement, November 22, 2026](https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2026/11/mamdani-administration-releases-more-9-11-records--as-portal-sur), describes a third release of 84,000 additional records and says the portal contains more than 440,000 pages. An [October 21 announcement](https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2026/10/mamdani-administration-releases-40-000-more-9-11-records-to-publ) gives earlier release and page counts. These are city-reported changing snapshots, not an independent count or evidence of any particular record. The [city portal](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/) was reached by a public link from [911 Health Watch](https://www.911healthwatch.org/effort-to-get-new-york-city-to-release-9-11-documents/); this text-only check returned no readable portal content. The `nyc.gov/sept11docs` redirect separately returned an HTTP 403 to a command-line request. Neither response shows that the collection is inaccessible to all users, that any record is absent, or that material was concealed.

An [independent searchable collection browser](https://911records.org/browse) displayed a WTC 7 collection and design-and-construction agency categories. An older indexed [7DDC folder listing](https://911records.org/browse/Design%20and%20Construction,%20Dept.%20of/NYC-WTC0007/7DDC/~) shows nine document entries, including 2003 building-related records and an environmental-report route. The displayed snapshot identifies one entry as removed by the City; this review did not retrieve or inspect it. The overview and older folder views display different totals and version labels; their dates, scope and causes were not independently established. Do not treat the listings, generated descriptions or counts as underlying evidence of steel origin, transfer, deliberate removal or any historical event. We inspected public page text and links only; no municipal record image, attachment, raw document or personal information was downloaded or published in this step.

## Next discriminating test

Search the official portal or its permitted public catalogue for 2001–2002 Department of Design and Construction recovery contracts, yard directives, suspension/access requests, selection or shipment inventories and specific WTC 7 candidate labels. Retrieve and inspect any directly relevant primary record, record the city identifier, source URL, acquisition date and hash, compare it with the earlier [steel-preservation review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/c954753fc58f6157b253e517fd30015d6a757e0a/investigation/broader-evidence/steel-preservation-decisions-2026-10-27/README.md), and retain conflicting accounts without selecting a preferred explanation. Keep W14A, W14B and other specimens separate unless documentary identifiers establish a join. The portal's health and environmental focus does not guarantee the needed records are included. Original decisions, transfer records and NIST attribution rationale remain TODOs.
