## A specific project-record lead

NIST's published report lists the **1984 WTC 7 Project Specifications** at physical p. **95**, lines **36–37**, and again at pp. **157 and 160**. The first extracted identifier is **WTCI-187-P**. The extracted text on the latter two faces gives **WTC1-187-P**, while the glyph appears as **I** in the inspected page images. Keep both spellings in retrieval. This is a text-layer comparison and an archive locator. The underlying specification was not recovered in this pass. [NIST NCSTAR 1-1A, p. 95](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-C13-72f0231e04aac8e35991907a17f599e5/pdf/GOVPUB-C13-72f0231e04aac8e35991907a17f599e5.pdf#page=95) · [p. 157](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-C13-72f0231e04aac8e35991907a17f599e5/pdf/GOVPUB-C13-72f0231e04aac8e35991907a17f599e5.pdf#page=157)

The same report says specification **§5A.5** called for contractor mill reports giving chemical and physical properties. It says **§5A.2.2** referenced a separate testing and inspection contract, which NIST reports it did not find, and identifies **§5A.12.13** as a shop and field weld inspection provision. This identifies specific file series to pursue. Actual mill certificates, inspection results and a piece-mark connection to W14A or W14B remain open. [NIST physical pp. 156–157, lines 32–35 and 4–7](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-C13-72f0231e04aac8e35991907a17f599e5/pdf/GOVPUB-C13-72f0231e04aac8e35991907a17f599e5.pdf#page=156)
