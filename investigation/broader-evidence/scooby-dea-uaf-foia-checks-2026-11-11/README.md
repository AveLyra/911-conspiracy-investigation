# Scooby checks: DEA incidents, UAF model lineage and a withheld-record appeal

November 11, 2026. These selected source checks address reports 9, 21 and 24. They do not complete verification of every claim in those reports or the 30-report corpus. The FBI request documents discussed below remain in the private case repository and are not republished here.

## DEA compilation and Orlando lead — report 21

The publicly circulated [DEA compilation scan](https://www.antiwar.com/rep/DEA_Report_redactedxx.pdf) was downloaded and opened: it has 60 PDF pages. PDF pp. 1, 2, 21, 31, 50, 51 and 60 were rendered; pp. 1 and 50 were visually read. It is a third-party-hosted copy of a government document; this check did not authenticate its complete transmission history or prove it is byte-identical to every cited version. The first page has no visible author signature or date; later attribution to June 2001 needs its own support.

Page 1 reports that numbers obtained from an art seller encountered at DEA Orlando were linked to ongoing MDMA investigations in four states and that Orlando opened an investigation coordinated with DEA headquarters. It separately expresses suspicion that the wider incidents may have involved organized intelligence gathering. The first is a reported investigative lead; the second is an assessment, not proof. The compilation does not establish what “linked” meant, the underlying telephone records, the investigation outcome, or a match to UMS, a hijacker, or the Saudi support network. The original trace, inputs and disposition would permit a direct test.

Page 50 reports two visitors photographed on a runway at Volk Field, recovery of exposed film, a developed photo of a person in a vehicle on the runway, no aircraft photographs, misdemeanor trespass charges and fines. These are the compilation's account, not this review's independent verification of the original local police case or disposition. The FAA/base/police records and photographs would test it. A reported security breach is not by itself evidence of intelligence affiliation, attack knowledge or a connection to UMS.

## UAF final-run lineage — report 24

The [UAF project page](https://files.wtc7report.org/) identifies its March 2020 final report, describes the study's conclusions, and says its input/output/simulation archive is 256 GB compressed and approximately 632 GB extracted. The archive was not downloaded, inspected, rerun or matched to particular results in this check. Its advertised existence is not proof that each figure has a complete reproducible input-to-output chain, and the lack of a completed match is not proof that such files are absent or that a model cannot be reproduced.

The [UAF report](https://files.wtc7report.org/file/public-download/A-Structural-Reevaluation-of-the-Collapse-of-World-Trade-Center-7-March2020.pdf), printed pp. 24 and 80, says the team used ABAQUS and SAP2000, and describes a separate Floor 13 analysis in which the expansion coefficient of four beams was increased approximately 40% to 11.5 × 10⁻⁶ /°F. It attributes that change to the modeled test's purpose. Simple arithmetic shows 11.5/8.2 ≈ 1.4024, but the claimed 8.2 value in a particular Model 16 input was **not independently located or inspected** here. It is therefore a conditional comparison, not an established discrepancy in an executed run. Obtain the exact model, included material definitions, logs, outputs and figure mapping before resolving which coefficient actually ran. A simulated response under chosen inputs does not by itself establish a physical cause or identify a participant.

## FBI January 2000 request and appeal — report 9

The research chat could not retrieve the request, FBI decision, appeal and tracker, and correctly declined to reconstruct them. A subsequent inspection of the private case repository located the actual October 27 request, November 5 FBI decision, submitted appeal, portal export and November 6 acknowledgment. The request seeks FBI-held January 2000 Mihdhar visa/travel messages and related receipt, routing, review and preservation records; it distinguishes contemporaneous receipt from later oversight copies.

The FBI's November 5 letter says it found responsive records in an investigative file, withheld them under Exemption 7(A), and administratively closed the request. It invokes a pending or prospective enforcement proceeding without naming it in the letter. The preserved portal export records appeal A-2027-00431 as submitted and received November 5, and a subsequent acknowledgment confirms receipt. The locally maintained status snapshot says the decision was pending at that check; **this is historical saved status, not a live portal check or proof that an appeal remains pending now**. The appeal's arguments and military-commission references are advocacy or contextual hypotheses, not the FBI's identified basis. The precise scope of the FBI's search, the claimed enforcement matter, segregation analysis and current appeal decision remain to be established from the agency's records.

## Method and limitations

Rendered the DEA scan using PyMuPDF, used OCR to locate passages and visually inspected the first and fiftieth pages. Read the public UAF project page and final report passages; checked the coefficient ratio with a calculator. Read the privately preserved FBI request, determination, appeal export and tracker without republishing personal correspondence. The UAF model archive, actual DEA telephone trace, local disposition records, FBI case file, and live OIP status were not inspected. Further claim-by-claim verification remains open across the reports.
