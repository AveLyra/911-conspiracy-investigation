## UAF lineage candidates (report 24)

The [UAF project page](https://ine.uaf.edu/wtc7) describes its final report, simulations and 256 GB compressed data archive. [UAF's March 2020 report](https://www.novarepublika.cz/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/A-Structural-Reevaluation-of-the-Collapse-of-World-Trade-Center-7-March-2020.pdf) is available through a third-party copy; the university's official page links the report. Printed pp. 75, 80, 84 and 86 describe assumptions, model comparisons and Figure 3.14. The UAF archive and native solver outputs were not obtained or rerun here. Earlier [PDF-layer analysis](../../wtc7/c79-and-timing-crosschecks-2026-10-21/README.md) and [G3005 audit](../../wtc7/uaf-g3005-native-section-2026-10-21/README.md) describe inspection of the two preserved report versions; this pass did not re-render the PDF layers from the original bytes.
