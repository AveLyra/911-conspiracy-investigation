The [NARA finding aid](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/9-11/finding-aid.pdf#page=20) independently lists an **FBI Logs** folder in Dana Hyde Box 5. Its folder listing alone does not authenticate every page of the mirror.

- **Physical p. 1, PDFKit text lines 4, 10–15:** a letter dated **May 6, 2004** describes documents responsive to Commission **Document Request 41-1** and says the FBI's rolling production could be supplemented. This is a source-reported transmittal, not an itemized receipt or verification of an enclosure's contents.
- **Physical pp. 2–3, text lines 3 and 7–20:** two withdrawal notices describe separate document-number fields, **18** and **19**, each naming **Washington Field Office FBI SIOC logs**, document date **September 11, 2001**, document type **Chronologies**, and withdrawal date **September 17, 2008**. Each notice says the item was removed because access was restricted. The notices describe the release treatment at that review; they do not establish current restriction, the withheld content, or why a particular restriction applied. Two notices need not mean independent underlying events.

These are records placed in the same released folder. The available pages do **not** establish that either notice concerns an enclosure to the cover letter, that Request 41-1 sought these precise documents, or that either FBI log equals the separate DOJ Command Center Watch Officer Log identified in [Commission staff note 268](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/9-11/staff-report-sept2005.pdf#page=95). Do not conflate FBI SIOC with DOJ's watch center or infer concealment, wrongdoing or duplication.
