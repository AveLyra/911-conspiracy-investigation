## Unresolved comparisons

- **Item number.** H-055a and H-005a differ by one digit. Both strings are present in the issued article, so the discrepancy cannot be attributed solely to the archive's text extraction. A printing error and a difference between identifiers in the underlying paperwork remain untested possibilities. The page gives no independent Port Authority record for choosing either string.
- **Dimensions.** The Georgia Guard's two dimensions, the attributed local three-dimensional description and the New York caption's approximate length remain separate. Six and a half feet corresponds to about **78 inches**, compared with the locally reported **52-inch** first dimension. The records do not define common measurement axes, crate versus steel dimensions or a measurement method. **Half-ton** is an approximate description and **923 pounds** is the reported weight.
- **Origin and route.** The Georgia account names Hangar 17 at JFK, the New York photo caption names the Brooklyn warehouse, and the New York magazine identifies a Long Island shipping terminal. These could refer to separate points on the route. The July 25 photo label, July 26 activity/upload descriptions, and August 2 receiving report are retained under their source labels. The sources leave the order and exact handoff times unverified.

The state publications provide contemporary evidence of reported outbound and inbound movement, including a named recipient and terminal route. They supply leads to the records that could tie the movement to one catalog entry. The reviewed pages do not establish original building, member mark, NIST specimen identity, present custody, or a complete shipment chain.

## Deferred records and tests

- [ ] **STL02 catalog and approval.** Locate both exact numbers in the dated catalog editions and photo index, along with the reported April approval, county application and any executed transfer instrument. Compare full item descriptions, dimensions, provenance notes and revision dates. Keep the broader inventory edition TODOs open.
- [ ] **STL02 transfer and custody.** Locate warehouse pick lists, Guard dispatch records, the carrier bill of lading and the Oconee receiving/acceptance or placement record. Compare a stable item/shipment ID, dates and locations across each step. A measured or marked photo and weight ticket would help distinguish the competing measurements.
- [ ] **STL03 building attribution.** Require an intake record, member mark, field photograph or engineering crosswalk before assigning a building or NIST specimen. This report supplies no such match.
