# OMB receipt leads for DOT’s FY 2006 accountability report

Public derivative of the November 9, 2026 Eastern source review · B129 / PD03–PD05. The prior study recovered the public DOT report and its component PDFs. This review traces a different copy, the report and transmittal records that the 2006 OMB instructions directed to the Office of Federal Financial Management (**OFFM**) and appropriate Resource Management Office (**RMO**). Five OFFM catalog descriptions span 2006 and supply transfer numbers. The series specifically titled for accountability reports ends in 2004 and describes a different subject scope. The actual November 2006 report submission and receipt remain unacquired. [Public report recovery](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/a411674551abcca8df26584f0f1776892ed8e5cb/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-par2006-sender-package-and-omb-routing-2026-11-09/README.md)

## OFFM series and limits

The July 2006 circular identifies separate OFFM and RMO draft and final routes. OMB’s contemporaneous organization page describes the RMOs as program and agency-facing offices and OFFM as a financial-management policy office. Use the creator field, series scope and transfer identifiers together. A date span indicates possible coverage. The catalog leaves actual custody of the specific report open. An earlier OFFM **Agency Chief Financial Officer Submission Files** series covers 1993–2000, a useful title lead with a different date boundary. A separate OMB **Program Records** series covers 2001–2006 and lists a container boundary from Transportation to Veterans Affairs. Its creator is the **Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs**, a different route from the OFFM/RMO instructions. The container boundary is descriptive and supplies no item-level evidence of a FY 2006 DOT PAR. Two bare `Department of Transportation` file-unit titles in the snapshot are respectively under hiring-freeze and workplace-safety records. Their parent fields illustrate why title and agency alone are insufficient. ## RMO schedule boundary

- **DAA-0051-2015-0001.** The RMO Review and Oversight item **0001** lists agency review records and cites predecessor **NC1-51-79-3, item 3**. The permanent RMO Publications item **0002** and Budget Submissions and Strategic Plan item **0005** each show `2009` as the first accumulation year and `From 2009 To 2017` as the planned initial transfer span. Those fields leave the 2006 submission outside the stated accumulation range for those two series. [Approved schedule, physical pp. 4–7](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/executive-office-of-the-president/rg-0051/daa-0051-2015-0001_sf115.pdf#page=4)
- **NC1-051-79-03.** The older Transportation, Commerce, and Housing Division schedule lists separate permanent Transportation Branch **Program Records**, item 11, and **Budgetary Records**, item 12. Item 11 uses a two-year cutoff and item 12 a fiscal-year cutoff. Both list EOP Depository and FRC transfer language at two years old and offer to NARS at eight years old. Keep those instructions and the distinct cutoffs as printed. The NARA notice dated August 9, 2022 lists supersessions for **items 3 and 9**. The public guide’s named Transportation Branch program holdings stop at `1969-76` in its historical entry. The legacy office lineage, applicable disposition authority and the specific 2006 transfer remain unverified. [Older schedule, physical pp. 1 and 6](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/executive-office-of-the-president/rg-0051/nc1-051-79-03_sf115.pdf#page=6) · [Historical guide, §51.9.13](https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/groups/051.html)
