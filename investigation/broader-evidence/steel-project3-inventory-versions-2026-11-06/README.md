# NIST Project 3 steel inventory across the 2003 briefings and final report

November 6, 2026 · Steel custody and attribution · STL02–STL03 / AUD011 / AUD016 / B188 / H005

NIST's August 2003 inventory slide and final 2005 report both count **236 items**, including **seven WTC 5 coupons**. The December 2003 meeting minutes describe **236 pieces from the two towers**. The slides attached to those same minutes give a count of **236 items** without a building breakdown. The descriptions are preserved separately. The item-level version history is still needed to trace what changed. The two tables sum to 236. Three class counts changed. That pattern is compatible with reclassification, changes in grouping, offsetting roster changes or a reporting error. Those explanations are untested. The aggregate differences cannot identify a specimen that changed classes, arrived or left. The two totals alone cannot establish identical custody.

## WTC 7 scope and contrary evidence

NISTIR 7015's FY2003 research summary describes metallurgical and mechanical analyses of steel from the **Twin Towers and WTC 7**. The same page's collection subsection describes **more than 200** pieces from the towers and supplies no WTC 7 specimen identifier. The final NCSTAR 1-3 §7.7 states that no inventory steel could be assigned to WTC 7 unambiguously and that properties were estimated from literature. Preserve both the earlier scope wording and later identification statement. The FY2003 wording cannot independently establish a WTC 7 specimen or test. The separately documented FEMA field attributions and NIST's unidentified entries for W14A/B remain unresolved. The GZ series provides a documented comparison for explicit building attribution within the same collection.
