# The FAA answer read into the May hearing and Garvey's later supplement

November 6, 2026 UTC · **B129 / PD01 / PD07**. The Commission's May 23, 2003 transcript records Richard Ben-Veniste saying that staff had received an FAA statement the prior evening and then reading it into the record. The quoted text and the 2006 OIG Appendix 5 copy share the **9:24 a.m.** formal-notice claim and the account of the Air Force liaison and phone bridges. A token comparison finds several wording and punctuation differences. A separate January 2004 written supplement is available and addresses the allocation of security duties and preattack threat assessment. Its posted body contains no replacement for the FAA notification answer. [May 23 transcript](https://govinfo.library.unt.edu/911/archive/hearing2/9-11Commission_Hearing_2003-05-23.htm) · [OIG Appendix 5](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=30) · [January supplement](https://govinfo.library.unt.edu/911/hearings/hearing7/witness_garvey.htm)

## Later materials kept separate

- **October 21, 2003 staff interview.** The publicly reproduced staff memorandum attributes to Garvey a lack of recollection of seeing several versions of the *Executive Summary* on **September 11**, and possibly **September 12**, after interviewers presented them. It says the first two versions cited a report of a gun on Flight 11. The source describes a separate set of documents and recollections. Those versions, who received them and when remain open. The Indiana University guide separately lists an October 21 Garvey interview prepared by the same staff member. The public reader's exact identity with the IU copy and the identity of the meeting cited in the January supplement remain unverified. [Public reader, document text lines 4 and 358–372](https://www.scribd.com/document/15877567/FO-B3-Public-Hearing-1-26-04-2-of-3-Fdr-Tab-9-18-MFR-10-21-03-Jane-Garvey-Interview-689) · [IU finding aid](https://archives.iu.edu/html/VAB8595.html)
- **January 27, 2004 written and oral testimony.** The posted written body describes a subsequent staff meeting and names security duties and threat assessment as its two topics. The oral transcript reports submission of the supplement, and the chair says the air-traffic-control and Defense situational-awareness topic will be handled at another hearing. The January oral transcript says **424 airports** and **approximately 250 foreign airports**. Keep the oral **424** beside the May prepared **429**, and both separate from the written supplement's content. The underlying airport list, scope date and audio are needed to test that numeric difference. The January transcript's separate **9:24** reference concerns a United dispatch message to **Flight 93**. The test found zero matches for **NORAD**, **9:24**, or **phone bridge** within the posted January written body's lines 21–36. It did not test other filings or the complete briefing binder.
