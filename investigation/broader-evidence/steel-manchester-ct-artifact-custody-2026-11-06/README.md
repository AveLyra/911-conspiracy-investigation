# Manchester, Connecticut, World Trade Center steel pickup trail

November 6, 2026 UTC · STL02–STL03 / B188 / H005

A Manchester Fire-Rescue-EMS memo dated **February 18, 2011** documents the proposed transfer of one World Trade Center steel artifact, with dimensions, weight and three possible dates for pickup at JFK. The **March 1, 2011** revised agenda places the proposed acceptance at **item 12.C** on the consent calendar. The current town memorial page describes a steel column on display and attributes it to the North Tower. Together these records narrow the search for the actual transfer and original artifact identifier.
