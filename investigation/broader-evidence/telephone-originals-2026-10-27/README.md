# Flight 77 telephone evidence: original-presentation and released-scan checks

2026-10-27. Continuation of AUD016-A1 and TC05/B105–TC06/B110. This supplement obtains underlying public records that the first review had not inspected. It preserves discrepancies without selecting a preferred value or explaining their cause. These are overlapping verification leads, not independent findings of wrongdoing.

## Findings

**TEL01 — The disputed zero-duration entry exists in the court's own distributed presentation.** The court's [P200054 index](https://coop.vaed.uscourts.gov/moussaoui/exhibits/prosecution/flights/P200054.html) links a ZIP containing the four-flight Flash presentations. In its `AA77.swf`, the text definitions associated with Barbara Olson contain `09:18:58`, `0 SECONDS ` and `DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE` / `UNCONNECTED CALL` (IDs 1691–1694; zero-based SWF offsets 3183776, 3183822, 3183887 and 3183950). The number in the presentation is already masked. We statically decoded these definitions; we did not run Flash or certify which screen was displayed at trial. This directly verifies the stored label, not its underlying carrier classification or a claim that no other calls reached the office. [Original ZIP](https://coop.vaed.uscourts.gov/moussaoui/flights.zip) · [Exact decoded definitions](SWF-SOURCE-PINS.json).

**TEL02 — A five-second duration discrepancy is preserved.** For the unknown connected entry starting **09:25:48**, the [May 20, 2004 Commission briefing scan, page 2](https://www.scribd.com/document/18886083/T7-B12-Flight-93-Calls-General-Fdr-52004-DOJ-Briefing-on-Cell-and-Phone-Calls-From-AA-77-408) says **159 seconds**. The court file stores **159 SECONDS**, ID 1998 at offset 5075752, paired in the sequence with time ID 1997 at 5075706. The [Commission's final report note 57, printed page 455 / PDF page 7](https://www.9-11commission.gov/report/911Report_Notes.pdf#page=7) says **2 minutes, 34 seconds**, or **154 seconds**. Both scan images and the final note were viewed. The other three listed starts and durations agree across these descriptions: 09:15:34 / 102, 09:20:15 / 274 and 09:30:56 / 260 seconds. The totals therefore differ, 795 versus 790 seconds; that arithmetic is not an independent observation. No carrier record has been used to decide which duration is accurate, whether they use identical definitions, or why they differ. [Comparison rows and arithmetic](CALL-COMPARISON.json).

**TEL03 — The May briefing's count and its displayed table differ.** Page 1 describes **eight calls** and identification of the called number for three. Its page 2 table displays **seven rows**, five with positive durations and two labeled no connection. The court SWF also contains **09:14:57**, **ON BUTTON / PRESSED** and **UNCONNECTED CALL** definitions (IDs 2001–2003; offsets 5075936, 5075982, 5076055), plus summary labels for Renee May: two calls; Barbara Olson: one call; unknown caller: five calls (IDs 2129–2130, 2136–2137, 2140–2141). These supply a concrete record to compare, but do not establish that an omitted button event explains the briefing's count. A button action, call attempt, operator leg and connected conversation may have different counting rules; those rules remain unverified. The briefing's own statement of complete identification concerns **completed calls**, not necessarily every attempted leg. [Briefing](https://www.scribd.com/document/18886083/T7-B12-Flight-93-Calls-General-Fdr-52004-DOJ-Briefing-on-Cell-and-Phone-Calls-From-AA-77-408) · [Court text locators](SWF-SOURCE-PINS.json).

**TEL04 — The actual Keyton form supports the earlier witness-description claim, with explicit limits.** A public mirror of [Team 7, Box 13, Flight Call Notes and 302s](https://www.les-crises.fr/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/Team7-Box13-Flight-Call-Notes-and-302s.pdf#page=48) contains Lori Lynn Keyton's FD-302 at physical page 48, production stamp **REQ. #35-13 000000736**. It records six to eight automated collect attempts, then an operator-assisted call identifying Barbara Olsen, and a second call a few to five minutes later that Keyton described as direct rather than collect. It records no caller ID and her uncertainty whether the caller used an onboard phone or cell phone. The form's investigation field is **9/11/01**; its transcription field is **09/14/2001**. The original agents' notes, audio and office switch logs were not obtained. This is a mirrored released scan, not authenticated native interview audio or proof of which electronic row belongs to the speaker. The public FBI bundle remains unacquired after the earlier 403; this pass did not repeat that unchanged endpoint.

**TEL05 — Collect/direct accounts conflict; neither is silently preferred.** Helen Voss's form at [physical page 51](https://www.les-crises.fr/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/Team7-Box13-Flight-Call-Notes-and-302s.pdf#page=51), stamp **000000739**, says she believed both calls were collect. Keyton's form says the second was direct and not collect. Voss also says Keyton relayed the caller's arrival and that the security officer arrived after the second call. Allen Ferber's [physical page 47](https://www.les-crises.fr/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/Team7-Box13-Flight-Call-Notes-and-302s.pdf#page=47), stamp **000000735**, describes arriving after two calls; he did not answer the initial request or witness the calls themselves. These accounts concern a shared office and information relayed there; they are not independent carrier confirmations. No motive is established by their difference.

**TEL06 — Caller attribution is explicitly inferential in the source, not a number-based match.** The [May briefing, page 2](https://www.scribd.com/document/18886083/T7-B12-Flight-93-Calls-General-Fdr-52004-DOJ-Briefing-on-Cell-and-Phone-Calls-From-AA-77-408) acknowledges no direct evidence for the unknown calls and describes a conclusion drawn from recipient and family interviews, including Keyton. Page 1 attributes the identified 09:12:18 call to credit-card information and other identities to interviews. The [final note 57](https://www.9-11commission.gov/report/911Report_Notes.pdf#page=7) retains uncertain matching between four connected entries and two reported conversations, alongside FBI/DOJ belief that all four went to the office. The [Ted Olson forms, physical pages 49–50](https://www.les-crises.fr/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/Team7-Box13-Flight-Call-Notes-and-302s.pdf#page=49), stamps **000000737–738**, describe two conversations, no watch check for the approximate initial time, and uncertainty about the calling device. The missing operator and office records must test this join. The unknown entries cannot be counted as four independently identified Olson conversations; the unconnected named entry cannot by itself establish that no other conversation occurred.
