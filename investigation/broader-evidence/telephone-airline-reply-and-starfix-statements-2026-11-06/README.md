## Airline reply, records holder and date

- **Team 7 Box 18, item 556, physical p. 1, lines 9–15 and 21–26.** The header prints **Tuesday, January 20, 2004, 4:26 PM**, without a zone. The reply describes a partial response. The item heading refers to **GTE Air phone Records**. Its body identifies **AT&T Claircom** as the service provider for the onboard phones on Flights **11 and 77**. It says American did not hold itemized records for those calls and received only monthly system-wide reliability reports that did not list individual calls by flight. It reports that Claircom ceased business in **2002**. These are airline representations about custody and reporting. Preserve both provider labels and the scope of what the airline says it received.
