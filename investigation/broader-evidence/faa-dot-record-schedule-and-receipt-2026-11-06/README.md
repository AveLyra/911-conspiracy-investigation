# DOT record-series crosswalk and the May 2003 FAA receipt

November 6, 2026 UTC · **B129 / PD01–PD05 / PD07**. The Commission host and the University of North Texas government-information archive returned byte-identical copies of the **May 23, 2003 Commission hearing PDF**. Physical page 78 records the reported receipt of the FAA's original statement the previous evening. A separate archival check found conflicting schedule item identifiers for one DOT administrative file series and a separate crosswalk between an older correspondence series and its superseding authority. These records help distinguish the original answer, its possible filing locations and later FAA action. The corrected answer and its delivery, the signed administrative decision, and recommendation implementation remain open.

## The original FAA answer

- **Receipt evidence.** At physical p. 78, extraction lines 15–22, Ben-Veniste says staff had been provided an FAA statement after communicating with Garvey the previous evening. Lines 23–39 contain the read-in, including the account of the Air Force liaison joining immediately, earlier bridge information, and formal notice about Flight 77 at **9:24 a.m.** A whitespace-only comparison of the 151-token quoted passage matches the held Commission HTML. Both web hosts served 119 pages and the same SHA-256. The PDF supplies a new exact page locator for the previously documented receipt account. The two transcript representations describe the same hearing. [Commission PDF, p. 78](https://www.911commission.gov/archive/hearing2/9-11Commission_Hearing_2003-05-23.pdf#page=78) · [University of North Texas PDF](https://govinfo.library.unt.edu/911/archive/hearing2/9-11Commission_Hearing_2003-05-23.pdf#page=78) · [Prior HTML and Appendix 5 comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/bcebd6b8d37a6b841c7615d3bdfe09b9b412d700/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-garvey-witness-supplement-2026-11-06/README.md)
- **Conflicting accounts remain separate.** The read-in attributes authorship to two officials as information relayed to the speaker. OIG p. 9 describes preparation involving a retired official and two current officials. Appendix 5 p. 30 has the same core claim and different wording, including the read-in's **“actions they were taken”** alongside Appendix 5's **“actions they were taking”**. OIG pp. 2 and 9–10 reports the inaccurate liaison timing, the uncorrected answer and its separate findings concerning intent. NORAD's separate answer gives **0924 EDT** as the first documented reference to a possible hijack of Flight 77, while the Commission final report traces the **9:24** transmission of the Langley scramble order to a **9:21** report that American 11 was still airborne and records **9:34** as the first military notice that American 77 was missing. ## DOT record-series distinctions

| Public authority | Observation and search implication |
| --- | --- |
| **NC1-398-80-02**, physical pp. 1–2 | The historical item covers administrative correspondence, memoranda and reports relating to operation of the Office of Automated Systems Policy. It gives three-year review and a separate transfer route for files with reference value. The May 12, 2021 cover says **“GRS 3.1, item 010 (DAA-GRS-2016-0013-0002)”**. GRS 3.1, physical p. 1, prints the same authority at **001** and pairs **010** with **DAA-GRS-2013-0005-0006**. These are different displayed pairings. Both item numbers and authorities should be retained when looking for a documented crosswalk or actual transfer. The general schedule also excludes system data and content from its scope. [Historical schedule and cover](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-transportation/rg-0398/nc1-398-80-02_sf115.pdf) · [GRS 3.1, p. 1](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/grs/grs03-1.pdf#page=1) |
| **NC1-398-81-03**, physical pp. 1, 3, and **DAA-0398-2024-0004**, physical pp. 1, 4–6 | Historical item 4 describes copies of congressional requests concerning matters such as budget obligations and outlays, with four-year retention. The cover, marked **“Accurate as of 09/17/2026”**, lists item 4 as **“T(Inactive)”** and names **DAA-0398-2024-0004-0002**. The **May 5, 2026** approval separately lists item 4 among the predecessors of **0002**, *Correspondence – All Other*, with a five-year cutoff-based retention rule that allows longer retention for agency business. **0001**, *Correspondence – Major – Senior Officials*, includes responses and enclosures and provides for permanent retention. These dated records help frame separate searches for routine and significant records. Which, if either, applies to any particular 2006–07 document remains open. [1981 schedule and later cover](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-transportation/rg-0398/nc1-398-81-03_sf115.pdf) · [2026 approval, pp. 4–6](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-transportation/rg-0398/daa-0398-2024-0004_sf115.pdf#page=4) |
| **DOT 1710.1**, §§3–5, and **GRS 13 / 5.5** | Indexed DOT text identifies **DOT F 1700-3**, the originator's background file, the Publishing and Graphics Division's master publication list and printing-production records. Its scope is individually published, stocked material and excludes documents coded under another departmental system. GRS 13 distinguishes control files from the record copy of a publication. GRS 5.5 lists printing requisitions and manuscript clearances as control records and excludes unique agency publications from its schedule. A print-job record and the signed Congressional report are separate acquisition targets. The DOT PDF text was inspected in a public index. Direct retrieval returned HTTP 403, so that particular order body was not separately preserved.
