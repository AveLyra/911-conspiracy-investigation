All four acquired 640-pixel-high JPEG reference views have now been visually inspected. Their borders/labels and widths differ; the two intended pairs show corresponding scene details. Do not call their files or raw decoded pixels identical without checking. No high-resolution TIFF, original negative or exposure worksheet was acquired. The archived June 2004 caption date is not independently timed by this test.

Use installed Pillow 12.3.0 and NumPy 2.3.5; OpenCV/SciPy are unavailable and will not be installed. Preserve original JPEG bytes. For a descriptive scene-similarity comparison only, convert each source to grayscale in memory, crop equal fractional margins, resize to 256 by 256 pixels with Pillow bicubic sampling, and compare the central 224 by 224 parent region with all equal-sized child windows. Maximum translations are 16 pixels in each direction after resizing. Report centered and best translated Pearson correlations, fitted translation and the same quantities for unrelated cross-column pairings. Run margins 5%, 10% and 15% as predefined sensitivity views, and identical-source controls to check implementation. No transformed image file is written.

Targets: P31/A26 and P29/A21. Cross-column comparisons P31/A21 and P29/A26 are limited negative controls, not a calibrated population of distinct exposures from the same viewpoint. No probability, universal threshold or same-negative conclusion may be derived from four views. Fit and evaluation use the same pixels; this is descriptive registration, not independent prediction. Distinct exposure times of a static scene, copied photographs, crop/rescan/contrast and reproduction effects may remain indistinguishable. Zero historical thermal/custody/chemical tests are performed.

Inspect border identifiers as visible at reference resolution, retaining partial/uncertain readings; do not reconstruct obscured writing or infer who changed it. A photograph number can be a reproduction label, not a unique original negative number. Exact decoded-pixel hashes are computed with shape recorded separately. All raw inputs and contrary/different results are retained.
