## ELKIN21 time accounts

The June 30 message prints **1428Z/1128L**. The July 6 reply gives **1528 UTC**. The handwritten rerecording notes show **1528** beside ELKIN21. Preserve all three observations. At UTC−4, the June local value **1128L** corresponds to **1528Z**. The notes are a second documentary occurrence of **1528** within the same preparation and response chain. Their source dependence and the event marking on the strips remain unresolved. A dated tape or ARTS export with a verified clock and a matching event definition could discriminate between the two UTC values.
