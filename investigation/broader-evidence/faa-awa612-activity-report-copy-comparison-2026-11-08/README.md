# Four copies of the FAA activity report and its continuation

November 8, 2026 Eastern. B129 / PD01 / PD07. Three newly acquired National Archives copies of **AWA-612** give the same visible 09:20 entries and handwritten annotation as the previously held Flight 77 copy. At 72 dpi in grayscale, all first-page pixel differences fall within the fax header area. The body of the page is identical in all four renders. One copy’s embedded text captures the NMCC notification that the other three text layers omit. The images preserve it in all four. [AAL 77](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7596158) · [UAL 93](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7597162) · [AAL 11](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7597929) · [UAL 175](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7598466)

The first page prints **0850** for the tactical-net request, followed by separate **0920** rows for the NMCC notification and the primary-net request. The handwritten bracket links those latter rows and labels the notification “by primary net.” The typed line still says the primary net was **requested**. The separate **0924** row describes an FAA regional operations center report about American 77 disappearing from radar. The forms give a potential source for the later public 09:20 line description. The document leaves the timing of an actual connection, duration of military participation, and receipt of a specific aircraft report open. The handwriting’s author and date remain open. Each AWA-612 PDF contains **nine physical pages**. The UAL 175 copy shows printed **Page 9 of 13** with fax header sequence **021**, dated September 14, 2001 at **21:02**. The already held AAL 77 **AWA-659** opens with the same activity report title and printed **pages 10, 11 and 12 of 13**, bearing consecutive fax header numbers **022, 023 and 024** at **21:03**. Its next page bears **025** at **21:04** and is an email printout. The title, printed pagination and fax sequence identify a candidate continuation across the two archival items. The checked sequence leaves the report’s printed page 13 unlocated. The original cover, manifest and complete fax would establish how these pages were assembled and whether the Commission received the same copy.
