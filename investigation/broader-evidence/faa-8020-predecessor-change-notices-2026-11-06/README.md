# FAA 8020.16 change notices and the accident-record custody trail

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD03–PD04. This follow-up acquires five FAA change notices for the predecessor to JO 8020.16A. The September 2006 notice contains an actual amendment to paragraph 72, the paragraph to which the FAA added the Domestic Events Network (DEN) in December 2010. The comparison also identifies dated custody and correction memoranda required for formal aircraft accident packages. [Preceding DEN study](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/1f952e7344728da3fb160ed1a7691c9d9514a299/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-den-recording-and-retention-directives-2026-11-06/README.md)

## Earlier provisions and the 2010 change

| FAA source | Observed scope |
| --- | --- |
| **N8020.173, effective September 10, 2006**, PDF pp. 1–2 | Changes paragraph **72A(3)** for an automated flight service station (AFSS). The station must include a certified copy in the accident file. The certified original copy may be kept separately and must be retained for the same period as the accident package. The notice expressly leaves the rest of paragraph 72 unchanged and gives no DEN instruction in this amendment. [September 2006 notice](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Notice/ND/N8020.173.pdf#page=2) |
| **N8020.173**, PDF pp. 2–3, and **N8020.175, effective June 24, 2007**, PDF p. 5 | Paragraph **82D(1)(C)** prescribes redistributing changes to a formal accident package along the package's distribution routes and attaching a facility manager or acting manager memorandum explaining the change. The September 2006 notice separately identifies routes through the service center or flight-service office, System Operations Litigation, FAA investigator-in-charge and NTSB for the stated facility classes. These provisions apply to accident packages. Their text gives no basis for assigning the Commission's QFR to that document class. [September 2006 notice, p. 3](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Notice/ND/N8020.173.pdf#page=3) · [June 2007 notice, p. 5](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Notice/8020.175.pdf#page=5) |
| **N8020.174, effective February 11, 2007**, PDF pp. 1–2, and **N JO 8020.178/182**, each PDF p. 1 | The February 2007 notice lists specific paragraphs, figures and an appendix withdrawn from FAA Order **8020.11B**, pointing to updated counterparts in **8020.16**. The 2009 and 2010 notices repeat that transfer of provisions. The listed changes concern accident and incident procedures. They leave open which older hijack-response procedures the FAA reviewed or withdrew under OIG recommendation 4. [February 2007 notice](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Notice/8020.174.pdf#page=1) · [January 2009 notice](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Notice/N8020.178.pdf#page=1) · [January 2010 notice](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Notice/N8020.182.pdf#page=1) |
| **N JO 8020.178, effective January 1, 2009**, and **N JO 8020.182, effective January 1, 2010**, each PDF p. 3 | Paragraph **82(d)(1)** keeps the original package in facility files and requires permission before releasing original documents from custody. The 2009 text names the **System Operations Litigation manager** or designee. The 2010 text names the **System Operations Litigation director** or designee. Both specify a facility-file custody memorandum recording the name, office, approving official and date forwarded. Paragraph 82(d)(1)(c) separately requires an explanation memorandum and distribution of corrections. The two titles and their dates are preserved as written. [January 2009 custody provision](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Notice/N8020.178.pdf#page=3) · [January 2010 custody provision](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Notice/N8020.182.pdf#page=3) |
| **JO 8020.16A, effective December 27, 2010**, PDF p. 30 and companion guide p. 1 | Paragraph **72(d)** specifies a request to the DEN for relevant accident/incident data or recordings when coordinated with the Litigation Liaison Office. The guide identifies the DEN as an addition to paragraph 72. This is a different clause and record scope from the September 2006 AFSS amendment. [DEN provision](https://www.faa.gov/documentlibrary/media/order/jo8020.16a.pdf#page=30) · [Change guide](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Order/Briefing%20Guide%20Basic%20JO%208020.16A.pdf#page=1) |

## Date and identifier discrepancies

- The N8020.173 message says **“CANCEL: 09/09/07”**. The FAA parent-order table also shows **2007-09-09**. The individual notice's FAA catalog says **“Date cancelled” 2007-09-10**. Preserve the one-day difference. The exact changeover date remains unresolved. [Notice face](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Notice/ND/N8020.173.pdf#page=1) · [Parent archive table](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/orders_notices/index.cfm/go/document.information/documentID/14327) · [Individual notice catalog](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/orders_notices/index.cfm/go/document.information/documentID/14758)
- The 2009 and 2010 notices say they replace expired GENOTs **06-35, 07-03 and 07-18**. The 2007 notice faces show **7/03** and **7/18** next to **N8020.174** and **N8020.175**, respectively. The acquired September 2006 message header reads **“GENOT RWA 6/49 SVC B”** above **N8020.173**. Its relationship to **06-35** remains unverified. Preserve both 2006 identifiers. ## Limits and open originals

The five notices contain amendments and references to unchanged text. A case-insensitive search across their 44 extracted or OCR pages found zero whole-word matches for DEN, Domestic Event(s) Network or words beginning with hijack. That is a text-search result, with possible OCR omissions. The inspected provisions give an earlier comparison for paragraph 72 and name types of paperwork to seek where paragraph 82 applies. They leave the operative 2005 base order and the pre-2010 central DEN policy open.
