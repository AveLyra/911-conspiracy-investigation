Public derivative of the November 6, 2026 steel release research · STL01–STL03 / AUD011 / AUD016 / B188 / H005. The Federal Register notice supplies the exact filename of the request packet. Its archived link led to a **16-page, 5,322,715-byte PDF** containing four identifiable request groups. ## Notice dates and source access

- **Published response window.** The notice dated August 11 and published Friday, August 14, prints **close of business August 24, 2009** under DATES. Its body gives **ten working days from publication**, excluding weekends and legal public holidays. Counting weekdays only yields **August 28** if counting starts after publication, or **August 27** if August 14 is included. The arithmetic is conditional on the dates counted. Neither count matches the printed August 24 date. The actual instructions applied to submitters and any correction or extension remain unresolved. The Federal Register document metadata lists an empty corrections array, which is scoped to the returned metadata. [Notice, PDF p. 1 lines 59–61, 101–108 and 152–165](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2009-08-14/pdf/E9-19535.pdf) · [Official text](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2009-08-14/html/E9-19535.htm) · [Official metadata](https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents/E9-19535.json)
- **Current link and archived delivery.** The HTTPS version of the notice’s original WTC-host URL ended at the current NIST investigation HTML page with HTTP **200**. The Wayback availability service pointed to a **2009-09-02 01:46:28 UTC** capture. Requesting its raw replay with the advertised port redirected to **2009-11-08 19:17:41 UTC**, returning HTTP **200 application/pdf**. The PDF hash identifies the delivered November replay body. The relationship to the earlier advertised capture and to the native requests remains unresolved.
