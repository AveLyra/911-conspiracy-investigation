## Database and export records to distinguish

- **May 2002 database copy.** The NCEA draft identifies EPA's Office of Environmental Information as the maintainer and states that the authors received a database copy in May 2002 with data current to mid-April. Section III lists IT Corporation trend reports dated **November 20, 2001**, **January 24, 2002**, and **May 1, 2002**, with the last current to **April 24**. Section IV.e.4 separately names a **May 16, 2002** Trends Report. Those are the document's two May labels. The originals needed to determine whether these identify different versions were not recovered. [NCEA draft PDF p. 33, lines 15–40](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPDF.cgi?Dockey=P100U0IN.PDF#page=33) · [PDF p. 103, lines 29–44](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPDF.cgi?Dockey=P100U0IN.PDF#page=103)
- **Later database exports and web outputs.** The 2003 contractor update identifies the NYC Response Monitoring Data Retrieval database, reports that it was inaccessible when the update was written, and describes separate analyte spreadsheets and public HTML outputs. It reports **16,204** TEM records in the database passage and **16,368** analyses in the latest HTML download. The arithmetic difference is **164**. The two reported totals stay separate. Row keys, cutoffs, duplicates and filters would be needed to explain the difference. The reported disagreement concerns air data and does not establish why the date of bulk-dust sample 03825B differs across reports. [Final update PDF pp. 5 and 9–11](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPDF.cgi?Dockey=9100KDEY.PDF#page=5) · [TEM totals, PDF p. 15, lines 18–34](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPDF.cgi?Dockey=9100KDEY.PDF#page=15)
