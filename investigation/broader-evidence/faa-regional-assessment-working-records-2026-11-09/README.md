# FAA New England lessons-learned records and Eastern Region interview notes

**November 9, 2026 | B129 / PD03–PD04 / PD07.** The National Archives catalog media host returned the New England region's comments and recommendations (ANE-7 and ANE-8). A Commission working-file scan preserves related comments with different wording and coverage. These records supply contemporaneous observations and proposed changes. The Eastern Region equipment assessment described in a 2003 interview remains an acquisition target.

## What the New England documents record

- **Meeting and routing.** The ANE-7 email dated September 21, 2001 describes a lessons-learned meeting with ZBW, BOS ATCT, A90 and BDR AFSS. It reports the ARTCC's concern about contradictory information from the Command Center and the regional office through headquarters. It separates operational from administrative work and raises position logs and recorded lines as follow-up questions. The email recommends another meeting. Physical p. 1, sorted text lines 1, 11–13, 21–38 and 40–44. [ANE-7 p. 1](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/e-Other/5-ANE-7-EMAIL-ANE-LESSONS-LEARNED.pdf#page=1)
- **Recording coverage and time.** In the equipment list, the scan reads “Phone lines within facility not recorded – crisis room and EOF”. Separate bullets refer to ZBW digital voice tapes and say “Record TMU positions”. Under the separate regional heading the scan reads “ROC lines not recorded with time”. The latter wording leaves open whether any audio existed without time information. The tape reference and the TMU proposal identify different retrieval targets. Neither bullet identifies a recorder, recording interval or circuit. Physical p. 10, lines 16–35, and p. 11, lines 10–15. [ANE-7 pp. 10–11](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/e-Other/5-ANE-7-EMAIL-ANE-LESSONS-LEARNED.pdf#page=10)
- **Proposed changes.** ANE-8 recommends scheduled, experienced staffing for the headquarters/Command Center hotline, advance communication with regional crisis centers, operational secure telephones, less dependence on commercial telephone service and review of contingency and air traffic procedures. These are recommendations. Their implementation, approval and circuit coverage require separate records. Physical p. 1, lines 5–36, and p. 2, lines 6–25. ## Eastern Region comparison

- The two distinct Commission scans of the McCartney handwritten notes show the same relevant handwriting and layout on pp. 2–3. Page 2 distinguishes AEA and ANE. Page 3 notes a running AEA air traffic log, a separate bridge with facility managers and a lead to ask the operations center manager which lines were recorded. The typed MFR separately attributes to McCartney the same log and manager leads and describes a post-attack communications and equipment assessment. These selected note passages support identification of the interview and leads. The notes and MFR record the same interview.
