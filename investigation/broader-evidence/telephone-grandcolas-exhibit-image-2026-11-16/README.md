# Third-party image of Flight 93 call-summary exhibit

November 16, 2026 · TEL02–TEL04 / AUD016-A1. A third-party image described by its host as a snapshot of prosecution exhibit P200055 displays three short residence-call entries together. It is a reproduction, not an authenticated rendering of the separately acquired court ZIP or a native carrier record.

## Source and observation

The [host's index](https://www.911research.wtc7.net/planes/evidence/calldetail.html) describes its images as snapshots of P200055. Its [Grandcolas image](https://www.911research.wtc7.net/planes/evidence/docs/exhibit/LaurenGrandcolas.png), retrieved November 16, 2026, is a **957 × 681** PNG, SHA-256 `2c8643b52046f41c222ab77ed93fd96be7aa621faaa74f11568753839c1e8943`. The downloaded image was visually inspected and is retained locally, not redistributed here. Its lower-left call box shows **09:42:03 / 2 seconds**, **09:42:25 / 3 seconds**, and **09:42:45 / 3 seconds**, alongside the same destination category. Its display associates the three values with the three times in that image.

The separately acquired [court P200055 ZIP](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/bf46236c949033a54d8ec6d52007ad95070388d1/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-p200055-court-bundle-2026-11-16/README.md) contains a Flight 93 call SWF. A local preliminary static-text probe of that SWF found the three time strings and duration strings, but did not reconstruct their layout or validate the parser. The third-party image is consistent with a **2/3/3** display and the [May 2004 NARA briefing](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/legislative/rg-148/2524787/t-0148-911MFR-00217.pdf#page=2); its chain of creation and pixel identity to this exact acquired SWF are unverified. It does not itself validate the parser or prove the court file's displayed rows.

The [NPS page](https://www.nps.gov/flni/learn/historyculture/phone-calls-from-flight-93.htm) describes three short attempts as **2/3/2** seconds under its 9:42 heading, without listing seconds in the heading. Preserve that sequence beside the image and briefing. Matching passenger, destination, order and interval are candidate association factors, not proof of a common row or reason for the difference. The sources may share data and are not independent measurements.
