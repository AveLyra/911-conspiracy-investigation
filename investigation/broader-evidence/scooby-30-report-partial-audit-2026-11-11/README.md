## Original-source checks completed in this pass

1. **Tower model calibration and observations.** [NIST NCSTAR 1-6D, vol. 1](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-6dv1.pdf), chapter 3, states that the WTC 1 south-wall pull-in magnitude was selected by trial and error to match observed bowing. Consequently, that same fitted observation is not an independent validation target for the selected force. This is a methodological limitation, not proof that the applied force was physically impossible or that another collapse mechanism occurred. The wider model chain and independent predictions require separate testing.
2. **WTC 2 timing.** [NIST's final towers report, printed p. 153](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1.pdf), records observed collapse initiation 56 minutes after impact and global instability in the presented analysis at 43 minutes. NIST characterized overall agreement as reasonably good and reported timing sensitivity to bowing factors. The 13-minute comparison is a disclosed model–observation difference, not evidence by itself of deliberate manipulation or explosives.
3. **Badge-list scope.** [NIST NCSTAR 1-7](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=101046), chapter 3, says its badge list included occupants, people absent that day, former occupants, deceased persons and test entries. That independently supports treating the list as a survey/recruitment frame rather than proof of a particular entry or a complete visitor-card event log. It does not identify the detained men's visitor card or its holder.
4. **Warning corpus.** The [CIA's 2026 release announcement](https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/cia-releases-presidents-daily-briefs-in-commemoration-of-the-25th-anniversary-of-9-11/) identifies 71 declassified President's Daily Brief products. The count does not establish an individual recipient's reading, advance knowledge of a specific plot, or action. The research report's product-by-product index and dissemination claims remain to be independently reviewed.
5. **SEC aggregate options volume.** The [separate SEC source check](../scooby-report-availability-correction-2026-11-11/README.md) inspected the 2004 Commission follow-up memorandum reporting the correction from 2,075 to 1,575 total UAL put contracts. That is an aggregate correction, not a reconstructed named-account allocation or a foreknowledge finding. The SEC report’s visible interface text was subsequently captured locally.


6. **FBI release accounting.** The [January 16, 2013 FBI letter](https://www.israellobby.org/UrbanMoving/01162013release_letter.pdf), hosted by the requester, says 215 pages were reviewed and 183 released; it also describes deleted-page information sheets. The report’s 177-page index total is a working premise, and its serial-33 count was inferred rather than directly verified. An apparent difference is not evidence that six substantive pages were suppressed. The original complete production and item-by-item accounting remain necessary.
7. **Dust specimen lineage.** [Harrit et al.](https://benthamopenarchives.com/contents/pdf/TOCPJ/TOCPJ-2-7.pdf) describe analyses of chips from four dust samples. [Millette’s 2012 report](https://www.911facts.dk/resources/Millette/9119ProgressReport022912_rev1_030112web.pdf) states that his team could not obtain the Harrit samples and instead selected four archived samples. His findings therefore cannot conclusively identify the exact chips used by Harrit, while Harrit’s interpretation is not thereby independently established. Traceable specimen and aliquot IDs, raw measurements, controls and independent replication remain important.
8. **Interrogation dependency.** The [9/11 Commission’s chapter 5](https://www.9-11commission.gov/report/911Report_Ch5.htm) explicitly states that chapters 5 and 7 relied heavily on captured members and that Commission access was limited to intelligence reports. This validates the need to distinguish interrogation-dependent private accounts from independently documented travel, finance or communications. It neither proves the accounts false nor validates each underlying account; the report’s individual dependencies and Senate-source claims still need checking.
