## Original-source checks completed in this pass

1. **Tower model calibration and observations.** [NIST NCSTAR 1-6D, vol. 1](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-6dv1.pdf), chapter 3, states that the WTC 1 south-wall pull-in magnitude was selected by trial and error to match observed bowing. Consequently, that same fitted observation is not an independent validation target for the selected force. This is a methodological limitation, not proof that the applied force was physically impossible or that another collapse mechanism occurred. The wider model chain and independent predictions require separate testing.
2. **WTC 2 timing.** [NIST's final towers report, printed p. 153](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1.pdf), records observed collapse initiation 56 minutes after impact and global instability in the presented analysis at 43 minutes. NIST characterized overall agreement as reasonably good and reported timing sensitivity to bowing factors. The 13-minute comparison is a disclosed model–observation difference, not evidence by itself of deliberate manipulation or explosives.
3. **Badge-list scope.** [NIST NCSTAR 1-7](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=101046), chapter 3, says its badge list included occupants, people absent that day, former occupants, deceased persons and test entries. That independently supports treating the list as a survey/recruitment frame rather than proof of a particular entry or a complete visitor-card event log. It does not identify the detained men's visitor card or its holder.
4. **Warning corpus.** The [CIA's 2026 release announcement](https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/cia-releases-presidents-daily-briefs-in-commemoration-of-the-25th-anniversary-of-9-11/) identifies 71 declassified President's Daily Brief products. The count does not establish an individual recipient's reading, advance knowledge of a specific plot, or action. The research report's product-by-product index and dissemination claims remain to be independently reviewed.
5. **SEC aggregate options volume.** The [separate SEC source check](../scooby-report-availability-correction-2026-11-11/README.md) inspected the 2004 Commission follow-up memorandum reporting the correction from 2,075 to 1,575 total UAL put contracts. That is an aggregate correction, not a reconstructed named-account allocation or a foreknowledge finding. The SEC report was visibly inspected in an earlier interface pass but its full accessibility snapshot was not retained in the present set.
