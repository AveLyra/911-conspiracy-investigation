## What the comparison adds

The district court citation points to the Port Authority's attempted submission. The docket index gives a separate signed-order locator and an additional cross-docket locator cited in the 2011 scheduling order. Keep all three references. Neither the docket descriptions nor the opinions supply the item schedule needed to tell whether the proposed, authorized, shipped and accessioned sets match. The opinions describe title and custody in different terms. Those descriptions can coexist and do not establish the date or terms of transfer for each of the 227 other artifacts.

The March 5, 2012 omnibus order has candidate counterpart entries **21 MC 101, docket 1604**, and **21 MC 100, docket 2780**. Both mirrors describe medical examiner examination and removal, cleaning, transfer of remaining Hangar 17 artifacts, and a steel-testing condition. The underlying orders, list, scope and implementation remain unverified. The docket entries describe authorization and conditions. They do not show that anyone exercised inspection rights, performed cleaning on a particular steel object, or transferred it. [21 MC 101 index](https://dockets.justia.com/docket/new-york/nysdce/1:2021mc00101/264866) · [21 MC 100 index](https://www.plainsite.org/courts/new-york-southern-district-court/in-re-world-trade-center-disaster-site-litigation/msy2xhjk/)

An LMDC progress report separately records an **October 20, 2005** meeting at which a Port Authority representative said NIST planned to return tested tower steel. The prior review recorded the agency-reported **2009** convoy and NIST's retention of extracted samples and a few whole pieces. The 2005 statement supplies a dated planning lead. The set proposed for return, the set shipped, and its overlap with the museum exhibit are unverified. [LMDC report, pp. 2–3](https://www.renewnyc.com/attachments/content/pdfs/ProgressReport_JulyDecember2005.pdf#page=2) · [NIST convoy account](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2009/10/convoy-returns-world-trade-center-steel-new-york-city)
