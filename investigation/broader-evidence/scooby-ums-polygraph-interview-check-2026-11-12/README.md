# Scooby report 5: FBI interview and polygraph source check

November 12, 2026. This checks selected recovered UMS-assessment claims against three FBI electronic communications in the 2013 release. They are primary records of what FBI staff wrote, not independent confirmation of every statement made by an interviewee or inference made by an examiner. Redactions prevent identifying subjects or safely joining records solely through matching dates.
