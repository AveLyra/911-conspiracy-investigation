# FAA Request 16 and the “Notifications to Military” sheet: candidate connection

2026-11-25 · FAA B129 / PD01–PD04 and PD07. This comparison identifies a possible source-to-submission link, not the corrected May 2003 response or evidence of its delivery.

## Records and observations

- **July 18, 2003 cover:** the FAA describes a second submission responding to DOT Document Request 2 and lists item **16** among items it says the submission fully addresses. This is the cover's representation. [AWA-1077, NARA 34913881](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913881) · [AWA-329, NARA 34913201](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913201). Both are 22-page released scans.
- **Request 16 response:** physical page **11**, printed page **8**, of both copies asks for FAA communications with NORAD on September 11, 2001. The response refers to logs, a **“Notifications to Military”** sheet, certified transcripts and certified recordings, followed by “see attached”; it also refers to additional relevant material in the item 20 production. The inspected page contains a list, not the actual named notification sheet. The response itself does not identify the sheet by date, page hash or copy identifier, and the statement does not establish recipient intake or complete delivery.
- **Separately preserved sheet:** the previously reviewed Commission archive copy is titled **“Notifications to Military”**, dated **09/20/01**, and bears a fax header dated **January 19, 2004**. Its specific 9:24 entry, attribution to NEADS logs and separate 9:26 entry are preserved with the second copy and the previously documented uncertainty. [Source comparison and fax details](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/9115d1dedb2838f2cabc8fce5ecc8a0212935149/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-september20-notification-sheet-fax-2026-11-09/README.md). The 2004 header is a mark on that copy; it does not establish how or when the 2003 submission was delivered.

## What is and is not established

The exact shared title makes the 2001-dated sheet a **candidate** for the item mentioned in the 2003 response. Other similarly titled sheets or versions remain possible. A shared title and related subject do not establish identity, attachment, transmission or receipt. The two 2003 scanned copies are related document copies, not independent sources for historical events. Their content, layout and margin marks are retained separately; the cause of scan differences is unknown.

The previously identified FAA/NORAD timing accounts and the DOT OIG's 2006 account remain as recorded; neither the sheet title nor this possible link resolves the historical timing, proves an intentional misstatement, or establishes a correction, an administrative decision, or recommendation closure. Those are separate questions.
