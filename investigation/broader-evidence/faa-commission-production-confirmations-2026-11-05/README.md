## The inquiry and its reply

- **June 13, 2003, AWA-831.** The Commission says it **received** three installments on **May 28, June 5 and June 9**. It asks DOT to confirm completeness in writing and singles out Request No. 1 items **5** (flight tracking, including NORAD communications) and **7** (after-action or self-assessment reports). The nine-page archive copy includes the May 7 request and the three named FAA cover letters, along with selected indexes or item descriptions. It does not contain all the operational records described in those letters. [June 13 inquiry and attachments](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913673)
- **July 10, 2003, AWA-826.** DOT expressly replies to the June 13 inquiry and identifies its subject as Request No. 1. For item **5**, it reports May 28 text radar data, a June 4 briefing, a later CD of the briefing without a separate FAA memorandum, and June 4 laboratory viewing for three flights. It says a communications transcript was still to be sent, and consultations were ongoing about remaining NTSB material. For item **7**, it reports June 5 and June 9 materials and attached Rapid Response Team reports, while saying the search for additional documentation was continuing. The reply describes some other items as fully responded to. Its recorded state varies by item. [July 10 reply, physical pp. 2–3](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913663)

The June 13 recipient-side statement corroborates the three installment dates. The July 10 letter qualifies what those installments covered. Its visible header and letter date on p. 2 both read **July 10, 2003**. The OCR header gives `JUL.16.2083`; the separate fax cover on p. 1 is dated **July 9, 2003** and declares **two pages including cover**, while the archive body contains **14 pages**. Keep the cover, scan, and OCR observations separate. The August 29 correspondence below refers to a **July 16** response about a different request and item. The two responses remain separately identified. [AWA-826](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913663) · [AWA-828](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913667)

## Later statements on outstanding material and search completion

| Record | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| **August 26 Commission memo, faxed August 27, AWA-827** | Follow-up to the August 19 letter. Identifies Request No. 2 items **21 and 22** and Herndon Command Center tapes and transcripts as the most important overdue categories. [AWA-827, physical p. 2](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913665) |
| **August 29 DOT reply, AWA-828** | Attributes a chronology including Secretary Mineta communications to its **July 16** response under item 21 and reports no other responsive documents. For item 22 it acknowledges press activity and an interview but reports no responsive transcript or other document. It projects that Herndon tapes will be available the following week and says copying would involve over **18 hours** across multiple active channels. That is a production estimate. The chronology version, underlying source and tape delivery require separate verification. [AWA-828, physical p. 2](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913667) |
| **December 22 Commission clarification, AWA-824** | Says Commission staff were still discovering relevant material that had not been produced, describes a December 12 staff meeting, and says the specified tape list left the broader subpoena requirement in place. It explicitly asks for complete recordings of the requested positions and time frames even if they contain silence, because the Commission needs timing information. The cover declares **four pages**. The archived PDF has **three**, and the cited tape list is absent. The February 10, 2004 fax imprint on p. 3 is separate from the December letter date. [AWA-824, physical pp. 1–3](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913659) |
| **July 8, 2004 DOT confirmation, AWA-823** | Responds to a **June 10** Commission letter. Qualifies its account by the Secretary's knowledge and inquiry of officials, says DOT and FAA took reasonable steps to find records responsive to four requests, and says all located responsive records had been **“produced or made available for review.”** It reports pages and recording hours in broad units. The scan carries a **July 12** DOT fax imprint. This supplies an agency search-completion statement, with no itemized matching or Commission acceptance. [AWA-823, physical p. 1](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913657) |

Preserve the contemporaneous August and December gaps beside the later DOT statement. They address different dates and use different conditions. The agency's July 2004 statement would be testable against item-level dispatch, inspection and receipt records. It does not identify the exact scope of any unlocated records, which items were copied versus viewed, or how each earlier omission was addressed. Likewise, an earlier overdue list leaves eventual delivery open. The May 2004 OIG report's finding of no indication of intentional withholding and its stated need for further Commission consultation remain separate from the production records. [OIG CC-2004-003, physical p. 2](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2004003.pdf#page=2)
