## Selected visible observations

Page 1 says five personnel are not listed on the position log provided in the package. Handwritten responses associate two with other logs and label data for three as not available; the underlying logs and the meaning of those notes have not been independently verified. Page 2 says the reviewer could not verify the on/off-position times for those five personnel from the provided position log. This is a limitation of the provided log, not proof that those employees were absent or that other logs did not exist.

For the Sector 42 transcript, page 1 flags transmissions `1306:19` and `1306:17` as out of order. It also requests several punctuation and legend changes. For the Sector 55 transcript it asks to change `Flight 4511` to `Flight 5411` in a named airline legend and to verify an inconsistent ending time against the tape. Page 2 requests changes of some agency labels to `Unknown` and separately identifies a `1322:50` attribution as `IPTT`. These are requests in a review sheet; their implementation, underlying audio and final values are not shown.
