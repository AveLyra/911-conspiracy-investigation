## A specific playback and custody record

The [official FBI Vault September interviews, part 04 of 08](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11%20Commission%20Report/9-11-interviews-2001-09-sep-04-of-08/view), **viewer page 18**, contains a report dated **3/27/02** describing a visit on **March 26, 2002**. Its footer bears **REQ. #35-13** and **000000348**. The body reports:

- A listener used headphones and a cassette player to hear a copy of a cassette tape described as in FBI custody.
- The listener was supplied a transcription. The report describes the recording as a **911 call made on September 11, 2001**, by a person who identified himself as a United Flight 93 passenger.
- The listener reportedly identified the recorded voice. The report subsequently describes other people receiving opportunities to listen and/or read the transcription. It does not report a separate voice identification by every listener.
- Its final paragraph states that the cassette containing the 911 call and its transcription remained in FBI custody.

**What this supports:** a concrete existing-record retrieval target: the cassette/copy and transcription used during that visit, their evidence-item and custody records, the original emergency-call source, and the documentation of the reported voice recognition.
