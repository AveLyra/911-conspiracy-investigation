## Source and checked observations

The [January 2009 Commission archive mirror](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip) member `911 NARA/Team 7 Files/Team 7 Box 12/T7 B12 DOJ Doc Req 35-13 Packet 4 Fdr- Entire Contents- Response Letters- Reports 403.pdf` contains 92 physical pages. The retained PDF's SHA-256 is `7ed35efb7825f26cb0c712d334a8b286c74bcc25c5b44e01f227059f446c6dba`. It is a reproduced scan, not an authenticated native case system. On November 25, 2026, pages 24 and 46–48 were rendered and visually inspected; remaining pages were not reviewed in this step.

- Page 46 visibly records an information-received date of September 11 and time **9:40 a.m.** Page 47 separately records an event date and **9:40 a.m.**, and describes the call as via a **cellphone**. Page 48 contains a related follow-up lead using the cellphone description. The scan shows the form's labels; it does not establish who supplied the device classification, a calibrated clock, or that either 9:40 field records call connection.
- Page 24's interview report is dated September 12 for transcription and investigation. Its introductory paragraph identifies an **Airfone** call; it attributes a call at **approximately 10:00 a.m.** to the recipient's account. The scan does not identify whether the device label came from the recipient or an agent.
- A later [briefing and public accounts](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/c95fa5a678664074960c1e772fb6ceca2c568a8f/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-wainio-briefing-archive-2026-11-25/README.md) separately report **9:53:43**, **9:53** and **9:54** under their own descriptions. Those entries and their native call-record joins were not independently checked in this visual step.

## Limits and outstanding inputs

The time fields and service labels are visibly present in the reproduced pages and remain preserved side by side. The documents may refer to the same call, but no call-ID join or clock calibration was performed. Different source fields, approximate recollection, initial classification or error are untested possibilities. These observations do not establish fabrication, motive, or actual service type. A scan-image check is not authentication of its historical content.
