## Unresolved differences

- **Agreement scope and shipment count.** Keep “a Piece of Steel” from the county summary and “The four beams” from the arrival report side by side. The sources describe different stages. Whether they refer to the same inventory and whether all four were covered by 10-568b remains open. A single inventory entry containing multiple sections, separate authorizations, or a difference in what was counted are untested possibilities. The executed agreement and a marked shipment list would distinguish them.
- **Building attribution.** Preserve the 2011 report's general Twin Towers wording and the 2015 report's North Tower description of one displayed beam. The two descriptions differ in specificity. A source chain to building, position, or recovery location would require item identifiers and upstream records.
- **Ceremony chronology.** Keep the 2011 ceremony announcement and 2012 dedication announcement as separate observations. The invitations leave the events as held and the installation date unverified.
