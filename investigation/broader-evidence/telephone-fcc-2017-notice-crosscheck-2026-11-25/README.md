## Interpretation and limits

The similarity between the indexed filename and the 2017 notice identifier is a reason to be cautious when prioritizing the 2022 filing as a possible Airfone record. It does **not** establish that the 2022 uploaded file contains the 2017 notice, that its content is irrelevant, or why it was cross-listed. A filename is not a content hash. The actual attachment was not obtained or inspected; the web reader returned an inaccessible-page error for its link. This does not establish that the record is missing, withheld, or deleted. No inference about 2001 call records or their custody follows.

The prior comparison also preserves the 2014 historical table's **four filings** and April 29, 2013 last date separately from the current exact index query's **five submissions**, including the later cross-listed 2022 entry. The difference is unresolved: selection, counting and date rules remain unverified. The present note does not change that record.
