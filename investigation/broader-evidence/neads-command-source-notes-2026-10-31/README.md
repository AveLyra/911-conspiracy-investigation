## The most useful new record

A separate bold note says that, from around **9:10 forward**, the Alderson **MCC OP Ch. 2** times **“must be redone and verified.”**

This is direct documentary support for retrieving and comparing the two versions and the actual verification work. It is not our completed measurement of an omission or clock error. We have not established which versions those labels denote, whether the warning was later acted on, or whether either is the reproduced NCT transcript or held MP3. A corrected final version may exist; this note cannot prove that it does not.

The same page attributes the American 11 still-airborne/toward-Washington information **to the identification section as the reported source to the mission crew commander**. It separately describes Battle Cab's role in a Langley decision. These are distinct source/decision steps. They narrow the next retrieval to ID-section inputs, verification/correction messages and matched calls; they do not establish the originating source or a direct join to the FAA's printed 1326/1329 exchanges.

## What could weaken a suspicious reading

The strongest relevant objection is that the interview/review process was explicitly identifying uncertain information and requesting transcript correction. If the versions and worksheets show the corrections were performed and accurately propagated, that would weaken a concealment interpretation while leaving the earlier error documented. Conversely, a source-supported unexplained omission, known uncorrected error or incompatible final account could strengthen a specific integrity concern. These tests have not been performed here.

The typed notes describe review of chat logs and recordings, including tape playback. That is a recorded interview-review activity, not merely an unprompted recollection; the exact reviewed media and versions remain unacquired here, and we did not independently replay or validate them.

Mixed real/exercise entries or later RADES additions could be ordinary logging/reconstruction practices; they could also impair historical reconstruction if mislabeled or undocumented. We need actual entries, definitions and edit history. A label alone establishes neither an innocent explanation nor fabrication. These notes and later narratives may depend on the same recordings/logs and are not independent corroboration simply because they appear in different documents.
