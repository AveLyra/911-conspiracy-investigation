# Great Lakes FAA records show what the October 2003 follow-up asked for

November 8, 2026 Eastern · **B129 / PD07**, with **PD01–PD04** dependencies. The National Archives holds the FAA Administrator's October 14, 2003 instruction stating that several prior responses to Commission document requests “may not have been complete.” The instruction asks for broader collection of responsive material and written replies when an office found none. The Great Lakes returns preserve a certification and explicit contact exceptions. These records concern collection and production in 2003. The later corrected answer and administrative decision remain open. ## Preserve the scope and metadata differences

- The **October 14** certification and the roster exceptions stand side by side. The roster gives no proof of later contact for the three p. 3 exceptions. The **October 16** certification has a different visible population. Do not use the three-person return as confirmation of the Regional Office roster. The travel-or-leave column shows **9/11/01** on AGL-6 p. 3 and **9/11/03** on p. 5. Both pages identify the employee population with **9/11/01** in their main headings. The column readings remain unresolved.
- The catalogue reports object sizes of **489,232** bytes for AGL-2 and **100,934** for AGL-4. The linked PDF bodies returned **1,052,468** and **156,104** bytes respectively. AGL-6 reports and returned **2,580,396** bytes. Preserve the catalogue fields and retrieved body lengths. The source of the differences and any edition relationship remain untested.
- Commission staff's October 8 account of what managers reported about earlier requests, FAA's October 14 possible-incompleteness warning, and the later OIG's finding of no indication of intentional withholding have separate scopes. The new records add headquarters instructions and specific regional returns. They leave the cause of any omission and the adequacy of the full production open.
