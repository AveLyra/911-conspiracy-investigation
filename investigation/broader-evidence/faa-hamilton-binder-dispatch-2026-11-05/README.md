## What the comparison establishes

June 10, 2004 was a Thursday. Relative to the message date, the planned Day 1 window maps to **Friday, June 11 morning** and the planned Day 2 window to **late Friday, June 11 or early Saturday, June 12**. The conversion uses calendar dates and gives no exact hour, dispatch timestamp or delivery confirmation. Those windows supply dates to test against packing lists, outgoing distribution logs and dated recipient copies. The archived Day 2 placement of the memo supplies an item to look for in that comparison. The email addresses the revised agenda and expected binder delivery, with no inventory that could tie that particular memo copy to any shipment.

Keep the **April 22 circulation disagreement**, the June 6 memo's stronger knowledge wording with its caveat, and the June 7 memo's qualified wording in their existing records. The June 10 plan postdates them. It does not adjudicate earlier accounts of withholding or requested circulation. The public reader's extracted **John Fanner** and the IU metadata's **John Farmer** also remain side by side. The latter is a catalog field and the former is a text-layer rendering. This check did not verify the author line on the IU scan.
