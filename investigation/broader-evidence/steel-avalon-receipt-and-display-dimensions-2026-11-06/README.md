## What the comparison leaves open

Keep **12 feet** as the reported pickup dimension and **9 feet 11 inches** as the reported design dimension. The records use different measurement descriptions. The received length could include a portion concealed in the mount, a piece may have been trimmed, an early estimate may differ from a later measurement, or different pieces may be involved. Each explanation remains untested. The original measurements, engineering datum and physical identity are unresolved. No reviewed record connects the Avalon beam to WTC 7 or to a NIST specimen.

The duplicate pickup paragraph, the borough newsletter and the repeated design dimension do not make either figure an independent measurement. The June vote provides a precise locator for the design record. The pickup report provides a precise date, sending facility and receiving organization for the custody record.
