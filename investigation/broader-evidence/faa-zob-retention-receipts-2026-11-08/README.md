# FAA voice and radar hold copies and United 93 message receipts

November 8, 2026 Eastern · **B129 / PD07**, with **PD01–PD04** open. Three newly recovered FAA scans document distribution of a September 2001 voice and radar preservation instruction. Two more scans record read notifications for a message headed **UAL93 NTAP**. These add dated routing evidence. The original message, its data and facility-level records of hold implementation remain open. [NARA FAA finding aid, Box 68](https://www.archives.gov/research/foia/faa-finding-aid-60-71)

## The hold, its clarification and forwarding

| Source | What the visible copy records |
|---|---|
| **ZOB-353**, physical pp. 1–2 | **Voice & Radar Data**, **9/14/2001 9:10 AM**, marked **Urgent** and **Receipt requested**. The recipient list includes Cleveland facility entries. The message addresses FAA air traffic facilities and instructs them to retain September 11 voice and radar data and keep it in separately locked storage. The two-page body has no clarification interval. [ZOB-353, p. 1 OCR lines 1 and 64–68, p. 2 lines 2–3](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7597451) |
| **ZOB-350**, physical p. 1 | Inner **9/14/01 11:13 AM** clarification. A forwarding header gives **9/14/2001 12:59 PM**, below a local instruction to meet the criteria. The local instruction has no separate timestamp on the page. [ZOB-350, OCR lines 2 and 6–17](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7597448) |
| **ZOB-355**, physical p. 1 | Outer **9/17/2001 8:09 AM** forward from a Cleveland facility account to **#ZOB Quality Assurance**. It reproduces the **9/14/01 11:13 AM** clarification and hold wording. [ZOB-355, OCR lines 1–5 and 9–16](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7597453) |
| **ZOB-140**, held comparison, physical p. 1 | Reproduces the **9/14/01 11:13 AM** clarification and hold wording under an FYI wrapper. The visible wrapper does not show the outer September 17 date or routing header present in ZOB-355. [ZOB-140, OCR lines 6–11](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7597306) |

The checked hold passages share the same instruction across the four scans, including the printed **“futher advised”** and **“separate lock and key”** wording. Three copies share the exact clarification fields **0000Z** on September 11 and **11:59 local** on September 11. They print no AM or PM marker for **11:59 local**. The time-zone labels are preserved as written and the actual scope applied at Cleveland remains open. The copies document forwarding and depend on the same underlying instruction. They supply neither inventory-level confirmation nor a hold-release record. The October 2003 request's **0800-1400 EST** and **0800-1200 EST** refer to a later tape extraction request. The relationship of each request to material held under the 2001 directive requires item-level records.
