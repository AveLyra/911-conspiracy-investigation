## Observation

The November 20, 2001 forward reproduces a November 16 message asking air traffic managers to secure September 11 data and document access. It calls for direct AAT-20 approval before releases, explicitly including releases to the FBI, FAA offices and NTSB; it also directs supervision of review, copying and handling, and documentation of people who accessed or received copies, including playback use. The message reports press leaks and attributes a request to tighten security to the FBI, and asks managers to brief personnel about limiting conversations until the investigation and identified danger to investigators ended. These are the author's reported circumstances and directions, not independently verified FBI terms, leak events, implemented controls or actual access decisions.

One phrase in the visible text reads “prevent future leaks and hinder this very critical and sensitive investigation.” The wording is preserved as printed; its intended meaning is unresolved. Neither this phrase nor the restrictions establish motive or wrongdoing.
