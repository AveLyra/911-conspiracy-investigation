# Wichita TSA steel custody and G-1043

November 6, 2026 UTC · STL02–STL03 / B188 / H005

The fabricator identifies the steel in Wichita's airport memorial as a cut section of **G-1043**. Wichita Airport Advisory Board minutes document the board viewing the piece in **December 2009**, before approving support for a memorial design in **June 2011**. The airport's account of the unveiling identifies a **24-inch section** in the finished display. Together these records supply a specific item identifier and a plausible route from TSA custody through fabrication and display. The executed transfer, fragment allocation and original building-member identification remain open. [Fabricator's project record](https://www.bbmetalarts.com/911wtc) · [2009 board minutes, PDF p. 7](https://www.flywichita.com/wp-content/minutes/2009/Minutes1272009.pdf#page=7) · [2011 board minutes, PDF p. 4](https://www.flywichita.com/wp-content/uploads-bu/Minutes-6-06-11-final.pdf#page=4) · [Airport unveiling account, PDF p. 5](https://www.flywichita.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/Remembering-9-11.pdf#page=5)
