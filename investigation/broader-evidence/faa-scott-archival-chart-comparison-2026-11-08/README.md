## Keep the conflicting accounts and their qualifications

The official hearing transcript remains separate. Saved HTML **line 233** attributes **9:02 / North Tower** to Scott, beside the charts' **9:02 row / 0903 / south tower**. At **line 238**, it describes **9:30** as airborne, beside the chart's first-radar-data wording; both retain **9:35** as reported airborne. At **line 242**, Scott explicitly discusses the chart's **9:43** impact label and a revised **9:37** time. The transcript documents an acknowledgment of those two values during the hearing. Original hearing audio was not compared, and no account is selected here as controlling. [Official transcript](https://www.911commission.gov/archive/hearing2/9-11Commission_Hearing_2003-05-23.htm).

The chart host's prose at saved HTML **line 421** also retains **8:43** for the Pentagon example, beside **0943** in both chart copies and the transcript's **9:43 / 9:37** account. That earlier discrepancy remains unresolved; this review assigns no cause to it. [Chart host](https://www.oredigger61.org/?p=151).

The same transcript's **line 228** distinguishes log-entry time from actual event time; **line 246** qualifies the chronology as NORAD/CONR's reconstruction and notes other agencies could have different logs. Those qualifications remain alongside the disputes. The June 7 staff memo, the later Commission reconstruction and Inspector General assessments remain distinct accounts; this source-copy comparison does not independently test their operational timing, knowledge or intent claims.
