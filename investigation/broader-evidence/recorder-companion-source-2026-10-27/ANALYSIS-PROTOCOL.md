# Post-acquisition lexical check

Adaptive after full reading of the acquired text,not blind preregistration. Check the exact candidate hash/size and both actual archive-index entities. Decode UTF8 strictly; retain original newline bytes and line strings. Count physical logical lines,distinct strings,repeated strings,blank lines and literal whole-line numeric/time values. Count bytes representing tabs/commas/newline conventions. These lexical counts cannot count independent sensors,validate channels,authenticate an NTSB export or calibrate a clock. Preserve Eastern as a label,not a verified time convention.

Record first/last selected line locators without concatenating abbreviated/repeated lines into invented full parameter names. Do not infer whether the host edited,truncated or extracted another file,or why companions were not exposed by this bounded index. Compare actual basenames with the official report's separately named attachments without assuming they describe the same entity. No old raw/frame/page calculations rerun; source hashes rechecked after reading. Source entities and headers remain local; eligible notes/selected labels/source pins/code/results only may be published.
