# FAA FOIA retention: categories, conflicting control-file periods and missing media locators

November 8, 2026 · B129 / PD04-PD05; REC01/REC03 acquisition support.

The [2011 FAA reply](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/c45f1335ff4bb4f5653af7960710afa4868779a5/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-radar-release-decisions-2026-11-08/README.md) described a retention-limited set of five radar-denial letters. Newly preserved historical schedules show why the file category and trigger must be identified before attributing any missing record to disposal.

## Keep the record classes and editions separate

| Class | FAA Order 1350.15C, August 29, 2001 | NARA GRS14, August 1995 | NARA GRS14, April 2010 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unappealed full/partial denial correspondence | **6 years after reply** | **6 years after reply** | **6 years after reply** |
| Control registers | **6 years after last entry** | **6 years after last entry** | **5 years after last entry** |
| Other control files | **6 years**, final agency action or court adjudication, whichever later | Same displayed period/triggers | **5 years**, same displayed triggers |
| Appeal correspondence | Later of the three stated agency/suit/court triggers | Same displayed triggers | Same displayed triggers |

FAA locators: [chapter 4, physical pp. 8-9 / printed 23-24, section 1270](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/order/finance/1350-15C/media/chapter4.pdf#page=8). NARA 1995: [physical pp. 92-94 / GRS14 printed 3-5, items 11-13](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/grs/grs-trs07-superseded.pdf#page=92). NARA 2010: [physical pp. 79-81 / printed 49-51, items 11-13](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/grs/index.pdf#page=79). The 1995 copy's superseded markings remain preserved.

The **six-versus-five control-file periods** remain side by side. No source is selected as controlling a particular FAA case. An operative change register, approved exception, file classification and hold/appeal history are still missing. Do not apply a control-log period to denial correspondence or confuse the separate Privacy Act category with FOIA.

Full-grant correspondence and specified unappealed no-record/description/fee responses have separate two-year instructions. Official copies of requested records have a later-of rule involving the underlying record class and related FOIA request. A correspondence disposition does not establish the fate of the original radar/audio record. [FAA physical pp. 8-9](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/order/finance/1350-15C/media/chapter4.pdf#page=8).

## A concrete file and media-label route

The previously acquired **JO 8020.16A**, December 27, 2010, describes a complete FOIA file separate from the accident file, including released and withheld records. Its continuation permits retaining outside-media-label copies when the media cannot be duplicated, to identify what was released and where to obtain it. Paragraph 102(c) separately describes radar extraction media, originals and holds. [Physical pp. 47-48 / printed 36-37, §§ 100-102](https://www.faa.gov/documentlibrary/media/order/jo8020.16a.pdf#page=47).

This supplies a specific target: the complete FOIA file, medium-label copy, source location and associated hold/release/custody records. It does not prove that an applicable file or label was created, that this 2010 instruction governed the 2007 request, or that underlying 2001 data survived.

## Conditional calendar check, not an actual disposal finding

For illustration only, assume a reply occurred on **August 16, 2007**, the log date for request 2007006970, and the six-year unappealed-denial category applied without an earlier applicable exception. Its six-year anniversary would be **August 16, 2013**, after the **August 5, 2011** cover letter. Thus ordinary expiry under that assumed category/date cannot by itself explain exclusion in that scenario.

The log date is not verified as a reply date; the later letter date is not the database-query date. The response, record class and exception/hold history are unacquired. Separate hypothetical five-/six-year control and two-year grant branches are retained in [SOURCE-PINS.json](SOURCE-PINS.json). None establishes the actual 2007 outcome, survival, unlawful destruction or the reason for the five-letter selection.
