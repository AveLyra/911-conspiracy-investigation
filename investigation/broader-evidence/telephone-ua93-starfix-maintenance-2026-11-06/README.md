# Flight 93 Star-Fix maintenance desk records

November 6, 2026 UTC · TEL02–TEL04 / AUD016-A1.

Two National Archives scans preserve the **November 21, 2003** Commission interviews of a Star-Fix desk operator and the maintenance manager. The operator describes an attempted transfer and a supervisor taking over at the desk. The manager says he made **two sets of notes**, one during the call and one afterward. He recalls turning one set over to the FBI and sending what he had to United's lawyers. The memorandum leaves unclear which set went to the FBI. The two note sets and their delivery records are the most direct leads for testing when the desk answered and the manager took over. [Operator memorandum, pp. 2–3](https://nara-media-001.s3.amazonaws.com/arcmedia/9-11/MFR/t-0148-911MFR-00010.pdf#page=2) · [Manager memorandum, p. 2](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/legislative/rg-148/2524787/2610691_Belme.pdf#page=2)

## Reproducible time check

The previously transcribed May 13 briefing gives **9:32:29 / 95 s** at PDF p. 3, block 7 row 2, and **9:35:40 / 353 s** at p. 2, block 6 row 1. If the printed labels are start times on the same clock and the durations run continuously from those starts, the nominal intervals end at **9:34:04** and **9:41:33**, with **96 seconds** between them. The printed **8:32:39 / INT** carrier counterpart candidate remains a separate earlier observation. This calculation checks the briefing labels only. [May 13 briefing, pp. 2–3](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/legislative/rg-148/2524787/t-0148-911MFR-00217.pdf#page=2) · [Earlier carrier-clock comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/6609c84408b4e4f0d175b6d96be372412227bb61/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-ua93-airfone-clock-attachment-2026-11-06/README.md)

Using Pacific and Eastern civil time on September 11, **6:30** and **6:40** convert to **9:30** and **9:40 EDT**. Against the **9:35:40** candidate start, the nominal differences are **−5:40** and **+4:20**, respectively. **9:40** falls within the calculated candidate interval. Whether the manager's estimate marks the start, his takeover or an unrelated clock reading remains open. The operator's event-relative account remains as stated. No clock was synchronized and no witness account was selected as controlling.
