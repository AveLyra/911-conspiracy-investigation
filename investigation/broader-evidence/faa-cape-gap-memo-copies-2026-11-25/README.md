## Observations

The [K90-26 PDF](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/e-Other/5-K90-26-Memo-Presidential-Commission-on-September-11%2C-2001---Voice-Recording-Minutes-Missing.pdf) pages 1 and 2 and the [AWA-327 PDF](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/e-Other/5-AWA-327-Memo-Voice-Recording-Minutes-Missing.pdf) page 1 show the same dated **April 19, 2004** subject and substantive paragraph: HIGH AR 1230–1600 UTC, missing **one minute and twenty seconds**, first DAT removed at **1538.37**, second started at **1540 UTC**, and loss described as **almost two minutes**. Retain all values as printed and the agency's tape-change explanation as an account.

The scans differ in presentation: AWA-327 has a handwritten-appearing signature over the typed name; neither K90-26 page shows that signature. K90-26 includes a document-control footer on both pages, and page 2 adds a concurrence-routing column and an “OFFICIAL FILE COPY” footer. These visible distinctions do not establish which version was created, signed, sent or received first, or authenticate a signature. The content comparison is limited to the subject and substantive paragraph; it is not a pixel-level or complete metadata comparison. The closely matching pages are versions of one underlying memorandum, not independent witnesses to the tape change.
