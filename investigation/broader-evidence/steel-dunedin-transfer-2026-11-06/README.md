# Dunedin steel transfer packet and the G-0118v inventory lead

November 6, 2026 UTC · STL02–STL03 / B188 / H005. The City of Dunedin's October 1, 2015 agenda attachment identifies the proposed World Trade Center steel transfer as **G-0118v**. The commission unanimously authorized signing the letter. The posted packet has blank signature lines, and a later city report records a memorial dedication. The transfer's execution, physical delivery and artifact identity each require separate records. ## Unresolved identifier discrepancy

The Dunedin letter reads **`G-0118v`**, including a lowercase **`v`**. The previously transcribed July 2010 Exhibit A records **`G-0118`**, at Clearwater PDF physical p. **71**, printed Exhibit A p. **3**, identifier row **41**, ledger row **163**. A case-sensitive complete-string lookup found **zero `G-0118v`** and **one `G-0118`** in the 531-row July ledger. The 354-row December ledger has **zero** of either exact string. Both ledgers were byte-compared with their previously published Git blobs before testing. [Dunedin letter, physical p. 2](https://dunedin.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?view_id=6&clip_id=1447&meta_id=89946#page=2) · [July ledger, lines 1298–1305](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/35bd9f3190f2126d0aa7ab7307f4b53e3c928240/investigation/broader-evidence/steel-exhibit-a-row-census-2026-10-29/IDENTIFIER-ROWS.json#L1298-L1305) · [December ledger](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/9e9940b96d779c882699c9567be5276a51946c07/investigation/broader-evidence/steel-december-july-list-join-2026-11-04/DECEMBER-IDENTIFIER-ROWS.json)

A parent and child label, a variant identifier, a clerical difference or a coincident stem are untested possibilities. The test measured literal list membership. The item's inclusion under a particular court order, whether the two labels refer to related steel, and the building of origin remain open. The records provide no item-level join to W14A, W14B, C12 or WTC 7.
