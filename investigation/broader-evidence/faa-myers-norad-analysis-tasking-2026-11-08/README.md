# February 17 Myers interview records a request for NORAD’s analysis

November 8, 2026 Eastern. B129 / PD01–PD04. The National Archives’ declassified copy of the Commission’s Myers interview memo records a request that predates the February 20 letter about after-action reports. The memo supplies a date, two offices and a subject for finding the underlying analysis and any follow-up. Its first page identifies the interview and gives the September 29, 2014 declassification date, matching the ISCAP release table. [Interview memo, p. 1](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-047-doc4.pdf#page=1) · [ISCAP release table, entry 2012-047-doc4](https://www.archives.gov/declassification/iscap/decision-table)

On **p. 6**, the Commission memo says Myers had seen NORAD after-action “analysis” that included information about FAA notifications. It says he would not call it an after-action report. It also records that he was asked to determine whether NORAD had conducted an after-action review and to provide it to the Commission if so. On **p. 7**, the memo records his commitment to call Eberhart and obtain analytic work after a discussion of whether Flight 93 could have reached its target. The first passage concerns an after-action review and the second follows a narrower discussion. Keep both scopes open until an actual response identifies the records. These are staff notes of the interview. The date Myers saw the analysis, the call and any delivery require other records. [Interview memo, p. 6](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-047-doc4.pdf#page=6) · [Commitment, p. 7](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-047-doc4.pdf#page=7)

The **February 20 Commission letter** described a broad request under DoD Request No. 1, item 10. The **March 16 DoD response** applied a military-report definition and pointed to weekly indexes. The NORAD enclosure distinguished a single comprehensive report from a series it said had been provided. The February 17 memo adds a particular analysis and a specific follow-up channel to that production trail. Myers’s terminology leaves open whether his materials fell within the narrower March 16 definition. The titles of interim reports alone cannot join those bodies to what he saw. [March correspondence and original locators](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/3324e3ae36db9189a79658db51ef04acb6663219/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-march16-after-action-correspondence-2026-11-08/README.md)

The final Commission report’s chapter 1 notes **238–240** identify aircraft performance, radar and operating assumptions for arrival estimates, interviews for pilot awareness, and a channel recording for the authority question. Those citations identify inputs to specific parts of the Commission’s analysis. They do not identify a return to the February 17 request. [Commission report, ch. 1 notes 238–240](https://www.911commission.gov/report/911Report_Notes.htm)
