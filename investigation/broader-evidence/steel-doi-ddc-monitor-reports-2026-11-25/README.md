## Source observation

In DOI Commissioner Rose Gill Hearn's [prepared statement to the House committee](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-109hhrg35501/html/CHRG-109hhrg35501.htm), the official HTML says monitors' findings were reported to **DOI on a frequent basis**, and **DDC received regular reports**. The corresponding oral statement likewise describes direct reporting to DOI and regular reporting to DDC. The prepared statement also describes the monitors' authority to inspect contractor books and records, and their physical presence. These are the commissioner's retrospective descriptions of the program, not a reproduced monitor report, chain-of-custody record, or proof of full coverage.
