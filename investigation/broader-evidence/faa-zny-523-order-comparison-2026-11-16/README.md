# FAA accident-package custody excerpt: 2000 and 2003 order comparison

## Sources and scope

The [ZNY-523 one-page excerpt](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/e-Other/5-ZNY-523-Document.pdf), SHA-256 `e24316af2315035cf3390b71cb73d16f582670f224be73fad0d1a776cd3e460e`, was compared with paragraph 72e on printed page 316, PDF page 52 of [FAA Order 8020.11B dated August 16, 2000](https://libraryonline.erau.edu/online-full-text/books-online/1177.pdf#page=52), SHA-256 `4a079eef3618704785a50283ca6a28fb26be694191570a7f0b8549893ef8e3cf`; and paragraph 72e(1), printed page 318, PDF page 52 of [8020.11B Change 1 dated May 12, 2003](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Order/8020.11B.pdf#page=52), SHA-256 `8144fe5bdb3badb2131a000875d0a7709036c2b5bcb4f0e804d68a4a0cbe00a4`. Both full PDFs retrieved November 16, 2026; comparison covered the cited passages, not all revisions or the order's complete applicability.

## Observations

The ZNY-523 excerpt starts “The facility preparing the accident package” and ends “Distribute the four complete copies of the package as follows:” without listing the destinations. The corresponding 2000 paragraph has that same wording. It continues with two packages to the regional air traffic division and two to the FAA investigator in charge, subject to the specified review and forwarding instructions. Matching words support a source-text association; they do not independently prove which edition generated the loose excerpt or when it was prepared.

The May 2003 passage instead begins “The FAA facility preparing the accident package” and introduces a numbered subsection 72e(1); it says “FAA facilities shall distribute” the four copies and includes a separate 72e(2) for an FCT facility. Both cited passages contain the permission and custody-memorandum language described in the [earlier excerpt review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/4cb5945ec34e29500003c06ef4f2720713f28024/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-zny-523-package-excerpt-2026-11-16/README.md). The different wordings and numbering are retained as separate observations.
