November 5, 2026 · PD07. The NARA object **t-0148-911MFR-00244.pdf** yielded a nine-page scan matching the title, dates and page count in the NARA index for **NAID 2609836**. This is a candidate predecessor to test alongside later chronology copies, including the October Eastern Region shipment index. - **Two reports about the phone bridge.** Page 2, lines 35–46, distinguishes a Primary Net and an internal Secondary Net. The author records the FAA account as **“there was NO recording of the Phone Bridge.”** The adjacent note attributes to Ken Mead an account heard at a meeting that **“there was a recording”** and that it was sent to DOJ. Preserve both. The second account is a report of what Mead heard. Neither passage identifies an evidence number, channel, recorder, recipient receipt or segment. Page 1's separate observation about no tape or video in the Operations Center does not define the scope of either bridge claim. [MFR pp. 1–2](https://nara-media-001.s3.amazonaws.com/arcmedia/9-11/MFR/t-0148-911MFR-00244.pdf#page=2)
- **Other tapes and clocks.** Page 5, lines 11–16, reports FBI review of FAA tapes at source on **September 12, 2001** for digital capture. It separately describes FAA/ATC and radar clocks as synchronized and command-center clocks as unsynchronized. The note supplies neither a recording inventory nor a measured offset. The follow-up memo says the FAA would provide filtered ATC audio to clarify background conversations. The named audio is a retrieval target distinct from a dedicated bridge recording. The scope and results of any further audio transfer remain open.
