## October 21 talking points

The printed email in Hamilton **02340** displays **October 21, 2003, 6:54 PM** and reports that Hamilton was expected to respond to a newspaper inquiry the next day. It references suggested talking points. The collection separately identifies **02341** as those talking points. The message image gives a reported attachment and an intended response. The actual attachment bytes and any interview or published answer remain open. [Message manifest](https://digitalcollections.iu.edu/concern/archival_materials/8910kj61z/manifest) · [Talking-points manifest](https://digitalcollections.iu.edu/concern/archival_materials/pr76ft70n/manifest)

Both the Hamilton talking points and Marcus item 280 p. 1 describe serious impairment from earlier delay and FAA cooperation after the subpoena. Marcus point 2 ends with **“the agency is cooperating.”** The Hamilton counterpart says the agency is **“making every effort to supply us promptly”** with responsive material. Both point to a separate Gonzales memorandum to executive agencies. Preserve the difference in tone, the positive description of later cooperation and the earlier criticism.
