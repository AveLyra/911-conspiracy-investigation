# Flight 77 telephone hardware and aircraft-specific records

November 6, 2026 · **TEL01 / TEL03 / TEL04 / AUD016-A1 / TC05–TC06**

A reproduced American Airlines maintenance-manual page cited for pre-September 11 deactivation carries **two visible date fields**. The footer says **01/28/2001**. The upper-left header says **REV 62, Jan. 28/2007**. The reproduction also identifies the engineering orders that could test the claim. The revision history and Flight 77's completed work records remain unacquired, so the dates on this page leave the date of deactivation on the specific aircraft open. The saved PDF is a published reproduction of a marked and partly obscured page. Neither date field identifies when the deactivation sentence first entered the manual or when the order was completed on Flight 77's aircraft. A manual could combine content from different revisions or apply to multiple equipment states. Those possibilities are untested. The period carrier-system records separately describe a broader commercial air-ground service discontinuation on **September 1, 2002**. That is a carrier-wide date with a different scope from the state of a single aircraft. The FAA describes an STC as a design approval and the Aircraft Registration Branch as a repository for aircraft airworthiness records received from FAA field offices. A 1998 general-aviation circular discusses Form 337 and expressly excludes the Part 121 carrier maintenance records described in its note. Seek both the FAA historical airworthiness file and American's own dated aircraft records. The comparator shows one route for an original installation record. It supplies no basis to assume that every deactivation step would have generated a registry form. ## Missing originals and tests

- [ ] **Manual versions.** Obtain complete copies of **23-19-00-0** as in force before September 11 and as reproduced in revision 62, the list of effective pages, the temporary revision transmittal, and signed **F0878 / F1463 / F1532** with issue dates, effectivity and supersession history. Check when the deactivation wording first appeared. Verify whether the footer date describes that wording.
- [ ] **N644AA aircraft file.** Obtain the registry's airworthiness index and the applicable original approval and installation, deactivation, removal, reactivation or test work packages, including their dated signoffs. Check specific aircraft and component serials and which cockpit, cabin and network functions each order affected. The Registry query returned **403**. Three FAA DRS routes returned the web application shell. The attachments and N644AA work history remain unacquired.
- [ ] **Call-level join.** Keep the September 20 report, exact zero-duration exhibit fields, the native Claircom and AT&T operator legs, clock definitions, and office-transfer records open. A completed preflight deactivation order covering the claimed call path would weaken a claim that the same path was available. Evidence that a distinct handset and network path remained installed and powered could support its availability. Neither hardware test, by itself, identifies who placed a call.
