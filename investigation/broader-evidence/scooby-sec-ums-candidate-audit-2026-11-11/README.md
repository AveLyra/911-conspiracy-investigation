# Candidate-by-candidate review: SEC trading and UMS release summaries

November 11, 2026. This is an audit of the **visible research-card summary candidates** for reports 28 and 29, not a completed audit of their full tables, attachments, all underlying records, or the remaining 28 reports. IDs reference a private mechanically segmented work queue; segmentation occasionally combines claims or cuts a sentence short. “Record says” verifies the source's statement, not the independent historical truth of every fact the source reports. “Not checked” remains open.

## Scope and method

Compared each numbered summary candidate against the cited primary or preserved records, recalculated the arithmetic, and distinguished source statements from underlying transactions or events. The public SEC packet includes personal and account information; this summary does not reproduce that data. The private candidate register and large local source PDFs are not republished. The separately identified full reports, their omitted tables, the other 28 reports, and the source dependencies marked open still require review.
