# Aircraft call identifiers and the AT&T operator handoff

Public derivative of the November 10, 2026 source review UTC · TEL02–TEL04 / AUD016-A1 / TC05–TC06. An issued AT&T patent for **air-to-ground** telephony names different fields at two stages. Its ground station sends an aircraft-side Call-ID to the call-processing platform. At the described handoff to the operator switch, the platform sends the destination number and the platform's own automatic number identification (ANI). The distinction gives a concrete reason to seek an original handoff record or key map for the Flight 77 calls. This is a design disclosure. The deployed Claircom configuration and the historical operator and receiving-office records remain unverified. [U.S. Patent 5,592,539, issued January 7, 1997](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/5592539)
