# FAA directives on recording and preserving the Domestic Events Network

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD03–PD04. The FAA's December 2010 accident and incident order gives a named route for requesting retention of relevant Domestic Events Network (DEN) material. The agency's companion change guide explicitly identifies the DEN provision as an addition. These are published procedural records to compare against the Inspector General's 2006 preservation recommendation. The FAA response and OIG recommendation disposition remain open. ## What this changes

The documents establish an event-specific preservation route by December 2010 and identify the office named for coordination. This is substantive policy evidence relevant to recommendation 3. The 2006 report's **six-month practice**, the 2008 facility provision's **three-year** consultation point and the 2010 **request-triggered** route have different stated scopes. Preserve them side by side. The word **“recorded”** in a definition gives no authority for a retention period. The guide's addition of the DEN to paragraph 72 gives no proof that FAA adopted that paragraph in response to CC-2006-085, that it covered the recommendation's full trigger, or that OIG closed it.

The order catalog gives predecessor 8020.16 **“Date issued” July 1, 2005** and **“Date cancelled” November 24, 2010**. JO 8020.16A's cover calls the predecessor **“dated September 13, 2005”** and labels the successor **effective December 27, 2010**. The successor catalog gives its own **“Date issued” November 24, 2010**. Preserve the date labels and sources. The original predecessor PDF linked from the FAA catalog returned HTTP 404 in this pass. The effective text and applicable change notices at the time of the 2006 report remain unverified.
