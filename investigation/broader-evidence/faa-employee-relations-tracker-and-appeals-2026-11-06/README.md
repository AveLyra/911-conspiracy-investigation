# FAA personnel records and the Employee Relations Tracking System

November 6, 2026 UTC · **B129 / PD02–PD04**, with **PD01** open.

The period FAA privacy order and its successor identify different records to seek for a personnel action. The **1994 order**, which the FAA catalog says was canceled in December 2008, gives separate handling instructions for proposed actions, reprimands and informal admonishments. The **2008 successor** names the **Employee Relations Tracking System (ERTS)** and the servicing Human Resource Office's paper decision file. Neither directive identifies an actual decision for **CC-2006-085 / 042R0122001**. The 1994 instruction for unsustained proposals and the later description of the servicing-office file apply to their stated categories and periods. Keep both. The older instruction does not establish what happened to the 2006 case, whether management only considered action, or whether a hold or different record series applied. ERTS, GETS, LERIS, the investigative tracker and OIG recommendation records are separate search targets until a native migration or case crosswalk ties them together. An empty or inaccessible appeal index would leave the decision and any informal action open. ## Open records

- [ ] **PD02.** Locate the signed management consideration or action/no-action decision and its dispatch or notice. A proposal, an informal admonishment and a final decision would leave different records. Look for management routing separately from any personnel file.

Locate the servicing Human Resource office identified by a case-specific dispatch or docket. Join any tracking entry to the underlying paper decision before interpreting its outcome. The only ERTS description acquired here is from the later order.
- [ ] **PD04.** Obtain the case-specific record class, amendments or superseding direction and any actual disposition or hold record. Check the period 1350.15 series and national office assignments separately from the 2004 Alaskan supplement. Search appeal or grievance records only as a distinct possible follow-up to a documented action.
