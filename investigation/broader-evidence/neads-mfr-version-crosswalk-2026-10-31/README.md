## What could change the assessment

An actual comparison of the original/Alderson versions could establish which calls and times differed. The redo worksheet or corrected transcript could show accurate correction and propagation, weakening a concealment interpretation while preserving the earlier defect. Conversely, source-supported known uncorrected errors or unexplained incompatible delivered versions could strengthen a specific integrity concern. The present MFR does not settle either possibility.

Native logs and edit history could distinguish ordinary later reconstruction, mixed logging conventions or actual alteration. Orders, routing/receipt records and source clocks could test the attributed headings/readiness/authority accounts. None of those tests has been performed from this six-page acquisition, and a staff summary's assertion is not an independent measurement.
