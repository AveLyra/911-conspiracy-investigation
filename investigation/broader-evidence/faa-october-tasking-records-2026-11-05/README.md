## What the record establishes and where it stops

The Commission staff's description of what managers said in the field and the dated FAA returns supply different views of the production process. They refer to different dates, populations and kinds of records. A later employee question about personal holdings leaves earlier headquarters tasking and facility-wide searches unresolved. A certification, an index line and the words **“YES, TURNED IN”** likewise leave the exact item and the Commission's receipt unresolved. The date on AWA-799 coincides with the date cited in AAT-20 certifications. A signed dispatch or authenticated attachment match would be needed to identify it as the referenced internal tasking.

DOT OIG later reported miscommunications and misunderstandings and said it found no indication of intentional withholding. It also said it needed further contact with Commission staff to complete its assessment of responsiveness. That qualification and the Commission staff's contemporary account both remain in the record. The acquired October records do not establish the cause of every omission or a final correction decision. [CC-2004-003, physical p. 2](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2004003.pdf#page=2)
