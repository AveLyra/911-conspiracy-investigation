# FAA management response and monitoring records in the DFW investigations

November 9, 2026 · B129 / PD02–PD04. Two Dallas–Fort Worth Terminal Radar Approach Control investigations document a sequence of distinct FAA follow-up records. The 2008 report identifies an **April 9, 2008 memorandum to the FAA Acting Administrator**. Its public index separately reports a management reply to the Secretary and a directive for regular status reports. A March 2009 audit preserves a signed FAA reply in its appendix and records the OIG's decisions on that reply. The documents give exact record functions to look for in the Commission referral **CC-2006-085 / 042R0122001**, whose correction and disposition remain open. ## Unresolved differences and limits

- **Recommendation counts.** The April 18 report prints **1–9**, including item 9 on reconsidering a facility award. The June 11 testimony PDF reports **eight** on PDF pp. 8 and 19. The April 9 memorandum and a copy of the package actually answered by FAA could establish which numbered set the reply addressed. [April report, pp. 18–19](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/OIG_Report-April_2008.pdf#page=18) · [Testimony, PDF p. 8](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/WEB_FINAL_6-9-08_revised_July_2008.pdf#page=8)
- **Earlier report date.** The OIG memorandum face reads **February 14, 2005**. Senate Report 110–418 p. 36 attributes the quoted finding to **February 14, 2004** and describes the response as following the **2004 report**. Keep both descriptions. The committee's underlying source citation and transmittal could identify the version it used. [OIG face](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/DFW021405_.pdf#page=1) · [Senate report](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CRPT-110srpt418/pdf/CRPT-110srpt418.pdf#page=36)
- **Event populations.** The April report breaks the 62 events into **39 classified as pilot deviations and 23 as non-events**, including **10 + 2** among the **12** cited suspected errors. OSC describes **62 as pilot deviations** and **11 of 13** cited suspected errors as misclassified. The March 2009 audit repeats the **39 / 23** split, drawing on the OIG investigation. The event-level crosswalk and the OSC case attachments could test these differences.
