## Interpretation and limits

The state report supplies a recipient, a reported inventory identifier and physical descriptors to search in the transfer and accession records. Whether **I-003e** denotes a cut from **I-0003**, a separately cataloged object, or some other relationship remains unresolved. The strings and generic descriptions permit a candidate search but leave the physical identity and any authority for this specific transfer open. The December and July lists have different coverage and label conventions. Literal absence from these two attachments cannot establish absence from the underlying inventory, loss, or improper transfer. The municipal packet reproduces a court attachment. The [previous order review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/3a71d497b6319f72dc24cad316786ff58860f168/investigation/broader-evidence/steel-woodstock-incorporated-orders-2026-11-04/README.md) records a conditional inspection/testing provision. Its application to this item remains unverified.
