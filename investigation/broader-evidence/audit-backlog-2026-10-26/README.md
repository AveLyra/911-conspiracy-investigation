## Completion and provenance rules

Track planned, acquired, inspected, verified and published separately. Acquiring
a report is not acquiring the originals it describes; verifying source wording
is not establishing historical purpose. Keep missing, demonstrably withheld,
lost and destroyed records separate. Preserve negative results and alternative
hypotheses, exact film cut/time or explicit unknown, and the evidence that would
change each conclusion. Connected circumstantial evidence can matter without a
confession; a demonstrated link and independent chains still require testing.

For FAA chronology retain the distinct DOT notification and liaison/bridge
findings. For preservation maintain separate FAA witness-cassette, CIA tape and
WTC steel chains; do not merge purposes. For briefings test the claimed71-item
release using individual dated products, recipients, delivery and actions.
Interrogation dependence is claim-specific, without counting later repetition
or post2004 evidence as independent inputs to the2004 report.

## Publication and verification scope

This publishes the frozen planning baseline. The per-task acquired/inspected/
verified/published flags preserve their native intake meanings; this public
planning artifact does not claim that the historical audits have been completed.
Later local investigative results are outside this packet. Publishing the plan
is separate from verifying its underlying allegations or obtaining every target.

Run `python3 verify_backlog.py` from this folder to check task/priority/source
coverage, packaged hashes and references. The verifier uses existing Git objects
for reference targets omitted by a sparse checkout. It performs no network calls,
source-authentication test or historical experiment. [Requested tracks](REQUEST.md)
retain the exact eight audit-scope blocks. [Manifest](MANIFEST.json) pins the
packaged files; its hash checks are preservation checks, not historical proof.
