# Requested audit tracks — planning snapshot

These exact eight request blocks define the planned audit scope. Source pointers
are not assertions that each linked source has been acquired or verified.

1. PRIORITY NEXT: CIA information hold, claimed FBI notification, and late oversight disclosure.
Trace the original Mihdhar intelligence report/draft, the instruction holding dissemination, routing/access logs, the cable stating the information had reached the FBI, and the later oversight-production record. Identify who made each decision, when, and the records supporting each account. Treat the pre-attack dissemination failure and the draft’s late discovery as separate propositions; test their connection rather than assume it. Preserve the conflicting accounts and the OIG’s inability to corroborate notification.
Starting source: https://oig.justice.gov/sites/default/files/archive/special/0506/chapter5.htm

2. FBI informant handling and payment.
Seek original handler reports, source evaluations, the $100,000 payment authorization/disbursement, and source-closure decision. Determine what the payment compensated and what information the source supplied. The OIG’s payment/closure account does not itself establish the payment’s purpose. Include contrary findings and the inconclusive-polygraph context.
Starting source: the DOJ OIG chapter above, especially footnotes 130–135.

3. FAA/NORAD and Mineta chronology.
Compare original recordings, clock references, operational logs, complete testimony, and successive official timelines. Preserve Mineta’s account beside competing chronologies. Separate reconstruction errors, operational decisions, and failures to correct known inaccuracies.
Sources:
https://govinfo.library.unt.edu/911/archive/hearing2/9-11Commission_Hearing_2003-05-23.htm
https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf
Keep the DOT findings distinct: insufficient evidence of intentional falsehood about hijacking notifications is separate from the uncorrected liaison/communications-bridge response and administrative-action recommendation.

4. Interrogation-dependent planning claims.
Map each important planning assertion to its earliest identifiable interrogation report, then identify independent travel, banking, communications, or witness corroboration. Distinguish reliability, voluntariness, access restrictions, and corroboration. Do not count later reports repeating the same interrogation as independent confirmation, or attribute post-2004 evidence to the 2004 Commission.
Starting source: https://www.9-11commission.gov/report/911Report_Ch5.htm (including “Detainee Interrogation Reports”).

5. Presidential warnings.
Inventory and inspect the individual briefing products: dates, versions, recipients, delivery evidence, warnings conveyed, and subsequent actions. The CIA’s 2026 announcement identifies 71 products; the announcement is not an audit of their contents or proof that every product preceded the attacks.
Starting source:
https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/cia-releases-presidents-daily-briefs-in-commemoration-of-the-25th-anniversary-of-9-11/

6. Evidence preservation and destruction.
Maintain separate chains for the FAA witness cassette, CIA interrogation tapes, and WTC steel-preservation decisions. Trace custody, preservation requests, instructions, destruction dates, surviving copies, and the particular questions lost material prevents testing.
The FAA cassette contained controllers’ recorded recollections; distinguish it from operational recordings.
Starting source: https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2004003.pdf
Additional existing leads requiring passage-level verification:
https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/media/16533/ocr
https://commdocs.house.gov/committees/science/hsy78961.000/hsy78961_0.htm
Preserve limits and contrary evidence alongside documented destruction; do not assume a single purpose across these events.

7. Next-tier financial allegations.
Retain options trades/beneficial ownership, the alleged ISI transfer, insurance accounting, and relevant corporate transactions as separate testable claims. Prioritize identifiable transactions and demonstrable connections over suggestive associations. Starting pointer:
https://govinfo.library.unt.edu/911/staff_statements/911_TerrFin_Monograph.pdf
This pointer is not a newly verified finding.

8. Audit our own evaluation across every track.
- Verify the exact film edition and timestamp behind each attributed documentary claim.
- Recheck “supported,” “false,” and “unsubstantiated” labels against the actual source and its limitations.
- Count independent evidentiary chains, not repeated reports, aliases, hosts, table rows, or calls automatically treated as separate conversations.
- Distinguish records we have not obtained from records demonstrably withheld, lost, or destroyed.
- Scrutinize dismissals and suspicions equally. Record negative results, alternative hypotheses, and what evidence would change each conclusion.
- Keep assistance, foreknowledge, deliberate nonintervention, demolition, and later concealment separate. Connected circumstantial evidence can matter without a confession; its independence and connections still require demonstration.
