## A separate route to the inspection and mill records

NIST summarizes §5A.5 of the 1984 specification as calling for contractor-furnished mill test reports covering chemical and physical properties. NIST says the separate testing and inspection contract referenced in §5A.2.2 was unavailable and describes shop and field weld examination requirements from §5A.12.13. These are specifications and NIST's report of access. The associated implementation and test results remain open. [NCSTAR 1-1A, vol. 1, PDF p. 156 lines 32–35](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-C13-72f0231e04aac8e35991907a17f599e5/pdf/GOVPUB-C13-72f0231e04aac8e35991907a17f599e5.pdf#page=156) · [PDF p. 157 lines 4–8](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-C13-72f0231e04aac8e35991907a17f599e5/pdf/GOVPUB-C13-72f0231e04aac8e35991907a17f599e5.pdf#page=157)

NCSTAR 1-1 says the WTC 7 inspection records were unavailable to its investigation and attributes to the Port Authority the account that the general contractor discarded them after seven years. NIST also says it obtained no documents from the general contractor. Its construction-inspection subsection says the project specifications gave no inspection requirements during construction. Keep that scope separate from the fabrication and weld-testing provisions and from the expert's separately labeled records below. [NCSTAR 1-1, printed p. 97, PDF p. 159 lines 15–22](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-C13-8daa429f4f4e901bcd87da64957b40c9/pdf/GOVPUB-C13-8daa429f4f4e901bcd87da64957b40c9.pdf#page=159) · [Printed p. 2, PDF p. 64 lines 7–14](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-C13-8daa429f4f4e901bcd87da64957b40c9/pdf/GOVPUB-C13-8daa429f4f4e901bcd87da64957b40c9.pdf#page=64) · [Printed p. 103, PDF p. 165 lines 6–10](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-C13-8daa429f4f4e901bcd87da64957b40c9/pdf/GOVPUB-C13-8daa429f4f4e901bcd87da64957b40c9.pdf#page=165)

[Filed appendix, JA-3005, lines 58–72](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/2022-01/Aegis%20-%20Colaco%20and%20Torero%20Expert%20Reports.pdf#page=174) · [JA-3006, lines 10–20](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/2022-01/Aegis%20-%20Colaco%20and%20Torero%20Expert%20Reports.pdf#page=175)

**Interpretation.** The expert's list identifies reported copies and gives specific identifiers for requesting or searching for them. The list and NIST's access account can both be true if the records are different or if copies survived outside the general contractor's files. Those possibilities are untested. The list supplies no basis for choosing a catalog explanation, identifying the documents NIST used, determining actual retention or disposal, or judging steel performance or collapse causation. Exact public searches for the two Cantor inspection ranges and the scan base name located the filed list in the scoped results, with the underlying pages and scan files still outstanding.

**Extraction variance.** On JA-3005 the printed transfer-truss calculations date is visually read as `1984`. The held PDF text layer at p. 174 line 16 yields `1964`. The image and extracted reading are preserved as distinct observations. The underlying calculation is unacquired.
