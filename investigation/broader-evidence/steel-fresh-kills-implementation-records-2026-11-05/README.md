## Reported placement decision and date differences

The September 2006 DSNY plan §1.5.2 says a design draft went to DEC on **April 27, 2001**, DEC commented in **August 2001**, and the final design went to DEC on **January 4, 2002** after an extension to incorporate WTC recovery work. It says the buried WTC material generally followed the draft design's grading sequence and reports approximately **1.3 million tons** buried. It separately describes a delayed and later canceled waste-relocation contract after reconsideration of placement near WTC material, with clean fill chosen for final cover sub-base grading. The plan names a **January 11, 2002** letter requesting a separate extension for the start of construction. The placement and cancellation decisions now have identifiable record targets. The report does not supply daily receipts, the canceled contract, or the underlying decision memorandum in these sections. [DSNY plan, §§1.3 and 1.5.2](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/dsny/docs/about_swmp_attach10_0815.pdf)

The plan §1.3 describes Modification No. 8 as **“entered into in December 2001.”** The 2016 city request, printed p. 3, says it **“was executed and went into effect on January 17, 2002.”** The December 28 executive order, the consent amendment's execution and effective date, and the construction-extension request are separate record targets. The difference between the plan and request remains open pending the executed amendment and transmittals. The closure plan's June 2002 receipt wording remains alongside the later regulatory draft's July 15 last-acceptance description. ## What was verified and what remains open

The native NYC Open Data response returned **249 distinct document numbers**. The source pins give its access date, SHA-256, selected rows and search scope. The two engineering PDFs and the 2006 plan were read in selected web text ranges. Direct PDF retrieval for items 204, 205 and 213 returned 403. The inferred public exhibit filename for No. 9 returned 404 and did not establish an actual exhibit URL. The report and catalog metadata are separate evidence layers.
