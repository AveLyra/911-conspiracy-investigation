## What this means and does not establish

The verified addition is narrow: the public reproduction presents inconsistent date fields across its first page and continuation headers. Confidence is high in that visible reading; the native letter's date and the reason for the discrepancy remain unknown.

Possible causes include drafting/header reuse, different page versions or later assembly/reproduction differences. These are untested alternatives, not findings. The observations do not establish backdating, fabrication, coordination, concealment or an error in NIST's collapse calculations. An innocuous document-production issue could explain the record; native versions and transmission records are needed to test that possibility alongside the others.
