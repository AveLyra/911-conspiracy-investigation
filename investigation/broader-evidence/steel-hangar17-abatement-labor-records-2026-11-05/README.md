## Dates and sample scopes to preserve

- **Hangar 17 contracts.** The prosecutor's **February 2002 to February 2004** period, the Board's **October 24, 2002** authorization with ratification authority, and the Board staff's **late 2004** expiration of specialized maintenance agreements stay separate. They may describe overlapping contracts, distinct contracts or different milestones. The signed agreements, extensions and daily work records would test the match. [Prosecutor, p. 2](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/doi/downloads/pdf/pr_danyasbestoscase.pdf#page=2) · [October minutes, p. 436](https://dspace.njstatelib.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/1453ab6a-5757-4c57-8258-44f8f2cbec3e/content#page=10) · [May minutes, p. 193](https://dspace.njstatelib.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/c960df66-1aba-40cd-9eb0-140585e3be89/content#page=6)
- **NIST shipping.** NCSTAR 1-3B gives **March 2002** for the start of shipments from recovery yards, while NCSTAR 1-3 gives **February of 2002**. Both mention pre-shipment asbestos and lead checks in the recovery-yard discussion before describing Hangar 17 separately. The two month values remain unresolved. Neither paragraph dates the first shipment of Hangar 17 pieces or links the two programs' asbestos samples. [NCSTAR 1-3B, printed p. 4](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-C13-799fd8322fd23d7bd4af8d96ea7e58b7/pdf/GOVPUB-C13-799fd8322fd23d7bd4af8d96ea7e58b7.pdf#page=32) · [NCSTAR 1-3, printed p. 28](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-3.pdf#page=76)
- **Inventory versions.** The **November 2002 Draft 2** cited by NIST stays alongside the separately reported **October 2004** two-volume inventory, **2006** two-volume catalog and **January 31, 2007** inventory reference. The actual editions and their object-level joins remain open. [Existing inventory comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/d2c9f1e5ddbaf4214ed7010a7916a9815e4451e9/investigation/broader-evidence/steel-port-authority-artifacts-authorization-2026-11-05/README.md)

The sources establish that the Board authorized the program, the District Attorney publicly reported the allegations, and NIST described a separate structural-steel transfer with a usable label convention. The DA's two accounts are from one release. They concern billing and environmental sampling, respectively. The reviewed material supplies no match between the alleged substituted asbestos samples and a NIST steel specimen or structural test. The unresolved February/March difference is a shipment-date question. A shared facility and time period alone leave object identity, building attribution and any effect on NIST's measurements undetermined.

## TODOs and discriminating tests

- [ ] **Charging and disposition records.** Locate the filed December 2004 indictment and the separately reported May 2004 charging papers and final docket entries. Use the December release's references to the May indictment and the scheduled January 12, 2005 Part 85 appearance to locate the latter. Check count-level pleas, dismissals, verdicts or other dispositions before describing either allegation as established conduct.
- [ ] **Actual contract and billing records.** Locate the signed earlier artifact cleaning and maintenance contract, work orders, change orders, extensions, labor reports and sign-offs. Join on contract number, work location, date and service before treating the prosecutor and Board as describing the same engagement.
- [ ] **Environmental-to-object crosswalk.** Locate sample field sheets, chain-of-custody forms, original and replacement lab results, object IDs, clearance records, and any corrective retesting. Match sample ID, sampled material, location and collection date to the cataloged object. A match to a NIST piece would still require its shipment/sectioning record and the laboratory's actual specimen ID and test method to establish relevance to any structural measurement. Document an object-level nonmatch if these records identify different pieces.
- [ ] **Steel movement and dates.** Obtain the Voorsanger November 2002 draft and later versions, the NIST selection list, dispatch and receipt records for the six whole pieces and six partial transfers, and the first dated recovery-yard manifests. Preserve accession changes and exceptions alongside the February/March accounts. Existing Beverly Hills certification/attachments, museum movement records and building-attribution TODOs carry forward.
