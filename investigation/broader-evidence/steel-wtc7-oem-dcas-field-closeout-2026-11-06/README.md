# WTC 7 OEM generator tests and commissioning records

November 6, 2026 · Steel and construction custody · STL02–STL03 / AUD011 / AUD016 / B188 / H005

The City’s files contain a July 31, 1999 operational test of the WTC 7 Mayor’s Office of Emergency Management (OEM) emergency generators. The test memo reports that all three ran at full load and that automatic transfer worked. The same memo says the test used a **temporary building fuel pump** and lists the project’s fuel-oil pump system among items requiring correction. This is evidence of a generator test under a documented temporary setup. The permanent fuel-system test and final acceptance still need to be recovered. [Aug. 2 test memo, PDF pp. 1–3](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000172389.pdf#page=1)

## Distinguish the records

Four three-page City scans of the Aug. 2 test memo have distinct bytes and formatting. All four text layers preserve the July 31 date, a temporary-use reference, a roughly seven-second timing and the concluding readiness statement. Count them as copies of the same account. On copy A p. 1, the printed reference to an enclosed test report is crossed out with a handwritten **“refer to.”** The original enclosure and the H.O. Penn and APT records remain separate acquisition targets. [Copy A](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000172389.pdf) · [Copy B](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000172539.pdf) · [Copy C](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000172543.pdf) · [Copy D](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000174096.pdf)

The October 1998 cover, March 1999 punch-list items, July performance under temporary fuel supply and September controls follow-up describe different dates and scopes. They show why equipment operation, project punch-list clearance, Port Authority approval and the lease’s formal completion process need separate records. The results provide no direct reading of tank-support loads or as-built geometry.
