# FAA primary-net request labels and the computerized event-log trail

November 5, 2026 · B129 / PD01 source check · Public derivative of the separately preserved source study

This study tests whether the FAA's early written chronology, the operations-center fax and the interview cited by the 9/11 Commission describe the same stage of setting up the primary net. It follows the [ADA-30 page comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/f425336303bbad671cb6943ff69941d4148b1aeb/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-ada30-layout-2026-11-05/README.md). It also identifies a separate typed event log that could be checked against the fax.

The **0850 primary** and **0850 tactical** request labels are a documentary discrepancy. The **0920** fax notification and **0926** summary group entry have different wording and may concern different stages. In particular, the State Department's **0928** fax notification is later than the **0926** group entry. The records preserve both. They supply no calibrated times, shared bridge identifier or confirmed entry/exit sequence. The May 2004 memorandum is retrospective and was cited in the Commission's own account, so it cannot be counted as independent corroboration of that account.

## An underlying record and its limits

The interview memo describes two event-recorder positions and says the event recorder used in-house software. It recounts an initial setup followed by a handoff to the primary-net recorder. Staff could pass handwritten notes to the event coordinator for typing. The memo also says the Washington Operations Center lines were unrecorded that day. The event log is therefore a specific target for contemporaneous written entries. The memo leaves open whether the log distinguishes heard traffic from reported traffic, covers every join and exit, or identifies the original time source. It also leaves open separate audio at a different facility. The NARA finding aid, box 6, lists the Weikert, Taffe and Battle folders. The index provides a custodian locator. The published reading view is a reproduction of the interview memo and its native file and page faces were unavailable in this unit. The statements above come from the memo body, separately from the host's generated description.
