## Attribution, temperatures and experiment

- **Building wording.** WPI p. 11 labels the photographed A36 member **“from WTC 7.”** FEMA's introduction says the first piece **“appeared to be from WTC 7”** and its Sample 1 section heading says **“(From WTC 7).”** Keep all three formulations with their locators. The matching close-up connects the presentations, and the overlapping authors and image reuse limit independence. Neither image provides an intake ID or a documented recovery location. WPI p. 59 describes the HSLA column as **“thought to be from either WTC building 1 or 2.”** The two steel groups remain separate. [WPI p. 11](https://web.wpi.edu/Images/CMS/MCSI/2005biederman2.pdf#page=11) · [WPI p. 59](https://web.wpi.edu/Images/CMS/MCSI/2005biederman2.pdf#page=59) · [FEMA p. 1, lines 7–10](https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/2017/05/09/AppendixC-fema403_apc.pdf#page=1)
- **Temperature labels.** FEMA physical p. 2, lines 9–11, reports a local steel-temperature inference approaching **1,000 °C**. WPI p. 56, lines 7–10, describes liquid formation at **“about 940C or lower”** depending on interface effects. These passages describe different quantities. Keep each label and value. Neither passage records a temperature measured during the incident. [FEMA p. 2](https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/2017/05/09/AppendixC-fema403_apc.pdf#page=2) · [WPI p. 56](https://web.wpi.edu/Images/CMS/MCSI/2005biederman2.pdf#page=56)
- **Experiment versus specimen.** WPI p. 79, lines 1–5, describes compacted **FeS powder** applied to ground **A36** in air for **12 hours at 1100C**. This is a controlled exposure described in the slide. It supplies a lead to the laboratory run and its comparison specimens. The deck asks how much sulfur was necessary and where it came from, and lists candidate sources as questions on p. 86. This record leaves the historical sulfur source, exposure time and pre- versus post-collapse sequence unresolved. [WPI pp. 76–79](https://web.wpi.edu/Images/CMS/MCSI/2005biederman2.pdf#page=76) · [WPI p. 86](https://web.wpi.edu/Images/CMS/MCSI/2005biederman2.pdf#page=86)
