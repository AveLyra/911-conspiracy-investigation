## DOT cross-references

- **DOT 8000.5A, December 27, 1989.** Section 1 expressly directs readers to **8000.1C** for investigation-report issuance and management follow-up. Section 3(b) calls for an operating administration liaison and notice to OIG of changes in representation. Section 3(f) describes investigation notice, significant developments and provision of results. The normal **30-day** notification in §3(d)(3) concerns a **referred hotline complaint**. Applying it to the 2006 report would require evidence of that routing. [DOT 8000.5A, pp. 1–3](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/DOT8000.5A_Office%20of%20Inspector%20General%20Investigative%20Procedures_27-Dec-89.pdf)
- **DOT 8000.8, January 9, 2001.** Sections 1 and 3 describe criminal-investigation scope, while §4(a) describes OIG authority and recognizes criminal, civil and administrative proceedings. Section 4(c) describes OIG coordination with management on action responding to investigation reports. Section 2 cancels **Chapter II of DOT 8000.4**. Preserve **8000.4** and **8000.1C** as separate orders. That cancellation clause does not identify 8000.1C. [DOT 8000.8, p. 1](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/DOT8000.8_Office%20of%20Inspector%20General%20Investigative%20Responsibilities_9-Jan-01.pdf#page=1)
- **Version references.** The FAA 2007 order's purpose cites **DOT 8000.5** and **DOT 8000.8**. The 1989 order is headed **8000.5A** and expressly cancels **8000.5** dated April 4, 1984. The 2020 DOT **8000.8A** cancellation clause names **8000.5A** and **8000.8**. Keep the identifiers exactly as displayed. The 2020 order supplies later version evidence. Which text the 2006 case handlers actually cited or applied requires the historical routing record. [FAA 1600.38E, §1](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Order/ND/1600.38E.pdf#page=1) · [DOT 8000.5A, §2](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/DOT8000.5A_Office%20of%20Inspector%20General%20Investigative%20Procedures_27-Dec-89.pdf#page=1) · [DOT 8000.8A, §§1–2](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/docs/dotorders/CROrder%208000.8A%20final%20signed.pdf#page=1)

The explicit investigative follow-up reference in 8000.5A gives a reason to seek the historical 8000.1C routing and liaison records alongside FAA's internal directive history. The earlier audit-context citation alone could not supply that link. The applicability of any provision and the timing or outcome of action on recommendations 1 and 2 in **CC-2006-085** remain open. [OIG report, p. 12](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=12)
