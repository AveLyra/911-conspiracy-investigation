# Cape audio: candidate match to timed FAA transcript

2026-11-25. A targeted automated test found a candidate correspondence between the [K90-39 audio](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/b-Voice-Recordings/5-K90-39-High-AR-1230-1400-UTC.mp3) and a [separately released FAA High AR transcript](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/e-Other/5-K90-33-Trans-High-AR.doc). **This is a candidate match, not a human-verified audio transcription or a calibrated clock alignment.**

## Test and observation

A 120-second clip beginning **1380 seconds** into K90-39 was extracted using FFmpeg 7.1.1 (`-ss 1380 -t 120 -ar 16000 -ac 1`). Whisper.cpp 1.9.2, CPU, `-ng -t 4 -l en`, with tiny.en produced a segment at clip offsets **93–95 seconds** containing “three four zero one code”; later, at **100–102 seconds**, it output “don't see them just yet, I'll let you know.” These are unverified machine outputs; the model made conspicuous errors elsewhere in the clip. The raw output is preserved locally, not presented as a transcript of the recording.

The FAA DOC, printed page 2 of 5, attributes a similar sequence to **1253:39–1253:44 UTC**, including “three four zero one code” and “don't see him just yet i'll let you know.” It calls the aircraft PANTA 45. Similar ordering, a specific code and follow-up language support a candidate correspondence, but the text is not identical and source dependence is possible. Human listening and an independent comparison are needed before accepting it. The FAA document is itself a transcript, not proof of clock accuracy, identity of the source tape, complete original audio or independent corroboration.

The hypothesized association places the candidate model segment at approximately **1473–1482 seconds file offset** and the transcript sequence at **1253:39–1253:44 UTC**. Model segmentation, extraction seek behavior and transcript timestamps have unquantified uncertainty; no precise offset, drift or full-file time map is inferred. One candidate near **1253** does not tell us whether **1315–1345 UTC** is represented in the file, whether any silence is missing, or whether the separate **1538–1540** gap is captured.
