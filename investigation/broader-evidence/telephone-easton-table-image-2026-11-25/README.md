## Observation

On physical PDF page 12, a table headed by flight columns visibly places “Call from Lee Hanson (father of passenger Peter Hanson) to Easton Police” in the `UNITED AIRLINES #175` column and the row labeled `RECORDED CALLS FROM OR ABOUT AIRCRAFT (providing information)`. The police call is from the passenger's father to police; the table does not itself say it is a recording of the passenger's call from the aircraft. The visible cell has no recording identifier, time, duration or custody details.

The [FBI officer-interview comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/6527631b0c0f68c4d663935af53644131536a745/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-easton-nh3718-packet-page-2026-11-25/README.md) separately describes an approximately 8:55 a.m. police notification and a recorded police line. The table and interview may share sources; the table does not prove the underlying audio existed, was retained, or corresponds to a particular cassette. Do not count these documents as independent recordings or resolve the separate early and later Hanson accounts. The earlier text-only reading and inconclusive PDFKit position test are preserved.

## Method and limitations

The preserved 26-page [Flight Info Tables PDF](https://dimnaut.info/truther/aircraft/FILES/Team7_Box18_FlightInfoTables.pdf), also identified in the [Commission archive ZIP](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip) as `NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009/911 NARA/Team 7 Files/Team 7 Box 18/T7 B18 Flight Info Tables Fdr- Entire Contents- Flight Info Tables 522.pdf`, was rehashed to SHA-256 `fd1d29832a12b0e07997868abe4208d941806122f7aea075b92eb7ae39efc541`. Physical page 12 was rendered at 1.7× with PyMuPDF, respecting its 90-degree page rotation, and visually checked November 25, 2026. This verifies the visible table position only; it does not validate the classification historically, establish native agency origin, or inspect the source audio. No raw page or personal contact data is republished.
