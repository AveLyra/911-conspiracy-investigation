# December 2001 debris-loading protocol and planned holiday pause

November 5, 2026. **STL01 / STL02**. Four PDF scans from the City's September 11 Document Portal preserve a contemporaneous FDNY plan and copies of a DEP Joint Incident Command summary. They identify the intended loading inspection procedure and a planned holiday break in off-site removal. They provide specific records to look for when joining Ground Zero loads to downstream receiving records. [Previous City routing review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/eefbdc3c134599b89ad3942590aedc37777667f4/investigation/broader-evidence/steel-nyc-debris-routing-daily-reports-2026-11-05/README.md)

## What the plan actually records

- **FDNY Incident Action Plan 87.** The cover gives **December 21, 2001**, operational period **0700–0700**. The **FDNY Recovery Team Protocol** describes a spotter watching as equipment lowered material to the ground before loading it onto a truck. The team would examine more closely when warranted, and one machine could do both lifts if a second was unavailable. The protocol describes this as a way to reduce missed remains and other items. It prescribes GPS plotting for victims, remains, and fire equipment/apparatus, with a manual grid-map fallback. Those location records serve a different purpose from DDC's truck GPS records or an engineering specimen log. A prescribed loading step does not establish execution for any load. [City release, pp. 1 and 8](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000154518.pdf#page=8)
- **Holiday schedule.** The December 21 DEP summary says off-site debris removal and pier/barge operations would stop from **December 24 at 19:00** through **December 26 at 07:00**, a **36-hour** planned interval. It describes a reduced crew operating two grapplers on the pile. The same section says **WTC 6** was taken down on **December 20**. These statements concern the plan and the reported WTC 6 work. Actual off-site movements, on-site handling and building attribution still require logs. [Cover and summary, pp. 1–2](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000154515.pdf#page=2) · [Separately filed summary, p. 1](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000154536.pdf#page=1)
- **Attachment trail.** Both summary copies list the **December 21 FDNY Incident Action Plan**, **December 19 DDC Incident Plan** and **WTC Daily Safety Report**. Plan 87 is a dated candidate counterpart to the first listing, released as its own PDF. The DDC plan and daily safety report bodies remain open. A related one-page cover memorandum is separately released as `NYC-WTC_000154535`. [First attachment list, p. 3](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000154515.pdf#page=3) · [Separate attachment list, p. 2](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000154536.pdf#page=2) · [Separate cover](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000154535.pdf)

## Preserve the text-layer difference

The summary bundled with the cover has **“4 lane”** in its embedded text for the December 26 Church Street bus lane. The separately filed summary has **“1 lane”** in its embedded text. In the first page image the glyph reads **1** with an adjacent mark, and the second page image reads **1**. Retain both machine readings and both visual observations. The underlying OCR process and origin of the adjacent mark remain unverified. The two scan image streams differ in bytes. They repeat the same relevant holiday language and should be treated as related copies, with no independent corroboration inferred from the count of files.
