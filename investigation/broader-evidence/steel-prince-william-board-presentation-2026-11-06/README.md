# Prince William steel arrival and conservation records

November 6, 2026 UTC · STL02–STL03 / B188 / H005

A same-day report describes four pieces of World Trade Center steel being trucked to **Woodbridge on June 16, 2011**. The county's Historic Preservation page separately says it **“acquired four steel beams”** in **2010**. The accounts may concern different stages. This follow-up adds evidence of the reported arrival and pinpoints board records for the transport budget, conservation and display. The Port Authority transfer and individual artifacts remain unidentified in the records reviewed.
