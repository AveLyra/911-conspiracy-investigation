# FAA regional chronology: intranet, data handling and originals

Reviewed 2026-11-25. An FAA regional chronology adds specific data-handling leads to the earlier [headquarters chronology comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/cfb46b9198908c159485070d09aee886a1727d94/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-intranet-chronology-minute-compare-2026-11-25/README.md). It does not establish the identities or ultimate disposition of the recordings.

## Observations

The 14-page [National Archives AEA-2879 chronology](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-e-other/5-AEA-2879-911-disk2-REDACT-SEALED.pdf#page=4), PDF page 1, labels its times local Eastern Daylight Time and calls entries preliminary and subject to change. Its PDF page 4 records these separate entries:

- **1:13 PM:** “AAL11 voice recordings posted on FAA intranet – AEA-505 setting up recordings. No information on the website – reference tapes.”
- **1:26 PM:** “All information to Hqtrs to be via AAT-20 website.”
- **1:50 PM:** “Radar data and WAV file received from ZBW.”
- **1:53 PM:** “No data to be released even internally per Canoles.”
- **2:26 PM:** “FBI requests tapes will be confiscated AEA-500 advised S. Carper not to release tapes.”

Page 5 records **2:55 PM:** “Upload data on AAT site per Canoles – keep originals in facility.” These entries report instructions or information as recorded, not demonstrated execution. Their scope, objects, recipients and sequence relationships remain unverified. The 1:13 entry does not identify the audio content, and the 1:50 entry does not say that the WAV file is the same as the earlier recordings.

## Comparison and limitations

The headquarters chronology reports a **1305** or **1306** notice that Flight 11 voice tapes were on an intranet and would be taken off; its **1324** entry reports Flight 11 data removed, and **1328** calls for all data to be placed on the AAT-20 intranet. The regional chronology's **1:13 PM** posting description falls between the headquarters notice and reported removal; its **1:26 PM** direction to use the AAT-20 site is separately recorded. Preserve the time labels and descriptions without assuming identical sites, files, completed operations, source independence, or synchronized clocks. The regional entry about keeping originals in facilities is potentially contrary to a claim that these entries themselves show destruction. It is a reported instruction, not proof of preservation or present survival.

The purpose of the reported restriction and the fate of the material remain unknown. Preservation, restricted handling and improper concealment are untested alternatives; none is established. No specific call, passenger recording or onboard recorder is identified by the quoted entries. A chronology is not the recordings, underlying commands, server log or chain of custody. The Commission-folder reproduction reviewed in a [prior Item 21 study](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/5d8fb6fe11486f727b824971ff49af8a4f450cb4/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-item21-receiving-file-counterparts-2026-11-08/README.md) contains related chronology material; this new reading of the official copy is not independent corroboration of those same underlying entries.
