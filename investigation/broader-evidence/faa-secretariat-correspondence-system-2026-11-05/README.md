## Named system and search fields

- **DOT/OST 041, Correspondence Control Mail (CCM).** The April 2000 notice places the system with the Executive Secretariat. Its record categories include correspondence, replies, and staff recommendations requiring senior-official action. Its retrieval description names correspondent, referring individual, and subject category. The notice separately identifies incoming date, subject and addressee, and response date and author as useful locator information. Those fields can help distinguish the incoming referral from an outgoing signed response. [65 FR 19554–55, physical pp. 79–80, p. 79 lines 142–179 and p. 80 lines 28–33, 58–65](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2000-04-11/pdf/00-8505.pdf#page=79)
- **Correspondence Control Management System (CCMS).** DOT's November 13, 2008 privacy assessment identifies CCMS as the system used to manage official correspondence to senior officials, describes tracking case assignments, and links it to DOT/OST 41 in most instances. The December 2014 assessment explicitly links CCMS to DOT/OST 041, describes participating operating administrations, and says authorized users can upload incoming correspondence and proposed responses, review edits and comments, and archive the package after signature. It also repeats the older correspondent, referring individual and subject-category indexing descriptions. Search targets include an assignment or routing entry, the incoming item, draft and comment history, and the signed package. A signed package requires separate proof of actual sending and receipt. [2008 assessment, sections on uses and system of records](https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/privacy/pia-correspondence-control-management-system-ccms) · [2014 assessment, printed pp. 2–3](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/docs/CCMS_OST_PIA_Approved_120814.pdf#page=3)

## Citation and retention descriptions kept separate

- The 2014 assessment prints **“70 FR 19554, April 11, 2000.”** The native notice and the DOT public SORN index identify **65 FR 19554** for April 11, 2000. The notice masthead separately says **No. 70**. These values identify the cross-reference; the reason for the difference remains untested. [2014 assessment, printed p. 3, Transparency](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/docs/CCMS_OST_PIA_Approved_120814.pdf#page=4) · [Federal Register masthead, physical p. 80](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2000-04-11/pdf/00-8505.pdf#page=80) · [DOT SORN index](https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/privacy/privacy-act-system-records-notices/DOT-OST)
- The 2008 assessment gives **a minimum of one year** for personal information held in CCMS. The 2014 assessment invokes **NC1-398-80-01** for the record series, describes permanent value and says system records may precede conversion to microfilm. NARA's 2022 inspection describes migrated early-2000s files and says the office no longer saved records on microfiche as the historical schedules described. Minimum retention of personal information, treatment of the official package, and later media practice are separate statements. The migration report does not name its source platform in the reviewed passage. The connection between CCMS and the specific migrated files remains open. [2008 assessment, retention](https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/privacy/pia-correspondence-control-management-system-ccms) · [2014 assessment, printed p. 5, Data Minimization & Retention](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/docs/CCMS_OST_PIA_Approved_120814.pdf#page=6) · [2022 inspection, p. 29](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/resources/executive-secretariat-mai-inspection-report.pdf#page=29)
