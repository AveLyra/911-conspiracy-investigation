## Ground Zero routing and audit records

At the **July 12, 2006** House hearing, DDC counsel David J. Varoli reported **1,642,116 tons** of steel and heavy debris removed during DDC's Ground Zero project. He described GPS installation on trucks in fall 2001, a central database of audit findings, DDC field logs used to cross-check payment requisitions, and KPMG's daily collection of copies of receiving slips in its materials audit. The DOI commissioner's prepared statement lists truck-manifest review and monitoring of the truck GPS system. These are specific record classes with named organizational roles. The hearing contains no produced manifest, GPS export or receiving ledger in the reviewed sections. KPMG's materials receiving slips may concern worksite inputs, so their relationship to outbound debris requires the original fields. [House hearing 109-76, prepared statements, HTML source lines 3998–4003, 4470–4472, 4543–4560 and 4580–4627](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-109hhrg35501/html/CHRG-109hhrg35501.htm)

The DDC total concerns **Ground Zero removals**, the DSNY **1,460,888 tons** concerns reported **Fresh Kills receipts**, and the district opinion's **1.651 million tons** is a **party allegation about Fresh Kills**. A valid reconciliation would require dated origin, destination, gross/tare/net and disposition fields, including alternate routing and any repeats. These three figures remain separate. The reported disposal totals and the prior 10-ton arithmetic discrepancy remain open.
