## Header order and duplicate pages

**Apparent 24-minute reversal.** The first page of ZOB-261 has a reply header of **9/25/2001 12:02 PM** and, below the reply separator, an earlier-message header printed **9/25/01 12:26 PM**. The standalone ZOB-244 also prints **9/25/2001 12:26 PM**. Both times on ZOB-261 were checked on the scan. On a common-clock reading the outer reply would precede the embedded message by **24 minutes**. The visible headers supply no time-zone field or clock validation. An export or rendering difference, a clock-setting difference and other explanations remain untested. The source data needed to distinguish them are the native messages with full header, routing and server-time fields, or a contemporaneous receipt log. The **3:25 PM** reply is on a separate page. [ZOB-261 pp. 1–2](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7597384) · [ZOB-244 p. 1](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7597367)

The machine extraction of ZOB-244 line 15 reads **1359-14002**. The visible line in ZOB-244 reads **1359-1400Z**, as does the visible quoted line in ZOB-261. Preserve the extraction and displayed values at their respective layers. No conversion or comparison to an operational clock was performed.
