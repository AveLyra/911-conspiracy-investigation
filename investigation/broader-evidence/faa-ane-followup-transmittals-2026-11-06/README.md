# FAA response file copy and 9/11 Commission transmittals

November 6, 2026 · **B129 / PD01 and PD07**, with **PD02–PD04** open

The National Archives holds the Commission’s **May 25, 2004** questions and two FAA cover versions for the **June 8, 2004** response in related records. One cover bears a signature mark. Another is labeled **Official File Copy** on **FAA Form 1360-14.1 (6-89)**. Its routing, concurrence and date fields are blank in the scan. The signed cover and file copy supply different evidence about the document. A separate NTSB docket shows the same form with completed entries, making the form number and routing fields concrete search keys for a clearance record. These examples leave the earlier **May 22, 2003** answer and the recommended correction as a separate trail. ## Separate September 2003 enclosure trail

**AWA-316** and **AWA-331** are two different 17-page PDF bodies. Both carry a **September 26, 2003** letter to a Commission staff recipient following the New England Region interviews. Both list a facility bulletin, regional operations log, American 11 accident-file index and FAA accident-file guidance. Both say the full American 11 accident file was being reproduced and would follow. They separately report four cassettes supplied on **September 25** and describe their coverage as Boston Center, Washington Center and Norfolk Tower communications about fighter launches. Keep the reported September 25 tape transfer, September 26 cover, and promised accident-file production as separate events. The letters do not supply an independent receipt or list all four cassette identifiers. [AWA-316, NARA 7601886, p. 1 lines 5–30](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/e-Other/5-AWA-316-FAA-Response-to-Issues-During-ANE-Commission-Interviews.pdf#page=1) · [AWA-331, NARA 7601898, p. 1 lines 5–30](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/e-Other/5-AWA-331-Letter-Items-That-Respond-to-Questions-During-ANE-Commission-Interviews.pdf#page=1)

The FAA covers share 12 verified identifiers and enclosure/transfer phrases. The file hashes differ, and same-index page rasters differ for all 17 pairs at the tested settings. That is an edition observation. It does not establish two transmissions or material content changes. The cover’s promise to provide the American 11 file and its statement that four tapes were already provided describe different records.
