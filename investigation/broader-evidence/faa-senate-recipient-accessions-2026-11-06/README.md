# Senate archival route for the DOT management reports

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD03–PD05. National Archives records document an electronic archiving project at the Senate Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs, the recipient named in three DOT management-report referrals. They also identify the Center for Legislative Archives' accession and box inventory systems. This gives the search a recipient-side custody route alongside DOT's outgoing report-control records. The actual reports and their case coverage remain unverified. [Senate referral calendar, physical pp. 161, 172 and 183](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CPRT-110SPRT46628/pdf/CPRT-110SPRT46628.pdf#page=172) · [DOT outgoing route](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/429beb89b3037587bf03d6f0b9b4cff4a6d78c7f/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-congressional-report-control-form-2026-11-06/README.md)

## Applying the route to the three referrals

The Senate entries for **EC–554** (January 29, 2007), **EC–3707** (October 17, 2007), and **EC–5797** (April 14, 2008) give the referral dates and report title. They leave the sender's creation date, actual receipt date, and report period unverified. The separate House entry **3550** supplies a **March 31, 2007** period for its own copy. EC–3707 remains a counterpart candidate pending an enclosure match. [EC–554, PDF p. 29](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-2007-01-29/pdf/CREC-2007-01-29-senate.pdf#page=29) · [EC–3707, PDF p. 41](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-2007-10-17/pdf/CREC-2007-10-17-senate.pdf#page=41) · [EC–5797, PDF p. 23](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-2008-04-14/pdf/CREC-2008-04-14-senate.pdf#page=23) · [House 3550, PDF p. 97](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-2007-10-02/pdf/CREC-2007-10-02-house.pdf#page=97)

Senate Resolution 474 measures ordinary access from how long the transferred records have **been in existence**. It separately treats previously public material, listed 50-year categories, full-committee exceptions and records barred from disclosure by law or executive order. Adding twenty years to the printed referral dates gives January 29, 2027, October 17, 2027, and April 14, 2028. These are search checkpoints conditional on the records having been created on those dates and falling under the ordinary rule. Actual creation and transfer dates, item classification, and public availability remain open. [Senate Resolution 474, §§ 1–3](https://www.archives.gov/legislative/research/senate-resolution-474.html)
