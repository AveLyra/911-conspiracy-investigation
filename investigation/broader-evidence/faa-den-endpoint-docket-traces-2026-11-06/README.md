## October 2015 — a certified facility transcript

The November 23, 2015 memorandum certifies a **partial** transcription at Atlanta ARTCC. Its stated window is **October 9 at 2300 UTC through October 10 at 0140 UTC**. At **23:30:59 UTC**, a speaker labeled **DEN** identifies the headquarters desk. At **23:31:22 UTC**, the Atlanta speaker reports retrieving tapes. This establishes that the facility transcript documents the exchange and that its speaker described local retrieval activity. The statement does not identify which tapes were being pulled.
