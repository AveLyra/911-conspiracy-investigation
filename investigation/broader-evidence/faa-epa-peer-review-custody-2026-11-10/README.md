# EPA's 2006 review of DOT OIG investigations and the FAA correction record

Public derivative of the November 10, 2026 source review UTC · B129 / PD02–PD05. DOT's FY 2009 and FY 2010 OIG budget materials identify a December 2006 EPA OIG review of DOT OIG's investigative safeguards and management procedures. The two descriptions preserve different names for the standards-setting council. The review's original report, sample list, workpapers and any link to the FAA referral remain unacquired. [FY 2009 plan, physical p. 75, printed plan p. 9](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/docs/oig_fy_2009_budget_estimate.pdf#page=75) · [FY 2010 plan, § VIII indexed excerpt](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/docs/oig_fy_2010_budget_estimate.pdf#:~:text=December%202006)

## What would connect the review to the FAA case

DOT OIG dates **CC-2006-085** to **August 31, 2006** and the previously preserved TIGR status for **042R0122001** records **December 12, 2006** as investigative closure. Those dates are **153** and **256** days after the March 31 endpoint given in the budget documents. The budgets describe the period in which safeguards and procedures were in effect. They do not specify the sampling frame or dates used to select case files. The December issue month alone leaves the sequence of the review and case closure unknown. [FAA referral and recommendations](https://www.oig.dot.gov/library-item/30630) · [Previously preserved case status and limitations](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/055ef54a857b46846d0038109bab7e108970f03a/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-postreport-disposition-2026-10-27/README.md)

- **A concrete comparator.** EPA OIG's signed September 13, 2024 review of the same DOT OIG function distinguishes its operations-review period (**October 1, 2022–April 12, 2024**) from the case sample's closure window (**October 1, 2022–September 30, 2023**). Enclosure 1 describes its review of administrative records, case files and the Investigations Management System. Enclosure 2 reports a random selection of **30 of 135** files and lists case numbers and closure dates. The extracted table contains **30 distinct case numbers**, each with a closure date inside the stated sample window. The randomness and the count of eligible cases come from EPA's account. This later report identifies specific enclosures and fields to look for in the original 2006 review. Historical sampling and case membership remain unknown. [Review scope, p. 1](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/2024-10/EPA%20JI%20Peer%20Review%20September%202024.pdf#page=1) · [Method and selection, pp. 3–5](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/2024-10/EPA%20JI%20Peer%20Review%20September%202024.pdf#page=3)
- **A case-level form to look for.** The later CIGIE Appendix D-1 asks whether the investigators considered administrative remedies, whether supervisory reviews occurred, and whether administrative outcomes and issued reports could be retrieved. It also instructs reviewers on certain questions to answer affirmatively when the file lacks specific information indicating a violation. A completed form would need to be read with the underlying records and applicable instructions. The 2006 version of any checklist and its use on this case remain unverified. [D-1, physical pp. 1–3, items 2, 22 and 32](https://www.ignet.gov/sites/default/files/files/committees/investigation/invprg1211appd1.pdf)

## Acquisition and reading limits

The source ledger includes URLs, UTC access timestamps, hashes and extraction anchors. Both budget PDFs were accessible only through the described indexed representations. The direct and alternate-host responses for them were HTTP **403** HTML, and the historic 2003 standards URL returned HTTP **406**. Response-body hashes identify these retrieval attempts. Document hashes for the budget PDFs and historic standards are unavailable. The 2024 EPA review, D-1 and the 2011 standards were acquired as PDF bytes. This pass used the PDF text layer for those sources, with eighteen anchor checks and a full check of the two sample-table pages. It did not authenticate signatures or inspect the individual case files in the table.
