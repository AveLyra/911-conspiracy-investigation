## What the official pages record

- **Page 11, FD-302.** The face prints **09/11/2001** for transcription, **9/11/01** for the investigation and **265D-NY-280350-302** in the case field. The approximate **9:00 a.m.** statement describes when Olson was told about the World Trade Center attack. It includes his qualification that he did not check his watch. The separate incoming-call paragraph estimates the first conversation at **about one minute**. The next paragraph places his attempt to reach the Attorney General and contact with the DOJ Command Center after that call. He reports a second office relay shortly afterward. These are reported ordering and duration estimates. [FBI viewer, p. 11](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11%20Commission%20Report/9-11-interviews-2001-09-sep-05-of-08/view)
- **Page 12, FD-302a.** The continuation prints the same internal case field and names the interview and date. It records that the second conversation ended and that Olson was unsure whether the calls used a cell phone or the telephone on the aircraft. These pages support the earlier reading of the two-page report. The 2006 release mark on p. 11 is a separate event from the 2001 interview and transcription fields. [FBI viewer, p. 12](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11%20Commission%20Report/9-11-interviews-2001-09-sep-05-of-08/view) · [Prior office interview comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/5031c6dfa73e1f59491f259e00f168c37cb21788/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-usage-report-2026-10-27/README.md)

The approximately **9:00 a.m.** value is not a measured timestamp for either incoming call. The FBI form's office sequence is consistent with searching for an Attorney General line attempt, a separate call to the Command Center, and the second transfer as distinct events. The office switch and Command Center logs are needed to test those joins. Keyton's account of a collect call and a later direct call and Voss's belief that both were collect remain distinct. The official Olson pages leave service and device identification open.
