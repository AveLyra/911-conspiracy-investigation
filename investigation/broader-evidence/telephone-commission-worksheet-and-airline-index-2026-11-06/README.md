# Airline recording index and the Flight 11 ground relay

November 6, 2026 UTC · **TEL02–TEL04 / AUD016-A1 / TC05–TC06**

An airline index preserved in two Commission folders identifies a **4:07** recording of the Flight 11 call to the Raleigh reservations center and two files, **20:50** and **3:51**, for the separate call from Raleigh to airline operations. The Commission's November 2003 interview memorandum says staff had access to audio for both calls. It also records the employee describing the two calls as simultaneous, with her relaying information to operations. This gives specific recording identifiers and a custody statement. The native audio and telephone-system records remain open.
