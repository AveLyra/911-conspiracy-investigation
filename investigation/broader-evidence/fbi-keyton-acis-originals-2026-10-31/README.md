## 2. Field explanations advance the dictionary trail, with explicit limits

Page 45 describes **ANSWER_STATUS 0 = no answer, 1 = answered**. Page 46 describes termination type 0 = normal / 1 = abnormal; termination direction **0 = air, 1 = ground**; reason code 0 = normal, 22 = invalid data, 24 = credit-card denied; and SCP duration as call length in seconds. Its unit/terminal/cellsite/handoff explanations give further configuration targets, not authenticated seat/caller identities.

I initially misread the air-side direction digit as 1 and announced a 1/1 conflict. The closer source-image check shows 0/1. **Do not count that preliminary analyst error as an FBI discrepancy.** [Reading correction](READING-CORRECTION.md) preserves the false preliminary claim and actual validation, without rewriting the source or previous reports.

The CALL_DIAL_TIME explanation on page 45 is qualified: times **“are believed”** to use the originating caller's time zone, described as **“eastern standard time.”** Preserve that wording; no EST/EDT conversion, calibrated offset or individual-row timezone is established here. A proposed loss-of-signal/turn/range explanation for abnormal termination in the report is a provider account of possibilities, not a determination of why any selected call ended.

The previously acquired [May 13, 2004 UA175 briefing, pages 2-3](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/legislative/rg-148/2524787/t-0148-911MFR-00216.pdf#page=2) already supplies field explanations: Connect is setup time in seconds; SCP duration is time connected to the dialed number; operator duration is time on line, with no separate unit in that bullet. It describes names known in United's system and later DOJ additions from interviews. The 2001 report refers to names already in its billing system. These are differently scoped descriptions to preserve and test against native versions, not established incompatible measurements or proof of fabricated names. [Field comparison](FIELD-COMPARISON.json).

The 2004 briefing's Eastern/two-UA93-Central-zone account remains beside the 2001 qualified standard-time wording. No common-row identity or clock correction is inferred. An interview-based field explanation is useful evidence, but does not authenticate installed vendor/version rules, timeout/answer supervision, row allocation, billing legs or real conversations. None of these GTE rules is automatically applied to AA77's separate AT&T/Claircom records. Earlier 35-row counts/long durations/uncertain glyphs are not recalculated or resolved here.

## 3. Keyton: official-hosted counterpart, dates and unresolved collect/direct accounts

[Part02 physical 69](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11%20Commission%20Report/9-11-interviews-2001-09-sep-02-of-08/at_download/file#page=69) records Lori Lynn Keyton's account. The form prints **transcription 09/14/2001** and **investigation 9/11/01**; its dictated-date field is visibly blank. Those are distinct form fields, not automatically inconsistent event dates. About 9:00 a.m. has no printed timezone. It reports approximately 6-8 automated collect attempts, a live-operator collect call, then a later call described as **direct, not collect**, a few to five minutes later. She had **no caller ID** and did **not know onboard phone versus cellphone**. Olson/Olsen spelling variants remain as printed, without inference about identity or cause.

The [earlier mirror physical 48](https://www.les-crises.fr/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/Team7-Box13-Flight-Call-Notes-and-302s.pdf#page=48) already supplied that account.
