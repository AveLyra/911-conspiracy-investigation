## Interpretation and competing possibilities

The NPS account is relevant counterevidence to any unqualified claim that FAA provided no corrected information whatsoever. It is a broad later account of multiple timeline errors, not an identified copy or delivery receipt for the particular 2003 QFR at issue in the OIG recommendation. The 2006 recommendation shows what OIG recommended then; it does not itself establish subsequent noncompliance or later completion. These observations do not establish a direct contradiction about the same document or time. They are preserved together without selecting a controlling account.

Untested possibilities include: NPS refers to a different corrected timeline; the specific QFR was corrected later; or the account combines multiple correction events. Other explanations remain possible. A dated QFR version and transmission/receipt, the evidence used for the NPS sentence, and FAA/OIG recommendation tracking could discriminate among them. Neither absence from this page nor the closing of the investigation establishes concealment or intent.
