# April 2007 FAA–TSA exercise follow-up records

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD03–PD04

GAO's recommendation record for **GAO-07-891R** identifies an **FAA after-action report for the April 2007 interagency exercise**. In a separate row for DHS, GAO describes a **joint TSA–FAA after-action report** for the most recent exercise and says TSA used DHS's **Corrective Action Program (CAP) System** to track exercise issues. The public GAO report carries the number **440616** at the foot of its signed transmittal. These are concrete retrieval keys for the exercise records, their circulation and supporting follow-up. The original exercise report and action records remain unacquired. [GAO status, exercise rows](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-07-891r#:~:text=April%202007%20interagency%20exercise) · [GAO report, PDF p. 9, line 46](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GAOREPORTS-GAO-07-891R/pdf/GAOREPORTS-GAO-07-891R.pdf#page=9)

## Where the supporting records may be filed

- **GAO recommendation file.** GAO's FY 2007 report describes a recommendation database, supporting documentation and review by a senior executive and **QCI** for recommendations reported as implemented. It separately lists three recommendations for GAO-07-891R in its activity appendix. The general workflow identifies database history, evidence reviewed and approval records to seek. Whether particular supporting records survive remains unverified. [GAO FY 2007 report, PDF p. 92 lines 6–21 and p. 158 lines 22–35](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GAOREPORTS-GAO-08-1SP/pdf/GAOREPORTS-GAO-08-1SP.pdf#page=92)
- **TSA/DHS exercise records.** GAO-09-369 distinguishes the National Exercise Schedule, the FEMA Secure Portal for after-action reports and the CAP System for tracking improvement plans. It says it found the three data sources insufficiently reliable for that review. The January 2011 DHS OIG report describes CAP as launched in **2007**, with separate FEMA remedial-action functions integrated beginning **February 2009** and available to that program in **July 2010**. Its footnote dates the failure of the older FEMA program server to **May 2010**. These sources identify separate record locations and a reason to seek original agency copies alongside a database export. They leave actual entry, migration, deletion and retention of any TSA exercise record unverified. [GAO-09-369, scope and methodology and exercise systems](https://www.gao.gov/assets/a289294.html#:~:text=We%20concluded%20the%20data) · [DHS OIG-11-32, PDF p. 6 lines 27–54](https://www.oig.dhs.gov/sites/default/files/assets/Mgmt/OIG_11-32_Jan11.pdf#page=6)

The **DEN Customer Guide** identified in the GAO-05-928T follow-up and the **interagency standard operating procedures reference guide** described under GAO-07-891R have different reported titles and scopes. Their identity or overlap remains unverified. Likewise, the April exercise and GAO's closure entries leave the separate DOT OIG report **CC-2006-085** unresolved. The public record checked here supplies no case-specific cross-reference to its recording-preservation or duplicative-procedure recommendations.
