# DOT recording approvals and the 2006 FAA preservation recommendation

Reviewed November 9, 2026 Eastern. B129 / PD03–PD04. The public release of a separate OIG investigation includes a nine-page fax copy of **DOT Order 1600.17C**. Its visible provisions identify written recording determinations, periodic reviews and an Office of Security route for implementing directives. These are specific records to seek when testing how the FAA documented its Washington Operations Center Complex (WOCC) recording program.

| **pp. 114–115, §§6(f), 7(b)(2)–(3), 8** | The order excludes certain inherent printed or image outputs from its equipment definition. Operating administrations maintain records of the written public-safety and public-service-monitoring determinations. Written delegations and implementing directives are distinct records. These are conditional records and routes. A dated determination or specific authority, approval trail and circuit or installation inventory would establish which category a WOCC or Domestic Events Network (DEN) recording activity used, if covered. An approval alone would leave actual capture and retention unverified. The federal department order and its FAA implementation are separate documents. ## Compare the 2006 findings without merging them

The OIG reported in **CC-2006-085**, physical p. 11 lines 11–18, that FAA then recorded most WOCC telephone lines, including the DEN. It reported **six months** of retention as practice, the absence of an FAA retention policy for those lines, and separate priority lines that remained unrecorded. Its recommendation **3**, p. 13 lines 2–3, asks for a formal preservation policy following notice of hijacked or suspicious aircraft. Preserve those observations beside the 2003 department order's generic records-management reference. The order alone leaves the later FAA implementation, coverage and OIG disposition open. [OIG finding, p. 11](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=11) · [OIG recommendation 3, p. 13](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=13)
