# FAA 2002 accident-package distribution instruction

## Sources and scope

A public mirror of the [FAA/NEADS/NORAD records release](https://www.governmentattic.org/6docs/9-11FAA-NEADS-NORAD_2001-2005.pdf#page=161) contains a forwarded April 25, 2002 FAA message on physical PDF pages 161–162, also reproduced on pages 168–169. The previously acquired 257-page PDF is 9,264,585 bytes, SHA-256 `74a28116bff5aaf19ed796f96856130ae50a90ac2cb493d5bf479c3f260791d8`; the source acquisition was October 27, 2026 and the pages were visually checked November 16. The forwarding copies are not independent accounts. The native April 25 message and underlying FBI communication are not established by this scan.

## Observations

The April 25 message says the American 11 accident package had been released and release of the other three was anticipated. It states that copies of the four accident packages required the originating office's consent. It attributes a distribution limitation to information from the FBI, describing forwarding to the FAA investigator in charge and NTSB, and calls for cover sheets restricting distribution and access. This is the FAA author's reported account of FBI guidance; the actual guidance, its scope and legal basis are not supplied by the excerpt.

The following page invokes pending litigation, designates an FAA point of contact and requests that files be secured and access be denied without that person's consent. It describes coordination, avoiding duplication and confusion, operational safety and security as purposes, and says review may occur with government counsel present. These are stated reasons and directions, not independent findings about whether every restriction was applied, lawful, necessary or improper. A separately dated April 30 forwarding message gives specific handling instructions for an AAL11 package.

The [May 16 FAA email](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/e054b5d0f25d7135fb250f1717ed9fcbb9d6a132/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-zny-364-package-access-2026-11-16/README.md) quotes the cover-sheet instruction and says region-specific format and wording were discretionary. That earlier analysis correctly described what the May 16 page did not explain; this additional source supplies the earlier author's stated context. It does not prove a specific denial, suppression, falsification or motive.
