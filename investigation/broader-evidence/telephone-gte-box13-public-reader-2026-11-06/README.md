## How this changes the retrieval plan

The earlier carrier interview records a **November 6 courier package** with a call-activity spreadsheet and seat-location material. The new reproduction shows an **email dated November 6** and identifies its attachment by name. It also shows a September 18 seizure-date field for a distinct item spanning two flights. Preserve the three observations separately. The dates and the references to worksheets give a reason to compare them if originals become available. A common date and subject leave open whether the emailed and couriered spreadsheets were identical. The later **July 2002 updated spreadsheet** and the Newark **Appendix A-3** remain separate targets.
