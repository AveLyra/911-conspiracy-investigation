# WTC 7 OEM drawing transmittal and complete 137-S file count

November 6, 2026 · Steel and construction custody track · STL02–STL03 / AUD011 / AUD016 / B188 / H005

A transmittal dated **August 26, 2003** records Weidlinger Associates sending NIST a CD-ROM of WTC 7 Mayor’s Office of Emergency Management drawings that Weidlinger says it received from Swanke Hayden Connell Architects that day. The letter asks that the project contact be informed when the transmittal is received. The acknowledgment and the original 2003 disc manifest remain open. The transmittal is preserved in a mirrored FOIA release, **WTCI-000137-S.PDF**, physical p. **2**, after a separator on p. **1** directing the reader to electronic media. ## 2003 transfer and 2013 release

The transmittal gives a document-specific route to the repository intake record and the CD image. It describes the source, enclosure and requested acknowledgment. The surviving transmittal and the 2013 release are different stages of custody. The content of the original 2003 CD, its exact relationship to the 2013 release files, and the version NIST used in any particular analysis require separate evidence. The 2003 letter states shipment by courier. The shipping-label image on physical p. **17** was screened at overview scale and is insufficient here to verify recipient acceptance.

## Verification scope

The new retrieval obtained ten previously unexamined members. The complete ledger covers all **14** non-directory archive members, including the four already held. Each new range was checked for status, exact length and range, and a matching entity tag. The local ZIP member name, compression method, CRC32 and decoded byte length were checked against the central directory. Saved byte lengths and SHA-256 hashes were rechecked. PDFium **5.14.0** counted PDF pages, and libtiff counted TIFF directories without decoding the full drawings. Poppler independently reported **24** and **26** pages for the two numbered PDFs. The two independently generated ledgers compared byte-for-byte. TIFF tag warnings involved scanner metadata fields. The first census attempts stopped at image-library limitations and were superseded by the successful count. Both disc labels and the transmittal were visually read. Both numbered PDFs were screened page by page in contact sheets, with selected title blocks read at higher scale. Their embedded text returned no selected markers, so text extraction cannot stand in for a visual reading. A full-scale review of every drawing note and all TIFF imagery remains unperformed.
