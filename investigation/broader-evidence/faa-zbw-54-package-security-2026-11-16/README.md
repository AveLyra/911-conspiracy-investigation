# Boston accident-package security memorandum and route slip: ZBW-54

## Source and scope

The [National Archives scan](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/3-AAL-11/e-Other/3-ZBW-54-39-Route-slip-5-3-02-package-REDACT-SEALED.pdf), [catalog 7598060](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7598060), was retrieved November 16, 2026: seven pages, 574,954 bytes, SHA-256 `8297404839ff061f006b8bfec88fe17cdbac399f91a7c9efb4469712dd73e103`. The [finding aid](https://www.archives.gov/research/foia/faa-finding-aid-60-71) identifies it as a May 3, 2002 memorandum and route slip concerning accident-package security. PDF pages 1–2 were visually inspected; all pages were text-extracted. No redacted contents or personal contact details are reproduced here.

## Observations

The May 3 route slip names AAL11 package `ZBW-ARTCC-148` and says two copies are attached. It says a memorandum limiting distribution and access to the FAA Accident Investigation Division and NTSB was placed inside each copy, attributing the restriction to FBI direction. Page 2 is an actual memorandum dated May 3 and addressed to the AAL11 accident package; it states the same limitation and attribution. Page 3 displays another rendering of a memorandum; its independent provenance was not determined. The scan does not include the complete packages or proof of delivery, receipt or application to other packages.

Pages 4–7 reproduce an April 2002 message chain concerning all four packages; selected content was previously reviewed in the [separate release-email crosswalk](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/f70b7fa366a9190f1be2116fdd3ded9566eb2218/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-zbw-53-release-crosswalk-2026-11-16/README.md). These repeated copies are not independent accounts. The current scan narrows the earlier TODO for an actual cover/memorandum: it supplies a visible AAL11 package memorandum, not the complete cover-sheet or access-decision history. The FBI direction is reported by FAA documents; its underlying terms and legal basis remain unverified. The source alone does not establish improper withholding or motive.
