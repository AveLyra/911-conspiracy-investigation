# The May 2003 NORAD briefing charts and the later staff notes

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD01–PD04. This review acquired four publicly posted chart reproductions associated with the May 23 hearing. It compares the selected cells with the Commission’s transcript and the February 4, 2004 staff notes. The charts supply a version to check against the February revision. The native deck and the revised chronology with its transmittal remain open.

## What the sources say about compilation

- In May, the transcript attributes to Scott **“the times on this chart come from our logs.”** He also distinguishes a time entered in a log from an event time. McKinley says other agencies could have different times. [Commission transcript, saved HTML lines 228 and 246](https://www.911commission.gov/archive/hearing2/9-11Commission_Hearing_2003-05-23.htm)
- The February staff notes say Scott **“never reviewed the logs”** and record that he never reviewed the NEADS tapes. They describe an effort to square a NEADS briefing/timeline with the September 18, 2001 NORAD timeline. Page 8 records no review of CONR chat logs before the hearing and a qualified recollection that some times may have come from the Internet, possibly FAA websites. A briefing could have carried times drawn from logs that Scott had not personally reviewed. That is an untested source path. No entry-by-entry source worksheet was recovered. [NARA staff notes, physical pp. 7–8](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2011-048-doc36.pdf#page=7)
