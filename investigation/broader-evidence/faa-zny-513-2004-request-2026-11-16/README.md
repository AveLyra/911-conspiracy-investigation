## Observations

The memorandum is dated September 17, 2004. It states that the originating FAA office processes September 11 data for consolidation onto an FAA web page, and asks for four aircraft accident packages: UAL175 `ZNY-ARTCC-148`, UAL93 `ZOB-ARTCC-287`, AA77 `ZDC-ARTCC-212` and AA11 `ZBW-ARTCC-148`. It requests overnight delivery with a signature upon receipt and asks for arrival by September 22. This records a request and stated reason; it does not itself show dispatch, receipt, contents, website publication or current location.

The [ZNY-528 custody form](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/4c305be9a15db31c4e061a4c6d92629823dfe9d1/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-zny-528-custody-2026-11-16/README.md) carries the same UAL175 package identifier but entries dated October 20, 2003. Both dates are preserved: the 2004 request cannot on its face authorize the earlier 2003 entry. The 2003 form does not identify the contents or transfer direction. No inference of lost records or improper transfer follows from the dates alone.
