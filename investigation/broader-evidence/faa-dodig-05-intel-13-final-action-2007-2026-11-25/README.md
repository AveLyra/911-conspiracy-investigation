## Limits and interpretation

The tables report the agency's **aggregate follow-up status at two dates**. They do not identify which individual recommendation or action remained pending, what work had been done, or the eventual decision and completion date. The two entries do not establish continuous inaction between those dates, deliberate delay, or present status. “Normal time needed for implementation” is the table's reported reason, not an independently verified explanation. The classified-description label is historical and does not contradict the existence of a later redacted public copy; do not infer current classification from it. Agreement to change directives and a pending final-action entry are different observations; neither should be treated as a complete implementation record.

These Defense follow-up tables do not establish whether FAA corrected its separate May 2003 response or considered administrative action. Preserve the separately reported FAA and DoD accounts and the NPS/DoD publication wording without resolving them.
