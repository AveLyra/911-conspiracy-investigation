# FAA follow-up records in a separate Office of Special Counsel case

November 6, 2026 UTC · **B129 / PD02–PD04, procedural comparison.** A 2003 Office of Special Counsel (OSC) letter gives an example of what can survive after a DOT Inspector General investigation. It names the follow-up inquiry, management reply, public-file copy, and recipients. The letter distinguishes a plan for corrective action from an adequate answer on accountability. This is **OSC DI-02-0207**, the FAA Red Team disclosure. Its statutory route and parties are separate from the Commission referral **CC-2006-085 / 042R0122001**.
