# DOJ office telephone records during the FTS2001 transition

November 6, 2026 UTC · TEL03–TEL05 / AUD016-A1. The Flight 77 telephone review leaves the receiving office's line, switch records and carrier billing open. The April 26, 2001 House oversight hearing and its written follow-up identify a documented DOJ telecom transition and a location-level order system. These are search routes for the office leg. They do not identify the Solicitor General's line or the operator in the witness accounts. ## What this adds

An FTS2001 rate on an invoice could coexist with an FTS2000 service under the written answer's description. To identify which service carried an office leg, join the location or trunk to a completed service order and its effective time, then compare the carrier or switch record. A billed collect charge, an attempted operator connection and a call handled by an internal office transfer could appear in different records or fields. Which systems captured those events at this office remains untested. The earlier FBI accounts describing a direct second call and a belief that both were collect remain separate. The transition records do not adjudicate them. [Earlier recipient accounts and evidentiary limits](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/c765a0a11042c7609415d09eb43c9dc1cf982d74/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-aa77-event-census-2026-11-02/README.md)

The federal billing audit register separately lists **A030136**, an interim audit dated **July 8, 2003**, for Sprint contract **GS00T99NRD2001** and presubscribed interexchange carrier charges. The register gives a contract-file locator. Its stated subject is charges. Any underlying office call-detail records would require separate verification.
