## Access route and wording

NARA distinguishes executive-agency FAA holdings from Commission legislative records. Unprocessed FAA records can be requested under FOIA using specific folder/item locators; not all are digitized. Commission records use archival reference/review requests, and classified withdrawn items may require mandatory declassification review. NARA expressly says Commission legislative records are not subject to FOIA. [FAA collection guide](https://www.archives.gov/research/foia/faa), [Commission FAQs](https://www.archives.gov/research/9-11/faqs.html).

For FBI originals, search the Vault before requesting nonpublic agency-held versions. DOJ processes records by component and provides a referral route when the custodian is uncertain. Seek existing records, attachments, versions and metadata; do not ask an agency to generate a new transcript, calculation, investigation or explanation. [FBI records guidance](https://www.fbi.gov/how-we-can-help-you/more-fbi-services-and-information/freedom-of-information-privacy-act), [DOJ routing and existing-record guidance](https://www.justice.gov/oip/make-foia-request-doj), [FAA request guidance](https://www.faa.gov/foia/foia_request).

Remove only request categories actually satisfied by complete accessible copies. An already-public derivative does not necessarily satisfy a separate native-source or materially different-version category. Preserve segregability, actual transfer/disposition evidence and distinct no-record/withholding/referral statuses. An unsuccessful search does not establish destruction. A demonstrated intentional alteration would establish that particular act; a broader conspiracy still requires independent evidence of coordination, purpose and consequence.
