## AWA-922's table and narrative preserve a conflict

- Physical **p. 5** lists **0916:00** for a report attributed to NEADS logs and immediately qualifies it with **“We believe this time to be inaccurate”**, because it precedes the distress indications. That page gives probable knowledge of distress as **0931:00**. The table on **p. 1** instead has **10:08*** for notification and **09:32** for probable knowledge of distress. The p. 5 account gives no 10:08 notification row. The date, author and assembly history of the five-page file are needed to attribute the note and establish when anyone saw it.
- Physical **p. 4** still gives **0924:00** under AAL77 and attributes the FAA notification to NEADS logs. This matches the table's minute, with the prior AWA-619 and actual NEADS ledger observations preserved separately. The Commission reports **09:34** for a notification that American 77 was missing, and **10:07** for the United 93 hijacking report. Those are the Commission's findings. The present comparison did not check primary audio or synchronize clocks. [AWA-922, pp. 4–5](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/e-Other/5-AWA-922-Table-First-Critical-Signs_Notification-Chronology.pdf#page=4) · [Commission chapter 1](https://www.911commission.gov/report/911Report_Ch1.htm)

The date-marked page establishes that the **NA** variant carries a September 18, 2001 revision label. The undated caveat establishes that a compiler questioned the 09:16 attribution in the available copy. The records leave open whether the same person created each table, the direction of revision across the files, and who knew which version when preparing or correcting later public and congressional statements. The OIG reported evidence of inaccuracy and said its investigation did not find evidence of knowing falsehood, purposeful omission or intentional failure to correct the statements. That investigative finding remains separate from the direct document readings. [CC-2006-085, printed pp. 5–7](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=5)
