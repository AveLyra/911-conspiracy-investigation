## What a one-hour offset would predict

The June 2002 interview gives the exact two raw time labels that also appear in the earlier summary transcription. Keep the summary's **INT** label separate from the interview's **Central Time** description. The following comparisons pair candidate rows using their previously transcribed seconds fields. They do not use a native call ID, and the duration fields in the two documents can share a source.

| Earlier summary | May 13, 2004 Commission memo | Conditional result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **8:30:32 / INT**, summary row 1, SCP **28** | **8:30:32**, **28 seconds**, physical p. 3, block 11, row 1 | Exact printed-time agreement. If a uniform **+1 hour** were applied to this pair, the prediction would be **9:30:32** and the memo would differ from that prediction by **−3,600 seconds**. |
| **8:32:39 / INT**, summary row 2, SCP **95** | **9:32:29**, **95 seconds**, physical p. 3, block 7, row 2 | The printed labels differ by **3,590 seconds**. The **+1 hour** prediction is **9:32:39**, leaving **−10 seconds**. |

[Commission memo, physical p. 3](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/legislative/rg-148/2524787/t-0148-911MFR-00217.pdf#page=3) · [Preserved earlier readings](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/681797a20a39d8c30e367c2220be15a191e3a409/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-may13-briefings-2026-10-29/PRIOR-READINGS.json)

A single one-hour shift does not reproduce both memo strings under these candidate pairings. The comparison leaves open whether the memo used different time presentations, whether a row was transcribed or paired incorrectly, and how the carrier actually set its clocks. The time differences are arithmetic on the reported labels. Neither is a calibrated event-time residual.

An independent civil-time source adds a distinction. DOT's 2006 rule lists **Fort Wayne as EST in April and July 2005**, and **EDT** on the corresponding 2006 dates. It explains that eastern Indiana's then-year-round standard time coincided with Chicago's summer clock. Preserve the interview's **Central Time** description beside DOT's **EST** label for the city and the different dates. Equal summer clock displays could coexist with different zone names. The rule does not give the 2001 station address, the switch time base or how the carrier's export labels were generated. [71 FR 3229, physical p. 2, Fort Wayne column, key and explanatory paragraph](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2006-01-20/pdf/06-563.pdf#page=2)
