## Observations kept separately

1. The October 30 report says the city transportation department had moved about **600 tons** of WTC-collapse debris from a Battery Park City park to a ballfield at West and Chambers streets. It reports that the Office of Emergency Management (OEM) requested advice on testing and disposal, and records: “OEM states that FBI does not need this debris.” It also records an interagency site visit arranged for October 31. This is the report's account of OEM's position; it is not the underlying FBI communication.
2. The October 31 report updates the same issue: “FBI directed material to be brought to Fresh Kills landfill. No involvement by DEP required.” It repeats the earlier description and visibly strikes through the sentence attributing the no-need statement to OEM. The scan preserves both statements; the source does not supply the underlying instruction or establish when a shipment occurred.
3. Separately, the October 30 report attributes to a contractor that most debris at 7 WTC had been removed and the site was at grade level. It does not identify the 600-ton lot as 7 WTC material or establish the composition of that lot.

## Interpretation and limits

The successive reports document a change in the written account and a specific reported disposal direction. A change in instructions or a correction to the earlier account are possible explanations, but neither has been tested against the original communications. The two accounts do not establish that debris was shipped, whether it was inspected or tested, whether any particular structural steel was included, or whether evidence was improperly destroyed. The 600-ton estimate concerns this described lot, not all WTC debris. The reports do not establish intent or a conspiracy.

## Open records / TODOs

- Obtain OEM's original request and supporting communications, the FBI instruction and any correction, and records of the reported October 31 site visit.
- Obtain inventories, photographs, test results, load and weigh tickets, transport and Fresh Kills receiving records, and any subsequent disposition or retention decisions for this lot.
- Determine whether the lot contained identifiable structural steel or material attributable to WTC 7, without assuming either. Compare the records' dates and handling instructions before assessing the relationship between the two reports.

The missing originals remain open dependencies; this finding is a route for further investigation, not a conclusion about them.
