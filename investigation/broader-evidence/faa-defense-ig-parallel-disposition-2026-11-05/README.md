## The 2005 Defense capability report and its follow-up

- **Opening and report identifiers.** The September 2004 announcement gives **D2004-N-0229** for a preliminary review of Defense testimony. The May 27, 2005 capability report gives **05-INTEL-13**, project **D2004-DINT01-0229.001**. The latter describes the testimony inquiry as its initial objective and reserves that issue for a later report. Keep both project labels. An internal project register would show their exact administrative relationship. [Opening announcement, physical p. 5](https://library.rumsfeld.com/doclib/sp/3357/2004-09-03%20From%20Joseph%20E%20Schmitz%20re%209-11%20Commission%20Referral%20Regarding%20Inaccurate%20Public%20Statements%20with%20Attachments.pdf#page=5) · [Executive summary and objectives, packet pp. 115 and 120](https://www.governmentattic.org/6docs/DOT-OIG-FAA9-11_2001-2006.pdf#page=115)
- **Two recommendations to a named office.** The capability report recommends that the Assistant Secretary of Defense for Networks and Information Integration/Chief Information Officer standardize recording and radar storage where possible at designated sites, and develop incident investigation and reporting procedures that include an independent team and archiving. The May 27 transmittal cites DoD Directive 7650.3 and asks that office for signed comments by **June 27, 2005**, including concurrence or disagreement and action dates. The packet includes an Appendix D distribution list. [Packet transmittal, p. 113](https://www.governmentattic.org/6docs/DOT-OIG-FAA9-11_2001-2006.pdf#page=113) · [Recommendations, p. 127, extracted lines 20–30](https://www.governmentattic.org/6docs/DOT-OIG-FAA9-11_2001-2006.pdf#page=127) · [Distribution, pp. 133–134](https://www.governmentattic.org/6docs/DOT-OIG-FAA9-11_2001-2006.pdf#page=133)
- **Comments and version fields.** The transmittal says **“Management did not comment on the draft report.”** The summary says the draft issued **March 18, 2005** and separately describes unsolicited concurrence from the **Director, Joint Staff**, whose comments appear in the packet. The report requests comments from **ASD(NII)/CIO**. Preserve both statements and the roles. Also preserve the **“DRAFT REPORT”** header on packet p. 128 within the dated report sequence. That header leaves the appendix's version history open. Neither the comments from Joint Staff nor the distribution list verifies a signed response or receipt from the requested office. [Management comments, p. 116, lines 1–6](https://www.governmentattic.org/6docs/DOT-OIG-FAA9-11_2001-2006.pdf#page=116) · [Appendix A, p. 128](https://www.governmentattic.org/6docs/DOT-OIG-FAA9-11_2001-2006.pdf#page=128) · [Joint Staff enclosure, p. 135](https://www.governmentattic.org/6docs/DOT-OIG-FAA9-11_2001-2006.pdf#page=135)
- **Later status.** The DoD OIG's April–September 2007 report to Congress lists **05-INTEL-13** as **“Incident Reporting and Forensic Capabilities (U)”**, with **ASD(NII)** as action office. Under *Reason Action Not Completed* it records **“Normal time needed for implementation.”** The action-description field reads **“Report is classified.”** That row identifies unfinished action at the reporting snapshot. It does not identify which recommendation remained open, establish the actual reason independently, or give a closure date. The short title and original title are preserved. [Semiannual report, printed p. 142](https://www.dodig.mil/Portals/48/Documents/SAR/SAR-1-apr-2007-30-sep-2007.pdf?ver=2017-02-02-144108-870)

Preserve the broad statement and specific recommendation separately.
