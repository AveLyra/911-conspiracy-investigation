## Selected differences on the first page

The three mirrored copies preserve separate 0920 rows for the NMCC notification and the primary-net request. In the Team 8 and New York copies, handwriting by those rows reads `by primary net`. In the Team 7 image, different handwriting in that area appears to read `Continuous open line`. The crossed-out wording, authorship and annotation dates remain open. The printed request, the recorded notification and the handwriting are separate observations. A native bridge log or recording would test when participants joined and what they received.
