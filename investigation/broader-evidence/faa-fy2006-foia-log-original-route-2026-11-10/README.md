# FAA FY 2006 request-log controls for the September 11 record trail

Public derivative of the November 10, 2026 source review. B129 / PD01–PD04, with REC01 and REC03 acquisition leads. A public reproduction of the FAA's FY 2006 request log gives five exact controls for NTSB referrals of FAA records concerning September 11. One specifies a UA93 CD. Separate entries identify communications, chronology and AA77 requests. These are leads to original packets, responses and transfers.
