# Exhibit 701: an internal account, specific serial leads and an August authorization request

The court-hosted September 10 exhibit 701 is **two pages**, addressed from Minneapolis to Minneapolis, attention its Special Agent in Charge, with routine precedence. All two pages were read. A directly relevant August 18 control, exhibit 472, was also acquired and all **26 pages** read; six selected August memorandum pages were reinspected. This adds primary-source detail to the earlier comparison without establishing actual headquarters replies or warrant outcomes.

## What September 10 actually records

[Exhibit 701](https://coop.vaed.uscourts.gov/moussaoui/exhibits/defense/701.pdf), physical page 1, says its purpose is to document Minneapolis requests of FBI headquarters and references **199M-MP-60130, serials 3, 13 and 19**. The same page attributes French-source information to an EC identified as serial 19. These are exact retrieval pointers; the three referenced records are not reproduced by that reference list.

Physical page 2 states that Minneapolis undertook to obtain criminal search warrants. This is an account of an investigative undertaking, not an attached application, issued warrant, headquarters reply or completed search. Its literal wording includes **“preventing rather than forestalling a terrorist attack”**. That wording is preserved without substituting an intended word or deriving motive from it. The typed approval field on page 1 is also preserved; it cannot be equated with OIPR clearance, a prosecutor's decision or judicial authorization.

The visible body is much narrower than a detailed request-and-response chronology a reader might expect from the synopsis. There is no visible page-number gap: an opening sheet and printed page 2. The short body alone does not establish an omitted section, a complete native record or improper removal. Source/version and completeness questions remain TODOs.

## The available August control supplies the actual requested routing

[Exhibit 472](https://coop.vaed.uscourts.gov/moussaoui/exhibits/defense/472.pdf), dated August 18, is a 26-page communication with immediate precedence, addressed to counterterrorism and other listed offices. Physical pages **1, 23 and 25** request that headquarters obtain DOJ Office of Intelligence Policy and Review authorization for Minneapolis to contact the Minnesota U.S. Attorney's Office for a parallel criminal matter. Page 23 connects that request with prospective prosecution, search warrants and financial/telephone records; page 25 repeats it as **lead 1**. A requested clearance is not the clearance or a refusal.

Page 1 has a handwritten numeral 3 adjacent to the case field. Its relationship to 701's serial-3 reference is a **candidate association**, not an authenticated case-index join. Page 2 itself references serial 2 and lists several enclosures. Do not identify a particular August 19 LHM as one of those enclosures solely by topic or page count.

Page 5 identifies a computer-generated simulator itinerary as placed in a case **1A envelope**. That is a specific supporting-record location, not proof that it survives today. Page 1 also has an August 21, 2001 administrative stamp bearing the London attaché legend. Its visible processing labels must not be relabeled as a universal delivery receipt: this is a qualified London-handling clue, not proof of receipt by FAA or every addressee.

## Contrary information and reading controls

The August 18 source also preserves contrary context: physical pages **4–5** record the instructor's view that Moussaoui had a genuine aviation interest; pages **22–23** distinguish investigative belief from what remained unknown about the plan. These statements do not eliminate suspicion, but they must accompany an account emphasizing dangerousness or obstruction. The OIG's separately reported September 5 local FAA briefing remains contrary evidence to total nonwarning; 701 does not independently establish that briefing or its content.

One suspected date/weekday issue did not survive closer inspection. The higher-resolution source at 472 page 5 says **Saturday, August 18**, not the provisional lower-resolution reading. All five weekday/date pairs in that paragraph match the calendar. The reading hypothesis is retained in the local quality-control record; no historical date anomaly is claimed and no original value was changed. The same paragraph's original itinerary is the stronger retrieval target.
