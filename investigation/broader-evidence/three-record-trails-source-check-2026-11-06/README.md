## Flight 93 carrier export and Star-Fix

The [public text reproduction of DOJ Request 35-13, Packet 5](https://www.scribd.com/document/18886049/T7-B12-DOJ-Doc-Req-35-13-Packet-5-Fdr-Entire-Contents-Response-Letters-Reports-404) reports receipt on **March 29, 2002** of a corrected spreadsheet via **Airborne Express shipment 50588655-972**. The shipment number was independently checked against the reader text, not the underlying scanned image or waybill. The report describes a conversion error in card-number information; no native corrected workbook, complete three-version comparison or actual changed caller attribution was recovered. Do not describe it as a demonstrated timestamp correction.

The [Team 7 Box 19 public reproduction](https://www.scribd.com/document/24392516/T7-B19-Key-302s-Fdr-Entire-Contents-FBI-302s) includes FBI text reproductions described as statements from Star-Fix manager Richard Belme (**M-INT-00001982 / 265A-NY-280350-302-1888**, lead SF162) and the operator (**M-INT-00001979 / 265A-NY-280350-302-1880**, same lead). Both identifiers were independently located in the reader text. These are not the original handwritten notes. A separately reported later manager interview distinguishes two note sets and leaves unclear which went to the FBI; the NARA document could not be independently opened in this pass. A positive attribution depends on the original version-to-version call keys, not a name next to a billing field alone.

## K-13 museum object, panel and coupon

The [museum catalog](https://collection.911memorial.org/Detail/objects/117241/rel/1) independently confirms accession **C.2009.310.6**, its K13 NIST marking and Port Authority donor. NIST's [2004 inventory](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-C13-68c9d9b444b822d972f26f0b7974a8d2/pdf/GOVPUB-C13-68c9d9b444b822d972f26f0b7974a8d2.pdf) text separately identifies the panel K-1 or K-13 and a flange coupon labeled K-13, received from Gross July 29, 2002. Neither listing supplies the required parent-to-coupon or museum transfer chain. The [2010 LMDC historic-resources report, PDF p. 4](https://www.renewnyc.com/attachments/content/pdfs/WTCHistoricResourcesReportJan2010.pdf#page=4) states that NIST returned approximately **230 items in 2009**. It does not identify K-13 or establish that the accession or coupon was among the items. These descriptions were independently checked; the actual accession, cutting, specimen preparation and transfer records remain missing.

The research cites a NIST examination reporting no significant heat-induced changes in specifically tested panel material, and states it could not resolve the previously reported differences between FEMA-match table entries because the relevant image failed. That inability to reproduce is a limit of this pass, not evidence the discrepancy does not exist. The prior [pinned public review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/fe6c52e9cc375b3b6958ef8ae8aaa41f537e2892/investigation/broader-evidence/steel-k13-panel-coupon-crosswalk-2026-11-06/README.md) reports visual checks of the tables and discusses WJE's competing timing explanations; the unverified present pass does not erase those checks. This research does not establish explosives placement or a UMS link.

## Debris ticket trail and piers

The [reproduced October 17, 2006 federal opinion](https://aele.org/law/2006FPDEC/wtc.html) describes load tickets and cites **Plaintiffs' Joint Appendix vol. 1, ex. 7** for DDC-tracked operations. The exhibit itself was not obtained, so its contents cannot be asserted. The opinion's separate Zone 5 description references piers 6 and 26. The independently checked [NYC Department of Sanitation 2002–2003 annual report, PDF p. 17](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/dsny/downloads/pdf/studies-and-reports/2002-2003_Annual_Report.pdf#page=17) states temporary facilities at piers **6 and 25** were operated by Weeks Marine using DSNY barges. Different source scopes, dates, possible errors and operational distinctions remain plausible explanations, not established resolutions. A research report cites another hearing account referencing 25 and 26, but that transcript could not be independently retrieved in this check.

No completed site ticket, matched barge movement or receiving entry was recovered. Proposed joins by ticket or cross-reference, vehicle, cargo, dates, barge and receiving identifier are tests to attempt, not fields verified on an acquired ticket. No specific shipment, specimen route, wrongdoing or UMS link follows.
