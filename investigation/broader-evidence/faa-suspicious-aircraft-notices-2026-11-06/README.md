# FAA suspicious-aircraft reporting notices and recording leads

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD03–PD04. Three archived September 2001 notices document a change in the route for reporting suspicious aircraft. A separately preserved FAA operations log records distribution activity and identifies three voice recording positions. An FAA briefing document about the later Capitol evacuation identifies an additional missing action plan. These sources address different events and record types.

## Voice recording positions and network identity

At **1300** under September 24, 2001, the ADA-30 operations log records three phone positions added to record voice and names **“CCP 1/2/3”**, expanded there as **“Center Crisis Phone.”** This provides a dated channel-label lead at the operations center. The separate FAA protocols list, AWA-231, calls the conference the **“Domestic Event Net (DEN)”**, says it was established on **September 11, 2001**, and includes the Washington Operations Center and ATCSCC among participants. That list states a network and participant relationship. It does not identify the CCP channels as the central DEN recording, give their retention period, or establish coverage on September 11. ## The Capitol evacuation action-plan trail

The FAA material indexed as **AWA-788 / NAID 7602294** discusses the Kentucky governor's plane and actions after the Capitol evacuation. The cover heading says **“Seven Point Action Plan”** while the narrative refers to an issued **“nine-point plan”** of immediate actions. Preserve both descriptions. The narrative says **“we are installing a direct radar feed”** from Potomac TRACON to the NCRCC and gives July as the completion target. The accompanying slide lists a direct radar feed, training and mandatory briefings under **“Actions Taken.”** The report and slide give different presentations of status. The brief has no item-level commissioning or training completion evidence. The issued nine-point plan remains a distinct target.
