# Flight 93 911 call: occupancy wording in two witness summaries

2026-11-14 UTC · AUD016-A1. A separate FBI interview summary of dispatcher John Shaw gives a caller-occupancy account that bears on the unresolved discrepancy identified in the [preceding local witness comparison](../telephone-felt-fbi-witness-conflicts-2026-11-14/README.md). That local predecessor was not public when this note was prepared; its later publication must be verified separately.

## Source records

- The FBI's summary of its September 12, 2001 interview with supervisor Glenn Cramer, transcribed September 19, says the caller described a 757 **“with lots of passengers”**. [Commission-release scan, physical p. 40](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009/911%20NARA/Team%201A%20Files/Team%201A%20Box%2033/T1A%20B33%20Four%20Flights-%20Phone%20Calls%20and%20Other%20Data%20Fdr-%20Entire%20Contents-%20FBI%20302s%20843.pdf#page=40), extracted lines 49–50, SHA-256 `3b2f5f67f4e649236e77922c8922f93dced518e2e3f8255473da89c1747a92fe`.
- The FBI's summary of its September 11 interview with dispatcher Shaw, transcribed September 13, says the caller said **“there were not a lot of passengers on board.”** [Same scan, physical p. 42](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009/911%20NARA/Team%201A%20Files/Team%201A%20Box%2033/T1A%20B33%20Four%20Flights-%20Phone%20Calls%20and%20Other%20Data%20Fdr-%20Entire%20Contents-%20FBI%20302s%20843.pdf#page=42), extracted lines 33–35. This is an agent's summary of a witness interview; it is not a recording or necessarily the dispatcher's exact words.
- A separately released FBI transcript attributes **“pretty empty”** to the caller, with following speech marked unintelligible. [Packet 4, physical p. 92](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009/911%20NARA/Team%207%20Files/Team%207%20Box%2012/T7%20B12%20DOJ%20Doc%20Req%2035-13%20Packet%204%20Fdr-%20Entire%20Contents-%20Response%20Letters-%20Reports%20403.pdf#page=92), extracted line 31, SHA-256 `7ed35efb7825f26cb0c712d334a8b286c74bcc25c5b44e01f227059f446c6dba`. Its cover calls it a verbatim transcription; this review did not test that claim against authenticated audio.

The Shaw summary and transcript have similarly directed occupancy wording; Cramer's account differs. Their relationship, the identity and completeness of the source recording, and what the caller actually said remain unresolved. These documents are not three independent recordings, nor a count of actual passengers. Differences in listening, recall, interview summary, transcript treatment or recording versions are untested possibilities. No fabrication or intent follows from the difference alone.
