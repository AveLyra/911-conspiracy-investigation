# Great Lakes log copies and FAA production covers

November 5, 2026 · B129 / PD01 / PD07. This follow-up looked for the July 16, 2003 item 21 cover, chronology and receipt. It recovered a later Commission-addressed packet with a regional chronology, a separate internal response with a matching text body, and a fax transmission result for an April 2004 briefing-book packet. ## The same chronology text in two production contexts

| Source | Observation |
| --- | --- |
| **AWA-332**, physical pp. 1 and 28 | The **October 14, 2003** FAA cover addressed to the Commission lists a Great Lakes Regional Operations Center chronology among its enclosures. The archived 33-page packet contains a Great Lakes page at physical p. 28. Its fax header reads **September 26, 2003, 15:16**. [Packet](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7601899) |
| **AWA-242**, physical pp. 1 and 7 | The Great Lakes Region's **October 16, 2003** memorandum to **ADA-2** says it acted on the Administrator's October 14 memo, completed another search, and that day located its Regional Operations Center log for September 11. It says the log and a lessons-learned paper are attached. Physical p. 7 has the corresponding Great Lakes text and a fax header of **October 17, 2003, 08:41**. [Regional return](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7601827) |
| **AWA-243**, physical p. 1 | The International Aviation office separately reports an **October 14** request and calls its **October 16** response preliminary under **DOT Request No. 2, paragraphs 3 and 13**. Its attachment description is emails, handwritten notes and one TSA–FAA agreement annex. The common dates and similar subject lines do not establish that this return covered the Great Lakes chronology. [Separate office response](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913167) |

Seven ordered text anchors match between AWA-332 p. 28 and AWA-242 p. 7, from the clock heading to the 10:40 Pentagon-status entry. Visual inspection of both upper text regions confirms the matching wording in that portion. The two fax headers and packet contexts differ. Preserve the **September 26 fax header**, **October 14 Commission cover**, **October 16 report of locating the log** and **October 17 fax header** as four observations. The evidence supports an earlier dated copy of the text in a Commission-addressed packet. It leaves the archive's assembly history, the actual date the Commission received p. 28, and whether the region located a source log or another copy on October 16 unresolved. The overlap is one evidence chain.

Both pages label the times **“Central Standard”** and give **9:13** for the South Tower entry and **9:25** for the entry labeled **“2nd Tower.”** The 9:13 line also includes **“after third explosion.”** Preserve that wording as a reported entry with the surrounding unconfirmed bomb report and changing aircraft information. The Commission report gives **9:59** for the South Tower and **10:28** for the North Tower. This study does not align the clocks or identify who originated each report. The copies supply no independent confirmation of the explosion description. [Commission report, ch. 9](https://www.9-11commission.gov/report/911Report_Ch9.htm)

## Other records relevant to the correction and delivery trail

- **AWA-245**, p. 1, is a question-and-answer sheet with a preparation note. It gives **9:09** for a possible-crash notice to Great Lakes, **9:15** for notice to Air Force Search and Rescue, **9:20** for contact with Chicago Center, and **9:24** for two separately described notifications. One is a report from Great Lakes to the Washington Operations Center of lost contact with AA77. The other is an attribution to NEADS logs of FAA notice of a possible AA77 hijacking. The Commission gives **9:34** for NEADS being told AA77 was missing. The preparation note and earlier AWA-334 chronology are additional written sources for the 9:24 attribution. The preparation sheet's date and circulation, source dependence between the records, and their relationship to the May 22 answer remain unverified. [Preparation sheet](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7601829) · [April chronology packet](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913205) · [Commission report, ch. 1](https://www.9-11commission.gov/report/911Report_Ch1.htm)
- **AWA-325**, pp. 1–2, is a **December 2, 2003** FAA cover. It states that searching for the October 16 subpoena located additional documents responsive to the July 2 request and describes an attached index and documents. The released two pages contain the cover only. They separately offer a hearing-preparation briefing book for review and distinguish Accident Investigation Office files from Air Traffic accident files. The missing index could show which July request items were supplemented. [December cover](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7601893)
- **AWA-343**, p. 1, records **April 19, 2004, 6:01 PM** under transmitted/stored and **6:02 PM** on the fax result report. It gives the destination label **9-11 COMMISSION**, result **OK**, and **12/12** pages. The released object has two pages, both cover material. The sender-side result supplies stronger delivery evidence than a cover alone. The 12-page fax body, Commission receiving copy and attachment list remain unacquired. The AWA-325 briefing book offer and AWA-343 briefing-book reference are distinct records pending an edition and page match. [April fax evidence](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7601905)
