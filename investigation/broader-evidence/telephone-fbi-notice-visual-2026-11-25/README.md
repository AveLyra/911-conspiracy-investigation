# FBI operations-log notices: visual check and handwritten annotations

**AUD016-A1 / TEL04.** Visual inspection strengthens the reading of two previously located archival withdrawal notices; it does not recover the logs or establish their current classification or availability.

## Source and visual scope

Inspected physical pages 2 and 3 of `DH B5 FBI Logs Fdr- DOJ Letter re Doc Request and 2 Withdrawal Notice 142.pdf`, a member of the [January 2009 Commission archive](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip). The 74,245-byte PDF has SHA-256 `55f66ecbf0d789a0bc8962e3c42deabf1ad650b7e4f2bc4a3f6e2e45f8cd4df2`; its hash was rechecked and both notice images rendered and viewed November 25, 2026. No log pages or original handwriting were authenticated. The [previous source report](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/6f567c83d4d10b8a50659af1c3e8b72ab9a29009/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-doj-fbi-logs-request-2026-11-25/README.md) used extracted text and remains preserved.

## Observations

Both images identify the folder as FBI Logs and the subject as Washington Field Office FBI SIOC logs, dated September 11, 2001; both show withdrawal September 17, 2008. Page 2 lists box **00002**, folder **0005**, document **18**, **10** pages, retrieval **382 00002 0005 18**, and system document ID **4444**. Page 3 lists the same box and folder, document **19**, **26** pages, retrieval **382 00002 0005 19**, and system document ID **4445**. These are archive-notice descriptions, not verified unique historical log entries.

Both images also have handwriting that appears to read **“national security classified.”** That annotation was not captured in the previous text-only summary. Its author, date, authority, meaning and current status have not been verified. The typed notices say access was restricted at the time of review; do not infer an ongoing restriction, the specific contents or reason for any classification, wrongdoing, or absence of later releases. The archive-member filename and finding aid identify a Dana Hyde Box 5 folder while the notices display box 00002; their relationship remains unresolved. The two notice numbers and page counts must not be converted into independent corroboration of an event.
