# Figure 13 material labels in the WPI/Buehler paper scan

November 6, 2026 UTC · SPT06 / B188/H005 / AUD011/AUD016. In the ten-page public scan attributed to Biederman, Sullivan, Vander Voort and Sisson, the experiment text identifies **A36**, while the caption for the figure it cites identifies **HSLA column material**. A reproducible image comparison and visual check connect the figure to the 2005 WPI deck. The material assignment remains open. They do not adjudicate the figure caption. The native micrograph and corresponding spectrum metadata, mount log, lab material lot and proof corrections could distinguish the possibilities. ## Other values and version limits

- The scan's p. 2 reports **0.28% C max** as *nominal* A36 composition. The JOM letter's cross-section caption and WPI slide 11 give **0.29C max**. Preserve both nominal values. Neither supplies a chemical assay or mill certificate for the depicted sample. - The scan's p. 5 describes **750 to 900 °C** as a likely transformation range in its Fig. 7 discussion. The WPI-linked IMS paper separately reports **550 to 850 °C** based on pearlite and decarburization observations. Keep those estimates separate from the conditional **940 °C** eutectic value, FEMA's local inference approaching **1,000 °C**, and the actual **1100 °C** set point reported for the laboratory run. The studies leave the region-to-region and sample-to-run mappings open.
