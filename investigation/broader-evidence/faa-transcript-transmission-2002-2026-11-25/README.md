# FAA electronic transcript: February 2002 transmission descriptions

Two pages in a publicly accessible Commission archive packet preserve different descriptions of how an electronic Flight 93 communications transcript should be transmitted. Their displayed dates and headers do not establish their full order or what was ultimately sent.

- Physical PDF p. 42, dated February 26, 2002, says an electronic copy exists, asks for a written request and says the data should be sent by certified mail; it describes email as insecure.
- Physical PDF p. 43 separately reproduces a written request for an electronic transcript through an email system and an instruction to email the file. The instruction's own timestamp is not shown.

The pages come from `T8 B17 FAA Trips 3 of 3 Fdr- Satori Review Timeline- FAA Data.pdf` in the [Commission records archive](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip), accessed November 25, 2026. The 46-page member's SHA-256 is `cee78be0535e9cb6e5834da575c9fceb55791afcf6331a31b44a5e11af2cab0c`; both pages were visually inspected. The scan is not a native email or proof of dispatch. The raw packet and personal contact details are excluded.

The two descriptions identify a question about handling and delivery, not a demonstrated security breach, concealment or final choice. They do not prove that the 2002 file was the same transcript requested in the [September 2001 reel-copy plan](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/1ebab9649ec0feece76648ac103706981f904c0a/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-cleveland-reel-plan-2026-11-25/README.md). Preserve the accounts and any competing versions without normalizing their order.

**Open records:** native message headers and attachments; written request and responses; dispatch and receipt records; transcript version and custody; and any documents joining it to the September 2001 request. Absence of these records from this review does not establish nondelivery or destruction.
