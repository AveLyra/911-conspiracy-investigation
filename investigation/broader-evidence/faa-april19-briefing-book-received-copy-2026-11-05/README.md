## Chronology versions and competing source accounts

- **AAL77:** The reproduced fax p. 4 carries **09/18/01, 5:30 PM** in the available text. It attributes a **0924:00** military notice to NEADS logs, places several Dulles controllers' primary-target observation at approximately **0925:00–0930:00**, and lists a **0933:00** approximate Secret Service notification. All three text anchors also appear in AWA-922 p. 4. This is a wording match across selected fields. The Commission reports a **9:32** Dulles radar observation and a **9:34** notice to NEADS that AA77 was missing. The approximate observation, Secret Service notice and military notice are distinct entries. [Fax p. 4](https://www.scribd.com/document/13883505/T8-B19-Miles-Kara-HQ-FAA-3-of-3-Fdr-Notification-Chronology-From-FAA-Administrators-Briefing-Book-for-Congressional-Hearing-247) · [AWA-922, physical p. 4](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/e-Other/5-AWA-922-Table-First-Critical-Signs_Notification-Chronology.pdf#page=4) · [Commission report, ch. 1](https://www.911commission.gov/report/911Report_Ch1.htm)
- **UAL93:** The corresponding fax text says there was no specific military notification time available and that UAL93 was believed to have been discussed on an open line. It describes **1004:00** as approximate primary-target termination and a believed crash time. AWA-922 p. 5 retains a **0916:00** entry qualified as inaccurate, and describes **1004:00** as an approximate crash. The AWA-922 table separately gives **10:08** for notification. These values, caveats and event labels remain separate. The chronology version order and underlying call or net evidence remain open. [Fax UAL93 page](https://www.scribd.com/document/13883505/T8-B19-Miles-Kara-HQ-FAA-3-of-3-Fdr-Notification-Chronology-From-FAA-Administrators-Briefing-Book-for-Congressional-Hearing-247) · [AWA-922, physical p. 5](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/e-Other/5-AWA-922-Table-First-Critical-Signs_Notification-Chronology.pdf#page=5) · [AWA-922 catalog](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7602360)
- **Edition marking:** The public text renders **5:30 PM** on the AAL11, AAL77 and UAL93 chronology pages and **5:30 FM** on the UAL175 page. A later account describes the September 18 briefing-book version as **5:20 PM** and points to this reproduction. Retain the later description and the text renderings separately. The underlying page images and book versions are needed to verify the mark and distinguish reproduction error from a distinct version. [Fax chronology pages](https://www.scribd.com/document/13883505/T8-B19-Miles-Kara-HQ-FAA-3-of-3-Fdr-Notification-Chronology-From-FAA-Administrators-Briefing-Book-for-Congressional-Hearing-247) · [Later account, “The Administrator’s Briefing Book”](https://www.oredigger61.org/?p=1077)
- **Commission interview comparison:** The separately reproduced **April 8** memorandum describes work on a briefing book for a **September 21, 2001** hearing. The **April 20** memorandum lists four comparison documents, including the **September 20** notifications sheet and the **NEADS MCC technician log**. It attributes to the interviewee that the September 20 **9:24** entry drew on a NEADS source, that she had not seen the technician log before the prior interview, and that she had used the NORAD press release. The memo separately says Commission staff represented that FAA investigators had the technician log. Distinguish the individual account from the staff statement about other personnel. The memo's list does not identify job 1110 as an interview exhibit. Timing alone cannot join the April 19 fax to the April 20 interview. [Interview memoranda, April 8 and April 20 sections](https://www.scribd.com/document/17218071/T8-B6-FAA-HQ-Darlene-Freeman-Fdr-4-8-04-and-4-20-04-MFR-852)
