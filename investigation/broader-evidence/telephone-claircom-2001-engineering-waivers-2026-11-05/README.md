# 2001 FCC engineering applications for Claircom and GTE Airfone


November 5, 2026. This **TEL03/TEL04** follow-up preserves two contemporary FCC records that identify the carriers' engineering applications and distinguish pre-September 11 proposals from an October 2001 grant. The bankruptcy plan, disclosure statement and confirmation order sought for the parallel carrier-custody trail remain unacquired. [Earlier carrier-custody review and bankruptcy TODOs](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/cb41b35033b5a40182f4b51cac98fea4c3487db1/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-claircom-transfer-filings-2026-11-05/README.md)

## Acquisition and reproducible comparison

- **April 9 applications.** FCC notice DA 01-1216 names separate requests, **0000421562 (Claircom, KNKG801)** and **0000420815 (GTE Airfone, KNKG804)**. The May 15 notice, p. 3, identifies ULS as the file route. The October order records one supporting comment, from Claircom. Neither application nor that comment was acquired. [FCC notice, pp. 1 and 3](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-01-1216A1.pdf) · [FCC order, ¶1](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-01-2336A1.pdf#page=1)
- **Off-hours proposal and grant wording.** The notice reports testing between **9:00 p.m. and 6:00 a.m. local time** and exchange of test schedules ten days before P-4 testing. The October order's paragraph 8 retains mutual coordination and the **ten-day** exchange. The numbered conditions in that paragraph give no clock-hour window. Preserve the proposal and order wording separately. The applications, carrier schedules and implementation records would show how the parties planned and actually ran tests. [FCC notice, p. 2](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-01-1216A1.pdf#page=2) · [FCC order, ¶8](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-01-2336A1.pdf#page=3)
- **Channel and equipment scope.** The request and subsequent order describe turning P-5 and P-6 into a communications channel, C-30, and the guardband change from **3.2 kHz to 3.6 kHz**. The order's auxiliary-transmitter conditions cover aircraft on tarmacs or in hangar areas and prohibit using those transmitters for commercial air-ground service to the public. The order was adopted **October 5, 2001** and released **October 9, 2001**. Its dates and scope are distinct from the April request and from the September 11 carrier records. [FCC order, ¶¶2, 4–9](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-01-2336A1.pdf)

The two PDFs were retrieved from the FCC and checked by SHA-256 and exact page text. The absence of an off-hours clause was checked within paragraph 8 only. The report documents a source-wording difference and a records lead. It leaves actual test use, equipment installation and September 11 calls unresolved.
