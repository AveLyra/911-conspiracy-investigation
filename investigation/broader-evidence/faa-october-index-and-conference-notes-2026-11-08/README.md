## October recording scope and service record

- **October 9 handwritten notes, Team 8 Box 5 member 817.** Page 1 lists operations-manager and traffic-management lines, regional and WOC teleconferences, open lines including the primary net, and military operations lines. It gives `8:00 A.M. TO 12:00 p.m.` with no visible zone on that line. Page 2 reports that “nobody recorded from the WOC” and separately records uncertainty about whether the DOT Crisis Management Center recorded. The two locations and their reported coverage remain distinct. The 2006 OIG report separately describes the FAA’s lack of earlier WOCC telephone-recording capability and later installation. The source search therefore has separate targets for WOC participation and recordings held at other endpoints. [OIG report, physical p. 11](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=11)
- **November 17 response, p. 3 lines 19–30.** Its quotation of the October 8 tasking describes landlines and specified hotlines for **0800–1400 EST**. Keep that window and zone as printed alongside the October 9 notes’ separate window. A dated channel assignment, original copy request and completed recording inventory would test how the two scopes were applied.
- **October 14 letter and October 16 subpoena, Team 8 Box 15 packet.** The ZIP member calls its first part a document index. Physical p. 1 is a dated cover with six enclosure categories that also appear in official AWA-332. Physical p. 2 dates the subpoena October 16. Physical p. 3 is a **blank return-of-service form**. Physical p. 4 is Attachment A, which requests FAA–NORAD and internal FAA logs, tapes and transcripts. The form leaves the exact service details open. The response’s assertion of service and the OIG’s separate account of issuance are distinct sources.
