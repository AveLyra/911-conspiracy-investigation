# WTC 7 OEM tank-support calculation sheets and release search

November 6, 2026 · Steel and construction custody · STL02–STL03 / AUD011 / AUD016 / B188 / H005

The released WTC 7 OEM alteration packet contains the engineer’s December 7, 1998 follow-up to the Port Authority’s question about whether the new fuel-tank framing was supported by the existing concrete slab or by existing steel beams. Both accounts are preserved. The response describes a first-floor assembly with a slab on gravel backfill and steel beams encased in the slab, and refers back to calculation sheets **22.a.1 and 22**. The scans provide the dated response and two actual calculation pages. They leave the installed load path and the complete calculation record open. [Public release containing 182-P](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NIST_FOIA_DOC-NIST-2013-000594_Feb_23_2013.zip)

The November slab-bending request, December support clarification and later conduit-support calculations answer different questions. Sheet 78 is evidence that the structural consultant addressed the support question and stated a basis for its response. It does not establish that the reviewer independently checked the original calculations or that construction followed a particular version.
