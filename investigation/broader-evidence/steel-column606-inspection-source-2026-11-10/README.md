# Column 606 inspection and photograph leads

Public derivative of the November 10, 2026 source review · STL02–STL03 / AUD011 / B188 / H005. Two federal publications reproduce a dated LERA inspection table identifying Column 606 in the installed Tower A structure. A later table names inspection photographs by number. The inspection evidence gives a second way to check the intended location identified in the drawing and the photographed fabrication mark. The photograph, drawing, inspection table and salvage inventory each answer different questions. ## Routing and preservation

NCSTAR 1-1C Table 3–14 identifies the 1993 and 1995 source records as `4078/66-L` and `4079/66-L` and reports routine repair recommendations for both. Its discussion says the Port Authority reported losing construction records on repairs following inspections. The statement describes reported record loss and does not establish the decision or repair history of any specific face or floor. - Recover the complete `WTCI-66-L` inspection package, including the 1993 report and appendices and the 1995 report's photos 20 and 31 with captions or field notes. Compare any photographs on face, floor, geometry and distinctive features. The inspected pages identify photo numbers and a source series. A floor-specific photograph and installation or erection documentation are still needed for a piece-level comparison.
- Seek a contemporaneous 606A as-built or shop record and the repair ticket, clearance or later floor-specific inspection. For salvage identity, the remaining targets are original October 2001 photo metadata and field notes, stamped-piece measurements or end views, and yard dispatch, transfer, intake and disposition records that carry the piece mark or a defensible cross-reference.
- Carry forward the separate W14A and W14B candidate attribution and C-90 caption and span discrepancies. The Tower A inspection and drawing cannot establish the origin of those items or their record-handling history.
