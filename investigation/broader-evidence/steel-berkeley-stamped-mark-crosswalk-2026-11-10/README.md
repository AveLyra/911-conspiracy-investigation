## A testable design-location interpretation

NIST NCSTAR 1-3, physical p. 77, sorted lines 14–21 and 27–41, says the typical tower letters are **A** for **WTC 1** and **B** for **WTC 2**, and identifies **47–70** as WTC 1 derrick divisions and **547–570** as WTC 2 divisions. It describes stencil codes and stamped codes with similar building information and distinguishes intended location from the as-built location in most cases. FEMA's earlier Appendix D, physical p. 4, lines 23–35, separately explains a sample piece-number, floor and derrick designation. NCSTAR 1-3A, physical p. 36, Figure 2–7, visibly locates **606** in the tower core layout. The **80** underneath it is a box-to-wide-flange transition floor under the figure legend. [NCSTAR 1-3 method](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=101016#page=77) · [FEMA Appendix D method](https://www.fema.gov/pdf/library/fema403_apd.pdf#page=4) · [NCSTAR 1-3A Figure 2–7](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=101017#page=36)

**Inference to test.** Under the cited tower conventions, **606 A** and diamond **53** support an intended **WTC 1 core column 606** candidate, with **48** and **51** as possible floor endpoints. **10 TON** fits the convention for an estimated piece weight. **SOUTH LEFT** remains an uninterpreted orientation or handling label. The actual 606 shop sheet, erection diagram, original marking key and measured part would test this reading. The marking and the plan leave installation, post-collapse custody and inventory identity open. [NIST marking-key citation, physical p. 42, lines 26–48](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=101430#page=42)

Draft and final NCSTAR 1-3B Table 3–3 give the same thirteen compared entries, including one additional candidate described as unconfirmed. There is no **606** in the compared entries. **HH or S-2** shares division **53**, with different fields **605** and **98–101**. The common division supplies a useful non-unique control. An exact standalone **606** screen across the embedded text of the **112-page draft**, **112-page final** and **114-page GovInfo edition** found no hits. One, one and three pages respectively had no extracted text. These limited results leave unattributed entries, image-only marks and unexamined inventory or transfer records open. [Table 3–3, physical p. 38](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=101430#page=38) · [GovInfo edition](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-C13-799fd8322fd23d7bd4af8d96ea7e58b7/pdf/GOVPUB-C13-799fd8322fd23d7bd4af8d96ea7e58b7.pdf)
