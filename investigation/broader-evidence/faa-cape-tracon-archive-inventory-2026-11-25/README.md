## Source observations

The same finding aid lists cassette **K90-40**, “High AR 1400-1538 UTC,” and **K90-41**, “High AR 1540-1600 UTC,” with digitized items [7598887](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7598887) and [7598888](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7598888), respectively. Their listed windows bracket but do not describe content in the interval between 1538 and 1540. These rounded labels cannot verify the memorandum's 1538.37 notation or the precise length of a gap; the listed files and DAT were not played or decoded.

Separately, the finding aid lists **K90-35**, “Flight Data 1400-1544 UTC,” and **K90-38**, “Sup Console 1450-1600 UTC,” with digitized items [7598882](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7598882) and [7598885](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7598885). Their listed windows overlap the reported interval, but they describe different positions. Whether those channels captured any relevant communications is unknown; listed coverage is not proof of overlapping content or independent recording.
