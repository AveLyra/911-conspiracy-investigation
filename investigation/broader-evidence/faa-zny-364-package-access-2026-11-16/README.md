## Observations

The May 16, 2002 forward reproduces an FAA air traffic investigations message discussing an earlier instruction that each of the four accident packages should have a cover sheet restricting distribution and access. The message says a question had concerned UAL175 but that the instruction applied to all four aircraft. It states that the earlier instruction gave no specific format or wording, leaving those details to the respective regions. The source is a forwarded printout, not the complete earlier instruction or a set of actual cover sheets.

The message does not identify the applicable access criteria, reasons for restrictions, who later received access, or whether any request was wrongly denied. The existence of a restriction alone does not establish concealment, improper withholding or motive. The previously reviewed [2001 package-copy cover](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/73a4ba53455b387f741f57d7b97eca01f3337e33/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-zny-355-package-copies-2026-11-16/README.md) describes intended routing, while the [2004 original-package request](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/bbe1d23a97dc3a797e824246e0470af30e9a1073/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-zny-513-2004-request-2026-11-16/README.md) requests signed delivery. These different dates and statements are retained separately; neither demonstrates what restrictions were applied at any actual transfer.
