# FAA warning records: court exhibits acquired; completeness and delivery remain open

Two official court-hosted exhibits were acquired and all eight page images inspected. This advances Overview lead 47 / existing FAA-intelligence leads 7 and 16 while the original-record TODOs remain open. It does not establish attack foreknowledge, deliberate concealment or a coordinated purpose.

## Exhibit 810: a concrete warning-memorandum counterpart with a page gap

The [court index](https://coop.vaed.uscourts.gov/moussaoui/exhibits/defense.html) identifies an August 31, 2001 FBI letterhead memorandum. The [actual exhibit](https://coop.vaed.uscourts.gov/moussaoui/exhibits/defense/810.pdf) is **five PDF pages**. The first is the opening sheet; subsequent sheets visibly carry printed page numbers **2, 5, 6 and 7**. Printed pages 3–4 are not present as page images in this downloaded PDF. The reason and the full original/version relationship remain unresolved. This is not a finding that the underlying pages were destroyed or improperly removed.

Physical page 1 states the purpose is to advise FAA about a potential commercial-aircraft security threat. Physical page 4 / printed 6 contains the threat assessment: Minneapolis believed preparations concerned seizure of a Boeing 747-400 for a terrorist act, while the actual extent of the plan was unknown. The same sheet discusses uncertainty about continued detention, retention of property and capability after release in France; physical page 5 / printed 7 continues the possible further-training concern. Physical page 2 also discusses the mode-control panel and aircraft doors, as attributed investigative information rather than our independent aviation finding.

Those subjects and passages closely correspond to the OIG's description of a seven-page local memorandum. [DOJ OIG Chapter Four](https://oig.justice.gov/sites/default/files/archive/special/s0606/chapter4.htm), held derived paragraphs 327–334, describes the local threat assessment, a September 4 instruction to supply a headquarters teletype instead, and a September 5 local FAA briefing. The index date August 31 and September 4 instruction are different dated events; their difference alone is not a contradictory memo date. The court-hosted counterpart does not authenticate the complete seven-page version, original authoring/custody, dispatch or FAA receipt. Similar text and a court exhibit stamp are insufficient to prove that full chain.

The original eleven-page headquarters teletype remains unacquired. The OIG's reported local briefing remains material contrary evidence to a claim of total nonwarning. Whether equivalent analysis reached other FAA recipients, and what those recipients did, still requires original documents and receipt/action records.

## Exhibit 953: warning possibility and low-likelihood assessment together

The [court index](https://coop.vaed.uscourts.gov/moussaoui/exhibits/defense.html) identifies [Exhibit 953](https://coop.vaed.uscourts.gov/moussaoui/exhibits/defense/953.pdf) as a combined substitution for CIPA documents 381, 398 and 403 concerning FAA intelligence. It is three pages, not the three underlying assessments. Its opening identifies September 1998, August 1999 and February 2000 FAA assessments/notes; it does not map every later sentence to an individual original document.

Physical page 1 includes the possibility of flying a hijacked aircraft into a building or national landmark, alongside hostage-taking scenarios and an option-of-last-resort assessment. Page 2 describes limited, questionable-reliability reporting about suicide hijacking and assesses a crash into a U.S. monument/city as an unlikely initial course of action. It also mentions an alleged foreign-airport suicide scenario. Page 3 says recent reporting suggested a desire for U.S. attacks, without specifics, with domestic aviation one of several targets. These statements must remain together: the substitute does not support either categorical institutional ignorance of the possibility or knowledge of the actual September 11 plan.

A court-authorized substitute is evidence of what that substitute states. It does not supply the original reports, exact intelligence sourcing, distribution, who read them or ensuing operational decisions. It is not a fourth independent warning in addition to its source reports.
