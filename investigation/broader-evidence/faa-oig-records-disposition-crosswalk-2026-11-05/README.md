## A case-control record and a separate management-action trail

- **OIG Form IG F 1600.4, Index and Control.** The historical disposition schedule identifies this form in its investigative index series. It describes fields including report title, case control number and summary of facts or references. The April 2000 system notice separately describes investigative case index cards and lookup by name or incident title. The 2012 notice describes lookup by case number for both paper and electronic records, alongside other keys, and adds field office as an electronic lookup key. These are documented search fields. The actual index record or electronic counterpart for 042R0122001 remains missing. [NARA schedule NC1-398-82-01, physical p. 3, lines 20–28](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-transportation/rg-0398/nc1-398-82-01_sf115.pdf#page=3) · [65 FR 19557–58](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2000-04-11/pdf/00-8505.pdf#page=82) · [77 FR 42800](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2012-07-20/pdf/2012-17696.pdf#page=4)
- **OIG system records about actions taken.** The 2012 notice describes investigation-related administrative-action reports and names tracking management actions among system purposes. This supplies a DOT OIG search route alongside FAA's own decision records. The scope wording concerns actions taken. The notice supplies no case-specific record and gives no assurance a decision to decline action would appear in the same field. Investigation status, management action, recommendation closure and correction receipt are separate targets. [77 FR 42799, physical p. 3, lines 164–174 and 185–199](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2012-07-20/pdf/2012-17696.pdf#page=3)
