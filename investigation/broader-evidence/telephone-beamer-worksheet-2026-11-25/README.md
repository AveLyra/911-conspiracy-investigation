## Interpretation and source dependence

The search worksheet itself flags the reverse ordering for reconciliation; it does not provide a confirmed correction or measured duration. The inspected Combs report contains the **9:36** lost-calls statement, but the table's and worksheet's attribution to Jefferson's report cannot be verified from that observation alone: 302-95630 remains unexamined here. The two working documents share wording and citations and may depend on common material; do not count them as independent witness corroboration. An ordinary source-label, time-zone, transcription, or compilation problem is possible, as are other explanations; none has been tested. We do not choose a corrected time, infer a call's actual duration, or infer intent from the discrepancy.
