# DOT Inspector General management reports and congressional recipient records

November 6, 2026 UTC · **B129 / PD03–PD05**. A January 2009 Senate referral supplies a separate recipient for a DOT report titled **Actions Taken on Office of Inspector General Recommendations**. The Senate entry does not name a reporting period. Two House entries give that same title for two different 2008 periods. This is a concrete case where title alone cannot match the copies. An earlier House referral also shows that the **31 U.S.C. 9106** citation found on later DOT management-report entries already appeared in January 1998. The Secretary's signed letters and enclosures remain unacquired.
