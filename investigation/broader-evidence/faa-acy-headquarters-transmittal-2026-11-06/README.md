# The FAA headquarters cover letter for Atlantic City's July 2004 recordings

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD01 / PD07 · FAA-to-Commission production trail

A **July 7, 2004** FAA Office of the Deputy Administrator letter to the 9/11 Commission lists **four Atlantic City Tower positions** and a **separate Command Center recording on line 5115**. The packet also contains copies of both July 6 facility memoranda discussed in the earlier review. This links those two memoranda to the same archived headquarters packet and identifies a Commission-facing cover letter. A signed delivery receipt, incoming record and the July 16 supplement's separate delivery remain unverified. [AWA-337, six-page packet](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7601902)
