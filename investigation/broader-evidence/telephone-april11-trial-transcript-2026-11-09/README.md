# Flight 93 telephone summary at the April 11 trial session

Public derivative of the November 9, 2026 Eastern source review · TEL02–TEL04 / AUD016-A1. The archived Volume XVII-A transcript records the prosecutor offering **P200055** on April 11, 2006, citing stipulation 36. Defense counsel stated there was no objection, the court received the exhibit, and the prosecutor asked to put it on the screen. Later the witness said he created a call chart after reviewing the calls. This gives an exact trial-session locator and identifies a witness who described how the chart was compiled.
