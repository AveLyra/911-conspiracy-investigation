2026-11-14 UTC · AUD016-A1. Two FBI interview summaries and the released FBI call transcript preserve material differences about a 911 call associated with Flight 93. The original call recording has not been authenticated or listened to in this comparison. The call transcript is described as a verbatim transcription on its FBI cover page; that description does not authenticate the underlying audio. Personal contact details in the raw scans are excluded here.

## Contrary evidence and uncertainty

An available [2002 book excerpt as reproduced in a secondary account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Airlines_Flight_93#Edward_Felt_911_call_details) attributes to dispatcher Shaw a denial that the caller mentioned smoke or an explosion, and reports a corroborating account from a family listener. This is a **secondary reproduction**, not an independently inspected recording or the original interview. The transcript's lack of those words is additional contrary documentary evidence. These observations remain alongside Cramer's recorded account; no one is treated as controlling here.

Potential explanations include imperfect real-time hearing, differences between what a caller reported and what listeners recalled, omitted or unintelligible content, or differences between recording/transcript versions. These are **untested hypotheses**. The discrepancies do not by themselves establish an explosion, an edited recording, deliberate misrepresentation, or a cause of the aircraft's loss.
