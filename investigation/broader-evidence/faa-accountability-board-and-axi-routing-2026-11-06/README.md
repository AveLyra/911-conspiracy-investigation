# FAA accountability routing and the missing 2006 management decision

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD02–PD04, with PD01 open

The FAA’s period Accountability Board order gives the Board a defined workplace-misconduct oversight remit and assigns disciplinary action to management. DOT OIG’s **2006** recommendation instead asks **FAA** to consider appropriate administrative action. The report’s public copy supplies no Board referral or management decision. An original routing sheet or signed decision remains necessary to identify the deciding office. [FAA Order 1110.125A, §§8–9](https://www.faa.gov/documentlibrary/media/order/1110.125a.pdf#page=3) · [CC-2006-085, p. 12](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=12)

## A later OIG example with separate decision and tracking records

In **AV2023024**, DOT OIG identifies the later investigation office as a successor to **AEO-500**. Its flowchart separates the investigation report sent to a requesting manager, the manager’s work with HR, and case closure. In the audit sample, **9 of 66** reports had a signature that differed from the signature block. **Two of those nine** were signed at the wrong management level for case priority. The audit also describes a tracker populated with accepted cases and separate example emails notifying managers of rejected cases. The sample dates **October 2017–February 2022**. It cannot establish either a signature defect or a missing tracker entry in 2006. [OIG audit, PDF pp. 5, 7, 14, 16 and 24](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/FAA%20AXI%20Final%20Report-AV2023024_Rev2.pdf#page=7)

The March 23, 2023 FAA response in the appendix gives different implementation targets by recommendation. The report calls all **11** recommendations **“resolved but open”** at issuance. In the official HTML captured November 6, 2026, the recommendation containers show **10 closed and one open**. The open row is **2**, on overlap between orders **1600.20** and **1600.38**. Rows **7** and **9**, on signature authority and tracking rejected cases, each display **Closed on 06.07.2024**. The appendix gives **September 30, 2023** as the planned date for both. Preserve planned dates, report status and later posted closure dates separately. The captured 2006 correspondence page has no equivalent recommendation containers. Page structure is evidence about the public listing and leaves the underlying case disposition open. [Agency response, PDF p. 29](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/FAA%20AXI%20Final%20Report-AV2023024_Rev2.pdf#page=29) · [OIG issuance status, PDF p. 23](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/FAA%20AXI%20Final%20Report-AV2023024_Rev2.pdf#page=23) · [Later recommendation listing](https://www.oig.dot.gov/library-item/39435)
