**Interpretation.** The memorandum makes the mistakes themselves the subject of its purpose statement. The machine-located exchange appears to distinguish the purpose of assembling the material from how the errors arose. That weakens an admission reading of the memorandum in isolation. Keep the exact written statement, the provisional machine renderings, the separate public-presentation purpose and the Defense assessment side by side. The scan cannot determine what was edited, whether anyone knowingly inserted a false value, or who approved the public material.
