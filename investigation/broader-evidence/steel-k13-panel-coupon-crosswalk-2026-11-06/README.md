# WTC steel K-13 in the museum and NIST inventories

November 6, 2026 UTC · **STL02–STL04 / B188 / H005**. The 9/11 Memorial & Museum catalogs a large steel object with the reported marking **K13 NIST** under accession **C.2009.310.6**. NIST used **K-13** both as an alias of a three-column North Tower panel and as the name of a separately listed lab coupon. The museum object is a candidate match to the large panel. The shared label alone cannot join the museum object, July 2002 coupon and the samples later tested from the panel. [Museum accession](https://collection.911memorial.org/Detail/objects/117241/rel/1) · [Final NIST inventory, physical p. 90](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-C13-799fd8322fd23d7bd4af8d96ea7e58b7/pdf/GOVPUB-C13-799fd8322fd23d7bd4af8d96ea7e58b7.pdf#page=90)

## What remains open

The panel's **Y** and **Does not match** entries are preserved under the same field heading. The actual FEMA source row and NIST's correspondence or worksheet documenting that comparison remain open. The catalog's suggested **onset** timing, WJE's stated uncertainty, and NIST's later interpretation **during collapse** have different evidentiary bases. Neither the accession number nor the repeated label identifies which laboratory sample was used or independently dates the deformation.

- [ ] **STL02.** Locate the Port Authority item tag and transfer schedule, museum accession and condition reports, and original intake photographs for **C.2009.310.6**. Compare the original markings, cut edges and damage geometry with the WJE and NIST images. A documented split could identify which portion was transferred. Incompatible marks or geometry would weaken the match.
- [ ] **STL02–STL04.** Locate the **July 29, 2002** coupon transmittal, its parent-piece and cut-location log, and the two Figure 6–14 sample preparation and measurement records. Test the join by exact sample identifiers and dated records. Keep the names of the large panel and the separate coupon apart until then.
- [ ] **STL03.** Recover the original FEMA row, photograph and NIST attribution worksheet behind the two FEMA-field values. The reviewed documents attribute the panel to the North Tower. The WTC 7 candidate attribution files remain separate.

Both inventory tables in each edition and the additional unidentified-list coupon rows were checked on page images. The WJE sheet and its figures were visually read. The NIST vol. 1 sampling account was read through selected text and figure captions. The acquisition adds a museum catalog record and checks the printed joins against four held public PDFs.
