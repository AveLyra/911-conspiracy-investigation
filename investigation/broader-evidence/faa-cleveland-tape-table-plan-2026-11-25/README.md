# Cleveland tapes: September 12 table and September 17 plan

Two records give different stages of a potential tape-custody trail. A table dated **September 12, 2001** labels a group of original tapes as provided to an FAA office. A separate **September 17** message describes a scheduled Cleveland visit involving original reels and requested certified copies. The same individual is named in both, but the actual tape and reel identifiers have not been joined. The table's date is not independently verified as a delivery timestamp, and the message describes a plan, not an established completed transfer. They may concern different objects or actions; the relationship remains unresolved.

The table appears on physical p. 8 of [NARA AWA-320](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-e-other/5-AWA-320-Package-ZOB-Miscellaneous-Data-REDACT-SEALED.pdf), SHA-256 `8d525f36445f571f418221aa1822c885e7d2f68adbcec255b22265da2031791c`, and on p. 46 of the [Commission archive](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip) member `T8 B17 FAA Trips 3 of 3 Fdr- Satori Review Timeline- FAA Data.pdf`, SHA-256 `cee78be0535e9cb6e5834da575c9fceb55791afcf6331a31b44a5e11af2cab0c`. The two page images were visually compared November 25, 2026; their matching headings and entries support a candidate same-table relationship, not independent corroboration. The September 17 message appears on p. 22 of the same 46-page packet; see the [earlier message analysis](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/1ebab9649ec0feece76648ac103706981f904c0a/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-cleveland-reel-plan-2026-11-25/README.md).

The September 12 table uses **“Unknown”** in the security field for the four rows under the office-provided heading and **“YES”** for rows under a separate locally secured heading. Those are table entries, not independent verification of tape integrity, delivery or later custody. Preserve the two headings and values without combining the groups.

**Open records:** original tape or reel identifiers, actual transfer times, signed releases and receipts, certified copies, and source audio. They could determine whether the September records refer to the same objects. No loss, withholding or wrongdoing follows merely from the different descriptions. Raw scans, signatures and personal contact details are excluded.
