# FAA Situation Room records point to a promised DOT fax and Secretary briefing records

November 9, 2026 Eastern · B129 / PD01–PD04 and Mineta operational-record acquisition.

At **0120** under the September 12, 2001 date heading, an FAA chronology records a DOT Crisis Center caller promising to fax a report about the aircraft NAV CANADA had accepted. The line says the report had been received by **S60**. This identifies a specific document and communication to look for on both sides. The original report, fax and receiving record remain outstanding. The selected entry appears in the Commission's Appendix T copy and the FAA's AWA-577 scan. The copies share selected entries and table order. Appendix T is marked **Draft** and the separate AWA-577 copy has a **CRISIS 2** heading. Matching selected fields supports a common source text. The scans neither verify independent witness accounts nor establish that the scheduled or requested transmissions took place. Preserve **253** and **136** beside their reported attributions, and the completed and delayed entries in order. The original movement lists, briefing title and version, delivery and acknowledgment are missing. [Field-by-field pins](SOURCE-PINS.json)

The Appendix T page images label the times **(EDT)** on physical pp. 3–5. The embedded layer on pp. 4–5 reads **(EOT)**. Both images of the 0120 entry read **S60**. Independent OCR returned **S60** from Appendix T and **560** from AWA-577. These are separate source and extraction observations. No source clock or timestamp was calibrated. The eight-page AWA-578 scan is a separate September 11 chronology with its own EDT label. The scoped check identified no counterpart to the September 12 fax entry in it.
