# Saudi/FBI bank and telephone records: original-record pursuit

26 October 2026. Additive supplement to [the preserved fourteen-finding review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/7c13591e60d5c07ee176184cd834374b31f3e145/investigation/broader-evidence/documentary-crosswalk-2026-10-26/SAUDI-FBI-REVIEW-v2.md).
This tranche follows its S007 financial and S013 telephone leads. It leaves the
M001–M134 baseline, earlier wording and discrepancy statuses unchanged.

**We located original carrier-produced billing/call-detail pages, a carrier
subpoena-return letter and an FBI data-loading form. We also acquired original
FBI interview and analysis reports. The specific bank deposit receipt,
repayment exhibits and cellular records behind the February 4 sequence remain
unacquired as separately identified, reviewed exhibits.** An FBI report describing
a bank record is not the bank record itself.

See [the detailed record](RECORD.md), [originals still needed](NEXT-RECORDS.md),
[source receipts and hashes](SOURCES.json), [the labelled table recount](CALL-ROWS.json)
and [requirements/disclosure checklist](CHECKLIST.json).

The most useful new leads are the conflicting financial descriptions, the
interview's explicit reference to payment originals in a **1A file section**, and
differences in call dates, counts and line descriptions. A limited original-carrier
sample also supports the FBI's description of eight international calls on June
23, 2000. Both confirming and conflicting observations belong in the record.

These are records to test assistance, funding and communication. They do not
establish who spoke during a call, what was said, knowledge of the attack plan,
or a connection to demolition. Same-source repetition is not independent
corroboration. No contradiction has been adjudicated or earlier evidence edited.

Unmodified downloaded sources and OCR derivatives are preserved in a separate
local research checkout. This public supplement contains reviewed original notes,
small non-identifying transcriptions, URLs, hashes and methods. It excludes source
PDF payloads, personal account/telephone numbers, addresses, private correspondence
and litigation strategy. Page images use guarded local media storage.

[Reading and verification limits](METHOD.md) distinguish visual inspection, text
extraction, OCR, row counting and remote publication checks. The saved
[Git text diff](diffs/publication-additions.diff) compares new documents with
`/dev/null`; [earlier-record status](diffs/unchanged-records.json) records unchanged
predecessors. Remote publication is verified separately after committing.
