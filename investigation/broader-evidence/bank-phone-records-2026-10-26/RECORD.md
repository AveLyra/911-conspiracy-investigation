# Bank and telephone evidence: observations, conflicts and limits

26 October 2026. BP identifiers are additive research observations, not verdicts
or independent-anomaly totals. Exact source URLs, access times, byte hashes and

## BP01 — Conflicting reimbursement assessments

The declassified Joint Inquiry, **printed p.422, footnote 1; physical PDF p.13**, places
two FBI accounts alongside one another. Its quotation of a **November 18, 2002
response, p.3**, says an equal cash deposit on the check date suggests reimbursement.
Its quotation of an **October 14, 2002 document** says there was no bank
documentation supporting reimbursement from Hazmi or Mihdhar. We acquired the
congressional reproduction, not those two underlying FBI originals.
[Joint Inquiry release](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-Y4_IN8_18-PURL-gpo71223/pdf/GOVPUB-Y4_IN8_18-PURL-gpo71223.pdf#page=13)

A **July 21, 2004 FBI EC**, **EO14040-002738; physical p.16**, describes a $1,558
cashier's check, an equal cash **deposit**, and the $9,900 account opening, all posted
within seven minutes on February 4, 2000. It reports teller corroboration; the
reference identifying the teller interview is redacted. This is the FBI's account
of the records and corroboration, not our independent bank/teller examination.
[FBI Part 5](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11-attacks-investigation-and-related-materials/9-11-material-released-in-response-to-executive-order-14040/documents-responsive-to-executive-order-14040-2-c-part-5/at_download/file#page=16)

The OIG's same-day repayment account and the 2025 court's repayment finding remain
beside these records. Neither repetition supplies the missing transaction images.
The court cites **KSA Ex91, FBI2808–09**, not plaintiffs' Ex91 (Ratchford declaration).
[OIG chapter 5, notes128–129](https://oig.justice.gov/sites/default/files/archive/special/0506/chapter5.htm)
· [2025 opinion, p.21](https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/2025-08/03-md-01570%2011182.pdf#page=21)

**Open test:** obtain both dated FBI documents, the original credit/debit ledger,
cash deposit/check images and teller statement. Equal amounts alone do not identify
the person who supplied cash. No preferred account is selected here.

## BP02 — A sign conflict inside a draft financial summary

**EO14040-002906; Part5 physical p.184, printed p.43**, visibly lists:

```text
02/04/2000   -$ 1,558.00   Cash
02/04/2000   -$ 1,558.00   Parkwood Apts. (Cashier's Check)
```

The page is marked **“DRAFT; SOURCING OF INFORMATION INCOMPLETE.”** The minus sign
on the cash entry is present in the rendered page, not merely a text-extraction
guess. The July 2004 EC describes a positive cash deposit. Preserve both signs and
their different document status. This does not establish two actual withdrawals,
nor permit repairing the draft to match the July 2004 account.
[Draft page](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11-attacks-investigation-and-related-materials/9-11-material-released-in-response-to-executive-order-14040/documents-responsive-to-executive-order-14040-2-c-part-5/at_download/file#page=184)

**Open test:** bank debit/credit columns and source worksheets, plus the draft's
creation/revision history. A transcription error is an untested possibility, not
an adopted explanation.

## BP03 — Cash amount and source must remain distinct

The released **August 4–6, 2003 Bayoumi interview FD-302**, transcribed August 18,
**continuation p.5, physical p.11, Bates000002669**, records his uncertain estimate
that the men possessed **$4,000–$8,000**. Later records describe a **$9,900 cash
opening deposit**. The original receipt identified as **plaintiffs Ex550/FBI8057**
has not been acquired here. The interview and reported receipt concern differently
described quantities and different levels of certainty; retain both without turning
the mismatch into a finding of lying.
[Released interview](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11%20Commission%20Report/9-11-investigation-2003-09-sep/at_download/file#page=11)

A 2024 Motley Rice news account attributes nearly $10,000 transferred into their
account to Bayoumi. Its own public averment §1637 instead describes Mihdhar's cash
deposit; the court p.21 also attributes the cash deposit to the hijackers. These
descriptions are preserved, not equated. A bank companion, cash depositor and
ultimate source of funds are separate propositions.
[News account, extracted block B056](https://www.motleyrice.com/news/september-11-2001-new-evidence-23-years-later)
· [Averment §1637, physical p.416](https://www.motleyrice.com/sites/default/files/documents/AT_HR/9-11/20240802%20Redacted%20public%20averment.pdf#page=416)

**Open test:** receipt, account-opening documents and cash-origin evidence. The
$9,900 amount alone does not establish intent to evade reporting requirements.

**Open test:** plaintiffs Ex12A, Ex5 p.197 and their underlying Sprint/local-carrier

## BP05 — April 19 versus April 20 remains open

**EO14040-002759, physical p.37** lists **April 19, 2000** for a call to Aulaqi's home
from the line believed used by Bayoumi's wife and says Bayoumi was then in Saudi
Arabia. The averment **§758, physical p.197** gives **April 20, 2000**, citing that Bates
page. We verified the April 19 rendering against the source image. Do not silently
change either date or infer who used the phone from travel alone.
[FBI page](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11-attacks-investigation-and-related-materials/9-11-material-released-in-response-to-executive-order-14040/documents-responsive-to-executive-order-14040-2-c-part-5/at_download/file#page=37)
· [Averment §758](https://www.motleyrice.com/sites/default/files/documents/AT_HR/9-11/20240802%20Redacted%20public%20averment.pdf#page=197)

**Open test:** originating and receiving carrier rows, original clock/timezone
fields and the pleading's worksheet. Date conversion or transcription is untested.

## BP06 — Count rows, line sets and people separately

The FBI table **EO14040-002757–002759, physical pp.35–37** says **67 contacts** for
December 16, 1998–December 20, 2000. A reproducible extraction counts **67 printed dated
rows**. In the inclusive December 6, 1999–February 4, 2000 window it counts **27 rows**:
**21** involving BAYOUMI#2 and **6** involving BAYOUMI#4. The averment **§§1641–1643**
uses **22 calls** for that window. Both counts and the unsupplied selection rule
are preserved. No subset is asserted to explain the pleading's total.
[FBI table](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11-attacks-investigation-and-related-materials/9-11-material-released-in-response-to-executive-order-14040/documents-responsive-to-executive-order-14040-2-c-part-5/at_download/file#page=35)
· [Averment §§1641–1643, physical pp.416–417](https://www.motleyrice.com/sites/default/files/documents/AT_HR/9-11/20240802%20Redacted%20public%20averment.pdf#page=416)

[CALL-ROWS.json](CALL-ROWS.json) preserves every printed row and its extracted-text
line; [recount_calls.py](recount_calls.py) reproduces the count from the pinned PDF.
Rows are not automatically unique conversations: matching two endpoints, repeated
entries and carrier billing conventions require checks. No statistical claim of
an unusually dense call cluster is made without comparable coverage and baseline.

KSA's public reply disputes shared-line attribution and the proposed call-circle
inference. Preserve this adversarial account alongside the plaintiffs' account;
neither is a carrier record. The 2025 court made substantive jurisdiction findings,
but reserved merits/credibility determinations; this review does not erase that.
[KSA reply, printed p.15/physical p.29 n.8](https://www.motleyrice.com/sites/default/files/documents/AT_HR/9-11/Docket%20%23%209795%20-%20Redacted%20Public%20Version%20%20KSA%20REPLY%20MOL%20ISO%20%5B9368%5D%20MTN%20to%20Dismiss%20%28Prev.%20filed%20under%20seal%20on%203.4%2C%202024%29.pdf#page=29)

## BP08 — An actual carrier-record sample and its custody trail

The FBI's **2(c)/2(d) supplemental release1** contains:

- **EO14040-003831; physical p.164:** January 7, 2002 FD-302 saying AT&T toll records
  were entered on December 27, 2001; it describes eight calls on June 23, 2000.
- **EO14040-003832; p.165:** data-loading request with originals-attached marked
  yes, assigned December 27 and completed December 28, 2001.
- **EO14040-003833; p.166:** AT&T November 29, 2001 subpoena-return letter describing
  **two accounts for one number**, enclosed account/toll material and an **18-month**
  retention period. This records the carrier's statement, not independent legal
  verification of its retention-law claim or proof about other carriers.
- **EO14040-003865 and003867; pp.198,200:** July 4, 2000 bill usage summary and actual
  carrier call-detail page: **eight June 23 rows, six to Saudi Arabia and two to the
  UK, total42 billed minutes**. This agrees with the FD-302's count/date/destination
  description. Recipient/account numbers are redacted and are not republished.
- **EO14040-003884; p.217:** a separate July 27, 1998 carrier call-detail page, showing
  that the public packet includes earlier original billing material too. It is not
  joined to the July 2000 account through redacted identifiers.

[Carrier return and loading trail](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11-attacks-investigation-and-related-materials/9-11-material-released-in-response-to-executive-order-14040/documents-responsive-to-executive-order-14040-2-c-2-d-supplemental-release-1/at_download/file#page=164)

## BP09 — Payment originals and a specific custody concern

The **October 28, 2001 rental-manager interview**, transcribed October 29,
**EO14040-003828–003830; supplemental1 physical pp.161–163**, describes the check as
**$775 deposit + $8 insurance + $775 rent = $1,558**. The averment §1638 instead
describes rent, security deposit and an application fee. Keep both component labels.
The interview says Bayoumi brought the check **alone**; the averment §1638 describes
him returning with both men. We have not obtained the cited later declaration and
deposition needed to compare those accounts directly.
[Interview p.2](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11-attacks-investigation-and-related-materials/9-11-material-released-in-response-to-executive-order-14040/documents-responsive-to-executive-order-14040-2-c-2-d-supplemental-release-1/at_download/file#page=162)
· [Averment §1638](https://www.motleyrice.com/sites/default/files/documents/AT_HR/9-11/20240802%20Redacted%20public%20averment.pdf#page=416)

The interview also reports a roughly February 15 **$1,010** cashier's check and says
its difference from the $775 rent was unexplained to the witness. The ledger,
receipt/check images, credits and allocation are needed; the difference is not
evidence of concealed funding by itself.

Finally, the witness thought the February deposit slip/accounting ledger had been
reviewed and copied because it appeared re-stapled, with a copy in place of the
original. The FD-302 says supporting payment documents/ledgers were attached and
original copies would be maintained in the **1A section of the file**. This is an
FBI record of a witness's observation/inference, not a forensic finding of tampering
or identification of whoever handled the papers. The immediately following released
page is a January 2002 telephone-record report, not an identified payment attachment.
We have not located those attachments; their absence from that position does not
establish that they were destroyed or omitted from every release.
[Custody/attachment passage, p.163](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11-attacks-investigation-and-related-materials/9-11-material-released-in-response-to-executive-order-14040/documents-responsive-to-executive-order-14040-2-c-2-d-supplemental-release-1/at_download/file#page=163)

## BP10 — A named enclosure is not the underlying carrier dataset

The **July 18, 2007 EC**, **EO14040-002797–002798; Part5 pp.75–76**, names a 42-page
frequency spreadsheet for phones used by Bayoumi, Mihdhar and Hazmi, with i2 link
charts. The continuation emphasizes a particular line but describes a broader
network exercise. We acquired the EC; we have not separately identified and
verified that entire 42-page enclosure or its source carrier returns. The averment
§§756–759 is a lead to these pages, not a substitute for the enclosure.
[EC cover](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11-attacks-investigation-and-related-materials/9-11-material-released-in-response-to-executive-order-14040/documents-responsive-to-executive-order-14040-2-c-part-5/at_download/file#page=75)
· [Averment §§756–759](https://www.motleyrice.com/sites/default/files/documents/AT_HR/9-11/20240802%20Redacted%20public%20averment.pdf#page=196)

The preceding S013 report's incomplete `02/2000` date and temporal wording remain
unresolved. None of these newly obtained pages supplies a verified replacement
for that missing day or silently changes its original wording.

The next steps are [specific missing originals](NEXT-RECORDS.md), not an inference
that any one conflict establishes coordination or that an unlocated record never
existed. Assistance, funding, foreknowledge and later record handling remain
