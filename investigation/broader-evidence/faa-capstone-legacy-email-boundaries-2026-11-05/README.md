DOT OIG's reported boundary moves earlier across its two forms, while FAA's moves later. The forms report scope in their own terms. The differences do not establish how many accounts or messages were captured, when particular messages were migrated, or whether the FAA still retains email from 2013–2015. None of the reported legacy periods reaches the 2003–2006 events at issue. The approved position list supplies a route for later correspondence involving the investigations office. Whether the office actually holds or transferred later follow-up about this case remains unknown.

## Case records and attachments

GRS 6.1 includes associated attachments in its email scope. It also calls for agencies to define the official version when records appear in multiple places, such as live mail and an archive. NARA guidance separately discusses linking email and attachments to case or project files using metadata pointers or by filing copies in those files. This distinction matters when the time span in a mailbox schedule falls short of the requested records. The existing OIG case-file and FAA correspondence trails remain separate retrieval routes for cross-filed emails and attachments.
