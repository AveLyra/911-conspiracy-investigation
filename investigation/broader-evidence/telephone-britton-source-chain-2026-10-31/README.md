## 1. What the underlying interview actually qualifies

The official part 02 report's first page records an interview on **09/17/2001**, with **Date of transcription 09/20/2001**; its continuation repeats **09/17/2001**. The interview subject remains masked here. No identity from a less-redacted mirror is used to fill that mask or identify a masked PG1602 actor.

The call paragraph begins **“At approximately 9:30am or 9:45am on 09/11/01”** (the **or**, two values and approximate qualification are retained). It reports a hijacking conversation, a number supplied for a callback, screaming and the line going dead. The next paragraph says the attempted callback got a message that the number was not in service. It then expressly says the recipient **assumed** Britton had borrowed a cellphone, given the number she supplied. This is an inference reported in an interview, not direct observation of the onboard equipment or an independently verified service classification.

The report also limits what the recipient said was conveyed: no identifying details about the hijackers, their number or armament, who was flying, the intended destination, or other passengers' activities. The continuation says Britton did not explain how she knew about the two World Trade Center impacts. Those are limits on this account, not proof the events did not occur. A callback failure is not proof that the original conversation was impossible; original routing, dialed-number and service records are needed. No personal/contact/profile data from the continuation is reproduced publicly.

## 3. Dates and the prior-interview claim remain separate

**PG1602 p. 10** is a communication dated **09/22/2001**, Pittsburgh to Pittsburgh. The information-received field says **09/20/2001, 11:18 PM**, source label **SOMERSET CRASH DP** (unexpanded). Its continuation on **p. 11** reports contact and a reply that the person had been interviewed by FBI New York. Some wording near the end of the first line is obscured by markings; the legible phrase **“INTERVIEWED BY FBI NEW YORK”** is retained without inventing missing text. The form's **Event Date 09/11/2001; Time 12:00 PM** is an administrative field whose relation to the reported call is unverified.

**PG1602 p. 12** records assignment on **09/21/2001 at 1:23 AM** and conditions setting an interview lead on a determination that the person **“HAS NOT ALREADY BEEN INTERVIEWED.”** The prior-interview reply, “Lead Required?: YES” and conditional assignment are all preserved. Neither the presence of an assignment nor a reply about an interview establishes the actual interview's record ID, performance, clearance or later closure. No missing return is treated as proof of inaction.

**Staff-report p. 99, note 371** cites: **“FBI report of investigation, recipient of call from Marion Britton, Sept. 14, 2001.”** The held official candidate instead has an interview date of **September 17** and transcription date of **September 20**. Both candidate dates and the **September 14 citation** remain. Note371 lacks the specific 25306 identifier in the printed citation. Thus we cannot yet establish that it cites this very report, that the citation is erroneous, or that the dates were changed. A different interview, a citation mistake or a version difference are untested possibilities. Obtain the actual cited September 14 record or a source crosswalk before adjudicating them.

## 4. A specific search-coverage question, not a reproduced defect

The working sheet says its data came from a set of FBI 302 files derived from searches using **phone call AND flight AND 175/77/93**, **phone AND call AND flight AND 175/77/93**, **cell phone AND flight AND 175/77/93**, and **cell AND call AND flight AND 175/77/93**, with the flight numbers checked independently. Its claim of a “complete set” is scoped to those searches; it does not establish complete FBI holdings, a full carrier database or a complete independent call census.

The writer adds: **“For some odd reason inputting 11 in the flight number field would search and return 175 instead of 11. Need to check with FBI on this.”** We read that statement in the reproduction; we did not rerun the query or acquire its native execution log, syntax, field definitions, database version, original/corrected results or follow-up. Treat it as a reported retrieval problem and a concrete TODO. It does not establish deliberate exclusion, actual loss of Flight 11 evidence or a live defect today. The relevant test is reconstruction from the original database/query records, preserving both successful and contrary results.
