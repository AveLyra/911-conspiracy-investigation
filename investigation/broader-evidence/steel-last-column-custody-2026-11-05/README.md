# Last Column custody, conservation and installation records

November 5, 2026 · STL02–STL03. This follow-up traces a single museum accession, **C.2009.310.9**, as a test of how a cataloged steel member can be linked to underlying transport and treatment records. The museum identifies it as a remnant of South Tower core **column 1001B**, with the Port Authority listed as donor. The identity stated by the catalog, the sequence reported by institutions, and proof of individual handoffs are separate. This is a different object from the WTC 7-attributed candidates and the two North Tower impact panels in the earlier review. [Museum accession record, Historical Notes and Source](https://collection.911memorial.org/Detail/objects/6760) · [Prior impact-panel crosswalk](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/tree/main/investigation/broader-evidence)

## New record routes

- **Hangar 17 condition and treatment.** LMDC describes climate-controlled storage and cataloging, detachment and magnetic backing of duct-taped material starting in **2004**. It reports **82 loose memorial items** detached for the move. A museum conservation account separately describes consolidation of mill scale and rust, and says **all the posters and pictures** were removed, backed and reattached over time. A later museum exhibit-rotation account reports replicas placed on the column in lieu of the displayed items it describes. Preserve the **82**, the **all posters and pictures** description and the reported subset represented by replicas with their own scopes and stages. Present-day display and pre-treatment surface condition require separate evidence. [LMDC, p. 5](https://www.renewnyc.com/attachments/content/pdfs/WTCHistoricResourcesReportJan2010.pdf#page=5) · [Museum conservation account](https://911memorial.org/connect/blog/stories-they-tell-conserving-last-column) · [Museum rotation account](https://loadtest.911memorial.org/connect/blog/new-artifacts-rotated-view-historical-exhibition)
- **Federal grant and final treatment file.** IMLS's **December 9, 2009** announcement identifies a **$200,000** Save America's Treasures grant for this object. Its project description reports damage to the steel surface and attached material from sunlight and humidity, and describes proposed conservation for exhibition. LMDC also reports the grant and describes planned monitoring and condition assessment. The announcement identifies an administering agency and a specific project. Award conditions, work performed and the final condition report remain open. [IMLS, administered grants, item 1](https://www.imls.gov/news-events/news-releases/imls-announces-95-million-save-americas-treasures-grants-preserve-cultural) · [LMDC, p. 5](https://www.renewnyc.com/attachments/content/pdfs/WTCHistoricResourcesReportJan2010.pdf#page=5)
- **Post-Sandy inspection.** The museum director describes about **seven feet** of standing water in the museum, water at the base of the protective enclosure, and a preliminary post-pumping finding that the inscriptions were intact. These are reported room/enclosure conditions and a preliminary inspection result. The condition of individual steel surfaces and any exposure inside the enclosure require the inspection sheets and monitoring data. [Museum director's account](https://www.911memorial.org/connect/blog/our-beacon-hope)
