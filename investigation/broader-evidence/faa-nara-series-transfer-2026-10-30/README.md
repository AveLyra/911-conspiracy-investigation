The late-received folder's displayed copy lists **Reference and Reproduction**, also on a hard drive; the displayed purpose field does not also list Preservation. ## Assessment and next discriminating records

Official catalog metadata is useful evidence for where to look; it is indirect evidence of the underlying historical transfer.

That possibility is recorded, not adopted as a demonstrated explanation of the specific counts. An incomplete-preservation hypothesis likewise needs a defined original population, a matched manifest and actual disposition/retention evidence.
