# Scooby report 14: Able Danger and Odigo provenance check

November 12, 2026. This checks selected recovered claims against the DoD Inspector General report, congressional records and contemporary Odigo reporting. The linked DoD IG copy is a third-party-hosted reproduction of the government report. Neither the underlying chart-history attachments nor an authenticated Odigo message export or FBI disposition was recovered in this pass.

| Claim | Source and limitation |
| --- | --- |
| Witness recollection about Atta and a particular chart changed. | The [2006 DoD IG report, printed pp. 18–19](https://irp.fas.org/agency/dod/ig-abledanger-alt.pdf), describes an August 30, 2000 memorandum and records that Capt. Phillpott later distinguished the chart discussed there from a separate document he recalled seeing. This is the IG’s account of interviews, not a complete original interrogation transcript or proof that every document he remembered has been identified. |
| Oversight inquiries found the pre-attack identification claim uncorroborated. | The [IG report, printed pp. 3–4](https://irp.fas.org/agency/dod/ig-abledanger-alt.pdf), concludes that the reviewed records did not establish that Able Danger identified Atta or another hijacker before 9/11, and discusses inconsistent witness recollections. The [Senate Intelligence Committee report, PDF pp. 20–21](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CRPT-110srpt57/pdf/CRPT-110srpt57.pdf#page=20), says staff interviewed all five claimants and reached a negative conclusion. These are substantive counterevidence, not direct inspection here of every underlying chart or interview. |
| Able Danger data and documents were destroyed. | [Kleinsmith’s congressional testimony](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-109shrg25409/pdf/CHRG-109shrg25409.pdf) says he and a colleague destroyed data and products not already passed on during an earlier shutdown. The IG separately reports large data destruction at two locations and describes a later dispute about documents in Shaffer’s office. The IG found no improper destruction in its review. Destruction of records is not by itself proof an Atta identification was on them, nor should distinct destruction episodes be collapsed into one event. |
| Odigo received a specific, authenticated advance warning naming the WTC. | [Contemporary reporting of an Odigo executive’s account](https://www.computerworld.com/article/1344074/fbi-probes-advance-im-warnings-on-attacks.html) describes a nonspecific threat, with the FBI investigating, and says it did not mention the World Trade Center. This reporting does not supply the original message, timestamp basis, sender identification or FBI disposition. Treat it as an attributed account, not an authenticated incident package or a proven connection to Able Danger or UMS. |

**Open:** original August 2000 memorandum and chart attachments, contemporaneous chart provenance, complete witness/interview and records-disposition files, Odigo native message and server logs, and FBI investigation records. Later recollections and administrative log entries are useful leads but do not establish a common operation. Other report-14 claims and attachments remain open.
