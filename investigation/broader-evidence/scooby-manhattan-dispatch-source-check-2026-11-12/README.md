## Interpretation and tests

The corroborated Ohio/Illinois activity is consistent with ordinary moving work and weighs against assuming those workers were at the WTC at those reported times. It neither excludes unrelated activities at other times nor proves an intelligence or explosives role. The dispatcher statement supplies a lead, not a positive identification or an actual assignment. The CART reference establishes an investigation, not intentional removal, concealment, or a particular result.

For the separate reported overdue Manhattan delivery, seek matching order, dispatch, invoice or bill of lading, receipt, crew and vehicle records; no such join was established in this check. Full CART reports and exhibits, their search scope, and negative as well as positive results are important. This review did not independently inspect the Doric witness record, the named underlying Scooby PDFs, or the complete 13-page Williamsport packet. Do not treat earlier search failures as proof that these records do not exist or that no work was performed.
