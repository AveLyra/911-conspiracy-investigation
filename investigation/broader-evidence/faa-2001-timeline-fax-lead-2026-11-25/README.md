The entry is a specific source-retrieval lead, not evidence that the later 2003 Commission answer was corrected.

The reported 2001 fax and the later May 2003 Commission question for the record are separate observations. Nothing in the inspected log entry identifies the later answer or shows correction delivery, FAA action, or intent. Do not merge this fax with the QFR or infer that its contents were false, concealed, or successfully transmitted.
