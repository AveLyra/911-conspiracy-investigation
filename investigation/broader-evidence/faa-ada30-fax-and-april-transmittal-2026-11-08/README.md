## Separate 09:24 descriptions

The Team 8 April packet reproduces the compiled chronology's `0924` row at physical p. 3, line 1, which attributes an American 77 report to the NEADS log. Its activity-report section at physical p. 6, lines 57–58, separately describes an FAA regional operations center report that American 77 disappeared from radar at `1256Z`. The two descriptions appear under the same cover in this archival compilation. Keep the attribution and endpoints separate. The original NEADS entry, sender and recipient records and any source worksheets would test what the compiler was referring to.
