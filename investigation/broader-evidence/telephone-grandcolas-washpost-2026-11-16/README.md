# A second newspaper account of the Grandcolas message

Research note, November 16, 2026. Scope: indexed publisher text for one additional contemporaneous article and comparison with the previously examined FBI and Chronicle accounts. The source article and family press release were not downloaded.

## Observations and provenance

Thompson](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/2001/09/12/jet-crash-victims-stories-start-to-emerge/2b343cc9-e403-48ff-8d57-20f9045ab326/), publisher text indexed and accessed November 16, 2026, describes a telephone call to the passenger’s husband and attributes to her a statement about a hijacking and the treatment of passengers. The currently indexed page displays September 11, 2001; its URL contains September 12, and a [later law review citation](https://paperzz.com/doc/7630973/third-party-liability-for-terrorist-acts) cites September 12, page A6. Preserve both dates; the original print edition and time-zone or publication history have not been examined. A direct command-line retrieval failed DNS resolution; no article file or hash is claimed. The observation is limited to search-indexed publisher text.

[Prior source review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/e9dc12fe06eeee936e7d48dde5450c2adac2fb53/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-sf157-chronicle-source-2026-11-16/README.md) records its separate access limitation and the unconfirmed relationship to the FBI’s described attachment.

## Interpretation and limits

The Post is an additional identifiable publication carrying a similar description. Its underlying source, the relationship between the two articles and any family press release, and the history of the publisher page are unknown. Similar wording is not independent corroboration of the message. The FBI account is a report of an account, not the underlying recording or press release. These observations do not establish fabrication, deliberate alteration, suppression or motive. No original message content is reproduced here.
