# Airfone records custody in the JetBlue–Thales closing

November 5, 2026 · TEL03/TEL04. The [earlier Airfone custody review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/5422b6db826eed48708752c01f7f85524a79e1aa/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-airfone-docket-originals-2026-11-05/README.md) traced transfers through LiveTV and Aircell. This follow-up isolates a separate records route in the subsequent sale of LiveTV to Thales. It compares selected provisions in the March and June 2014 agreements with the closing filing and issuer accounting disclosures.

## Records route

- **Both agreements address retention by the buyer and seller.** Section 5.02 in the March 13 agreement and the June 10 amended agreement calls for seven years of retention of covered pre-closing business records by the buyer, and by JetBlue where the specified delivery or possession condition applies. The clauses except tax records handled under §7.05 and give the other party at least 30 days' notice before early destruction so it can take possession. JetBlue's 8-K reports the LiveTV closing on June 10, 2014. The seventh anniversary was June 10, 2021. Actual notices, transfers, destruction and subsequent retention remain unverified. [March exhibit, §5.02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1158463/000115846314000029/jblu20140331ex21purchaseag.htm) · [June exhibit, §5.02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1158463/000115846314000056/jblu20140630ex102.htm) · [Closing 8-K, item 2.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1158463/000115846314000040/ltvclosing8-k.htm)
- **The Airfone liability has its own document trail.** Both agreements exclude Airfone-related Federal Universal Service Fund accruals from the closing working-capital definition of current liabilities. Section 1.02 points to **Schedule 9.02** for the Airfone transaction definition. The June exhibit lists that schedule and the **Exhibit 5.07 Transition Services Agreement** without their substantive text in the reviewed public version. Sections 9.02 and 9.05 describe indemnification and records exchange in connection with covered claims. JetBlue separately reports an Airfone-related **$3 million liability** assigned to it in the LiveTV sale. The liability may point to retained accounting or claims files. It leaves the custody of specific telephone records open. [June exhibit, §§1.01–1.02, 5.07, 9.02, 9.05 and exhibit list](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1158463/000115846314000056/jblu20140630ex102.htm) · [JetBlue 2014 Form 10-K, p. 73](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1158463/000115846315000013/a201410-k.htm#:~:text=a%20%243%20million%20liability%20relating%20to%20Airfone)
