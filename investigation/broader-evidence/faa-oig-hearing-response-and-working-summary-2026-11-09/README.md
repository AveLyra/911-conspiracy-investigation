## The September 17 summary and its Commission note

Hyde Box 7 tab 7-23, item 574, is an 11-page scan. Its p. 1 explanation says staff received the summary from FAA and were told it had been used to brief the White House. P. 2 is an image-only FAA cover showing the event date **September 11, 2001** and source label **AAT-20**. The three flight sections begin on pp. 3, 6 and 9 and identify **American 11, United 175 and United 93**. Section footer dates on pp. 5, 8 and 11 read **September 17, 2001**. The packet supplies a reported briefing-use lead. The actual briefing, recipient and completeness of any other version remain unverified. [Summary and note locators](SOURCE-PINS.json)

## May 2003 recommendation and testimony records

The May 21 email in Team 7 Box 7 p. 1 lines 20 and 28–33 reports **95** FAA/TSA aviation-security recommendations across reports from **1993 through March 2003**. It labels **11** closed as **“done or OBE”** in the visible scan. The embedded text reads **“done or QBE.”** The email describes **53** promises to implement and **31** recommendations needing a response or satisfactory response, within its remaining **84**. The arithmetic reproduces as **95 − 11 = 84** and **53 + 31 = 84**. Keep a promise, adequate response and completed action as distinct fields.
