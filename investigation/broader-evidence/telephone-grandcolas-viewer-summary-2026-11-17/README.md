# Viewer-reported summary of the Grandcolas video

November 17, 2026 · TEL02–TEL04 / AUD016-A1. A viewer reported listening to the message in the [video identified in the 2022 announcement](https://www.send2press.com/wire/flight-93-on-september-11-final-call-new-animation-video/) and summarized it as containing innocuous details like “Something happened but I'm ok.” The viewer suggested it was probably referring to the hijacking. This is the viewer's paraphrase and inference, not a verified transcript, a verbatim quotation from the recording, or an independently verified identification of what the speaker meant. The researcher has not independently played the video or verified its timecode, completeness or provenance.
