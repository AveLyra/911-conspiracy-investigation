# Commission staff records on circulating the FAA and NORAD referral evidence

November 5, 2026 · B129 / PD01–PD04. The June 2004 correspondence distinguishes a disputed April interim report, a later request to circulate a briefing memo, a memorandum addressed to commissioners, and a separate proposal about when to refer the matter. Indiana University’s inventory identifies a June 7 copy in Hamilton’s hearing materials and a June 10 binder email. The April 6 report and complete distribution records remain unacquired.
