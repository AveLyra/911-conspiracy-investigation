## What the referral and minutes scans add

- **Marcus Box 6 referral scan, pp. 1–3.** The August **20, 2004** stamp is visible on the joint OIG reply, p. 1 lines 16–31. The reply refers to the **July 29** letter and assistance arranging records access through NARA. The separately filed copy of the referral is dated **July 29** and lists six enclosure categories. Its second page still gives **August 26** as statutory termination. Preserve that date alongside **August 21** in the Commission's transfer letter and the earlier DOT synopsis date of **July 24** for the referral. The scan upgrades the earlier indexed text comparison with readable page images. It is a public mirror copy of a Commission-side packet. It supplies no independent signature or delivery authentication. [Prior correspondence comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/944349f4362f7e3e623ee9d8675faa19927f3732/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-joint-referral-acknowledgment-2026-11-05/README.md) · [NARA file-unit description](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/2604844) · [Commission transfer letter](https://www.archives.gov/files/historical-docs/9-11-commission-letter.pdf)
- **Referral packet, p. 15 lines 1–19.** The June 6, 2004 internal memo describes the staff's evidence about possible false statements as circumstantial and inconclusive. The July referral says the Commission did not investigate whether the inaccurate statements were knowingly false. These source statements distinguish internal concern, the decision to refer and the later investigative question. [DOT OIG findings, physical pp. 2 and 9–10](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=9)
- **Front Office Box 1 minutes.** The archive member's filename uses **May 22 and May 23**, while the face title and opening lines of physical p. 1 identify a meeting of **May 21–22, 2003**. Keep the filename and page reading separately. This body is a minutes record. The title does not supply the May 22 answer or the May 23 hearing transcript.
