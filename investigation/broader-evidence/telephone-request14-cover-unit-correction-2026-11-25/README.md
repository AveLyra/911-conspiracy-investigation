# Correction: the Request 14 cover describes pages, not enclosures

AUD016-A1 / TEL01–TEL04 · 2026-11-25. Our recent archive and similarity notes described the cover as referring to **50 enclosures**. That wording was wrong: the document describes **fifty pages of documents enclosed**, not a count of 50 separate enclosures. The immediately preceding chat update repeated the error. We preserve the affected reports and their versions; this is an additive correction.

## Source and verified scope

The [2009 Commission archive mirror](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip) contains the 28-page Team 7 Box 13 Request 14 response PDF acquired in the [prior review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/12f56e7554306cebd6998979492c00e602d610bb/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-request14-archive-office-call-2026-11-25/README.md). Its SHA-256 is `957eecd527e1f1ee56867704b630c81edb766e76b8519b95abb5e660ac335f20`. On 2026-11-25 we rendered and visually examined physical **page 1** at 144 dpi; the retained research-store PNG has SHA-256 `85e5a46afdbfa5f4dffa6489e7ef320f0c198410cefae3883031a2e55ffc9188`. The April 26, 2004 cover says “Enclosed are fifty pages documents” and states that Bates-stamped versions would be made available later. The text is reproduced as printed; it does not specify 50 separately countable attachments. The image is not republished here.

The archived PDF contains **28 physical pages including its cover**. The earlier 28-image reader likewise included a cover and 27 additional images. These counts, and the letter's **50 pages of enclosed documents**, remain separate observations. The record does not establish which pages were included in the initial delivery, how the later Bates-stamped version was assembled, or why the copies available now contain 28 physical pages. Subtracting the counts does not identify missing pages, establish who omitted anything, or demonstrate withholding or intent. The page-similarity tests do not answer those production questions.
