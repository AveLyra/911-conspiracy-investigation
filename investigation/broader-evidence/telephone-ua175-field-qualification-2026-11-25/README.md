# Qualification: Flight 175 duration fields have a prior explanation

**AUD016-A1 / TC05-B105 / TC06-B110.** Our recent call-record reports left native field definitions as TODOs. That is correct for the original vendor specification, but a reader could incorrectly infer that no explanation exists at all. The investigation had already acquired and published a 2004 briefing that describes the fields. This is a qualification of our wording and attribution to existing work, not a new carrier finding.

## Source and observations

The May 13, 2004 [Flight 175 briefing, NARA 2609807, PDF p. 3](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/legislative/rg-148/2524787/t-0148-911MFR-00216.pdf#page=3), describes **Duration Connect** as seconds taken to connect to the dialed number, and **SCP Duration** as seconds the call was connected to that number. It describes operator duration separately. The same page lists the Starfix entries **08:52:01 / 75 seconds**, **08:56:19 / 31 seconds**, and **08:57:28 / No Conn**. The acquired three-page copy has SHA-256 `e581efca33a84473d2f08f577f328123759b176d2513c47adc1ba958bd9c980c`; page 3 was visually rechecked November 25, 2026. Its historical Commission-sensitive and law-enforcement-sensitive markings and redactions remain on the scan; no raw scan or personal telephone information is reproduced here. The briefing is a reported explanation, not the native vendor specification.

The [October 29 review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/681797a20a39d8c30e367c2220be15a191e3a409/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-may13-briefings-2026-10-29/README.md) already preserved those definitions, the call labels and the ambiguous `2?7` carrier reading. A [separate FBI-recorded carrier-manager explanation](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/f120c724216fa87619a2c966612da8f6a597b1fc/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-ua93-office-call-field-correction-2026-11-25/README.md) gives a similar description for a Flight 93 spreadsheet. The [earlier Flight 175 comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/e86f6556620057e14b03dc8138b6e125e355c656/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-ua175-summary-review-2026-10-28/README.md) reports **18 / 75** and **24 / 31** in Connect / SCP columns for the two corresponding time labels. These documents may share underlying records and are not independent timing measurements.

If the reported definitions apply to these particular fields and records, the numbers describe different call stages; that is a testable interpretation, not proof of the original event sequence. The 2002 letter's two reported connections, one substantiated conversation and 267-second description of the third attempt alongside an unconnected label remain preserved. Do not fill the obscured `2?7` value from another source or infer that a positive value means a conversation.
