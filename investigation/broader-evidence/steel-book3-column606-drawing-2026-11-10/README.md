# A contract-drawing match for the photographed Column 606 mark

Public derivative of the November 10, 2026 source review. STL02–STL03 / AUD011 / B188 / H005. The Berkeley photograph shows `606 A` and the numbers `48` and `51`. The archived Tower A core-column schedule contains a `606A` row with upper splice story `51` and lower splice story `48`. That narrows the intended design location and provides a dimensional test for a candidate physical piece. [Berkeley 504, physical p. 3](https://digicoll.lib.berkeley.edu/record/291574/files/504-WTC-Astaneh-a-photo-of-steel-member-with-identification-number-October-2001.pdf#page=3) · [Contract drawing 3-A1-15, physical p. 19](https://archive.org/download/WTCI000013L/WTCI-000013-L.PDF#page=19)

## Observations

- **Index and definitions.** The scanned index on physical p. 3 pairs `3-A1-15` with `606A` and `3-A1-31` with `806A`. The notes on physical p. 4, items 1–7 and 14–15, define the tower suffix, upper and lower splice stories, type, nominal built-up box dimensions, rolled shape, required yield strength and lower splice detail. The **A** in the schedule denotes a tower suffix. NIST identifies Tower A with WTC 1 in its account of marking conventions. ## Conflicting representations and source limits

- **Visible drawing versus OCR.** The scanned p. 19 note reads `FOR STORY 106 SPLICE SEE DWG 3-AB2-3`. The searchable derivative's sorted extraction, p. 19 line 11, reads `w  FOR STORY 106 SPLICE  SEE DWG 3-A62-3^)`. The text layer on that page omits the left-hand column identifiers and story fields in the inspected row area. The visible table and OCR remain separately preserved. The selected OCR is unsuitable for a complete row comparison. [Image-container p. 19](https://archive.org/download/WTCI000013L/WTCI-000013-L.PDF#page=19) · [Text PDF p. 19](https://archive.org/download/WTCI000013L/WTCI-000013-L.pdf#page=19)
- **Field versus schedule ordering.** The photograph shows `48` followed by `51` with uncertain intervening punctuation. The drawing orders `51` then `48` under upper and lower splice fields. The proposed interpretation is that the photographed numbers name those endpoints. The original marking key and shop/erection sheets would test the semantics of the photographed order. The `53` diamond, `10 TON`, and `SOUTH LEFT` remain separate observations. [Berkeley 504, p. 3](https://digicoll.lib.berkeley.edu/record/291574/files/504-WTC-Astaneh-a-photo-of-steel-member-with-identification-number-October-2001.pdf#page=3)
- **File lineage.** The Internet Archive item labels the uppercase filename as an original upload and the lowercase PDF as a derivative. Each has 146 physical pages. Both are public archive representations of the drawing book. Their byte and format differences establish distinct files within one archive chain. The museum lists a separate Tower A index image at accession `2004.02.2646`, item 486, finding-aid line 10443. The catalog description alone leaves its identity with the scanned index open. ## Interpretation and open tests

The label and story pair support intended WTC 1 core column 606 as a specific design match. The dimension and lower-splice fields allow a test if the member, end views, or calibrated dimensions become available. The photograph-to-design comparison leaves installation, actual dimensions, yard and cutting history, NIST accession, and current custody open. The 806A comparison controls only for the non-uniqueness of the floor pair. It supplies no independent reading of the photographed first digit.

- [ ] **STL02.** Seek the May 1, 1967 fabrication-marking communication, 606A shop and erection sheets, type 327 and splice 301E details, and field measurements or end views. Test the 606A dimensional signature and compare the 806A control. Trace the photographer's original image and any custody, salvage or disposition ledger.
- [ ] **STL03.** Preserve the earlier C-90 caption and floor differences and the W14A/B attribution gap as distinct questions. Seek the original classification and custody worksheets for those specimens. This tower drawing supplies no crosswalk to those items.
