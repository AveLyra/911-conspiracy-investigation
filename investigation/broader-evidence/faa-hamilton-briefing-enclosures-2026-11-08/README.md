# FAA briefing table and caveat in Hamilton's hearing materials

Public derivative of the source comparison. Original archive access derivatives and OCR are preserved in the native study.

November 8, 2026 Eastern. B129 / PD01 / PD07.

The archived FAA background materials pair a table giving **10:08** for United 93 military notification with a narrative giving **0916:00** and explicitly questioning that time. The two pages match distinctive fields on physical pages **1 and 5** of the separately archived FAA file **AWA-922**. A companion staff explanation says the briefing was received in response to subpoena. This is direct evidence of the pages and their placement in the surviving hearing materials, plus an attributed receipt statement. The precise subpoena return, assembly date and pre-hearing distribution remain open.
