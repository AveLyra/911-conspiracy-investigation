# CIA decision, dissemination and preservation leads: independent source verification

October 26, 2026. This check supports the core reported leads as statements in government reviews, while retaining unresolved originals, conflicting explanations and adverse findings. It adds eight linked research entries, CL001–CL008 / M153–M160, including one newly noticed low-weight elapsed-time difference. These are not eight independent evidence chains or findings of conspiracy.

The most useful concrete preservation lead is CIA physical p.78 / printed p.53 footnote13: it reports the late-2002 CTC DUCS database deletion, uncertainty about when this draft was deleted, and recovery of a surviving January13 Lotus attachment in January2004. The same page describes an earlier instruction to retain this particular draft. All those statements must stay together.

**Type:** Source-supported review finding; original messages unacquired.

The review found no reply. DOJ III.A.4 separately reports the attributed note and unanswered mid-January inquiry.

- The attribution in the analyst's note is not an acquired direct order, recording or authenticated identity of the actual decision-maker.
- CIA pp.78 and80 / printed pp.53 and55 and DOJ III.A.4 describe access by FBI detailees. Failure to disseminate the formal CIR does not mean no FBI employee could access the underlying information.
- Both reviews report failed formal dissemination. Repetition through related oversight reviews is not another independent contemporaneous witness.

### CL003 / M155 — DUCS database deletion, a retention instruction and a surviving Lotus copy are different preservation events

**Type:** Source-supported preservation lead; deletion purpose and exact scope unverified.

CIA physical p.78 / printed p.53 footnote13 says CTC deleted its DUCS database in late 2002 when DUCS was decommissioned. It expressly says the review did not know when this particular draft cable was ultimately deleted. The copy obtained in January 2004 was the version preserved in the January 13, 2000 Lotus Note.

- The same page reports mid-February instructions to delete other dead cables while specifically retaining this draft CIR; DOJ III.A.4 likewise reports that retention request. Those instructions do not establish what was ultimately retained or migrated.
- Database deletion is a reported event in a later review, not an acquired native deletion log. The surviving email copy rules out a claim that no copy survived, but does not establish completeness of all versions, attachments, backups or audits.
- Decommissioning is the reported context. It does not itself establish lawful retention compliance, improper destruction or an intent to obstruct oversight.

**Source locations:** CIA-MIRROR [78](https://documents.theblackvault.com/documents/cia/DOC_0006184107.pdf#page=78); printed 53 · [DOJ-CH5: III.A.4](https://oig.justice.gov/sites/default/files/archive/special/0506/chapter5.htm); derivative [B087](SOURCE-EXCERPTS.md#doj-b087).

**Next discriminator:** Obtain DUCS decommissioning, migration, retention schedules/holds, backups and deletion/audit records; join them to the draft's versions, the mid-February retain instruction and the surviving Lotus attachment.

**Limit:** No finding that every copy was destroyed, that this draft was selectively targeted, or that deletion concealed attack involvement.

## Bearing on the investigation

The findings support questions about permission to disseminate, unverifiable claims of passage, preservation of electronic records, oversight search completeness and the evidentiary basis of retrospective explanations. The strongest objection to a deliberate-conspiracy interpretation is that the acquired records are later reviews with missing originals, memory limitations and documented alternative channels; they do not establish the motive for the specific hold. Conversely, those limits do not erase affirmative findings of repeated information-sharing and operational failures or turn an unresolved delivery claim into proved receipt.

The record can test institutional accountability without presupposing an attack-facilitation operation. Deliberate withholding, mistaken passage claims, editing/release problems, incomplete records and administrative failures remain hypotheses to discriminate with actual records; none is selected here. No link to WTC7 access, demolition preparation, manufactured attribution or a higher-level attack order is established. Identity guesses and secondary-site real-name identifications are excluded.

All five BR bundles and ten bank/phone BPR targets remain in the preceding master. BR03 now has a review-report mirror available while its native draft/routing/recipient records remain absent. The earlier CIA maintenance-page observation remains true for that request and is not rewritten. New questions are tracked in [OPEN-QUESTIONS.json](OPEN-QUESTIONS.json).
