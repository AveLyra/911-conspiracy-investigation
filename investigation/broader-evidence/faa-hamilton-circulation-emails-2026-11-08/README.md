## Newly inspected routing and position evidence

**June 4, 9:31 AM, displayed forward from Chris Kojm to Lee Hamilton.** Kojm describes staff dissatisfaction over referral and circulation, reports a conversation with Hamilton the preceding day, and asks for guidance. He reports hearing-related legal advice and expresses his own preference to **“forget about referrals”**. That is a documented expressed position, not an adopted Commission decision, proof that the reported advice was actually given, or proof of wrongful concealment. The actual advice, meeting outcome and resulting instructions remain retrieval targets. Source item **VAB8595-03991**, p.1, OCR lines 2–26.

The same copy preserves materially different positions. The quoted Shaeffer response disputes a consensus to delay referral or withhold the memo, describes an earlier dissent and requests prompt commissioner circulation. Marcus recalls agreement to defer and restrict circulation, while also expecting commissioners to be briefed on the concerns. Zelikow describes hearing/ethics constraints, recalls proposing immediate referral, and states a preference to decide the issue rather than leave it unresolved. Preserve these positions separately; a single forwarding chain is not independent confirmation of each participant's recollection. Item 03991, p.2 OCR 35–50; p.3 OCR 6–24 and 32–48; p.4 OCR 2–12; p.5 OCR 15–40; p.6 OCR 4–10.

## June 10 binder email: plans and attachment reference

Item **VAB8595-04020** displays Stephanie Kaplan to Commissioners, **June 10, 2004, 6:54 PM**, with the hearing-binders subject. The body identifies a **revised agenda** as attached. It describes expected delivery of a Day One briefing binder the next morning and separate Day Two binders later the next day or early Saturday; it also describes planned team briefings. It does not name the June 7 evidence memo as an attachment. The agenda bytes, binder assembly list, delivery records and recipient acknowledgments were not acquired. Source p.1, OCR 3–19. [Manifest](https://digitalcollections.iu.edu/concern/archival_materials/js957486c/manifest).

The June 7 memo's placement in one archived hearing-material sequence remains a distinct observation. The present records strengthen the routing trail and establish a recorded delivery plan. They do not prove that the memo was included in every delivered binder, that delivery occurred as planned, or that all commissioners read it.
