# WTC steel preservation: loss, survivors and disputed investigative access

Local AUD011/AUD016 research. This pass disposes substantively of the existing B188/H005 preservation claims at the released-record level; it does not complete the full goal or authenticate a specimen's history. Earlier assessments and FAA/CIA chains remain unchanged. No publication, chat delivery, commit/push, outreach, fees, filing, legal-record promotion or deletion.

**The preservation criticism survives scrutiny.** Steel was processed before complete engineering examination, some marked specimens were accidentally processed, and the resulting losses prevent particular physical tests. **The stronger claim that all useful steel disappeared, or no materials investigation occurred, is contradicted.** Surviving steel, coupons, inventories, photographs and published metallurgical work exist. WTC 7 presents a narrower unresolved problem: field-attributed candidates reached NIST's published inventory, but were classified as unidentified, and their item-specific attribution rationale remains unacquired.

This is evidence of impaired scrutiny and preservation failures. It is not evidence identifying a particular concealed collapse mechanism. The agency's loss of an independent physical check limits confidence that can rest on that check; it neither validates nor falsifies its numerical collapse sequence by itself.

## New primary record and the intervention claim

The held May1,2002 hearing reproduces a **March6 hearing charter**. Its account of an NSF-funded researcher's intervention and the city's unwillingness to suspend recycling is committee background, not a May1 witness's direct account or a reproduced disposal order. The newly acquired March6 hearing supplies Astaneh-Asl's first-person account of unsuccessful city contacts followed by recycler access; it does not reproduce the original request, city reply, contract or name the refusing official. Corley assigned the sales decision to the city, with uncertainty about its first date. Those distinctions prevent assigning a specific order to a named official without evidence. [March6 hearing](https://commdocs.house.gov/committees/science/hsy77747.000/hsy77747_0.HTM), printed182–184; [May1 reprint](https://commdocs.house.gov/committees/science/hsy78961.000/hsy78961_0.htm), printed27.

The report-level concern is stronger than an abstract inference from a missing specimen: attempts to obtain access and continued processing are affirmatively described. But continued access to a working salvage yard, a blanket suspension of recycling, and legal authority to impound individual items are different remedies. Astaneh-Asl's testimony does not independently prove the charter's exact suspension-request wording.

## Survivors and the WTC7 attribution question

FEMA Appendix D's complete27page edition and all13 spreadsheet pages were recovered in earlier work. The older14page mirror lacks the rows; that acquisition limitation must not be repeated as a current gap. The fresh review reuses those bytes and rechecks the disposition note, accidental-processing entry and candidate rows. The named source is the **published PDF summary**, not the native workbook/version history. [Official Appendix D](https://www.fema.gov/pdf/library/fema403_apd.pdf), physical14–16.

The candidate crosswalk already established—and this pass rechecks—that FEMA A/W14A and B/W14B correspond to NIST's W-14A or A and W-14B. NIST expressly describes retaining original identifiers, while noting dual codes and U-series renaming. TableA-1 gives the two entries locations205 andPL; TableA-7 places them among unidentified wide-flange sections. Their building/column/floor fields are blank. The two tables are one inventory, not two independent custody chains. Locations refer to the report period, not verified storage today. [NCSTAR1-3B](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-3b.pdf), printed5,64,68.

A blanket statement that no FEMA-WTC7-attributed candidate reached NIST is incompatible with that documentary record. A narrower statement that no received piece was accepted as unambiguously WTC7 remains compatible. Neither fact determines whether field attribution was mistaken, documentation inadequate, or the agency's rejection unsupported. The original yard/intake photographs, dimensions, drawings, transfer logs and attribution worksheets could discriminate those possibilities. No exact C12a/b match appears in the searched six-page TableA-1 under the tested forms; this is not proof of nonreceipt under another identifier. No Sample1 join is established.

NIST's own material-properties report identifies the practical consequence: its WTC7steel properties were estimated rather than measured from confidently attributed recovered material, and it could not perform the corresponding metallography. That is a real limit on an independent validation route; it is not proof the adopted material values were wrong. The separate inventory report's qualification must accompany the broader recovery language. [NCSTAR1-3](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-3.pdf), printed114–115.

FEMA Appendix C documents metallurgical examination of a different specimen that **appeared** to be from WTC7, with original building location unknown. Its more categorical heading cannot erase that introductory qualifier. It reports severe corrosion/sulfidation and leaves sulfur source, rate and pre-/post-collapse timing unresolved. This contradicts “nobody examined potentially WTC7steel,” but does not authenticate a connection to a specific failed support, the inventory candidates, a demolition device or the initiating event. [Appendix C](https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/2017/05/09/AppendixC-fema403_apc.pdf), C-1,C-13. No metallurgy was experimentally replicated here.

## Disclosure, liability and scope of inference

The hearing records concern about restricted drawings and agreements described by lawmakers as preventing testimony. Corley calls the Port Authority agreement unusual; at the close he clarifies that FEMA's confidentiality agreement was routine. The actual Port Authority instrument remains unacquired, so its complete terms/effect cannot be established from a question alone. Shea offers scientific-deliberation protection as a confidentiality rationale. These accounts require distinct evaluation rather than one generic secrecy claim.

Liability protection is a documented institutional interest in the hearing account. It warrants testing actual agreements, correspondence and timing. It is not a demonstrated intent to hide demolition. Conversely, rescue needs or ordinary salvage incentives cannot erase the documented lost opportunities, and the chairman's benign interpretation is an interpretation, contested within the same hearing. Avoiding delay and preserving selected evidence were not necessarily mutually exclusive.

The May1 discussion of911call transcripts supplies another correction: Barnett withdrew his request because the model would not be ready for the BPAT report; Corley said additional news video still existed and remained useful. That exchange is not evidence those recordings were destroyed, and it concerns neither the FAA recollection cassette nor CIA interrogation tapes. No new media listening occurred.

## What loss actually prevents testing

For a member already recycled without an adequate coupon or record, investigators lose direct tests of fracture surface, connection/weld behavior, deformation and cut chronology, paint/fireproofing condition, local metallurgy and chemical gradients. Recovering a safely selected coupon can preserve some material tests but not full-member geometry or all surface evidence. Photographs may preserve shape and markings; they do not reproduce the full chemical specimen. Sample coverage is not a random sample permitting an easy missing-percentage correction.

Where location and identity are unresolved, even a surviving piece cannot directly validate a named column/floor sequence. For the two received candidates, loss of the **attribution record** is not yet established: we have not acquired it. This leaves a feasible documentary/photographic test rather than proof all physical testing is permanently impossible. Prospective testing would additionally require present specimen existence, access, authorization and a frozen comparative protocol; none is claimed here.
