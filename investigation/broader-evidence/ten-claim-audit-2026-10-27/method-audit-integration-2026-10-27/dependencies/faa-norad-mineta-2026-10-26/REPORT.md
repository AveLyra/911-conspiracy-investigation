## Mineta: preserve testimony and uncertainty together

The complete acquired May23,2003 transcript contains the exchange. Mineta describes hearing the approach-distance reports and the orders-still-stand reply, explicitly identifies the aircraft as the one that hit the Pentagon, says he arrived about09:20 and places the exchange five or six minutes later. He also repeatedly says he did not know the particular order; shootdown was his inference. Neither an explicit stand-down instruction nor an authenticated shootdown order is in that testimony. His recollection remains affirmative evidence to investigate, not something erased by its uncertain interpretation. FA005/FA011; H03-P060–077/P089–117.

The later Commission account places Cheney’s shelter-conference-room arrival perhaps09:58, expressly acknowledges conflicting evidence, and describes later incoming-aircraft/engagement exchanges after10:10. It admits no documentary evidence for the purported immediate post-arrival authorization call and that sources were incomplete. Its inference does not independently refute Mineta here; Mineta’s recollection does not independently invalidate the multiple cited logs. Do not turn an uncertain flight/time reconstruction into either proof of nonintervention or dismissal of a real discrepancy. FA006; H04-P240–246; staff17pp23–25.
