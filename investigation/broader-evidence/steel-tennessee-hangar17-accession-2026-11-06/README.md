## Reported transfer

The receiving museum dates its application to **January 6, 2016** and reports an **April 18, 2016** approval naming **F-0032.003** and **F-0032.005**. It reports that a fine-art courier picked up the material at **JFK Hangar 17 on May 25, 2016** and delivered it in **Nashville on June 21, 2016** after a legal-agreement exchange and transport planning. The 2026 museum account is the source for all four dates. The photos independently expose the printed numbers within that same publication, but cannot independently date approval or movement. [Museum transfer account](https://tnmuseum.org/Stories/posts/how-a-file-cabinet-from-the-world-trade-center-site-came-to-the-tennessee-state-museum#:~:text=April%2018%2C%202016)

For the steel provenance inquiry, this is a concrete control case. The museum describes Hangar 17 holdings that include an office artifact from an adjacent building. A WTC Archive tag can establish inventory association while leaving a specific origin building to other evidence. The cabinet and drawer give no item-specific evidence about the WTC 7 steel candidates or their custody. [Museum origin account](https://tnmuseum.org/Stories/posts/how-a-file-cabinet-from-the-world-trade-center-site-came-to-the-tennessee-state-museum#:~:text=130%20Liberty%20Street) · [Earlier steel-tag comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/f45c447b37b03af71f8f9440c979ceac49a1d1c8/investigation/broader-evidence/steel-hangar17-inventory-page-2026-11-06/README.md)
