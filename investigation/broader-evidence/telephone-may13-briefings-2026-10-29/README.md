## The briefers' definitions — preserve attribution and scope

[UA175 physical p. 1](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/legislative/rg-148/2524787/t-0148-911MFR-00216.pdf#page=1) says Commission staff received a spreadsheet titled **UA 175 GTE Airfone Records**. Pages **2–3** describe its fields. The memo attributes the work to a study of GTE records with technical assistance from GTE engineers, cellular-record examination and recipient/family interviews. The briefers' confidence that they identified all completed calls is their assessment, not an independently reproduced result here.

| Field / locator | Explanation recorded in the MFR | Remaining limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Duration Connect**, p. 3, second bullet | Time in seconds taken for the call to be connected to the dialed number. | A reported setup-time definition; not automatically conversation length or proof that every attempted setup succeeded. Failure/default/timeout rules remain missing. |
| **Duration Operator**, p. 3, third bullet | Time an operator is on the line; none for the UA175 calls. | That bullet does not separately state the unit. It does not authenticate a particular UA93 operator reading or identify which ground/air leg continued. |
| **SCP (Switch Control Processor) Duration**, p. 3, fourth bullet | Time in seconds the call was connected to the dialed number. | The source's expansion and definition are preserved. Vendor/version rules, answer supervision, transfers, disconnects and actual logged behavior are not independently verified. |
| **ACIS Call ID**, p. 2, first bullet | A unique computer-assigned identifier for each Airfone call. | Source-level uniqueness statement; no allocation/reuse rules or authentication of the retained images' keys. |
| Name columns, p. 2, fifth bullet | Names appear in this spreadsheet where known within United's system; DOJ added names on other versions based on interviews with recipients. | Direct evidence of a reported compilation process. A name in a later chart is not necessarily a name independently generated by the carrier. Actual worksheets/interviews/version history remain needed. |
| Time fields, pp. 1–2 | UA175's zone field was blank because calls were within one zone; a later bullet says its call date/time was Eastern. The note says two UA93 calls occurred while the plane was in the Central zone. | These are source explanations, not a clock calibration or identification of the exact two UA93 rows. Do not convert INT into an authenticated timezone. |
| Terminal/distribution units and seat location, p. 2 | Combined unit fields locate the Airfone and seat bank. | The receiving data and physical installation/configuration still need authentication; seat-bank labels are not independent proof of caller identity. |

The same pages discuss a final dialed code, validation, handoffs and operator involvement. No credit-card approval, positive setup value or named row is treated as proof a particular person held a conversation. These GTE explanations are not silently applied to AA77's separate AT&T/Claircom records.

## UA175: a source-specific control against confusing the duration fields

[UA175 p. 3, call table](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/legislative/rg-148/2524787/t-0148-911MFR-00216.pdf#page=3) has **11 rows: nine numeric length labels and two No Conn labels**. The earlier [UA175 review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/9ea289e6e2d7ec284eff654ed96f52364b91edb6/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-ua175-summary-review-2026-10-28/README.md) preserved positive Connect readings or positive candidates for all eleven summary rows. The two No Conn time labels are **08:54:14** and **08:57:28**. This supplies a direct comparison control: positive values in a field described as setup time must not simply be counted as completed conversations.

The 75/31 labels agree with the earlier selected SCP readings, while 18/24 occupy a differently described field. All readings and descriptions remain side by side; no preferred historical value is selected. The memo supplies an attributed interpretation to test against the native export, not a reconstruction of how failed attempts were recorded. Unaccepted fields are not set to zero and **2?7 is not filled from a different source's 267**.

The 2002 United letter's three attempts / two reported connections / one substantiated conversation remains preserved. Its 267-second description of the third attempt is not erased by the later No Conn label. Receipt, network connection, an attempt and a conversation remain different assertions. The newly acquired memo is part of the same investigation/source chain, not independent measurement of the calls.

## UA93: retain the long durations, zeros and uncertain readings

[UA93 pp. 2–3](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/legislative/rg-148/2524787/t-0148-911MFR-00217.pdf#page=2) lists **35 Airfone rows** in eleven seat-bank blocks, with **26 positive-second entries and nine zero-second entries**, followed by two separately labeled cellular entries. This is a count of what the MFR records, not 35 verified conversations, 37 independent callers or an independent recreation of the FBI's reconstruction.

The **09:37:41** row in block 4 explicitly says **7,565 seconds**. The **09:43:48 operator** row in block 5 explicitly says **3,925 seconds**. Keep these beside the [earlier source comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/b2ac3d295182d0bc48fd53b6255b188caf54e9b1/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-full-row-comparison-2026-10-28/README.md): summary SCP **7565** versus carrier-copy SCP **7555**, and uncertain operator readings **3?25** versus **14?5**. The clearer MFR does not retroactively make an uncertain glyph certain, prove independent corroboration or explain the differences. These long labels do not establish an ongoing airborne conversation after a crash; source-specific leg, disconnect, timeout and billing history are still required.

A limited comparison finds **28 unique same-printed-time candidates** between the new MFR and the preserved earlier UA93 readings. Of these, **23 have an accepted prior summary SCP value, and all 23 match the MFR's listed seconds**. These are candidate time associations only. Two repeated-time groups remain ambiguous, and three MFR time strings do not exactly occur in the earlier transcription. Matching magnitudes do not prove the definition is implemented correctly or the source charts are independent.

## Audit our own readings before calling a difference suspicious

Two of those three apparent time differences may be **our earlier transcription errors**. The old dataset contains **09:37:01** and **09:48:46**; the MFR has **09:37:03** and **09:48:48**. A fresh inspection of the unchanged low-resolution summary image favors the latter readings. The carrier counterpart is harder to read. Both the old dataset and current observations are preserved, with no source rewritten. These are reading-quality questions, not established FBI alterations or historical timing conflicts. The earlier tests checked supplied transcriptions and therefore could not certify glyph accuracy.

Another candidate comparison retains **08:32:39 / INT** in the earlier summary reading versus **09:32:29** in the new UA93 MFR, p. 3, block 7 second row. The source's general note about two Central-zone calls does not establish this row's conversion or remove the differing seconds. The MFR separately retains **08:30:32** in block 11. No common-event identity, clock offset, recording delay, cause or controlling timestamp is adjudicated. Seek the original export, clock fields and briefing/summary compilation worksheets before treating these as calibrated event differences.
