## March–April 2002 correspondence

A **March 9, 2004** FAA reply answers the Commission staff's **March 8, 2004** request for FAA–NORAD communication about release of a post-attack briefing using FAA radar data. It describes the March and April 2002 emails as the documentation held and says no formal approval process was required. The supplied pages preserve three different steps. On **March 29, 2002**, the liaison reports leaving the incoming letter with FAA Headquarters branch heads and describes a prospective mail or fax of a FOIA release copy. On **April 3**, a NORAD correspondent refers to an email approving release of videos made using FAA radar data supplied by RADES and asks about the scope of that approval. The **April 4** reply gives a no-limitations or restrictions answer in that exchange. Keep the 2004 statement about formal process, the earlier reported approval and the prospective dispatch distinct. The original letter, release copy and video files remain unacquired. These pages do not establish the September 17 press-draft receipt. Physical pp. 1–4, p. 1 lines 6–14, p. 2 lines 5–8, p. 3 lines 9–23 and p. 4 lines 14–19.
