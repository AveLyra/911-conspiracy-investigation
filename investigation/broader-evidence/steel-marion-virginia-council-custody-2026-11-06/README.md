## What the records can test

The heading's presentation language and the body's prospective wording remain an unresolved difference in how the first meeting describes the status. The later sources add design and display milestones. None of the reviewed passages supplies a completed transfer, a dated pickup or receipt, or an actual installation date. The university's building description lacks a cited member mark or recovery record. The design team and town could share the same provenance account, so their records provide separate milestones with unresolved source dependence on the object's origin. Neither a WTC 7 attribution nor a connection to a FEMA or NIST specimen is established.
