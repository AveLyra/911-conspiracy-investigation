Its member size and ZIP CRC were checked on acquisition November 25; SHA-256 `a14b7e47d2225c727415547eccd1d825bb50492302641d8f8b29217372026eee`. Text of all three pages was extracted; physical page 3 was visually checked. The memorandum is a staff account, not a recording of the 2004 interview.

## Conflicting and qualifying descriptions

- The **2001 FBI-hosted text**, document 127, gives a call received at approximately **8:45 a.m. Central**, seven minutes of conversation before a reported cockpit entry, and another eight minutes of conversation. Document 128 reports a last exchange at approximately **9:00 a.m. Central** and approximately **20 minutes** holding the connection open thereafter. It separately describes a later call involving another passenger; preserve those events separately.
- The **2004 memorandum**, page 3, reports that Jefferson held on the phone for **15 minutes** after the last words she heard, then the call was terminated. These 15- and 20-minute descriptions are estimates in different reports, not carrier-measured durations. The same page records her belief that information through the closing of the first-class curtain described events that had **already happened** and that Beamer was updating her. This later qualification matters when reading the earlier report's ordering; it does not independently establish when the cockpit was entered, prove which account is correct, or resolve the clock discrepancy.
- The same **2004 page 3** says Jefferson took a few basic notes on a small pad and says the FBI had those notes. This is a report of her statement in 2004; it does not establish the notes' current existence, completeness, precise contents or disposition. The source also records her uncertainty about whether another operator took notes. Preserve the worksheet's earlier requests for GTE logs, operator notes and recording information alongside this more specific notes lead.
