# Raleigh approved a World Trade Center steel donation in January 2012

November 6, 2026 UTC · STL02–STL03 / AUD011 / AUD016 / B188. Raleigh's archived January 17 minutes record an **8–0** vote approving acceptance of an approximately **24-inch** piece of steel attributed to one of the fallen towers. The minutes preserve different positions from the City Attorney's Office and City Administration on the proposed contract. A local fire history separately describes World Trade Center steel on display at the Raleigh Fire Museum. The contract, shipment and accession records would be needed to connect the approved item to the displayed steel.
