## PD01. The recovered QFR packet answers a different request

**AWA-819, NARA 34913651**, is a 19-page public scan. The signed cover is dated **June 8, 2004** and identifies the Commission's **May 25, 2004** *Questions for the Record No. 1* request. The enclosure begins with National Airspace closure and reopening questions. It includes later questions about flight activity and records. The page image confirms both cover dates. The catalog's generic title gives a useful locator, while the letter itself supplies the date and scope. [Cover, PDF p. 1](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialive/51/9136/34913651/content/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-e-other/5-AWA-819-Letter-Responses-to-Questions-for-the-Record-No-1-REDACT-SEALED.pdf#page=1) · [Catalog item](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913651)

DOT OIG separately identifies the earlier **May 22, 2003** QFR answer, titled *FAA Communications with NORAD on September 11, 2001*, submitted that night and read into the hearing record on **May 23, 2003**. Its report describes inaccurate statements on American 77 notification and when the Air Force liaison joined the phone bridge. The recommendation to correct that earlier answer remains a distinct record trail. AWA-819 supplies the actual dated 2004 cover and enclosure. The 2003 corrected answer and recipient acknowledgment remain unacquired. [DOT OIG CC-2006-085, physical pp. 9–10 and 12](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=9)

The prior AWA-819 media request saved a **5,454-byte HTML body** despite HTTP 200. The migrated public route returned **2,328,905 PDF bytes** and HTTP 200. Both observations are retained. The prior response is an access result rather than another version of the letter. The N90 Tab 20 attempt in this study returned HTML with the same hash as that earlier response. The ZNY Tab 18 response advertised a PDF, but the download stopped at the configured size check before any body was saved. Neither tab was read. [Source and access pins](SOURCE-PINS.json)

The **New York Center** and **New York TRACON** readings for the October 28 batch remain side by side. The inventory and facility list corroborate the existence of distinct named facilities and records under each. The N90 memorandum corroborates a follow-up records inquiry. The sources reviewed here do not tie either facility to the exact batch. A local reproducibility check preserved the separate columns and three ZNY position codes after visual inspection of the table, including the **Full Partial** entry. An itemized Commission receipt, matched recording labels or a dated certification for the October 28 batch would be discriminating evidence.
