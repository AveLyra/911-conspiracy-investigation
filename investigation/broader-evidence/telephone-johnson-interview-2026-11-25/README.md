## Observations and comparisons

- **Time and handoff:** p. 23 reports Johnson answered at approximately **8:40 a.m. Central**, put the caller on hold and consulted Jefferson. Page 24 gives her total speaking/consulting interval as **less than ten minutes**, including estimated holds. In the [September 2001 Jefferson report](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/f8bca24df9dba31adedf6ddf2db31b29bca82431/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-combs-jefferson-originals-2026-11-25/README.md), Jefferson's call-receipt estimate is **8:45 a.m. Central**. The two operators describe different positions in a reported handoff; the times are estimates, not automatically conflicting measurements of the same event. Clock source, exact handoff and call-leg matching remain open.
- **Flight and names:** Johnson's p. 23 says the caller identified himself as Beamer and, as she recalls, said **“United flight 99”** from Newark to San Francisco. The Jefferson report describes **UAL Flight 93**. In the same Johnson page, **“JOHNSON”** and **“JACKSON”** both appear in the account. These are the visible records, not corrected values. The interview notes, source statements and carrier record are needed to test transcription, recollection or other untested explanations. The scan alone does not establish a different flight or deliberate falsification.
- **Screen print:** Johnson's p. 24 says a print of administrative call information was made **when the call was disconnected** and was to be passed to engineering to determine the aircraft location. The separate [September 2001 Combs interview](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/f8bca24df9dba31adedf6ddf2db31b29bca82431/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-combs-jefferson-originals-2026-11-25/README.md), p. 6, describes Jefferson making a print screen **during** a conversation to document payment information and says it was provided to an agent. These descriptions may concern one or more prints; no identity, contents, timing or custody join has been established. Do not publish personal/payment fields.
- Johnson's p. 24 references an interview documented as **265A-NY-280350-OUT, serial 3255**. That reference is a target for verification, not proof of an independently acquired interview. The report does not resolve whether Johnson made contemporaneous notes.
