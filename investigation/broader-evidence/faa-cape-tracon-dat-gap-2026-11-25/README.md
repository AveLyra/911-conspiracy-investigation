# FAA Cape TRACON recording-gap memorandum

Reviewed 2026-11-25. A publicly released FAA memorandum identifies a specific gap in one Cape TRACON sector recording and supplies an agency explanation. It is a distinct recording lead; no connection to the [Boston Center disks or FAA intranet entries](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/0c90baf73b589663aefa5f61870ae6c76327df72/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-regional-voice-disks-nextday-2026-11-25/README.md) is established.

## Source observations

The [April 19, 2004 memorandum](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/e-Other/5-AWA-327-Memo-Voice-Recording-Minutes-Missing.pdf), from the Cape TRACON manager to the Presidential Commission inquiry, describes the **HIGH AR sector** recording for **1230 UTC to 1600 UTC** as missing **one minute and twenty seconds**. It says the first digital audio tape was pulled off at **1538.37** “to hold until further notice” and a second began recording at **1540 UTC**. The memo also describes the loss as **“almost two minutes.”** The two duration descriptions and both printed time values are preserved as written. The memo does not give seconds for its 1540 label or explain the notation and precision of 1538.37; an exact duration cannot be verified from these fields alone.

The author attributes the gap to the tape change. That is an identified source account, not an independently verified test of the original tapes, any contemporaneous instructions, or alternative channels. The memorandum does not establish what, if anything, was communicated during the interval, why the tape was pulled, or whether any other recording covers it. It does not establish intentional alteration, concealment or a connection to passenger calls. Do not treat the National Archives finding-aid category “Cleveland Center Addt'l Info-Memo” as the operating facility: the memorandum identifies **Cape TRACON** and **HIGH AR**. [NARA finding aid, box 94](https://www.archives.gov/research/foia/faa-finding-aid-84-95)
