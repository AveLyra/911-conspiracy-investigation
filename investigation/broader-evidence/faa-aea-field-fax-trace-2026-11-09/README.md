**November 9, 2026 | B129 / PD03–PD04 / PD07.** Four FAA scans add contemporaneous evidence of a 2001 collection request, two tower fax-machine reports and a handwritten response to an Eastern Region question. They identify specific records and routing fields for a search of the later production. The Eastern equipment assessment and actual October 2003 delivery remain unverified.

## September 2001 collection request

**DCA-52**, NAID 7596264, is an urgent Eastern Region QA email dated **9/17/01 1:29 PM**, marked **Receipt requested**. It says Washington headquarters still needs items, asks for electronic, fax or overnight forwarding, and separately identifies material already held. It lists **ZNY R-42** for UAL 175 and several outbound or sector transcripts for AAL 77. It asks for **inbound AAL 77 transmissions from IAD and DCA**, coordination on recorded lines, flight progress strips, employee statements and certified cassettes. The closing instructions request approximate notification times and suggest signed action timelines. The receipt field and request wording leave actual receipt and subsequent return open. Physical p. 1, sorted text lines 1–6, 10–25 and 28–33. [DCA-52](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/1-AAL-77/e-Other/1-DCA-52-Art-Blank-email.pdf)

The cassette bullet in the **AAL77** block has printed **UAL175** struck through and handwritten **AAL77** above it. The separate UAL 175 block also has its own cassette request. Preserve both printed labels, the strike-through and the handwritten label. Authorship, timing and any corrected copy actually circulated are unresolved. The AAL 77 header and the handwritten label identify a candidate match for a later return. Compare the outgoing message, received image and a cassette manifest before assigning the bullet to a particular production.
