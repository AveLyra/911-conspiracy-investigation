# Public military correction, retrospective allegation and the FAA records still missing

November 8, 2026 · B129 / PD01–PD04. This comparison adds a first-person retrospective source and checks its correction claim against a contemporary hearing. It does not recover the FAA's corrected2003answer, receipt, signed action/no-action determination or recommendation disposition.

## Three source accounts, kept separate

**2010 recollection.** The Miller Center dates Zelikow's interview **October4,2010**. He recalls demanding a military public correction and names the **“Chief of Staff of the Air Force”**, without identifying or dating that appearance. He alleges officer-level concealment of unreadiness, reports no evidence above that level, and says Myers continued a misleading account. These are retrospective assessments, not proof of intent or institutional findings. [Interview](https://millercenter.org/the-presidency/presidential-oral-histories/philip-zelikow-oral-history-part-ii) · derived lines168–170,390–400.

**Contemporary public record.** At the **June17,2004** hearing, Ben-Veniste asks Arnold whether he accepts the staff's factual conclusions; the transcript records Arnold's affirmative answer. Arnold also says he lacked or did not recall relevant facts at his earlier appearance. Eberhart separately denies a coordinated scheme to cover for anyone. The roster identifies Myers as **Chairman of the Joint Chiefs**, Eberhart as **NORAD commander**, and Arnold as **former CONR commander**. It does not identify this panel as an appearance by the Air Force chief of staff. That leaves the identity of the event Zelikow recalled unresolved; it does not prove his account false. [Archived hearing](https://govinfo.library.unt.edu/911/archive/hearing12/9-11Commission_Hearing_2004-06-17.htm) · derived lines25–29,297–302,332–359 and445–450.

**2006 review.** DoD's **September12,2006** public memorandum reports a March2004 letter validating the Commission chronology, while saying officials did not publicize revised timelines when they became aware of the discrepancies. It reports no evidence of knowing deception and no indication of further erroneous statements after awareness. Its discussion of a particular book distinguishes the dates and access of that book's inputs. It also says no written response to the report draft was required or received. Preserve this account alongside the public hearing and later recollection. [DoD memorandum, physical p.3](https://web.archive.org/web/20120707002543id_/http://www.dodig.mil/Ir/reports/06-intel-12.pdf#page=3) · held page3OCR lines4–23, visually checked against the page.

## What the comparison establishes and leaves open

The hearing is direct documentary evidence of a **public acceptance of revised facts** by the named witness. It is contrary evidence to a blanket claim that no public correction occurred. That narrow observation does not establish that every inaccurate statement was corrected, determine when each officer knew, or verify the revised operational chronology independently.

Keep the following unresolved distinctions visible:

| Question | Preserved observations | Missing discriminating evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Which public correction? | Undated officer-title recollection; dated hearing and named speakers; reported March letter. | Identified appearance/statement, letter and attachments, commissioning or preparation correspondence, and receipt. |
| What was done after knowledge? | Contemporary explanations/denial;2006 investigative assessment;2010 retrospective allegation. | Claim-by-claim evidence of knowledge dates, original timeline versions, dissemination and interviews, with the precise later publication/passage being challenged. |
| Which correction channel and period? | Communication to the Commission, public hearing testimony, broader publicity and later publications are separately described. | Original release/clearance/distribution records and time bounds behind the2006 assessment; no source is selected as controlling the others. |
| FAA follow-up? | These military-source accounts concern different statements and agency roles from the specificFAA-QFR recommendation. | Actual correctedFAAanswer/receipt, signed consideration/action/no-action record, and separate recommendation implementation/closure. |

No finding of coordinated deception, officer intent, attack participation or high-level involvement follows from combining these records. A witness allegation, witness denial and an investigative finding are different evidentiary categories; their differing scopes and dates remain unresolved.
