# Numbered NTSB request 01-096 and the Indianapolis shipment trail

November 5, 2026 · B129 / PD07. The preceding review located a **September 21, 2001** FAA inventory entry for forwarding the Washington Operations Center Activity Report to NTSB. The new FAA archive scans tie **01-096** to **September 20, 2001**, identify versions of the requested scope, and document a later facility shipment account. The September 21 report, the October facility shipment and the FAA's eventual response to the May 2003 Commission question each have their own delivery trail. ## What Indianapolis reported sending

- **September 25.** Indianapolis reports placing AAL77 radar data on a website that day. The email lists DART filenames **ZID_AAL77dartA.TXT**, **ZID_AAL77dartB.ZIP**, **ZID_AAL77dartC.ZIP**, and **ZID_AAL77dartD.TXT**, as well as an NTAP entry, and says the remaining data would follow by **10/09/01**. The email gives search keys. The website, file contents, any downloads and the decoding remain open. [ZID-78 p. 4, lines 1 and 8–38](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7596612)
- **October 9 shipment and October 10 follow-up.** The October 9 route slip identifies Indianapolis support data for **01-096**. The October 10 email reports that Indianapolis forwarded transcripts, voice tapes, personnel statements, operational logs and an ALNOT for **AAL77 and UAL93** by overnight mail on October 9. It reports that one statement was unfinished and was omitted, with faxing proposed. [ZID-122 p. 1, lines 7–14](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7596445) · [ZID-230 p. 8, lines 1 and 13–25](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7596524)
- **October 11 file note.** The note states that material was submitted **to NTSB on October 9**, and records **“STATEMENT NOT SENT. FAXED 10/11/01”** for the omitted statement. Keep the FAA headquarters routing and the file note's NTSB destination side by side. The note appears in two archived packets and supplies an FAA-side account of the fax. The NTSB receiving inventory and fax acknowledgment remain unlocated. [ZID-78 p. 1, lines 6–9 and 31](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7596612) · [ZID-230 p. 4, lines 10–14 and 58](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7596524)

This establishes dated FAA-side request and shipment records. The October material has a different date and sender from the September 21 entry for the Operations Center Activity Report. The October packet contents, recipient receipt, September 21 report version and source of the reported **09:26** event all remain open.
