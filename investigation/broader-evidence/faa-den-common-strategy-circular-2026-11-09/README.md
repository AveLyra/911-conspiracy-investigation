# The September 2006 FAA reporting circular and the DEN preservation question

November 9, 2026 Eastern. B129 / PD03–PD04. FAA Advisory Circular **90-103** supplies a dated reporting procedure close to DOT OIG's August 31, 2006 recommendations. It describes flight-crew interference reports over the Domestic Events Network (DEN). The two official PDF copies show the same extracted text, with one page-render difference. [FAA circular](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC90-103.pdf) · [GovInfo copy](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-TD4-PURL-LPS107703/pdf/GOVPUB-TD4-PURL-LPS107703.pdf) · [Source pins](SOURCE-PINS.json)

## Reporting, recording and preservation

- **September 11, 2006** appears on the circular and is the FAA catalogue issue date. Paragraph 4 gives **September 14, 2006** as its effective date. These are distinct fields. Paragraphs 5 and 6 direct immediate ATC reporting through the DEN and request immediate aircrew reporting to ATC for flight-crew interference. The ATC record fields include UTC, aircraft and operator identifiers, location, altitude, transponder code and disturbance level. [FAA catalogue](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/advisory_circulars/index.cfm/go/document.information/documentID/74456) · [Circular p. 2, paragraphs 4–6](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC90-103.pdf#page=2)
- The OIG report describes existing DEN recording and a six-month retention practice at the time of its review. Recommendation 3 calls for a formal policy to preserve recordings following notification of hijacked or suspicious aircraft. Recommendation 4 calls for reviewing and eliminating inconsistent or duplicative procedures. The circular gives a reporting route for its specified events. The full four-page text supplies no telephone-recording preservation clause, case-linked management response or recommendation disposition. [OIG report pp. 11–13](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=11)
- The circular's paragraph 9 cites **Common Strategy, Detailed Guidance** dated **January 3, 2005**, and **FAA Notice 7110.422**. The OIG footnote describes the Notice as dated **11/14/05**. The FAA catalogue gives **2005-12-01** as **Date issued** and **2006-11-30** as **Date cancelled**. Preserve the different date labels and their provenance. The reason for the two 2005 values and the actual notice version/withdrawal chain remain open. The catalogue labels the notice restricted. Its public metadata was read. [Circular p. 3, paragraph 9](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC90-103.pdf#page=3) · [OIG p. 11, footnote 7](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=11) · [FAA Notice catalogue](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/orders_notices/index.cfm/go/document.information/documentID/14486)

## Later cancellation and scope

The FAA's December 2, 2015 cancellation memo says the circular's information is in JO 7610.4, the AIM and AIP, and describes the aircrew reporting procedures as a required element of operators' TSA-approved security programs. The FAA catalogue records cancellation on that date. The memo offers a later route to the reporting rules. It leaves open the provision-by-provision version map, any central recording preservation requirement and the disposition of the OIG recommendations. The memo was read through its public PDF text index after the direct download timed out. ## Open records

- **PD03–PD04.** Obtain the dated administrative records linking each of recommendations 3–4 to a management response, approved preservation or procedure change, implementation evidence and disposition. Seek the contemporaneous versions of the cited authorities, their transmittal and supersession records, and the difference between DEN event reports and preserved central recordings. A concrete test would join a report's identifying fields to its central recording, preservation instruction, custody and receipt.
- **PD01–PD03.** The actual corrected May 2003 answer and delivery acknowledgment, signed consideration or action/no-action decision, and recommendation-specific closure remain separate open originals. No event recording, actual preservation outcome or individual disposition was tested here.
