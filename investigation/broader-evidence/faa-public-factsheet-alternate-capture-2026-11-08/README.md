# October 2003 archive capture of the FAA fact sheet

November 8, 2026 Eastern. B129 / PD01 / PD07 and NR208. An **October 25, 2003** Wayback capture of the FAA’s August 12, 2002 fact sheet carries the same 09:24 Eastern Daylight Time account as the previously held **June 26, 2004** capture. The two replay bodies are byte-identical at 6,933 bytes. The newly recovered capture is from `www1.faa.gov` with lowercase `newsroom`. The June capture is from `www.faa.gov` with `Newsroom`. Both dates follow the May 2003 Commission answer. The fact sheet’s printed August 2002 date and the archived capture dates describe different events. [October 2003 replay](https://web.archive.org/web/20031025224053id_/http://www1.faa.gov:80/newsroom/factsheets/2002/factsheets_020812.htm) · [June 2004 replay](https://web.archive.org/web/20040626232523id_/http://www.faa.gov:80/Newsroom/factsheets/2002/factsheets_020812.htm)

In both earlier bodies, HTML line 104 says FAA notified NEADS at 09:24 about the suspected hijacking of American 77 and that FAA and NORAD opened a line to discuss American 77 and United 93. In the already held November 1, 2004 capture, lines 109–111 give **09:20** for an information-sharing line with government and military participants and show an **October 21, 2004** revision label. The replacement changes the event described, its stated time, and the specificity of the participants. Both descriptions remain preserved. Their times cannot be compared as measurements of a single event without the original call records. [November 2004 replay](https://web.archive.org/web/20041101003838id_/http://faa.gov/newsroom/factsheets/2002/factsheets_020812.htm)

DOT OIG reports that it raised the fact sheet error with FAA in October 2004 and that FAA deleted the erroneous wording. It separately reports a September 2004 approach about the other FAA page. The October 2003 body documents the earlier wording on the fact sheet. The captured date and revision label leave the exact approval and posting times open. The November body independently establishes the captured replacement wording. The two identical earlier bodies represent one version and cannot independently corroborate the operational claim. [DOT OIG report, printed p. 8](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=8)
