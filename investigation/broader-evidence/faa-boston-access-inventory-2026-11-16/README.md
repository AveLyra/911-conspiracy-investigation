## Observations

The memorandum's date field is blank; its text says it responds to a headquarters request dated November 5, 2002 and lists material held by Boston ARTCC concerning AAL11. Its inventory includes an accident package, rerecordings, radar and log materials. This is an agency representation of holdings at an unspecified document date, not independent verification of present custody or the completeness of individual items.

On physical page 173, the list identifies item 32 as a November 20, 2001 communication concerning accident-package security; item 34 as an “Information Access Sheet” dated January 31, 2002; item 37 as an April 15, 2002 document-retention communication; item 38 as an April 30, 2002 communication concerning release of the accident package; and item 39 as a May 3, 2002 memorandum and route slip concerning package security. The inventory gives descriptions, not those items' full text. It also lists three distinct dated ZBW/FBI custody documents; an earlier review already noted the October 18 item. Do not count inventory mentions as proof of multiple independent transfers or of wrongdoing.

The separately acquired [April 25 instruction and April 30 forwarding message](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/4c37c514667ac880f107f2e23cf3fb0b209d8ae2/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-2002-package-distribution-instruction-2026-11-16/README.md) discuss restricted access and package release. The shared April 30 date and subject are a candidate association with item 38, not a verified identity or evidence that the access sheet records a particular decision.
