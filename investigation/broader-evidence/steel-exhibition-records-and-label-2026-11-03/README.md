## Photograph: caption and label are different evidence

The public [WTC steel-column photograph](https://www.flickr.com/photos/wallyg/55558340145/), attributed on its page to **Wally Gobetz / wallyg**, was actually viewed at **683×1024** and at the offered **4000×6000** size. These are two representations of one photograph, not two witnesses or independent views. The site labels the photograph as taken August 9, 2026; that field is not an independently authenticated capture time.

The caption attributes the displayed double-column fragment to the **North Tower**, describes a **36-foot, three-column assembly originally weighing 20 tons**, and places it on the **west facade, ninth column from the southwest corner, floors 104–107**. These are the photographer's assertions. The caption's source and method of attribution were not supplied or authenticated in this review. Its assembly dimensions are not an analyst's measurement of the surviving fragment.

Two faithful, native-pixel detail crops of the central plinth label and lower-right card were read. Their text is insufficiently legible for a reliable transcript, accession number or member mark. **No label wording was reconstructed from the caption, enhanced by AI, or treated as read.** No material composition, fracture, damage-mechanism or temperature test was performed. The photograph and crops remain local; the public derivative contains no image or cropped-image redistribution. The [offered-size page](https://www.flickr.com/photos/wallyg/55558340145/sizes/o/) identifies the license as [CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/).
