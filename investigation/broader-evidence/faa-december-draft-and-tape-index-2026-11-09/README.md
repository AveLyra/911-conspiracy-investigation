# FAA tape production from the December 12 meeting to the line-identification worksheet

November 9, 2026 Eastern · B129 / PD04 / PD07. Four newly acquired public Commission archive members document competing accounts of Washington Center tape production, the precursor to the December 22 request, selected tape-review observations, and a later request to identify lines on tapes described as received. The exact member paths, archive ranges, hashes, page locations and reading scopes are listed separately. [Public Commission archive package](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip) · [Source pins](SOURCE-PINS.json)

## What the December 12 meeting record says

NARA lists the December 12, 2003 meeting with FAA Chief Counsel Andrew Steinberg as MFR **2610655**, four pages, status **O**, box **18**. The matching four-page public archive scan has the face marking **COMMISSION SENSITIVE**. The catalogue status and document marking are distinct observations. I acquired the mirror member and read the selected scan passages. The direct NARA item page was unavailable through the reader, so its object bytes were outside the comparison. [NARA MFR index, line 1261](https://www.archives.gov/research/9-11/commission-memoranda.html) · [Item locator](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/2610655)

| Source passage | Account to preserve |
| --- | --- |
| MFR p. 1 lines 39–61 | Steinberg describes full FAA cooperation and reports that Commission staff turned down offers of technical assistance. The memo records Dana Hyde’s contrary account that she and another staff member accepted several offers. The source supplies no offer-by-offer list. |
| MFR p. 2 lines 40–54, page image checked | Hyde describes an FAA letter saying **ZDC TMU admin phones** were **“not recorded.”** She separately attributes to a quality-assurance representative a statement that the **TMU/OMIC lines** were taped and the facility was told the Commission did not want them. These are differently labeled sets. The letter, station map, and actual recorder assignment are needed to establish whether they cover the same lines. |
| MFR p. 2 lines 52–62 and p. 3 lines 2–18, selected image passages checked | Hyde describes wanting all TMU/OMIC/MOS recordings at ZDC from **8 am – 12 noon** and says those positions’ recordings had not been provided. The memo attributes to Tom Davidson a direction to provide pertinent information and a belief that the Commission already had the communications with the Command Center. It records Shirley Miller’s acknowledgment of possible internal miscommunication. The memo establishes that these accounts were recorded. The selection instruction, production inventory and copies remain separate evidence targets. |
| MFR p. 4 lines 4–16 | The Commission and FAA counsel are described as differing over the approach to interview procedures. The Commission agreed to send a letter on issues raised at the meeting. The memo supplies the commitment and leaves actual transmittal open. |

## What the tape-review records add

- **Team 8 item 202, p. 1 lower section and p. 2 upper half.** The notes are filed as December 21. The handwritten Dulles identifier **7945 B-1** appears beside **“no time stamp”** on p. 1. Page 2 has separate Dulles entries read as **7945 B2**, **7945 B3** and **7945 B4**, with repeated time-related notes whose full wording is uncertain. These are source-specific tape-review locators. The item supplies no verified clock fit or mapping from those labels to the December TRACON production. The date is the archive description. [Commission finding aid](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/9-11/finding-aid.pdf)
- **Team 8 item 806, p. 2 lines 1–2.** A worksheet in the Doug Gould February 20, 2004 folder describes **“VOICE-ACTIVATED TAPES RECEIVED LAST YEAR”** as identified by station and asks **“WHICH LINE IS REPRESENTED ON EACH TAPE”**. The image confirms the heading. This is evidence that the worksheet reports receipt of some station-identified tapes and that line identification was still requested on the face of the worksheet. The folder date does not date the individual worksheet or its receipt events. The underlying media, intake date, exact line crosswalk and a completed return remain open. [Finding aid, Team 8 Box 5, Doug Gould Meeting](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/9-11/finding-aid.pdf)
