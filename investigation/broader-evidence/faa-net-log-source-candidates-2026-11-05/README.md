## NTSB request and related FAA records

- **AWA-910, physical pp. 1 and 3.** A **September 20, 2001** NTSB Office of Aviation Safety support request is directed to the FAA Recommendation and Quality Assurance Division, **AAI-200**. It also says NTSB specialists would assess existing materials in **AAT-200** possession. It requests landline recordings including military notifications, personnel statements for notifications on unrecorded lines, and transcripts, logs and teleconference records for facilities, associated regions and headquarters. The form bears request field **01-xxx**. Its printed FAA fields for date received, AAI-200 log number, forwarding, return and recipient are unfilled in the released copy. The form identifies a separate acquisition route. The data inventory and what actually passed to NTSB remain unverified. [NTSB request](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913717)
- **AWA-907, physical p. 1.** An AAT-20 table bears **September 13, 2001, 8:44 PM** and states that its event times are **UTC**. The AA77 row shows **1247/1256** under radio/transponder loss, **ZID / 1309** under facility/ROC notification, and a separate **1300** possible-accident remark. The date and table columns are visible. These entries preserve earlier FAA reporting and its own labels. The exact source of the 1300 remark and its relationship to 1309 remain open. The table does not identify a 09:26 conversation. [FAA table](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7602353)
- **AWA-913 and AWA-900.** AWA-913 is a **26-page** handwritten-notes scan. Its opening has **“Principals Only Telecon 1130.”** The first six pages were visually screened for scope, with no identified original 09:26 event row in that selection. AWA-900 is a separate one-page handwritten AA77 note. Its page was screened as a candidate and was outside the substantive time comparison. The catalog title and opening heading do not identify either item as the FAA net log cited in the sheet. [Teleconference notes](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913721) · [AA77 notes](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7596206)
