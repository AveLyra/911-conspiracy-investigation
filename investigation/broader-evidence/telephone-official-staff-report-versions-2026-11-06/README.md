# Two National Archives scans of the August 26, 2004 aviation staff report

November 6, 2026 UTC · **TEL01–TEL04 / AUD016-A1 / TC05–TC06**. A second official scan preserves the Flight 77 staff report's **September 14, 2001** and **September 4, 2001** witness citations. Both scans also refer to an NTSB **United Flight 77** study in some notes and an **American Airlines Flight 77** study in others. The NTSB source acquired for comparison bears the latter title. These are differences in the recorded citations. The two scans are copies of the same dated staff product and do not independently establish call events. [120-page NARA scan, physical pp. 94–95](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/9-11/staff-report-DELETE.pdf#page=94) · [121-page NARA scan, physical pp. 94–95](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/9-11/staff-report-sept2005.pdf#page=94) · [NTSB source title, p. 1](https://www.ntsb.gov/about/Documents/Flight_Path_Study_AA77.pdf#page=1)
