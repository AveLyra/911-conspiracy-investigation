# DoD case numbers for the Noble Eagle timeline and CONR chat log

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD01–PD04. Two released Defense Department records give parallel retrieval routes for the source documents behind the Commission’s air-defense chronology. The undated **Noble Eagle 9-11 Timeline** has **78** in the earlier table’s **Page #** column and is indexed as **11-M-3269** in the later mandatory declassification review (MDR) log. The **Continental Region Chat Log** has **99** in the earlier table’s corresponding field and is indexed as **11-M-3270**. These are candidate matches between administrative descriptions. The underlying timeline and chat log remain unacquired. ## Historical disposition and the count discrepancy

- The DoD processing release cover, dated **June 30, 2011**, identifies **10-F-0912** and describes the release as administrative records for six Air Threat Conference Call requests or appeals. The inventory on physical p. 41 is headed **05-F-0204**. Physical p. 42 is an **SD Form 472** for that case, with **INITIAL**, **RECORD FOUND** and **DENY** marked. The detailed record-description fields on the form are blank. The retained form records component action at the time. [DoD cover and form](https://www.governmentattic.org/4docs/DoD9-11ConfCallRequests_2006-2010.pdf#page=42)
- The p. 41 face has **13 subject rows**, counted visually from Noble Eagle through Air Threat Conference Call. A **March 24, 2009** internal email on p. 73 cites the **20070719 Form 472**, describes **15 documents** denied in full under **(b)(1)**, and identifies the Air Threat Conference Call as **document 15**. The email concerns a later transcript request, **08-F-1835**. Its p. 72 case-history sheet records one responsive transcript and reuse of the earlier form. Preserve all three counts with their respective records. The full 2007 matrix and the final outgoing **05-F-0204** determination remain unacquired. The records leave the relationship between the 13-row face and the 15-document matrix open. [Inventory](https://www.governmentattic.org/4docs/DoD9-11ConfCallRequests_2006-2010.pdf#page=41) · [Later case sheet and email, pp. 72–73](https://www.governmentattic.org/4docs/DoD9-11ConfCallRequests_2006-2010.pdf#page=72)
- The **January 3, 2018** cover for **17-F-0168** describes release without excision of a **956-page** document from the Office of the Secretary of Defense’s Records, Privacy, and Declassification Division. The hosted PDF has **958 physical pages**, including a host cover and the agency letter. Its log entries give **Case Number** and **Subject**. Those fields provide MDR identifiers and leave each underlying document’s disposition and current release status open.
