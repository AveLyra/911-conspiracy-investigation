# January hearing files trace a reported FAA rebuttal

November 9, 2026 Eastern · B129 / PD01 / PD07. At the January 27, 2004 Commission hearing, Commissioner Bob Kerrey said the FAA had given the Commission “five or six pages of rebuttal” to the Joint Committee. Later in the same hearing he described reading the FAA response and offered documents to airline witnesses. This is evidence of reported Commission access to an FAA rebuttal by January 27. The version, date, intake record and any attachment to Garvey’s supplement remain unresolved.
