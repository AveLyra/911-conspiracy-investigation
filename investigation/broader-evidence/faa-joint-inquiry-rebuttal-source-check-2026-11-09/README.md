## Two statement comparisons

**August 1998 World Trade Center threat report.** Hill’s prepared statement attributes the assessment of the reported plot’s likelihood to the FAA. Her oral summary repeats that attribution. AWA-811 describes FAA opening an intelligence case file and following up, then attributes the assessment to an apparent informal opinion from an Intelligence Community analyst. Commission Chapter 10 note 14 separately attributes a view of improbability to an FAA official interviewed in 2004, gives a **September 30, 1999** closure date for the file and cites the undated rebuttal **p. 1** on formal FAA analysis. The related AWA-811 discussion spans physical **pp. 2–3**. Preserve the competing attributions, the formal-analysis qualification and the separate pagination. The original **ICF 980162**, underlying analyst record and approved rebuttal version would test who said what and whether the assessment was adopted. **Bojinka scope.** Hill’s prepared statement groups the CIA aircraft scenario with the Bojinka plans and describes it as discussion outside the indictment. AWA-811 places the CIA scenario outside its definition of the plot and reports that the FAA had received no information showing steps to advance the aircraft-as-weapon idea. Keep the grouping, discussion, charging scope and reported FAA knowledge separate. The two texts address different parts of the evidence chain. The underlying inquiry, investigation and distribution records remain the test of any claimed connection.
