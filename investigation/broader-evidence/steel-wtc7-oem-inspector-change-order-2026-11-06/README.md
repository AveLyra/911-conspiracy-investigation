# WTC 7 OEM fuel-tank room hatch inspection and follow-up

November 6, 2026 · Steel and construction custody · STL02–STL03 / AUD011 / AUD016 / B188 / H005

A March 21, 2000 architect memo to the City records a **preliminary Port Authority walk-through** of the WTC 7 Office of Emergency Management fuel-tank room. It says the Port Authority requested a **self-closing hatch tied to the Inergen discharge system**. A June 2 fax requests a proposal to remove the existing hatch and install a replacement that would close on activation. These documents establish a dated field-review and corrective-work trail. The requested work, installation and acceptance each require separate proof. ## Keep the packet variants and approval stages separate

The March fax cover describes a product cut and memo. One copy is a one-page cover. Another City PDF pairs that cover with a January 7 letter about relocating cabling for the seventh-floor generators and domestic water tank. The separate March memo/product file supplies the expected two document types. Preserve each retrieved arrangement. The fax markings and original enclosure list are needed to determine how the packets were transmitted and later assembled. The January letter has a different subject and date.
