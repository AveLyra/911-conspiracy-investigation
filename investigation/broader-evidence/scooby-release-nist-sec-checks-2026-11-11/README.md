# Scooby claim checks: release accounting, NIST sensitivity and options trades

November 11, 2026. This review checks selected additional claims from reports 27–29. It does not complete every claim in those reports or the larger 30-report review. Agency or investigator conclusions are attributed to their sources; reproduced arithmetic is not a physical experiment or trade-ledger reconstruction.

## UMS FBI release: actual preserved-file accounting — report 29

The [January 16, 2013 FBI letter](https://www.israellobby.org/UrbanMoving/01162013release_letter.pdf) for FOIPA 1169683-001 states that 215 pages were reviewed and 183 released, and describes insertion of deleted-page information sheets. The letter does not itself supply a page-by-page manifest.

The project previously acquired individual files from the requester's online index. Its preserved [source-link list](../../urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/derived/irmep-pdf-links.json) includes a duplicate link, and its [file inventory](../../urban-moving-systems/fbi-van-lab-release-2026-10-06/derived/irmep-inventory.json) records one release-letter package and 14 distinct record PDFs. This pass independently opened all 15 locally preserved PDFs using PyMuPDF and recomputed each SHA-256 digest. All 15 file digests and page counts match the inventory. The 14 record PDFs total **177 physical PDF pages**; the release-letter package separately has three pages. In particular, the locally preserved Serial 33 PDF has **22 pages**, which the research chat had treated as an inference. The source-link manifest maps that file to Serial 33.

This establishes the page count of the preserved files, not the exact contents of the agency's original transmittal or whether the website is complete now. The later serial PDFs include release artifacts and records of differing dates. The 183 FBI-reported released pages, 177 split-file pages, separate three-page letter package and deleted-page sheets need item-level reconciliation; they cannot be blindly added or equated. In particular, a six-page arithmetic difference does **not** establish that six substantive investigative pages were suppressed. Compare the original recipient's complete transmission, an item/page manifest, duplication and deletion sheets before making that claim.

## WTC 1 bowing sensitivity — report 27

[NIST NCSTAR 1-6, volume 2, printed pp. 226 and 245](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-C13-2c9d34da3f9db1c28102b1e75bd23b69/pdf/GOVPUB-C13-2c9d34da3f9db1c28102b1e75bd23b69.pdf) says exterior-wall models were used to estimate the pull-in force magnitudes and locations needed to produce observed bowing. For the WTC 1 Case B global analysis at 100 minutes, it reports 14.5 inches of inward bowing with 4 kip pull-in and 42.8 inches with 5 kip, while the 5 kip modeled wall remained stable. NIST stated that a slight additional increase would cause instability. The force increase is 25%; 42.8/14.5 ≈ 2.95. These are NIST's modeled outcomes and inference for those cases, not two measured outcomes or a reproduced model run.

Because observed bowing helped select force inputs, agreement with that same observation is not an independent validation of those inputs. The sensitivity merits independent force constraints, uncertainty analysis and out-of-sample prediction checks; it does not by itself demonstrate that the force was impossible or that explosives caused collapse. The full calibration chain, model inputs and independent predictions are not established by these two pages alone.

## SEC/Commission UAL option-volume reconciliation — report 28

A [September 2003 Commission interview memorandum, PDF pp. 13–14](https://cryptome.org/nara/sec/sec-03-0916.pdf#page=13), records an initial account of 2,075 total UAL puts and 2,000 attributed to an adviser for September 6. The same file's [May 2004 follow-up, PDF pp. 21–22](https://cryptome.org/nara/sec/sec-03-0916.pdf#page=21), records SEC officials' explanation that actual aggregate volume was 1,575 and that a 500-put canceled sale was included in an SEC source report. The officials said the correction did not change their conclusion. This is what the interview memoranda report, not an independently inspected OCC execution/cancellation ledger.

The [Commission staff financing monograph, PDF pp. 151–152](https://www.9-11commission.gov/staff_statements/911_TerrFin_Monograph.pdf#page=151), says an adviser purchased 95% of that day's UAL put volume, describes its alternative investment explanation and reports an FBI investigation reached the same conclusion. The monograph is a **staff report**, whose preface says commissioners did not approve its text. Numerically, 0.95 × 1,575 = 1,496.25; because 95% is expressed without precision and actual contracts are integers, this is not a verified account-level count. The 2004 memorandum does not assign the canceled 500-sale to the adviser's purchase. Neither subtracting 500 from both earlier numbers nor treating the multiplication as a trade record is justified. Compare authenticated time-stamped account executions and cancellations, dates, positions and investigative documentation to test both foreknowledge and ordinary-strategy explanations.
