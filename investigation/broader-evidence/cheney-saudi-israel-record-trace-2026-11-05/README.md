# Cheney, Halliburton, Saudi relationships and Israeli-intelligence claims

**Research note, November 5, 2026.** Records establish business relationships, post-attack official contacts, disputed procurement communications and significant missed intelligence opportunities. They do **not** establish a pre-September 11 agreement among Cheney, Halliburton, Saudi officials, Mossad and Urban Moving Systems (UMS), or that any coordinated the attacks. The specific decision, date, participants and contents of communications matter more than the existence of broad relationships.

## Direct links and their limits

### Cheney and Halliburton: employment and remaining financial interests

Halliburton announced that Cheney resigned as chairman and CEO effective **August 16, 2000**, and was allowed to retain previously granted stock options. [Halliburton announcement](https://ir.halliburton.com/news-releases/news-release-details/halliburton-announces-terms-cheney-departure).

Halliburton's 2001 proxy says Cheney did not own its common stock and that an irrevocable agreement dedicated after-tax proceeds from his remaining vested and unvested options to charity, with an administrator deciding when to exercise them. The [company's proxy statement](https://ir.halliburton.com/static-files/1d4891d7-de97-4dcd-b74d-37c5ad813329#page=9) is a company disclosure, not independent verification of every later transaction. The White House reported **$194,852 in deferred Halliburton compensation in 2004**, describing the amount as fixed under a 1998 election, plus interest. [White House 2004 tax announcement](https://georgewbush-whitehouse.archives.gov/news/releases/2005/04/text/20050415-3.html). The payments show a continuing financial relationship, but the fixed deferred amount does not establish that his personal payments increased with later contracts. Insurance, bankruptcy exposure, remaining options, tax effects and reputational or professional interests should be distinguished rather than assumed to have the same value or direction. [FactCheck.org's review of the agreement and deferral](https://www.factcheck.org/2004/09/kerry-ad-falsely-accuses-cheney-on-halliburton/).

### Halliburton and Saudi Arabia: company presence

Halliburton's SEC-filed year-end **December 31, 2001** subsidiary list includes **Rezayat Brown and Root Saudi Company Limited**, identified as Saudi Arabian. [2001 Exhibit 21](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/45012/000004501202000011/exhibit_21.txt). The list establishes a reported company relationship at year-end; this source alone does not establish when it began, its beneficial owners, a particular Saudi-government contract or a communication about the attacks. It does not establish that Cheney was involved with that entity after leaving Halliburton.

### Cheney and the Saudi ambassador: documented contact after the attacks

The George W. Bush Presidential Library's finding aid identifies a photograph of Bush, Saudi ambassador **Prince Bandar bin Sultan**, Cheney and Condoleezza Rice together on **September 18, 2001**. [NARA finding aid 2022-0048-F](https://www.georgewbushlibrary.gov/sites/default/files/2022-08/20220048f-findingaid.pdf). A separate NARA inventory describes photographs taken **after** a September 20 meeting with Bandar. [NARA finding aid 2015-0021-F](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/vice-presidential-records/pdfs/2015-0021-F-finding-aid.pdf). These are primary archival descriptions of post-attack proximity or meetings; neither specifies the conversation, proves an instruction, nor dates an agreement before the attacks. A FOIA log entry that alleges a September 13 meeting is the requester's description, not archival confirmation of that specific date or subject.

An independent post-attack communication lead concerns Iraq. Bob Woodward reported a January 2003 briefing of Bandar in Cheney's office about U.S. military planning; the available source is contemporaneous journalism, not a released transcript. [CBS interview concerning Woodward's reporting](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/woodward-shares-war-secrets/). If verified, it is evidence of an Iraq-policy channel in 2003, not prior knowledge of 9/11.

### 2002–03 KBR procurement and the disputed vice-presidential coordination

GAO reports that the Army issued KBR a **$1.9 million November 2002 task order** to plan for possible restoration of Iraq's oil infrastructure. GAO concluded the task order was outside the scope of the existing LOGCAP contract; the Defense Department disagreed. The planning was then used to help justify a sole-source restoration contract. GAO found the subsequent award generally complied with applicable legal standards, and reported nearly **$1.4 billion obligated in fiscal year 2003**. Obligations are neither actual profit nor Cheney's personal compensation. [GAO-04-605](https://www.gao.gov/assets/a242676.html).

A March 5, 2003 Army Corps official's email, as reproduced in a later Pentagon talking-points document, said an Iraq oil-restoration action had been coordinated with the vice president's office. The Pentagon asserted that this meant notification and declassification of a project and said Cheney had no role in KBR's selection. [DOD release, Bates 29310](https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/FOID/Special_Collection/Donald_Rumsfeld_Snowflakes_Litigation_Release/11-L-0559_Ninth_Release_Bates_28535-29534.pdf); [contemporary reporting on the dispute](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/2004/06/02/e-mail-links-cheneys-office-contract/7723b271-2521-4d63-9c53-be43be84c618/). The quoted email establishes what the author wrote; its precise referent and the extent of the office's participation remain contested. Neither the quotation nor DOD's self-serving explanation alone settles whether officials influenced the award. Both the communication and the GAO finding postdate 9/11 and are not evidence of advance knowledge of the attacks.

## Saudi support and Israeli-intelligence hypotheses

The [2025 civil court ruling](https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/2025-08/03-md-01570%2011182.pdf#page=40) allowed preliminary inferences concerning assistance by certain Saudi-linked actors; it was not a finding that Saudi leadership ordered 9/11. The [FBI's 2021 closure assessment](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11-attacks-investigation-and-related-materials/9-11-material-released-in-response-to-executive-order-14040/documents-responsive-to-executive-order-14040-section-2-b-ii-part-01-of-01) reached a contrary assessment of knowing conspiracy. These are different records, legal contexts and standards; neither supplies a Cheney–UMS–Mossad communication.

The [UMS review](../../urban-moving-systems/ums-rooftop-affiliation-followup-2026-11-05/README.md) identifies FBI inquiry into possible intelligence ties and unresolved affiliation-related material. The inquiry and a polygraph examiner's opinion do not prove Mossad employment or direction. No authenticated communication identified in this review connects Mossad or UMS to Cheney, Halliburton, the Saudi assistance network or the hijackers as part of an attack operation. An alleged Israeli warning list remains unlocated, and an influential report of a 200-militant warning had a source problem: the [Los Angeles Times correction](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-2001-sep-21-mn-48188-story.html) says its U.S. official's account came from a British newspaper; the CIA denied that particular warning. This does not disprove every possible warning or intelligence contact.

## Separate intelligence and Saudi-flight issues

The [DOJ inspector general](https://oig.justice.gov/sites/default/files/archive/special/s0606/chapter5.htm) documents that a January 2000 CIA draft communication about Mihdhar's U.S. visa was not passed to FBI headquarters, a July 13, 2001 suggestion to share an identification was not acted on until August 30, and Hazmi's 2000 U.S. travel was not shared with the FBI until August 22. It also documents FBI search and information-sharing failures. These are specific failures worth examining; the cited OIG account does not establish a Saudi or Israeli instruction to withhold information, or knowledge of the attack plan by the officials involved.

The September 13, 2001 Tampa–Lexington travel lead contains a factual conflict. An [FBI summary released by NARA](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-048-doc20.pdf) reports a security account of four people driving and challenges a reported flight, including a claim that no flights arrived or departed Lexington that day. The [9/11 Commission staff's terrorist-travel report](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-Y3-PURL-LPS53197/pdf/GOVPUB-Y3-PURL-LPS53197.pdf) describes a September 13 flight and cites airport, FAA, witness and fuel records. The differences in reported travelers mean the records should not automatically be assumed to describe the identical group. Obtain the underlying identifiers and records before resolving the conflict; a discrepancy does not by itself prove fabrication, a special exemption or White House direction. Separately, the Commission said a flight of bin Laden relatives left Boston on September 20 after airspace reopened; its findings do not mean each passenger was individually interviewed. [Commission report, chapter 10](https://9-11commission.gov/report/911Report_Ch10.htm).

## Family investment and accounting safeguards

A contemporaneous *Wall Street Journal* account reports a **$2 million 1995 commitment** by members of the bin Laden family to a Carlyle fund and says former President George H. W. Bush met family members twice. [Reproduced article](https://www.immigrantsolidarity.org/Peace/Sep%2027%2001--WSJ.htm). The family members and Osama bin Laden must not be treated as interchangeable. A [contemporary critique of *Fahrenheit 9/11*](https://eppc.org/docLib/20041005_F911v1.0final.pdf#page=18) attributes a major component of a much larger Bush–Saudi figure to Saudi contracts of Carlyle-owned BDM. Corporate revenue, investment commitments and personal Bush-family payments are different quantities; the details need primary corporate verification before a dollar total is asserted as personal benefit.

## What would change the assessment

The most probative material would be dated, authenticated communications or records linking **specific people and decisions**: the full March 2003 email thread and earlier KBR selection materials; Cheney-office contact logs and contemporaneous notes identifying what was shared with Bandar and when; Halliburton–Saudi entity ownership and contract records; CIA/FBI release and withholding instructions; and authenticated Israeli liaison traffic identifying recipients, dates and content. Compare any UMS or Max job records to WTC tenant and access records, and obtain the final FBI chemistry report or testing disposition. A routine contact, business relationship, intelligence warning or later benefit can occur without coordination of the attacks; a credible alternative must account for those facts without adding a shared purpose unsupported by the documents.
