# FAA teleconference notes and two September 11 chronology forms

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD01 / PD07 · FAA headquarters source and production trail

The National Archives preserves the handwritten **AWA-341** notes, the eight-page **AWA-342** *11 SEP 01 CATASTROPHIC CRISIS* timeline, and three seven-page **AWA-610** copies of *Chronology of Events September 11, 2001*. The April 16, 2004 FAA reply describes handwritten notes and an apparent earlier iteration with the AWA-342 title. It also says the chronology had already been provided to the Commission, most recently on **November 17, 2003**. The title and record types make the acquired files candidates for those enclosures. The native laptop file, transmission manifest, and recipient's attachment-level intake remain open. [April reply, pp. 1–2](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7601904) · [Handwritten notes](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913209) · [Crisis timeline](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913211) · [UAL93 chronology copy](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7597160)

## A new route to a specific Atlantic City recording

- **AWA-342 p. 7**, **1705** row (OCR lines **20**, **66–67**) attributes to **MIV AFSS and ACY ATCT** a report about yelling and screaming on **121.5** at **1248Z**. The document labels the main column EDT. The embedded **1248Z** converts to **08:48 EDT**. The **1705** entry label and the cited earlier time are different fields. [Crisis timeline, p. 7](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913211)
- **ACY-6 p. 2**, upper-left margin, has a handwritten reading of **“1246 - the scream on LC”**. The annotation does not expressly state a zone or frequency. Under a UTC reading, **1246** would be **08:46 EDT**, two minutes from the embedded AWA-342 report time. Keep the two observations apart. Same communication, different communications, transcription uncertainty and differing time conventions remain untested. The sources do not identify the speaker, the aircraft or the subject. [ACY preparation notes](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7599650)
- The prior cassette label study places the early **ACY-13** copies at **1257–1318** for Clearance Delivery and **1302–1318** for Ground Control. **1248Z** precedes those windows. The note identifies **Local Control**, a separate position. Seek the MIV and ACY original guard-channel or position records and the actual **1705** report, separately from the Commission's **0835 EDT** Boston Center inquiry. ## Copy and reading controls

The three AWA-610 bodies have distinct hashes. At **72 dpi** in grayscale, the two redacted copies have identical corresponding page geometry and raster bytes on physical pp. **1, 2, 3, 5 and 7**, with differences on **4 and 6**. The UAL93 copy has different raster bytes from both on all seven pages. Both WTC impact rows occur on p. **1** of all three. The matching pages and overlapping text are dependent evidence. The raster result does not classify each differing mark or authenticate a revision order. [AAL11 copy](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7597927) · [UAL175 copy](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7598464) · [UAL93 copy](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7597160)

Five PDFs totaling **37 pages** were acquired and hashed. The two eight-page bodies were screened by Tesseract. Selected time and description pairings were checked visually against the table layout, because the OCR column order separates the times from the report text. AWA-341 p. **1** is handwritten and includes a DAL1989 secure-area entry. The notes were not transcribed exhaustively or attributed to a particular author. The PDF title's September 11 event date supplies no creation timestamp.
