# WTC steel provenance and the NIST records schedules

The January 2003 National Construction Safety Team rule requires NIST and the team to document the source and chain of custody of each piece of evidence collected in an investigation under the Act. Two generations of NIST records schedules add separate leads for project files and notebooks that could contain that documentation. The most useful next record is the **item-level provenance and custody register**, alongside the records officer's decision identifying the schedule and cutoff actually applied to the investigation files. [Interim rule, 68 FR 4696](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2003-01-30/pdf/03-2084.pdf#page=4) · [Approved schedule, pp. 3–6](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-commerce/rg-0167/daa-0167-2019-0001_sf115.pdf#page=3)

## What the sources say

| Record | Observation and locator |
| --- | --- |
| January 30, 2003 interim rule | The rule took effect January 30, 2003. Section 270.330(b) directs the team and NIST to record each piece of evidence's source, document its chain of custody, preserve it appropriately and keep it securely. These duties concern evidence collected and preserved in investigations under the Act. December 2001 yard work predates this rule. [Rule, PDF pp. 1 and 4](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2003-01-30/pdf/03-2084.pdf#page=4) |
| January 1, 2026 CFR edition | Section 270.330(b)(1)–(4) contains the same four duties. This is a later codification of the rule, so it counts as the same authority when assessing corroboration. [15 CFR 270.330, printed p. 351, PDF p. 12](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2026-title15-vol1/pdf/CFR-2026-title15-vol1-part270.pdf#page=12) |
| Earlier schedule N1-167-92-001 | Items 27(a) and 28(a) designate selected project files and notebooks permanent and specify NARA transfer when 30 years old. Item 27(b) directs destruction of nonselected project files at 30 years. Item 28(b) directs destruction of nonselected notebooks at 30 years or when agency business no longer requires them, whichever is later. The cover crosswalk marks all four items inactive and identifies their successors. [Earlier schedule, PDF pp. 2 and 27–29](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-commerce/rg-0167/n1-167-92-001_sf115.pdf#page=27) |
| Successor schedule DAA-0167-2019-0001, approved October 19, 2020 | Item 0001 designates selected project files and notebooks permanent and specifies annual cutoff at project close and NARA transfer 15 years after cutoff. Its initial transfer entry lists a planned records date span of 1991–2005. Item 0002 specifies the same cutoff and destruction 15 years later, with longer retention authorized for business use. The schedule directs selection review by the appropriate division chief and lists criteria including widespread media attention, significant controversy and extensive government scrutiny. The attention must concern the NIST research project itself. [Successor schedule, PDF pp. 3–7](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-commerce/rg-0167/daa-0167-2019-0001_sf115.pdf#page=3) |

The **30-year and 15-year instructions are preserved separately** with their respective versions and time bases. The crosswalk explicitly identifies supersession. The records reviewed here leave open which authority NIST assigned to the WTC investigation files, whether it selected them as permanent, the cutoff date it recorded, any exception or hold, and whether transfer or destruction occurred. The planned 1991–2005 span is an entry in the schedule. A transfer inventory or accession receipt would be needed to verify actual holdings. Both schedules describe documentary series. The custody or disposition of physical steel requires separate specimen records. [Earlier schedule crosswalk, PDF p. 2](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-commerce/rg-0167/n1-167-92-001_sf115.pdf#page=2) · [NARA schedule index](https://www.archives.gov/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/index.html?dir=/departments/department-of-commerce/rg-0167)

## Steel observations carried forward

FEMA's December 19, 2001 Claremont entries `A/W14A` and `B/W14B` are field-attributed WTC 7 candidates. NIST's inventories list `W-14A` at `205` and `W-14B` at `PL` as unidentified, while flagging their appearance in the FEMA report. The reason for the different attribution remains open, as does the physical match between the two sets of labels. The reports supply published inventory observations. The custody trail and basis for the identification decision remain separate evidence targets.
