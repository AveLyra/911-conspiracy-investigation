# Cleveland's October 2003 Commission tape submission

November 8, 2026 Eastern · **B129 / PD07**, with **PD01–PD04** open. An October 14 Cleveland FAA memorandum describes a second submission of September 11 voice tapes. It records both an original and a revised time window and explains how staff attempted to reconstruct recording positions. Two October 16 scans record an earlier forwarding claim about United 93 documentation. Together they identify what the sender reported and give leads to the actual media and recipient's records.
