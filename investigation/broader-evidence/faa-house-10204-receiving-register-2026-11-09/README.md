# Congressional routes for DOT’s FY 2006 accountability report

Public derivative of the November 9, 2026 Eastern source review · B129 / PD03–PD05. The Senate recorded a referral for DOT’s FY 2006 Performance and Accountability Report (PAR) under **EC–9158**, on the same day the House recorded **10204**. The Senate receiving committee’s published calendar lists EC–9158. The House Journal supplies an additional locator for 10204. These records provide separate retrieval keys for the cover letters, enclosures and committee files. The underlying letters, enclosures and receipt stamps remain unacquired. ## Retrieval controls

- **The number alone can point to an unrelated item.** House Journal item `9158.` in the September 6 proceedings concerns an acquisition rule and is separate from Senate `EC–9158.`. Senate `EC–9134.` on December 5 also came from the Secretary of Transportation, but concerns FY 2004 highway and safety construction funds and goes to Commerce, Science, and Transportation. Use chamber, Congress/session, number, subject, referral date and committee together. Keep House 3550 on its own trail. [House Journal, physical and printed p. 1107, lines 115–126](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GPO-HJOURNAL-2006/pdf/GPO-HJOURNAL-2006-2-1.pdf#page=1107) · [Senate EC–9134, physical p. 82, lines 125–135](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-2006-12-05/pdf/CREC-2006-12-05-senate.pdf#page=82)
- **House committee locator.** The NARA standing-committee list groups the former Committee on Government Reform with the successor committee under catalog link 10518159. That is an organization-level lead. NARA describes separate preliminary-inventory coverage through the 87th House and 93rd Senate Congresses and distinguishes later inventories and box lists. The bounded RG 233 export listing returned eight object keys and explicitly indicates truncation. Those keys alone do not identify an accession, committee box or item. [NARA House list, raw HTML lines 484–485](https://www.archives.gov/legislative/research/browse/house-standing.html) · [NARA finding-aid guide, lines 408–412](https://www.archives.gov/legislative/research/getting-started/whats-not-online.html)
- **Separate public-access triggers.** NARA describes ordinary House and Senate access periods of 30 and 20 years respectively. Its House Rule VII transcription is labeled **117th Congress**, with separate treatment of previously public records, special orders and categories subject to a longer period. Senate Resolution 474 §2 distinguishes its twenty-year default from specified fifty-year records and allows different committee conditions. Treat the Senate age threshold as a potential trigger to revisit the catalog. The date of existence, transfer, category, conditions and actual availability of either packet require item-level evidence. [NARA access summary, raw HTML lines 344–350](https://www.archives.gov/legislative/research/rules-of-access.html) · [House Rule VII, lines 349–373](https://www.archives.gov/legislative/research/house-rule-vii.html) · [Senate Resolution 474, lines 340–355](https://www.archives.gov/legislative/research/senate-resolution-474.html)
