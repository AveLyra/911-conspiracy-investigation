These statements are preserved separately with their dates and sources.

## Interpretation and limits

The reproduced response supplies an actual dated management document, but the **visible** concurrence concerns a proposed wording change. The redacted paragraph cannot be used either to verify or to dispute the IG's broader summary of concurrence. The statements have different scopes and dates; no direct contradiction or reconciliation is established. A proposed development, a concurrence, an agreement to change directives, an actual change and operational implementation are distinct events. This document does not show which occurred or when. Its reported equipment-development status concerns a future capability, not a measurement of any particular 2001 recording.

Physical page 18 / printed page 10 of the IG report identifies instructions and officials consulted during its review; its list supplies routes to historical policies and source records, not an exhaustive evidence inventory. The report and response do not answer whether FAA delivered its separate corrected May 2003 QFR or decided on administrative action.
