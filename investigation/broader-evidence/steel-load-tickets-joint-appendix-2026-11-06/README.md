# WTC debris load tickets and trucking manifests

November 6, 2026 · Steel custody · Public-source follow-up

A federal opinion identifies the City’s **Load Debris Tickets**, with cargo and destination fields. It also cites a specific filed exhibit for the Department of Design and Construction’s (DDC) tracking of trucking manifests. These are two leads to contemporaneous transport records. The filed exhibit and completed tickets remain outstanding.
