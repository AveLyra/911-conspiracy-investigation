The source descriptions mix a table's reported **start**, an interview's reported **end**, a later clock comparison and other summarized labels. A stable offset, rounded times, source mismatch or transcription error are possible hypotheses, but none was tested. Do not automatically subtract three minutes, treat 9:47 as a confirmed start, or equate “less than one minute” and 71 seconds. These observations do not establish a forged call or recording, motive or a historical cause.
