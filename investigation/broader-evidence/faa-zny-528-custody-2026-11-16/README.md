# Flight 175 accident package: New York custody form ZNY-528

## Source and scope

The [National Archives scan](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/4-UAL-175/e-Other/4-ZNY-528-Chain-of-Custody-Form.pdf), [catalog 7598631](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7598631), was obtained November 16, 2026: 31,956 bytes, SHA-256 `6bc3e42e45cc57b49f58e1881dca8dd7ba668dfa9e4ab0639a229cb3677b4926`. Both pages were rendered and visually inspected; page 2 is blank apart from scan-edge marks. The [finding aid](https://www.archives.gov/research/foia/faa-finding-aid-72-83) describes the item as a chain-of-custody form for `ZNY-ARTCC-148`.

## Observations

The form describes an aircraft accident package and marks it `ZNY-ARTCC-148 (UAL175)`. Two entries in the main chain-of-custody section visibly show `10-20-03` and `2:25p`; their titles appear as operations supervisor and `SAC - OIG - JRI-2`. There are two further handwritten witness entries. The form does not explicitly label the direction of transfer or identify the contents of the package; the witness handwriting and exact full titles warrant a separate legibility check. Names, handwritten signatures and contact information are not reproduced here.

This record identifies a documented custody entry bearing the same package number as the September 17, 2001 [ZNY-423 request](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/d5e213bfa526751f420fd3a6f2f7eabb110df3e6/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-zny-423-hold-2026-11-16/README.md) and [ZNY-426 email](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/bc316c92c4b6181834fca6581c8a3751d4b319d3/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-zny-426-hold-2026-11-16/README.md). The common identifier is a retrieval link, not proof that the package contained every responsive item, that the requests were delivered or complied with, or that this form establishes the complete chain.
