# Beamer call: evidence transcript and post-conversation interval

November 25, 2026 · TEL01–TEL04 / AUD016-A1. An acquired public Commission-release packet contains a transcript of later telephone conversations concerning the Beamer–Jefferson call. It is not a recording or verbatim transcript of the aircraft call itself.

## Source and provenance

The [January 2009 Commission archive ZIP](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip), member `911 NARA/Team 7 Files/Team 7 Box 12/T7 B12 Flight 93 Calls- Todd Beamer Fdr- 9-15-01 FBI 302 Transcript- UAL SAC Nick Leonard re Jefferson-Beamer Call.pdf`, was acquired by a bounded range on November 25. Member name, uncompressed length **479,299 bytes** and ZIP CRC `b96c6105` matched the central directory; SHA-256 `c2189d15f0ffdf23e6db65cf7ed3cc5c5be54b96963b3e0e8ad5ae17abc97052`. The 21-page scan has an evidence cover identifying **1A-1A64148** and a recording of a conversation. The filename contains **9-15-01**; the precise recording date and complete custody history have not been independently established. Extracted text was inspected for scope; physical pages 18–19 were visually checked. The actual tape was not acquired or heard.

## Observations and conflicting accounts

- On physical page 19, within the later Jefferson–Beamer family conversation, the transcript records the speaker labeled `U` saying she held the phone for **“about ten more minutes”** after the last reported exchange. The surrounding transcript identifies the conversation as involving Lisa Jefferson and Lisa Beamer; that is the transcription's identification, not independent speaker authentication.
- The [2004 Commission memorandum](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/e2044cac2b64f01d73d08760a64fc0d363b5ba49/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-jefferson-comparison-2026-11-25/README.md), page 3, records **15 minutes** holding on after the last words. The same earlier review preserves the FBI-hosted interview text's **another twenty minutes** after the last exchange. They are descriptions from related accounts, not independent timed measurements. The events and exact start/end boundaries may differ, but that possibility has not been tested; no value is corrected or chosen.
- Page 18 records the family member asking whether there would be a tape of the original call. The speaker says she does not have one and describes having needed to leave her position to record it. This is a reported statement about her own access and actions, not proof that no other operator, system or party recorded anything.
- Pages 4–6 contain a separate airline representative's reading of a document described in that reading as a written recollection. Its timing descriptions and transmission history are separate from the later conversation on pages 7–21. The source gives the opportunity to retrieve the actual written document and the recorded later calls; neither has been authenticated or acquired here.

## Open tests and limitations

1. Obtain the recording and evidence receipt/custody records for **1A-1A64148**, its full transcription and any corrections; compare the relevant interval against the audio. The source packet's transcription and identification cannot on their own authenticate every word.
2. Seek Jefferson's contemporaneous notes and the written recollection described in the packet; establish their dates, versions and custody. Obtain native carrier/operator connection logs and time-base definitions to distinguish speech ending, a held connection and disconnect.
3. Preserve the 10-, 15- and 20-minute descriptions and the earlier **9:45 / 9:36 (sic)** worksheet and Combs report as distinct source records. Approximation, different event boundaries or reporting/transcription differences are untested possibilities. No source establishes a fabricated call, purposeful alteration or motive.

Raw audio, full family conversation and personal contact information are not republished. Finding an evidence identifier does not establish that the tape survives or contains the original aircraft call.
