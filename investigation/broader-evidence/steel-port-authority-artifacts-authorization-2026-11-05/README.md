# The Port Authority's October 2002 WTC artifacts authorization

> Public derivative of the separately preserved November 5, 2026 Port Authority artifacts study.


November 5, 2026 · STL02–STL03 / B188 / H005. The earlier historic-preservation review left the October 24, 2002 authorization open. The New Jersey State Library's Port Authority collection contains the dated meeting minutes, including the adopted resolution. The catalog identifies the Port Authority's board-minutes archive as its download source. The separately cataloged PATH subsidiary minutes from the same day have their own cover, agenda and pagination. [Port Authority catalog item](https://dspace.njstatelib.org/items/a4a280d0-be81-40b3-bacc-4e972cf6f906) · [Separate PATH item](https://dspace.njstatelib.org/items/60d126e3-9dae-49ec-b994-ece4c4d221fc)

## What the board records establish

| Record | Source observation |
| --- | --- |
| **October 24, 2002**, printed p. 435 | Staff described a collection including damaged steel and reported that some items were selected for the damage they showed and others for the parts of the towers they represented. Staff estimated that approximately **80,000 square feet** at JFK Building 17 could be available for **three years**. They described cataloging and digital photography underway, a database to be created, and a management plan with a loan procedure in development. [Physical p. 9](https://dspace.njstatelib.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/1453ab6a-5757-4c57-8258-44f8f2cbec3e/content#page=9) |
| **October 24, 2002**, printed p. 436 | The minutes record the adopted resolution, twelve in favor and none against. It authorized a preservation program for Port Authority-collected artifacts at an estimated **$5.75 million** over **four years**. It gave the executive director authority to make or ratify relevant agreements, with their form subject to General Counsel approval. Staff's report separately described a catalog numbering and photography system, decontamination contracts, and possible additional costs for alternative storage. [Physical p. 10](https://dspace.njstatelib.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/1453ab6a-5757-4c57-8258-44f8f2cbec3e/content#page=10) |

The Board unanimously approved the October minutes. This is an approval of the record of the earlier meeting. [Physical p. 6](https://dspace.njstatelib.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/c4bc560d-da43-478a-8da4-d4678b07e0e3/content#page=6) |
| **May 26, 2005**, printed pp. 192–194 | The Board's later account repeats the **$5.75 million** and estimated **four-year** terms, names Voorsanger and Associates and subconsultant Art Preservation Services, and reports expected Port Authority custody of **five to seven years**. It records adoption of a further **$4.9 million** authorization, to **$10.65 million** total, including a Meridian maintenance supplement at an estimated **$525,000**. The 2005 authorization also calls for advising commissioners before action on individual contracts or agreements under it. [Physical pp. 5–7](https://dspace.njstatelib.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/c960df66-1aba-40cd-9eb0-140585e3be89/content#page=5) |

The **three years**, **four years** and **five to seven years** describe projected space availability, program scope and expected custody, respectively. None is an actual transfer or closing date. The amounts describe successive authorizations. The test **$5.75 million + $4.9 million = $10.65 million** reproduces the stated total authorization without establishing expenditure. The May minutes' separate estimate of **$4.1 million spent through early 2005** remains under its own label. [May 2005 minutes, printed pp. 193–194](https://dspace.njstatelib.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/c960df66-1aba-40cd-9eb0-140585e3be89/content#page=6)

## Inventory statements preserved separately

- The October 2002 minutes describe cataloging and photographing in progress and a future database. The April 2004 environmental review response to Comment 199 says the Port Authority had completed an inventory of Hangar 17 items and the State Museum had prepared a draft inventory of its own holdings. [2002 minutes, p. 435](https://dspace.njstatelib.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/1453ab6a-5757-4c57-8258-44f8f2cbec3e/content#page=9) · [April 2004 response, physical p. 44](https://renewnyc.com/attachments/content/pdfs/eis/04-12-2004/vol1/27%20Response%20to%20Comments.pdf#page=44)
- The May 2005 minutes say a two-volume *WTC Archive: Artifacts Inventory Report* was completed in **October 2004**, and that the inventory would be updated. The January 2012 LMDC report describes a two-volume catalog completed in **2006**. The previously reviewed transfer packet refers to a **January 31, 2007** inventory. Keep these dates and descriptions alongside one another. The record titles and dates available here leave edition identity and coverage unresolved. ## What this changes and what's still open

The contemporaneous adopted resolution is now located, along with the subsequent vote approving the minutes. The authority and the staff-described workflow provide concrete entry points for tracing steel under this program. The record describes selective collection, so the catalog scope cannot be assumed to represent all recovered steel. The reviewed pages provide no item-level source-to-hangar join or WTC 7 building attribution. The later account refers back to the original board decision and shares that evidence chain.

- [ ] **STL02, inventories and custody.** Seek the database export and dated catalog editions, photo index and unaltered intake records. Test object numbers, member marks, dated photos, source location and transfer receipts across versions.
- [ ] **STL02, decision implementation.** Seek the executed consultant agreements, their task orders and deliverables, the management plan and loan procedure, advisory-committee minutes, and decontamination/movement and disposition logs. Compare any records with the voted scope, authorization date and later maintenance supplement. Retain the separately outstanding certified copy and underlying routing receipts if needed for evidentiary use.
- [ ] **STL03, building attribution.** Seek the actual site or yard intake and engineering evidence needed to connect an artifact to a specific member or building, and any NIST attribution worksheet or change record. A memorial artifact number cannot supply that crosswalk by itself.

The archived bytes, catalog chain, page references and text-extraction methods are recorded in the [source manifest](SOURCE-PINS.json). The key October scan pages were visually checked. The rest of the meeting and the separate PATH packet were used only for identification and context. Implementation, object histories and the underlying inventories remain open.
