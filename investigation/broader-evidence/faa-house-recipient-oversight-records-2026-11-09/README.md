# DOT senior staff and congressional recipient records for the FAA follow-up trail

Public derivative of the November 9, 2026 Eastern source review · B129 / PD01–PD04

Kenneth Mead's prepared statement to a House subcommittee describes DOT follow-up on recommendations and identifies **senior staff meetings attended by heads of DOT operating administrations** as a place where the Secretary reinforced attention to inspector-general findings. This statement is in the printed hearing's scanned pages, beyond the available HTML transcript. Mead says he resigned in **February 2006**. The FAA referral report is dated **August 31, 2006** and names **Todd J. Zinser**, Acting Inspector General, as sender. The testimony supplies a record series to check across the transition. It gives no case-specific agenda, assignment, direction or disposition. [House hearing, physical pp. 69–71](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-110hhrg41854/pdf/CHRG-110hhrg41854.pdf#page=69) · [FAA referral memorandum, p. 1](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=1)

## The two routing leads

- **DOT Secretary and operating administrations.** In the prepared statement, Mead discusses both audit and investigative findings before describing follow-up to see whether recommendations were implemented and reporting to the Secretary and Congress when they were not. He separately describes the senior staff meetings. The oral remarks emphasize the dual reporting relationship, regular access to the Secretary and congressional oversight. None of these passages identifies the follow-up procedure for the August referral. Seek senior staff agenda packets, attendance and action-item logs, written Secretary or FAA assignments, and OIG updates keyed to the case. [Prepared statement, physical pp. 70–71, printed pp. 66–67](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-110hhrg41854/pdf/CHRG-110hhrg41854.pdf#page=70) · [Oral remarks, physical pp. 66–67](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-110hhrg41854/pdf/CHRG-110hhrg41854.pdf#page=66)
- **Legislative receipt and review.** Eleanor Hill recalls earlier Senate Governmental Affairs Committee staff review of each IG semiannual report. The House's **3550** referral names the **House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform** and concerns a **Secretary's management report** for the period ending **March 31, 2007**. Those are distinct recipients and document categories. NARA's historical guide describes direct committee referrals in §24.14 and identifies executive communications, calendars, minutes and distinct subject files among predecessor committee papers in §§11.99–11.101. The described **47 feet** of executive communications is in the predecessor grouping for **1953–1968**. The guides leave 110th Congress custody open. A receiving-committee executive communication register and DOT outgoing control sheet would test the 3550 delivery and exact enclosure.
