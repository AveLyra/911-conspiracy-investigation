## Assessment and open tests

The approval records make a 757 passenger-telephone configuration a documented design lead. They leave the installation, effectivity and operational state on N644AA open. A signed preflight deactivation or removal applying to the claimed service path would weigh against availability on that path. A dated record establishing an installed, powered and serviceable path would support availability. Call attribution would still require the call-specific records. Both tests remain open.

- [ ] Obtain the issued and historical certificates, approved model or effectivity attachments and installation drawings, including SA9013NM-D, ST00284NY, ST01247NY and ST01290NY. Test the design and scope relationships.
- [ ] Obtain the N644AA airworthiness index and the carrier's signed installation, deactivation, removal, reactivation and test work packages. Compare against engineering orders **F0878 / F1463 / F1532** and their dated effectivity and completion lists. Retain the manual's visible **REV 62, Jan. 28/2007** header and **01/28/2001** footer from the preceding review. No date is selected for the deactivation wording. [Reproduced page, p. 35](https://www.consensus911.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Rowland_Morgan_2010_Voices.pdf#page=35)
- [ ] Keep the Flight 587 Form 337, September 20 usage report, zero-duration exhibit, operator or carrier legs and transfer records open. No new call event or call attribution was verified.
