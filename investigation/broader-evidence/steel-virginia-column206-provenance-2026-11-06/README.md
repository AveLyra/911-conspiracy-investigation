# Virginia memorial steel and NIST panel A206: 92–95

November 6, 2026 · Steel provenance · STL02–STL03 / B188 / H005

Virginia State Police attributes its planned May 2026 donation to a larger artifact from **WTC 1, east exterior column 206, floors 92–95**. NIST identifies a recovered panel spanning the same location as **N-12**, also **M-13**, and maps two recovered columns, including the center column line **206**. This gives an item-specific custody lead. The surviving public records reviewed here leave the identity of the donated fragment and its relationship to the NIST specimen open. [Virginia State Police advisory](https://vsp.virginia.gov/virginia-state-police-to-receive-steel-from-world-trade-center/) · [NIST panel map, printed p. 81](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-3cv1.pdf#page=210)

The two K-2 locations remain unresolved. An editorial label error is an untested explanation. NIST's figure-source files, original specimen tags, stamp photos and drawing crosswalk could distinguish it from a specimen-tracking error. The State Police advisory describes the parent artifact as near the edge of the damage on the **east face** and gives blast or ensuing fire as possible sources of damage. Goochland's account makes a more specific claim about the visible rippling on its pieces. The relationship between the pieces and the reported damage mechanisms remains untested. [State Police advisory](https://vsp.virginia.gov/virginia-state-police-to-receive-steel-from-world-trade-center/) · [County account](https://www.goochlandva.us/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=1148&ARC=2061)
