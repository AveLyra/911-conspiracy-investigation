# WTC 7 OEM lease and inspection closeout records

November 6, 2026 · Steel and construction custody · STL02–STL03 / AUD011 / AUD016 / B188 / H005

Two City archive scans preserve the March 25, 1998 lease for the original WTC 7 Mayor’s Office of Emergency Management (OEM). Article 6(H) calls for a **Landlord’s Completion Notice** carrying necessary **Port Authority sign-offs** and a **certified air-balancing report** approved by the landlord’s engineer. It names the City as recipient and sets a three-business-day response period. This supplies a second record route to the OEM alteration closeout through the City’s tenant file. The notice, enclosures and the City’s certification or dated receipt remain outstanding. [Lease copy A, physical pp. 22–23](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000166927.pdf#page=22) · [Lease copy B, physical pp. 21–22](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000167068.pdf#page=21)

## Preserve the two lease copies and the A-8 ambiguity

- **Lease variants.** The scans have 95 and 98 physical pages. Their selected Article 6 and 7 passages agree. Lease A has two consecutive Schedule 1 pages. Both show a question mark after the third printed item, and the first also carries a handwritten note that appears to read **“Millwork? Furniture?”** Lease B shows one Schedule 1 page with the same three printed items and no visible question mark or note. The annotations’ authorship and status are open. [Lease A, pp. 93–94](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000166927.pdf#page=93) · [Lease B, p. 98](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000167068.pdf#page=98)
- **DD-5 image and OCR.** The visible fuel-tank drawing label is **DD-5** in both exhibit lists. The embedded text of Lease B reads **DD-3** for that line. Preserve the image and extraction observations separately and search using the visible sheet number. This does not establish that any later drawing carried the same number. [Lease A, p. 79](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000166927.pdf#page=79) · [Lease B, p. 84](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000167068.pdf#page=84)
- **A-8 may name a report.** The October 30, 1998 FDNY memo references **“the A-8”** for the reported 10,000-gallon figure while describing the visited tank as approximately 6,000 gallons. A separate FDNY checklist and its instructions use **“A-8 or other report”** and direct that the completed checklist be filed in the **Building Record Folder**. That gives a specific records route and a plausible document class for the memo’s reference. The specific A-8 is still missing. Keep open whether the memo meant a report, a drawing, or a report drawing from another source. [FDNY tank memo, p. 1](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000169174.pdf#page=1) · [FDNY checklist and instructions, pp. 13–14](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000169180.pdf#page=13)
