## What the routing evidence can test

The DOT directive's congressional-report definition and the FAA directive's scope exclude routine witness supplements or requests made to witnesses during congressional hearings, respectively. The May 2003 answer was given to the **9/11 Commission**. The two orders supply a search route for covered congressional reports and leave the route for the corrected Commission answer open. The report-control approval log would document intake for approval. A signed report or routing form would establish a separate stage. Dispatch or recipient records would still be required to establish delivery.
