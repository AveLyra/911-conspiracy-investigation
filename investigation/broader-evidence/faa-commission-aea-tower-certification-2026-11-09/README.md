## Newark shipment and separately numbered forms

The October 20, 2003 Newark index says it describes materials sent via FedEx on October 8. It lists a Daily Record of Facility Operation, FAA Form 7230-4, as item 12 and says it was “omitted from original package, please see attached.” The same index says the rerecording contains selected transmissions and that the full originals remain at the facility. The packet includes two Newark daily-record sheets dated September 11, 2001, visibly marked **Page 1 of 2** and **Page 2 of 2**. A separate list within the packet describes **“EWR ATCT 7230-4 (9/11/01) - 3 pages.”** The two visible sheets and the three-page list entry are separate observations. Which forms each copy set contains and whether the sets are coextensive remain open. [Item 698, PDF pp. 1, 3–4, 11](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip)

FAA inventory box 9 separately lists EWR-1 through EWR-3 as Form 7230-4, EWR-24 through EWR-26 and EWR-30 as FedEx airbills, EWR-29 as a certified index and EWR-31 as a tape custody transfer. These catalogue entries identify concrete targets for checking which original or copy traveled in which shipment. The underlying airbills and tape transfer were not acquired here. The earlier Washington National receipt records a distinct facility and October 9 intake. The reviewed Newark record gives no equivalent recipient signature for item 12. The printed count, the six lettered entries and the seven displayed range lines are preserved separately. The cover describes a selection relevant to the Commission inquiry. A cassette/side inventory and the recipient's accession record could show how the seven lines map to physical copies. The second records a separate routing lead, with no item-level join to a subsequent Commission delivery.
