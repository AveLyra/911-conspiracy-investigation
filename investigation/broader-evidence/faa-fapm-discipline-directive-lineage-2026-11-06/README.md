# FAA personnel directives and the 2006 administrative decision search

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD02–PD04, with PD01 open

FAA Order **3200.9** cancels the standalone orders on **Conduct and Discipline** and **Adverse Actions, Appeals and Grievances** and directs that their text be incorporated into the Federal Aviation Personnel Manual. A 2005 government brief separately points to **FAA Personnel Management System, Chapter III, ¶ 3(g)–(q)** for the notice, reply and written decision in a major adverse action. These are new document and section identifiers for the 2006 follow-up search. The signed action or no-action decision for **CC-2006-085 / 042R0122001** remains unlocated. ## What to seek in the 2006 record

Order 3200.9 §§ 4–8 describes **AHRIS** as a way to distribute and access the manual, with paper access also available. It names the **Program Director for Personnel** for maintaining the manual and routes sign-off by subject, including to the **Program Director for Labor and Employee Relations**. It permits more specific directives and implementation procedures. Seek the applicable manual issuance, its effective date and the applicable delegation of deciding authority. AHRIS access points to policy editions. The earlier **ERTS**, **GETS**, **LERIS**, and OIG tracking leads concern different systems or record classes. Their applicability to this inquiry requires a case-specific crosswalk. [Order 3200.9, §§ 4–8, p. 3](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Order/3200.9.pdf#page=3) · [Earlier tracking-system study](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/2c561583a33746bf37f417b9687440053304cfba/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-employee-relations-tracker-and-appeals-2026-11-06/README.md)
