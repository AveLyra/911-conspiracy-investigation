## Comparison

The later license point is approximately **0.70 km** from the October 2001 reference point using the printed angles, a spherical Earth and a 6,371.0088 km radius. That separation is smaller than the rule's general **1.6 km** reference radius. It is a geographic compatibility check, with no datum transformation or retrospective compliance finding. The reference point and location record remain separate observations. Preserve **026** and **27** in their carrier and regulatory fields. The common city and proximity provide a candidate site association, with no evidence of a number-conversion rule.

The same interview reports a Central Time label and separately names an Illinois switch. The later federal rule's civil-time table for Fort Wayne gives **EST** on its 2005 spring and summer examples. Keep the two descriptions and their dates. The later license coordinates leave the 2001 clock source, daylight-saving settings and the **8:32:39** versus **9:32:29** candidate comparison unresolved. None of these records determines which network component supplied the timestamp.
