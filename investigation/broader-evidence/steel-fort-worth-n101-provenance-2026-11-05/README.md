## Documentary join and controls

The reported label, building, center column and **100–103** span agree between the museum's formal identification and NIST's entries. The same-column **S-9 or C-63** comparison shows why a column number or the description “full panel” alone cannot identify this piece. The same-location **W-14B** comparison shows why a storage code cannot supply a building assignment. This is a documentary join. The museum expressly attributes the building location to NIST, so the matching statements do not independently authenticate the physical panel or its pre-collapse position.

Preserve **100–103** as the NIST inventory/span and splice values, and **101–103** as the museum's supported-floor description. Preserve **Impact Steel**, NIST's table grouping and the exact fire-observation field in their own scopes. The available records leave the origin and intended meaning of the formal designation open. It cannot establish direct aircraft contact. Keep **approximately 4 tons** and **approximately 6,500 pounds** separately as published. Units, rounding, actual scale readings and whether either weight includes fittings are unverified.
