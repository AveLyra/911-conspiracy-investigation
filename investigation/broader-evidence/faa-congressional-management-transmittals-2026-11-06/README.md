The 1988 enactment of the management-side reporting provision distinguishes audit management decisions from final action. Its definition of final action includes completing required actions and also provides for a management decision when management concludes action is unnecessary. Its itemized overdue statement addresses audit reports with a management decision and unfinished action, subject to timing and specified exclusions. The investigation’s own recommendations and the management-series title are different records.
