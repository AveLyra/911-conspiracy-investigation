## Referral-system limitations and version leads

- **An actual tracking limitation.** In audit **ST2018076**, OIG identifies ALERTS and describes agents as having no requirement to log referrals evaluated as requiring no action until **mid-2015**. It also says its hotline data do not indicate which referrals were made by operating-administration officials. Table 2 gives FAA office self-reports of **26**, **351** and **5** (total **382**) alongside a combined ALERTS value of **204**, using cutoff dates **June 6, 2017** and **May 26, 2017**, respectively. Keep the totals, observation windows and collection methods separate. The no-action field concerns referral intake, a different event from an FAA management decision on recommendation 2. [OIG audit, physical pp. 12–13 and p. 20 lines 23–41](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/DOT%20Criminal%20Referrals%20Final%20Report%5E08-22-18.pdf#page=13)
- **A directive-custody lead.** OIG reports that the central electronic location lacked **8000.5A** and **8000.8** from mid-2015 into 2017 after the DOT Library site stopped serving them. It reports that OST requested copies from **OIG External Affairs** for posting to its internal Directives Inventory. Those are two concrete custodial leads for the old copies and their reposting record. The reported gap concerns that period and central location. [OIG audit, physical p. 9 lines 18–34](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/DOT%20Criminal%20Referrals%20Final%20Report%5E08-22-18.pdf#page=9)
- **Preserved order references.** The audit's FAA table and narrative give **2150.3B**, while the nearby direct-referral sentence gives **2150.2B**. Both official PDF copies contain both identifiers. Neither wording establishes what was applied to the 2006 investigation. The National Transportation Library supplies the earlier **2150.3A** text. §308 on physical pp. 35–36 distinguishes OIG investigation and reporting responsibilities from enforcing aviation safety regulations. FAA catalogs the order as issued **1988-12-14**, canceled **2007-10-01**. The library record has a publication date of **1999-09-01**. Preserve issue, cancellation and library publication as different fields. This older regulatory-enforcement order still requires a case-specific reference before assigning it to the 2006 follow-up. [Audit, physical p. 10 line 41 and p. 11 lines 40–45](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/DOT%20Criminal%20Referrals%20Final%20Report%5E08-22-18.pdf#page=11) · [FAA catalog](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/orders_notices/index.cfm/go/document.information/documentID/7409) · [Library record](https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/40682) · [Library PDF](https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/40682/dot_40682_DS1.pdf#page=35)

## A separate recommendation-response example

OIG warned that internal review steps could delay referrals. The **August 15, 2018** OST response reproduced in the appendix disputes the assertion, points to the lack of a documented example of delay and argues that preliminary fact development can help OIG. It agrees those steps should be prompt. The audit's methods section says the supplied data lacked receipt times needed to quantify promptness. The recommendation texts, management reply and final public status below concern **ST2018076**. [Audit physical p. 20 lines 27–33 and response at pp. 28–29](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/DOT%20Criminal%20Referrals%20Final%20Report%5E08-22-18.pdf#page=28)

| ST2018076 recommendation | Appendix and final audit | Public OIG status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** · Update the orders | OST concurred and planned updates by **January 2019**, subject to internal reviews. | Closed **12.04.2020** |
| **2** · Align agency procedures | OST partially concurred and planned an agency-order review by **July 2019**. | Closed **11.29.2022** |
| **3** · Annual mandatory training | OST concurred and proposed one-time training for existing staff and training for new entrants, with development by **July 2019**. | Closed **09.07.2021** |

The audit reported all three **resolved but open**. The status page still displays the original annual training recommendation. The appendix's alternative and the closure label alone leave the implemented training program and supporting closure evidence open. The dated response, original recommendation, status and underlying implementation are separate records.
