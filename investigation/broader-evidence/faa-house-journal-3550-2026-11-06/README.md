The two House publications both cite Rule XII clause 8. The contemporaneous House Manual says this clause directs executive departments to address communications to the Speaker for referral under Rule XIV clause 2. This is evidence about the referral procedure. The House Journal and Congressional Record can share the same underlying referral, so agreement between the entries does not independently corroborate the report contents or prove what the FAA did. [110th House Manual, §827, printed p. 614](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/HMAN-110/html/HMAN-110-houserules.htm)
