# FAA executive discipline and the 2006 administrative decision search

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD02–PD04, with PD01 open

The DOT Inspector General's 1998 index identifies **Personnel Reform Implementation Bulletin 027**, effective **October 29, 1996**, as covering FAA executive conduct and discipline as well as performance appraisals. A 2017 FAA manual index lists executive removals and discipline separately, under **EXS-7.9**. These are specific records to seek alongside the general personnel-system rules already identified. The 2006 OIG report refers to two current FAA executives in recommendation 2. Their appointment classifications, the policy actually applied and the signed decision remain unverified. ## What would distinguish the records

The word *executive* in the 2006 report leaves the underlying personnel classification open. Locate the case-specific routing or authority citation first. If the response cites an executive-system policy, match the actual provision and delegation of authority to the executive issuances. If it cites general employee procedures, match those separately to the period PMS text. A decision to decline action could be recorded in management correspondence even if no formal removal or disciplinary process began. The 1998 and 2017 indexes and the unrelated appeal decisions cannot establish what happened after the OIG recommendation.
