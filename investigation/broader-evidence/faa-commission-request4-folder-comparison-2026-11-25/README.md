# FAA April 2004 teleconference response: Commission-folder counterparts

2026-11-25 · FAA B129 / PD01–PD04 / PD07. Three documents in a released Commission-file archive folder give a candidate source-to-enclosure path for an FAA response already examined in NARA's FAA records. This is a provenance comparison, not a newly found original dispatch, proof of receipt, or the corrected May 2003 answer.

The three PDFs were retrieved through two byte-range requests on 2026-11-25. Each ZIP member's filename, compressed/uncompressed length and CRC-32 matched the saved directory index. The hashes identify these mirror bytes, not historical authenticity. No raw scans, signatures or contact details are republished.

The April 16, 2004 letter responds to DOT Request 4. It says a headquarters chronology had previously been supplied to the Commission and describes enclosed handwritten notes and an undated document titled **“11 Sep 01 Catastrophic Crisis.”** The archive's timeline is an eight-page document with that heading; the adjacent notes are also eight pages. On selected pages 1–2, the mirror timeline's heading and entries and the mirror notes' handwritten content correspond visibly to the separately held [AWA-342 timeline](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913211) and [AWA-341 notes](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913209), respectively. The [AWA-340 letter](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7601904) is a separate NARA scan of the same dated response; both letter pages were visually checked here. Copy bytes, layout and scan boundaries differ. No page-by-page comparison beyond those selected pages was performed for the notes or timeline.

## Evidentiary limits

The letter's enclosure statement, shared folder name and matching selected content strengthen a **candidate association** among the released materials. They do not establish that the particular scanned bytes were the actual original enclosures, the identity of the Commission folder creator, the shipment date or a recipient acknowledgment. The NARA and Commission-folder reproductions are related copies, not independent corroboration of 2001 events. Their exact copy ancestry and any text or redaction differences on uninspected pages remain open.

The timeline and later chronology contain separate reported time and event descriptions; preserve the existing [comparison of those records and their conflicts](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/2e8a072977f1fd387a2cb9e3e0336e5268a7d895/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-knowledge-correction-trail-2026-10-26/README.md) and [headquarters source review](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913211). Neither the letter nor these candidates resolves the later corrected-answer, administrative-action or recommendation-disposition questions.
