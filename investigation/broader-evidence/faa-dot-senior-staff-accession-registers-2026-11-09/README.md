# DOT accession records for senior staff briefings and FAA follow-up

Public derivative of the November 9, 2026 Eastern source review · B129 / PD03–PD05

The original NARA accession workbook supplies an exact **Deputy Secretary appointment-file accession covering August 31 and December 12, 2006**. Separate Peters appointment and travel accessions begin after the August referral date and cover December 12. These are dated container-level leads for the senior staff and briefing trail identified in former DOT Inspector General Kenneth Mead's testimony. Their indexes and contents remain unacquired. ## Briefings, calendars, invitations and committee minutes

- **Briefing books.** DAA-0398-2024-0005 item **0001** describes appointment, travel and subject briefing books including schedules, agendas and topics of discussion. Some briefing material can be filed apart from the calendar entry. The item is permanent and lists a planned initial span of **2001–2009**. The first page refers calendars to the DOT Capstone authority and correspondence to a separate schedule. The different series descriptions leave the actual calendar, meeting packet and dispatch independently searchable. The approved span does not match an accession to this item. [Current schedule, physical pp. 1, 4–5](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-transportation/rg-0398/daa-0398-2024-0005_sf115.pdf#page=4)
- **Invitations.** Item **0002** separately describes accepted and declined invitations and gives a temporary seven-year instruction after cutoff. It is a different file class. Whether a particular 2006 internal meeting generated any such file or came under that authority is open. [Current schedule, physical p. 5](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-transportation/rg-0398/daa-0398-2024-0005_sf115.pdf#page=5)
- **Older official committee records.** NC1-398-80-01 physical p. 5 has an amended item **7**. Typed **7/a(2)** describes records about establishment, organization, membership and policy of internal committees and gives a two-year instruction after committee termination. Handwritten **7/b(1)** separately describes agendas, minutes, final reports and related records documenting DOT-sponsored official boards or committees. It provides permanent disposition for the record copy, temporary instructions for other copies, and distinct treatment for other committee records. The NARA cover dated **06/10/2026** displays **7/A/1** and **7/B/1/A** as **P(Active)** and **7/A/2**, **7/B/1/B** and **7/B/2** as **T(Active)**. The current briefing schedule explicitly names older items **5** and **6**. These texts leave the 2006 senior staff meetings' status as a sponsored board or committee and any disposition under item 7 unresolved. The crossed-out original item 7/b language and handwritten replacement both remain visible on the preserved scan. [Historical schedule, cover and physical p. 5](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-transportation/rg-0398/nc1-398-80-01_sf115.pdf#page=5)
- **Secretarial reports.** Historical item **3**, **REPORTS (SPECIAL)**, describes biweekly highlight reports and similar Secretarial-level reports. This supplies a separate title to look for in inventories or original issue files. It leaves the relationship to the Mineta **Reports** accession and any 2006 briefing unsettled. [Historical schedule, physical p. 4](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-transportation/rg-0398/nc1-398-80-01_sf115.pdf#page=4)
