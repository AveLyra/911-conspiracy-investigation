# NIST’s March 2003 steel catalog and JFK transfer status

Public derivative of the November 6, 2026 native steel custody review, STL02–STL03. The source ledger records the acquisition paths, dates and hashes. Three contemporaneous NIST publications identify a dated steel database and report two stages of a 12-specimen JFK transfer. The final inventory supplies candidate identifiers for looking up the transfer paperwork.

## Contemporaneous records

| Record | Possession and catalog status | JFK transfer language |
| --- | --- | --- |
| April 29, 2003 NCST Advisory Committee presentation | **Nearly 250** in possession. **235** cataloged as of **March 28, 2003**. The slide names a database with photographs and member markings. | “12 specimens are in process of being transported to NIST” |
| May 7, 2003 media handout | **Nearly 250** in possession. **235** cataloged as of **March 28, 2003**. The same database description appears. | “12 specimens transported to NIST” |
| May 2003 NIST SP 1000-3 §3.6 | **Nearly 250** in possession. **235** cataloged as of **March 28, 2003**. The report again describes photographic records and member markings. | “has transported 12 specimens to NIST” |

The statements date NIST’s public descriptions. An eight-day interval separates the slide dates. Shipment, arrival and catalog entry may have had different dates. A staged transfer or a wording change are untested possibilities. The May formal report and handout are two NIST statements and supply no independent receiving record. **Nearly 250 in possession**, **235 cataloged** and the later **236-item inventory** are separate descriptions. Their dates, populations and counting rules are needed before comparing them item by item. Five rows have location **JFK/PL**, defined in the key as the main piece at JFK and a portion at NIST. One row, **B-1024**, reads **JFK**. Six rows have a NIST location code alone. **B-1044-1** is a separate decking entry associated with **B-1044**. These counts and location codes are a different record from the narrative split of six whole and six partial. They do not specify which six moved whole, whether all rows represent distinct parent pieces, or whether the listed location applied on the transfer date. An associated row, later movement, or different catalog rules could affect the count. Those explanations are untested. ## WTC 7 attribution

The B-series rows have three explicit WTC 1 assignments, three WTC 2 assignments and six empty building cells. **W-14A or A** and **W-14B** occur elsewhere in the final inventory as unidentified wide flanges with codes **205** and **PL**, respectively. Earlier FEMA field documentation attributes the candidates to WTC 7. The reviewed JFK and final inventory passages supply no W14-to-B-series alias. The published labels and blanks leave specimen provenance open.
