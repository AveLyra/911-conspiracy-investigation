## The source shows why a row is not necessarily a specimen

Selected visible descriptions provide specific counterexamples to one-row/one-steel-specimen accounting:

- **NIST-068:** physical 76, row 24, describes two beams; **NIST-076**, same page row 30, describes three columns. **NIST-119**, same page row 52, describes a washer and nut.
- **B-0068:** physical 69, row 52, describes granite; **F-0001**, physical 70 row 33, describes a reinforced-concrete column; **D-0001**, physical 70 row 31, describes a PATH train car.
- **NIST-085:** physical 76, row 38, describes aluminum sheathing. The NIST prefix therefore cannot itself be treated as a material-composition finding. **L-0017**, physical 75 row 43, also describes aluminum with cutouts.

[SELECTED-DESCRIPTORS.json](SELECTED-DESCRIPTORS.json) preserves the exact selected strings and row locators. These examples are not a full transcription or quantitative classification of all descriptions. They do not establish each object's composition by testing, present location, condition or original building.

Preserve **531 rows, 530 strings, 104 NIST-prefixed rows, the separately reported 236 NIST samples and approximately 230 returned items** as differently scoped observations. This unit does not subtract one from another to manufacture missing-piece numbers. The actual object/assembly/coupon/alias, date, selection and custody crosswalk is missing. Nor does the presence of surviving entries establish that every useful specimen survived, that transfers occurred as authorized, or that scientific access was actually exercised.

## Method and limits

The original was rendered at 220 dpi with Poppler. Local Tesseract text/TSV outputs were used only as candidate/navigation aids. An exact-pattern OCR diagnostic missed or misread identifiers on every page, so OCR results were not silently substituted for the visual ledger. Raw OCR, page images, acquisition receipts and the original remain preserved locally on the verified media store. Nothing was inferred behind redactions or extracted from hidden text.

This is a municipal-hosted court-copy representation. The scan/header/hash do not authenticate a native signed court file, January 2007 inventory, historical database, shipment, receipt or today's legal rights. The original order/agreements and their qualifications remain in the preceding review. This unit does not newly reread every earlier order or all 533 agenda pages. It performs no specimen testing, source repair, material-loss inference or adjudication of motive, misconduct or conspiracy.

The strongest limitation is that arithmetic from a manual transcription is only as good as the reading and source copy. All nine pages were checked, and the earlier 104-row subset matches, but an independent reader and native inventory could change individual readings or explain row/object relationships. They are concrete next controls, not tests already completed.
