## Observation and candidate inventory match

The January 31, 2002 Boston quality-assurance memorandum states that originals of specified September 11 Boston personnel logs (7230-1), FAA Forms 7230-4 and 7230-10, and an automated equivalent needed in support of `ZNY-ARTCC-148` are contained within `ZBW-ARTCC-148`. Those are the New York UAL175 and Boston AAL11 accident-package identifiers, respectively, as documented in the earlier [package request](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/bbe1d23a97dc3a797e824246e0470af30e9a1073/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-zny-513-2004-request-2026-11-16/README.md). The memorandum is a contemporaneous statement of where records were held, not independent verification of their contents, later custody, or every copy made.

The earlier [Boston inventory comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/20779585bcb55af2d7a66bfd9d2ec5eba6921fbf/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-boston-access-inventory-crosswalk-2026-11-16/README.md) lists a January 31, 2002 memorandum concerning the ZNY accident file: item 35 in one inventory and item 33 in the other. Date and description support a candidate match to this scan; they do not prove the two entries refer to this exact scan. The separate information-access sheet remains unlocated.
