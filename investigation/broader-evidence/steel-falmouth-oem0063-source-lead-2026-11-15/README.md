# Falmouth steel artifact: a reported custody route

STL01–STL03 / AUD011. Source inspected 2026-11-15T15:09:41.469825+00:00. A 2012 organizer account supplies a specific New York City request number for an artifact later displayed in Falmouth, Massachusetts. This is a provenance lead, not a validated connection to a NIST specimen or a particular building.

## Source observations

[The Raising of a Monument, *Trowel*, Winter 2012](https://massfreemasonry.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/2012_Winter_Trowel.pdf#page=4), physical PDF page 4 (printed pp. 4–5), reports that a steel artifact was displayed at a 2002 Gloucester exhibition, subsequently held by an architect, moved to Falmouth in May 2012, and ceremonially transferred there on September 11, 2012. A boxed reproduction of the transfer certificate identifies the City of New York Office of Emergency Management and **OEM Request Number 0063**. The Freeport, Maine beams mentioned as inspiration are separate objects and are not evidence of this artifact’s earlier custody.

A [2015 local news account](https://www.capecod.com/newscenter/cape-cod-pauses-remembers-911-terror-attacks/) describes the Falmouth steel as from the World Trade Towers and reports a prior Gloucester museum display. The organizer’s account describes World Trade Center steel without specifying a building. Keep both descriptions with their sources. Neither provides the original source basis for a building attribution; the similar display descriptions are not a verified identifier join or independent proof of origin.

## Limits and next records

The organizer’s account is a reported chain and an illustrated certificate transcription, not the original OEM request or a complete physical custody record. The source does not establish a connection to FEMA/NIST W14A, W14B, or any tested specimen. No loss, suppression, tampering, or intent follows. The original OEM 0063 request and response, associated artifact identifier, original certificate and exhibit/transfer records remain TODOs. Dimensions, markings and traceable custody records would be needed before comparing it with a specific known specimen.
