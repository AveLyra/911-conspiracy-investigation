## Underlying inventories and a prior Commission letter

- **AWA-176 and AWA-179 have the same 29 named facility rows in the same order.** The two lists distinguish New York Air Route Traffic Control Center (**ZNY**) from New York TRACON (**N90**). AWA-179 adds a separate miscellaneous certification line and lists eight colored tabs, including an **AAT-20 Inventory Index** under the brown tab. Its note describes formatting used to denote certifications received. AWA-176 names production dates, including **37 boxes on October 10**, **12 packages on October 14**, **5 packages on October 16** and an October 21 entry without a box count. It describes the delivered records as certified copies, with originals then under individual facility Air Traffic Managers. These are reported locations and delivery counts. They supply no current custody or item-by-item receipt. The summary also lists request No. 2 question **7** twice. The printed order and duplicate were retained in the local source. [AWA-176, text lines 3–10, 13–41 and 43](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7601663) · [AWA-179, text lines 3–12 and 16–48](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7601666)
- **The August 19 Commission letter distinguishes access decisions from actual receipt.** AWA-822 is a two-page fax copy of the letter to the Transportation Secretary. It says the Commission planned to assess DOT's decisions on pending access issues and whether important overdue documents actually arrived, using **close of business September 5, 2003** as the cutoff. The visible fax imprints are dated **August 21, 2003**, identify DOT/OST General Counsel and label the pages **P.2/3** and **P.3/3**. The scanned PDF contains the two letter pages. The first fax page and the intended routing or acknowledgment remain open. [AWA-822, physical pp. 1–2](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/e-Other/5-AWA-822-Letter-Commissions-Request-for-DOT-Policy-Choices-and-Key-Docs.pdf)
- **The signed November 17 response remains a separate target.** The May 2004 OIG letter attributes a **November 17, 2003** response and further transmittal to FAA, describes no indication of intentional withholding, and says Commission contact was still needed on responsiveness. The Word bodies identify the subpoena they address and have a draft cost field. The archived versions supply no executed cover, date of actual submission or Commission receipt. The Commission's October concerns and the earlier FAA volume claims retain their different source scopes and periods. [OIG CC-2004-003, physical p. 2](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2004003.pdf#page=2) · [Commission statement, October 15, 2003](https://govinfo.library.unt.edu/911/press/pr_2003-10-15.pdf)
