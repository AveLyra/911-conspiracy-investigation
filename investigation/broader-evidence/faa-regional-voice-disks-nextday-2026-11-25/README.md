# Regional FAA chronology: next-day voice-disk and request entries

Reviewed 2026-11-25. The [AEA-2879 chronology](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-e-other/5-AEA-2879-911-disk2-REDACT-SEALED.pdf#page=7) adds potential custody and acquisition leads to the [prior intranet review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/7f86390eb464f3f5cb82fb386c64ceabb00e4004/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-regional-intranet-custody-2026-11-25/README.md). Its first page says times are local Eastern Daylight Time and entries are preliminary and subject to change. Its page 7 marks the change to **12 September 2001 at 00:01 AM**.

## What the record says

- At **02:13 AM**, page 7 reports a request coordinated through the headquarters crisis management center from the New York FBI office for copies of all voice tapes of air-traffic-control communications for the four aircraft. The listed facilities include Boston Center (ZBW) for AAL11 and UAL175. At **02:25 AM**, the chronology says all facilities were notified of the request. These are chronology entries, not the request, notices or receipts themselves.
- At **04:20 AM**, page 8 reports that an attempt at IAD to provide a requested voice tape failed because the recorder was “OTS,” while a requested EWR tape was locked in a safe. The entry does not establish which tape content was requested, whether later recovery succeeded, or whether either tape relates to the ZBW disks.
- At **6:10 AM**, page 8 says: “ZBW voice disks received for AAL11 and UAL175 by AEA-504 (Hayes) being copied”. It does not provide disk identifiers, chain-of-custody receipts, audio content, copy destinations, completion status or later disposition.
- At **6:45 AM**, page 8 separately states: “Per AEA-500 only one copy of data.” Its object and relationship to the 6:10 entry are unspecified. Preserve both without assuming they concern the same data or resolving their apparent tension.

The preceding day's entries also report the intranet handling and an instruction to keep originals at the facility, as documented in the linked predecessor. Neither those entries nor the next-day disk entry proves that the disks contain the same material as the earlier intranet files, or proves completed copying, transmission, concealment or destruction. Similar evidence labels do not establish a chain of custody. The individual named in the chronology is a source locator, not evidence of wrongdoing.

## TODOs and discriminating evidence

Locate the original New York FBI request and distribution notices, facility response and receipt records, ZBW disk inventories and identifiers, AEA-504 transfer and copying logs, resulting copies, and file metadata. Compare date, file identifiers and content only if those records or media are obtained. Seek the IAD/EWR request and later completion or noncompletion records separately. Continue to preserve the unresolved FAA corrected-answer and administrative-disposition TODOs; this chronology does not establish their outcome.
