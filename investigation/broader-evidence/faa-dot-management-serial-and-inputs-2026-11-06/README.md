# The Secretary’s audit follow-up reports and the records used to prepare them

November 6, 2026 UTC · **B129 / PD02–PD05**. The federal publications catalog identifies the Transportation Secretary’s semiannual report as **TD 1.1/4**, **OCLC 23231716**, and **depository item 0982-C-22**. A public FHWA order also names two types of records the Office of the Secretary used to prepare its report on final action. These give the investigation separate searches for published copies and for the tracking records behind them. The 2006–2007 management reports and their case coverage remain open. ## Records behind the published reports

FHWA’s April 30, 1990 order describes an audit process within FHWA. Paragraph 11 identifies **periodic OIG final-action status reports** and **operating-administration final-action reports** as the two inputs used by the Office of the Secretary. For a missed target it describes entering a new projected date and an explanation. On completion it calls for the audit number and title, recommendation, management decision, completion date and any financial recovery or funds put to better use. It also allows an OIG status form to be completed and returned as the final-action report. A search for separate closure memoranda alone could miss a completed status form. [FHWA Order 2910.2B, ¶¶4, 10–11](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/legsregs/directives/orders/29102b.cfm#:~:text=MANAGEMENT%20REPORTING)

Paragraph 3 dates **DOT Order 8000.1C** to **July 20, 1989** and gives its audit and investigation title. Paragraph 9 cites its **Chapter 1, paragraph 4** in FHWA’s audit instructions. The order provides an exact chapter locator and names report inputs. The governing investigation chapter, any FAA equivalent and applicability to **CC-2006-085 / 042R0122001** remain unverified. An FHWA audit timetable cannot establish the due date of an FAA investigation action. [FHWA Order 2910.2B, ¶¶3–4, 9](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/legsregs/directives/orders/29102b.cfm#:~:text=DOT%20Order%208000.1C)

NARA’s separate RG 0398 accession table lists a five-container set of Maria Cino appointment records, **TR-0398-2021-0015**, with dates **April 12, 2005–December 18, 2007**. It also lists **TR-0398-2022-0008**, one container of Secretary Norman Mineta reports. That row’s title gives **February 8, 2001–November 8, 2005**, while the start/end columns give **2/8/2021–11/8/2021**. Preserve both ranges. These are accession-level descriptions and no folder list or case inclusion was verified. The Cino transfer is separate from the previously identified eleven-container Mineta/Peters appointment transfer. [NARA accession table](https://www.archives.gov/research/accessions#:~:text=TR-0398-2022-0008) · [Prior appointment transfer review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/c1b0dd4e464e5da13f5c46097b06360f033eaa4e/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-dot-transition-addressee-2026-11-06/README.md)

**Open records.** Locate the 2006–2007 issue bodies and enclosure lists under the exact period and series keys. Seek the completed OIG status and FAA final-action forms, the Office of the Secretary compilation records, and the applicable FAA or DOT investigation routing policy. If a form names a case or recommendation, compare its decision, projected date, completion date and evidence references against the signed action or no-action determination and the corrected-answer delivery records. Record exclusions before drawing any inference from a missing row.
