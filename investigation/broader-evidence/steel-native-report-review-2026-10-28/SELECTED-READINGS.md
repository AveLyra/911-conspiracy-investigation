# Selected source readings and exact locators

These are checked paraphrases and factual labels, not a verbatim transcription or native-data release. Physical pages refer to the downloaded file. Printed labels remain separate. WJE graphics/full text are not republished here.

- S01 — V1 physical296 / printedxliii, executive summary E.1: describes the outside WJE tasks and points to Appendix F for the report and G for NIST review; expressly excludes WTC 7 steel from this tower damage/failure study.
- S02 — V2 physical173: WJE Final Report dated26November2003, title Visual Observations of the Steel Recovered from the World Trade Center Site; NIST order SB1341-03-Q-0155; WJE2003.0323.0. This identifies the published appendix, not the native delivered draft.
- S03 — V2 physical178 / printed462: accepted tower as-built locations and item numbering came from NIST; unidentified means exact as-built location not conclusively determined. The same page, headed26November2003, cites NCSTAR1-3B(2005).
- S04 — V2 physical178 / printed462: unaided general visual observations covered236 pieces; not all236 received detailed examination. Simple optical/ultrasonic aids anticipated in planning were not used. No exact total of detailed examinations is supplied in this paragraph.
- S05 — V2 physical178 / printed462: handwritten field notes were later transcribed into attachment tables; primarily digital photographs, plus some film prints later digitized; whiteZ0–Z9 markings identify areas of interest. Work occurred more than a year after collapse, with weathering and fracture degradation acknowledged.
- S06 — V2 physical184 / printed468 §3.6: six pieces believed to come from structures other than WTC1/2; body says over half generally showed fire effects and cannot distinguish pre-collapse from post-collapse exposure. It does not identify them as WTC7.
- S07 — V2 physical239–244 / printedC-523–C-528: six worksheets dated31July2003 / CP & JH, building/column/floors unknown. Their recorded states are three fire indications, one no-distinct indication, two explicitly unassessed. Piece-level labels and qualifications are in the CSV.
- S08 — V2 physical252 / printed474 §G.2.1(3): eight possible-fire panels listed: K-2,N-8,N-9,N-12,S-9,S-10,C-11,M-4. V2 physical254 / printed476 introduces nine panels and one core column; physical254–257 adds K-1 to those eight and also discusses C-88b.
- S09 — V2 physical253–254 / printed475–476: NIST describes resource/time limits and later-developed paint/fire-exposure methods, rejects an inferred simple relation between visual cues and steel-temperature excursions, and offers other possible causes of missing paint/buckling. These are source interpretations, not adjudicated explanations here.
- S10 — V2 physical255–257 / printed477–479: item-level paint/visual/photographic comparisons include both agreement and different assessments. S-10 and C-88b have no paint available for inspection, so no NIST paint conclusion; that is not a negative temperature reading. Unidentified C-11/M-4 lack as-built fire-location context.
- S11 — V2 physical258 / printed480: NIST expressly recognizes visual-versus-paint discrepancies, suggests paint degradation without local steel exceeding250°C as a possible explanation, and says seven panels had photographically observable pre-collapse fires but neither recovery-based technique indicated exposure. M-2 is an example with over16 continuous minutes of external flaming at floor98. No complete seven-panel list or independent exposure/temperature measurement is supplied by this paragraph.
- S12 — V2 physical251–259 / printed473–481: disagreements coexist with described agreements on weld/impact/core/truss/connection observations. The published review itself is contrary evidence to an assertion that every adverse contractor observation was suppressed. It does not prove an earlier draft was reproduced unchanged or timely delivered.
- S13 — I3B physical87 / printed59 TableA1: C-31 orS-7 is wide-flange, storage205; 67635 appears under Markings. Accepted Bldg/Column/Floors fields are blank. I3B physical88 / printed60: C-42 wide-flange/storage205 with no accepted location. These were visually checked in the actual column geometry.
- S14 — I3B physical96 / printed68 TableA7: C-72b,C-78,C-85,M-11 are listed among unidentified wide-flange pieces; C-31/S-7 and C-42 are absent from that one list but present in A1. This bounded list difference does not establish nonreceipt, disposal or absence from the entire inventory. W14A/B remain separate unidentified candidates.
- S15 — V1 physical3 / printed286, physical4 / printed285, physical71 / printed218, physical296 / printedxliii: selected source pages are in descending printed order as physical positions increase. Do not assume physical page equals printed page plus a fixed offset. Source bytes were not reordered. A machine-only numeric-footer scan was ambiguous and is not treated as a complete page-order authentication.
