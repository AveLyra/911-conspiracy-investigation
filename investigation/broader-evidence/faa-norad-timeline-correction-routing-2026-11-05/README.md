## The same time has two descriptions

The 2006 DoD memorandum says that the **actual time** for the sentence describing notification of the **American 77 hijacking** was **9:34 a.m.** The Commission's July 2004 report says that at **9:34**, NEADS was notified that **American 77 was lost**, and states that NEADS did not receive notice it was hijacked. Preserve the message descriptions and recipient labels separately. The DoD memo says **DoD** in its sentence. The Commission describes **NEADS**. Agreement on the minute leaves the message content and routing unresolved. Test the labels against the actual report to NEADS, the precise Commission timeline delivered to NORAD, and any separate DoD notification the memorandum relied on. [DoD memorandum, physical p. 1, OCR lines 30–31](https://web.archive.org/web/20120707002543id_/http://www.dodig.mil/Ir/reports/06-intel-12.pdf#page=1) · [Commission report, ch. 1, p. 34, extracted lines 28–31](https://www.9-11commission.gov/report/911Report_Ch1.pdf#page=34)
