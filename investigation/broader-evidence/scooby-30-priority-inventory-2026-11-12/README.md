## Consequential discrepancies and theoretical implications

* **UMS intelligence:** The [August 2002 visit record and 2003–04 inquiry records](../scooby-ums-hq-visit-originals-2026-11-12/README.md) narrow the record search but do not establish that the visit produced a particular final headquarters assessment. Redactions and missing records do not establish an intelligence affiliation or deliberate destruction.
* **WTC access:** The [visitor-card and Zim check](../scooby-zim-card-body-checks-2026-11-11/README.md) preserves an important access lead, but cardholder, actual access and mover remain unresolved. A real job or visitor card alone would not establish explosives placement.
* **Chemical evidence:** The [disposition record and transfer-reference review](../scooby-van-transfer-reference-check-2026-11-12/README.md) documents a disposition trail but not a laboratory receipt, final result or deliberate concealment. This distinguishes an unresolved test from a negative test.
* **Saudis and CIA:** The [Bayoumi](../scooby-bayoumi-recovered-claims-2026-11-11/README.md) and [routing](../scooby-cir-routing-source-check-2026-11-12/README.md) checks distinguish reported assistance and a documented hold from any unproved coordinated CIA/Saudi/Israeli plan. Independent or bureaucratic explanations remain viable.
* **Collapse models and physical evidence:** NIST [states it did not test tower steel for explosive or thermite residues](https://www.nist.gov/world-trade-center-investigation/study-faqs/wtc-towers-investigation), question 29; this is a testing limitation, not a positive residue result. Its observational and modeling evidence remains relevant. [NIST sensitivity checks](../scooby-nist-comparison-check-2026-11-12/README.md), [UAF lineage](../scooby-uaf-lineage-source-check-2026-11-12/README.md), and [dust provenance](../scooby-dust-candidate-audit-2026-11-11/README.md) retain assumptions, alternative explanations and unresolved reproducibility.
* **Financial inference:** The [SEC reconciliation](../scooby-options-source-check-2026-11-12/README.md) separates a corrected aggregate count from account ownership and knowledge; the [insurance review](../scooby-insurance-judicial-check-2026-11-12/README.md) separates proceeds and legal awards from net profit or advance knowledge.

## Continue only where decision-relevant

Prioritize original headquarters assessments; WTC holder and event records with UMS/Max job documents; laboratory accession, results and disposition mapping; Classic/Miami customer and notebook records; and CIA routing/source records. For models, seek native inputs and independently discriminating observations. Review a full report, table or attachment when it could materially alter one of these conclusions, challenge a source citation, or resolve a contradiction. Existing checks need not be repeated merely to restate background. When the necessary primary record remains inaccessible, preserve the uncertainty and the concrete locator instead of presenting repeated searching as resolution.
