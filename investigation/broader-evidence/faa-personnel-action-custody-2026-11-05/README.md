# FAA personnel decision records and grievance tracking

November 5, 2026 · B129 / PD02–PD04. DOT OIG **CC-2006-085**, recommendation 2, called for consideration of appropriate administrative action. This review traces the separate personnel decision and recordkeeping routes against the FAA investigative tracker identified previously. No entry for the case, signed decision, or completed corrective action was recovered. [OIG recommendation 2, p. 12](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=12) · [Prior FAA investigative tracker comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/909704bd2adedecbc660e5dcfdc119a997757450/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-investigative-tracking-custody-2026-11-05/README.md)

## What could identify a decision

- **A proposed or taken action in 2006.** The June 19, 2006 **OPM/GOVT–3** notice locates covered records in personnel or designated offices of agencies where the action was processed. It covers current and former employees, including covered Senior Executive Service employees, against whom a specified action was **proposed or taken** under the listed provisions. Its file description includes a notice of proposed action, supporting material, employee reply and agency decision as applicable. Its note treats files compiled on MSPB appeal or as part of an EEOC complaint under their separate systems. This is a contemporaneous locator for a covered proposed or completed action. It leaves a recommendation that was only considered, and which personnel provisions applied to the individuals in the 2006 matter, open. [71 FR 35350–35351, physical pp. 9–10](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2006-06-19/pdf/06-5459.pdf#page=9)
- **A separate grievance database in FY 2006.** The FAA performance report describes deploying the **Grievance Electronic Tracking System (GETS)** and measuring processing time from its entries. It says regional, center and headquarters labor-relations users supplied the data and that **completed records are not deleted** in its description of that database. This is the FAA's description of GETS for that report. Its scope is grievance tracking. Current survival, the date range of legacy entries, and any migration from GETS into another system require separate evidence. [FY 2006 report, printed pp. 77 and 87, physical pp. 88 and 98](https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/about/plans_reports/archive/Combined_FY_2006_FAA_PAR_Final_v6_12-14-2006.pdf#page=98)
- **Later labor-relations case records.** The March 2010 assessment describes **LERIS** as a workload tracker for labor-management relations, employee disciplinary matters and employee assistance, with historical case data. Its notice and consent section says inclusion requires that the person filed or was involved in an FAA grievance. Its system notice section identifies **DOT/ALL 1** and describes searching by name. The later assessment linked by DOT in June 2026 describes Labor and Employee Relations (**AHL**) custody, and retrieval by name **or LERIS-generated employee case file number**. It names fields for **proposed action**, **proposal issued date**, **final decision**, **effective date** and **disposition** in the described data exchange. It describes management no-action, training or disciplinary responses specifically for the **Accountability Board harassment workflow**. The scope of historical disciplinary-only coverage, the handling of a decision declining action in this inquiry, and any historical migration remain open. [2010 assessment, sections on overview, notice and retention](https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/privacy/pia-labor-and-employee-relations-information-system-leris) · [Later assessment, physical pp. 3–6](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/2026-06/Privacy%20%20-FAA%20-%20LERIS%20-%20PIA%20-%202026.pdf#page=3)

A GETS grievance entry, a LERIS personnel case, an FAA investigative referral, an OIG recommendation status and a signed decision are different records. The FY 2006 statement about GETS does not establish whether the historical rule applied to other personnel files or whether anything was actually transferred or disposed of. The public documents describe possible record routes and retain the conflicting scope and schedule wording.

## Open originals and tests

- [ ] **PD02.** Locate the signed FAA consideration or action/no-action determination, including the approver, reasons and notice or referral. If a covered personnel action was proposed or taken, seek the action file in the agency processing office and any separately assigned grievance or appeal number. A decision recorded only in a management memorandum requires the management correspondence trail.
- [ ] **PD03–PD04.** Seek the 2006 agency office/case assignment, applicable personnel and investigative file plan, GETS legacy coverage and any migration manifest or cross-reference to LERIS. Compare the proposal, final decision, effective date, disposition and source documents separately if a relevant entry is acquired. Test record class, cutoff, appeal or hold and actual custody before drawing a retention conclusion.
- [ ] **PD01 and PD03.** Preserve the corrected May 2003 answer and recipient acknowledgment, and the OIG follow-up and recommendation-specific closure records as separate open dependencies.
