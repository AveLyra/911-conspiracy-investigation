# GAO follow-up on the Domestic Events Network and the FAA records it names

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD03–PD04.

GAO's public recommendation record for **GAO-05-928T** reports that in **July 2007** the FAA supplied a **DEN Customer Guide** updated in **June 2007** and an **Interagency Airspace Protection Working Group charter** dated **October 20, 2005**. GAO lists the DEN performance and procedure recommendation as **Closed – Implemented** for DHS, DoD and DOT. This identifies a specific FAA-to-GAO submission and two enclosures. DOT OIG report **CC-2006-085** issued separate recommendations about retaining recordings and reviewing procedures. Its management response and recommendation-by-recommendation disposition remain unacquired. ## Dates and scope to preserve

- **Working group versus charter.** DHS reported in July 2005 that the working group was already meeting and had been chartered. GAO's later entry names an October 20, 2005 charter and describes it as creating a forum for improving DEN procedures. GAO's DOT entry also describes the absence of a forum for evaluating DEN procedures at the time of its review. Preserve all three descriptions. The earlier charter and dated meeting records would distinguish continuity, a changed mandate and different uses of the term forum. [July letter, PDF p. 31](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GAOREPORTS-GAO-05-928T/pdf/GAOREPORTS-GAO-05-928T.pdf#page=31) · [Later status record](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-05-928t#:~:text=At%20the%20time%20of%20our%20review)
- **Regional operations plan.** The same GAO testimony gives **April 2005** for agencies completing the National Capital Region Coordination Center plan and **May 2005** for participating agencies approving a plan. Preserve both dates with their verbs. The actual versions, signatures and distribution history remain open. [April entry, PDF p. 19 lines 6–10](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GAOREPORTS-GAO-05-928T/pdf/GAOREPORTS-GAO-05-928T.pdf#page=19) · [May entry, PDF p. 16 lines 20–28](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GAOREPORTS-GAO-05-928T/pdf/GAOREPORTS-GAO-05-928T.pdf#page=16)
- **DHS report and Senate referral.** The indexed September 2005 DHS annual report also describes a working group that predated October. It names the Homeland Security Council Director of Aviation and Transportation Security as chair. The July letter gives the TSA chair account above. The index leaves dates of the chair assignments open. Direct download of the DHS PDF returned HTTP 404. A separately acquired Senate entry gives **EC–3936**, September 22, 2005, and referral of the DHS first annual report to Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs. This supplies a recipient-side locator. [DHS report index](https://www.dhs.gov/xlibrary/assets/NCR_DHS_Congressional_Final_090105.pdf) · [Senate referral, PDF p. 4, printed S10376, EC–3936](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-2005-09-22/pdf/CREC-2005-09-22-pt1-PgS10373-9.pdf#page=4)
