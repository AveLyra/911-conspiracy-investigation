# Claircom asset disclosures and SkyWay bankruptcy record locators


November 5, 2026. This **TEL01/TEL03/TEL04** follow-up extends the carrier-custody review. It compares SkyWay's filed business and accounting disclosures with the earlier acquisition descriptions and pins the bankruptcy docket and specific records that could document custody after reorganization. [Prior FCC and SEC comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/b7acacb001d8faf682e048e667ba1de1148c710e/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-claircom-asset-custody-2026-11-05/README.md)

## Company disclosures and purchase scope

| Record and locator | Observation |
| --- | --- |
| SkyWay Form 10-KSB, printed pp. 3–4 and 10–11, indexed lines 156–203 and 662–687 | SkyWay reports temporary authorization covering **136 sites**, **33** sites reactivated, **95** surveyed and **60** leases signed. It says transmitting equipment still needed replacement before it could transmit information. The company distinguishes site authorization, reactivation, equipment readiness and lease rights. |
| Same Form 10-KSB, printed p. 49, indexed lines 2878–2882 | Reports a January 2003 **$250,000 installment payable** under an asset purchase agreement and a separate accounting description of **$250,000 expensed** for ground-site lease contact information and a temporary restriction on transfer of certain patent rights. This passage does not name the seller. The executed agreement is needed to verify its relationship to the January 16 Claircom agreement identified in the earlier company disclosure. |
| Earlier SEC complaint, Doc. 1, p. 11, ¶¶45–46 | Quotes the August 2003 company claim about a **166-tower** acquisition across the U.S., Mexico and Canada, disputes it and cites the August 6 AT&T letter. The earlier FCC order describes acquisition of the former system. The different scopes remain preserved. |

The **136 authorized sites** and the **166 towers** in the quoted acquisition claim describe different quantities. Keep the values and their source labels together. The company disclosures and SEC allegations do not establish which historical records, if any, transferred. The signed schedules and delivery inventory would test that directly. [Form 10-KSB](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1128723/000107087604000086/skyway10ksb2004.htm) · [SEC complaint](https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/complaints/2009/comp20960.pdf#page=11) · [FCC order, ¶11](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-04-287A1.pdf#page=9)

## Bankruptcy and court records

- **Bankruptcy docket 8:05-bk-11953-PMG, Middle District of Florida.** A court-hosted order in adversary proceeding **8:07-ap-240-PMG**, pp. 2–3, identifies main-case **Doc. 420** as the reorganization plan, **Doc. 422** as the second amended disclosure statement, and **Doc. 494** as the confirmation order. It describes a stock purchase by World Capita, Inc. and continued corporate existence under the name World Capita Communications, Inc. This identifies a later records-holder lead. It gives no custody determination for Claircom call data. The public SEC registration order independently supplies the bankruptcy case number. [Bankruptcy court order, pp. 2–3](https://pacer.flmb.uscourts.gov/fwxflmb/opn/getopn2.fwx?id=00001341&kid=097068106080065113087073) · [SEC registration order, §III.1](https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/admin/2007/34-55787.pdf#page=2)
- **SEC civil case 8:09-cv-00455.** Doc. 53, pp. 1 and 20, concerns corporate default and relief. Doc. 208, p. 38, directs judgment for one individual on the claims tried. Doc. 255, pp. 1 and 4, distinguishes those outcomes and certifies the appeal. The acquired orders do not supply the signed Claircom agreement, the AT&T letter, or an asset-by-asset finding. An SEC notice separately identifies a February 22, 2013 qualifying judgment or order. The underlying 2013 text was outside the acquired set. [Doc. 53](https://cases.justia.com/federal/district-courts/florida/flmdce/8%3A2009cv00455/224575/53/0.pdf) · [Doc. 208](https://cases.justia.com/federal/district-courts/florida/flmdce/8%3A2009cv00455/224575/208/0.pdf) · [Doc. 255](https://cases.justia.com/federal/district-courts/florida/flmdce/8%3A2009cv00455/224575/255/0.pdf) · [SEC notice](https://www.sec.gov/enforcement-litigation/whistleblower-program/notice-covered-actions/award-claim-2013-024)
