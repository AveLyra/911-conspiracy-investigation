# FAA production indexes and the October 28 recording batch

> Public derivative of the November 5, 2026 FAA production-index study.


November 5, 2026 · B129 / PD07. Two additional native Word files and related FAA records provide item-level leads for testing the October subpoena-response drafts. The drafts both refer to **20 additional October 28, 2003 recordings**, with **New York Center** in AWA-178 and **New York TRACON** in AWA-177. The new records preserve that conflict and identify separate delivery, certification and internal intake records. An October 17, 2003 memorandum from the Regulation and Certification office separately names the Administrator's October 14 memorandum and addresses its request for Commission information. It gives another office-level lead to the tasking record. The October 14 memorandum itself remains unacquired in this study. [AWA-756, physical p. 1, native lines 6–13](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7602278)

The AWA-177/AWA-167 comparison matches descriptions within the same FAA records chain. It leaves actual file identity, enclosure order and Commission receipt unverified. The LAN list demonstrates that both facilities had separately described material marked received. Neither it nor the N90 certification description assigns the October 28 batch to a facility.
