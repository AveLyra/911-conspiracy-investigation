# WF1899: a voicemail-access lead with unlocated collection and message identities

A newly read FBI-form record, **WF1899**, reports that **Ted Olson had new voicemail at his former law firm** and that his former secretary would provide access to the FBI. Its follow-up instructs contact with the firm's office administrator **“FOR ACCESS TO MAIL OF OLSON.”** This supplies a concrete office-message/access/disposition target for the telephone investigation.

**The record does not identify who left the messages or say they were Barbara Olson's calls from Flight 77. It does not document completed access, collected audio or a final result.** The difference between **VOICE MAIL** in the intake and **MAIL** in the instruction remains preserved. No message medium, sender, caller, flight or carrier-row identity is selected from context alone.
