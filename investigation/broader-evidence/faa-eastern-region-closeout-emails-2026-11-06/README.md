# Atlantic City records sent for the Commission's 2004 follow-up

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD01 / PD07, with recorder custody and clock dependencies

A **July 16, 2004** Atlantic City Tower memorandum corrects the tower's earlier certified index. It reports that three position logs had been located and describes an additional window of cassette rerecordings. June emails show that the tower asked for clarification after receiving different descriptions of the records sought. The July 6 response reports what the tower searched and sent to the FAA's Eastern Region. These records establish a dated supplementation trail for the Commission's 2004 inquiry. The corrected **May 22, 2003** FAA answer sought in the **August 31, 2006** OIG report and any acknowledgment remain unacquired. [July 16 memorandum](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34911943) · [2006 OIG recommendation 1](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=12)

## Checks and unresolved differences

- **Clock labels.** Converting only the printed times at UTC−4, the June request's **0835 EDT** center is **1235 UTC**, earlier than either stated rerecording window. The **1030 EDT** scramble-order reference converts to **1430 UTC**, which falls inside the first package reported on July 6. The two reported rerecording windows convert to **0857–0918 EDT** and **1025–1136 EDT**. They are outer bounds for selected positions, and the extent of the requested tolerance around 0835 is unspecified. These checks leave the recording completeness and the tower's reported negative search separate.
- **Elkin21.** Preserve **1428Z/1128L** as printed on June 30 and **1528 UTC** as printed on July 6. Under the same UTC−4 assumption, **1128L** corresponds to **1528Z**, while **1428Z** corresponds to **1028L**. The sources leave the reason for the one-hour UTC difference open. The other two printed UTC departures agree across both records. The cassette labels, clock reference and original recorded departure or strip would test the reported times.
- **Versions and delivery.** Eleven acquired PDFs have distinct SHA-256 values. Several contain forwarded copies of the same messages, so eleven distinct files do not establish eleven distinct communications or independent accounts. The facility's enclosure and shipment statements are separate from dispatch tracking and Commission receipt. NARA's AEA-2298 download returned **HTTP 200 with HTML**, leaving the airbill unacquired.
