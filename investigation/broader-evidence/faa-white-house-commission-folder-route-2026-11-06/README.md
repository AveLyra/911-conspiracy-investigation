## Public briefing check

The archived briefing index lists four transcripts from **August 31 through September 8, 2006**. A reproducible search of the body of each transcript returned **zero** hits for the case number, FAA, its full agency name, Zinser, NORAD, inspector general, American 77 and Flight 77. The public September news index separately lists three White House release links for September 1, concerning jobs, Labor Day and personnel. Those listings and the four transcripts cover the specified White House web records. They leave any FAA or DOT press-office release and private routing unverified.
