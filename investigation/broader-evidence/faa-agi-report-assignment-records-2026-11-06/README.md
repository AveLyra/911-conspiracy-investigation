# FAA report assignment and review records

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD03–PD05, with PD01–PD02 open. The FAA's FY 2009 budget submission names **Government and Industry Affairs (AGI)** as a sender-side route for congressional report assignments, timelines, edits and reviews. It also describes daily contact reports and weekly activity reports. This identifies records to look for alongside the Office of the Secretary's report-control form and transmittal package. Case-specific assignment and the management report enclosures remain open. [FAA FY 2009 budget submission, Safety & Operations pp. 80–81](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/docs/faa_fy_2009_budget_estimate.pdf#:~:text=Detailed%20Justification%20for%20Staff%20Offices%20%E2%80%93%20AGI) · [Earlier DOT report-control findings](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/429beb89b3037587bf03d6f0b9b4cff4a6d78c7f/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-congressional-report-control-form-2026-11-06/README.md)

## Roles and documents by source

| Source | Observation and limit |
| --- | --- |
| **FAA WA 1220.1**, May 30, 1980 | Designates the **Special Assistant for Legislative Affairs (AOA-10)** for report control. It distinguishes recurring reports coordinated with DOT **M-34** from one-time reports liaised through **B-15**. The current catalog lists **AGI-7** and labels the order **Active**. Keep the printed and catalog office codes separately. [Order, physical pp. 1–2](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Order/1220.1.pdf#page=1) · [Catalog 1391](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/orders_notices/index.cfm/go/document.information/documentID/1391) |
| **FAA 1100.148B**, December 6, 1988, Appendix 1 | Transmits **DOT 1100.62B**, dated November 4, 1988. The appended manual names the **Office of Government and Industry Affairs** as responsible for managing the FAA Reports to Congress program and serving as Reports Control Officer. The header on physical p. 20 shows **1100.62B** on its face. The embedded text extracted by pypdf shows **1100.628** on that page. The values are preserved as separate representations. [Manual cover and Appendix 1](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Order/1100.148B.pdf#page=1) · [Appendix p. 41, physical p. 20](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Order/1100.148B.pdf#page=20) |
| **GAO-05-266**, April 2005 | The FAA-sourced chart and table identify the FAA staff office as **Government and Industry Affairs** and describe its congressional advisory and coordination role. The table summarizes organization responsibilities. It leaves the specific internal report-control assignment and administrative correspondence routing open. [Physical pp. 10–11, printed pp. 6–7](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GAOREPORTS-GAO-05-266/pdf/GAOREPORTS-GAO-05-266.pdf#page=11) |
| **FY 2009 FAA submission**, printed pp. 80–81 | Under **Anticipated FY 2008 Accomplishments**, the submission describes assigning a compiling office and due dates, reviewing and editing drafts, and forwarding final reports to the **Office of the Administrator (AOA)** for review and approval. It describes daily congressional-contact reports to AGI management, weekly activity reports to the Administrator and senior DOT officials, and input to the Administrator's weekly White House report. Its FY 2009 request separately describes the Reports to Congress liaison role and coordination between FAA, OST and OMB. These are prospective or program descriptions. [FAA budget, AGI section](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/docs/faa_fy_2009_budget_estimate.pdf#:~:text=Anticipated%20FY%202008%20Accomplishments) |
| **FY 2010 FAA submission**, printed pp. 115–116 | Under **Anticipated FY 2009 Accomplishments**, the submission repeats the assignments, draft review, approval and reporting functions. Its FY 2010 request describes the same liaison functions. These two agency descriptions appear to share wording and offer limited independent corroboration. [FAA budget, AGI section](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/docs/faa_fy_2010_budget_estimate.pdf#:~:text=Anticipated%20FY%202009%20Accomplishments) |
| **FAA 1360.16A**, effective January 19, 2006 | Separately identifies the FAA Executive Secretariat **AOA-3** for correspondence policy, controlled items and its correspondence system or successor. Search this route for the OIG referral and response. The applicability of congressional report procedures to a response to the 9/11 Commission, or to a post-sunset recipient, needs independent evidence. [FAA correspondence order, physical pp. 1–2](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Order/ND1360.16A.pdf#page=1) |

The sources use office-level and position-level designations from different dates. Preserve **AOA-10**, **AGI**, **AGI-7**, and **AOA-3** with their source descriptions. The 1988 manual and budget submissions supply the AGI route. A historical assignment or routing sheet would establish the specific unit or officer, the submitting FAA office, and the records actually generated. Review and approval, Secretary-level dispatch, and congressional receipt would still require separate evidence.
