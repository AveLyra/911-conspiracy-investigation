# Pier 6 steel loading chronology and Weeks Marine exhibit trail

November 6, 2026 · Steel custody · B188 / H005 / STL01–03

A Department of Labor remand decision records two dates for Weeks Marine's Pier 6 operation. It finds that the crane arrived **September 17, 2001** and that the company's off-loading of large structural steel from trucks began **September 18**. It cites specific exhibits for both findings. A worker testified to a **September 13** start for his work, which the judge rejected. The underlying exhibits remain the records to obtain. [February 12, 2008 remand decision, pp. 6–7](https://www.oalj.dol.gov/DECISIONS/ALJ/LHC/2004/BA_v_WEEKS_MARINE_INC_2004LHC00660_(FEB_12_2008)_121138_CADEC_SD.PDF#page=7) · [Benefits Review Board decision, pp. 3–5](https://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/brb/decisions/lngshore/unpublished/Nov08/08-0432.pdf)

## Keep loading, dredging and award records separate

September 18 is **six calendar days before** the reported September 24 start of the Pier 6 emergency dredging job. The dates describe different activities. They leave open the berth, vessel and receiving destination for an individual early steel load. The dredging contract candidate **DACW3301C0026** still lacks an explicit pier name in the federal procurement record. Neither the loading decisions nor the agency procurement table supplies the signed contract, **P00001**, or a permit. [Pier 6 procurement study and Corps date sources](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/f1b4fe6a35f71e775e254408a2c873a15e95501f/investigation/broader-evidence/steel-pier6-procurement-record-2026-11-06/README.md)

The Corps' historical FY2001 and FY2002 *Dredging Contracts Awarded* PDFs contained no exact candidate number, **$790,500** bid or Pier 6 job label in their extractable text. The FY2001 DQM page captured for this review returned **46 table rows** and had no exact candidate identifier. It displays **DACA5101C0031** for Fire Island to Jones Inlet with **$14,995,500**, while the prior USAspending comparison row reads **DACW5101C0031** and **$76,667.00** in the action obligation field. Preserve both prefixes and the different amount fields. The current records supply no explicit crosswalk for those rows. Neither the older bid tables nor the captured dashboard establishes complete coverage of emergency awards.
