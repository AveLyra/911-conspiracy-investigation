## Observations and preserved discrepancies

Page 6 shows a July 31, 2002 United letter reporting three calls at **08:52 / 75 seconds**, **08:56 / 31 seconds**, and **08:57 / 267 seconds**; it says the first two connected and the third did not. The writer reports only one conversation could be substantiated and describes a possible confusion with Flight 93 as a hypothesis. Page 7's email describes three Flight 175 times **08:52:01, 08:56:19, 08:57:28 Eastern** and says the last did not connect. These closely correspond to the earlier third-party-image review; this is another copy of that source lineage, not an independent corroboration.

For page 7, the PDF embedded text extraction displays **Flight 176**, while the visually inspected page image displays **Flight 175**. Preserve both outputs: text extraction is a derivative of the scan, not a separate historical claim. The unrelated five-call list, different aircraft, and call-attempt versus connected/conversation distinctions remain as described in the prior review. The 75/31-second descriptions and separate carrier-summary fields are not adjudicated by this copy.
