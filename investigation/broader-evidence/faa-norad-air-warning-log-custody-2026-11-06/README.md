# Two archival routes to the NORAD Air Warning Center log

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD01–PD04. Declassified Commission notes identify a handwritten **Air Warning Log** at pages **5064–67**. A separate preliminary staff memorandum attributes entries at **1026** and **1057 EDT** to an **Air Warning Center Log**. These records give a second retrieval route alongside the previously indexed **NCT0011172–NCT0011183** candidate for NORAD's written-answer attachment. The underlying log and the July 14, 2003 cover memo remain unacquired. [Commission notes, p. 3](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-042-doc10.pdf#page=3) · [Preliminary memorandum, p. 5](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-042-doc24.pdf#page=5) · [Earlier written-answer study](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/3e24ed125aaf23487b9ac7a4427126bfdd79b468/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-dod-hearing-source-request-2026-11-06/README.md)

## Preserve the attributed times and versions

NORAD's Answer 3 attributes a vice-presidential authorization to **1041 EDT** and a DEFCON 3 declaration to the NORAD commander at **1046 EDT**. The preliminary memo says **1012 EDT** for verbal authority reaching JCS, describes **1041** as a discussion of a DEFCON recommendation, and attributes the **1046** declaration to the **VCJCS** from the NMCC chronology. Its separate **1057** log citation describes the CJCS direction and NORAD review. These source descriptions may track different actors and stages. The sources leave the exact sequence, forwarding and acknowledgment unresolved. The **1402 EDT** citation in Answer 5 remains separate from these earlier authority and status entries. [NORAD answer, pp. 1–2](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-047-doc5.pdf#page=1) · [Preliminary memorandum, pp. 1 and 5](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-042-doc24.pdf#page=5)

The memo cites **Appendix 13 to Annex C of CINCNORAD CONPLAN 3310-96**, **as amended on 24 September 1999**. The written answer calls its Attachment 1 **CONPLAN 3310-96 change 2 Appendix 13**. The DoD index gives **NCT0011166–NCT0011171** for an Appendix 13 entry and **NCT0010924–NCT0011084** for **CJCSI 3121.01A**, a separately cited document. The memo labels **NCT0010924** as a DoD delivery number for the latter instruction. The Appendix 13 wording, cover, change register and stamped pages are needed to test whether the references identify one edition, a changed edition or different copies. The preliminary memo's visible excerpts leave portions redacted and do not supply the full Appendix 13. [Preliminary memorandum, pp. 1–3](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-042-doc24.pdf#page=2) · [NORAD answer, attachment list](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-047-doc5.pdf#page=2) · [DoD index reproduction](https://www.scribd.com/document/15556058/DM-B3-DOD-2-of-2-Fdr-All-Document-Indexes-in-File-272)
