# DOT correspondence-control forms and the FAA correction trail

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD01–PD05 · Historical routing records

The 1993 DOT Correspondence Handbook identifies **DOT F 1320.65** or an automated grid, **Executive Secretariat transmittal forms**, and a **signed, dated return copy** for a direct-reply assignment. These are specific records to look for alongside a corrected answer or signed administrative decision. The August 31, 2006 OIG memorandum names **The Acting Secretary** and the **Federal Aviation Administrator** on its first page. This gives a basis to check both the DOT **S-10** and FAA **AOA-3** routes. The case-specific control entry and routing category remain open. [Handbook, physical pp. 11 and 32](https://www.governmentattic.org/12docs/DOTstyleManual_1993.pdf#page=11) · [OIG memorandum, p. 1](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=1) · [Prior FAA AOA-3 research](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/2b030c70ecb95e0929661ba04df3e039b4db6942/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-aoa3-correspondence-control-2026-11-06/README.md)

## Open discriminating records

1. **PD01–PD02.** Check the S-10 and AOA-3 incoming assignments using both report and investigation IDs, date, subject and addressee. Seek the assignment slip or its electronic counterpart, signed or cleared decision, file-copy grid, official outgoing answer and independent receipt. A retained original response should be compared against the 2003 answer and the August 2006 recommendation.
2. **PD03–PD04.** Join any correspondence closure to recommendation-specific OIG tracking and the signed decision on consideration or action. Seek the case route, operative controlled-item instructions and any implementation/closure evidence for recommendations 1 and 2. The report separately records recommendations 3 and 4. [OIG recommendations, pp. 12–13](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=12)
3. **PD04–PD05.** Check the FAA supplement, archived or successor electronic grid, records of migration and coverage, and the unacquired DOT 2050.3A. If a document went for appropriate handling, trace the actual action-office copy and any return correspondence as well as the Secretariat index.

The 73-page handbook packet was preserved and hashed. The review used its text layer for navigation and visually checked the cited forms, reproduced order and selected procedure pages. The findings identify search fields and expected record types. The corrected QFR, actual decision, recipient acknowledgment and implementation history remain unresolved.
