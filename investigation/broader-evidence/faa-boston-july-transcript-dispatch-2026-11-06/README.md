November 6, 2026 · **B129 / PD07**, with **PD01–PD04** open

Together they document a Boston Center-to-FAA headquarters route. The surviving documents do not identify this as the earlier **July 5** or **July 9** response referenced by the later FAA supplement. ## Another version difference in the September release

The **September 29** memo **ZBW-63 / NAID 34915875**, p. 1, reports a **September 24, 2003** release at Boston Center. It lists two September 2001 bulletins, a flight-path map and high- and low-altitude sector charts described as in use on **September 11, 2001**. Both chart pages in the released scan, physical pp. **2–3**, show a **June 15, 2002** revision mark. Preserve the historical-use description and later revision mark. The 2001 editions and any change history are needed to determine whether the later pages reproduce the earlier configuration. The September 24 release statement concerns this list. It does not settle delivery of the full American 11 accident file promised in the September 26 cover.
