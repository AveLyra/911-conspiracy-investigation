## What this adds

The reply points to a Commission-side incoming letter and a specific records-access arrangement before the DOT case-opening entry. The two inspectors general’s offices may have retained separate outgoing or coordination copies. The reply’s stated plan and the later case-open status do not establish what either office received from NARA, whether the two packages were identical, or when the individual enclosures arrived. The two reader copies repeat portions of the same underlying referral. Their agreement is a source comparison, with no independent authentication of the reproduced signatures or provenance.

The [NARA series description](https://www.archives.gov/research/9-11/commission-series.html) links Marcus’s subject files as [NAID 2538480](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/2538480). The [finding aid, physical p. 9, extracted line 16](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/9-11/finding-aid.pdf#page=9) lists **Referral [NORAD/FAA Misstatements]** under **Box 6**. This is a location to check for the two letters and an access record. The folder contents were unacquired. The six listed enclosure categories do not separately name the May 2003 written answer. Whether a copy traveled with a listed enclosure or another delivery remains open.
