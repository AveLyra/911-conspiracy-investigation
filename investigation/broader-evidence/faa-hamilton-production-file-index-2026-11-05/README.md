## November 17 response

The **May 4, 2004** DOT OIG letter **CC-2004-003**, p. 2, says FAA responded to the subpoena on **November 17, 2003**, detailing prior production and transmitting more material. OIG attributes **over 6,000 documents and other materials** before the subpoena to that response. AWA-754 instead describes **over 6,000 documents** during the summer. Preserve the wording and periods separately. The OIG cites FAA's response for the count, so this does not independently verify it or establish that AWA-754 is the November 17 letter. The signed response, enclosures and receipt remain unacquired. [OIG p. 2](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2004003.pdf#page=2) · [AWA-754 p. 1](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7602277)

OIG reports it found no indication of intentional withholding and attributes production concerns to miscommunications and misunderstandings. It also says further Commission contact was needed to finish the responsiveness inquiry and that its results would be reported separately after coordination. Preserve this contemporaneous qualification alongside the Commission's October account of serious deficiencies and the later FAQ's account of resolution. The letter's primary findings concern a separate New York Center controller recollection cassette. [OIG pp. 1–2](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2004003.pdf#page=2) · [Commission statement](https://govinfo.library.unt.edu/911/press/pr_2003-10-15.pdf) · [Commission FAQ](https://www.9-11commission.gov/about/faq.htm)
