# Fresh Kills steel accounting and a Municipal Archives accession

November 5, 2026. This review follows one municipal route within the steel preservation and custody track. It compares the reported Fresh Kills debris handling and accounting with a catalog entry for a specific steel object. It keeps the receiving-site scope separate from all World Trade Center salvage routes and from engineering specimen inventories.

## Observations and limits

| Record and locator | What the source reports | What the record leaves open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DSNY 2001 annual report, printed p. 9, PDF text lines 227–237 | By December 31, 2001, Fresh Kills had received **795,762.5 tons of debris** and **119,508.1 tons of steel** had been recycled. The section says “All of the steel that was sent to the landfill, structural or light, was recycled.” It describes a manual search area for steel and large debris and names DSNY Planning and Budget as a source of FEMA reimbursement data. | The year-end figures are an interim snapshot. The report gives neither an item-level disposition ledger nor a count of pieces examined for engineering purposes. |
| DSNY 2002–2003 annual report, printed p. 15, PDF text lines 464–474, 482–483 | **1,460,888 tons** received, **185,727 tons** of steel shipped to Hugo-Nue Schnitzer, and **1,275,171 tons** landfilled. The report separately describes memorial use of structural steel. It dates the formal closing ceremony to **July 15, 2002**, the last steel load and project close to **August 21, 2002**, and refers to an “official end to the WTC operation in July 2002” under Landfill Closure. | No reconciliation schedule or field definitions accompany these totals on the reviewed page. The July and August descriptions are preserved under their own headings and event labels. |
| NYC Municipal Archives, Collection Guides, accession **ACC-2002-065**, record group **RG098**, Description and Dates | The catalog describes an **18 × 22 inch, 75-pound** steel fragment selected following a Community Assistance Unit-arranged visit to Fresh Kills in **July 2002**. The title describes salvage **in 2001**, and the Dates field says **2001 September 11**. The entry is marked unprocessed. | The date field, title and narrative are retained separately. The reviewed catalog view supplies no signed transfer, receipt date, piece marking, current custody confirmation, original building attribution or match to a FEMA or NIST item. |

The two reported 2002–2003 categories sum to **1,460,898 tons**, **10 tons above** the reported inbound total, if treated as mutually exclusive and measured on the same basis. The arithmetic is reproducible from the displayed figures. Whether the categories use the same time window, scale basis or boundaries, and whether the printed values were amended, remain open. The residual does not identify any missing shipment or specimen. The separately stated vehicle counts, 14 returned, 17 placed in museums and 1,389 sent for destruction, sum to the reported 1,420 vehicles. No vehicle weights were assigned to the tonnage lines.

The 2001 report's all-recycled wording sits alongside the later memorial-use description and the steel selection recorded by the Archives. The July selection follows the December 2001 cutoff. The inclusion of the accession or memorial pieces in either DSNY accounting category, and their ultimate route, are unverified. The accession provides a specific route to seek an underlying record and physical marks before attempting a building match.
