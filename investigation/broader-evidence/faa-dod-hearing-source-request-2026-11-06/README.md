# The Commission's June 2003 NORAD questions and the released answer

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD01–PD04. The Commission's **June 4, 2003** question letter, preserved in a public Team 7 reader, asks NORAD for written answers by **June 18** about testimony at the **May 23** hearing. An official National Archives release, **ISCAP 2012-047-doc5**, contains seven matching questions with NORAD's answers. It preserves a written version of the **0924 EDT** American 77 claim and names two attachments. Both attachments are absent from the two-page release. [Commission question letter reproduction](https://www.scribd.com/document/16762319/T7-B21-Team-7-Briefing-Doc-Requests-Fdr-Doc-Req-of-Interest-to-Team-7-Tab-Entire-Contents-257) · [NORAD answer, pp. 1–2](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-047-doc5.pdf)

## Question, answer and later account

| Subject | Preserved record |
| --- | --- |
| **Open line, Q1/A1** | The Commission asks which lines existed, when, for how long and whether NORAD knows of recordings or transcripts. NORAD describes **0840 EDT** as an FAA call about American 11, followed by separate calls and an open FAA–NEADS loop **after 0924 EDT**. It says the exact time was undocumented and reports no awareness of recordings of those conversations. It gives no loop duration. Scope matters because the earlier FAA inquiry concerned when its headquarters liaison joined a bridge. The released NORAD answer identifies FAA and NEADS in general and leaves the liaison's presence, the line identity and overlap with other bridges open. [NORAD answer, p. 1](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-047-doc5.pdf#page=1) · [DOT OIG, pp. 9–10](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=9) |
| **Flight 77, Q2/A2** | The question says **United Airlines Flight 77** and asks what was known before a reported **9:24 am** FAA notification. NORAD explicitly substitutes **American Airlines** and retains **0924 EDT** as the first documented reference to the flight as a possible hijack. The Commission's final report attributes the **9:24** notification to a report that **American 11** was heading toward Washington and describes **9:34** as notice that **American 77 was lost**. These are distinct written accounts. The underlying source reference for NORAD's answer is unnamed in A2. [NORAD answer, p. 1](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-047-doc5.pdf#page=1) · [Commission final report, ch. 1, pp. 34–35](https://www.9-11commission.gov/report/911Report_Ch1.htm) |
| **Rules and log, A3/A5** | The written answer discusses a **1041 EDT** authorization and **1046 EDT** DEFCON 3 in A3. A5 separately reports **1402 EDT** for a Washington-bound weapons-free authorization and cites the Air Warning Center log. The attachment list literally dates that log **11 Sep 03**, while the questions concern **9/11/01**. The date label is visible on the page. The missing log is needed to determine what it dates and what order, audience and clock the entry describes. [NORAD answer, pp. 1–2](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-047-doc5.pdf#page=2) |

The request and answer correspond by the seven question numbers and subjects in the same order. A hearing date, letter date, requested production date, index date and actual receipt are different events. NARA's detailed decision table gives **2003/05/23** in the document-date field. Its grouped release list gives **2004** for appeal documents **2–5**, and the answer says it concerns the **23 May 03** testimony. Neither catalog field establishes when the answer was drafted or delivered. Preserve the **June 4** letter, **June 18** deadline and **17-Jul-03** index date separately. ## Attachment and identifier evidence

- **Two specific adjacent index entries.** The Commission DoD index reproduction lists **NCT0011166–NCT0011171** for Appendix 13 to Annex C of CINCNORAD CONPLAN 3310-96 and **NCT0011172–NCT0011183** for the Air Warning Center Log. Both show **17-Jul-03** in the rightmost field. The answer calls the former **Attachment 1**, with **change 2**, and the latter **Attachment 2**, with **11 Sep 03**. The index entries are strong retrieval candidates by title and adjacency. Exact enclosure identity and delivery require the underlying pages and transmittal. [DoD index reproduction, lines 1980–1988](https://www.scribd.com/document/15556058/DM-B3-DOD-2-of-2-Fdr-All-Document-Indexes-in-File-272) · [NORAD answer, attachment list](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-047-doc5.pdf#page=2)
- **Bates and handwritten RDOD readings.** The answer's partly clipped page stamps read **NCT0011184** and **NCT0011185** on inspection. The public index text gives **NCT00011184–NCT0011185** for NORAD QFRs. Retain both readings pending the native index face. The answer's handwritten top-right identifier appears to read **RDOD 03002461**. A release-register reproduction assigns that same identifier to **Joint Staff personnel on duty** with Bates **011186–188**. The DoD index independently places the Joint Staff roster immediately after the QFR at **NCT0011186–NCT0011188**. Preserve the handwriting and both index descriptions. Mislabeling, a reading error, or a grouping convention remain untested. [NORAD answer, p. 1](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-047-doc5.pdf#page=1) · [DoD index reproduction](https://www.scribd.com/document/15556058/DM-B3-DOD-2-of-2-Fdr-All-Document-Indexes-in-File-272) · [Release register, line 255](https://www.scribd.com/document/78046326/Releases-Spreadsheet-2012-01-04)
