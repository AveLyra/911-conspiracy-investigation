## Keep the editions and guide records separate

- The December 2003 face names **President's Council on Integrity and Efficiency** and **Executive Council on Integrity and Efficiency**. The November 2011 edition says it supersedes the December 2003 PCIE/ECIE standards. The later Appendix D-1 describes the standards as **“adopted by the CIGIE in December 2003.”** Preserve those statements and the separate **PCIE** and **CIGIE** attributions in the FY 2009 and FY 2010 DOT budget materials. The adoption record and the December 2006 review's actual authority citation would test the relationship. [2003 cover](https://oig.treasury.gov/system/files/Documents/Inspector%20General%20Deskbook%20Volume%204%20%282%20of%203%29.pdf#page=52) · [2011 preface, physical p. 5](https://www.ignet.gov/sites/default/files/files/invprg1211appi.pdf#page=5) · [D-1, physical p. 1, lines 6–7](https://www.ignet.gov/sites/default/files/files/committees/investigation/invprg1211appd1.pdf)
- The 2003 file provision says **“established immediately upon”** and lists **“File access record.”** The 2011 file provision says **“should be established upon”** and lists **“Access control of the files.”** The later edition also includes electronic file controls and adds calculated savings, if applicable, to the results examples. Both versions contain the same case-initiation sentence about documenting a decision to refer or take no action, verified after joining whitespace. This is a scoped comparison. DOT's operative definitions and application remain open. The reprinted **GAO-02-437** describes a draft under field testing and gives **April 30, 2002** as the scheduled pilot end. GAO also says the draft recommended sampling closed investigative files. These are a guide citation and a draft/pilot account. The 2002 guide, FY 2006 revision and operative sample criteria remain unacquired.
