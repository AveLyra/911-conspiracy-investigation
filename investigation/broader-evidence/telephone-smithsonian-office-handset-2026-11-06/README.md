# Flight 77 receiving-office handset and museum custody

November 6, 2026 UTC · TEL03–TEL05 / AUD016-A1. The Smithsonian's catalog supplies a physical-object lead for the receiving-office side of the Flight 77 call review. The museum's 2003 project chronology gives separate dates for an agreement to give the telephone and a reported pickup. These records give specific identifiers and dates to test against the actual accession packet and Department of Justice equipment records. The catalog attributes **two calls** to this office telephone. The [Commission staff report, note 265](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/9-11/staff-report-sept2005.pdf#page=94) preserves **four connected calls to unknown numbers** and says the available records could not identify which rows represented the two reported connections. Note 266 distinguishes a reported **operator-assisted collect** call from a later **direct** call. The [earlier call-event review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/c765a0a11042c7609415d09eb43c9dc1cf982d74/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-aa77-event-census-2026-11-02/README.md) preserves these accounts alongside the alternative collect-call description and the zero-duration record. The museum's object description supplies no carrier identifiers, call timings or evidence of the transfer path. Its basis may overlap the witness accounts. Retain the different event descriptions and leave the row-to-conversation assignments open.

The export row has a CC0 metadata flag and no embedded `online_media` key in its `descriptiveNonRepeating` block. The indexed Smithsonian object page describes an image with **“Usage Conditions Apply.”** These are different representations. The image and hardware label were outside the scope of this review. The public catalog date, field values and accession number cannot supply the exact model, serial or a historical extension assignment.
