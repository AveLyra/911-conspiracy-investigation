## What the new record changes

The March report describes **blind billing** and different routes for billing through merchants, a card issuer or a long-distance provider. That adds a reason to seek billing confirmations separately from the carrier's call export. The statement that a sheet included all calls, the later account of an identifier correction and the reported seconds unit address different attributes. Preserve them together. They leave open the actual values before and after export, whether the error affected any call or person match, and the field's start and stop events. No change in a duration, time stamp or call count was verified.
