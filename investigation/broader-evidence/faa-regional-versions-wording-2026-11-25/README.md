# FAA regional chronology: 1:53 entry varies between versions

Reviewed 2026-11-25. This is an additive comparison to the [regional chronology study](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/7f86390eb464f3f5cb82fb386c64ceabb00e4004/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-regional-intranet-custody-2026-11-25/README.md). The two scans retain conflicting wording; their compilation and transmission histories are not established.

## Observed wording

| Time label | NARA AEA-2879, PDF page 4 | Commission-folder chronology, PDF page 9 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1:13 PM | “AAL11 voice recordings posted on FAA intranet – AEA-505 setting up recordings. No information on the website – reference tapes.” | “AA11 voice tape on FAA intranet – AEA-505 setting up tapes. No information on the website – reference tapes.” |
| 1:53 PM | “No data to be released even internally per Canoles” | “No data released even internally per Canoles” |
| 2:26 PM | “FBI requests tapes will be confiscated AEA-500 advised S. Carper not to release tapes.” | “FBI requests tapes will be confiscated advised S. Carper not to release tapes.” |

The 1:53 formulations differ: one reads as a direction and the other as a description of release. Neither establishes the nature, exact scope or execution of a restriction. The 2:26 formulations differ in whether AEA-500 is named before the advice. Preserve both rather than assigning the advice to a particular person from the shorter entry. The 1:13 variants also differ in describing a voice tape versus voice recordings and setting up tapes versus recordings; neither identifies the content.

Both page images contain the 2:55 PM instruction “Upload data on AAT site per Canoles – keep originals in facility.” This is a reported instruction, not proof that an upload or preservation took place. The closely matching sequences are potentially related copies, not independent corroboration. The Commission-folder page bears a fax header showing **SEP. 11 2001 5:53 PM**; a header is not proof of the entry's creation time, actual transmission, receipt, authorship or authenticity. No chronological precedence or explanation for the variants is established.

## Open tests and scope

Obtain the native regional chronology and revision history, the fax transmission records and recipients, originals of the instructions and related server/file inventories, backups, recordings and custody/transfer records. Compare exact file identities and versions before concluding whether events refer to the same material. Harmless transcription or editing changes, different sources, and other explanations remain untested. Neither record by itself establishes concealment, destruction, motive or a specific passenger recording. The FAA corrected answer, delivery and signed administrative disposition remain separate TODOs.
