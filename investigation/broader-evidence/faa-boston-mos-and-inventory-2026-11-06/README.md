## What the September 19 record contains

The fax cover is dated **September 19, 2001**, identifies **ZBW MOS**, and says **9 pages including the cover**. The next page reproduces a **2:02 p.m.** email requesting a receipt. The three-page public object ends with a timing sheet. The sheet says the intervening intervals are **“dead space on the rerecording”** and bears a handwritten statement that wave files were forwarded to **ANE-505** on **9/19/01**. No separate receipt appears in the scanned object. The fax count describes an original transmission and the page count describes this release. They cannot show how many pages reached the recipient or why the released scan is shorter. [Fax p. 1](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/3-AAL-11/e-Other/3-ZBW-30-20-Fax-cover-9-19-01-partial-transcripts-REDACT-SEALED.pdf#page=1) · [Email p. 2](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/3-AAL-11/e-Other/3-ZBW-30-20-Fax-cover-9-19-01-partial-transcripts-REDACT-SEALED.pdf#page=2) · [Timing sheet p. 3](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/3-AAL-11/e-Other/3-ZBW-30-20-Fax-cover-9-19-01-partial-transcripts-REDACT-SEALED.pdf#page=3)
