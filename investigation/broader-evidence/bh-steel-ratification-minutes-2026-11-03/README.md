# Beverly Hills recorded approval of the WTC steel transfer

**November 3, 2026.** The restored share made the preserved September 8, 2011 council minutes readable. They record adoption of the steel-transfer ratification as **resolution 11-R-12833**. The related consent-calendar vote lists **five ayes and no noes**. This supplies a decision record missing from our earlier agenda-packet review. Physical delivery, specimen identification and testing remain unverified.

Research identifiers: STL01–STL04; AUD011, AUD016; B188, H005; T01–T07; BH-M01–BH-M04. This is a public-source research update, not a court filing or a scientific test.

## What the finding changes

**BH-M01 is complete to its bounded reading scope:** the source is accessible, matches its acquisition hash, and the pertinent motion, item, vote and approval block have been inspected. **BH-M02 advances partially:** we now have recorded adoption and a resolution number. The actual signed resolution, certification, approved attachments and version history remain unacquired.

Recorded ratification is evidence against a blanket assertion that this transfer had no documented council authorization. It does not prove that all preservation decisions were sound, every condition was implemented, or other transfers were properly authorized. Agenda, minutes, agreement and copied order are related chain records, not independent measurements of the steel's origin or condition.

Nothing read here joins the municipal artifact to **W14A/W14B, C12a/b, FEMA Sample 1**, a retained NIST specimen, a particular tower or WTC 7. Nor does it establish dispatch, delivery, condition, later alteration, retained coupons or a completed test. The earlier copied court-order testing provision remains separate from evidence of its implementation or present legal enforceability. It is not treated as a testing right for every public requester.
