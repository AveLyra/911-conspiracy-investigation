# W14A, W14B and C12 photographs: source and marking cross-check

November 25, 2026 · AUD011 / AUD016. This publishes a qualified derivative of the November 19 research photo review, which remains preserved locally. The previously published report concerned the field dimensions; it did not contain this photo inspection.

## What was checked

A prior local review recorded visual inspection of 17 images from a third-party archive of NIST Release 32. On November 25, all 17 retained files were rehashed and their lengths checked against their local receipts: **17 of 17 matched**. This is file integrity against those receipts, not authentication against camera originals. In this verification, only representative W14A photo 69, W14B photo 63, and C12 photo 40 were visually rechecked.

The representative images visibly show a painted **A**, a painted **B**, and a **C12** marking, respectively. Those markings are consistent with their catalog labels but cannot establish an item’s unique identity, date, custody, building or floor. The W14A image shows a long bent piece and standing water; these visual features do not establish how or when it deformed or corroded. The C12 image cannot by itself assign the photographed piece to C12a or C12b. No person in the images is identified here.

## Conflicting attribution records

The [FEMA Appendix D field table, rows 20–21](https://www.fema.gov/pdf/library/fema403_apd.pdf#page=16), identifies A/W14A and B/W14B with a WTC 7 description. The [NIST inventory, Table A-7](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-3b.pdf#page=96), places W-14A or A and W-14B under unidentified wide-flange sections. Both descriptions remain unresolved. The photographs do not select an origin or prove either record wrong.

The earlier [dimension comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/5317f50f/investigation/broader-evidence/steel-w14a-section-dimension-screen-2026-11-06/README.md) preserves field dimensions, section-table limits and the same attribution conflict. These photographs and the field photo references may have common provenance and are not independent corroboration of origin.
