## Assessment and discriminating evidence

The 2003 and 2008 passages use closely related project descriptions; their documentary relationship is unverified and they must not be counted as independent evidence of a specimen or test. The publication abstract's wording appears to assert analysis of WTC 7 steel, while the 2005 report explicitly describes the WTC 7 properties as literature-based and the FAQ discusses failure to clearly identify recovered steel. This discrepancy is **unresolved**. The entry could describe intended project scope, include an imprecise summary, or refer to material not identified in the text reviewed; these are untested alternatives. The 2008 entry does not identify a sample or an actual test. The comparison does not establish that NIST tested a WTC 7 specimen, that it did not test one outside the cited report, or that anyone knowingly misstated the record.
