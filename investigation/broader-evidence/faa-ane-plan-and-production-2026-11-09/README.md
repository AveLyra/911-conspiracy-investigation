# FAA New England contingency plan, draft chronology and October collection memo

**November 9, 2026 | B129 / PD03–PD04 / PD07.** Three official FAA scans were recovered from the National Archives. The October 8, 2003 directive expressly requests draft and final after-action evaluations. The New England fax instance identifies a specific route to look for the Eastern Region equipment assessment and related material. It records the collection instruction. The individual evaluation, headquarters intake and Commission receipt remain to be acquired.

## October 8 directive and fax instances

**ANE-2**, NAID 7601599, is a three-page scan of the October 8, 2003 *Presidential 9/11 Commission Follow Up* action memo. The page faces identify the Director of Air Traffic and the Eastern, New England and Great Lakes regional divisions among the addressees. Item 1 calls for copying complete accident files. Item 2 includes after-action material, quality assurance material, email and personal notes and quotes a Commission request spanning draft and final evaluations. Its continuation names internal FAA communications. Item 3 requests employee-contact certification for a full day. Item 5 separately specifies a rerecording request for TMU and other identified landlines and hotlines, with the printed window **0800–1400 EST**. The request gives separate internal routing for the main collection and those rerecordings. Keep the employee population, tape window and after-action-document scope separate.
