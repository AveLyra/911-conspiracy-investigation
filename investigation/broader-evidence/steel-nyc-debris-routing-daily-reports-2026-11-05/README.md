# October 29–31 debris routing in New York City’s daily reports

November 5, 2026. **STL01 / STL02** follow-up to the [Fresh Kills screening and load records review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/699ac9c0a038b6bd4ed0515ac2898f2676af8712/investigation/broader-evidence/steel-fresh-kills-screening-and-load-records-2026-11-05/README.md). Two PDFs from the City's September 11 Document Portal preserve three dated Department of Environmental Protection daily reports. They describe a specific debris pile, its reported origin and the later routing direction. They give a route to the actual dispatch and receiving records.

## Three daily records

| Report date and page | Wording and observations |
| --- | --- |
| **October 29, 2001**, `NYC-WTC_000154497`, physical p. 2 of the first PDF | Under **Debris moved by NYC DOT**, the memo attributes to OEM a request for testing/disposal guidance for material moved from a park within Battery Park City to the ball field at **West Street and Chambers Street**. It reports **about 600 tons** of collapse debris and states **“OEM states that FBI does not need this debris.”** The visible word is *tons*. The PDF text layer gives *toms*. [City scan, p. 2](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000154496.pdf#page=2) |
| **October 30, 2001**, `NYC-WTC_000154496`, physical p. 1 | The earlier origin, ball field, approximate tonnage and FBI wording recur. An update places the proposed multiagency site visit at **West and Warren Streets**, **11:30 on October 31**. Keep the two intersections with their respective labels. The same page separately reports that the contractor at **7 WTC** said most debris there had been removed and the site was at grade level. The memo has no explicit join between that item and the ball-field pile. [City scan, p. 1](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000154496.pdf#page=1) |
| **October 31, 2001**, `NYC-WTC_000154512`, physical p. 1 of the second PDF | Under the same site-visit heading, the update says **“FBI directed material to be brought to Fresh Kills landfill.”** The older FBI-not-needed sentence is visibly crossed out in the carried-forward paragraph. The PDF text layer is garbled across the line. The source shows a direction attributed to the FBI and a visible strike-through. It leaves the author and time of the mark and the basis for the direction unknown. [City scan, p. 1](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000154512.pdf#page=1) |

The matching heading, locations and repeated wording support treating the three reports as a candidate sequence about the same pile. The memos preserve different accounts of what the FBI needed or directed. A need for the material and a direction about its destination may also address different questions. The underlying communications would distinguish a changed decision, a clarification, or different meanings of those statements. No dispatch ticket, scale weight, receipt, engineering inspection or particular building of origin was verified. The consecutive memos are related reports and may reuse text.
