# Flight 93 telephone recording leads in FBI serials 1123, 11721 and 11722

November 9, 2026 · TEL01–TEL04 / AUD016-A1 / TC05–TC06. A Flight 93 interview scan reports a possible recording of the **Glick conversation**. The Commission's working memo says **“No tape indicated”** for that call and separately seeks a recording of the call to **911**. The memo cites **11722**. The recording account appears in **11721**. The two reports sit two physical pages apart in the same Box 17 packet. A third interview, **1123**, supplies the secondhand Gronlund account cited by the same memo.
