## Observations

The January 2009 [Commission archive ZIP](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip) contains `NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009/911 NARA/Team 7 Files/Team 7 Box 13/T7 B13 DOJ Doc Req 35-13 Packet 8 Fdr- Entire Contents.pdf`. On physical PDF page 5, footer `000000594`, a St. Louis report visually says Peter and Sue Hanson were on “United Airlines Flight #11”. This is a multistep account relayed through other people; its label is a report observation and does not establish aircraft identity. It separately describes a call from an airborne aircraft. Redacted identities were not reconstructed.

On physical page 8, footer `000000597`, a separate report says Peter Hanson was on “United Airline Flight 175” and describes an earlier telephone interview, a subsequent in-person interview and the planned placement of an agent's telephone-interview notes in the 1A section. This is not proof that the notes survive or were later accessed. The two descriptions, sources and contexts remain side by side. The cause of the different flight labels is unknown; error, confusion or other explanations are untested. Neither discrepancy establishes fabrication or intent. Separately preserved early and later accounts remain unchanged.

## Method and remaining work

The previously acquired 60-page packet was rehashed to SHA-256 `1eb792c87940c09acb693de0396f74b00a77cf8f758bac6193083cad9778aae6`. Physical PDF pages 5 and 8 were rendered at 1.5× with PyMuPDF and visually inspected November 25, 2026. Only these two pages were checked in this step; this does not authenticate the original report, establish the actual call, or independently corroborate aircraft identity. The 2,343,595-byte packet is a Commission working-file reproduction; no raw page, personal data, contact information or concealed text is republished.
