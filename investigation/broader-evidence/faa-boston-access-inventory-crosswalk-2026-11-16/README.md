## Observations

The earlier inventory says it responds to a November 5, 2002 headquarters request and lists materials held by Boston ARTCC concerning AAL11; its own date field is blank. Its item 34 is an “Information Access Sheet, dated January 31, 2002.” The September 25, 2003 memorandum describes material “contained in the Accident File held by Boston ARTCC” and lists an identically described and dated sheet as item 32. These are two references, not two independently verified sheets. The sheet itself was not located in bounded searches; neither reference shows its contents or a particular access decision.

The earlier inventory explicitly lists an accident package and facility master tapes as items 1–2. The 2003 list begins with radar records and does not separately list those two entries. The two introductions describe their scopes differently; their relationship, completeness and reason for the difference remain unresolved. No disappearance, disposal, transfer or motive can be inferred from this comparison. The 2003 inventory also lists 2003 Commission-related and other communications that were not in the earlier list.
