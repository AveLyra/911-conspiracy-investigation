The draft also gives a relative first-office-call description of **several minutes after** the second World Trade Center crash, for which it prints **9:02:40 a.m.**, and describes an office transfer. Its separate **9:19** paragraph supplies no caller identity and makes no explicit join to the first or second office call in the text recovered here. The final report puts the first call between **9:16 and 9:26**, and note 57 says the available records do not identify which of the four connected-to-unknown-number entries correspond to the two conversations. Preserve the draft and final time descriptions, and the separate Keyton and Voss direct-versus-collect observations, as distinct source records. A label test shows **09:18:58** is two seconds before **09:19:00**. Truncating to the minute gives **09:18** and rounding to the nearest minute gives **09:19**. This is a reproducible test of possible label conventions. Neither convention is documented as the rule the analysts used, and neither can establish a common event or explain the connection classifications. The working draft and the FBI report it invokes cannot be counted as independent corroboration of a connection.
