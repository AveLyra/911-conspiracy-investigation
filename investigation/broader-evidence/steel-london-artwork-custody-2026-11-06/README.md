## Descriptions to preserve side by side

- **Dimensions.** The Potters Fields proposal gives **5.0 m to 8.5 m** for individual columns, the LLDC report says **“30 foot tall pieces”**, and the recipient gives **28 feet** for the artwork. They concern differently described objects or design stages. Preserve all three without choosing a measurement or assuming what endpoint the February report used. The original member measurements and an as-built drawing would test whether any two statements concern the same dimension. [Southwark ¶8](https://moderngov.southwark.gov.uk/documents/s15022/Report.pdf#page=2) · [LLDC §4.1](https://www.queenelizabetholympicpark.co.uk/sites/default/files/attachments/27%20February%202014%20Agenda%20and%20papers_0.pdf#page=138) · [Charity artwork page](https://since911.com/about-us/wtc-artwork/since-9-11/)
- **Siting terms.** The charity says the gift was conditional on the artwork being **“permanently sited in a prominent London location”**. The 2014 committee report records the lease-period limit and DD2753 §4.3 describes permission as **“temporary”**. These are statements about gift terms and planning permission, respectively. How the actual agreements relate remains open. [Charity unveiling account](https://since911.com/about-us/wtc-artwork/unveiling-of-the-artwork/) · [LLDC §3.1](https://www.queenelizabetholympicpark.co.uk/sites/default/files/attachments/23%20September%202014%20Agenda%20and%20papers.pdf#page=49) · [DD2753 §4.3](https://www.london.gov.uk/media/109870/download#page=3)
- **Planning dates.** Keep the **March 25, 2014** forecast, the **July 22, 2014** reported committee action, and the DD2753 §3.3 reference to **“initial planning consent in 2015”** as separate statements. The first is a schedule, the second a report of a resolution, and the third a retrospective description. Obtain the decision notice and dated condition documents to test the sequence. [February report §§2.1, 4.7](https://www.queenelizabetholympicpark.co.uk/sites/default/files/attachments/27%20February%202014%20Agenda%20and%20papers_0.pdf#page=137) · [September report §3.1](https://www.queenelizabetholympicpark.co.uk/sites/default/files/attachments/23%20September%202014%20Agenda%20and%20papers.pdf#page=49) · [DD2753 §3.3](https://www.london.gov.uk/media/109870/download#page=3)

## Deferred originals and discriminating checks

- [ ] **Identify the individual steel members.** Port Authority release agreement and schedules, Hangar 17 tag and dispatch entries, pre-shipment photographs, bill of lading, customs and recipient intake documents. Join stable marks and photographs across records before asserting the building, floor, or a NIST specimen link. The cited sources do not establish such a link.
- [ ] **Trace treatment and storage.** Recover fabrication drawings and shop work orders, the Battersea Park installation and removal receipts, Duxford and Ruislip intake/release records, and the Olympic Park as-built survey. Compare pre-treatment and post-treatment dimensions, member IDs, cuts, connections, finishes and handling dates.
- [ ] **Recover operative siting records.** Locate decision notices and approved drawings for **10-AP-3257** and **13/00558/FUL**, **ADD229**, the 2014 lease and executed 2024 counterpart with schedules, and the later decision notice described in DD2753. Compare the reported original gift condition, terms of occupation and actual decision dates without treating the different sources as interchangeable.
- [ ] **Trace inspections.** Use DD2753 §4.1 to look for LLDC’s dated structural inspection and condition reports, reference photos, repair or coating logs and successor custody records. Verify whether a check was performed and whether it identifies specific members. The decision describes the maintenance arrangement and supplies a locator for records of performance.
