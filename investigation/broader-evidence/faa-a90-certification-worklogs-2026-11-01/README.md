## Certification concerns asking employees, not certifying complete production

[A90-74 physical page 2](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialive/99/9114/34911499/content/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-e-other/5-A90-74-Facility-Certification-REDACT-SEALED.pdf#page=2), dated **October 15, 2003**, goes from Manager, Boston TRACON, A90-1 to Director of Air Traffic, AAT-1. It certifies that employees who were at the facility on September 11 had been asked for relevant documentation. The examples include government records, emails, radar/audiotapes and personal notes.

That is affirmative evidence of a reported collection inquiry. The actual sentence does **not** certify that every employee answered, that each answer was accurate, that all responsive records were received, or that the Commission received every item. Treating it as an independently verified completeness declaration would overstate it. Conversely, its limited wording is not evidence that someone refused or concealed records.

[Physical pages 3–4](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialive/99/9114/34911499/content/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-e-other/5-A90-74-Facility-Certification-REDACT-SEALED.pdf#page=3) attach a sign-on log with a printed window of **9/10/01 10:00:00 PM–9/11/01 10:00:00 PM** and headers **9/12/01 7:27:58 AM** and **7:28:00 AM**. [Physical pages 5–6](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialive/99/9114/34911499/content/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-e-other/5-A90-74-Facility-Certification-REDACT-SEALED.pdf#page=5) contain two presentations of questions covering records received/generated from **September 11 at midnight through 11:59 pm**. These are differently described scopes and clocks; do not silently convert one into the other, identify header meaning without system records, or declare the roster incomplete. The source of each list, timezone, scope instructions and response crosswalk remain needed.

The question sheet distinguishes **government records**, which employees are described as **required** to provide, from **personal notes**, which they are **strongly encouraged** to provide. The deadline blanks are unfilled in the inspected form. That distinction is a concrete completeness question: ask what records were classified in each category and what responses were retained. It is not proof personal notes existed or were intentionally excluded. The manager's certification includes personal notes among the requested material.

The fax cover on [physical page 1](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialive/99/9114/34911499/content/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-e-other/5-A90-74-Facility-Certification-REDACT-SEALED.pdf#page=1) is dated **10/15/03** and states **four pages including the cover**. Preserve both counts. The current eight-page archive object is not necessarily the original four-page fax job. This difference alone establishes neither a missing historical enclosure nor four additional records sent.

## The collection email separates cover documents from data and tracks staff time

[A90-98 page 1](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/e-Other/5-A90-98-Email-on-9-11-Commission-Data-Collection.pdf#page=1) is a printed email dated **10/10/2003 11:26 AM**, subject **Tapes, Memos, Certification Letters   Track time**. It asks for copies of the memo, index or letter as data are sent onward, and directs certifications to **AAT-20**, referring to a teleconference.

Preserve the exact sentence **“I/we do not what the data.”** It is printed that way. Reading what as want is plausible, but it has not been authenticated as an intended correction. The surrounding request clearly asks for copies of cover documents. That makes a separate administrative-document trail a useful retrieval target; it does not prove the sender prohibited preservation of data or that data were not delivered to another recipient.

The final paragraph requests weekly accounting of **time expended at each facility**, with an early-next-week/midday-Wednesday reporting reference. This is personnel-effort tracking, not a timeline of recorded September 11 events. The scan has no native mail headers, actual attachment identifiers or item-level delivery acknowledgment. Do not use its printed sent label alone to authenticate receipt or identify an entire downstream packet.

The [earlier preserved A90-71 memorandum](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/665d6502ab844e1eb15e83b6403205a6f896936a/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-a90-landline-delivery-2026-11-01/README.md) names **TMU: one tape; OSIC: two tapes; OS: no visible numeral**. ## Historical procedure excerpt supplies specific preservation and copying targets

[A90-99 physical pages 4–6](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialive/5/9115/34911505/content/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-e-other/5-A90-99-Reproducing-Documentation-Info-REDACT-SEALED.pdf#page=4) include an FAA **7210.3R** extract, with visible date **2/24/00**, section 8 and printed pages **4-8-1/4-8-2**. Page 4 has an overlaid preparation/review note and a presentation notice; page 5 is the alternate, unobscured presentation; page 6 continues the text. Both presentations stay preserved. No hidden text or redaction was recovered.

| Exact provision | What the excerpt describes | Limit on applying it here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4-8-1(a), page 5, upper left | Direct copying from an original with prior **ATD and AAT-20 approval**; qualified FAA employee retains custody and attends copying; continuous recording, minimizing original starts/stops to the maximum extent possible | Obtain the applicable version, approvals, request, copying method and original/copy identifiers before asserting that this provision governed a particular Commission derivative |
| 4-8-1(b), page 5, left middle | Certified rerecording of portions copied; facility retention under applicable criteria; original returned to service | A general provision is not a source-specific disposition record or proof any September 11 original was overwritten |
| 4-8-2, pages 5–6 | Preservation requests trigger removal from service and a warning label; written 20-day notice and a conditional additional 10-day notice before the described return-to-service outcome | Requires actual request, notices, approval/release, other holds and applicable accident-retention criteria; not an automatic 30-day destruction rule |
| 4-8-3, page 5, upper right | Printed reductions of computer data carry a disclaimer distinguishing internal processing from the exact control-position display | Need native data, configuration, processing and display reconstruction; a printed table alone cannot authenticate an exact controller-screen reproduction |
| 4-8-4, pages 5–6 | Separate copy/media/computer-cost provisions | Historical examples are not a current fee quote, Commission invoice or authority to incur fees |

The computer-data disclaimer reads: **“This document is derived from computer magnetic recordings of internal computer processing. It is not an exact representation of the control position display.”** The excerpt therefore supplies a specific reason to keep native data, processed output and a reconstructed display distinct. It does not establish an actual display error or deceptive reconstruction.

### Continuous direct copies beside disclosed skip-silence processing

The direct-copy provision's continuity language belongs beside A90-71's explicit disclosure that skip silence shortened the landline rerecordings. Those statements raise a **specific applicability and copy-generation question**, not an established violation. The rule text has a specified direct-request copying context; A90-71 reports a Commission-related output. The full applicable order and amendments, special instructions, approvals and identities of source/output copies remain unjoined. Do not erase either statement or assume the provision either necessarily applied or necessarily did not apply.

The strongest objection to an adverse reading is that a continuous preserved original and a shortened listening copy could serve different purposes under different authorized instructions. That remains untested; the excerpt does not prove such an arrangement existed. Obtain both generations and their instructions, compare surviving speech and time anchors, and report actual differences. No native audio comparison was possible or performed in this unit.
