# FAA position records: four time matches, distinct labels and unresolved clocks

Research date: November 1, 2026. Existing B129 / PD01–PD04 / FAA01–FAA04 record trail. This is one bounded inspection of held public primary records, not a new download, a whole-packet authentication or completion of the original-record work plan.

The located position report supplies all four preselected on/off intervals, and each agrees with the corresponding statement's time fields after removing the report's colon punctuation. This is useful for locating the underlying records. **It is not independent validation:** the statement forms direct writers to make their times correspond with SISO. One position label remains different, `RA42` versus `D42`; the report's timezone and historical clock accuracy are not established. Separate roster and overtime records also prevent treating an `RDO` entry as standalone evidence that a writer was absent.

A separate within-form discrepancy is preserved: two weather descriptions and their adjacent time boxes differ by four hours while both label their values UTC. The values' cause and effect remain untested. These findings do not establish coordinated falsification, foreknowledge, suppression or a cover-up.

| First report subsequent to accident | `0951 UTC` | `09/11/01 1351 UTC` | Adjacent minus body = 240 minutes |

Both pairs explicitly use UTC labels. The separate conditions-at-accident narrative also says `0851 UTC`, but it has no matching adjacent DATE/TIME box in this comparison; no third pair is manufactured. We preserve all printed values and do not choose a preferred one, relabel a value as local time, or apply a four-hour correction to the accident, SISO, radar or recorder timelines.

**Untested hypotheses:** a time-convention or export/editorial error, or another preparation error, could produce these labels. Their likelihood and specific cause are not established. The original weather product, form-preparation worksheets, native version history and applicable time instructions could discriminate. Technical weather decoding and comparison of the weather products elsewhere in the packet were not performed here. The disagreement warrants a source-quality check; by itself it shows neither a physical four-hour offset nor deliberate falsification.
