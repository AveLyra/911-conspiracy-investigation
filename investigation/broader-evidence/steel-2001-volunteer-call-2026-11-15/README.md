# 2001 engineering account identifies a volunteer-call record

**STL01–STL03.** The [December 2001 JOM account](https://www.tms.org/pubs/journals/JOM/0112/News/News4-0112.html), volume 53(12), pp. 4–7, reports that the Structural Engineers Association of New York called for volunteers to work at two New Jersey scrap yards receiving WTC steel. It says engineers looked particularly for steel from aircraft-impact and fire floors. It also reports a separate effort to collect photographs and observations. This is a contemporaneous journal account of a reported call, not the call notice, roster or recovery log. The source was read through a public web reader on 2026-11-15; a direct retrieval separately returned HTTP 403, so no source-byte hash or original HTML preservation is claimed.

**Different source scopes.** [NIST NCSTAR 1-3B](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-3b.pdf#page=31), September 2005, physical PDF p. 31, reports four major debris-receiving locations where volunteers worked during the cleanup and lists several sought categories, including badly burned WTC 7 pieces. The 2001 account reports a call involving two New Jersey scrap yards. The records describe different periods and scopes; the names of the two yards and the relationship between the counts are not established by the passages reviewed. Preserve both accounts without assuming either a contradiction or an explanation. The NIST source bytes have SHA-256 `d234d025912dab100a0dd479f826b2adff0fc23acd5acb42efc93a4a8bcd9bc3`; the page was visually reviewed in the [separate guide lead](../steel-selection-guide-2026-11-15/README.md).

**Interpretation and limits.** The contemporaneous account supplies a targeted original-record lead: the volunteer solicitation and any attachments, rosters and yard reports. It does not establish that all yards or steel were inspected, authenticate any specimen's origin, identify the later credited selection guide, or show improper selection or concealment. The 2001 source is a journal report, and source dependence with NIST is unknown; do not count them as independent confirmation of each item.
