# Two September FBI interview records for the Flight 77 family relay

November 6, 2026 UTC · **Telephone TEL01–TEL05 / AUD016-A1.** Four page images in a public Team 7 Box 17 reproduction show two related FBI interview records. The Washington Field report says **“exactly 6:13 a.m. Pacific Time”** and records the family’s belief that the daughter used her cellular phone. The Las Vegas interview says **“approximately 6:13 am”** without a printed zone and records Nancy May’s uncertainty about whether the phone was onboard equipment or cellular. The Las Vegas form also names Patty Carson as the family’s airline contact. These are two accounts from the same family and a direct source for what the reports recorded. The onboard equipment and call joins remain unverified. The explicit **Pacific Time** label belongs to the Washington Field interview’s **6:13** value. The September 12 Las Vegas form, the official response, and the earlier Dallas relay’s **about 6:05 Pacific** retain their own precision and labels. The Commission’s **9:11/9:12** attempted and connected entries are separate carrier-derived claims in the staff report. No value is selected as controlling, and the new time-zone label is confined to the source that prints it. [Dallas relay](https://www.les-crises.fr/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/Team7-Box13-Flight-Call-Notes-and-302s.pdf#page=53) · [Staff report](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/9-11/staff-report-sept2005.pdf#page=31)

The new Las Vegas form narrows the relay question by naming the same airline recipient as Carson’s interview. It describes the family collectively and reports a response to Ronald, while Carson identifies Nancy as the caller she answered. One shared call involving both parents, separate contacts, or different summary wording remain possible. Likewise, one reported male voice and several reported people assisting differ in what each account describes. The reports share family witnesses, and the source behind the differing detail remains open. The forms include production labels **265A** and visible internal **265D** references. Preserve those fields with their separate document roles and seek the release manifest before joining the controls. The reader export footers showing **September 12, 2003** are separate from the investigation and transcription dates.

**Open inputs.** Seek the official release or original FBI production for both item IDs and their case-control mapping. Compare original interview notes, recordings, and Washington Field and Las Vegas assignment and receipt records. A matching lead ID, subject, recipient and timestamp could test whether the Las Vegas narrative points to the held WFO interview. The same-night Carson contact record and the January 20 airline email remain separate open targets. Airline callback and PBX logs, the family’s notes, carrier records, and timing definitions are still needed to test device, routing, and clock fields.
