## Source observations

The [National Archives K90-33 electronic document, Tape Certification3](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/e-Other/5-K90-33-Tape-Certification3.doc), NAID 7603276, bears the date **April 19, 2004**, addresses the Presidential Commission inquiry and describes an attached certified voice re-recording. Its certification text states the data was derived from facility DVRS data for **1230 UTC to 1600 UTC on September 11, 2001**. The extracted electronic document contains a signature line and typed names; a signed attachment or delivery receipt was not examined. The document does not itself identify a K90-39 catalog number, playback length or individual recording position. Its attachment was not examined in this step.

The separately released [April 19 gap memorandum](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/e-Other/5-AWA-327-Memo-Voice-Recording-Minutes-Missing.pdf) refers to a HIGH AR sector recording for **1230–1600 UTC**, states **one minute and twenty seconds** was missing, and describes the loss as **almost two minutes**. Its listed values **1538.37** and **1540 UTC**, and its account of a tape change, remain preserved. Same date and range make a possible relationship between documents worth testing; they do not establish that both identify the same re-recording or media.

In the [prior automated audio probe](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/e3744475a317ae346693dfc67765414cd0d2b829/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-cape-automated-audio-probe-2026-11-25/README.md), a speech-recognition model suggested an opening announcement of **1230–1600 UTC**. That output remains unverified and may be wrong. The Archives finding aid separately titles the K90-39 MP3 **1230–1400 UTC**, while the cassette description lists **1230–1315; 1345–1400 UTC**. Preserve these values and the source descriptions side by side; no actual timeline or continuity has been independently established. Similar wording may reflect shared documentation, not independent confirmation.
