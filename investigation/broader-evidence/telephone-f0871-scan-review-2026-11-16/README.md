# F0871XX: recovered scan of a reported deactivation work release

November 16, 2026 (Eastern) · AUD016-A1 / TEL03–TEL04. A [2007 posting](https://screwloosechange.blogspot.com/2007/09/aa-77-airfones-final-story.html) says it reproduced an airline engineering order and added redactions. Its visible text-reader page did not display the image. A [2010 account](https://www.twf.org/News/Y2010/0116-PhoneCalls.html) preserves the historical image-page URL. That URL returned a small HTML wrapper containing a [direct JPEG link](https://2.bp.blogspot.com/_go0r2XYwpws/Rut6XRAnZ9I/AAAAAAAAAL8/h3RSUc1GUjw/s1600/F0871+001.jpg); the JPEG was retrieved and visually examined November 16, 2026. JPEG: 1162 × 1599 pixels; SHA-256 `7d99d8ef92fe818fb32e8719a6b179cc0ef6a35c0e28b52550d932a8d3d81dab`. It is a third-party, visibly redacted representation, not an authenticated carrier original. It is linked, not redistributed. No obscured identities are reconstructed.

## What is visible

- The page bears the heading “American Airlines’ Maintenance Work Release,” ECO **F0871XX**, and the title “Telephone Circuit Breaker and Toggle Switch Deactivation.” Its work description says the Claircom telephone system is being deactivated and that this removes power from the telephone system.
- The “to be accomplished” field lists **B757 Aircraft (121)** and the series **610–643, 5BR–5ES, 5ET–5FP**. The scan includes handwritten March 2002 dates near several signoff fields; their specific signatories are obscured. The page alone does not establish that any individual aircraft received the work or that the listed series include N644AA.
- The work-assignment block references **work card 0001**, **work card 0002**, and **B.O.W.** The attachment line refers to one copy of the work release and all work cards to **Box 85**. These are concrete source-record retrieval leads; this scan does not include the cards or establish their survival.

## Competing accounts and limits

The 2007 posting argues that the order determines the phone state on Flight 77, while [a 2010 critique](https://www.twf.org/News/Y2010/0116-PhoneCalls.html) challenges the interpretation and authentication of the document. The visible scan substantiates that a document with the above fields is being circulated; it does not authenticate the scan or resolve the aircraft's prior state. The [separately reproduced maintenance-manual page](https://www.consensus911.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Rowland_Morgan_2010_Voices.pdf#page=35) separately shows the maintenance-manual page's **REV 62, Jan. 28/2007** header and **01/28/2001** footer and competing interpretations. Neither a later work release nor a conflicting date on another reproduced page establishes installation, deactivation, or operation of N644AA on September 11, 2001. No account is selected as controlling.
