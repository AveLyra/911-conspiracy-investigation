# Scooby report 22: five-row dependency table source check

November 12, 2026. The saved accessibility capture contains the five-row table that the earlier visible-summary check had not examined. This is a check of selected table claims against Commission records, **not** inspection of the underlying classified interrogations, native bank records, original intelligence reports, Spanish court filings, all report text or attachments. A Commission footnote is primary evidence of what the Commission cited, not independent proof that every cited underlying record says what the Commission reported.

## Evidentiary effect and next records

The table usefully distinguishes traceable movements and transactions from claims about private conversations, orders and motive. Its confidence labels are judgments rather than calculated probabilities, and none becomes established merely through replication of the Commission’s footnotes. Original interrogation reports and interview conditions, PENTTBOM p. 48 and the native travel and communications records, original transaction records, the Spanish case record, the earlier staff statement and draft-edit trail are needed for stronger independent tests. The table does not establish a UMS or Mossad role.

Other report-22 body claims, source attachments and every other report’s remaining claims remain open; this five-row source check is not completion of the 30-report audit.
