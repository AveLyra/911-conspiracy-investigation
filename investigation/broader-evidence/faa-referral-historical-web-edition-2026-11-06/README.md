## What is in the packet

- **Appendix 5**, PDF p. **30**, presents the original FAA response to the Commission's **May 22, 2003** question. It cites **9:24 a.m.** for formal notice about Flight 77 and describes earlier information through phone bridges. OIG p. **4** separately reports the Commission's **9:34 a.m.** account of FAA notifying NEADS that the flight was missing and its finding that FAA never notified DOD that it had been hijacked. OIG p. **9** calls the **9:24** answer inaccurate and separately discusses when the Air Force liaison joined the bridge. The different communication, recipient and content labels matter. No operational clock was remeasured here.
