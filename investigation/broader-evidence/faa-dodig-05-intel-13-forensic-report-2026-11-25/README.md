## Source and observations

[DoD inspector general report 05-INTEL-13](https://web.archive.org/web/20161222001502id_/http://www.dodig.mil/Foia/ERR/05-INTEL-13.pdf), “Forensic Capabilities and Incident Reporting Related to Air Defense Actions,” May 27, 2005; Internet Archive capture December 22, 2016; acquired November 25, 2026; PDF SHA-256 `5d86b354683e384cb692727c8c0e366b179de5076fe0dac1a75a8335100b429f`. The 28-page reproduction contains redactions and historical classification markings; its cover bears a declassification stamp. The source does not establish present classification or that all underlying records are public. A [DoD reading-room entry](https://www.dodig.mil/FOIA/FOIA-Reading-Room/Article/1238772/05-intel-13-u-forensic-capabilities-and-incident-reporting-related-to-air-defen/) identifies the report.

- Physical page 13 / printed page 5, directly viewed, says key NEADS positions were recorded on September 11 using a Dictaphone device. It says personnel encountered a transcription problem and those tapes were not used to construct the press release or in reporting on the events until Commission staff reviewed them. This is the IG's report of a process and limitation, not proof that all positions were recorded, all tapes survive, or that the accounts are accurate in every detail.
- Physical page 16 / printed page 8 says DoD relied on NORAD for information and that preparation of Commission testimony focused on information developed immediately after the event; it says accuracy checks were not taken. This is a reported finding, not access to the underlying interview, briefing and drafting records. The report also describes staffing pressures and an operational focus; these are accounts to test, not established motive.
- Physical page 17 / printed page 9 recommends improved recording and archiving and incident-investigation procedures. It says the responsible assistant secretary did not comment on the draft, while the Director of the Joint Staff provided unsolicited comments concurring with the need to implement the recommendations. The [2006 Defense IG memorandum, page 2](https://web.archive.org/web/20161222015721id_/http://www.dodig.mil/Ir/reports/06-intel-12.pdf), separately says the assistant secretary agreed to change applicable directives. Preserve the differently dated descriptions; neither establishes actual implementation or closure, and no conflict is resolved by assuming when any response occurred.

## What this can and cannot establish

The report offers concrete tests of how existing NEADS tapes were handled and whether they were available to those preparing the original timeline. It does not identify the actual tape-to-statement mapping, establish that any particular recording was destroyed, or prove concealment or intent. Its reported transcription obstacle and recording limitations are contrary considerations alongside the reported lack of use and checking. The review's initial objective concerning knowingly erroneous information was addressed separately, according to the 2005 report; do not treat this engineering review as a finding that the FAA's May 2003 answer was corrected.
