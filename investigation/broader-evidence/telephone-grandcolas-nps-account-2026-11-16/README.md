- **NPS's reproduced FBI notes:** immediately following that description, the page says agents interviewed the recipient on September 12, 2001, listened to the message and wrote notes. The passage characterizes the message as referring to flight problems and reassurance, and does not itself reproduce a hijacking statement. Preserve the introductory description and reproduced notes separately; they have different levels of detail and the original audio was not examined.
