## Access points and unresolved dates

It lists pedestrian access as **yes, yes, no, no, yes**, respectively. The report describes a safety map said to be in effect and quotes a **50-foot** rubble-pile criterion. ## A beam-specific retrieval lead

The **October 2, 2001** DEP daily report says a **steel box-beam** damaged an interceptor sewer. It reports a planned discussion with a Grace Industries representative about delaying the repair because expanding the excavation would encroach on a temporary road and stop nearby work on the pile. That is a reported problem and proposed discussion. The report does not supply a beam mark, exact interceptor segment, original building, repair decision, removal date, destination or chain of custody. Its specific location/work-order vocabulary offers a way to identify the item from contemporaneous drawings, field records and photographs. [City daily report, PDF p. 1, lines 16–30](https://sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us/apps/content/September11_MD/NYC-WTC_000154435.pdf#page=1)
