# Contemporary steel-collection and WTC 7 image appeals

October 27, 2026 research unit; acquisitions October 28 UTC. Existing SPT01–SPT05, B188/H005 and AUD011/AUD016 remain open. **Two archived SEAoNY notices now supply contemporary collection and visual-evidence instructions. The native inventory workbook and candidate-specific transfer/attribution records were not recovered.**

## What the new records add

The [November 27, 2001 volunteer notice](https://web.archive.org/web/20011205031550id_/http://www.seaony.org:80/wtc-112701.htm) describes an urgent, unpaid yard-search operation that explicitly included WTC 7. Its workflow includes photography/video, dimensions, coupons and retention marks. It describes the larger effort as associated with FEMA and ASCE. These are contemporary published instructions and intentions, not proof of completed work on any particular specimen. The notice also reports that the search had not yet located tower impact-zone steel. That is its time-specific search account, not proof that none was later recovered. Its date and scope have not been joined to individual intake histories.

The [May 2002 image appeal](https://web.archive.org/web/20020608091451id_/http://seaony.org:80/wtc-051002.htm) prioritizes WTC 7 and identifies south/west views as relatively scarce. This could matter when assessing observational coverage, but supplies neither an image census nor a measured directional bias. It does not prove every requested view was received or used.

These documents are contrary evidence to an assertion that the organizers never sought WTC 7 steel or images. They do not establish successful WTC 7 recovery, complete preservation, authenticated origin, adequate resources or validation of a collapse mechanism. They must not be substituted for the field workbook, photo index, receiving manifest or attribution decision.

## Preserve the organizational accounts side by side

The November notice associates the overall effort with FEMA while specifying uncompensated volunteers. [FEMA Appendix D, physical page 1 / printed D-1](https://www.fema.gov/pdf/library/fema403_apd.pdf#page=1) separately places steel collection and storage outside the FEMA/ASCE-sponsored BPS effort, and describes SEAoNY's volunteer organization. Both passages remain preserved. The scope of institutional association, funding, responsibility and preservation authority is not established by choosing one description over the other.

Different project scopes or an informal arrangement are possible explanations; neither is selected. The decisive records would be actual authorizations, interorganization agreements, budgets and expenditure decisions, assignment records and correspondence. Unpaid staffing does not establish zero institutional expenditure. An association described in a public appeal is not a signed funding or custody agreement. No unlawful abandonment, concealment or motive is established here.

A minor geographic description also differs: the notice uses **Staten Island, NY**, while FEMA D-1's Fresh Kills entry uses **Staten Island, NJ**. Preserve both strings. This is a reference-quality observation, not proof of a different yard, an actual misrouted shipment or a problem with the W14/C12 attribution. Its cause is untested.

## Still-needed originals and falsifiable questions

The collection workflow identifies specific originals to pursue: yard/team assignments; actual visit logs; camera/film and image indices; measurements and coupons; retention-mark instructions; disposition records and intake crosswalks. The public appeals do not establish that every category was created or survives. The image appeal likewise motivates the actual contributor permissions and received-media index; it does not authorize redistribution of the contributions.

For W14A/B and C12a/b, those records must join a particular piece and photo series to the later NIST aliases and unidentified classifications. No such join is made here. The native spreadsheet, version history, original January field photographs, dispatch/receipt and attribution rationale remain missing. Existing WTC#7 versus unidentified descriptions and February/March shipment accounts are unchanged and unresolved.

An inventory or receipt could confirm effective preservation and weaken an adverse interpretation. A documented ignored retention instruction or unexplained candidate-specific classification change could strengthen one. Neither follows from a public request for help. The notices, FEMA summary and later reports may describe the same operation; they are not independent confirmations of misconduct.
