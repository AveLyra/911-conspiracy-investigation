# FAA headquarters chronology: Flight 11 intranet entries

2026-11-25 · B129 / PD01 / PD07 and operational-record preservation. A released FAA headquarters chronology contains three entries relevant to locating Flight 11 material. These are entries in one chronology, not proof of what happened to underlying files or media.

## Observations preserved in sequence

Two separately retained scans of the eight-page document titled **“11 SEP 01 CATASTROPHIC CRISIS”** were compared: [NARA AWA-342](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/34913211) and a Commission-file copy in the [January 2009 archive mirror](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip), Team 8 Box 5, `FAA Response to Freni Book Fdr- Timeline.pdf`. The document heading says **“all times given in EDT.”** The date in its title does not establish when the chronology was created or last edited.

| Document entry | Value and wording in the two checked page images |
| --- | --- |
| **1306**, PDF p. 5 | “AEA-500 advised that AAL 11 voice tapes on intranet web site; will be taken off and no more data put on web sites” |
| **1324**, PDF p. 6 | “AAL 11 data removed from intranet” |
| **1328**, PDF p. 6 | “all data to be placed on AAT-20 intranet” |

These are the document's time labels and statements. The first expresses a reported plan; the second reports a removal; the third is a separate prospective instruction. Their exact object, custodians, event times, and relationship have not been established. Do not assume that “all data” means the same files as the voice tapes or that one entry proves the other was carried out. The source does not specify whether the voice tapes were air-traffic, airline, passenger-call or other recordings. No on-board recording or transcript is identified by these lines.

The already reviewed [April 2004 FAA response and Commission-folder copies](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/61d5e8b12b3ba0a12c2df6c5af17c1991b8ec70e/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-commission-request4-folder-comparison-2026-11-25/README.md) supply a candidate production path for the chronology. They do not establish the selected scan as an original attachment or prove receipt. These two scans of the chronology are not independent event witnesses.

## What would test the entries

Obtain the contemporaneous intranet access and server-change logs, page or file inventories, source copies and backups, retention/transfer records, responsible-office instructions, and any corresponding communications. Compare precise identifiers, dates, site names, permissions and clock records; distinguish removal from a web location, relocation, access restriction, preservation and destruction. The third entry does not itself prove successful placement. None of the three entries establishes destroyed recordings, concealment, motive, or an altered or incomplete audio file.

The separately identified corrected May 2003 answer, delivery acknowledgment, signed action/no-action and recommendation dispositions remain open. No connection between these intranet entries and that later correction is established.
