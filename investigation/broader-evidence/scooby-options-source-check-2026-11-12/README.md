# Scooby report 28: options-volume and investigation scope check

November 12, 2026. This checks selected recovered report-body claims against Commission staff’s contemporaneous SEC interview memorandum and financing monograph. Neither is the underlying account ledger, exchange execution/cancellation feed, or independent statistical replication.

| Claim | Original-record check and limitation |
| --- | --- |
| The SEC’s September 6 UAL put total was corrected from 2,075 to 1,575. | The [May 2004 Cella/Ribelin follow-up, PDF p. 21](https://cryptome.org/nara/sec/sec-03-0916.pdf#page=21), reports that the 2,075 included a canceled 500-put sale and that the actual volume was 1,575. It describes OCC figures as generally counting both sides. This is a contemporaneous memorandum of a reconciliation, not inspection of the exchange trade feed; the passage does not assign the canceled sale to a particular account. |
| A corrected aggregate does not establish a revised adviser-level position. | The same memorandum corrects total volume but does not disclose a revised account allocation. The [financing staff monograph, Appendix B](https://www.9-11commission.gov/staff_statements/911_TerrFin_Monograph.pdf#page=151), provides a percentage-based explanation and a broader strategy account. A rounded percentage multiplied by corrected market volume is arithmetic, not an observed execution record. |
| The SEC also identified unresolved volume discrepancies in September 6 calls and September 7 UAL figures. | The [follow-up, PDF pp. 21–22](https://cryptome.org/nara/sec/sec-03-0916.pdf#page=21), records that Cella and Ribelin could not reconstruct the original September 6 call figure or explain the September 7 numbers beyond a possible duplicated September 5 line. That does not mean no trades occurred on September 7. It also gives the AMR calculation (4,516 ordinary sides plus 40 LEAP sides) / 2 = 2,278, rather than the printed 2,282. The native OCC files are needed to reproduce the reconciliation. |
| The monograph’s $85,000 loss refers to a separate September 7 fund and an overall position, not the September 6 adviser’s put trade. | [Appendix B, printed p. 149 / PDF p. 152](https://www.9-11commission.gov/staff_statements/911_TerrFin_Monograph.pdf#page=152), separately describes a September 7 fund, profitable puts and a reported loss in value across its positions. These statements are not inherently inconsistent, but the position-level profit/loss and ownership have not been independently reconstructed. The monograph is staff work; its preface says commissioners did not approve the text. |
| The official inquiry found no evidence of foreknowledge. | The monograph reports that conclusion and describes the SEC/FBI review and ordinary explanations. It is strong counterevidence that should not be omitted; it does not by itself disclose all raw account, execution and cancellation records needed to independently verify every individual explanation or rule out every hypothetical trade. |
