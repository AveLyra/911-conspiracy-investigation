# Flight 93: an approximate time in the FAA chronology

AUD016; documentary comparison only. Visually inspected November 25, 2026. This additive record retains the earlier [FAA chronology review](../faa-ua175-chronology-visual-2026-11-25/README.md).

## Observations and provenance

* An [FAA chronology hosted by the National Security Archive](https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/NSAEBB/NSAEBB165/faa4.pdf), physical PDF page 2, says: “10:10 a.m., approx.: United Flight 93 crashed in Stony Creek Township, Pa.” Its heading says Eastern time is used throughout. It includes entries through October 24, 2001; its precise preparation date is unestablished. The nine-page scan, acquired November 25, 2026, is 2,141,938 bytes; SHA-256 `e0e9f2182e8cf16ef23ea24e0a3abf58993b25e3da7a19344dee31c277ea76be`.
* The [9/11 Commission report](https://www.9-11commission.gov/report/911Report_Ch1.pdf#page=30), printed page 30, records 10:03:11 and says that time is supported by NTSB analysis and Commission examination of radar, recorder data, satellite data, and air-traffic transmissions. This is the Commission's stated basis; those underlying measurements were not independently analyzed here.
* A [United Airlines executive's statement to the Commission](https://www.9-11commission.gov/hearings/hearing7/witness_studdert.htm) gives approximately 10:06. The statement does not itself establish the measurement or clock behind that estimate.

These reported times conflict and are preserved without choosing a controlling time. The FAA's time is explicitly approximate; its rounding or uncertainty cannot be quantified from that label alone. The approximately seven-minute difference between the first and second labels is not a measured duration, a demonstrated clock error, or evidence of any particular cause. Source dependence among the accounts is unverified.

## Scope and next test

Rendered and visually inspected all nine pages at 1×, with page 1 also examined at 1.5×. The scan has no extractable text on pages 1–3. No source reference for the page-2 10:10 entry was identified in this visual review; this does not prove no source record exists. Several later entries expressly attribute particular information to media, but that does not identify the source for the crash time. The report does not identify a preparation date beyond covering events through October 24.
