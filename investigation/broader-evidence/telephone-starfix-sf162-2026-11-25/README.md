# Flight 93 Star-Fix: SF 162 enclosures and Combs fax lead

November 25, 2026 · TEL02–TEL04 / AUD016-A1. A released September 2001 FBI communication identifies specific original notes and written-statement enclosures concerning the United maintenance-desk call, as well as a separately reported fax of a Combs interview. These are retrieval leads; neither the original notes nor the fax was independently recovered in this step.

## Source and observations

We inspected physical pages **31–34** of the [January 2009 Commission archive ZIP](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip) member `911 NARA/Team 7 Files/Team 7 Box 12/T7 B12 DOJ Doc Req 35-13 Packet 4 Fdr- Entire Contents- Response Letters- Reports 403.pdf`. The 92-page PDF was acquired November 25 by a verified bounded range; size **3,529,428 bytes**, ZIP CRC `930f2025`, SHA-256 `7ed35efb7825f26cb0c712d334a8b286c74bcc25c5b44e01f227059f446c6dba`. Pages 31, 32 and 34 were visually checked; page 33 was read from the text layer. Redacted identities were not reconstructed.

- The **September 12, 2001 San Francisco communication**, p. 31 / document **000000153**, cites lead control **SF 162**. Its enclosure line describes originals and copies of two interviews dated September 11 and **two FD-340s containing interview notes and written statements**. It identifies Richard Belme as one interview subject; the other subject is redacted in the release. The communication describes intended enclosures; it is not an independent receipt verifying delivery or present custody.
- Page 31 and continuation p. 32 / **000000154** report that the San Francisco FBI operations center received a faxed Combs FD-302 dated September 11 from Chicago, and that it was attached to reference serial control **WF98**. Page 33 asks Chicago to forward the Combs FD-302 to New York. The communication does not identify that fax by the later-visible serial **302-107853** or **302-95682**; no specific join or completed forwarding is established.
- The accompanying Belme interview, p. 34 / **000000156**, reports that he supplied a handwritten statement and reproduces a typed account that places his response to the Star-Fix call at approximately **0640 PST**. This typed reproduction is not the handwritten original. A [previous 2003 Commission interview review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/1e4baca5c7b93418afab59edf60a4776d85feae6/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-ua93-starfix-maintenance-2026-11-06/README.md) preserves Belme's **6:40 a.m.** estimate alongside a reported **6:30 a.m. Pacific** attributed to the FBI and a separate **9:35** airline account. Keep those source statements and their event boundaries separate; no synchronized clock or call-leg join is proven.

## Retrieval and limits

- [ ] Locate **SF 162**, the two FD-340 items, original notes and written statements, their actual 1A numbers, transmittals, receipt and disposition; compare any obtained handwriting to the typed statement and determine which of the two note sets discussed in the 2003 interview each represents.
- [ ] Locate the fax and **WF98** reference plus forwarding/receipt records, identify which Combs FD-302 was sent, and compare its pages and versions with 302-95682 and 302-107853. Do not infer a match from shared names or dates.
- [ ] Obtain the call queue, operator transfer and switch logs and independently documented clock definitions before interpreting timing or identifying the caller.

A list of enclosures is not proof that the items survive today. Reported recollections, typed statements, fax and original notes are distinct sources; none by itself establishes a cause for the differing times or any deliberate conduct.
