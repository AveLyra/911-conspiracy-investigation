# FAA delegation records and directives custody for the 2006 follow-up

November 6, 2026 · **B129 / PD02–PD04** · **PD01** remains open.

FAA Order **1100.1A** identifies where written authority for different kinds of decisions was documented. NARA schedule **NC-174-227, item 3** identifies the **directives case files** that generally held the supporting organizational records. These are distinct leads for locating the applicable delegation and the office that could have maintained it. Neither source identifies who actually handled the decision in **CC-2006-085 / 042R0122001**. [FAA order, ¶12](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Order/1100.1A.pdf#page=14) · [NARA schedule, physical p. 2](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-transportation/rg-0237/nc-174-000227_sf115.pdf#page=2) · [OIG recommendation 2](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=12)

## Delegation sources and their scope

| Record | What it supplies |
| --- | --- |
| **1100.1A**, reprinted with Changes 1–6, ¶¶11(c) and 12(a)–(b), physical p. 14 | The Office of Management Systems maintains the master organization manual and related directives. The order requires written documentation of delegations, provides for implicit authority unless expressly reserved, and distinguishes a position description reflecting authority from the instrument that delegates it. Legal requirements and significant authority binding the agency are specified grounds for an explicit **Special Delegations** entry. [FAA order, printed p. 11](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Order/1100.1A.pdf#page=14) |
| **1100.1A Change 6**, ¶12(c)–(e), physical p. 15, headed **3/2/87** | Describes publication of direct Administrator delegations in the organization manual. It separately says authority inherent in an executive or supervisory position, such as discipline, need not be published there. It says redelegations are published in supplementary organization directives. A search limited to the organization manual would leave other authority records unchecked. [FAA order, printed p. 12](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Order/1100.1A.pdf#page=15) |
| **1100.2C**, Chapter 34, heading dated **2/6/89** | The historical headquarters chart identifies **ALR-200**, Employee Relations Division, and lists conduct and discipline and adverse actions, grievances and appeals among its functions. The chapter describes employee-relations program administration and advice to FAA management. The 1989 label provides a search alias for directives and predecessor files. A dated 2006 organizational chart, delegation or dispatch is still needed to identify the office and authority for this matter. [Headquarters order, Chapter 34](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Order/FAA_Order_1100.2C.pdf) |
| **1100.1B**, September 14, 2015, Chapter 1 ¶4 | The successor expressly cancels **1100.1A**, **1100.2C**, **1100.5C** and **1100.148B**. The catalog independently lists the cancelled orders and titles. This supplies the change boundary for the manuals. The case-specific delegation and applicable HR policy editions remain separate. [2015 cancellation clause](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Order/FAA_1100_1B.pdf#page=4) · [FAA catalog](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/orders_notices/index.cfm/go/document.information/documentID/1028209) |

## Custody and different transfer intervals

| Record | Observed status and interval |
| --- | --- |
| **NC-174-227**, item 3, original form | Classifies **Organizational planning files** as **Permanent**. The item includes documents on changes in organization, functions, relationships and delegations, and says these papers are generally maintained in directives case files. The handwritten entry specifies transfer to a Federal Records Center after **four years or earlier** and offer to the National Archives in **ten years**. The schedule describes a records class. It supplies no transfer receipt for a particular delegation or decision. [Original form, physical p. 2](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-transportation/rg-0237/nc-174-000227_sf115.pdf#page=2) |
| **NARA cover note**, as of **March 7, 2025** | Lists **item 3** among the active items, subject to subsequent supersession. The public RG 237 schedules index links the file as **NC-174-000227, Miscellaneous Records**. Keep the cover's as-of date and its qualification. [Cover note](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-transportation/rg-0237/nc-174-000227_sf115.pdf#page=1) · [RG 237 index](https://www.archives.gov/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/index.html?dir=/departments/department-of-transportation/rg-0237) |
| **DAA-0237-2020-0001**, item 0001 | Lists **NC-174-227/3** among the authorities targeted by a proposed replacement. Its proposed intervals are **five years** to inactive storage, **15 years** to electronic pre-accessioning and **25 years** to NARA accessioning after cutoff. The form and item are marked **withdrawn**, and the schedule status reads **Returned Without Action**. Preserve these as proposed intervals with their own status. [Cover](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-transportation/rg-0237/daa-0237-2020-0001_sf115.pdf#page=1) · [Item, physical p. 3](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-transportation/rg-0237/daa-0237-2020-0001_sf115.pdf#page=3) |

NARA's 2018 accession list gives two possible predecessor-file routes. **LTI-0237-2018-0026** is a one-container, unrestricted *Directives Case Files* accession covering **May 1, 1984–September 1, 1996**. **LTI-0237-2018-0020** is a separate one-container, unrestricted accession with the same series title covering **September 1, 1987–December 31, 1997**. Their dates overlap the 1996 personnel reorganization. Both end before the 2006 referral. The accession entries identify holdings at the container level and give no folder inventory or link to a particular delegation. [NARA quarterly accession list, RG 237](https://www.archives.gov/research/accessions/2018-quarter-3#:~:text=LTI-0237-2018-0026)
