# Virginia Beach memorial steel and the B245 attribution

November 6, 2026 UTC · STL02–STL03 / AUD011 / AUD016 / B188 / H005

An indexed Virginia Task Force 2 page gives a specific WTC 2 designation, **B245, floors 55–58**. WAVY's ceremony report describes steel displayed at Virginia Beach's Diezel Fire Training Center as **North Tower** material. Keep both descriptions. The task force page extract gives no item or installation identifier linking its panel to the steel in the ceremony report. If both describe the same object, their tower labels conflict. NIST's column plan can check the meaning of the reported column designation. The physical mark and custody trail still need to be checked. [Task force steel page](https://www.vatf2.com/9-11-wtc-steel/) · [WAVY report as syndicated by AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/virginia-beach-fire-honors-9-172521000.html)

Two possibilities remain open. The descriptions could concern one object with inconsistent labels, or they could refer to different objects or components. The 2024 notes give a dated route to check how material described before the installation relates to the later display. The item-level join remains unverified. These records concern tower descriptions and supply no WTC 7 specimen link.
