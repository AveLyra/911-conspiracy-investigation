# Scooby original-record checks: UMS, Classic, Bayoumi and evidence custody

November 11, 2026. These checks address selected claims from six reports, not every claim in those reports. They compare the reports with FBI-created records; a record's description of a source, interview, opinion or investigation is not automatically independent proof of its underlying assertion. Unavailable originals remain open.

## UMS polygraph and headquarters inquiries — report 5

The two-page [New York Serial 368](https://www.israellobby.org/UrbanMoving/1169683-001%20---%20303A-NY-C237934-Serial%20368%20---%20Section%201%20%281037763%29.pdf) was retrieved and visually inspected. It is dated January 3, 2002. The details describe an examination on November 13–16, 2001, whereas the synopsis says November 13–16, **2002**, a future date relative to the memorandum. The source therefore contains an apparent date error; the different dates must be preserved. The release host's index separately says January 3, **2001**, which conflicts with the document header. Original examination records would resolve the dates.

The examiner's opinion distinguished responses to prior-knowledge questions, reported as not indicative of deception, from responses to intelligence-affiliation questions, reported as indicative of deception. The document says FBI headquarters quality control concurred. These are reported polygraph interpretations, not proof that affiliation existed or that foreknowledge was impossible. The released text does not establish the separate April–August 2002 headquarters-inquiry link.

[Serial 459](https://www.israellobby.org/UrbanMoving/1169683-001%20---%20303A-NY-C237934-Serial%20459%20---%20Section%201%20%281037765%29.pdf) bears a June 4, 2002 date and New York National Security Division heading; its deletion sheet lists three entirely withheld pages. The visible cover does not identify the underlying subject or prove that it is the later headquarters matter. Search the underlying case identifiers, visit report and final assessment; do not infer identity from similar dates or redactions.

## Bayoumi intelligence allegation — report 6

The [June 14, 2017 FBI communication, EO14040-002638–2639](https://911familiesunited.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Bayoumi-is-GIP-Cooptee.pdf#page=1), was retrieved, rendered and visually inspected. It records information attributed to a redacted source and date alleging Bayoumi was paid as a Saudi GIP cooptee through Ambassador Bandar and relayed information of intelligence interest. The writer states that this information confirms earlier allegations of Saudi intelligence involvement. The source's identity, access and reporting date are not visible, so the underlying source cannot be independently evaluated from these pages. The record does not itself establish CIA recruitment direction, advance knowledge of the attacks, or UMS contact.

The [2021 Encore closure assessment, pp. 2–3 and 9–11](https://vault.fbi.gov/9-11-attacks-investigation-and-related-materials/9-11-material-released-in-response-to-executive-order-14040/documents-responsive-to-executive-order-14040-section-2-b-ii-part-01-of-01/at_download/file), investigated knowing assistance and assessed that Bayoumi and the two other named subjects did not knowingly conspire to assist the hijackers in furtherance of the attack. That conclusion is contrary evidence on attack knowledge, not a determination that the 2017 source never existed or that intelligence affiliation and logistical assistance did not occur. The 2025 civil jurisdiction ruling applies a different procedural test and must be evaluated separately; neither document identifies a UMS bridge.

## Computer and Exxon records — reports 10 and 11

The [October 17, 2001 FBI Serial 325, p. 4](https://www.israellobby.org/UrbanMoving/1169683-001%20---%20303A-NY-C237934-Serial%20325%20---%20Section%201%20%281037758%29.pdf#page=4), says CART had conducted a preliminary analysis of hard drives seized at UMS, but the material immediately following is redacted. It does not identify the contents of an assignment, customer, crew, vehicle or WTC transaction. The same page describes a **possible** match between an Exxon video van and the van under investigation, expressly conditional on their being identical. It does not visibly name K1966; treating that memo as a definite identification or as an independently proven link to a particular accession would overstate it.

The locally preserved FBI scan, [section 5 pp. 64–66 and 69–71](https://www.themoreuknow.com/pdf/fbi%20report%20section%205.pdf#page=64), was rendered and inspected. The September 28 referral requests enhancement of two white vans at displayed times 08:29–08:31; no clock calibration is shown there. The October 18 lab report identifies K1966, a TDK T-160 marked AGBH009, received October 1, and says selected frames were digitized and enhancement was minimal. These pages do not report a conclusive van identity or an independently corrected time. The lab reference and matching Exxon description support further comparison but do not, without a direct cross-reference, prove that the earlier conditional memo's footage and K1966 are the same physical item. Obtain the footage or authenticated derivative, clock records, comparison worksheets and final findings.

## Classic notebook and translation — report 15

The [September 2001 FBI memorandum, section 5 p. 50](https://www.themoreuknow.com/pdf/fbi%20report%20section%205.pdf#page=50), was visually inspected in the locally preserved scan. It records Miami's belief that Classic International Movers had moved an unnamed alleged hijacker and reports Classic's number in a notebook seized from the five detainees. These are reported investigative leads: neither the original customer record nor a call, meeting, identified hijacker or shared purpose appears on that page. The later [section 6 p. 47](https://www.themoreuknow.com/pdf/fbi%20report%20section%206.pdf#page=47) repeats substantially the same account; repeated reporting may derive from the same source and is not independent confirmation.

The [October 4, 2002 return memorandum, section 6 p. 6](https://www.themoreuknow.com/pdf/fbi%20report%20section%206.pdf#page=6), was also visually inspected. It lists 1B25/E01889034 and 1B25/E01889039 for return, followed by a partly redacted originating-case reference and identifiers including 1B4/E01889578, 1B30 and 1B11. It says the reviewed translations were personal in nature and a linguist found no foreign-intelligence material in those documents. This is contrary evidence about the materials reviewed, not an assessment of all possible evidence. The mixed grouping alone does not prove which notebook held the Classic entry; earlier [September shipment handwriting](../../urban-moving-systems/fbi-ec-crosswalk-verified-2026-11-11/README.md) supplies a separate mapping to examine.
