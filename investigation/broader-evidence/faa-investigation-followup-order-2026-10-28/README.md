## Qualifications that must remain with those leads

Chapter II §9(b) says DOT elements will **normally be requested** to provide action notice **within 60 days**. Preserve that wording. This is not an unconditional 60-day deadline finding against the FAA case; the governing edition, actual request, receipt and extensions/actions have not been established.

Chapter I is explicitly **Audits** and has different applicability, database/reporting provisions and timing. Its audit database's fields or six-month/60-day rules cannot simply be assigned to the investigation. Chapter II §3(c)'s escalation contains warranted-action conditions; §3(e)'s referral of disputed actions excludes personnel actions. Do not convert either into an automatic finding that executives had to be disciplined or every disagreement had to go to one official. D02,D04,D08.

The source also contains historical report-handling/release language in §7(c). No current FOIA withholding conclusion is drawn from it, and report publication is not treated as certification that all follow-up was completed. These are historical policy observations, not adopted rules for the user's access rights or litigation position. §6(b)'s usual administrative continuation after a prosecution declination does not establish that any such referral or declination occurred here. D05–D06.
