# The Hilliard steel shipment and Norwich Township's carrier record

November 6, 2026 UTC · STL02–STL03 / AUD011 / AUD016 / B188 / H005

Norwich Township's **June 7, 2010** minutes record unanimous approval of motion **100607.02** recognizing **Bulk Transit** for delivering 9/11 artifacts to Hilliard's First Responders Park. The fire department report on the preceding page gives **New York City to Hilliard** as the route. This is a contemporary municipal record naming a carrier and destination. It gives a date and motion number for locating the supporting paperwork. It leaves the loaded items, their weights, and their receipt by individual destinations unverified. [Township minutes, PDF pp. 30–31](https://cms5.revize.com/revize/norwichtwpoh/meeting%20minutes/2010/Minutes%202010.pdf#page=30)

The weight descriptions are compatible numerically, while their accounting bases are unknown. The convoy, the Hilliard delivery, and the pieces displayed at the park could be different sets. The early selection report predates the 2010 escort. Whether the 2023 account refers to those same three pieces or a later selection is open. The two city sculpture descriptions could refer to different objects or different components. Keep both pending an installation drawing, materials schedule or object inventory. The two city accounts may draw on shared records or recollections. The township minutes and the contemporary reprint corroborate the named carrier while leaving each item's identity and origin open.

The Hilliard lead concerns a **May 2010 Hangar 17 shipment**. The earlier Westerville study traces **C-40** through a separately reported 2009 route. No reviewed source links Hilliard's display to that NIST specimen or identifies a WTC 7 specimen.
