## What these records can test

The 2010 staff report supplies a requester and a month to check in city and transfer records. The 2014 report and draft describe a receiving history and give physical descriptors that could be compared with a marked beam, inventory or weight ticket. The terms identify records that could document condition and handling at installation. The references in a single council packet may share their sources. The reported November request and reported 2009 receipt leave the order, exact dates and number of handoffs unresolved. The source difference between **Federal Government** and **New York Port Authority** leaves the actual addressee and approval authority open.

The city’s reported receipt and later dedication provide evidence of a municipal custody route. The reviewed passages supply no unique artifact number, court-list row, NIST specimen mark or WTC 7 attribution. The design and installation records could establish the identity or condition of the displayed beam if they include a stable mark or pre-installation photo that can be joined to receiving records. The approved conveyance and actual condition history remain separate questions.
