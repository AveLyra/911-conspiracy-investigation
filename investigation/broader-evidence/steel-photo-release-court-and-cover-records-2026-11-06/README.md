## Release 32 inventory and limits

The compressed-copy mirror identifies **09-42**, **Interim Response #32**, and **October 8, 2010** as its reported release date. Its captured item metadata lists **35** top-level file entries. These include **28 RAR packages**, two text notices and five supplemental or archive metadata entries. The listing includes `42A0375 - G33D9.rar`, the package corresponding to the previously documented W14A/B and C12 photo paths. No agency cover letter is listed as a standalone item in that captured metadata. The RAR package contents and the original release delivery were outside this check. [Mirror metadata](https://archive.org/metadata/nist-foia-09-42-r32) · [Previously verified field-photo review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/6fec9041aeb8aab23b27f2d1d83b9d2aecb7eac7/investigation/broader-evidence/steel-gms-target-photo-bodies-2026-11-05/README.md)

The mirror’s reported October release predates the November protective-order motion. The dates supply a testable sequence. The available records leave open whether the motion, order or dismissal concerns any of the target photographs or their metadata. The mirror also does not establish that the uploaded package is the complete original NIST production.
