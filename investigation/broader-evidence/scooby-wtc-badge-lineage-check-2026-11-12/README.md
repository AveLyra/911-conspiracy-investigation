# Scooby report 18: WTC badge and backup lineage check

November 12, 2026. Selected claims from the recovered access-data report are compared against NIST and NYC Health documents and contemporary trade reporting. No underlying roster, database image, FBI receipt, cardholder identification or access transaction was obtained in this pass. These data sources must not be assumed identical.

| Claim | Source check and limit |
| --- | --- |
| NIST received an occupant badge list in May 2003 and an updated list in August. | [NIST’s July 2004 collection chronology](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2004/07/status-data-collection-efforts) lists the occupant badge list under May 2003 and an updated PANYNJ list under August 2003. Its [occupant study](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=101046), §3.2.2, describes a sampling frame containing people absent on September 11, former occupants and test entries. This establishes a reported roster acquisition and its survey use, not entry events on a particular day. |
| A separate health-registry list had 95,442 names from badge issuances over five years. | The [NYC Health Registry data manual, §4.5.3](https://www.nyc.gov/html/doh/wtc/downloads/pdf/wtc/wtc-datafile-manual.pdf), says DOHMH supplied RTI the Port Authority list in May 2004. It also reports 46,650 records were not pursued for contact because locating information was insufficient. The manual does not establish that every one-day or temporary visitor pass was included. A name on the list alone would not prove presence on September 11. |
| E-J’s president said the FBI downloaded an offsite backup. | [EC&M’s October 2001 report](https://www.ecmweb.com/cee-news-magazine-archive/article/20895636/e-j-electric-workers-survive-world-trade-center-attack) attributes to Tony Mann a statement that agents downloaded a backup at a California subcontractor, including worker information and photographs. This is contemporaneous reporting of a named participant’s account, not an inspected FBI evidence inventory, forensic image or independent confirmation of complete contents. |
| The operational system retained access information for 18 months. | A [2002 EC&M report](https://www.ecmweb.com/cee-news-magazine-archive/article/20895023/learning-from-9-11) describes a system storing employee and visitor entry/exit information for 18 months and offsite backups of photographs and personnel records. It does not specify the cutoff, schema or full contents of the specific backup described in 2001. Distinguish an operational retention description from an authenticated FBI production. |

**Implication for the visitor-card lead:** a roster match could identify a person or authorization, depending on field definitions; an authenticated event record could support a card transaction, subject to correct card-to-person linkage and clock interpretation. A roster miss does not rule out a visitor whose temporary card was excluded. The FBI property receipt records a WTC visitor card but does not publicly identify its holder or prove building entry or a UMS job.

**Open:** Port Authority roster schemas and temporal coverage; original visitor-card issue records and card number; FBI backup receipt, image/inventory and chain of custody; visitor and event-table scope; last replication time; transaction direction and clock calibration; and any positive or negative comparison with the detained men. Other report-18 claims and attachments remain unreviewed.
