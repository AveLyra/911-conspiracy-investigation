Its February 5, 2002 cover, forwarded messages and two displayed change sheets correspond in subject and content to the earlier reviewed [AEA-1124](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/4-UAL-175/e-Other/4-AEA-1124-MEMO--FORMAL-ACCIDENT-PACKAGE-REF-ZNY-ARTCC-148-UAL175-ON-CORRECTION-FEB-5-2002.pdf), catalog [7598362](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7598362), five pages, 262,340 bytes; SHA-256 `e26e5570efc704df0e24b51259f82337e39ae789a622e99ad4c276caa6c2433c`. We visually inspected ZNY-357's change sheets, PDF pages 3–4; extracted text from those pages and AEA-1124 pages 4–5 yielded normalized-character SequenceMatcher ratios of 0.9952 and 0.9952. This measures textual similarity, not authentication or exact byte identity. The PDFs differ in length and hash, and AEA-1124 has an extra cover view. Do not count their overlapping correction requests as independent corroboration. The [earlier source review and 33-entry ledger](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/608171664400ed8d1e95f70277ea994702a3cbd7/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-package-corrections-2002-2026-10-30/README.md) already preserved the actual February changes and the separate May email; this crosswalk does not claim those changes are newly discovered.

## Visible change-sheet examples and limits

ZNY-357 page 3 displays questions about Sector 55 transcript ordering (`1301:06` versus `1301:05`; `1305:34` versus `1305:33`), each followed by “Corrected,” without giving replacement values. Page 4 asks whether a transcript end should be `1344`, requests a tape check, and displays “Corrected.” It requests that six personnel statements line out `AAL11`; one also requests lining out `12:42`, each marked “Corrected.” It displays a question about inverted weather products and a response that they are not inverted in the original/remaining copies. These are reported questions and responses, not proof of independent tape checks, implemented final versions, illicit alteration or motive. The change sheets are labeled pages 1 and 2 of 22; the scan contains only those two sheets, not proof that other pages were destroyed.

The separate [January 18 ZNY-356 cover](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/bf7e33aef7d224215671f948bd94e58da0f8562c/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-zny-356-package-corrections-2026-11-16/README.md) describes an earlier corrections attachment and explanatory email absent from that one-page scan. The February forwarded chain says earlier corrections were received and additional changes were requested; it does not establish that the February change sheets are the January cover's missing attachments. Obtain the January attachments, original and corrected statements/transcripts, complete change document, actual tapes, certifications and delivery records to test the joins. The 2003 Commission-answer correction is a separate issue.
