# Claircom shutdown and disputed asset-transfer scope


November 5, 2026. This **TEL01/TEL03/TEL04** follow-up traces the equipment and records-holder leads for the AT&T/Claircom air-to-ground service. The earlier carrier-enclosure review identifies provider printouts and missing native event records. A later asset purchase is a separate custody lead. No record examined here assigns a September 11 call or defines an event code. [Earlier carrier-enclosure review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/ad7be60185f5d3a49f0e0b7564f4da3610afaf92/investigation/broader-evidence/telephone-carrier-enclosures-2026-10-27/README.md)

## What the FCC records establish

- FCC 03-95, paragraph 12 and footnote 36, says AT&T Wireless discontinued its commercial air-ground operations **September 1, 2002**. It names a Claircom license-cancellation application for **KNKG801**, file **0001161399**, filed **January 16, 2003**. The ULS action notice separately gives **January 17, 2003** as the action date, with purpose `CA` and action `G`. Its neighboring entry lists file **0001161405**, call sign **WPQX262**, the same action date and codes, and the literal applicant name `Claircom License Corporation`. The first entry uses `CLAIRCOM LICENSEE CORPORATION`. Keep the service, filing and action dates and both applicant labels separate. [FCC 03-95, ¶12 and n. 36](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-03-95A1.pdf#page=8) · [FCC ULS action notice, p. 4](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-230457A1.pdf#page=4)
- FCC 04-287, paragraph 11, says Skyway Aircraft acquired the former ClairCom air-ground system. It describes a **Special Temporary Authorization** on shared spectrum and cites **WPYZ281** plus Skyway comments of **November 26, 2003** and **April 1, 2004** in **WT Docket 03-103**. The order and license filings provide separate retrieval routes. The paragraph does not list specific transferred assets, records, or media. [FCC 04-287, ¶11 and nn. 32–34](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-04-287A1.pdf#page=9)

## Acquisition descriptions to preserve side by side

| Record | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Sky Way Aircraft financial statement filed as an exhibit, Note 2 | Reports an **Asset Purchase Agreement signed January 16, 2003** with Claircom Communications Group, Inc. This is a company disclosure, with no executed agreement or schedules verified in this review. [EDGAR financial-statement exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1128723/000110801703000645/skyway8kfins.htm) |
| SkyWay December 23, 2003 press release filed as Exhibit 99.1 | Says it had purchased the **AT&T/Claircom network** and describes planned reactivation under temporary FCC authority. [EDGAR Exhibit 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1128723/000110801704000097/e991.htm) |
| FCC 04-287, ¶11 | Describes acquisition of the former ClairCom air-ground system. [FCC order](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-04-287A1.pdf#page=9) |
| SEC complaint, **8:09-cv-00455-SDM-TBM**, Doc. 1, p. 11, ¶¶45–46 | Alleges the purchase comprised tower-location and specification information plus some spare parts. It describes an **August 6, 2003 AT&T letter** disputing the company's claim about acquiring the 166-tower network, and quotes the letter denying inclusion of Mexico and Canada operations or equipment technology, information or data. These are allegations and quoted correspondence in a pleading. [SEC complaint, p. 11](https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/complaints/2009/comp20960.pdf#page=11) |

The descriptions differ materially in scope. An FCC operating authorization, the assets conveyed by agreement and custody of legacy records require separate proof. The complaint's geographic language does not establish whether any U.S. billing data, backups, switch logs, or engineering files transferred or survived. The FCC orders and action notice were acquired and hashed. The SEC material was read through publicly indexed sections. Direct downloads of the SEC files returned HTTP 403, so their native bytes and the quoted AT&T letter remain unacquired. [Source pins and exact locators](SOURCE-PINS.json)

## Deferred originals and test

- [ ] **Executed January 16, 2003 agreement.** Locate the agreement, purchased/excluded asset schedules, delivery inventories and any software, database, backup or record-retention provisions. Compare each record type against the company disclosure and FCC description.
- [ ] **August 6, 2003 AT&T letter and court exhibits.** Locate the letter, its attachment and any admitted exhibits or court findings specifically addressing which assets transferred. The SEC complaint identifies the letter but leaves the native document and litigation disposition of this allegation open.
- [ ] **FCC application and system filings.** Locate **0001161399**, **0001161405**, the WPYZ281 STA and the two Skyway submissions in WT Docket 03-103. Verify equipment and records descriptions and whether the licensing file refers to a separate agreement.
- [ ] **Native carrier/engineering and operator records.** Seek actual legacy data and media inventories, event formats, operator routing and matched call records under the existing telephone work plan. The asset and licensing trail leaves the 2001 recipient, switch leg, time and duration questions open.

No September 11 call record, transfer manifest, asset schedule or call-data retention determination was obtained in this step.
