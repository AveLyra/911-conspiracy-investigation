## Preserved records and publication status

REPORT.md, RECORD.md, PROTOCOL.md, findings.json, targets.json and source-pins.json
are exact copies of the preceding local study. Their local/unpublished wording
records the status before the user's separate instruction to publish. It is
preserved deliberately; this README and the publication scope record describe
the subsequent publication. SOURCE-STUDY-MANIFEST.json inventories that earlier
local study, not the public delivery set.

Original PDFs, acquired response bodies, OCR and native-page images remain
unchanged in local research holdings. They are not bulk-exported here, because
some contain personal contact/account information. The public catalog retains
source links, exact hashes and physical page references. Its relative holding
locators identify the source study's files; they do not imply all raw files are
present in this public package. No source, conflicting value or redaction was
rewritten to make the publication cleaner.

The NARA media acquisition receipt and Commons structured provenance are
included under source-provenance/. Byte identity supports copy provenance; it
does not independently establish every historical recollection. No scientific
or historical reconstruction was performed for publication.
