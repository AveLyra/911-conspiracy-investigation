## RF001: an underlying witness memorandum is now acquired

The MFR is dated April20,2004 and identifies Team1A and its preparer. It is a
staff memorandum of an interview, not a verbatim deposition transcript or audio
recording. Its six physical pages were read and visually inspected in full.

Physical4 records the following sequence: Bin Don and Bayoumi ordered food and
sat waiting; Hazmi and Mihdhar entered speaking Arabic; Bayoumi addressed them
and invited them to sit. It reports an invitation to San Diego, an apparent
telephone-number exchange whose precise sequence the witness could not recall,
and departure after about **thirty minutes**. It also records the witness's
emphatic denial of advance information about the meeting and description of it
as **purely a coincidence**. Both assistance-related observations and that
contrary interpretation remain together.

The MFR says his Arabic was **limited**, the exchange was vague to him, and
Bayoumi sometimes translated. This qualifies use of him as an independent
witness to the conversation's content. It does not permit either treating him
as fluent or assuming that he understood no words whatsoever. Compare the
2016 FBI summary's reported inability to understand Arabic, rather than silently
choosing a language-proficiency description.

The memoir-like interval and spoken recollections are not a calibrated record
of minutes, advance knowledge or the conversation itself. The later two-minute
account, thirty-minute accounts and the coincidence belief must not be merged.

## RF003: earlier interview and sequence conflicts have exact targets

The official NOTES physical67, Chapter7 notes15-17, identifies the
**October8,2001 FBI interview of Isamu Dyson**, the April20,2004 Bin Don interview,
and Bayoumi's October16-17,2003 interview. Note17 expressly describes a change
between a recollection of prayer at the consulate before lunch plus one later
mosque visit, and the later account of both prayer sessions at the mosque.
The acquired MFR physical3-4 records mosque visits before and after the meal.
The October2001 interview has not been acquired here, so that side is still
known through the Commission's account of it.

The MFR physical1-3 places the trip in **January2000**, without establishing a
precise day. FBI2C5 physical14 says **approximately02/01/2000**. Neither is
silently converted to the other. Recollection precision, interview prompting,
calendar attribution and a substantive contradiction remain competing issues to
test using the original interviews and dated travel records.

These are specific consistency questions, not proof of a rehearsed story or an
arranged attack-related meeting. The witness's own coincidence belief remains.

## RF006: technical testimony, purpose and the court's own inference differ

P10199 paragraphs1900-1908 describes Schiff's horizon/line-of-sight interpretation,
pre-flight-planning use and an asserted relationship to50-70-mile calculations.
K9795 physical48-49 argues that the equation is commonplace and undated, invokes
Schiff deposition passages and **Moss report pp7,12-16**, and disputes the
relationship to attack flight paths. Both are advocacy accounts. A quoted
expert conclusion in a brief is not a new independent finding.

The newly acquired D11180 physical23-24 upholds the treatment of both aviation
experts. It accepts the construction of Moss's alternatives, describes Schiff
as unqualified to testify about Bayoumi's relationship with the hijackers, and
retains the reliability/helpfulness of his technical testimony subject to
limitations including credibility commentary. It notes neither side objected
to those portions of ECF10615. It does not exclude all of Schiff's testimony
or adjudicate the mathematical interpretation as proving an attack plan.

D11182 physical41 nevertheless makes a substantive preliminary inference about
Bayoumi's knowledge from the drawing and other evidence; physical45 expressly
leaves disputed meaning and credibility for merits determination. Judicial
inference, expert admissibility, an engineering calculation and historical
intent are different evidentiary propositions. Keeping all these records does
not require treating them as logically incompatible or selecting a controlling
historical explanation.

Highest-value missing inputs: complete Ex678M,679B,11N,678DD; Schiff's Ex148
and full Ex104 deposition; Moss's ECF9118-3 with exhibits; ECF10615; relevant
underlying flight/visibility assumptions and independent dating/context. No

## Limits and preservation

This is a source-recovery and dependency audit. It does not establish that the
men manufactured an attribution, that an agency knowingly facilitated the plot,
or that any identified actor participated in a WTC7 demolition operation. An
alternative explanation must be tested rather than presumed; a gap is not
affirmative evidence of its most incriminating possible cause.

Twenty-five selected page rasters were produced in the guarded Intel store.
The six-page MFR was read completely; selected other pages were inspected,
not every page in every acquired report. Source PDFs and earlier work remain
unchanged. The initial locator assumption treating another study's JSON page
field as zero-based was corrected after direct PDF checks showed it was
one-based. Earlier renders remain; correct pages were added. This repairs our
locator use without changing any source wording or resolving a historical conflict.

The public court and media sources include personal information. Original bytes
remain local; this report omits contact details. No legal-record promotion,
outreach, fee, deletion, commit, push or publication is represented as completed.
