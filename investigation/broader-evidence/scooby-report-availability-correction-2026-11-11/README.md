## A source-checked finding from the SEC report

A [May 11, 2004 9/11 Commission memorandum recording follow-up discussions with SEC officials Cella and Ribelin, PDF pp. 21–22](https://cryptome.org/nara/sec/sec-03-0916.pdf#page=21), says their reconciliation put total September 6, 2001 UAL put volume at 1,575 contracts, rather than the SEC report's earlier 2,075. The memorandum attributes the 500-contract discrepancy to a canceled sale that an SEC volume source included. This verifies what the contemporaneous Commission memorandum records the officials saying; it is not an independent reanalysis of the native OCC trade ledger.

The correction concerns **aggregate volume**. It does not by itself allocate the canceled transaction or establish that any named adviser bought exactly 1,500 contracts. The same follow-up records SEC officials' position that the discrepancies did not change their conclusion that the investigation found no illicit trading; that conclusion and the missing underlying account allocations should both be retained. The saved research report's broader numerical and portfolio claims remain to be independently checked, as do the other reports.

**Implication for the working theory:** An administrative volume correction is evidence about data quality and attribution. It neither proves foreknowledge nor conclusively resolves every historical trade. Identify account-level executions, cancellations, chronology and records establishing knowledge before drawing an actor-specific inference.
