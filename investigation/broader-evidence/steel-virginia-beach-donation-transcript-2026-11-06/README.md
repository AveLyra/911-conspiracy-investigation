# Virginia Beach steel donation account and installation records

November 6, 2026 UTC · STL02–STL03 / AUD011 / AUD016 / B188 / H005

The Virginia Department of Fire Programs published a timed transcript of a 2024 episode recorded at Virginia Beach's fire training center. The episode contains a participant's account of receiving the steel from a North Carolina metal artist. It gives the reported donation conditions, panel dimensions, a crane handling event and a plan for an upright display. It explicitly treats a floor attribution as unverified. This adds an intermediate custody lead and a way to test whether the later descriptions concern the same object. The donation agreement and item mark remain unacquired.
