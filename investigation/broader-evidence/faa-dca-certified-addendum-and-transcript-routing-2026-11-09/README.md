The face readings include slashed zeros. The whole-page OCR gave **ADVISORY NO. 84** on DCA-21 p. 2 line 10 and **ADVISORY NO- 84** on DCA-22 p. 1 line 8 for the visible **04** label. DCA-22 p. 1 line 11 OCR gives **ADVISORY NO-87** where the face gives **07**. The unmodified OCR observations and the visual transcriptions are preserved separately in the source pins.

On both faces, **07** carries **TMU111326LK**, **09** and **10** both carry **TMU111337HM**, and **16** carries **TMU111451HM**. The comparison mechanically confirms the same seven manually transcribed labels in different order. The shared code on 09 and 10 illustrates why the code alone cannot uniquely identify a message. The sequence and selected text matches establish corresponding displayed advisories. Their order leaves the native send order, arrival times and exact physical copy relationship open.
