# Library of Congress structural-steel collection lead

November 25, 2026 · steel custody, AUD011/AUD016. A Library of Congress account identifies an additional potential route to collection and transfer records for one reported World Trade Center steel section. It does not identify a WTC 7 specimen or connect the section to the W14A/W14B candidates.

## Source observations

- A [September 2002 Library of Congress Information Bulletin](https://www.loc.gov/loc/lcib/0209/debris.html), paragraphs beginning “Highsmith helped connect” and “Approximately 5 feet,” reports that its Prints and Photographs Division received a section described as approximately **400 pounds**, **5 feet by 1½ feet**, from the World Trade Center. It describes a connection through a photographer and officials responsible for recycling tower steel, and attributes an upper-story interpretation to a thickness-based assessment. The report does not provide the original item ID, source tower, exact floor, recycler records, or authenticated dimensional measurements.
- A [2014 Library blog account](https://blogs.loc.gov/loc/2014/02/curious-collections/), paragraph beginning “The heaviest,” also describes a 400-pound World Trade Center section in the same division and says it was stored off-site at that time. The accounts may describe the same object and share sources; no accession ID or inventory join has been obtained, so that relationship is a candidate, not verified independent corroboration or proof of current location.
- The 2002 account separately describes a large beam transported cross-country for a memorial project. The report does not establish that this was the section received by the Library. Keep those movements and objects distinct.

## What this does and does not test

The Library account is a contemporary institutional publication and a lead to a collection, not an original donor agreement, accession record, signed transfer, photograph-to-object chain, or laboratory record. The 2002 writer's attribution and physical description remain reported claims; do not infer building or floor from them. Nothing here links this object to FEMA W14A, W14B, C12, the NIST unidentified specimens, or any specific WTC 7 member. No mechanism, concealment or intent is established.

## Open records

- Check whether the 2002 section and 2014 item have a common unique identifier before joining the reports. Check whether any retained donor or original-location evidence supports or limits the reported tower/upper-floor attribution.
- Retain the W14A/W14B field and NIST inventory descriptions independently, and do not use this object to fill their custody gap.
