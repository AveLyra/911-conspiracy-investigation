# FAA Pittsburgh radar transfer labels and the NTSB study directory

Public derivative of the November 10, 2026 source review. B129 / PD01–PD04 and REC01 / REC03 acquisition. Three FAA inventories list the same Pittsburgh radar filename under the same date. The National Archives catalog lists that filename on the FAA's **AWA-715** disc. The NTSB's study lists a Pittsburgh input under a different name. The FAA records give a 2001 context for the **9/21** listing and a stronger inventory-to-catalog match. The original transmittal, NTSB receipt and study-file lineage remain open. An exact case-insensitive filename screen of the 12 AWA-715 UA93 names against the study's pp. 9–10 extracted text yielded **zero** matches. The native AWA-715 release record and the NTSB study CD remain separate targets.

### Other fields worth carrying forward

The selected voice rows on **AEA-2353 p. 7** give EWR **1204–1226 GC** and **1235–1247 LC**, with a blank tape field, `Partial` transcript, `9/13` received and `9/21` to NTSB. **AWA-606 p. 5** and **AEA-2354 p. 7** give `Tape`, `Partial`, `9/13 &26` and `9/27` for both rows. Preserve the distinct date and tape fields. The signed or logged receipt and actual tape and transcript identifiers would distinguish an additional tape shipment from a revision of the same transfer entry. The earlier `9/25 1530` sheet also lists `9/26` in other To NTSB cells. That is a reason to seek actual dispatch and receipt before treating all values as completed transfers.
