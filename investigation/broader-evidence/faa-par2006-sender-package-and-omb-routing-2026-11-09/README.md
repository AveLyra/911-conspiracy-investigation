# Archived DOT FY 2006 report and its posted components

Public derivative of the November 9, 2026 Eastern source review · B129 / PD03–PD05. An archived copy of DOT’s original contents page led to a complete-looking **412-page public FY 2006 Performance and Accountability Report (PAR)** PDF and three separately posted component PDFs. The full public PDF contains both scanned pages of the financial-audit management reply. The posted financial-audit component ends after the first page of that reply. This establishes a real difference between public packages. The House and Senate enclosures and the FAA case-specific disposition are separate records still to locate. ## Prior-year attribution discrepancy

The OIG semiannual report covering October 1, 2005–March 31, 2006, describes the November 15, 2005 nine-challenge report as incorporated into the **FY 2006 Performance and Accountability Report**. Its own report table identifies **PT-2006-007**, `11/15/05`. The standalone PT-2006-007 cover gives **November 15, 2005** and discusses challenges for **FY 2006**. The official FY **2005** DOT PAR reader also prints PT-2006-007 beneath its own FY 2005 running title. Preserve the semiannual statement and the observed FY 2005 placement. The full archived FY 2006 report instead has a separately identified **PT-2007-004**, with challenges for FY **2007**. This comparison leaves the earlier statement’s intended reference and any additional circulation unresolved. The OIG landing page separately gives **November 18, 2005** as the public release date of PT-2006-007. ## Sender and receipt leads

- **DOT’s reporting packet.** The July 2006 OMB circular describes a draft to the Office of Federal Financial Management and the appropriate Resource Management Office ten working days before final issuance. It describes signed electronic finals through the CFO route, delivery to those OMB offices, Treasury, Financial Management Service and GAO, and four hard copies to OMB. These describe contemporaneous sender and receiving records to seek, separate from the two congressional referrals. The circular establishes instructions. The actual draft, approved final, sending or receiving evidence and version identifiers remain open. [OMB A-136, physical pp. 9–10, p. 9 lines 19–27 and p. 10 lines 26–33](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/sites/default/files/omb/assets/omb/circulars/a136/a136_revised_2006.pdf#page=9)
- **FAA’s agency-side controls.** The FAA report recapture at a second official URL has the same hash as the October 29 preserved copy. Its Administrator’s message is dated **November 3, 2006**. The PDF title contains `v6 12-14-2006.doc` and its creation metadata has a separate December 21 date. At p. 47, FAA reports a September 30 statement of assurance and monthly corrective-action reviews sent to DOT’s Office of the Secretary. At p. 49 it describes an Audit Coordination Committee in the financial statement audit section. The reported September 30 memorandum, monthly status reports and committee action records are concrete controls to check against case-specific correspondence. The described financial-control process has no established join to CC-2006-085.
