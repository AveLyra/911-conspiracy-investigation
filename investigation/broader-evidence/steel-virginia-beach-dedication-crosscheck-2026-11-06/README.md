# Virginia Beach dedication records and the installed steel panel

November 6, 2026 UTC · STL02–STL03 / AUD011 / AUD016 / B188 / H005

WTKR's original September 2025 dedication report explicitly attributes the installed steel to the **South Tower's north face, floors 55–58**, and reports a **2024 donation by an artist from Raleigh, North Carolina**. WAVY's September 2026 ceremony report describes **North Tower** steel. Published photographs from the two articles show the same distinctive arrangement of openings, damaged plate edges and a painted mark on the upright panel. The visual comparison substantially strengthens the link between the two published descriptions of the installed panel. Its source building remains unresolved. [WTKR dedication report](https://www.wtkr.com/news/in-the-community/virginia-beach/9-11-memorial-made-of-steel-from-world-trade-center-dedicated-in-virginia-beach) · [WAVY report syndicated by AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/virginia-beach-fire-honors-9-172521000.html)

## Published photo comparison

The WTKR article's `wtkr-12.img` and WAVY gallery's `wavy-05.jpeg` show an upright panel with three vertical members and three horizontal plates. On the pictured face, both show a dark roughly square opening near the middle plate's center and a jagged area at its right end. The broad lower plate in both has a dark ragged opening near the center, a separate damaged area to the right, and pale two-line lettering near its lower edge. These landmarks are on the panel rather than the plaque, flag, concrete base, or surroundings. WTKR's other views and WAVY's wider views provide location context. The WAVY gallery also includes other memorial objects, so those images cannot be assigned to the steel panel by gallery membership alone. [WTKR source images](https://www.wtkr.com/news/in-the-community/virginia-beach/9-11-memorial-made-of-steel-from-world-trade-center-dedicated-in-virginia-beach) · [WAVY image 05](https://hermes.media.static.aol.com/media/2026/09/12/f50a1d76-65c8-3569-b40d-e81729c074c5/750b3a03-3839-4ae8-82f9-b5923e7594aa.jpeg)

The match is a visual comparison of published views. It does not measure dimensions, authenticate the paint, read every character, or link the steel back to the donor's prior custody. The paint is visible at the same location. Its full text and separators remain unverified at the available resolutions. The complete WTKR broadcast was not acquired. The public dedication article and its images were acquired through the publisher's separate article route. The three-member form and venue are compatible with the 2024 description, while the 2024 panel lacks an identified photo or receipt for a direct join. No reviewed source establishes a WTC 7 specimen link.
