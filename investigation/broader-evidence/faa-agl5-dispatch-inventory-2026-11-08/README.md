## An underlying custody record and two limits on the join

1. **Item 35 and AGL-65.** AGL-5 p. 9, OCR lines 16–17, describes a faxed copy of a **9/12/01** custody memo and attributes it to Wayne Pierce. AGL-65 is a one-page faxed form dated **9-12-01** headed **Chain of Custody of Official Government Records**. Its signature area is consistent with the inventory attribution. The handwritten list describes original voice recordings relating to UAL93 along with other material and includes **4B 1344–1650**. The name, date, subject and fax format support a strong document candidate. The two physical copies listed by item 35 and the complete transfer history remain unverified.
2. **Reel label across two dates.** The retained October 8, 2003 ZOB-278 custody form carries a handwritten **4B 1344–1650** reading. Preserve it beside AGL-65's September 2001 label. This is a repeated reel-and-time description across two forms. An item identifier, transfer and return sequence and position-to-track register would test physical continuity and relation to later re-recordings. [ZOB-278](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/7597400)
3. **The September 25 email time.** UAL93 item **2** prints **9/25/01 @12:26pm**, consistent with the earlier read of ZOB-244 and the nested email in ZOB-261. ZOB-261's enclosing reply still displays **12:02 PM**. The inventory is a separate description of a candidate message. It supplies neither an independent clock nor proof of website upload. Preserve the original displayed headers, their absence of clock validation, and the separate later reply.
