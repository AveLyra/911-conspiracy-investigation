expected action coverage, public-link/path checks, payload disclosure scan,
Git diff/whitespace review, commit-range identity and exact staged-file review.

hypothesis. No PDF was authored; layout checks are not applicable.

The exact text diff against the preceding public main is retained locally for
review. No unpublished preparation branch or unrelated local files are part of
this change. No existing history is rewritten.
