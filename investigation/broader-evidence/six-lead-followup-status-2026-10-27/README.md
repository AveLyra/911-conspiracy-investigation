## Bounded follow-up targets

### Recorder

The NTSB request targets the attachments `DCA01MA064_tabular.csv`,
`D226A101-3G.pdf` and `757-3b_1.txt` identified in the
[official AA77 factual report](https://www.ntsb.gov/about/Documents/AAL77_fdr.pdf),
the separate Time Correlation Report, original/intermediate readouts and exports,
retained configuration/error logs, applicable installed-format and failed-page
rules, custody and identification records, and product transmittals/receipts.
A public companion label list is not the missing telemetry or original agency
CSV; see the [published companion qualification](../recorder-companion-source-2026-10-27/README.md).
A successful derivative reproduction does not authenticate aircraft identity,
recovery history, sensor validity or the agency's processing decisions.

### Trading

The SEC request targets the May 15, 2002 Enforcement memorandum
ENF02-13 / HO-9325 and its missing appendices/support; the original newsletter,
amendments and delivery evidence held by the agency; existing order/execution/
cancellation/allocation and exposure records; the May 2004 fax/OCC reconciliation;
and referral responses and recorded dispositions. Existing releasable linkage
codes and field definitions are sought, without demanding a newly invented
customer database or coding system. The
[released memorandum facsimile](https://unredacted.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/04/9-11-sec-report.pdf)
is a source locator, not proof of completeness or of the explanation's causal share.

Matching post-receipt decisions and orders would strengthen the newsletter
explanation for the linked subset. Mismatches would weaken a specified version
of that explanation; earlier ordinary information, independent analysis and
ignored recommendation limits remain alternatives. A mismatch alone does not
establish advance knowledge of the attacks.

### Materials

The unsent data inquiry requests native spectra and maps, quantification basis,
standards/corrections, geometry/support, same-region phase evidence and the
specimen-to-DSC/run crosswalk. The Harrit and Millette reports concern different
specimens and preparations; neither may be silently treated as a matched test
of the other's exact material. Preserve the affirmative analytical evidence
and contrary observations in both. Conditional elemental arithmetic does not
uniquely identify metallic aluminum, and a confirmed energetic component would
still need independent building, timing and operational attribution.

### Windows/audio and models

Received human judgments are retained. Reported bangs are observations to test;
speech describing an explosion is not an inventory of actual explosive events.
A/B/C excerpts share compilation lineage, while the comparator concerns another
building. Sample-clock arithmetic and waveform extrema are not original event
clocks or calibrated pressure. Approximate window outlines do not measure open
area or establish breakage time/cause.

For the model, prioritize the exact input/run/output and production crosswalk.
Missing historical dependencies are existing-record targets, not permission to
invent parameters or repair an executed baseline. Existing request coverage
must be checked before another request is proposed. A surrogate fit is not a
historical replay, and an unresolved input does not establish a false collapse
explanation. No existing request scope or legal position is changed here.
