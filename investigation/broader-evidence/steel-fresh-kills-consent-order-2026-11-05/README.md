# Fresh Kills consent-order trail and separate closeout dates

November 5, 2026. This study follows the operational closeout TODO from the [Fresh Kills steel accounting review](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/00eda5b68c42bdfbd854d89f07f400bdb256df79/investigation/broader-evidence/steel-fresh-kills-municipal-accounting-2026-11-05/README.md). It locates the legal authority for continued disposal of World Trade Center debris at Section 1/9, the case identifier for the consent order, and the specific records to check against the reported end dates.

## Authority and exact record identifiers

- **Executive Order No. 113.44, dated December 28, 2001.** The text temporarily suspended Environmental Conservation Law § 27-0706(b) to allow disposal of debris removed from the WTC site at Fresh Kills Section 1/9 after January 1, 2002. The codified text appears in the Cornell reproduction of 9 NYCRR § 5.113, web lines 894–904. The official DSNY plan attachment independently describes the same order and its connection to the consent order. The signed governor's instrument and contemporaneous State Register page remain open. [Codified text](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-york/9-NYCRR-5.113) · [DSNY September 2006 plan, Attachment X, printed p. 1](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/dsny/docs/about_swmp_attach10_0815.pdf)
- **DEC case D2-90001-89-03, Modification No. 8 to the 1990 Order on Consent.** The city's September 23, 2016 request states that No. 8 was executed and effective January 17, 2002. It identifies Appendix A-15 and cites a **January 4, 2002 Final Cover Design Report**, milestone subject 6. The state's annotated draft of No. 10 identifies No. 8 as Appendix F and says it changed Paragraph G of No. 7 and Appendix A-15 milestone 8. The acquired draft exhibit PDFs refer to Appendix F and end with their draft narratives. The two inspected containers have zero embedded files. The city publishes a locator for a larger No. 10 compilation with appendices. Direct retrieval of that compilation returned 403 and the indexed passages did not establish that the complete signed No. 8 was available for inspection. [City request, PDF physical p. 3, printed p. 3](https://extapps.dec.ny.gov/docs/permits_ej_operations_pdf/fkltr9232016.pdf#page=3) · [Annotated draft, ¶34 and Schedule of Amendments](https://extapps.dec.ny.gov/docs/permits_ej_operations_pdf/fkexhibit2.pdf#page=11) · [City compilation locator](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/dsny/docs/SWM-Freshkills/223_FKL_OrderOnConsent_Mod10.pdf)

The annotated draft's text layer gives **January 1, 2002** at ¶34 and at the milestone 8 description. The unannotated scan's text layer yields **“January 7,2002”** in those positions, although it yields January 1 elsewhere on its ¶34 page. The city request separately states January 1 for the disposal cutoff and January 17 for execution. Preserve the unannotated text reading as a possible recognition artifact pending a page-face check. The current record establishes a text-layer difference. Whether the printed dates differ remains open.
