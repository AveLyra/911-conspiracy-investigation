# Cape recording comparison: reproducible waveform search

2026-11-25. The FAA archive's [K90-30 recording](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/b-Voice-Recordings/5-K90-30-Sup-Console-1229-1253-UTC%3B-Flight-Data-1229-1253-UTC%3B-High-AR-1247-1259-UTC.mp3) was acquired to the verified media store to test the [candidate K90-39 transcript alignment](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/799c5146af5942869456902cf23c3fc07539ea54/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-cape-audio-transcript-candidate-2026-11-25/README.md). The result is **inconclusive**: the search did not demonstrate that the two recordings contain the same passage or that they do not.

## Method and result

The source labels describe K90-30 as including High AR **1247–1259 UTC** and [K90-39](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-Miscellaneous/b-Voice-Recordings/5-K90-39-High-AR-1230-1400-UTC.mp3) as High AR **1230–1400 UTC**. These are catalog labels, not verified time coverage or independent-recording evidence. Prior automated recognition suggested a possible transcript correspondence near **1253 UTC** in K90-39; the words have not been verified by listening.

The accompanying [test script](test_match.py) decodes both files to mono 2 kHz audio using FFmpeg, takes a **10-second K90-39 window beginning at file offset 1473 seconds**, and searches all of K90-30 using mean-centered normalized waveform correlation. The highest absolute score was **+0.220343** at **3475.715 seconds** into K90-30; the next two non-overlapping peaks were **+0.171314 at 2963.3985 seconds** and **+0.145014 at 3309.5825 seconds**. Repeating the same run produced the same printed values. A trivial identical-array self-correlation was **1.0**; this checks arithmetic on identical samples, not sensitivity to different tape generations, time drift, channel mixes or encoding.

A separate exploratory tiny.en speech-recognition pass on a 40-second K90-30 excerpt starting at **3470 seconds** suggested an announcement concerning a **1247–1259 UTC** High Arrival re-recording. That output is unverified; no human listened, and a partial transcript does not determine whether the correlation peak is a false positive. No threshold or negative-control distribution was established, no speed correction was applied, and no real-world time is assigned to the waveform peaks. The score alone cannot support a match. Different source generations, polarity, speeds, edits or channel composition could weaken waveform correlation; a chance or common sound could produce a peak. No implication about intent, alteration, absence or completeness follows.

## Reproduce and next test
