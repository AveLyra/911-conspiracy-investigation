# FAA ADA-30 page comparison and the 09:20 public correction

November 5, 2026 · B129 / PD01 / PD04 · Public derivative of the separately preserved source study

This comparison advances the source check behind the FAA's October 2004 public chronology edit. The test was whether the preserved FAA page images distinguish a request to set up a line, notification of a military command and receipt of the aircraft-specific message described in the earlier public chronology. It uses the public scans pinned in NR209 and the previously preserved FAA web versions in NR208. The correspondence between the 2004 editor and any specific underlying record remains open.

The **08:50 EDT** conversion, the **0905** source label and the **0920** rows remain separate observations. The labels and named parties differ. Their relationship needs the original definitions and bridge records. On the page itself, the 09:20 text distinguishes a request and a notification. A handwritten statement about the line remaining open leaves actual join time, listening and relay unresolved. The 09:24 radar report supplies a separate proposition from the published NEADS hijacking-notification claim. AGL ROC is an FAA regional operations center. NMCC and NEADS are different recipients.

## Comparison with the edited public chronology

The June 2004 archived fact-sheet row at raw HTML line **104** says **0924**, naming **“NORAD’s Northeast Air Defense Sector”** and asserting notice of **“the suspected hijacking of American Flight 77”** plus an open line to discuss American 77 and United 93. The November 2004 archive at lines **109–111** says **0920** and describes a phone line with other government agencies and the military to share information on missing or suspicious aircraft. The row marks the revision **10/21/04**. Preserve both texts and their separate source pins. The earlier entry's subject and named recipient are absent from the replacement. The visible FAA fax provides a candidate source for the replacement's 09:20 time. It supplies no approval trail tying the entry to that fax, and the 09:24 text on the fax does not establish who received an American 77 message.

[Earlier FAA fact sheet](https://web.archive.org/web/20040626232523/http://www.faa.gov:80/Newsroom/factsheets/2002/factsheets_020812.htm) · [Revised FAA fact sheet](https://web.archive.org/web/20041101003838/http://faa.gov/newsroom/factsheets/2002/factsheets_020812.htm) · [DOT OIG CC-2006-085, printed p. 8](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf)

The Commission cites **Chronology ADA-30** for timing of the FAA conference in note 188 and for FAA representatives joining the separate White House video conference at **9:40** in note 189. The institutional copy's 0940 remark is masked. AWA-612 p. 2 records **“Computer room activated.”** at **0940**. Preserve both locators. The fax record and the excised chronology have different visible text and page structure, and this check cannot identify the exact page or version the Commission used for note 189. A shared time label alone leaves the two activities and sources distinct. ## Verification and open records

The three PDFs matched prior SHA-256 and page-count pins. An in-memory OCR pass covered all **25** pages of the excised copy and selected fax pages. OCR served as a locator. The readings above were checked on excised p. 1, AWA-612 pp. 1–2 and AWA-659 pp. 7 and 9. Rendered page images remained in memory. The underlying source files and the earlier study remain preserved. No redactions were reconstructed, and no audio was examined.
