# A signed DOT management response in a separate FAA investigation

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD02–PD04. The July 11, 2003 release for **CC-2003-123**, an inquiry into FAA assistance in locating an aircraft, contains a dated, signed DOT General Counsel memorandum to the Inspector General. The memorandum records concurrence and identifies proposed responses and responsible offices. This is a concrete example of where a DOT and FAA management response can appear in a public investigation packet. **CC-2003-123 and the 9/11 Commission referral CC-2006-085 are separate cases.** [OIG cover letter](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/lieblet.pdf#page=1) · [General Counsel memorandum](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/lieblet.pdf#page=21) · [Commission referral report](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/cc2006085.pdf#page=12)

The public packet supplies the sender, recipient, date and subject of a management response as well as a signed image of the response's first page. The testimony supplies a later report of completion for one action. The policy citations and surviving successor order are a separate trail. These records do not establish a tracker closure date, actual distribution of the July 2003 order or delivery of the planned study. The House and DHS descriptions can share underlying FAA records.

## What to look for in CC-2006-085

- [ ] **PD02.** Look for a dated management response to the Inspector General in the DOT General Counsel, Secretary and FAA Administrator correspondence and in OIG release enclosures. A subject may name the remedial action or report recipient instead of using **CC-2006-085**. Establish the actual sender, recipient and linkage from the record. The 2003 example establishes no office assignment for 2006.
- [ ] **PD03–PD04.** Look separately for the transmittal and acknowledgment, the document recording consideration or final disposition, any subsequent evidence of implementation, and the recommendation-specific tracking entry. Use original file or enclosure IDs to test the joins. The 2003 example supplies no rule requiring a public enclosure or a particular disposition in the later case.
- [ ] **PD01.** The actual corrected May 2003 QFR, its approval and delivery evidence remain open. A general statement of concurrence or completed corrective action would need to be tied to the actual corrected text and its recipient.
