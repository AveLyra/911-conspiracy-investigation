# W14 structural descriptions: a limit on candidate attribution

AUD011/AUD016 · B188/H005 · November 25, 2026. This bounded source comparison tests whether a W14-series description alone can identify the original building of a recovered piece.

## Source observations

- FEMA's [WTC 3 chapter](https://www.fema.gov/pdf/library/fema403_ch3.pdf), physical page 2 / printed 3-2, says WTC 3's steel columns were standard W14-series wide-flange shapes throughout, up to W14×500 at the second floor. - FEMA's [WTC 7 chapter](https://www.fema.gov/pdf/library/fema403_ch5.pdf), physical page 4 / printed 5-4, says the diagonals of its described Truss 1 were W14 shapes. Downloaded November 25, 2026; PDF SHA-256 `8f1f4dd1dd419c7fa2877e20dc8f3c152da0887e85c604e5cf8730a9fd554f22`.
- FEMA's [field table](https://www.fema.gov/pdf/library/fema403_apd.pdf#page=16) describes A/W14A and B/W14B as WTC#7 candidates. NIST's [inventory](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar1-3b.pdf#page=96) lists W-14A or A and W-14B as unidentified wide-flange sections. Preserve both accounts and their provenance as unresolved; the [existing comparison](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/905c696056973d9121206ff56e7df5afa13a4e52/investigation/broader-evidence/steel-attribution-roots-2026-10-27/README.md) records their separate identifiers and field measurements.

## Interpretation and limits

A generic W14-series designation alone would not distinguish WTC 3 from WTC 7: FEMA reports such shapes in both. These are different described structural uses—WTC 3 columns and WTC 7 Truss 1 diagonals. The field codes W14A/B are not by themselves verified section designations, and this comparison does not establish that either candidate came from WTC 3, from the WTC 7 truss, or from any particular member. W14×500 is an upper example in the WTC 3 description, not an identified candidate section. WTC 3 is an alternative building to investigate, not a conclusion or an exhaustive list of alternatives.

FEMA's field attribution may rest on location, observations, markings, connections or records beyond a generic section family; this check does not test that evidence. NIST's unidentified classification is likewise not an explanation of its decision. The descriptions could be narrowed by original photographs, full measured geometry and connection details, drawings, identifying marks and documented recovery/custody; none was newly matched here. No concealment, motive, physical loss or cause is inferred.
