## What the records establish about the agency's account

- **Retention limits the sample.** The August 5, 2011 response, **2010-003775**, reports five surviving denial replies and says processed radar was routinely released. The letter describes a selection of replies that had not reached their records-destruction date; the actual query was not acquired. Five is the agency's reported retained subset, not the total historical number of radar requests or denials. [Reply, physical p2, lines13-22](https://www.governmentattic.org/5docs/FAA-RadarDataDenials_2009-2010.pdf#page=2).
- **Individual replies describe mixed outcomes.** **2009-006494** and **2009-006996** report enclosing Houston terminal-radar CDs while withholding Houston ARTCC ERIT material for 2009 events. The CDs, actual files and delivery receipts were not acquired. The five selected letter faces concern2009/2010 requests, not an identified 2007 AA77 response. [Physical p7](https://www.governmentattic.org/5docs/FAA-RadarDataDenials_2009-2010.pdf#page=7), lines17-27; [p10](https://www.governmentattic.org/5docs/FAA-RadarDataDenials_2009-2010.pdf#page=10), lines12-32.
- **The category matters.** December 8, 2010 guidance pairs an ERIT withholding statement with release of specified CDR target/flight-plan material, assigning review to facility QA and Technical Operations. An analysis in the packet describes ERIT as uncertified/uncorrected and favors NTAP; that preference is an agency assertion, not our independent reliability test. An internal discussion acknowledges uncertainty about filtering scope and workload. [Guidance, pp3-4](https://www.governmentattic.org/5docs/FAA-ERIT-releases_2005-2010U.pdf#page=3); [analysis, p19](https://www.governmentattic.org/5docs/FAA-ERIT-releases_2005-2010U.pdf#page=19); [discussion, p12](https://www.governmentattic.org/5docs/FAA-ERIT-releases_2005-2010U.pdf#page=12).

The separately released December 2, 2005 memorandum addresses a described radar-system database. Its historical withholding advice and the later guidance remain separate in scope and date. No current legal validity, case applicability, technical equivalence or motive is adjudicated here. [Memo, physical pp3-4](https://www.governmentattic.org/5docs/FAA-SensitiveRadar_2005.pdf#page=3).

## Conflicting dates and source versions remain preserved

The September 1, 2011 letter **2010-003775R**, physical p58, describes the appeal as dated **August 26, 2011** at lines11-14 and **August 6, 2011** at lines20-22. Neither date is selected or explained away. The original appeal and intake records are missing. Its disclosed outcome concerns redactions in the response letters; it does not establish that raw radar was released. [Both passages](https://www.governmentattic.org/5docs/FAA-ERIT-releases_2005-2010U.pdf#page=58).

The January 11 response's **49** responsive-page statement, the combined packet's **66** physical pages and its separately marked later supplement retain their own units and provenance. Reprints and multiple agency accounts are not independent corroboration of the same data. [Literal observations and all source hashes](SOURCE-PINS.json).
