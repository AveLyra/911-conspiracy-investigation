Public derivative of the November 6, 2026 native steel review · STL02–STL03 / AUD011 / AUD016 / B188 / H005. NIST's mechanical-properties report assigns a flange specimen to WTC 2 column **301**. Its separate connector appendix says column **301** was not recovered. The records identify a specific panel and coupon series for checking the scope of each statement against the original cutting and lab records.

**What remains unresolved.** A flange fragment may have been available while a connector-bearing column section was unavailable. A difference in record scope, a separate fragment, or a mistaken label are untested possibilities. The available pages cannot decide among them. The test rows are documentary evidence of reported tests and supply concrete sample IDs. They do not establish that any of the samples was among the three column sections NIST reported transferring to Boulder by July 11, 2002. The `B` location code remains undefined in the inventory key. The diagram and test/connector tables are publications by the same investigation and may share earlier identifications. ## Values preserved from the test report

- **Room-temperature elongation.** The prose gives **16% and 17%** for C14-C1-RF. The four Table A–1 elongations are **16%, 17%, 15%, 17%** for **L1, L2, T1, T2**, respectively, at a two-inch gauge length. The first two match the two longitudinal rows. The prose does not identify the individual runs there. Preserve both scopes pending the lab worksheet. [Prose, PDF p. 78 lines 21–26](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=101021#page=78) · [Table, PDF p. 201](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=101021#page=201)
- **Spandrel fit.** C14-SP longitudinal tensile-strength strain-rate coefficient `m` is **0.0108 ± 0.0015** in Table 4–1 and **0.0109 ± 0.0015** in Figure A–61. Both values were read from page images. The difference is **0.0001**. The supplied pages leave the fit inputs and cause of the difference open. No refit was performed. [Table, PDF p. 119 lines 58–67](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=101021#page=119) · [Plot, PDF p. 259 lines 51–57](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=101021#page=259)
