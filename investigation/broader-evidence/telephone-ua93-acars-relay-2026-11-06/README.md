# Flight 93 maintenance message and operations-center relay

November 6, 2026 UTC · TEL02–TEL04

A United flight-message printout in the Commission's Team 7 files preserves two different minute values for the maintenance desk’s query to Flight 93. The Commission staff report gives a separate **9:45–9:50 a.m.** window for the operations manager receiving a phone relay from maintenance. The printout adds an aircraft-specific message and log locator. The exact relay time and the order of the message and relay remain open. ## Conditional clock check

NASA's ACARS reference describes a day-hour-minute timestamp that can follow the sender address. If both numeric groups are read as September 11 UTC minute labels, they correspond to **13:46 UTC / 9:46 EDT** and **13:47 UTC / 9:47 EDT**. These are adjacent minute labels. The display supplies no seconds, and the United-specific meaning of the second field is unverified. Both nominal minutes fall within the staff report's relay window. The overlap gives no ordering between the maintenance query and Chicago receiving the telephone relay. [NASA/CR–2000-209922, printed p. 9, table 2-1 and line 2](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20000052926/downloads/20000052926.pdf)

The flight message shows a ground-side inquiry about a reported incident. The specific report that prompted it, delivery or cockpit receipt, any reply, and the personnel who received the telephone relay at particular times remain unverified. The written hearing statement places the maintenance desk's receipt of the attendant's call at **9:35** and describes an onward report. That is a different leg. The previous review preserves the competing desk-time accounts and call rows.
