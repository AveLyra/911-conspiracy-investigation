## Reproducible arithmetic, not a resolution

**269 seconds = 4 minutes 29 seconds.** Numerically, **9:53:43** falls in the 9:53 minute and is 17 seconds before 9:54. Therefore both minute labels are arithmetically compatible with possible different display conventions. Whether either publisher used truncation, rounding, a different clock, a different event or another procedure is **unknown**. The sources' apparent conflict remains open; this calculation does not validate the briefing row, establish the call's time or show how either page was prepared.
