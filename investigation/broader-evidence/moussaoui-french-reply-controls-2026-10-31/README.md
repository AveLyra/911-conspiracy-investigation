## What exhibit 346 adds

The [three-page August 22 EC](https://coop.vaed.uscourts.gov/moussaoui/exhibits/defense/346.pdf#page=1) is an FBI representation of information attributed to French sources, rather than the request or article seen in 336/337. Page 1 has an August 22, 2001 date, a Paris origin, attention listings including Harry Samit and Mike Maltbie, a reference to Serial 1, and administrative references to August 16/17 calls and an August 22 fax. The case-number area bears a handwritten serial-looking 10. Printed approval/drafting fields and ink are preserved but are not independently authenticated signatures, upload records or recipient acknowledgments. Pins FR01–FR02.

[Page 2](https://coop.vaed.uscourts.gov/moussaoui/exhibits/defense/346.pdf#page=2) reports a multi-step association: a masked person reportedly worked for Khattab in Chechnya; a subsequent interview reportedly said someone had been recruited to go to Chechnya by Moussaoui and described him as dangerous. Identities and parts of the source chain remain redacted. This is substantive reporting to investigate, **not independent proof of the interview statements or an explicit, independently demonstrated bin Laden/al-Qaeda connection**. The readable passage does not itself establish a specific hijacking plan. No masked person is identified by combining this account with the similar biography in 337. Pin FR02.

Pages 2–3 request passport travel-page photographs and a photograph of the second subject for further examination. These are requested inputs, not completed examination, delivery or receipt. The memo says no further information was provided at that time. The original fax, source interview and enclosure/distribution package remain missing. Pins FR01–FR03.

**What this changes:** the earlier controls were insufficient to identify the underlying French intelligence; 346 now supplies an actual attributed substantive account. Its pre-August-24 face date and named attention fields make exact receipt and inclusion in the later review package important tests. **What it does not change:** naming a recipient does not prove reading, and a redacted reported association does not settle the legal-sufficiency dispute or authenticate every asserted relationship.

## What 59B and 642 add

The [seven-page 59B court copy](https://coop.vaed.uscourts.gov/moussaoui/exhibits/defense/059B.pdf#page=1) has an EC dated August 30, 2001. Page 1 expressly references case 199M-MP-60130, Serial 10; it also describes information received August 29, an August 27 telephone contact, an August 22 fax at 1:10 PM and an August 30 Abbott–Maltbie telephone conversation. The enclosure field includes an original-serial-16 reference. These are more specific retrieval keys than subject/date resemblance, but neither the handwritten 10 in 346 nor the later serial reference independently authenticates a native package or proves delivery. Pin FR04.

The translated block in 59B, pages 2–4, includes an interview dated September 28, 2000, descriptions of associations and radicalization, unfavorable source characterizations, and limits on recent direct knowledge. The later EC's August 29, 2001 receipt claim is distinct from the much earlier interview date. **Do not backdate availability of the supplemental information to August 22 or August 24 merely because it describes earlier events.** No original French interview/fax or qualified translation comparison was obtained. Redacted identities, kinship and agency/source affiliations are not reconstructed from differences in masking. Pins FR05–FR06.

[642, physical page 1](https://coop.vaed.uscourts.gov/moussaoui/exhibits/defense/642.pdf#page=1), shows Abbott's email to Maltbie and Samit, displayed Thursday, August 30, 2001, 11:32 AM. It describes the attached EC and French information. Its postscript expressly calls the attachment a **draft**, characterizes the translation as rough but sufficiently accurate, and says upload will occur **Friday or Tuesday**. This is an author's representation, not an authenticated native send timestamp, a certified translation, a completed upload or proof of recipient reading. The retained EC representations have formal drafting/approval fields; that appearance and the explicit draft warning must both remain in the record. Pin FR07.

The substantive new version question is therefore **which attachment and translation became the uploaded/final record, when, and which version each recipient or reviewer received**. Neither court copy has been selected as controlling. Different display dates, formal fields, signatures, production labels and page layouts are not silently normalized into a finality or custody narrative. The native message/attachment, original French text, final upload and acknowledged recipient copies remain TODOs.

## Information sharing and the proposed France option

The source-protection paragraph in [59B page 4](https://coop.vaed.uscourts.gov/moussaoui/exhibits/defense/059B.pdf#page=4) permits use while requiring that the information's origin be protected. The end of that page and [page 5](https://coop.vaed.uscourts.gov/moussaoui/exhibits/defense/059B.pdf#page=5) additionally permit dissemination to British authorities as necessary under the same restriction. Corresponding passages appear in [642 pages 5–6](https://coop.vaed.uscourts.gov/moussaoui/exhibits/defense/642.pdf#page=5); its cover email also reports British-sharing authority. Preserve the restriction **and** permission together. These passages do not support a blanket claim that all onward sharing was prohibited; they also do not prove anyone actually shared the complete packet. Pin FR08.

59B page 4 and 642 page 6 report interest in exploring Moussaoui's deportation/transport to France and possible further custody/questioning. They describe a U.S. law-enforcement escort and transport of the laptop, mobile phone and other pertinent evidence to French authorities. Both say that evidence should **not be returned to Moussaoui** during the trip. Further arrangements are expressly conditional on the FBI pursuing that course. The leads in 59B page 6 and 642 page 7 ask Minneapolis, with headquarters concurrence/assistance, whether it wants to explore the option; other offices receive read-and-clear leads. Neither this wording nor the presence of leads proves a completed transfer, search, evidence handover, closed investigation or order to stop work. Pins FR09–FR10.

This is a specific alternative-action lead worth tracing. Actual recommendations, responses, approvals/refusals, legal authority, transport/custody records and examination results would distinguish an explored option from an executed or rejected one. None was obtained here. The proposal weighs against a blanket assertion that nobody considered further investigative avenues, while leaving adequacy and follow-through unresolved.

## Interpretation, confidence and next tests

**High confidence, confined to the readable court representations:** 346 contains substantive source-attributed reporting; 59B identifies later supplement/serial/call/fax pointers; 642 explicitly calls its attachment a draft; permission/protection and conditional investigative proposals coexist; both selected custody clauses contain the negation. These observations do not authenticate native documents or establish the truth of the underlying allegations.

**Limited inference:** the record supports a more specific inquiry into who received which intelligence and draft version before particular decisions. It does not yet show knowing omission, deliberate dilution, coordination or attack foreknowledge. To strengthen such an interpretation one would need a matched receipt/version/decision chain showing material received information knowingly omitted or misrepresented. A tentative or inaccurate source, a different actual review package, inclusion elsewhere, ordinary draft revisions or substantive follow-through could weaken it. These are untested alternatives, not explanations selected to resolve the record.
