## Conflicting fields and compilation boundaries

- **FOIA-2011-00131.** The request description prints **9/11/2011** for reports concerning Flights 93 and 77. In the same row the accident-date field prints **9/11/2001**, and the closed-date field prints **09/06/2011**. Preserve all three fields. The submitted request and disposition could show whether the divergence originated in the request or in its log description. No determination was made. [PDF p. 69, printed 25/67](https://www.governmentattic.org/12docs/FOIA_Logs_NTSB_2008-2012.pdf#page=69)
- **Letter versus bound copy.** The December 5, 2012 letter for **FOIA-2012-00232** describes a requests report from **9/1/2008 and 12/5/2012 (7 Pages)**. The 111-page public PDF separately contains two report title blocks dated **04/02/2013**. They describe requested-date periods **01/01/2008 and 12/31/2009** and **01/01/2010 and 12/31/2012** with printed totals of **40** and **67** pages. Physical pp. 3–4 are two images of printed 1/40. The relationship between the seven-page enclosure and the later reports in the bound copy remains open. Seek the original seven pages and the packaging or transmittal for the later reports. [Letter p. 2](https://www.governmentattic.org/12docs/FOIA_Logs_NTSB_2008-2012.pdf#page=2) · [Report title blocks pp. 3 and 45](https://www.governmentattic.org/12docs/FOIA_Logs_NTSB_2008-2012.pdf#page=45)
