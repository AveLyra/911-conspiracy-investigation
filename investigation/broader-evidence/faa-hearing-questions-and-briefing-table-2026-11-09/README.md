November 9, 2026 · B129 / PD01 / PD07. Three released Commission scans connect an FAA briefing-table copy, draft hearing questions and the March 31, 2004 interview they cite. ## What the briefing packet records

Hyde Box 7 tab 7-22, item 573, physical p. 1 says the document was received in response to a subpoena. Staff state their belief that it was created in the weeks after September 11. Physical pp. 2–3 carry Commission fax **No. 2788** and the visible **May 25, 2004, 4:19 PM** header. P. 2's embedded text reads **`MAY. 2b. 2004`** and **`4 M 9 P M`**. The fax dates the reproduced packet. The original version and its circulation before the May 2003 hearing require a dated source or delivery record. ## What the March 31 interview adds

- **Log possession and use.** The draft p. 7 says witnesses told staff Griffith received a copy of the log. The interview memo p. 11 lines 29–33 records a comparison with Griffith of the briefing and one original log. P. 12 lines 6–10 reports that logs were given to FAA and that Griffith expressed confidence the military received information despite unknown times in the log discussed. An agency receipt, a copy discussed in an interview and an individual's receipt are different claims.
- **Coordination and uncertainty.** Memo p. 11 lines 4–9 and 34–43 describes multiple summary iterations, information exchange with Arnold, disputed notification times and collection of the logs. P. 12 lines 11–19 describes heated conversations, says they did not discuss the times in great detail, and attributes to Griffith the view that DOD's testimony to Congress was wrong. It records that he had no information about the FAA press release on military notifications. The draft's p. 9 question about whether someone higher up directed a particular account remains a question. In public testimony, Arnold said he had not recalled the facts or had the timing information at the May 2003 hearing and accepted the later staff reconstruction. The review leaves individual knowledge, decisions and any instructions unresolved. [June 17 transcript, Arnold exchange](https://www.911commission.gov/archive/hearing12/9-11Commission_Hearing_2004-06-17.htm)
- **A wording difference in the draft.** P. 9 line 7's embedded text reads **“infrequent contacts”**. The visible line and fresh crop OCR read **“in frequent contacts.”** The memo reports exchange and discussions but gives no contact log. Both draft readings are preserved with their source layers.
