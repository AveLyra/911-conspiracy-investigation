# Atlantic City recording durations and the Millville website log

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD07 and operational audio custody. NARA's Box 115 yielded two full Atlantic City MP3s under **AEA #2310** and **AEA #2311**. Their position and time labels match the corresponding Ground Control and Arrival Data/Departure Data windows in the earlier Box 4 inventory. Both files decode in full. Their playback durations materially exceed their labeled UTC spans. The Millville Log Info PDF supplies a separate result. Its recoverable attachment is a website access log. The guard-channel recording sought for **1246–1248Z** remains open.
