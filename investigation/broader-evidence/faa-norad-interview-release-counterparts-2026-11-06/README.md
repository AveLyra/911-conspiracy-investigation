# Handwritten records of the February 2004 NORAD timeline interview

November 6, 2026 UTC · B129 / PD01–PD04

The National Archives' **ISCAP 2012-042 document 31** preserves handwritten notes from the March 1, 2004 interview. Page 16 records that Eberhart had not seen the revised NORAD timeline. Page 17 separately records that he did not know the Commission had received it and that he did not specifically order its drafting. The typed memorandum records his denial and adds a bracketed staff account that later interviews indicated he had been briefed from the timeline delivered on **February 23, 2004**. These are distinct statements about exposure, delivery and assignment. The handwriting adds direct evidence of what the note-taker recorded during the interview. The briefing record itself remains missing. [Handwritten notes, pp. 16–17](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-042-doc31.pdf#page=16) · [Typed memorandum, p. 8](https://www.archives.gov/files/declassification/iscap/pdf/2012-042-doc29.pdf#page=8)

The records came from the same interview and do not constitute independent witnesses. The handwritten notes and formal memorandum preserve a reported denial. The basis of the later staff account requires the interviews and briefing materials it refers to. A briefing using information from a version, review of that version as a document, and knowledge of its delivery can be different events. Which occurred remains open.
