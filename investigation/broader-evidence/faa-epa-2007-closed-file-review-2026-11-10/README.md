# How EPA OIG sampled and tracked closed investigations in its 2007 review

Public derivative of the November 10, 2026 source review UTC · B129 / PD02–PD05. EPA's public copy of **08-A-0036**, issued **November 20, 2007**, provides a contemporary example of the records used to review inspector-general investigations. It covers EPA's own three resource centers. The separate **December 2006 EPA review of DOT OIG**, its case-selection list and its findings on any individual DOT case remain unacquired. [EPA report cover](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPDF.cgi?Dockey=P100VNO8.PDF#page=1) · [Earlier DOT follow-up queue](https://github.com/AveLyra/911-conspiracy-investigation/blob/055ef54a857b46846d0038109bab7e108970f03a/investigation/broader-evidence/faa-postreport-disposition-2026-10-27/OPEN-RECORDS.md)

## What the EPA report documents

| Record locator | Source observation and limits |
| --- | --- |
| **Printed pp. 1–3**, PDF pp. 5–7 | The 2007 report cites the **December 2003 PCIE/ECIE** standards, December 8 Attorney General guidelines and peer-review guideline appendices **B–D**. It describes applying the guidelines' **Section 7, Sample Selection**, to **163** EPA investigations closed in calendar 2006. It reports **30** judgmentally selected closed cases, distributed among three centers. It describes three preliminary center reports and written or incorporated responses. This gives period-specific record names for a search. The original 2006 DOT review's selection basis and files remain unknown. |
| **Printed p. 3**, PDF p. 7, lines 7–26 | The report says **29 of the 30** selected cases had significant reportable actions documented, and counts **60 criminal-conviction-related actions, 13 civil-recovery actions and 40 administrative actions**, totaling **113**. It reports **full compliance** and describes its findings as procedural and as having no effect on outcomes. The case count, action count and compliance judgment have different denominators and purposes. A judgmental sample selected for outcomes supplies no estimate of their frequency across all **163** cases. |
| **Printed pp. 18 and 20**, PDF pp. 22 and 24 | The report describes the prior **Investigatively Closed** category, 90-day status reports and a March 27, 2007 direction to eliminate that category after the April 1 submission. It also reports **12** closed-file instances with no Quarterly Case Review Sheet. One supervisor reported sheets had been removed from **9 of 10** files at one center before headquarters transfer, based on treating them as management records. Available staff reported that supervisory reviews took place. In a separate finding, documented reviews were **May 2005** and **November 2005**. The report says the reviews fell short of quarterly frequency, while the supervisor reported having completed the required reviews without recording them in the plan. Preserve those accounts and locations together. |
| **Printed pp. 24–25**, PDF pp. 28–29 | The report identifies **three closed files** still in the field despite a rule referring to transfer within **20 days of receipt** of the headquarters closing memorandum. It describes a federal appeal, an administrative appeal and an outstanding arrest warrant. Management says the files were held at the assigned attorney's direction and discusses a future policy and tracking change. The exit briefing separately refers to **20 days after the memo was issued**. The dates of issue and receipt, the revised policy and the transfer records would be needed to test the timing. |

The 2007 review is a concrete example of how a closure label, management-file retention, shipment to headquarters, imaging and an administrative outcome can leave separate records. The earlier **2024 EPA review of DOT OIG** describes a **random sample of 30 of 135** closed files and supplies a case list. EPA's 2007 internal report instead describes judgmental selection from **163** and supplies aggregate counts in the inspected sections. The periods, reviewed organizations and methods differ. Neither sample design is assigned to the unacquired **2006** review. [2007 methodology, PDF p. 6](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPDF.cgi?Dockey=P100VNO8.PDF#page=6) · [2024 sample, PDF pp. 4–5](https://www.oig.dot.gov/sites/default/files/2024-10/EPA%20JI%20Peer%20Review%20September%202024.pdf#page=4)

## Discrepancies kept open

- On printed **p. 2**, PDF **p. 6**, line **8** says **“23 select criteria from the QAR Guidelines.”** Line **31** says **“22 selected criteria from the QAR Guidelines, Appendix D”** and the next lines describe **9** additional criteria and **930** total points. The second description gives **30 × (22 + 9) = 930**. Applying the same nine additions to the first count would give **960**, a conditional comparison. The completed case checklists would establish which items the reviewers applied. [Methodology](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPDF.cgi?Dockey=P100VNO8.PDF#page=6)
- The same page reports **18 of 27 (66 percent)** for the Western center's open cases. The fraction evaluates to **66⅔ percent**. Preserve the printed count and display percentage and the computed value. No rounding rule was acquired. [Methodology](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPDF.cgi?Dockey=P100VNO8.PDF#page=6)
- The raw PDF is **455,162 bytes**, SHA-256 **078418e196e5703534875a97526ed828013bec5d3e889b441c5939bd3091f907**. On those same bytes, `file` 5.41 reports **“PDF document, version 1.6, 17 pages”**. Poppler `pdfinfo` 26.05.0 and PyMuPDF 1.27.2.2 return **29**. Preserve all three outputs. The physical locators in this report identify pages in the rendering that was actually inspected.
