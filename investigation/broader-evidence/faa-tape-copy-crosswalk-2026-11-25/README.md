# FAA tape instructions: visual copy comparison

A visual comparison of the FAA records in [NARA AWA-320](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-237/7419198-9-11-faa/5-e-other/5-AWA-320-Package-ZOB-Miscellaneous-Data-REDACT-SEALED.pdf), physical PDF pages 11, 14, 32 and 33, with a one-page Commission archive member narrows two earlier source questions. Earlier local custody research identified the instructions and requested visual review of pages 14 and 33. This note reports document characteristics, not actual tape custody or compliance.

The [Commission archive](https://archive.org/download/911datasets/NARA_9-11_Commission_Records_Released_Jan_14_2009.zip) member `T8 B17 FAA Trips 3 of 3 Fdr- Memo from Jim May to ARTCC AF Facility Managers- Copy Tapes- No Media Contact.pdf`, accessed November 25, 2026, is 43,172 bytes with SHA-256 `c18e5db7ff3d10eff55a5891caceb1f741393df6a66c875317b53b307b5950c4`; the archived ZIP member length and CRC32 `495d4e2a` were verified. AWA-320 has SHA-256 `8d525f36445f571f418221aa1822c885e7d2f68adbcec255b22265da2031791c`. These hashes identify copies reviewed, not native originals.

The one-page archive member and AWA-320 pages 11 and 14 visibly have matching layout and markings and appear to reproduce one instruction, with differing redaction treatment. They are not independent orders. The images read **0800** to **2100** Zulu for the specified duplication period; embedded text in the one-page archive scan reads **0000** for the start. Both observations are retained; the earlier investigation already recorded the 0800 visual reading. This mismatch concerns extraction versus image, not proof that the order changed. Independent source images and native text remain useful checks.

AWA-320 pages 32 and 33 show related representations of a September 27 memo referring to two enclosed voice cassettes. Page 32 includes a routing/file block; page 33 has a signature image. The shared subject and wording make them related copies, not evidence of two distinct deliveries. Neither representation establishes actual delivery, cassette contents, or current custody. Signatures, contact information and source scans are not reproduced in this note.

**Open records:** native instruction; any signed releases, duplication and receipt logs; original media and associated inventories; and provenance of the memo versions. The instruction is not proof of compliance, and the documented copies are not proof of concealment, destruction or wrongdoing. Earlier source accounts and conflicting observations remain unchanged.
