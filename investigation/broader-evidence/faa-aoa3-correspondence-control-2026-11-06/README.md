# FAA correspondence control for the 2006 correction inquiry

November 6, 2026 UTC · **B129 / PD01–PD05**. FAA Order **1360.16A**, effective January 19, 2006, identifies the **FAA Executive Secretariat, AOA-3**, as the office responsible for correspondence policy, controlled items and the correspondence tracking system or its successor. The order supplies an FAA-side search route for the August 2006 OIG referral and any response. A later Federal Register notice explicitly links FAA correspondence-control records to **DOT/OST 041** for system-of-records coverage. The case-specific control number and records remain unacquired. [FAA order, §§ 1 and 4](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Order/ND1360.16A.pdf#page=1) · [2022 Federal Register notice, printed p. 61650](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2022-10-12/pdf/2022-22126.pdf#page=2)

The documents identify the FAA office and describe both the legacy FAA correspondence records and the DOT-wide notice. Keep **ACCIS**, the 2006 order's **Correspondence Control Management System or any successor system**, and the **DOT/OST 041** notice in their respective source contexts. The transition in published system coverage leaves the actual migration dates, file ranges, indexing continuity and relationship to any microfilm unverified. The microfilm policy itself leaves the custody or disposition of this case's records open.
