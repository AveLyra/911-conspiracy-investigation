# Three short Flight 93 telephone entries: displayed durations differ

November 16, 2026 · TEL02–TEL04 / AUD016-A1. Two public summaries describe three short attempted calls associated with Lauren Grandcolas to a residence, but display different duration sequences. This is a one-second discrepancy in published summaries; it is not evidence of a fabricated call or an independently measured conversation.
