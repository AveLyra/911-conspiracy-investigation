## Correspondence tracking and accession routes

- **A migrated system with records from the early 2000s.** NARA's March 14, 2022 inspection reports that the DOT Secretariat's electronic workflow captured drafts and edits, routed records for approval, scanned signed finals, and returned the final folder to the originating department. It also describes approval-button signatures for many records and standardized file and package metadata. Inspectors reported that older migrated records could be opened, while noting a lack of formal quality control for continued access and permanent records overdue for transfer. Those findings describe conditions at the inspection. No entry for this FAA matter or its later status was tested. [Inspection, pp. 27–30, especially p. 28 lines 20–30 and p. 29 lines 2–15](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/resources/executive-secretariat-mai-inspection-report.pdf#page=28)
- **Receipt and enclosures are named in the permanent correspondence series.** Item **DAA-0398-2024-0004-0001**, approved May 5, 2026, describes senior officials' signed major correspondence, including copies of incoming correspondence, OST responses and enclosures. It defines controlled correspondence by the need to record receipt and respond. The schedule gives **2000** as the approximate first year, **2000–2009** as the initial transfer span, and NARA transfer 15 years after its calendar-year cutoff after closure. The schedule also covers correspondence saved to network drives after delivery by email and excludes material retained as part of a case file. The initial span is a schedule field. Actual accession, package completeness and entry for the correction remain open. [Scope p. 1, lines 18–31, and item 0001, pp. 4–5](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-transportation/rg-0398/daa-0398-2024-0004_sf115.pdf#page=4)
- **Briefing books are a separate permanent series.** Item **DAA-0398-2024-0005-0001**, approved June 12, 2025, covers appointment and subject briefing books. The schedule notes that some material is separate from the calendar entry. It gives **2001** as the approximate first year, **2001–2009** as the initial transfer span and NARA transfer 15 years after calendar-year cutoff. The briefing series could hold a management-side copy or context if officials were briefed. A briefing would still leave formal disposition and delivery to be verified. [Briefing schedule, pp. 4–5](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-transportation/rg-0398/daa-0398-2024-0005_sf115.pdf#page=4)
