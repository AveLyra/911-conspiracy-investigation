# Retained research records, October 10, 2026

This supplement preserves 26 files retained locally during the worktree cleanup:
NBC video frame metadata, public-source acquisition response headers, and one
font configuration. The frame records and other unchanged files are preserved
byte for byte. Session-cookie and request-trace headers were removed from the
public response-header copies. Public headers use LF line endings and normalized trailing whitespace. [The manifest](manifest.json) records every
original and published hash, the removed field names, and the backup location.

Untouched originals are stored in an age-encrypted archive on the private
`roryscot/911:archive/preservation-supplement-2026-10-10` branch. A complete local
decryption round-trip matched the original archive bytes. The existing AveLyra
SSH private key restores that archive; no new recovery key was generated.

These are acquisition and reproducibility records, not additional verification
of a scientific result, historical allegation, or collapse cause. Existing
study protocols and limitations remain controlling. The original
[52,079-record preservation manifest](../preservation-2026-10-10/manifest.json)
remains unchanged and pinned to its earlier publication.
