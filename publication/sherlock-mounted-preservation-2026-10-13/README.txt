Mounted Sherlock media preservation

3459 original file paths are preserved unchanged with their SHA-256 hashes in media-manifest.json. The archive contains WTC7 video frames, visual and numerical study outputs, source videos, research reports and data sheets. This is preservation, not scientific validation or a finding of misconduct.

The full research context is retained separately in an encrypted private backup. Browser profiles, cookies, raw HTTP headers, internal records, and working notes are outside this public media archive. No original files were deleted.
