## Descriptive visual result

For Figure5-158, both passes identified the distinctive gray foreground roof
corner, tall slender capped upright, lower parapet/railings and orange form in
the same relative background region. The most clearly resolved orange form
in this declared sample is source index120, PNG `frame-000003.png`, encoded
PTS `20019/5000` seconds. This is a frame locator, not a historical event time.
Fainter orange forms are visible in the first two samples. Subsequent selected
stills did not resolve a distinct orange form, but haze, changing framing and
unobserved intervals preclude interpreting that as fire cessation.

For Figure5-157, samples360,420,474 show the wider dark/banded facade, bright
right-side strip and neighboring facade, gray foreground roof/uprights and
part of the far-left gridded enclosure. The observer correctly distinguishes
partial foreground agreement from a full match. The reference's distinctive
diagonal dark facade band is not adequately checkable through the candidate
haze. The close-up correspondence is stronger than this wider correspondence.

Both passes preserve material differences: crop, haze, tonal rendering and
orange silhouette. The report JPEG is considerably darker/more contrasty than
these acquired samples. We have not established whether that reflects source
generation, image processing, display differences or another instant of the
same view. It is not evidence by itself of either faithful photometry or
intentional exaggeration. No calibrated brightness/temperature inference is
made. The PNGs retain the native720×480 raster; accompanying metadata retain
the source SAR8:9 and interlace flags. No aspect correction or deinterlacing
was applied, and no geometric metric is inferred from an uncorrected raster.

The sampled file progresses from close framing to wider framing. This does
not prove the temporal ordering of the report's actual two stills, an
uninterrupted zoom, absence of edits or a six-second pulse. An exact-frame
claim requires a denser reference search and source-transform accounting.

The strongest objection to overinterpreting the positive result is that a
copied or edited recording at another instant from the same camera position
can satisfy scene geometry and current catalog checks. Conversely, the newly
resolved positive orange/flame-like appearance must not be discarded just
because it does not establish severity or mechanism. This unit supplies neither an
exaggeration finding nor independent validation of NIST's thermal inputs.
The integrated assessment remains without an independently established strict
ordering of causal alternatives; that does not imply equal probabilities.
