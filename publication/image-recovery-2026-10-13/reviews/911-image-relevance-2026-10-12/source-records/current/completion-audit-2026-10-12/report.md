# WTC7 assessment and remaining discriminating tests

October 12, 2026. Research only. The new comparator and documentary results
do not establish a new robust ordering of fire-triggered collapse and quieter
deliberate intervention. They do sharpen what a satisfactory explanation must
demonstrate: the timed loss of the actual remaining support paths, not merely
damage to some connections or a replay after prescribed support removal.

The full investigation is incomplete. The [requirement map](requirements.md)
distinguishes completed bounded studies from unresolved charter obligations.
This assessment uses the [27 pinned inputs](inputs.json), the [scope](SCOPE.md)
and [equal review criteria](REVIEW-PROTOCOL.md). It does not newly authenticate
every original, reproduce the physical experiments, or certify the whole
inherited corpus. Earlier source-reading and replication limitations remain.

The most important two-sided result is that **substantial damage can leave
effective support, while a connected fire-exposed floor system can also suffer
substantial partial failure**. Both shortcuts—“damaged means absent” and
“connected means unable to fail”—are therefore unsuitable substitutes for
building-specific force, contact and timing accounting. The narrower claim
about WTC7's rapid widespread support loss is not decided by either experiment.

## Physical compatibility and overall evidence

### NIST's particular sequence

Actual fire observations, early interior-related visible motion, developed
conditional structural calculations and relevant component experiments provide
affirmative evidence. They are not merely the authority of an agency or an
animation. The published collapse/arrest contrast is also informative: in the
reported model, local damage does not inevitably cause global collapse.

The strongest objection remains the missing independently testable transition
from the actual exposure and local failures to sufficiently rapid loss of
remaining global support. Bearing, contact, alternate attachments, restraint
and redistribution must be retained where justified; failed bolts alone are
not a calculation that these capacities vanish. Native matched collapse/arrest
states and histories have not been located in the reviewed holdings. A
published contrast is not an independently reproduced pair.

The new comparators strengthen the need to test that transition. They neither
complete it nor demonstrate that it was physically impossible. A robust,
observation-compatible result from authenticated inputs would strengthen this
specific route; robust arrest or incompatible sequence/timing would weaken it.
Neither outcome alone resolves every other fire pathway or an accompanying
intervention.

### Other fire-triggered pathways

Failure of a particular NIST assumption would not falsify every fire pathway.
Conversely, “some other fire pathway” cannot inherit explanatory credit without
specifying its own exposure, initiation, support retention, propagation and
observable predictions. Fuel-system possibilities require their own installed
equipment, operating-state, release, ventilation and heating evidence. They
must not be silently merged with ordinary office-fire claims.

The current additions identify no newly demonstrated building-specific
alternative fire chain. They supply useful constraints on proposed chains,
not a replacement mechanism immune to falsification.

### Deliberate support removal

Suitably placed support loss is compatible with the terminal motion at a broad
mechanical level. Rapid loss of effective support and the unclosed fire-to-
propagation chain justify investigating deliberate intervention; an actor's
identity is not a prerequisite for discriminating physical evidence.

The strongest objection is that a prescribed removal pattern explains motion
conditional on a state, without demonstrating how that state was historically
produced. WTC7-specific mechanism, preparation or authenticated material
evidence could independently change this comparison. The new contract accounts
do not supply that bridge: common project names, differing dates and possible
access cannot stand in for an executed operation.

Conventional conspicuous blast scenarios and potentially quieter thermal,
mechanical or mixed methods remain separate hypotheses. Conditional acoustic
weight against a specified loud scenario applies only with a credible detection
opportunity; it does not transfer automatically to every intervention. The
user-reported bang in C and repeated comparator sounds remain reported events
of unresolved origin, not silence and not identified charges. The NBC picture
screen adds no new listening or recording-specific detection calibration.

### Ordering and uncertainty

The strongest objection to withholding a strict overall ordering is cumulative:
observed fire, early interior-related motion and developed conditional mechanics
can rationally support a weak provisional fire preference. That preference
depends on historical applicability and the evidentiary weight assigned to the
models and experiments. It is not a measured likelihood or a conclusion of
innocence. The new work does not supply a robust likelihood comparison against
quieter intervention.

The fact of fire alone does not distinguish fire acting alone from intervention
during an acknowledged fire. Its causal weight depends on evidence connecting
that exposure to structural response; repeated photographs are not repeated
independent demonstrations of that connection.

Accordingly, this update adopts **no new strict ordering**, not equal odds and
not a claim that rational conditional preferences are forbidden. It does not
upgrade intervention through elimination or downgrade affirmative fire evidence
because the chain is incomplete. Mixed or unresolved possibilities remain open
without receiving extra credit for being unconstrained. No numerical probability
is justified by this purposive comparator collection.
