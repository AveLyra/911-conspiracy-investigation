# Counterfactual state–corporate conspiracy inquiry: a falsifiable research frame

**Prepared:** 2026-09-04

**Reviewed:** 2026-09-07

**Status:** Working research only; not a factual finding, expert report, or pleading theory

**Question:** If a senior U.S. government/corporate network had deliberately caused or enabled the September 11 attacks in order to facilitate war and related interests, what evidence should exist, what currently documented facts are relevant, and what would count against that hypothesis?

## Why maintain a separate counterfactual track

This is a **red-team** inquiry: deliberately take the hypothesis seriously enough to derive predictions and seek records that could confirm or defeat it. It is not a license to convert motive, association, or an anomaly into proof of causation. The main technical inquiry remains hypothesis-neutral; this file holds the alternative causal theory so that neither track quietly borrows assumptions from the other.

Use these labels throughout:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Observed** | A source directly records the event, relationship, or item. |
| **Compatible** | The observation could occur if the conspiracy were true, but is not distinctive of it. |
| **Discriminating** | The observation would be materially more expected if the conspiracy were true than if it were false. |
| **Contrary** | The observation is materially less expected if the conspiracy were true. |
| **Missing** | The asserted evidence cannot presently be located, authenticated, or tied to the proposition. |

The relevant proposition must be specific. “The government knew” is too vague to test. This memo uses the demanding version: senior officials and private participants intentionally arranged the attacks or their essential enabling conditions, then concealed the operation. A lesser proposition—warnings were mishandled, officials used the attacks opportunistically, or later policy was influenced by oil and security interests—has different predictions and must not be treated as proof of the demanding one.

## Counterfactual predictions

If the demanding hypothesis were historically true, at least some of the following classes of evidence should be recoverable or leave independent traces:

1. **Decision and command:** a dated instruction, meeting record, secure communication, diary, tasking, or credible participant account linking an identifiable decision-maker to the event.
2. **Operational access:** records placing organizers, contractors, or intermediaries in positions to alter aviation security, building access, emergency response, or forensic custody; legitimate access alone is insufficient.
3. **Coordination with the hijackers:** communications, money, travel, source handling, or other contact that connects U.S. participants to the actual attack team rather than only to later policy.
4. **Foreknowledge with specificity:** a contemporaneous, attributable prediction of the target, method, and timing that could not plausibly derive from public warning, emergency communications, or rumor.
5. **Physical implementation:** authenticated traces of preparation or use of a proposed destructive method—procurement, installation, residues with sound provenance, charge/explosive signatures, or documented cutting/alteration—rather than visual resemblance alone.
6. **Concealment with intent:** a traceable order or coordinated deviation from preservation procedures whose purpose was to suppress relevant evidence, not merely an improvised disaster-recovery decision.
7. **Benefit plus a causal chain:** benefits or contracts connected by pre-attack planning to the attack decision. Subsequent benefit alone is not a causal chain.

The test is symmetric: a verified record in any category would raise the hypothesis; repeated, well-proven absence where the hypothesis predicts a trail lowers it. A secrecy claim cannot make every missing record confirmatory, because then the hypothesis becomes unfalsifiable.

## What the counterfactual lens changes—and does not change

It changes the *order of questions*: start by asking what a concealed operation would have needed and whether its prerequisites leave evidence. It does **not** change the meaning of the evidence.

- A relationship to oil, intelligence, or defense contracting can establish a possible motive or a conflict requiring scrutiny. It cannot establish that an individual ordered an attack.
- A later Iraq contract can support an inquiry into procurement and influence. It cannot by itself turn a later beneficiary into a pre-attack planner.
- Lost or unavailable physical material weakens confidence in any exact reconstruction. It is not evidence of intentional destruction without an order, custody irregularity, witness evidence, or other intent-bearing fact.
- An official investigation can be incomplete, opaque, or wrong in a technical detail without being a knowing cover-up. Conversely, institutional prestige cannot substitute for source release and reproducible analysis.

## Highest-value evidence searches

These searches should begin with source inventories and custody records, not conclusion-oriented web material.

| Priority | Record family | Discriminating question | Result that would matter |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pre-9/11 White House, NSC, CIA, FBI, FAA, NORAD, and Secret Service records | Is there attributable, contemporaneous foreknowledge of a particular attack that led to a non-protective act or a cover story? | A dated source, recipient, content, and action/inaction chain. Generic threat warnings do not suffice. |
| 2 | Hijacker finances, travel, communications, and source-handling files | Is any alleged domestic sponsor connected to a named hijacker or essential facilitator? | Corroborated contact, payment, tasking, or handling evidence with a documented custody path. |
| 3 | WTC 7 access, maintenance, tenant, security, recovery, and debris-custody records | Was there unauthorized preparation or an intentional departure from preservation practice? | Records or witnesses showing who had access, what changed, and who ordered particular removal or disposition. |
| 4 | Physical evidence inventories and laboratory files | Do samples with a credible chain of custody support a proposed destructive method? | Replicable testing, blinded controls, documented sample provenance, and method-specific findings. |
| 5 | Iraq policy and contracting records dated **before** September 11 | Did an identifiable actor connect a contemplated attack or false attribution to a plan for war or contracts? | Pre-attack documents linking the asserted means, target event, and expected policy/commercial outcome. |

For every proposed item, record: source and original location; date created and acquired; author/custodian; chain of custody; exact claim supported; independent corroboration; alternative reading; and what would falsify the proposed inference. Preserve negative results.

## Boundary with the current NIST FOIA case

The pending matter concerns NIST’s WTC 7 modeling materials. It may support a narrow, legitimate question: can the public independently inspect the technical basis and limits of NIST’s stated causal model? It is **not** a factual vehicle for accusing named people or companies of orchestrating 9/11. This memo therefore creates no `FACT-*`, timeline, exhibit, claim-matrix, or pleading entry.

The appropriate litigation-safe formulation remains: obtain and evaluate the relevant technical records under FOIA, while keeping any counterfactual causal hypothesis out of the administrative narrative unless independently verified primary records warrant a separate, deliberate decision.

### Present evidentiary posture

The leading result is **not** a confident binary ranking. The evidence-scope distinction in the [technical synthesis](wtc7-collapse-evaluation-synthesis.md#bottom-line) controls this working assessment:

1. **With conditional weight assigned to the broader published engineering analyses, fire-triggered progressive collapse remains the provisional first choice.** That preference depends materially on disputed calculations; observed fires, debris damage, and eastern/penthouse changes do not independently establish the hidden fire-to-failure chain. Using only the independently checked subset here, a strict ordering against deliberate support removal is unresolved, not 50–50.
2. **NIST’s precise model chain remains an audit target, not a settled black box.** The relevant question is whether complete released or produced materials permit a baseline rerun and sensitivity tests for geometry, connection behavior, member-treatment rules, damage transfer, and timing.
3. **Deliberate support removal remains underdetermined.** It is kinematically compatible with aspects of the descent but presently lacks authenticated method-specific physical and operational support in this review. The absence of that support should be weighted against demonstrated search coverage and preservation limits; this review does not justify a reliable explosive-versus-thermitic ordering.
4. **State–corporate orchestration is less supported still.** Background associations, later contracting, and institutional error are compatible with many histories; the reviewed record contains no direct link to attack planning, execution, or concealment.

This is a working research judgment, not a case assertion. It should be revised only by source-pinned technical reruns or authenticated records that bear on a prediction listed above.

### Highest-value next checks

1. Freeze and hash any produced model package, then map its dependency graph before executing it.
2. Run the documented baseline before changing assumptions; preserve solver version, inputs, outputs, and failed runs.
3. Pre-specify sensitivity tests for connection geometry, failure/element-treatment rules, damage-transfer timing, and fire scenarios, including the Floor 11–13 observation mismatches; compare the same observable outputs, including roofline motion, member histories, and energy/contact behavior where available.
4. Treat a successful rerun as reproduction of a configured model, not validation of its causal explanation. Compare it against independently measured video and audio observations with stated uncertainty.
5. Keep the separate operational-evidence search focused on direct planning, access, finance, foreknowledge, and custody records. Do not let model uncertainty stand in for any one of those evidence categories.
6. For the separate withholding issue, ask a qualified expert whether identified reconstruction errors materially undermine the particular predictive capability invoked as dangerous. Historical mismatch does not automatically make reusable connection models harmless. Keep that analysis in the [model-fidelity withholding memo](wtc7-model-fidelity-withholding-challenge.md), not as a concealment finding here.

## Provisional bottom line

The counterfactual is useful only as an evidence-generation device. The presently reviewed material establishes historical connections, later Iraq-related contracting, technical and preservation limitations, and serious institutional failures. It does **not** establish an intentional state–corporate operation, an Iraq–al Qaeda operational partnership, explosive demolition, or deliberate destruction of evidence. The next responsible step is to seek the five record families above and allow authenticated evidence—including negative evidence—to change the hypothesis.
