## Executive finding

The strongest new source recovery is Jacqueline Maguire's original signed September 7, 2005 declaration. It substantiates the court's account of the 85-record search and says the released impact CD-ROM contained **copies of two time-lapse recordings** from Pentagon parking-lot cameras. The public trial exhibit contains two descriptions with inconsistent clip numbering, but the numbers do **not** demonstrate a missing item. The September 12, 2001 FD-597 lists 16 surveillance tapes, a CCTVware recorder/hard-drive unit (model 951-0008), and a key received from Greg Goff. No public item-level crosswalk recovered here proves those particular tapes or that unit generated the P200022 CD-ROM or joins each to its current disposition. Acquisition/capture-rate and clock-correction findings remain blocked by missing native records.

A search of the accessible completed research index and linked available results found no completed N15 follow-up beyond the November 7 baseline. This is an original-source desk continuation, **not a claim that a separate billed Deep Research service ran**.

## New, narrow technical conclusions and stopping rules

- The signed Maguire declaration independently removes ambiguity between **one responsive CD-ROM** and **two camera time-lapse recordings**, without identifying the source recorder.
- The trial stills display **September 12, 2001 around 17:37:19–17:37:24**, incompatible on its face with a September 11 event-clock overlay. This is an **observed displayed-date mismatch**, not proof of a particular conversion bug, cause, corrected clock or acquisition cadence.
- Some court stills explicitly identify **zoomed** variants of the same displayed second (for instance A/B, E/F, G/H and I/J), so 12 JPEGs do not represent 12 independent instants.
- P200022's downloadable MPEG **compilation playback metadata**, even if inspected, would **not** identify native camera capture frequency. Those require the native media/frame timing, CCTVware 951-0008 manual/configuration and export software/forensic logs.
- Nothing here establishes a DAT original, 1 frame/second native recording, or software-specific time-offset correction: the Austin/Pennington transcript and relevant technical chain were not authenticated.
- Court-reported absence of a plane in Citgo/Doubletree views has defined coverage limits; unknown other camera contents cannot be promoted to clear approach footage. No authenticated missile footage or missile inference follows from the incomplete custody ledger. The aircraft identity question has other evidence streams (NTSB Flight 77 radar/FDR) independent of these custody gaps.

## Source dependence and strongest competing explanations

- **FD-597 + NCT index**: the index references the receipt; it is **not a second observation** of the 16 tapes.
- **Maguire affidavit + Bingham opinion**: the opinion summarizes the declaration, so the 85 arithmetic has one primary witness/inventory origin. Court approval is legal review, not independent examination of 85 recordings.
- **ST00004 + P200022 catalog + 12 public JPEGs**: all concern the same trial exhibit; they are separate descriptions/presentations, not three independent acquisition sources.
- **Judicial Watch requester account + court appellate chronology**: the former is party reporting; the latter supplies court-documented timing and legal grounds, not source-tape receipt identities.
- **Ordinary media processing explanation**: a responsive compilation assembled from two parking-lot views, with exhibit numbering inconsistencies and exports, is compatible with available documents; **not confirmed** without the build manifest.
- **Omission, misfile, additional copy, or unreleased original**: cannot be excluded solely from absent item crosswalk. Such alternatives need a specific object, its inventory identifier and contrary records.
