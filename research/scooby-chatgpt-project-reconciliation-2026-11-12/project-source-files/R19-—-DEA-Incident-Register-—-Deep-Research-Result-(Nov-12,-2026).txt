# Scooby Investigation 15 — DEA art-student draft: source-span incident register and bounded dispositions

**Desk audit, 12 November 2026.** Source: [DEA, *Suspicious Activities Involving Israeli Art Students at DEA Facilities*, public 60-page reproduction](https://antiwar.com/rep/DEA_Report_redactedxx.pdf). The face of this draft has no signed or dated cover. “June 2001” is a reported date, not a verified cover date. This is a register of **source narrative clusters** (not independent incidents or proved intelligence operatives). A range may contain several incidents and several paragraphs may narrate the same incident. Separate citations within each range use the source's own paragraph numbers; do not infer PDF page from paragraph number.

**Source transcription caution:** The web-readable mirror was used to navigate the scanned original and may expose text hidden by defective electronic redactions. Only identities visible on the original reproduction are intended; no guessed or apparently de-redacted agent identities are relied upon. Minor spelling variants in the transcription are not resolved as identity matches.

**Coverage:** All canonical paragraph numbers 1–182 are represented by at least one row below. Paragraph 19 is not actually printed: the Caribbean section records “No response.” Paragraph 101 is likewise not printed: an unnumbered Little Rock visit follows ¶100. Paragraph 75 is reused for two different cases; the Houston section reuses 70. These are preserved. **The draft's 125-entry person/organization indexing section is a separate derivative cross-reference, not 125 additional incidents**. Multiple unnumbered visits embedded within a numbered paragraph are aggregated here where names or precise separation are missing; an exact unique-incident count is therefore not possible from this draft alone.

Every source-chain column refers to a field-office report *as repeated in the DEA compilation*, unless an actual underlying report is explicitly acquired. “Direct” means that the described source was a claimed participant/observer, not that this review obtained the original signed statement. No later adjudication is independently verified unless specified.

## Three case histories: provenance and stopping points

**Orlando — ¶¶95–100, [original pp.31–32](https://antiwar.com/rep/DEA_Report_redactedxx.pdf#page=31).** On May 2, a DEA employee described a maroon Mitsubishi photographing the building, but did not obtain plate/driver identification. On May 3, Peer Segalovitz was interviewed by DEA and the FBI, and INS reportedly arrested him for unauthorized employment on a B-2 visa. The separate Akyuz Shmuel Sagiv interview ended in release. Another maroon-car observation at 1:30 p.m. did not identify the vehicle as the first car or associate either with a named seller. The opening's assertion of MDMA-related telephone returns is **not joined to a readable caller, account, number or DEA case number**. The **already-sent November 5, 2026 Orlando DEA FOIA request** covers the original encounter notes, phone-trace inputs and returns, headquarters coordination, negative results, and actual disposition. The available sent thread has no substantive DEA production. **Stop:** no independently established MDMA match or operational-tasking link.

**Miami FPS — first ¶75, [original p.25](https://antiwar.com/rep/DEA_Report_redactedxx.pdf#page=25).** The DEA compilation reproduces an FPS report dated February 9 identifying Ohad Aharon and Yafit Segal, solicitation tickets and separate citations for counterfeit Social Security documents, and their release. Segal described a for-profit art-selling business (Universal Art) and subsequent independent selling, an ordinary commercial explanation for the paintings without explaining the false documents. It says a State Department matter was open, **not that the citations were adjudicated or that espionage was found**. The subsequent second paragraph numbered 75 concerns different people on February 15 and is not corroboration of this case. A **separate November 9, 2026 DEA/FPS request already exists** concerning Miami FPS/Universal Art; no new request is made. **Stop:** no FPS citation identifiers or State Department case number supplied.

**Volk Field — ¶¶178–182, [original pp.50–51](https://antiwar.com/rep/DEA_Report_redactedxx.pdf#page=50).** On May 19, Tsvi Watermann and Gal Kantor reportedly sought permission to visit the base museum, then were observed on an active runway. Documents and vehicle rental identify them, and a developed film reportedly showed a person in the car on the runway, with **no aircraft images**. The compilation records sheriff handling, misdemeanor trespass and payment of $210 fines. Its tourism explanation is attributed to their interviews; restricted-area entry and ordinary tourism are not mutually exclusive. **Stop:** no original photographic set, incident reference, county charging number, or signed payment/disposition was recovered. An official disposition is **reported by the DEA draft**, not independently established from court records.

## Dependence, contrary evidence and six-link attribution test

* **Affiliation:** Passport, visa, current employer, art-company address and reported military service can establish a person's background or a commercial connection. They do not identify an intelligence employer.
* **Tasking:** None of the three selected cases exposes an authenticated instruction, handler, intelligence account or operational order.
* **Access:** Orlando/ Miami show agency-area solicitations; Volk shows actual runway entry, independently distinguishable from a completed intelligence operation. WTC/UMS access is not established by any of these.
* **Knowledge:** No acquired contemporaneous record links any identified person in the three selected cases to a named hijacker, the 9/11 operational plan or a specific foreknowledge statement.
* **Action:** Commercial sales, document possession, restricted-area conduct and immigration offenses are documented at differing evidentiary strengths; no verified intelligence product is recovered.
* **Effect:** No demonstrated compromise of a DEA investigation, air-base defense activity or 9/11 prevention has been joined to these incidents.

**Positive alternatives and limitations:** The Orlando drug-number association remains a specific unverified statement, not automatically disproved. The widely cited 2003 DEA testimony on an international MDMA investigation commencing in Miami in July 2001 concerns a separately described operation; it has no recovered shared case/person/phone identifier with Orlando. The 2002 FBI/DOJ public denials are official retrospective positions but not publicly reconstructed incident-by-incident evidence tests. Conversely, the draft's repeating field narratives, agency alerts and later press descriptions cannot be counted as independent observers.

**Review boundary:** This desk audit inspected the supplied 60-page public scan and a derivative text rendering and checked specified public records and the already-sent Orlando request. It has **not** acquired original agency case jackets, a signed final version, new private correspondence or paid records. No external contacts, new FOIA submissions, fees or changes to pending matters occurred. This is not a new paid Deep Research run.
