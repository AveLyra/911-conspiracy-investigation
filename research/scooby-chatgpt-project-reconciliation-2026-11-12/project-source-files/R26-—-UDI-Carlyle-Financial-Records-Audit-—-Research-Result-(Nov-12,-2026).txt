1. Original credit agreement recovered

The most important new finding is that the August 13, 2001 credit agreement is publicly available, contrary to the acquisition gap recorded in Scooby's November 11 financial review.

United Defense — Credit Agreement, Exhibit 4.4

Original SEC filing • October 22, 2001 S-1 • Accession 0000928385-01-502154

The agreement identifies Iron Horse Investors, United Defense Industries, lending institutions and the administrative and syndication agents. It is dated August 13, 2001, and establishes an $800 million credit facility. A July 2, 2002 amended-and-restated version is also publicly available.

The August 13 versus August 31 discrepancy is not yet resolved. The original agreement uses August 13, as does the December 2001 prospectus's refinancing description. UDI's 2004 Form 10-K instead dates the refinancing August 31. The original transaction may have involved distinct agreement, funding or accounting dates, but closing and bank-disbursement records are needed to establish that explanation.

Two further findings constrain the analysis:

The intervening sales are substantial. Carlyle and affiliated holders reported selling 11,805,336 shares in August 2003 and 1,491,346 in November. Their reporting scope differs from the TCG-only figures; the proceeds cannot be assigned to one fund without the individual selling schedules. 
sec.gov

The business did not enjoy uniform protection. Crusader was cancelled in 2002, approximately 180 employees were affected, and termination-cost settlement remained incomplete in June 2004. This provides specific adverse evidence against a guaranteed-profit interpretation. 
sec.gov

The reported bin Laden family investment remains a separate question. Contemporary reporting describes a roughly $2 million fund interest and an October 2001 exit, but I found no authenticated subscription, capital-account or redemption record identifying the legal recipient. Neither UDI ownership tables nor repeated newspaper accounts establish that an individual received later offering proceeds.
