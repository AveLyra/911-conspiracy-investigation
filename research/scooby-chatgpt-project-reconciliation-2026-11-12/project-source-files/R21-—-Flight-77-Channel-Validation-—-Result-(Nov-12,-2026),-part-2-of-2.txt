Limits on the arithmetic
The pressure differences above are differences between published engineering values, not geometric
descent rates near impact. No extrapolation to an impact time was performed. No pressure-setting
adjustment, static-error correction, radio-height substitution or terrain subtraction was silently added.
Those operations require the specific conversion, calibration and time alignment that remain unresolved.

this export's columns, not sensor installation. A blank cell can reflect the export schedule and must not automatically be
coded as zero, invalid or forward-filled telemetry.

Calculations and independence rules
The declared ATC composite interval reproduces as 2,639 seconds =43min59s. A four-hour UTC-to-EDT
conversion is a timezone operation and is separate from any recorder-clock correction. No seconds-level
correction was adopted without its original derivation.
Primary radar range/azimuth can be an independent physical measurement after target association and
clock calibration. Transponder altitude can share aircraft air-data sources with the FDR. Tracked/coasted
coordinates add processing and prediction. Repeated plots, NTSB's combined study and the
Commission's synthesis therefore cannot be counted as multiple independent terminal-height/clock
measurements.
The current fetched radar representations differ in byte size from catalog metadata. The DCA difference equals its LF count,
consistent with newline normalization; the IAD difference is one byte larger than its LF count. The handwritten PDF also has
an unresolved representation-size difference. Exact fetched hashes and these limits are preserved in the manifest.

Positive and contrary evidence retained
The NTSB factual report says the recorder operated normally and identifies a validated group. That is

unconfirmed/improperly recorded fields, so its general statement cannot validate every label. The
recovered transmittal and matching file sizes support the archived attachments' provenance, while their
mirror chain and runtime identity remain bounded. [S1 p.2; S6]

configuration or a validated equivalent raw-to-output implementation; channel calibration and
time/latency records; the separate Time Correlation Report; radar dictionary, site/clock/scanner metadata
and target-association chain; independent preceding-flight observations/logs for known sensor states.

What was acquired from the author-decoder record
The archived output index supplies Dulles, Los Angeles, Chicago and Orlando landing CSVs,
FlightTimes.csv and AllSubframesFltDeckDoor.csv. They derive from the same recovered recorder data
and author software. FlightTimes records 25 sync-loss boundaries with Excel-added interval calculations;
it is not an independent airline log. The landing names and apparent ground segments do not supply
independently observed door states or calibrated runway-height tests. [S12]
The recovered source ZIP has a main project ApplicationVersion 1.10.0.0 and a backup1.7.0.0, while
assembly/file versions remain1.0.0.0. The changelog says version1.1 adjusted acceleration formulas to
match NTSB CSV output,1.4.1 added a pressure-altitude variant based on the TXT, and1.7 added raw
coarse/fine outputs. No output-to-build/input-hash/run-log join was recovered. Agreement achieved by
reference to NTSB output is not independent sensor calibration. No downloaded code was executed.
