1. **Carrier remains unidentified** (report pp. 1, 5, 9). No Zim-specific vendor award, executed moving contract, paid invoice, bill of lading, subcontract schedule, job-specific insurance certificate, dock/elevator booking, receiving ticket, or delivery signature was recovered. 2. **Relocation milestones are not shipment dates** (pp. 2–3). The report distinguishes the September 4 business opening, a September 5 personnel-relocation claim, the October 18 formal dedication, continuing New York operations, and post-attack IT recovery. Conflicting headcounts refer to different populations and remain unresolved. None establishes the date or contents of a specific truckload.
3. **Project roles do not establish carriage** (pp. 4–5). Hunter/Jim Pirot, project manager Nadav Kaplan, KPI Projects International, Lyall Design Architects, Gee’s Group, and Zim personnel have reported project, design, development, staffing, or systems roles. No authenticated record makes them the physical carrier or identifies a carrier’s crew.
4. **Strongest counterevidence to a complete, emergency withdrawal** (pp. 3, 5): the Norfolk move was publicly announced in April; reports place personnel and computer/communications functions at the WTC on September 11; a later report describes retained New York space. This weighs against a sudden, complete pre-attack departure but does not identify the ordinary mover or rule out a subcontract.
5. **Best next record join** (pp. 8–9): Zim customer-side procurement/payment or bill-of-lading records paired with dated Norfolk receiving/acceptance evidence for the same goods. Continue the existing Pirot, Port Authority and FBI matters without duplicating their scopes. No contact, request, amendment, archive order, fee, or pending-matter change was made.
