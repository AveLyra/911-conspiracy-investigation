Finding
The physical carrier remains unidentified. No Zim-specific moving contract, vendor award, paid invoice,
bill of lading, subcontract schedule, job-specific insurance certificate, dock/elevator booking, receiving ticket
or delivery signature was recovered. Neither Urban Moving Systems (UMS), Max Movers nor an alternative

technology stages. It does not establish a single shipment date or complete preattack withdrawal from the
World Trade Center. A contract would identify an engagement; proof of performance requires a shipment,
completion or receiving record connected to that engagement. Permission to enter a building is another
separate fact.

Reconciliation
The dates need not describe the same event. Operational opening, a personnel transition, ceremonial
dedication, retained regional functions and technical recovery can coexist. The sources support separating
these categories; they do not prove which load moved on which day. The phrase about nine days before the
attack must not become a verified September 2 dispatch date by arithmetic alone.
The headcounts remain unresolved. Transferred managers, local hires, the remaining New York
department, people physically present on September 11 and eventual Norfolk employment are different
populations. Even after making those distinctions, the November 14 figure cannot be treated as a verified
count of staff physically transferred. A personnel/department roster and dated payroll or move register are
needed. This uncertainty does not invalidate the well-supported distinction between headquarters relocation
and continuing New York operations.

Strongest evidence against the proposed link
Against the “emergency complete withdrawal” account: the September relocation was public in April.
Contemporary reporting places staff and computers at WTC on September 11; a later account describes
planned retained New York space. [S1, S6, S8] These facts weigh against that characterization. They do not
determine which ordinary carrier was hired.
Against treating UMS's role as established: the located explicit attribution is a 2005 Madsen allegation,
not a contemporaneous move record. Bellaciao displays September 20, 2005; a later Scooby review
recovered an Online Journal-layout version dated September 21, PDF p. 4. The passage supplies no named
firsthand moving witness, transaction identifier or supporting document. These are a publication trail, not
independent corroborating accounts. The PDF mirror failed during this run; the Bellaciao text was read
directly. [S12, P2]
Against the proposed evidentiary shortcuts: carrier registration/insurance, shared business operations,
a visitor-card receipt and an unidentified Manhattan job do not provide the missing customer-shipment
match. Conversely, no affirmative record was found proving that UMS or Max performed no part of
the work. A bounded negative search is not that proof. A confirmed ordinary move would establish
commercial work; it would not by itself establish intelligence affiliation, foreknowledge or explosives activity.
