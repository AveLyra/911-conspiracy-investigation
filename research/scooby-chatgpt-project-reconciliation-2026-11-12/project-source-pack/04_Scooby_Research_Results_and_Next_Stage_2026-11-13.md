## R13 — Iron-rich spheres

**Chat:** [Iron Sphere Audit](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-p-6aed53a21aec8191b9aab8307b9c8e49-scooby/c/6af73dd6-cb60-83ea-ab81-d20a0282c689)  
**Result:** *Scooby R13 — Iron-rich spheres: denominator and provenance audit* (13 Nov 2026).

The audit reports that RJ Lee Table 3's 5.87% (TP-01 WTC-affected dust) and 0.04% (selected background office-building dust) are mean classified compositions of iron-rich spheres. Their ratio is 146.75, but this is not a ratio of iron mass. Table 3's caption refers to statistical p-values while the displayed columns say “Mean of Composition (%)”; the two percentages are not p-values. The report dates the 130 Liberty Street sample collection to June 8, 2002 and background collection to February 4, 2003, so this comparison does not directly measure dust collected on September 11, 2001.

The reviewed RJ Lee report interprets morphology/chemistry as evidence of high-temperature processing, but it does not directly measure molten structural-steel mass or establish when each sphere formed. A Fe/O signal alone does not settle metallic versus oxide phase. The USGS atlas is an independent institutional characterization, but the audit did not establish matched samples or a quantitative iron-sphere comparison; detailed USGS table access returned 403 and original Iron-03/04 image/spectrum bytes were not reconciled.

**Next stage and result:** The GPT-6 Pro source-recovery follow-up is complete; see the R13 recovery manifest in its chat. USGS's official TIFF directory gives exact Iron-03/04 photomicrograph and spectrum-image paths, rounded file sizes, and modification timestamps. These metadata locate files but do not provide their pixels, checksums, specimen histories, or acquisition dates. Table 1's footer advertises an 18 MB ZIP containing an XLS table and TIFFs, but its actual filename/bytes were not recovered. EPA's NEPIS text includes a first-party RJ Lee November 18, 2004 submission describing SEM examination of 100 particles, “volume percentages,” and per-sample directories containing counts/images; it does not connect that later protocol to the December 2003 Table 3 denominators. The May 2003 Insurance Claim Report, Volume III remains unlocated; RJ Lee's 2003 report precisely cites Volume III §§5.1 (statistical analysis) and 5.2 (protocols, TP-01 dated May 10, 2002). **Go** to update the source register with file/protocol leads; **no-go** on specimen-level reconstruction until original files, particle-to-sample/run mapping, and applicable raw classifications/uncertainty tables are recovered.
