## Assessment

The submission identifies useful, concrete records trails. It does **not** establish that UMS or Max Movers accessed the WTC for a demolition operation, planted explosives, worked for Mossad, coordinated with the hijackers or Saudi intermediaries, or arranged the attacks to produce an Iraq war. Those are distinct propositions requiring distinct evidence. An intelligence affiliation, if established, would not alone establish a demolition role or advance knowledge.

The most efficient follow-ups seek identifiable records across the missing links: (1) dated building access and moving-job records, (2) the conditional Exxon video match and final FBI assessment, and (3) the original Classic/Miami lead and seized notebook entry. Separately, test the alleged CIA–Saudi liaison and the physical-evidence claims without treating a result in either area as attribution to UMS.
