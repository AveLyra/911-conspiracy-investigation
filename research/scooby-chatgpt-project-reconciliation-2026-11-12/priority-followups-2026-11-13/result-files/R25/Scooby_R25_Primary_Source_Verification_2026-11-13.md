## Reconciliation and conflict assessment

**Interpretation:** The records describe different milestones: withholding of invoices, removal of that withholding after definitization, and a residual indirect-rate process before final payment/closeout. Nothing requires those milestones to occur together. The Senate's Q16 identifies the unresolved exception as indirect rates for TO1–4; it does not say those orders received no earlier payments. [S1–S3]

The broader company wording on p. 17 must be retained, not silently narrowed. It says the outstanding RIO issues were resolved while discussing fuel-related audit findings and settlement of questioned costs. Reading this as settlement of those issues is compatible with the Army's rate exception; reading it as completed contract closeout is not supported. The record does not supply the settlement instrument or separately dated answers needed to eliminate every timing/scope ambiguity. [S2–S3]

Within the examined passages, no primary record expressly denies the $57.4 million withholding or the later release. The apparent conflict concerns the breadth of the company's settlement language, not a reproduced bank transaction. This was a bounded comparison, not a search of every later closeout record.

**Accounting boundaries:** Neither withholding nor release establishes money recovered *from* the contractor. A refund/offset must be evidenced separately. An accepted or negotiated cost is not itself proof of the settlement date of a cash transfer. Base/award fees are not a net-profit calculation. No ceiling, obligation, company-wide earnings figure or unrelated LOGCAP recovery was substituted for the missing RIO payment data.

LOGCAP TO0031/0042 and PCO Oil South/RIO II remain separate. The 10-K's adjacent discussion of those programs does not assign their balances to RIO I.
