# WTC 7 competing collapse-model audit

Question: Given public NIST, UAF, FEMA, and UCL materials, which claims about WTC 7 collapse initiation and propagation are directly supported, model-dependent, or underdetermined, and what matched tests would discriminate them?

Decision: Prioritize public-record retrieval and reproducible experiments that can distinguish competing collapse mechanisms without treating any model output as historical proof.

Minimum decision-relevant evidence: A reviewed, source-pinned comparison that preserves competing models, assumptions, falsifiers, missing inputs, and result limits.

Decision-change observations: Lower confidence in a proposed mechanism if a source-pinned matched simulation fails under its declared assumptions.; Do not elevate a prescribed support-removal comparator to a historical cause without independent evidence of an initiating mechanism.; Revise the ranking if independently reproduced multiview predictions materially favor one mechanism under shared geometry and event definitions.

Decision owner: independent-research-review

Available data sources: Public NIST NCSTAR 1A, 1-9, and 1-9A reports and public NIST FAQs/errata.; Public Hulsey, Quan, and Xiao 2020 UAF technical report.; Public Orabi et al. 2022 Fire Technology paper and its open manuscript.; Public FEMA 403 preliminary report.; Repository research notes only as pointers to their underlying public sources.

Unavailable or out-of-reach data: NIST full simulation results, connection calculations, break-element code, and complete model handoff materials.; A locally executable, source-pinned UAF model archive with exact case-entry decks and run histories.; Camera-original or equivalently authenticated video records and a shared model-render/view package.; Authenticated physical evidence linking any prescribed support-removal scenario to a historical mechanism.
