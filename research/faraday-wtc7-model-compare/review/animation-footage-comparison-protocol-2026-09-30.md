# Animation–footage comparison protocol (review only)

**Status:** exploratory visual comparison completed; not an approved or registered experiment. This written protocol documents the scope and limits after initial source/frame review; it was not frozen prospectively, and the displayed timestamps were selected after viewing the sequences. It does not authorize a claim about physical cause or validate the model.

## Scope

Compare visible progression in the candidate NIST simulation derivative and the repository’s Camera 3 secondary copy. NIST describes a whole-building “View from Northwest” for the physics-based visualization and locates Camera 3 near street level west of West Street near Harrison. This supports a broad view-family match, not a calibrated camera match.

Because the candidate files are derivatives and no defensible clock synchronization or exact viewpoint calibration is established, compare each sequence on its own source-video clock. Do not treat equal columns, timestamps, or apparent durations as synchronized observations. Do not infer physical displacement, collapse rate, cause, or numerical agreement.

## Observation procedure

1. Review full source clips and identify the full-building simulation segment after the hard cut.
2. Sample representative frames at source-video timestamps without interpolation, perspective correction, crop registration, or retiming. The accompanying sheet shows animation samples at 16.8, 17.8, 18.8, 20.8, 23.3, and 24.8 s and Camera 3 samples at 10.5, 11.5, 12.0, 12.5, 13.5, and 14.0 s. These are independent sequence samples, not synchronized pairs.
3. Record only directly visible features: apparent continuity and deformation of the model’s exterior mesh, gross model silhouette, camera-footage roofline/facade movement, and occlusion by smoke/foreground.
4. Preserve disagreements and visibility limits. Do not tune a time offset after comparing the sequences.

## Acceptance criteria

A completed comparison must include an inspectable side-by-side progression sheet, source identities and hashes, source-clock labels, direct observations separated from interpretation, and an explicit synchronization/viewpoint limitation. It must not claim quantitative agreement, prove an explanation, or treat this review artifact as registered evidence.
