# NYC September 11 records: PDF review and access log

Research date: September 16, 2026

This dossier logs 19 accessible PDF sources: 7 archive-related originals or reproductions, 4 historical comparison PDFs, and 8 disclosure-history filings/exhibits. The available text of 15 PDFs was read through (67 pages in those files); 4 longer PDFs were reviewed only in part. All 19 files together contain 351 pages, but 351 pages were NOT reviewed. Three additional archive PDFs, A01–A03, were newly read in full in this batch (11 pages). A04–A07 include work reported in the preceding conversation. No original PDF files were successfully saved to the local workspace; the deliverable is a source-linked review, not a PDF archive.

## Method and limits

Page references are one-based PDF positions; stamped identifiers are included where verified. Full-text review means the available PDF text was read from beginning to end, not that every graphical element was audited. Selected page images were checked where stated. A source’s allegation, proposed procedure, laboratory measurement and completed action are different forms of evidence and are not interchangeable. A new portal posting or newly read scan is not proof of first public disclosure. No previously unreleased WTC 7 engineering fact was established in this batch.

### A01 — Fuchs memorandum on Nadler’s indoor-air concerns

**Date:** 2002-02-28 · **PDF length:** 2 pages · **Review:** full available text

**Review extent:** Both pages read; both page images inspected. Key recommendation: PDF p. 2 / NYC-WTC_000147323.

Ester Fuchs summarizes Nadler’s objections and DOH’s contrasting account of indoor testing. She conditionally warns that justified concerns would mean residents were not being informed adequately. She recommends discontinuing wet-mop/rag advice and seeking FEMA support for indoor cleanup.

## Historical comparisons

### B01 — EPA worker-protection letter and DOH health/safety memorandum

**Date:** 2001-10-05 and 2001-10-06 · **PDF length:** 5 pages · **Review:** full available text

**Review extent:** Entire PDF read. Images checked: PDF p. 2 (EPA letter) and p. 4 (DOH reoccupancy discussion).

EPA describes enforcement gaps in worker protection. Kelly McKinney’s DOH memorandum records disagreement between OEM and DEP about reopening blocks near Ground Zero, despite two days of low fiber counts. It reports truck-route and remaining-debris concerns and says McKinney was told the Mayor’s Office was directing opening the next week.

**Limit:** These are attributed contemporary accounts. The memo places the final opening decision with DOH. It is not WTC 7 collapse evidence.

**Release-history status:** Court-filed June 20, 2024; therefore demonstrably public before this portal release.
