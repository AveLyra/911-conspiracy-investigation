# WTC 7 — NYC September 11 Document Portal: research leads

**Review date:** September 16, 2026.  
**Status:** Working research. Catalog-level shortlist only. Not pleading text, not a completed crawl, and not a finding that any listed document or fact was previously unreleased.

## Answer first

**Specific WTC 7 records are worth investigating, but none have been verified here as previously unreleased information.** The portal’s search and PDF endpoints failed through the tools available for this review, so this is a review of an independently captured catalog—not a completed crawl of the underlying documents.

The city’s **September 8, 2026** announcement expressly includes a WTC 7 collection. An independent September 9 catalog snapshot lists **3,025 documents** in that collection. That establishes a substantial research target, not that every document or fact is newly public.

The independently captured folder catalog dated September 11, 2026 lists three portal **sources**: **DEP Hard Copies (68 Boxes)**, **DORIS Giuliani**, and **WTC 7**. These are distinct snapshots; the counts are not a current live inventory. Catalog columns are source, box, folder label, document count, total pages, and first Bates identifier. Where a row contains multiple documents, the identifier and URL identify the **first document**, not a combined PDF of the whole group. Catalog labels are not verified PDF titles and do not establish that work described in a label was performed.

**The research now has specific cross-collection leads and a verified disclosure history, but still no verified previously unreleased WTC 7 engineering fact.** The underlying portal PDFs remain inaccessible through the retrieval methods tested here.

## Two routes into WTC 7

1. **Separate `WTC 7` source collection** (3,025 documents in the September 9 summary)—construction, OEM, FDNY, fuel-system, and related folders (prioritized below).
2. **`DEP Hard Copies (68 Boxes)`**—primarily DEP environmental and post-collapse response material, but the catalog also contains **WTC 7–labelled folders**, not only sampling records. That is a second route beyond the dedicated collection.

## What the “not located until 2025” history actually establishes

These are **verified primary records** from the Chevat v. NYC DEP Article 78 proceeding (*Chevat v. New York City Dept. of Envtl. Protection*, Index No. 155678/2024). They explain custody and search disputes; they do **not** by themselves establish a motive for prior non-location or any WTC 7 engineering fact.

| Record | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| **City Law Department letter, September 16, 2025** ([PDF](https://www.911healthwatch.org/files/2025-0916-NYC-Letter-withdrawing-cross-motion.pdf)) | Withdraws DEP’s dismissal motion after asserting a diligent search had found no responsive records; acknowledges **newly located boxes believed to contain responsive material**. The letter says **“multiple boxes,” not 68**. | That every September 2026 portal document is novel; that DEP’s later 68-box figure applies to this letter; any WTC 7 structural fact. |
| **Law Department World Trade Center Unit preservation directive, circa 2002** ([PDF](https://www.911healthwatch.org/files/Document-3-Law-Dept-Preservation-Directive.pdf); also described at NYSCEF #53 in [reply affirmation](https://www.911healthwatch.org/files/Reply-As-Filed-12-21-2025.pdf)) | Original WTC documents were **collected and scanned**; personnel were told **not to dispose** of them and to preserve them for possible future litigation; contact the Law Department before disposal. Petitioner papers add that agencies delivered September 11 documents for Law Department scanning and received them back—making the **scanning system and custody trail** concrete investigation subjects. | That every responsive record remains indexed and searchable today; that DEP’s 2024 “no records” certification was accurate. |
| **Supreme Court decision, May 28, 2026** ([PDF](https://www.911healthwatch.org/files/Exhibit-1-DEP-Decision.pdf)) | Finds DEP’s **February 29, 2024** FOIL **appeal denial arbitrary and capricious**; grants the petition; permits **pre-hearing discovery** concerning searches and earlier representations. | A motive for the failure to locate records; that any particular WTC 7 drawing is newly public. |

**Novelty distinction:** Some disclosure **predated** the September 2026 portal. Musa Ali’s **December 19, 2025** affirmation ([PDF](https://www.911healthwatch.org/files/Deft-Aff-in-Opp-to-Discovery.pdf), NYSCEF Doc. 59) states DEP **reviewed, scanned, and produced 123 documents on October 16, 2025**, followed by in-person box inspections in November and December 2025. Accordingly, **“newly posted in September 2026” cannot automatically mean “never previously disclosed.”** Separate work must compare portal identifiers to earlier productions and FOIL correspondence (including `NYC-WTC_000173529`).

Litigation papers elsewhere describe **68 boxes** of responsive DEP material (~340,000 pages). That figure comes from the Article 78 record, not the September 16, 2025 withdrawal letter’s “multiple boxes” wording.

## One apparent “discovery” that would not be new

NIST’s **September 2005 NCSTAR 1-1J**, printed page 27 / PDF page 61, section 6.3, already described removal of existing sprinkler piping and heads from the seventh-floor OEM generator room, along with the first-floor fuel-tank room’s fire-suppression arrangements (including the Inergen system and related fire-protection details). A newly posted record mentioning those features would not, by itself, reveal a previously unknown fact—although the underlying source document might be newly accessible.

The NIST report’s searchable text was available for that pin. Its screenshot endpoint failed in this session, so no independent visual confirmation of that page was obtained here.

## Test for a genuinely new disclosure

For each candidate, inspect the actual PDF; record author, date, revision, page stamps, attachments, and exact relevant passage. Search earlier releases by those identifiers and by distinctive text, not solely by the newly assigned NYC-WTC identifier. Compare the contents against earlier reports and released source files. Separate a newly accessible source document from a fact already summarized publicly. A construction sketch or change request alone does not establish completed work, structural inadequacy, or a collapse mechanism.

A failed search for an older copy should be reported as “no earlier copy located in the sources searched,” not “never previously released.”
