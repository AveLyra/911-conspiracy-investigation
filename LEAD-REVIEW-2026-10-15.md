# Inclusive review of investigation leads — October 15, 2026

This review broadens what the investigation retains and makes findable. A lead
does not have to look sinister, contradict NIST, be relevant to a pleading, or
survive every ordinary explanation before it belongs in the research record.
Small metadata differences, uncertain source relationships, reporting errors,
missing original records and methodological limits can identify useful tests.
Their inclusion and their evidentiary weight are separate decisions.

The tables below revisit existing published research. They are a navigation
and follow-up register, not new measurements, a fresh primary-document audit,
or an accusation. Each row identifies what the cited research actually reports,
why it is worth retaining, and what remains unestablished. Read the linked
source unit for its original dates, methods, qualifiers and subsequent updates.
Existing DISC identifiers remain controlling for the released-file inventory.

## Inclusion and preservation rules

Retain a specific observation, discrepancy, provenance gap, model dependency,
failed comparison, or original-record locator when its source and investigative
question can be stated. Retain a previously raised allegation as a referral
when its source is still unverified; do not silently promote it to a finding.
An ordinary explanation is a hypothesis to test and record alongside the lead,
not a reason to erase the observation. A later test gets its own dated result;
the earlier conflicting values, accounts and failed attempts remain preserved.

Prioritize by the possible consequence and the availability of a discriminating
test. Grade separately (1) whether the observation is established, (2) whether
it affects a technical conclusion, and (3) whether it supports historical
misconduct. Strong support for (1) does not automatically support (2) or (3).
Include favorable evidence, ordinary context and failed suspicions as well.

Multiple descriptions or copies of one source are not independent corroboration.
Several errors in one report may share an editorial or production process.
Testing a proposed pattern requires the complete relevant population, including
unremarkable and contrary cases, its dependencies and the ordinary error rate.
Counting the rows here cannot supply a probability of concealment or demolition.

## Referrals that still need an original source

Earlier discussion also raised removal of bomb dogs, unexplained work on
supposedly empty floors, specific explosives/thermite claims, and proposed
financing or intelligence relationships. Keep these as source-authentication
referrals, including inconvenient possibilities, without supplying a missing
witness, date, building, sample or operational link by inference. A documentary,
audio or material claim needs its own exact source, custody/context, competing
account and discriminating test. Nothing in this register authenticates those
claims or links the Urban Moving Systems employees to preparing WTC 7.

If a claim is reported through a documentary, retain the edition, timecode,
exact assertion and underlying witness/document. Do not count multiple films
repeating it as independent evidence. A thermite/material allegation needs
sample identity, collection/custody, controls, raw analytical data and a
mechanism-specific comparison; it is not established by a generic bang,
unusual metallurgy, a hot sample, or an unlocated final laboratory result.
